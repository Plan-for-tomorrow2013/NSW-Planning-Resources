# 10.8 Development on land in Area 4

Development on land identified as "Area 4" on the *Key Sites Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) all lots comprising the land are amalgamated into a single lot, and
- (b) the development includes adequate provision for pedestrian and road infrastructure that is configured and located in a way that will allow a suitable level of connectivity within the Cherrybrook Station Precinct, and
- (c) at least 1,700m2 of the land will be used for the purposes of a single public open space that is at ground level, and
- (d) at least 50% of the public open space will receive at least 4 hours of direct sunlight between 9am and 3pm on 21 June each year.
