# 10.1 Definitions

In this part—
*Cherrybrook Station Precinct* means land identified as "Cherrybrook Station Precinct" on the *Key Sites Map*.
*Cherrybrook Station Precinct Design Guide* means the *Cherrybrook Station Precinct Design Guide* published by the Department in August 2026.
