# 8.8 Ground floor business premises and retail premises in Zones E1 and SP4

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within the Bella Vista Station Precinct or the Kellyville Station Precinct identified as "Active street frontage" on the *Active Street Frontages Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a building, or the change of use of a building, on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the ground floor of the building will be used only for the purpose of business premises or retail premises.
