# 4.1D Exceptions to minimum lot sizes for certain subdivision at Gables

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 4" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to the subdivision of land to which this clause applies resulting in a lot that has an area of at least 300m2 but less than 450m2 if—
- (a) the consent authority is satisfied that the lot will have sufficient area to enable, and will otherwise be suitable for, the erection of a dwelling house on the lot, and
- (b) a development control plan has been prepared for the land.

**(3)**
This clause applies despite clauses 4.1, 4.1AA and 4.1B.
