# Dictionary — K

### Kellyville Station Precinct

*Kellyville Station Precinct* means the land identified as "Kellyville Station Precinct" on the *Sydney Metro Northwest Map*.

### Key Sites Map

*Key Sites Map* means the *The Hills Local Environmental Plan 2019 Key Sites Map*.

### kiosk

*kiosk* means premises that are used for the purposes of selling food, light refreshments and other small convenience items.
> Note: See clause 5.4 for controls relating to the gross floor area of a kiosk.
> Kiosks are a type of [*retail premises*](r.md#retail-premises)—see the definition of that term in this Dictionary.
