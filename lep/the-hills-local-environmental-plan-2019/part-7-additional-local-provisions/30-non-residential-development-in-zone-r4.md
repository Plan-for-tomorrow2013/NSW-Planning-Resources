# 7.34 Non-residential development in Zone R4

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are to ensure development for the purposes of shop top housing and mixed use development that includes a relevant non-residential use—
- (a) is appropriate in scale for the location, and
- (b) does not detract from the established centres hierarchy, and
- (c) provides for active street frontage.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development for the following purposes on land in Zone R4 High Density Residential—
- (a) shop top housing,
- (b) mixed use development that includes a relevant non-residential use.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) only the ground floor of a building resulting from the development will be used for a relevant non-residential use, and
- (b) the gross floor area used for each of the following purposes will not exceed 200m2—
  - (i) a single business premise,
  - (ii) a single restaurant or cafe,
  - (iii) a single shop.

**(4)**
In this clause—
*relevant non-residential use* means the following—
- (a) business premises,
- (b) restaurants or cafes,
- (c) shops.
