# 7.35 Development at Middleton Avenue, Castle Hill

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land in Castle Hill—
- (a) Lot 209, DP 249973, 27 Middleton Avenue,
- (b) Lot 210, DP 249973, 29 Middleton Avenue,
- (c) Lot 211, DP 249973, 31 Middleton Avenue,
- (d) Lot 212, DP 249973, 33 Middleton Avenue.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on the land is 50m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land is 3.3:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) at least 5% of the total floor area on the land will be used for the purposes of affordable housing (the *affordable housing component*), and
- (b) all dwellings comprising the affordable housing component will be used for the purposes of affordable housing for at least 15 years from the date the occupation certificate is issued for the building containing the dwelling.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 30 November 2026.
