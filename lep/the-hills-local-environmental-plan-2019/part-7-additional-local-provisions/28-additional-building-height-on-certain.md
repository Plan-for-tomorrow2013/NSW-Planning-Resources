# 7.32 Additional building height on certain land at Samantha Riley Drive, Kellyville

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area M" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a building height of up to the building height shown for the land on the *Incentive Height of Buildings Map*.

**(3)**
Subclause (2) does not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied a development control plan is prepared and provides for the following—
- (a) design principles drawn from an analysis of the site and its context,
- (b) the subdivision of land that promotes a high standard of urban design and amenity,
- (c) the location and nature of a public domain to encourage the increased use of public transport, walking and cycling,
- (d) a road and footpath network that provides access to all development lots,
- (e) a staging of works across the site to deliver the public domain.
