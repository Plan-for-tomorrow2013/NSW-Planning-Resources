# 7.36 Development at 2 Fishburn Crescent, Castle Hill

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 1316896, 2 Fishburn Crescent, Castle Hill.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on the land is 88m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land is 4.1:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 5% of the total gross floor area of all buildings on the site will be used for the purposes of affordable housing for at least 15 years from the date of the issue of the occupation certificate.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 30 November 2026.
