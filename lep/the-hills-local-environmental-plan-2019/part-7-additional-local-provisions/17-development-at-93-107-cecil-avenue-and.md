# 7.18 Development at 93-107 Cecil Avenue and 9 and 10 Roger Avenue, Castle Hill

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 93-107 Cecil Avenue and 9 and 10 Roger Avenue, Castle Hill (the *subject land*), being—
- (a) Lots 20 and 27, DP 15399,
- (b) Lots 1 and 4, DP 531559,
- (c) Lots 1 and 2, DP 547897,
- (d) Lots 1-4, DP 581293,
- (e) Lots 1 and 2, DP 591676,
- (f) Lots 5 and 6, DP 705913,
- (g) Lots 21 and 22, DP 778595,
- (h) Lots 5 and 6, DP 29141.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development that results in more than 460 dwellings on the subject land.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development that results in less than 8,025 square metres of commercial floor space on the subject land.
