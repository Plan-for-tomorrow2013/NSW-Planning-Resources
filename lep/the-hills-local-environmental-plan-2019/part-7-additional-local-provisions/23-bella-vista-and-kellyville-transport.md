# 7.27 Bella Vista and Kellyville Transport Oriented Development Precincts Design Guide

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 3" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the *Bella Vista and Kellyville Transport Oriented Development Precincts Design Guide*.
