# 7.12 Maximum commercial floor space at 488-494 Old Northern Road, Dural

**(1)**
This clause applies to that part of Lot 2, DP 839151 (being land at 488-494 Old Northern Road, Dural) that is in Zone E1 Local Centre.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development that results in more than 6,900 square metres of the gross floor area of all buildings on the land to which this clause applies being used for the purpose of commercial premises.
