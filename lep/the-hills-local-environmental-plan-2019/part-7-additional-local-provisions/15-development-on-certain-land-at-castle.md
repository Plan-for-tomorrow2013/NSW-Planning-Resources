# 7.16 Development on certain land at Castle Hill

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 1" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the potential effects of the development on proposed road infrastructure in the locality.

**(3)-(5)** (Repealed)
