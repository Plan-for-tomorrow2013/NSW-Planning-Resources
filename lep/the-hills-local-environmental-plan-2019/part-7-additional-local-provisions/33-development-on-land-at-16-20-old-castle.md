# 7.37 Development on land at 16-20 Old Castle Hill Road, Castle Hill

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land in Castle Hill—
- (a) Lot 10, DP 881332, 16 Old Castle Hill Road,
- (b) Lot 11, DP 881332, 18 Old Castle Hill Road,
- (c) Lot 1, DP 204335 and Lot 20, DP 222257, 20 Old Castle Hill Road.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.4, the floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land may be up to 11.1:1.

**(3)**
Clause 7.11 does not apply to development on the land.

**(4)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 31 December 2026.
