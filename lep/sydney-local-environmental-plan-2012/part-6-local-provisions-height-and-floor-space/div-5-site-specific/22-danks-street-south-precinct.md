# 6.43 Danks Street South Precinct

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to provide that a building in the Danks Street South Precinct demonstrating design excellence is not eligible for additional floor space.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as the "Danks Street South Precinct" on the *Locality and Site Identification Map* (the *Danks Street South Precinct*).

**(3)**
Clause 6.21D(3)(b) does not apply to development in the Danks Street South Precinct.
