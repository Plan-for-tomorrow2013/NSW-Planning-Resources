# 6.60P 881-885 Bourke Street and 207-229 Young Street, Waterloo

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lots 1 and 2, DP 1308636, 881-885 Bourke Street and 207-229 Young Street, Waterloo.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, a building on the land may have a height of up to 115m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land may be up to 2.5:1.

**(4)**
Clause 6.43 does not apply to development carried out under this clause.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 30 September 2026.
