# 6.60O 383-395A Kent Street, Sydney

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to encourage commercial development on land to which this clause applies that includes a pedestrian link connecting Kent Street and Sussex Street, a shared loading dock facility and the removal of commercial car parking.

**(2)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 778342, 383-395A Kent Street, Sydney.

**(3)**
The maximum building height for a building on land to which this clause applies is RL 189.8m.

**(4)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a floor space ratio of up to the ratio of the sum of the following to the site area—
- (a) the gross floor area permitted as a result of applying the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*,
- (b) the gross floor area resulting from applying a floor space ratio of 7.65:1,
- (c) any accommodation floor space for which the building is eligible under clause 6.4,
- (d) any end of journey floor space for which the building is eligible under clause 6.6,
- (e) if the building is a building demonstrating design excellence—the gross floor area, determined by the consent authority, of up to 10% of the sum of the amounts specified in paragraphs (a)-(d).

**(5)**
The total floor space ratio calculated under subclause (4) must not exceed 20.3:1.

**(6)**
Subclauses (3), (4) and (5) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the development will not result in a building used for the purposes of residential accommodation or serviced apartments, and
- (b) there will not be a commercial car park on the site, and
- (c) the development will include a pedestrian link connecting Kent Street and Sussex Street, and
- (d) the development will include a shared loading dock facility that will be available for use by surrounding businesses.

**(7)**
Clauses 6.5A and 6.21D(3) do not apply in relation to development carried out under this clause.

**(8)**
To avoid doubt, clause 6.18 prevails over this clause to the extent of any inconsistency.
