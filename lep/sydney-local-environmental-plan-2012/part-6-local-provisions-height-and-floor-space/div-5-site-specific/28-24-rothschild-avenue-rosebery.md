# 6.50 24 Rothschild Avenue, Rosebery

**(1)**
This clause applies to 24 Rothschild Avenue, Rosebery, being Lots 410 and 456, DP 7534 and Lots 1 and 2, DP 456612.

**(2)**
Clause 6.14 and Division 4 do not apply to a building on land to which this clause applies.

**(3)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development consent must not be granted for development, on land to which this clause applies, for the purposes of residential accommodation or tourist and visitor accommodation.
