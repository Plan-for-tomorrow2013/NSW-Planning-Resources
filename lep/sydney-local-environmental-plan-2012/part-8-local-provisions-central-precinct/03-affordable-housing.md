# 8.3 Affordable housing

**(1)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on land in the Central Precinct.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 30% of the total gross floor area used for the purposes of residential accommodation will be—
- (a) used for affordable housing, and
- (b) owned and managed by a government agency.

**(3)**
Clause 7.13 does not apply to development to which this clause applies.
