# 7.30 Waterloo Estate (South)—Area 2

**(1)**
This clause applies to Waterloo Estate (South)—Area 2.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the *Design Guide—Waterloo Estate (South)* published by the Department in November 2022.

**(3)**
Clause 6.21D(3)(a) does not apply to a building on land to which this clause applies.
