# Schedule 6C Contribution requirement for certain development on Planning Proposal Land

*clause 7.13B*

| Planning Proposal Land | Contribution requirement |
|---|---|
| Land identified as "Area 1" on the *Affordable Housing Map* | The total amount of— |
|  | the contribution applying to the development under clause 7.13, and |
|  | if the part of a building intended to be used for a purpose other than residential accommodation has a floor space ratio of less than 1.5:1—9% of the gross floor area equivalent to the amount by which the floor space ratio of the building exceeds 1.5:1 |
| Lot 15, DP 10721, 111 Cooper Street, Waterloo | The total amount of— for 87.5% of the total floor area of all buildings—the contribution applying to the development under clause 7.13, and for 12.5% of the total floor area of all buildings—9% of that total floor area. |
| Lots 6, 7 and 9, DP 10721 and Lot 8, DP 1147179, 221-223 Cope Street, Waterloo | The total amount of— for 67% of the total floor area of all buildings—the contribution applying to the development under clause 7.13, and for 33% of the total floor area of all buildings—9% of that total floor area. |
| Lots 1-41, SP 79210, 233 Cope Street, Waterloo | The total amount of— for 73% of the total floor area of all buildings—the contribution applying to the development under clause 7.13, and for 27% of the total floor area of all buildings—9% of that total floor area. |
| Lots 1-58, SP 69476, 110 Wellington Street, Waterloo | The total amount of— for 68% of the total floor area of all buildings—the contribution applying to the development under clause 7.13, and for 32% of the total floor area of all buildings—9% of that total floor area. |
| Lots 10 and 11, DP 10721, 116 Wellington Street, Waterloo | The total amount of— for 66% of the total floor area of all buildings—the contribution applying to the development under clause 7.13, and for 34% of the total floor area of all buildings—9% of that total floor area. |
