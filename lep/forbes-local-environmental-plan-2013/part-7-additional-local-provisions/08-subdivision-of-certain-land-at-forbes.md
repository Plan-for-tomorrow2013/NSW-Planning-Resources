# 7.9 Subdivision of certain land at Forbes

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure dwelling houses are not developed on certain land at high risk of flooding.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "River Road Area" on the Lot Size Map.

**(3)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development consent may be granted for the subdivision of land to which this clause applies only if—
- (a) the subdivision is for the purpose of the erection of a dwelling house on each lot resulting from the subdivision, and
- (b) each lot resulting from the subdivision will have a minimum lot size of 2 hectares and consist of at least 1,000 square metres of land that is not identified on the *Flood Planning Map* as "Area 1", and
- (c) a dwelling house will not be located on land identified on the *Flood Planning Map* as "Area 1".
