# 7.10 Residential development prohibited on certain flood prone land in Forbes

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Restricted Development Area" on the *Restricted Development Area Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of residential accommodation is prohibited on land to which this clause applies.
