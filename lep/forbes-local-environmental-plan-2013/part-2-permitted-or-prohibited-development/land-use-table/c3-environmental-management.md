# Zone C3 Environmental Management

## 1 Objectives of zone

- To protect, manage and restore areas with special ecological, scientific, cultural or aesthetic values.
- To provide for a limited range of development that does not have an adverse effect on those values.
- To ensure development is compatible with the flood affectation on the land.

## 2 Permitted without consent

Bee keeping; Home-based child care; Home occupations; Water reticulation systems

## 3 Permitted with consent

Animal boarding or training establishments; Bed and breakfast accommodation; Boat launching ramps; Boat sheds; Building identification signs; Business identification signs; Camping grounds; Dual occupancies (attached); Dwelling houses; Eco-tourist facilities; Emergency services facilities; Environmental facilities; Environmental protection works; Extractive industries; Farm buildings; Flood mitigation works; Helipads; Home businesses; Home industries; Home occupations (sex services); Hostels; Jetties; Kiosks; Oyster aquaculture; Pond-based aquaculture; Recreation areas; Roads; Tank-based aquaculture; Water recreation structures; Water recycling facilities; Water supply systems

## 4 Prohibited

Industries; Local distribution premises; Multi dwelling housing; Residential flat buildings; Retail premises; Seniors housing; Service stations; Warehouse or distribution centres; Any other development not specified in item 2 or 3
