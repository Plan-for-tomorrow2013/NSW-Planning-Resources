# 7.19 Development on certain land at Wyee Point

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Wyee Point Area 1" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development for the purposes of an integrated tourist community, being a tourist destination containing tourist facilities, attached dwellings, dwelling houses, multi dwelling housing and residential flat buildings on land to which this clause applies.

**(3)**
In this clause, *an integrated tourist community* means a community that contains the following—
- (a) tourist facilities, including a range of accommodation types (including dwellings), and
- (b) a combination of land uses including community facilities, recreation areas, recreation facilities and retail premises, appropriate to the community's needs.
