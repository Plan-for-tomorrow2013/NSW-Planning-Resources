# 7.26 Development on certain land at North Wallarah Peninsula

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to encourage housing diversity appropriate for the unique environmental and residential character of the Central Precinct of the North Wallarah Peninsula.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Zone R1 General Residential that is identified as "North Wallarah Peninsula Area 3" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(3)**
Land to which this clause applies may be subdivided into 3 or more lots if the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) each resulting lot will face a road, and
- (b) each resulting lot will be at least 300m2 and no more than 450m2, and
- (c) a dwelling house or semi-detached dwelling will be erected on each resulting lot.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted to development involving the erection of a dual occupancy on a lot unless the lot is at least—
- (a) for a corner lot—500m2, or
- (b) otherwise—620m2.

**(5)**
The lot on which a dual occupancy is erected under subclause (4)(b) may be subdivided into 2 resulting lots with a minimum lot size of 310m2.
