# 7.7 Development on sensitive Aboriginal landscape areas

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to recognise and conserve sensitive Aboriginal landscape areas.

**(2)**
The consent authority may require an Aboriginal Heritage Impact Statement to be prepared before granting consent to the carrying out of development on land identified as "sensitive Aboriginal landscape area" on the *Sensitive Aboriginal Landscape Area Map*.
