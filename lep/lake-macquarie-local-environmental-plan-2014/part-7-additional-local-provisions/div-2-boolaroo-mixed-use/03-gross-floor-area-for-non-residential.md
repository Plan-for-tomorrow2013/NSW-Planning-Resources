# 7.32 Gross floor area for non-residential purposes

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure there is an appropriate mix of residential and non-residential land uses on land to which this division applies.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this division applies unless the consent authority has considered whether at least 2,000m2 of the gross floor area of all buildings on the land will be used for one or more of the following purposes—
- (a) centre-based child care facilities,
- (b) commercial premises,
- (c) community facilities,
- (d) entertainment facilities,
- (e) health services facilities,
- (f) information and education facilities,
- (g) recreation facilities (indoor),
- (h) registered clubs.
