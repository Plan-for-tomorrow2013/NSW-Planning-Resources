# 4.2C Dwelling houses in Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that a dwelling house is erected on land to which this clause applies only if the dwelling house would support the lawful agricultural use of the land.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a dwelling house on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the land is being, or is proposed to be, lawfully used for intensive plant agriculture or extensive agriculture, and
- (b) the dwelling house will be required to support the carrying out of that intensive plant agriculture or extensive agriculture, and
- (c) services for the supply of water and electricity to support that agricultural use are available or adequate arrangements have been made to make them available when required, and
- (d) the dwelling house is not likely to cause any land use conflict with existing agricultural uses being undertaken on neighbouring properties within the Zone.
