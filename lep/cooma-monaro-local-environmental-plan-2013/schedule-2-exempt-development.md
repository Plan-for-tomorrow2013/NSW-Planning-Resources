# Schedule 2 Exempt development

*(Clause 3.1)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies exempt development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional exempt development not specified in that Policy.

> Note: Exempt development may be carried out without the need for development consent under the Act. Such development is not exempt from any approval, licence, permit or authority that is required under any other Act and adjoining owners' property rights and the common law still apply.

## Advertising signs

**(1)**
Must be located in Zone RU5, R1, E1, E2, E3, E4, MU1, SP2 or RE2.

**(2)**
Maximum area—
- (a) if located on land in Zone E4 General Industrial or Zone SP2 Infrastructure—1.5m2, or
- (b) if located on land in any other zone—1m2.

**(3)**
Must not be illuminated.

**(4)**
Maximum 1 sign per premises.

**(5)**
Must be related to a lawful use carried out on the land on which the sign is located.

**(6)**
If mounted to the front fence or front wall of a building, must not protrude beyond the physical limits of that fence or building.

**(7)**
Must not be located on public land.

**(8)**
Must not be located on or in, or in relation to, a heritage item or in a heritage conservation area.

## Advertising structures

**(1)**
Must be located on land in an employment or mixed use zone, Zone SP2 Infrastructure or Zone RE2 Private Recreation.

**(2)**
Must not be erected on or above a roof line.

**(3)**
Maximum height—2m.

**(4)**
Maximum area—1m2.

**(5)**
Must be constructed in accordance with an engineer's certification for the structure and footings.

**(6)**
Must not be illuminated.

**(7)**
Must be constructed of weather-proof material.

**(8)**
Maximum 1 structure per premises.

**(9)**
Must not be erected on public land.

**(10)**
Must not be located on or in, or in relation to, a heritage item or in a heritage conservation area.

## Building identification signs

**(1)**
Must be located on land in an employment or mixed use zone, Zone RU1 Primary Production, Zone RU5 Village, Zone R1 General Residential, Zone SP2 Infrastructure or Zone RE2 Private Recreation.

**(2)**
Maximum area—
- (a) if located on land in Zone SP2 Infrastructure, Zone E4 General Industrial or Zone RE2 Private Recreation—1.5m2, or
- (b) if located on land in any other zone—1m2.

**(3)**
Must not be illuminated.

**(4)**
Maximum 1 sign per premises.

**(5)**
Must relate to a lawful use carried out on the land.

**(6)**
If mounted to the front fence or front wall of a building, must not protrude beyond the physical limits of that fence or building.

**(7)**
Must not be located on public land.

**(8)**
Must not be located on or in, or in relation to, a heritage item or in a heritage conservation area.

## Business identification signs

**(1)**
Maximum area—1m2.

**(2)**
Must not be illuminated or flashing.

**(3)**
May be either a shop window sign, under awning sign or front awning sign.

**(4)**
Maximum 1 sign per premises.

**(5)**
Must relate to a lawful use carried out on the land.

**(6)**
If flush-mounted to the front fence or front wall of a building, must not protrude beyond the physical limits of that fence or building.

**(7)**
Must not be located on public land.

**(8)**
If there is no awning on the premises, no sign is to be extended more than 4.6m above ground level.

**(9)**
Signs attached to an awning must be at least 2.7m above ground level (existing).

**(10)**
Shop front signs must not cover more than 25% of the facade.

**(11)**
Must not cover mechanical ventilation inlet or outlet vents.

**(12)**
Must not be located on or in, or in relation to, a heritage item or in a heritage conservation area.

## External lighting

**(1)**
Must not be for the lighting of private tennis courts or sports fields.

**(2)**
Must not cause glare to adjoining properties or street.

**(3)**
Must not be a permanent free-standing structure.

**(4)**
Maximum height—3m.

## Farm dams in Zone RU1

**(1)**
Must be 1ML or less in capacity.

**(2)**
Must contain a means of managing water overflow.

**(3)**
Must be a minimum of 50m from any property boundary.

**(4)**
Must not involve works within 40m of the banks of a named watercourse.

**(5)**
Must not contain a spillway more than 1m in height.

**(6)**
Must comply with the *Water Management Act 2000*.

## Inflatable signs (balloons and blimps) in employment or mixed use zones

**(1)**
Must not be attached to the roof or parapet of a building.

**(2)**
Must be securely fixed to the ground.

**(3)**
Maximum height—3m.

**(4)**
Must not be inflated with flammable or explosive gases.

**(5)**
Maximum 1 per business.

## Outdoor dining areas

**(1)**
Must be located at least 3m from any road.

**(2)**
Must be located within 2m of property boundary.

**(3)**
Outdoor furniture must not result in the approved seating capacity of the premises being exceeded.

**(4)**
Must not extend beyond the frontage of the premises to which it relates.

**(5)**
Must not be fixed or fastened to the footpath or building.

**(6)**
Outdoor furniture and other equipment such as umbrellas, umbrella stands, pot plants and barriers used to define the seating area must be removed at close of business each day.

**(7)**
Must be located on sealed footpath.

> Note: Approval is also required under the *Local Government Act 1993* and under the *Roads Act 1993* if the road is a classified road.

## Property address signs

**(1)**
Maximum area—
- (a) if located on land in a residential zone—0.5m2, or
- (b) if located on land in any other zone—1m2.

**(2)**
Maximum height—
- (a) if located on land in Zone E4—2m, or
- (b) if located on land in any other zone—1.8m.

**(3)**
If located on land in a residential, rural or conservation zones—must not be illuminated.

**(4)**
If mounted to front fence or front wall of building, must not protrude beyond the physical limits of that fence or building.

**(5)**
Maximum 1 sign per premises.

## Pylon signs (employment or mixed use zones)

**(1)**
Must not be illuminated or flashing.

**(2)**
Maximum area—2m2.

**(3)**
Maximum height—4m.

**(4)**
Maximum 1 sign per premises.

**(5)**
Must relate to a lawful use carried out on the land.

**(6)**
Must not be located on public land.

**(7)**
Must be installed in accordance with the manufacturer's specifications.

## Real estate signs (advertising premises for sale or lease)

**(1)**
Maximum area—
- (a) if located on land in a residential, rural or conservation zone—2m2, or
- (b) if located on land in an employment or mixed use zone—4m2.

**(2)**
Maximum 2 signs per premises.

**(3)**
Maximum 1 sign at frontage of premises.

**(4)**
Must be removed within 7 days of sale or lease of premises.

## Temporary signs for religious, educational, cultural, political, social or recreational events

**(1)**
Must not include commercial advertising apart from name of event sponsor.

**(2)**
Must not be displayed earlier than 21 days before, or later than 7 days after, the event.

**(3)**
Must not be taped to poles, hoardings or buildings.

**(4)**
Must be bunting or a banner or poster.

**(5)**
Must be securely fixed and not impede the movement of vehicles and/or pedestrians.
