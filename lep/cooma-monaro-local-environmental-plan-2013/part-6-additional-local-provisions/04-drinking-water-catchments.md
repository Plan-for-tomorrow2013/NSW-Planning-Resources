# 6.5 Drinking water catchments

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to maintain water quality and the natural environment in the Googong Drinking Water Catchment.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Drinking water catchment" on the *Drinking Water Catchment Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the development will not have an adverse impact on the quality of water entering the drinking water storage or the natural environment of the land.

**(4)**
Before determining a development application for development on land to which this clause applies, the consent authority must consider the following—
- (a) whether the development will involve the installation of new, or an increased use of existing, effluent disposal systems on the land,
- (b) whether effluent generated by the development will pollute nearby watercourses or groundwater,
- (c) whether the development is likely to increase siltation of nearby watercourses,
- (d) whether any agriculture associated with the development is likely to increase nutrient levels or bank erosion of the watercourses.
