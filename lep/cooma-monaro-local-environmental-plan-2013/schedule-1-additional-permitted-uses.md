# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## Use of certain land on the Monaro Highway, Nimmitabel

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Monaro Highway, Nimmitabel, being Lots 179-183 and 185-189, DP 756849 and Lot 1, DP 248788.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a waste or resource transfer station is permitted with development consent.
