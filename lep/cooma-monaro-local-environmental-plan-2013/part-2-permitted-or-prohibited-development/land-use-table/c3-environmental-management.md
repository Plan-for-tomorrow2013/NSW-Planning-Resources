# Zone C3 Environmental Management

## 1 Objectives of zone

- To protect, manage and restore areas with special ecological, scientific, cultural or aesthetic values.
- To provide for a limited range of development that does not have an adverse effect on those values.
- To provide for low-density development that will have minimal impact on the natural environment and demand for services and infrastructure.

## 2 Permitted without consent

Environmental protection works; Extensive agriculture; Home-based child care; Home occupations; Horticulture; Viticulture

## 3 Permitted with consent

Bed and breakfast accommodation; Biosolids treatment facilities; Car parks; Cemeteries; Community facilities; Dairies (pasture-based); Dwelling houses; Electricity generating works; Emergency services facilities; Environmental facilities; Farm buildings; Function centres; Helipads; Oyster aquaculture; Places of public worship; Pond-based aquaculture; Recreation areas; Research stations; Roads; Secondary dwellings; Sewage treatment plants; Tank-based aquaculture; Waste or resource transfer stations; Water recycling facilities; Water supply systems

## 4 Prohibited

Industries; Local distribution premises; Multi dwelling housing; Residential flat buildings; Retail premises; Seniors housing; Service stations; Warehouse or distribution centres; Any other development not specified in item 2 or 3
