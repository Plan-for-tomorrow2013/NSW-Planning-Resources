# 4.4A Maximum gross floor area for retail premises

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to limit the size of retail premises in neighbourhood retail centres.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purpose of retail premises on land in Zone E1 Local Centre unless the gross floor area used for that purpose will not exceed 300 square metres.
