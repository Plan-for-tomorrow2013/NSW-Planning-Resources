# 4.1A Minimum lot sizes for dual occupancies, multi dwelling housing and residential flat buildings

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to achieve planned residential density in certain zones.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development on a lot in a zone shown in Column 2 of the Table to this subclause for a purpose shown in Column 1 of the Table opposite that zone, if the area of the lot is equal to or greater than the area specified for that purpose and shown in Column 3 of the Table.
| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Dual occupancy | Zone R1 General Residential, Zone R2 Low Density Residential and Zone MU1 Mixed Use | 500 square metres |
| Multi dwelling housing | Zone R1 General Residential and Zone MU1 Mixed Use | 1,000 square metres |
| Residential flat building | Zone R1 General Residential and Zone MU1 Mixed Use | 1,000 square metres |

**(3)**
Development consent may be granted to development on a lot that is not serviced by a sewage reticulation system in Zone RU5 Village for a purpose shown in Column 1 of the Table to this subclause, if the area of the lot is equal to or greater than the area specified for that purpose and shown in Column 2 of the Table.
| Column 1 | Column 2 |
|---|---|
| Dual occupancy | 1,800 square metres |
| Multi dwelling housing | 2,500 square metres |
| Residential flat building | 2,000 square metres |

**(4)**
Development consent may be granted to development on a lot that is serviced by a sewage reticulation system in Zone RU5 Village for a purpose shown in Column 1 of the Table to this subclause, if the area of the lot is equal to or greater than the area specified for that purpose and shown in Column 2 of the Table.
| Column 1 | Column 2 |
|---|---|
| Dual occupancy | 1,000 square metres |
| Multi dwelling housing | 1,000 square metres |
| Residential flat building | 1,000 square metres |
