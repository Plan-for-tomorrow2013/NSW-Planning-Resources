# 4.3 Height of buildings

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to ensure the height of buildings complements the streetscape and rural character of Cooma-Monaro,
- (b) to protect the heritage character and significance of existing buildings,
- (c) to ensure the height of buildings does not impact on the amenity of neighbouring properties, in terms of visual bulk, access to sunlight, privacy and views.

**(2)**
The height of a building on any land is not to exceed the maximum height shown for the land on the *Height of Buildings Map*.
