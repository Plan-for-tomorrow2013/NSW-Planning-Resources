# 7.13 Development on land in Zone E4

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that development on certain land in Zone E4 does not unduly impact the amenity of nearby residential land.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development on land that is—
- (a) in Zone E4 General Industrial, and
- (b) identified as "Area A" on the *Land Zoning Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the impact of the development on nearby residential sites, including the impact of noise and hours of operation.
