# Schedule 2 Exempt development

*(Clause 3.1)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies exempt development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional exempt development not specified in that Policy.

> Note: Exempt development may be carried out without the need for development consent under the Act. Such development is not exempt from any approval, licence, permit or authority that is required under any other Act and adjoining owners' property rights and the common law still apply.

## Fuel tanks in Zone RU1 Primary Production

**(1)**
Must be located above ground.

**(2)**
Must be less than 6m above ground level (existing).

**(3)**
Must not be used for commercial or industrial activities unless ancillary to primary lawful land use.

**(4)**
Must not have a capacity more than 5,000L.

**(5)**
Must be constructed of prefabricated materials, freestanding and not rely on other structures for support.

**(6)**
Must be located at least 30m from each lot boundary.

**(7)**
Must be located at least 100m from a classified road.

**(8)**
Must be located at least 40m from a river (inclusive of watercourses, tributary, branch or other watercourse).

**(9)**
Must not be located on a ridgeline or in a visually prominent location.

**(10)**
Must comply with AS 1940—2004, *The storage and handling of flammable and combustible liquids*.

**(11)**
Must not be located within bush fire prone land.

## Signage in Zone E2—above awning signs

**(1)**
Must not cover windows or doors above awning level.

**(2)**
Must be non-illuminated.

**(3)**
Must not cover more than 50% of the above awning building face.

**(4)**
Must be centred on the wall.

**(5)**
Must not be located on or in, or in relation to, a heritage item or draft heritage item.

**(6)**
Maximum number—1 per lot.

**(7)**
Maximum projection—100mm.

## Signage—bracket signs

**(1)**
Maximum number—1 per lot.

**(2)**
Maximum dimensions—0.6m long and 0.3m wide.

**(3)**
Must be at least 2.7m above ground level (existing).

**(4)**
Must be located less than 3m above ground level (existing) or must be below fascia, whichever is the lesser.

**(5)**
Maximum projection—300mm.

## Signage—drop awning signs

**(1)**
Must not cover more than 50% of the blind.

**(2)**
Must be a sign that is painted onto, fixed flush to or part of a roll down blind, which is secured to an awning.

**(3)**
Must be non-illuminated.

## Signage—fascia signs

**(1)**
Must be painted onto or attached to the fascia or return of an awning and not exceed the height of the fascia or return.

**(2)**
Must be non-illuminated.

**(3)**
Must not extend more than 100mm from the fascia or return end of the awning.

## Signage—wall signs

**(1)**
Must be attached to the wall of a building (other than the transom of a doorway or display window).

**(2)**
Must not cover more than 50% of the wall surface.

**(3)**
The upper and lower 1m of the wall must be free of signage.

**(4)**
Maximum number in residential and conservation zones—1 per lot.

**(5)**
Maximum number in rural, employment, mixed use and recreational zones—2 per lot.

## Signage—real estate signs

**(1)**
Must be non-illuminated.

**(2)**
Must be removed within 14 days of sale or lease.

**(3)**
If advertising an entire estate or complex, must be removed at sale or lease of 90% of lots or tenancies, or within 5 years, whichever occurs first.

**(4)**
Maximum number—2 per lot.

**(5)**
Maximum area for a residential site—3m2.

**(6)**
Maximum area for a business or industrial site—5m2.

## Signage—top hamper signs

**(1)**
Must be attached to the transom of a doorway or display window.

**(2)**
Maximum number—2 per frontage.

**(3)**
Must not extend below the head of the doorway or window to which it is attached.

**(4)**
Must be non-illuminated.

## Signage—under awning signs

**(1)**
Must not project beyond awning edge.

**(2)**
Must be at least 2.7m above ground level (existing).

**(3)**
Maximum number—1 per lot.

## Signage—window signs

**(1)**
Must be painted or displayed on the interior or exterior of the window.

**(2)**
Must not occupy more than 50% of the window surface.
