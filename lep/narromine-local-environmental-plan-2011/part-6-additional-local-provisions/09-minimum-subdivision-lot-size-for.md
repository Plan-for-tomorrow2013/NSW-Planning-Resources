# 6.10 Minimum subdivision lot size for certain land in Zone R5

Land identified as "Area A" on the *Lot Size Map* may be subdivided with development consent to create lots of at least 4,000m2 if the consent authority is satisfied each lot will be serviced by a reticulated water supply system and sewerage system.
