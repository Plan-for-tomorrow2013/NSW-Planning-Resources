# 4.1A Minimum lot sizes for dual occupancy, multi dwelling housing and residential flat buildings

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to achieve planned residential density in certain zones.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development on a lot in a zone shown in Column 2 of the Table to this clause for a purpose shown in Column 1 of the Table opposite that zone, if the area of the lot is equal to or greater than the area specified for that purpose and shown in Column 3 of the Table.
| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Dual occupancy (attached) | Zone R1 General Residential | 800 square metres |
| Dual occupancy (detached) | Zone R1 General Residential | 800 square metres |
| Multi dwelling housing | Zone R1 General Residential | 800 square metres |
| Residential flat building | Zone R1 General Residential | 800 square metres |
| Dual occupancy (attached) | Zone RU5 Village | 800 square metres |
| Dual occupancy (detached) | Zone RU5 Village | 800 square metres |
| Multi dwelling housing | Zone RU5 Village | 800 square metres |
| Residential flat building | Zone RU5 Village | 800 square metres |
