# 1.2 Aims of Plan

**(1)**
This Plan aims to make local environmental planning provisions for land in Narromine in accordance with the relevant standard environmental planning instrument under section 3.20 of the Act.

**(2)**
The particular aims of this Plan are as follows—
- (aa) to protect and promote the use and development of land for arts and cultural activity, including music and other performance arts,
- (a) to encourage economic development through tourism activities, business, employment initiatives and fostering industry growth,
- (b) to protect and conserve the natural environment including surface and ground water, soil, air and native vegetation by encouraging sustainable development,
- (c) to encourage sustainable agricultural practices, including intensive agriculture, by minimising land use conflicts and facilitating farm adjustments.
