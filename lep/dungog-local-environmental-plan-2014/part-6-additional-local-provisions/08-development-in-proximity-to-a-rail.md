# 6.9 Development in proximity to a rail corridor on Verge Street

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that development for the purposes of residential accommodation, centre-based child care facilities, community facilities, respite day care centres or seniors housing in the proximity of operating or proposed railways is not adversely affected by rail noise or vibration.

**(2)**
This clause applies to Lot 41, DP 802112, Verge Street, Dungog.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development—
- (a) that is on land to which this clause applies that comprises, or is within 60 metres of, an operating railway line or land reserved for the construction of a railway line, and
- (b) that the consent authority considers is, or is likely to be, adversely affected by rail noise or vibration,
unless the consent authority is satisfied that the proposed development incorporates reasonable mitigation measures, including measures relating to the location and design of the proposed development, to minimise the impact of rail noise or vibration.
