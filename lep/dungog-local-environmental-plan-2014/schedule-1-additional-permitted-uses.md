# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Common Road, Dungog

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Common Road, Dungog, being Lot 64, DP 597832.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a dual occupancy or dwelling house is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at Glen Martin Road, Glen Martin

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 345 Glen Martin Road, Glen Martin, being Lot 383, DP 806712.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a dual occupancy or dwelling house is permitted with development consent.

## 3 Use of certain land at 656 Gresford Road, Vacy

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 656 Gresford Road, Vacy, being Lot 123, DP 1063557.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a dual occupancy or dwelling house is permitted with development consent.

## 4 Use of certain land at Dungog Common Recreation Reserve

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at Dungog Common Recreation Reserve that is in Zone C3 Environmental Management—
- (a) Lots 162, 163, 167-170, 236 and 301, DP 752457,
- (b) Part Lot 337, DP 729762,
- (c) Lot 7302, DP 1162438.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a recreation facility (outdoor) that is a mountain bike park is permitted with development consent.

## 5 Use of certain land at 68 Grey Street, Clarence Town

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 12, Section 8, DP 758250, 68 Grey Street, Clarence Town.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a single service station is permitted with development consent if the consent authority is satisfied that the service station will—
- (a) be above ground level (existing), and
- (b) be capable of operating unstaffed, and
- (c) have a gross floor area of no more than 25m2, and
- (d) not be used for a purpose specified in the Dictionary, definition of *service station*, paragraph (a)-(e).

**(3)**
This clause is repealed on 15 April 2027.
