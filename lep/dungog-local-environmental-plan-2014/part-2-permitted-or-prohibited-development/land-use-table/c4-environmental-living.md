# Zone C4 Environmental Living

## 1 Objectives of zone

- To provide for low-impact residential development in areas with special ecological, scientific or aesthetic values.
- To ensure that residential development does not have an adverse effect on those values.

## 2 Permitted without consent

Environmental protection works; Home-based child care; Home occupations

## 3 Permitted with consent

Bed and breakfast accommodation; Boat launching ramps; Camping grounds; Car parks; Caravan parks; Cellar door premises; Centre-based child care facilities; Community facilities; Dual occupancies (attached), Dwelling houses; Eco-tourist facilities; Emergency services facilities; Environmental facilities; Farm stay accommodation; Home businesses; Information and education facilities; Jetties; Kiosks; Markets; Neighbourhood shops; Oyster aquaculture; Pond-based aquaculture; Recreation areas; Recreation facilities (indoor); Recreation facilities (outdoor); Research stations; Respite day care centres; Roads; Roadside stalls; Sewerage systems; Signage; Tank-based aquaculture; Veterinary hospitals; Water recreation structures; Water supply systems

## 4 Prohibited

Industries; Local distribution premises; Service stations; Warehouse or distribution centres; Any other development not specified in item 2 or 3
