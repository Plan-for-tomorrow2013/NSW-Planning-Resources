# 85 Service supplies

**(1) Gas and electricity**
Gas and electricity services to development on bush fire prone land are to be provided in accordance with the relevant criteria in *Planning for Bush Fire Protection*.

**(2) Water supplies**
When granting consent to any development on bush fire prone land, the consent authority is to be satisfied that adequate water would be available to land holders and fire fighting authorities to enable the effective defence of habitable buildings in the event of a bush fire attack, in accordance with *Planning for Bush Fire Protection*.
