# 3 Relationship to other environmental planning instruments

**(1)**
This plan amends *Blue Mountains Local Environmental Plan No 4* by replacing clause 3 with the following clause—
Land to which this plan applies This plan applies to certain land within the City of Blue Mountains, being that land shown on the Map, within the meaning of *Blue Mountains Local Environmental Plan 2005*, by distinctive colouring as "Area subject to LEP 4".

**(2)**
This plan amends *Blue Mountains Local Environmental Plan 1991* by replacing clause 2 with the following clause—
Land to which this plan applies This plan applies to certain land within the City of Blue Mountains, being— that land shown on the Map, within the meaning of *Blue Mountains Local Environmental Plan 2005*, by distinctive colouring as "Area subject to LEP 1991", or that land not shown on the Map within the meaning of *Blue Mountains Local Environmental Plan 2005*.

**(3), (4)** (Repealed)
