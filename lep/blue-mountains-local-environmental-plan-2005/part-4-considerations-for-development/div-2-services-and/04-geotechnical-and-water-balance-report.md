# 97 Geotechnical and water balance report

A geotechnical and water balance report prepared for the purposes of clause 94 (4)—
- (a) is to include sufficient technical data to meet the requirements specified in any relevant development control plan that provides guidelines for the on-site disposal of wastewater, and
- (b) must consider and assess—
  - (i) the wastewater treatment system, and
  - (ii) site selection criteria, and
  - (iii) the capability of the development site to provide for the disposal of wastewater in accordance with clause 44 (Environmental impact).
