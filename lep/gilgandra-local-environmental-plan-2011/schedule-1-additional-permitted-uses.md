# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at 186 Avondale Road, Gilgandra

**(1)**
Development for the purposes of a dwelling house is permitted with consent on Lot 39, DP 753411, Gilgandra, as that Lot existed on the commencement of this Plan.

**(2)**
This clause ceases to have effect on the fifth anniversary of the commencement of this Plan.

## 2 Use of certain land at 233 Jack Renshaw Parkway, Tooraweenah

**(1)**
Development for the purposes of a dwelling house is permitted with consent on Lot 100, DP 1007379, Tooraweenah, as that Lot existed on the commencement of this Plan.

**(2)**
This clause ceases to have effect on the fifth anniversary of the commencement of this Plan.
