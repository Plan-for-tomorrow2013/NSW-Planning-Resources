# 4.1B Exceptions to minimum subdivision lot sizes for certain split zones

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to provide for the subdivision of lots that are within more than one zone and cannot be subdivided under clause 4.1, and
- (b) to ensure that the subdivision occurs in a way that promotes suitable land use and development.

**(2)**
This clause applies to each lot (an *original lot*) that contains—
- (a) land in a residential, employment, special purpose or recreation zone, and
- (b) land in Zone RU1 Primary Production, Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots, RU5 Village, Zone C1 National Parks and Nature Reserves, Zone C2 Environmental Conservation, Zone W1 Natural Waterways or Zone W2 Recreational Waterways.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.1(3), development consent may be granted to subdivide an original lot to create other lots (the *resulting lots*) if—
- (a) one of the resulting lots will contain—
  - (i) land in the following zones that has an area not less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to the land—
    - (A) a residential zone,
    - (B) an employment zone,
    - (C) a special purpose zone,
    - (D) a recreation zone,
    - (E) Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots,
    - (F) Zone RU5 Village, and
  - (ii) all of the land in the following zones that was in the original lot—
    - (A) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
    - (B) Zone C1 National Parks and Nature Reserves,
    - (C) Zone C2 Environmental Conservation,
    - (D) Zone W1 Natural Waterways,
    - (E) Zone W2 Recreational Waterways, and
- (b) all other resulting lots will contain land that has an area not less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to the land.
