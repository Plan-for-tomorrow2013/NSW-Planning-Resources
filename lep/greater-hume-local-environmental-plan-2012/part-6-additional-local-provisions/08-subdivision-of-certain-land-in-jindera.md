# 6.8 Subdivision of certain land in Jindera

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land in Jindera—
- (a) Lot 2, DP 240938, 1037 Urana Road,
- (b) Lot 224, DP 1280394, Wagner Drive.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the subdivision of the land unless a development control plan has been prepared for the land.
