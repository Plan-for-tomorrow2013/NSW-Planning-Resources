# 8 Adoption of Model Provisions

The *Environmental Planning and Assessment Model Provisions 1980* (except the definitions of *boarding-house*, *car repair station*, *industry*, *recreation facility*, *residential flat building*, *site area* and *tourist facilities* in clause 4 (1) and clauses 5 (1), 7, 15 and 16) are adopted for the purposes of this plan.
