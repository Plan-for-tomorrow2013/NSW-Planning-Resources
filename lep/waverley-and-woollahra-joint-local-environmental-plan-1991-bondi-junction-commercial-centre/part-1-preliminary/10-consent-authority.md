# 10 Consent authority

For the purposes of this plan, the consent authority—
- (a) in respect of land described in clause 4 (1) (a)—is the Council of the Municipality of Waverley, and
- (b) in respect of land described in clause 4 (1) (b)—is the Council of the Municipality of Woollahra.
