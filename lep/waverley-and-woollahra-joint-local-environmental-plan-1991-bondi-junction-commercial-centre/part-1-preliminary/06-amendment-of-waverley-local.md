# 6 Amendment of Waverley Local Environmental Plan 1985

*Waverley Local Environmental Plan 1985* is amended—
- (a) by inserting at the end of clause 3 the following subclause—
Despite subclause (1), this plan does not apply to so much of the land shown on the map as is land to which *Waverley and Woollahra Joint Local Environmental Plan 1991—Bondi Junction Commercial Centre* applies.
- (b) by omitting clause 32.
