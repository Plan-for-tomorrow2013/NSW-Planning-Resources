# 5 Relationship to other environmental planning instruments

**(1)**
This plan—
- (a) amends *Waverley Local Environmental Plan 1985* in the manner set out in clause 6, and
- (b) amends *Woollahra Planning Scheme Ordinance* in the manner set out in clause 7.

**(2)**
*State Environmental Planning Policy No 22—Shops and Commercial Premises* does not apply to development on land within Zone No 3 (a2) or 3 (a3) to which clause 16 applies.
