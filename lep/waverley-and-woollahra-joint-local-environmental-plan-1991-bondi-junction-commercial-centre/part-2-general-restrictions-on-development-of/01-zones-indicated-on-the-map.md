# 11 Zones indicated on the map

For the purposes of this plan, land to which this plan applies shall be within a zone specified hereunder if the land is shown on the map in the manner specified hereunder in relation to that zone—
- Zone No 3 (a2) (Business General Zone)—coloured light blue with dark red edging and lettered "3 (a2)".
- Zone No 3 (a3) (Business Secondary Zone)—coloured light blue with dark red edging and lettered "3 (a3)".
- Zone No 3 (a4) (Business Special (Low Intensity) Zone)—coloured light blue with dark red edging and lettered "3 (a4)"
- Zone No 3 (a5) (Business Special (Services) Zone)—coloured light blue with dark red edging and lettered "3 (a5)".
