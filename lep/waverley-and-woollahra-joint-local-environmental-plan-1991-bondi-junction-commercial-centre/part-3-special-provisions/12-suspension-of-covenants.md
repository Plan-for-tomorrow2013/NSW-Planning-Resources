# 26 Suspension of covenants

**(1)**
For the purpose of enabling development to be carried out in accordance with a consent granted under the Act, any agreement, covenant or instrument imposing restrictions as to the erection or use of buildings for certain purposes or as to the use of land for certain purposes, to the extent necessary to serve that purpose, shall not apply to the development.

**(2)**
Pursuant to section 28 of the Act, before the making of this plan, the Governor approved of this clause.
