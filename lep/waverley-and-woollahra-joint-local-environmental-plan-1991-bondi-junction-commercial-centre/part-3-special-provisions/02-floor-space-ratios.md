# 14 Floor space ratios

**(1)**
A building shall not be erected on land within Zone No 3 (a2), 3 (a3), 3 (a4) or 3 (a5) if—
- (a) the floor space ratio would exceed the ratio indicated on the map in respect of that land, or
- (b) in the case of 2 floor space ratios being indicated on the map in respect of that land, the floor space ratio would exceed the greater of the 2 ratios.

**(2)**
If, on the map, 2 floor space ratios are indicated for the same land—
- (a) in respect of buildings on land within Zone No 3 (a2), the floor space ratio of a building or any part of a building used for residential purposes shall not exceed the lesser of the 2 floor space ratios, and
- (b) in respect of buildings on land within Zone 3 (a4), the floor space ratio of a building or any part of a building used for non-residential purposes shall not exceed the lesser of the 2 floor space ratios.

**(3)**
A building for the purposes of a hotel may be erected with a floor space ratio which does not exceed the maximum ratio indicated on the map for the land to which that building relates.

**(4)**
In determining the floor space ratio of any building or buildings for the purposes of this clause, no account shall be taken of any floor area which is part of a car parking station.
