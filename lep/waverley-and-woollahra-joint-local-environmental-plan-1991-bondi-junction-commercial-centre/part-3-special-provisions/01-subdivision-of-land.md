# 13 Subdivision of land

**(1)**
A person shall not subdivide land to which this plan applies without the consent of the Council.

**(2)**
Subclause (1) does not apply to subdivision under the *Strata Titles Act 1973*, but nothing in this clause affects *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021*, Chapter 2, Part 3.
