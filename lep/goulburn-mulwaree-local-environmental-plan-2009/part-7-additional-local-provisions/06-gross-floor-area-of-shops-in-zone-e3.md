# 7.6 Gross floor area of shops in Zone E3 and MU1

Development consent must not be granted for development for the purpose of shops unless the gross floor area does not exceed—
- (a) if the development is on land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use—450 square metres, or
- (b) if the development is on land in Zone E3 Productivity Support—150 square metres.
