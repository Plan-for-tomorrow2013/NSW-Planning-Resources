# 19 Development of land within Zones Nos 1 (a), 1 (b) and 1 (c) near designated roads

The Council must not consent to a use of land for the purpose of any building, work or place listed in Schedule 3 that will involve direct vehicular access between that land and a road connecting a designated road if the access to that road will be within 90 metres (measured along the road alignment of the connecting road) of the alignment of the designated road.
