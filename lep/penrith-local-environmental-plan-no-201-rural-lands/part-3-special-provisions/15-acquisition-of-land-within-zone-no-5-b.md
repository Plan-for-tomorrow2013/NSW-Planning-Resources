# 24 Acquisition of land within Zone No 5 (b)

**(1)**
The owner of any land within Zone No 5 (b) may, by notice in writing, require the council to acquire the land.

**(2)**
On receipt of a notice under this clause, the council must acquire the land unless the land might reasonably be required to be dedicated for public roads.
