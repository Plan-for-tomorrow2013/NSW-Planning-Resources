# 13 Erection of dwelling-houses on land included in Schedule 2

Notwithstanding the provisions of clause 12, the council may only consent to the erection of a dwelling-house on land referred to in Schedule 2 where the land is consolidated into a single allotment in accordance with that Schedule.
