# 6 Model Provisions

This plan adopts the *Environmental Planning and Assessment Model Provisions 1980* except for the definitions of *health care professional*, *map*, *professional consulting rooms* and *recreation establishment* in clause 4 (1) and clauses 5 (2), 5 (3), 15-23, 30, 32 and 34.
