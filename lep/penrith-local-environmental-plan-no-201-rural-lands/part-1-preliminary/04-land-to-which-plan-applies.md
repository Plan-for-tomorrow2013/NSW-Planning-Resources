# 3 Land to which plan applies

**(1)**
This plan applies to land within the City of Penrith shown edged heavy black on the map.

**(2)**
This plan does not apply to land referred to on the map as "deferred matter" or to land to which *Penrith Local Environmental Plan 2002 (Villages of Mulgoa and Wallacia)* applies.
