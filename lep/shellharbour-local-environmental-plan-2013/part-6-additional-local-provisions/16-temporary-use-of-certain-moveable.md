# 6.17 Temporary use of certain moveable dwellings for secondary dwellings

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on land in a residential zone.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of the temporary use of a single moveable dwelling, other than a moveable dwelling that is a manufactured home, as a secondary dwelling is exempt development on the land.

> Note: Approval under the *Local Government Act 1993*, section 68 may be required to install a moveable dwelling.

**(3)**
To be exempt development under this clause, the development must—
- (a) be for the use of a moveable dwelling that is—
  - (i) a registrable vehicle, and
  - (ii) on wheels, and
- (b) if the moveable dwelling is a motor vehicle—
  - (i) have a maximum length of 12.5m, and
  - (ii) have a maximum width of 2.5m, and
  - (iii) have a maximum height of 4.3m, and
- (c) if the moveable dwelling is a trailer—
  - (i) have a maximum length of 12.3m, and
  - (ii) have a maximum width of 2.5m, and
  - (iii) have a maximum height of 4.3m, and
- (d) be located on a lot that is—
  - (i) a Torrens title lot, and
  - (ii) at least 450m2, and
- (e) be located on the same lot as a single existing lawful dwelling house, and
- (f) not be located on the following land—
  - (i) flood prone land,
  - (ii) land occupied by a heritage item or draft heritage item,
  - (iii) land within a heritage conservation area or draft heritage conservation area,
  - (iv) land in a foreshore area,
  - (v) land in an environmentally sensitive area,
  - (vi) land declared an area of outstanding biodiversity value under the *Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016*,
  - (vii) land declared critical habitat under the *Fisheries Management Act 1994*, Part 7A,
  - (viii) contaminated land, and
- (g) not be located on, or only be accessed from a public road by, bush fire prone land, and
- (h) be located—
  - (i) if the site is a corner lot—behind the building line of the principal dwelling to the primary road boundary and at least—
    - (A) if the site area is less than 600m2—2m from the secondary road boundary, or
    - (B) if the site area is 600m2 or more—3m from the secondary road boundary, or
  - (ii) otherwise—behind the front building line of the principal dwelling, and
- (i) be located at least 1.8m from the principal dwelling and any structures attached to the principal dwelling, and
- (j) have a minimum setback of—
  - (i) if the site area is not more than 900m2—
    - (A) 0.9m from the side boundary, and
    - (B) 3m from the rear boundary, or
  - (ii) if the site area is more than 900m2 but not more than 1,500m2—
    - (A) 1.5m from the side boundary, and
    - (B) 5m from the rear boundary, or
  - (iii) if the site area is more than 1,500m2—
    - (A) 2.5m from the side boundary, and
    - (B) 10m from the rear boundary, and
- (k) be located at least 3m from the trunk of a protected tree, and
- (l) not be on a slope greater than 5%, and
- (m) ensure an area of at least 20m2 serves as private open space for the primary dwelling, and
- (n) not be installed over an easement, and
- (o) be connected to existing reticulated sewerage, stormwater drainage and potable water systems for the site, and
- (p) not involve the removal, pruning or other clearing of a protected tree unless undertaken in accordance with a permit, approval or development consent for the removal, pruning or other clearing of the protected tree, and
- (q) not contravene any conditions of a development consent currently operating on the land, and
- (r) not result in less than 2 parking spaces for the site, and
- (s) be equipped with a smoke alarm installed in accordance with the *National Construction Code* and the *Australian Building Codes Board Housing Provisions Standard 2022*, and
- (t) remain on wheels but be secured to the site in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions, and
- (u) only be attached to the principal dwelling to the extent necessary to connect to utility services, and
- (v) be verified by a suitably qualified engineer as being structurally adequate, and
- (w) be safe and suitable for use as a residential premises.

**(4)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 4 September 2028.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*Australian Building Codes Board Housing Provisions Standard 2022* means the *Australian Building Codes Board Housing Provisions Standard 2022* published by the Australian Building Codes Board, as in force immediately before the commencement of *Shellharbour Local Environmental Plan 2013 (Amendment No 27)*.
*draft heritage conservation area* has the same meaning as in *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*.
*foreshore area* has the same meaning as in *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*.
*motor vehicle* has the same meaning as in the *Road Transport Act 2013*.
*National Construction Code* means the *National Construction Code* produced and maintained by the Australian Building Codes Board, as in force immediately before the commencement of *Shellharbour Local Environmental Plan 2013 (Amendment No 27)*.
*protected tree* has the same meaning as in *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*.
*registrable vehicle* has the same meaning as in the *Road Transport Act 2013*.
*trailer* has the same meaning as in the *Road Transport Act 2013*.
