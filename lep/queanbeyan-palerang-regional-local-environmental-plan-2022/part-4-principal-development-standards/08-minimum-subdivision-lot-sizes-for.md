# 4.1F Minimum subdivision lot sizes for certain split zones

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to provide for the subdivision of lots that are within more than one zone but cannot be subdivided under clause 4.1,
- (b) to ensure the subdivision promotes suitable land use and development,
- (c) to ensure the subdivision protects the environmental and heritage attributes of the land.

**(2)**
This clause applies to each lot (an *original lot*) that contains—
- (a) land in one or more of the following zones—
  - (i) a residential zone,
  - (ii) an employment zone,
  - (iii) a mixed use zone,
  - (iv) Zone RU5 Village, and
- (b) land in one or more of the following zones—
  - (i) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
  - (ii) Zone RU2 Rural Landscape,
  - (iii) Zone RU3 Forestry,
  - (iv) Zone C2 Environmental Conservation,
  - (v) Zone C3 Environmental Management,
  - (vi) Zone C4 Environmental Living.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.1(3), an original lot may be subdivided to create other lots (the *resulting lots*) if—
- (a) one of the resulting lots will contain—
  - (i) land in one of the following zones that has an area not less than the minimum lot size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to the land—
    - (A) a residential zone,
    - (B) an employment zone,
    - (C) a mixed use zone,
    - (D) Zone RU5 Village, and
  - (ii) all the land in the following zones that was in the original lot—
    - (A) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
    - (B) Zone RU2 Rural Landscape,
    - (C) Zone RU3 Forestry,
    - (D) Zone C2 Environmental Conservation,
    - (E) Zone C3 Environmental Management,
    - (F) Zone C4 Environmental Living, and
- (b) all other resulting lots will contain land that has an area not less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to the land.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted for the erection of a dwelling on a part of a resulting lot comprising land referred to in subclause (3)(ii).
