# 7.27 Development on land subject to South Jerrabomberra Regional Jobs Precinct Process

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on land identified as "Land subject to South Jerrabomberra Regional Jobs Precinct Process" on the *Land Zoning Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the *South Jerrabomberra Regional Jobs Precinct Masterplan* published by the Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure in March 2025.
