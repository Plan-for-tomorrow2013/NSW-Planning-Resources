# 7.20 Animal boarding or training establishments

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure animal boarding or training establishments for which development consent is required will not have an adverse impact on the amenity of surrounding land uses.

**(2)**
In deciding whether to grant development consent for animal boarding or training establishments, the consent authority must consider—
- (a) the impact of noise and other emissions from the development on surrounding land, and
- (b) whether the development will unreasonably have an adverse impact on the amenity of surrounding land uses.
