# 1.2 Aims of Plan

**(1)**
This Plan aims to make local environmental planning provisions for land in Lockhart in accordance with the relevant standard environmental planning instrument under section 3.20 of the Act.

**(2)**
The particular aims of this Plan are as follows—
- (aa) to protect and promote the use and development of land for arts and cultural activity, including music and other performance arts,
- (a) to encourage the proper management, development and conservation of natural and man-made resources in Lockhart,
- (b) to facilitate growth and development while minimising the cost of such growth and development to the community,
- (c) to assist in the delivery of amenities and services, as well as residential and employment opportunities, to meet demand,
- (d) to provide for a range of development opportunities that contribute to the social, economic and environmental resources of the area,
- (e) to conserve the environmental heritage of Lockhart.
