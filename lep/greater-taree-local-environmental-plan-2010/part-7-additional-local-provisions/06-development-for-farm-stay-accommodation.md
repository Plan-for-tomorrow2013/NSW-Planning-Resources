# 7.7 Development for farm stay accommodation

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone RU1 Primary Production and Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purpose of farm stay accommodation on land to which this clause applies unless a dwelling is permitted to be erected on that land under clause 4.2A(3).
