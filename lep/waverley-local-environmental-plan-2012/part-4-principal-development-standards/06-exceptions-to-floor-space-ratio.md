# 4.4A Exceptions to floor space ratio

Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio for an attached dwelling, dual occupancy, dwelling house or semi-detached dwelling on land in Zone R2 Low Density Residential, Zone R3 Medium Density Residential or Zone R4 High Density Residential is as follows—
- (a) for lots with an area less than 100 square metres—1:1,
- (b) for lots with an area of 100 square metres to 550 square metres—[[(550 − lot area) × 0.0011] + 0.5]:1,
- (c) for lots with an area greater than 550 square metres—0.5:1.
