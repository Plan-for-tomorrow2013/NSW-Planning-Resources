# 6.8 Maximum gross floor area of retail premises on certain land in Bronte

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to limit the gross floor area of retail premises on certain land at Macpherson Street and St Thomas Street, Bronte.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the land shown coloured light blue and marked "1" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purpose of retail premises on land to which this clause applies if the gross floor area of the retail premises exceeds 500 square metres.
