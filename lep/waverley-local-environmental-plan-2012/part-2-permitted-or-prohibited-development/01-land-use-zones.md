R4 High Density Residential

E1 Local Centre
E2 Commercial Centre
- Mixed Use Zones
MU1 Mixed Use
- Special Purpose Zones
SP2 Infrastructure
- Recreation Zones
RE1 Public Recreation
RE2 Private Recreation
- Conservation Zones
C2 Environmental Conservation
