# 7.14 Use of certain land at Clergate Road and Leeds Parade, Orange

**(1)**
A subdivision of land identified as "Area 2" on the *Lot Size Map* must not result in more than 700 lots used for the purposes of residential accommodation on the land.

**(2)**
Land identified as "buffer area" on the *Slope Constraint Area Map* is a buffer area for the purposes of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*, clause 1.19(1)(e)(i).
