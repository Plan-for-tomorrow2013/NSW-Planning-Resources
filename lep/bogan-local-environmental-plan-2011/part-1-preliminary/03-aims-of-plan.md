# 1.2 Aims of Plan

**(1)**
This Plan aims to make local environmental planning provisions for land in Bogan in accordance with the relevant standard environmental planning instrument under section 3.20 of the Act.

**(2)**
The particular aims of this Plan are as follows—
- (aa) to protect and promote the use and development of land for arts and cultural activity, including music and other performance arts,
- (a) to protect, enhance and conserve agricultural land through the proper management, development and conservation of natural and man-made resources,
- (b) to encourage a range of development, including housing, employment, recreation and community facilities, to meet the needs of existing and future residents of Bogan,
- (c) to promote the efficient and equitable provision of public services, infrastructure and amenities.
