# 14A Dual occupancies (detached) in Zone No 7 (c)

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone No 7 (c).

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development for the purposes of dual occupancies (detached) if the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the development will not impair the use of the land or adjoining land for agriculture or rural industries, and
- (b) each dwelling will use the same vehicular point of access to and from a public road, and
- (c) the land is physically suitable for the erection of dual occupancies (detached), and
- (d) the land will accommodate the on-site disposal and management of sewage for each dwelling, and
- (e) the development will not have an adverse impact on the scenic amenity and character of the rural environment, and
- (f) the development will not have a significant adverse impact on the following—
  - (i) the native flora and fauna,
  - (ii) water quality.

**(3)**
Despite another provision of this plan, a lot on the land must not be subdivided if—
- (a) a dual occupancy (detached) has been erected on the lot in accordance with this clause, and
- (b) the subdivision would result in each dwelling comprising the dual occupancy (detached) being located on separate lots.
