# 26 Development within Zone No 7 (l)

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within Zone No 7 (l).

**(2)**
The council shall not consent to the carrying out of development on land within Zone No 7 (l) until it has taken into consideration the likely effects of the development on the flora and fauna found in the locality.
