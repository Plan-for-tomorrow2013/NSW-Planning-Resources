# 14 Dual occupancies (attached)

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within Zone No 1 (a1), 1 (a2), 1 (b), 1 (d), 1 (e), 2 (a), 2 (b), 2 (t), 7 (a), 7 (c), 7 (d), 7 (d1), 7 (f), 7 (i) or 7 (l).

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development for the purposes of dual occupancies (attached) on a lot on land to which this clause applies if the consent authority is satisfied the development will result in no more than 2 dwellings on the lot.

**(3)**
A person must not subdivide land on which development has been carried out in pursuance of this clause if the subdivision would permit the separate ownership of each dwelling.

**(4)**
For the purposes of subclause (2), the number of dwellings on a lot does not include a rural workers' dwelling erected in accordance with clause 12(4).
