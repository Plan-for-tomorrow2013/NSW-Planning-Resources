# 28A Tourist facilities and recreation establishments fronting classified roads

**(1)**
This clause applies to development for the purpose of tourist facilities or recreation establishments when carried out on any land other than land within Zone No 2 (a), 2 (b), 2 (t), 3 or 6 (a).

**(2)**
Notwithstanding the provisions of clause 9, the council shall not consent to the carrying out of development to which this clause applies on any site where such site fronts a classified road and vehicular access is not available to the site otherwise than directly from the classified road.

**(3)**
For the purpose of this clause, *classified road* has the meaning ascribed to it in the *Roads Act 1993*.
