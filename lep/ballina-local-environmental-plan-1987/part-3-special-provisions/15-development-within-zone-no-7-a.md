# 24 Development within Zone No 7 (a)

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within Zone No 7 (a).

**(2)**
A person shall not clear, drain, excavate or fill land to which this clause applies without the consent of the council.

**(3)**
The council shall not consent to the carrying out of development on or adjacent to land within Zone No 7 (a) unless it has taken into consideration—
- (a) the likely effects of the development on the flora and fauna found in the wetlands,
- (b) the likely effects of the development on the water table, and
- (c) the effect of the wetlands of any proposed clearing, draining excavating or filling.
