# 34A Development of land on Cabbage Tree Island, Wardell

**(1)**
Notwithstanding the other provisions of this plan, two dwelling-houses may, with the consent of the council, be erected on Lot 261, DP 755691, Cabbage Tree Island, Wardell.

**(2)**
However, council must not grant consent to the development described in subclause (1), until such time as the two dwelling-houses occupying Lot 94, DP 755691, Cabbage Tree Island, Wardell, have been demolished.
