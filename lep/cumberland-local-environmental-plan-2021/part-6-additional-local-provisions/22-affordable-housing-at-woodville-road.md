# 6.22 Affordable housing at Woodville Road Corridor

**(1)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on land identified as "Affordable Housing Contribution Area—Woodville Road Corridor" on the *Affordable Housing Contributions Map*.

**(2)**
The consent authority may, when granting development consent to development to which this clause applies, impose a condition requiring an affordable housing contribution of 1% of the gross floor area of the residential component of the development.

**(3)**
A condition imposed under this clause must provide for the affordable housing levy contribution to be satisfied by—
- (a) a dedication, in favour of the Council, of land comprising one or more dwellings, each having a gross floor area of at least 50m2, with any remainder paid as a monetary contribution to the Council, or
- (b) a monetary contribution paid to the Council.

**(4)**
The affordable housing levy contribution must be calculated in accordance with the Woodville Road Corridor Affordable Housing Contribution Scheme.

**(5)**
The demolition of a building or a change in the use of land does not give rise to a claim for a refund of a contribution.

**(6)**
In this clause—
*Woodville Road Corridor Affordable Housing Contribution Scheme* means the *Woodville Road Corridor Affordable Housing Contribution Scheme* adopted by the Council on 27 August 2025.
