# 6.16 Development in the Commercial Precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to the land known as the Commercial Precinct, shown edged pink and identified as "Commercial Precinct" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Retail premises are permitted with development consent on land to which this clause applies in Zone E3 Productivity Support.
