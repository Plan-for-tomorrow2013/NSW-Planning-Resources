# 6.25 Additional floor space ratio and building height in Area 23

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 23" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on land to which this clause applies is the building height shown for the land on the *Incentive Height of Buildings Map*.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of a building on land to which this clause applies is the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Incentive Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will include at least 3,000m2 of publicly accessible open space.
