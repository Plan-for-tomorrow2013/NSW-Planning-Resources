# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## Use of certain land near the Macquarie Marshes Nature Reserve

**(1)**
This clause applies to the land identified as "Buffer Area" on the *Macquarie Marshes Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of intensive plant agriculture is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**
Development for the purpose of an artificial waterbody is permitted with development consent.
