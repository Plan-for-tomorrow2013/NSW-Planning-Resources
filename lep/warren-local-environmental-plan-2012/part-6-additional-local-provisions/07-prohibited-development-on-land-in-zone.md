# 6.8 Prohibited development on land in Zone RU6

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the following purposes on land in Zone RU6 Transition—
- (a) artificial waterbodies,
- (b) earthworks.

**(2)**
In this clause, *earthworks* does not include ploughing, scarifying, tilling or tipping associated with agriculture.
