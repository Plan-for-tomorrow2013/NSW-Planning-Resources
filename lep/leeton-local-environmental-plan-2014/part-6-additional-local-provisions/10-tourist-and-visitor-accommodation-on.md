# 6.11 Tourist and visitor accommodation on land within Zone RU1

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to enable appropriate forms of tourist and visitor accommodation to be developed in conjunction with primary production uses,
- (b) to encourage development that will not have an adverse impact on primary production activities in the existing area.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Zone RU1 Primary Production.

**(3)**
Before determining a development application for development for the purposes of tourist and visitor accommodation on land to which this clause applies, the consent authority must consider—
- (a) the suitability of the site for the development, and
- (b) the likely impact of the development on adjoining and adjacent land by reason of noise, odour, dust, smoke, hours of operation, traffic, light emissions and similar factors, and
- (c) site specific soil assessment across the development site, and
- (d) existing or potential salinity issues on the development site and adjoining sites, including monitoring and ongoing management, and
- (e) the effect of the development on flora and fauna, including threatened species, on the proposed development site and adjoining and adjacent land, and
- (f) the Aboriginal archaeological values of the development site, and
- (g) the impact of the development on traffic and transport, including traffic volumes, and how the development will feed into the existing urban transport system, and
- (h) the impact of the development on the rural scenic quality of the site and adjoining land, including potential impacts on significant vistas and ridgelines.
