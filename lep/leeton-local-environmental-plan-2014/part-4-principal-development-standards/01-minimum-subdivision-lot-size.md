# 4.1 Minimum subdivision lot size

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to identify minimum lot sizes appropriate for the locality,
- (b) to ensure that the subdivision of land to create new lots is compatible with the character of the surrounding land and does not compromise existing development or amenity,
- (c) to allow the subdivision of land and the erection of dwellings for intensive plant agriculture.

**(2)**
This clause applies to a subdivision of any land shown on the *Lot Size Map* that requires development consent and that is carried out after the commencement of this Plan.

**(3)**
The size of any lot resulting from a subdivision of land to which this clause applies is not to be less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to that land.

**(3A)**
Despite subclause (3), the size of any lot resulting from the subdivision of land within the area identified as "W" on the *Lot Size Map* is not to be less than 1,200 square metres, if the lot will be connected to the Council's reticulated water supply and sewerage systems.

**(3B)**
Despite subclause (3), land within the area identified as "Z1" or "Z2" on the *Lot Size Map* may be subdivided to create a resulting lot of at least 4,000 square metres, if the consent authority is satisfied that each lot will be connected to the Council's reticulated water supply system.

**(4)**
This clause does not apply in relation to the subdivision of any land—
- (a) by the registration of a strata plan or strata plan of subdivision under the *Strata Schemes Development Act 2015*, or
- (b) by any kind of subdivision under the *Community Land Development Act 2021*.
