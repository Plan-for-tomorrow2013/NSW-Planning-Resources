# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## Use of certain land at Walsh Road, Stanbridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Walsh Road, Stanbridge, being Lot 95, DP 751732.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a dwelling house is permitted with development consent on the lot.

**(3)**
Development consent under subclause (2) must not be granted after 2 years from the commencement of this Plan.
