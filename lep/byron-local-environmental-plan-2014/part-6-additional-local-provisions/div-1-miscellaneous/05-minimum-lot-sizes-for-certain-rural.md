# 6.7 Minimum lot sizes for certain rural tourist and visitor accommodation

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of tourist and visitor accommodation on a lot in Zone RU1 Primary Production or Zone RU2 Rural Landscape unless the lot is at least 15ha.

**(2)**
This clause does not apply to development for the purposes of bed and breakfast accommodation or farm stay accommodation.
