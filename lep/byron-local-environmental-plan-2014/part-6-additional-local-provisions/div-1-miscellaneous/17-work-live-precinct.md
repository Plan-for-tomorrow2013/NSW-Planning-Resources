# 6.16D Work-live precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to part of Lot 2, DP 1159910, 66 The Saddle Road, Brunswick Heads, identified as "Work-Live Precinct" on the *Local Clauses Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of attached dwellings, multi dwelling housing or residential flat buildings unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) each dwelling included in the development will contain a dedicated workspace that complies with subclause (3), and
- (b) the floor space of the living area of each dwelling will be less than the floor space of the workspace, and
- (c) the development will be consistent with the objectives of Zone E3 Productivity Support.

**(3)**
The workspace must—
- (a) be designed to enable the workspace to be used for a land use permitted in Zone E3 Productivity Support, and
- (b) be directly accessible from the living area.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted for subdivision of land unless the consent authority is satisfied that vehicular access to each resulting lot will not impact pedestrian or vehicular movement on Gulgan Road, Brunswick Heads.
