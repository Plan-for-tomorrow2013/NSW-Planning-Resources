# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Roose Road, Peak Hill

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Roose Road, Peak Hill, known as "Peak Hill Gold Mine", being those parts of Lot 7010, DP 1126439, Lot 7011, DP 1127170, Lots 1, 3 and 4, DP 1005447 and Lots 145, 376, 380, 453, 466-468 and 489, DP 755113 that are in Zone RU1 Primary Production.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of agriculture is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of land at 3577 Henry Parkes Way, Bogan Gate

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 2, DP 1064474, 3577 Henry Parkes Way, Bogan Gate.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of heavy industries is permitted with development consent.
