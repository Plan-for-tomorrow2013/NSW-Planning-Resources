# 6.8 Parkes Radio Telescope

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to protect the operational capability of the Parkes Radio Telescope.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Parkes Radio Telescope Buffer Area" on the *Parkes Township Buffer Map*.

**(3)**
Before granting development consent for development on land to which this clause applies, the consent authority must consider the following matters—
- (a) whether the proposed development is likely to adversely affect the operational environment of the Parkes Radio Telescope,
- (b) any opportunities to relocate the proposed development outside the land to which this clause applies.
