# Zone RE1 Public Recreation

## 1 Objectives of zone

- To enable land to be used for public open space or recreational purposes.
- To provide a range of recreational settings and activities and compatible land uses.
- To protect and enhance the natural environment for recreational purposes.

## 2 Permitted without consent

Environmental protection works; Flood mitigation works; Moorings; Roads; Water reticulation systems

## 3 Permitted with consent

Aquaculture; Boat launching ramps; Boat sheds; Building identification signs; Business identification signs; Camping grounds; Caravan parks; Centre-based child care facilities; Community facilities; Emergency services facilities; Environmental facilities; Extensive agriculture; Information and education facilities; Jetties; Kiosks; Markets; Mooring pens; Public administration buildings; Recreation areas; Recreation facilities (indoor); Recreation facilities (major); Recreation facilities (outdoor); Respite day care centres; Sewage reticulation systems; Water recreation structures; Water supply systems

## 4 Prohibited

Dairies (pasture-based); Any other development not specified in item 2 or 3
