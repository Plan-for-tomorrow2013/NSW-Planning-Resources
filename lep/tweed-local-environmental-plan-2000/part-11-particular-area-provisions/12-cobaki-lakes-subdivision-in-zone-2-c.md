# 53F Cobaki Lakes—subdivision in Zone 2 (c)

**(1)**
This clause applies to land known as Cobaki Lakes, as shown edged red, coloured scarlet and lettered "2 (c)" on the map marked "*Tweed Local Environmental Plan 2000 (Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979—Section 75R(3A) Order 2011) Cobaki Lakes Land Zoning Map*".

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted for the subdivision of land to which this clause applies for the erection of a dwelling house if the area of each lot created is at least 120 square metres.
