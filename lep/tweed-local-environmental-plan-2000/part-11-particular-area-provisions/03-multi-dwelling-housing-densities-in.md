# 51A Multi-dwelling housing densities in Zone 2 (a)

**(1) Objective**
- to control the density of multi-dwelling housing in Zone 2 (a) (the Low Density Residential zone) by the use of a development standard.

**(2)**
Multi-dwelling housing proposed to be erected on land within Zone 2 (a) is to be at a density not greater than—
- (a) one dwelling per 450 square metres of site area, or
- (b) if the site is within 300 metres of a business centre as indicated on the Business Centres Map—one dwelling per 250 square metres of site area.
