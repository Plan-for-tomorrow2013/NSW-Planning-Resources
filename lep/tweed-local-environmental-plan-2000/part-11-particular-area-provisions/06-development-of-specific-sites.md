# 53 Development of specific sites

**(1) Objective**
- to enable development for additional purposes on certain land.

**(2)**
Nothing in this plan prevents land specified in the Table in Schedule 3 from being developed, with development consent, for the purpose specified for the land in that Table, subject to any applicable conditions so specified. The other provisions of this plan apply provided they are not inconsistent with this clause.
