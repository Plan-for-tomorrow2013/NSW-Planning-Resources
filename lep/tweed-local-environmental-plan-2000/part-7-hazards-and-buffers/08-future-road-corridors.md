# 38 Future road corridors

**(1) Objective**
- to cater for the alignment of, and development in proximity to, future roads.

**(2)**
Development, other than exempt development or agriculture, must not be carried out on land in or adjoining a future road corridor shown on the zone map, except with development consent.

**(3)**
The consent authority must not grant consent unless it has considered the effect of that development on the future alignment of the road corridor.
