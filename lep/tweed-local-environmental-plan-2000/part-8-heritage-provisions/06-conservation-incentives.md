# 46 Conservation incentives

The consent authority may consent to the use, for any purpose, of a building that is a heritage item or is within a heritage conservation area, or of the land on which the building is erected, even though the use would otherwise be prohibited by this plan, if it is satisfied that—
- (a) the proposed use would not adversely affect the heritage significance of the heritage item or heritage conservation area, and
- (b) the conservation of the building depends on the granting of the consent.
