# 41 Heritage items and conservation areas

**(1)**
For the purposes of this Part, heritage items are those items listed in Schedule 2.

**(2)**
Those portions of the villages of Uki and Tumbulgum located within the broken dashed line on the zone map are heritage conservation areas.
