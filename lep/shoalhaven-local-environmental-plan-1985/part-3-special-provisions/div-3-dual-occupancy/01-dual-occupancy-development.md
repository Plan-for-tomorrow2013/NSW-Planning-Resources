# 18 Dual occupancy development

The Council shall not grant consent to dual occupancy development on an allotment of land within Zone No 2 (b1) or 2 (b2) unless the area of the allotment is less than 800 square metres and the Council is satisfied that amalgamation of the allotment with other land in that zone is not feasible.
