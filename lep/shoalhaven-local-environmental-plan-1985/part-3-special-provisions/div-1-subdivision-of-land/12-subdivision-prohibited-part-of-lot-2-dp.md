# 13C Subdivision prohibited—part of Lot 2, DP 714140, Riverview Road, Nowra

**(1)**
This clause applies to so much of Lot 2, DP 714140, Riverview Road, Nowra, as is within Zone No 2 (a4) and shown edged heavy black and lettered "2 (a4)" on the map marked "*City of Shoalhaven Local Environmental Plan 1985 (Amendment No 208)*".

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this plan, the Council must not grant consent to the subdivision of the land to which this clause applies.
