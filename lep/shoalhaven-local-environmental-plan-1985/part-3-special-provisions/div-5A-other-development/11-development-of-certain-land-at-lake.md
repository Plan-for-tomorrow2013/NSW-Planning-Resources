# 39BC Development of certain land at Lake Conjola

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 26, DP 1016153, as shown edged heavy black on the map marked "*City of Shoalhaven Local Environmental Plan 1985 (Amendment No 207)*".

**(2)**
The Council must not consent to the carrying out of development on land to which this clause applies for the purpose of camping grounds, caravan parks, dwelling-houses or golf courses.
