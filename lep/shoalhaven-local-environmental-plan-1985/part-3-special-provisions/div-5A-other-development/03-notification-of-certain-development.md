# 37A Notification of certain development

**(1)**
All development applications require notification to be given by the Council, except those for consent to development that the Council considers to be of a minor nature.

**(2)**
The form and extent of any such notification are to be determined by the Council with regard to the circumstances in each case.

**(3)**
Nothing in this clause affects the giving of notice in respect of designated development.
