# 40A Development in the vicinity of regional services corridor

The Council must not consent to the excision of an allotment from land within or in the vicinity of a regional services corridor, if the creation of the allotment is for a purpose which in the opinion of the Council is, or is likely to be, adversely affected by the regional services corridor.
