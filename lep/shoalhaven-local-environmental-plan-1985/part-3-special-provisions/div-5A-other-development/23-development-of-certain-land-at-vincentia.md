# 40G Development of certain land at Vincentia

**(1)**
Notwithstanding the provisions of clause 9 (3) and the objectives stated in the Table to clause 9 in Item 1 of the matter relating to Zone No 3 (a), the Council may grant consent to the carrying out of retail uses on so much of the land shown on the map marked "*City of Shoalhaven Local Environmental Plan 1985 (Amendment No 131)*" as is within Zone No 3 (a).
