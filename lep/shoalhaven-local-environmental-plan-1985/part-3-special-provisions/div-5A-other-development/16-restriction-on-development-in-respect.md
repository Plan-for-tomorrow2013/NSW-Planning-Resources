# 40 Restriction on development in respect of certain road proposals

Where, on the map, a proposed new road is shown by broken black lines, the Council may not consent to the development of land of which the proposed road forms part unless the proposed development makes provision for the opening of a road in reasonable conformity with the proposed road.
