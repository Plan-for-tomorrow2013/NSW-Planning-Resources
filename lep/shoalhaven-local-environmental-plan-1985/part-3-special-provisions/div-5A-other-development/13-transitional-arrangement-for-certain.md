# 39D Transitional arrangement for certain concessional allotments

An application for consent to subdivide land in accordance with clause 11 (4), as in force immediately before the commencement of *City of Shoalhaven Local Environmental Plan 1985 (Amendment No 127)*, that had not been finally determined when that plan commenced is to be determined as if that plan had not been made.
