# 37 Suspension of covenants, agreements and instruments

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on land within any zone.

**(2)**
For the purpose of enabling development to which this clause applies to be carried out in accordance with this plan or with a consent granted under the Act, any agreement, covenant or instrument that restricts the carrying out of that development shall not apply to the extent necessary to serve that purpose.

**(3)**
Nothing in subclause (2) affects the rights or interests of any public authority under any registered instrument.
