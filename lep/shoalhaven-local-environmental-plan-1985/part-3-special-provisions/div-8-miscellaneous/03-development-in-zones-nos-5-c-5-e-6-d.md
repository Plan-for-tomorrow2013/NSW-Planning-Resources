# 49 Development in Zones Nos 5 (c), 5 (e), 6 (d) and 7 (f2)

**(1)**
Until land referred to in clause 47 is acquired by the public authority concerned, development for any purpose may, with the consent of the Council, be carried out on that land.

**(2)** (Repealed)

**(3)**
In determining whether or not to grant consent under subclause (1), the Council must take into consideration—
- (a) the effect of the proposed development on the costs of acquisition,
- (b) the imminence of acquisition, and
- (c) the costs of reinstatement of the land for the purpose for which the land is to be acquired.
