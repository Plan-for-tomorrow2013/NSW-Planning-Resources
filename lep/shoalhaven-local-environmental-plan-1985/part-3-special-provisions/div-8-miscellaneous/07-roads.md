# 52 Roads

**(1)**
Except as provided by subclause (2), nothing in this plan restricts or prohibits a public authority from carrying out development required in connection with the construction, reconstruction, improvement, maintenance or repair of a road within an existing road reserve.

**(2)**
Subclause (1) does not apply to any widening, realignment or relocation of an existing physically constructed road which involves works which may have a significant environmental impact.

**(3)**
A person (other than a public authority) must not carry out any work referred to in this clause without the consent of the Council, except for the maintenance or repair of an existing physically constructed road and where the agreement of the owner of the land concerned has been obtained.
