# 54D Certain development by public authorities

If, in the absence of this clause, development by or on behalf of a public authority, being—
- (a) the construction of water storage dams, or
- (b) sewage treatment works, or
- (c) electricity transmission lines,
may be carried out with development consent, the development may be carried out without that consent.
