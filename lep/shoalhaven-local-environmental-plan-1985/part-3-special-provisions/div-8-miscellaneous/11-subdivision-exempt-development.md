# 54B Subdivision—exempt development

A subdivision that is exempt development listed in *Development Control Plan No 89 Exempt and Complying Development* as adopted by the Council on 23 June 2003 is identified as being a subdivision in respect of which an accredited certifier may be a certifying authority.
