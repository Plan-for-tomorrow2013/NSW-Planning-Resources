# 54 Caravan parks prohibited

**(1)**
Despite any other provision of this plan, a person must not carry out development for the purpose of a caravan park on land fronting Inyadda Drive, Manyana, as shown edged heavy black on the map marked "*City of Shoalhaven Local Environmental Plan 1985 (Amendment No 136)*".

**(1A)**
Despite any other provision of this plan, a person must not carry out development for the purpose of a caravan park or dwelling on land within Zone No 7 (f1), shown on the map marked "*City of Shoalhaven Local Environmental Plan 1985 (Amendment No 193)*".

**(2)**
For the purposes of this clause, *caravan park* means land which is used or intended to be used for the parking of a moveable dwelling or the erection of camps, or both.
