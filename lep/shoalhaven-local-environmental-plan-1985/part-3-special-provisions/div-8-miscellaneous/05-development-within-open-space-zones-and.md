# 51 Development within open space zones and on public reserves or other public land

The Council must not consent to the carrying out of development on land within Zone No 6 (a), 6 (b), 6 (c) or 6 (d), or on public reserves or on land owned or controlled by the Council, unless it has considered—
- (a) the need for the proposed development on the land,
- (b) the impact of the proposed development on the land, and
- (c) the need to retain the land for its existing or likely future use.
