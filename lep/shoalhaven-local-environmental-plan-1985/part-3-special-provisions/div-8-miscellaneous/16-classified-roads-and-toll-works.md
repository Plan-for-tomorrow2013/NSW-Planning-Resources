# 54G Classified roads and toll works

**(1)**
If, in the absence of this clause, development for the purpose of a classified road or tollway, or a proposed classified road or tollway, may be carried out with development consent, the development may be carried out without that consent.

**(2)**
In this clause—
*classified road* means a classified road within the meaning of the *Roads Act 1993*.
*tollway* means a work declared to be a tollway under section 52 of the *Roads Act 1993*.
