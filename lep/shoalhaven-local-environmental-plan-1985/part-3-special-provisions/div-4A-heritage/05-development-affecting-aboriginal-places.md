# 20I Development affecting Aboriginal places of heritage significance

Before granting consent to development required by clause 20E that will be carried out in an Aboriginal place of heritage significance, the consent authority must consider the effect on the heritage significance of—
- (a) the place, and
- (b) any Aboriginal object known to be, reasonably likely to be, located at the place.
