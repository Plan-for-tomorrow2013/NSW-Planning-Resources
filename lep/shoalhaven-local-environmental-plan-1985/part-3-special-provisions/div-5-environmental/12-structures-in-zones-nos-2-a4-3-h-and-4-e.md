# 30 Structures in Zones Nos 2 (a4), 3 (h) and 4 (e)

In respect of an application for consent to erect a structure on land within Zone No 2 (a4), 3 (h) or 4 (e), the Council must make an assessment of—
- (a) the likelihood of floodwaters entering the structure,
- (b) the effect of soil instability, and
- (c) the likelihood of damage due to coastal erosion,
and may attach to any consent conditions which, in the opinion of the Council, will prevent or reduce the incidence of flooding or instability.
