# 24 Water catchment areas

**(1)**
This clause applies to land bounded by a broken line on the map and lettered "Water Catchment Area".

**(2)**
The objective of this clause is to protect the quality of stored water that is to be used for drinking.

**(3)**
In determining an application for consent to development on or of land to which this clause applies, the Council must take into account the objective of this clause and the adequacy of measures proposed by the applicant to avoid or mitigate any adverse effects of the proposed development on the quality of stored water that is to be used for drinking.
