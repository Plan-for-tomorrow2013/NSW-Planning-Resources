# 35A Buffers

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within a buffer area identified on the map.

**(2)**
The Council must not consent to any development on land to which this clause applies which in the opinion of the Council would lead—
- (a) in relation to a quarry—to the sterilisation of the resource that is extracted at the quarry, or
- (b) in relation to any other matter—to an impediment to the particular purpose indicated by scarlet lettering on the map.
