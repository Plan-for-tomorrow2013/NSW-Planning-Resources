# 22 Activities in Zone Nos 1 (c), 7 (a), 7 (c), 7 (d1), 7 (d2), 7 (e), 7 (f1), 7 (f2) and 7 (f3)

**(1)**
A person must not, without the consent of the Council, ringbark, cut down, top, lop or otherwise destroy any tree having a height of 3 metres or more on land within Zone No 1 (c), 7 (a), 7 (c), 7 (d1), 7 (d2), 7 (e), 7 (f1), 7 (f2) or 7 (f3).

**(2)**
A person must not, without the consent of the Council, on land within Zone No 7 (a)—
- (a) clear the land of trees,
- (b) place fill upon such land,
- (c) alter the level of the surface of such land, or
- (d) clear or remove vegetation from such land.
