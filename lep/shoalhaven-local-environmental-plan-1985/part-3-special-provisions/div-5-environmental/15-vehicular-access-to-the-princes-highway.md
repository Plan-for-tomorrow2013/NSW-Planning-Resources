# 33B Vehicular access to the Princes Highway (Murramerang Street), Ulladulla

**(1)**
This clause applies to Portion 213, Parish of Ulladulla, County of St Vincent and Lots 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5, DP 21356, Princes Highway (Murramerang Street), Ulladulla.

**(2)**
The Council must not consent to the development of land to which this clause applies that involves vehicular access to the Princes Highway unless the Council is satisfied—
- (a) that, having regard to the nature and scale of the proposed development, likely traffic generation will not be excessive, and
- (b) that there is no feasible alternative access way for vehicles, and
- (c) that there will be no significant adverse impact to public safety or the efficiency of the Princes Highway in the area.
