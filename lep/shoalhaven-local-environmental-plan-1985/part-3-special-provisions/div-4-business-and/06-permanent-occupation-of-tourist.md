# 20B Permanent occupation of tourist facilities

**(1)**
Despite any other provision of this plan, the Council may consent to development of land described in Schedule 14 only for the purpose of—
- (a) a tourist facility, or
- (b) a use or activity that is an integral part of a tourist facility.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this plan (including subclause (1)), the Council may grant consent to development of land described in Schedule 14 for the purposes of permanent residential occupation if—
- (a) the development is part of development referred to in subclause (1), and
- (b) no more than 25% of units within the development referred to in subclause (1) are to be used for the purposes of permanent residential occupation.

**(3)**
The Council may grant consent for the purposes of permanent residential occupation of a maximum of one unit under subclause (2), where the development involves less than four units.
