# 16AB Restriction on subdivision

Any land on which a dwelling-house is erected in conjunction with medium density residential development must not be subdivided to create a separate legal title for the dwelling-house except under the *Strata Schemes (Freehold Development) Act 1973* or the *Community Land Development Act 1989*.
