# 15B Dwelling-houses within Zone No 7 (d2)

Despite any other provision of this plan, a dwelling-house may, with the consent of the Council, be erected on an allotment of land within Zone No 7 (d2) on which another lawfully erected dwelling-house is or has been erected if the first-mentioned dwelling-house is to wholly replace the second-mentioned dwelling-house.
