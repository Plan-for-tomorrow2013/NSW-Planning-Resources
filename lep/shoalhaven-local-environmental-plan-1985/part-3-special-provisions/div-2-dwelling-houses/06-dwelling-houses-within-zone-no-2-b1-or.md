# 16AA Dwelling-houses within Zone No 2 (b1) or 2 (b2) in conjunction with medium density development

The Council may consent to the erection of a dwelling-house on land within Zone No 2 (b1) or 2 (b2), but only if it is satisfied—
- (a) that the dwelling-house will form an integral part of a medium density housing development, and
- (b) that the dwelling-house will maintain or enhance the streetscape or residential amenity of surrounding land.
