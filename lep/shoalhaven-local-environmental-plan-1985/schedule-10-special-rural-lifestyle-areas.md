# Schedule 10 Special Rural Lifestyle Areas

*(Clause 46 (5))*

| Special Rural Lifestyle Area No and Name | Objectives of Special Rural Lifestyle Area |
|---|---|
|  | The objectives are— to allow for rural residential development in a variety of lot sizes to suit topographical and environmental conditions, and to have larger sized lots (1 hectare) fronting The Wool Road and near the estuarine wetlands with smaller allotments (4000 square metres) elsewhere, and to preserve as much tree cover as possible, and to limit road access to The Wool Road to one intersection point. |
| (Cabbage Tree Lane Area, Nowra Hill) | The objectives are— to encourage rural residential development with a variety of allotment sizes appropriate to the location, and to allow for the upgrading and realignment of Cabbage Tree Lane, and to minimise direct access to Cabbage Tree Lane, and to have appropriate lot sizes to the west to enable effective bushfire hazard reduction procedures, and to have appropriate lot sizes to the south to allow for a noise transition zone, and to protect the creek system and the areas in Flat Rock Creek catchment from sedimentation and pollution. |
| Coolangatta Mountain Area, Shoalhaven Heads | The objectives are— to allow and promote the expansion of the Coolangatta Village Motel/Tourist facility, and to enhance the heritage items of the area, and to allow ancillary commercial tourist activity and the sale of products especially packaged under the Coolangatta label, and to secure safe traffic access to Bolong Road through adjoining areas. |
| Coolangatta Mountain Area, Shoalhaven Heads | The objectives are— the objectives are to allow for creation of up to 50 residential lots and the erection of a dwelling house and/or the carrying out of dual occupancy development (only where the development results in attached dwellings) on such allotment and/or 50 tourist accommodation units, and to provide for landscape screening to maintain the rural and scenic character of the adjoining areas, and to limit the height of any dwelling or tourist accommodation to single storey and a loft not having an overall height greater than 6 metres, and all development to be in accordance with the ability of the land to absorb effluent. |
| South Coolangatta Mountain Area, Shoalhaven Heads | The objectives are— to allow for up to 50 tourist accommodation units of not more than 80 square metres gross floor area each, and to retain and protect existing screen vegetation, and to minimise removal of vegetation and ground disturbance in the development and its associated access roads, and to minimise the height and bulk of any buildings. |
| South Coolangatta Mountain Area, Shoalhaven Heads | The objectives are— to allow for the creation of up to 12 rural residential allotments and the erection of a dwelling house and/or the carrying out of dual occupancy development (only where the development results in attached dwellings) on each allotment in accordance with the ability of the land to absorb effluent, and to protect and enhance screening vegetation to the south, and to prohibit the erection of building above the 50 metre AHD contour, and to protect the trees above the 50 metre AHD contour. |
| East Coolangatta Mountain Area | The objectives are— to allow for the creation of up to 6 rural residential lots and the erection of a dwelling house and/or the carrying out of dual occupancy development (only where the development results in attached dwellings) on each allotment in accordance with the capability of the land to absorb effluent, and to limit the height of any dwelling to single storey and a loft area with an overall height of 6 metres, and to provide landscape screening to maintain the rural and scenic character of the area, and to prohibit the erection of buildings above the 38 metre AHD contour. |
| Bryces Road Area | The objectives are— to allow for the creation of up to 18 rural residential lots and the erection of a dwelling thereon and/or the carrying out of dual occupancy development (only where the development results in attached dwellings) in accordance with the ability of the land to absorb effluent, and to provide landscape screening to maintain the rural and scenic character of the area. |
| Backforest Road Area | The objectives are— to allow for the creation of up to 4 rural residential lots and the erection of a dwelling thereon and/or the carrying out of dual occupancy development (only where the development results in attached dwellings) in accordance with the ability of the land to absorb effluent, and to provide landscape screening to maintain the rural and scenic character of the area. |
| North-East Coolangatta Mountain Area, Shoalhaven Heads | The objectives are— to allow up to 19 rural residential lots each and the erection of a dwelling house thereon and/or the erection of a residential flat building on two of the lots containing a total of not more than 10 dwellings all located within the building envelope shown on the map, and the provision of adequate screening to maintain the rural character of the surrounding area. |
| (Tallow Wood Road, West Burrill Lake) | The objectives are— to provide for up to 6 rural residential lots each with sufficient area to accommodate a dwelling-house, effluent disposal and vehicular access, and to allow for the erection of a dwelling-house or the carrying out of dual occupancy development where the development results in attached dwellings on each of the rural residential lots created, and to allow for the siting of buildings so as to minimise the hazard from bush fire, and to preserve as much tree cover as possible, in particular that on the upper slopes, and to provide for building sites with sufficient area to accommodate a dwelling-house, effluent disposal and vehicular access, to be identified at the subdivision stage, and to ensure that appropriate arrangements are made for the treatment and disposal of effluent, and to ensure that appropriate arrangements are made for the treatment and disposal of surface storm water drainage. |
| (Tallow Wood Road, West Burrill Lake) | The objectives are— to provide for up to 5 rural residential lots each with sufficient area to accommodate a dwelling-house, effluent disposal and vehicular access, and to allow the erection of a dwelling-house or the carrying out of dual occupancy development where the development results in attached dwellings on each of the rural residential lots created, and to allow for the siting of buildings so as to minimise the hazard from bush fire, and to restrict the height of buildings to 1 storey, and to ensure that run-off is diverted away from direct discharge to Burrill Lake by way of a contour bank, and to provide for building sites with sufficient area to accommodate a dwelling-house, effluent disposal and vehicular access to be identified at the sub-division stage, and to preserve as much tree cover as possible, and to ensure that appropriate arrangements are made for the treatment and disposal of effluent, and to ensure that appropriate arrangements are made for the treatment and disposal of surface storm water drainage. |
