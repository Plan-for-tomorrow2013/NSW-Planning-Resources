# 7.16 Subdivision of land at Black Hill Road, Black Hill

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Black Hill Road, Black Hill, being part of Lot 1131, DP 1057179 and part of Lot 119, DP 1154904, that is in Zone C4 Environmental Living.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.1(3), development consent may be granted for the subdivision of land to which this clause applies, but only if—
- (a) the land will be subdivided into no more than 4 lots, and
- (b) no more than 1 dwelling house will be erected on each lot.
