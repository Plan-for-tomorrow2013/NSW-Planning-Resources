# 4.2F Erection of a dwelling house associated with a rural use other than for the purpose of agriculture

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to provide for the erection of dwelling houses on land in the following zones in certain circumstances—
- (a) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
- (b) Zone RU2 Rural Landscape,
- (c) Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots,
- (d) Zone RU6 Transition.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development consent may be granted to the erection of a dwelling house on a lot in a zone to which this clause applies and on which there is currently no dwelling house if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the lot is being used for a lawful purpose other than agriculture, and
- (b) the use of the lot requires an ancillary dwelling, and
- (c) the dwelling house will not require its own separate access.
