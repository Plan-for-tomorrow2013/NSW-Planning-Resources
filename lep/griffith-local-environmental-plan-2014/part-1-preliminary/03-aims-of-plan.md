# 1.2 Aims of Plan

**(1)**
This Plan aims to make local environmental planning provisions for land in Griffith in accordance with the relevant standard environmental planning instrument under section 3.20 of the Act.

**(2)**
The particular aims of this Plan are as follows—
- (aa) to protect and promote the use and development of land for arts and cultural activity, including music and other performance arts,
- (a) to prevent unnecessary urban sprawl by promoting business, industrial, rural and residential uses within and adjacent to existing precincts related to those uses,
- (b) to minimise land use conflict in general by creating areas of transition between different and potentially conflicting land uses,
- (c) to provide a variety of development options to meet the needs of the community with regard to housing, employment and services,
- (d) to manage and protect areas of environmental significance,
- (e) to recognise the historical development of the area and to preserve heritage items associated with it.
