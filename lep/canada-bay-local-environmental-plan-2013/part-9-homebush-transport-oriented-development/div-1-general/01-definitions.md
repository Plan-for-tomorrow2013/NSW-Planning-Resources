# 9.1 Definitions

In this part—
*Homebush TOD Precinct* means the land to which this part applies.
*Homebush Transport Oriented Development Precinct Design Guide* means the *Homebush Transport Oriented Development Precinct Design Guide* published by the Department in November 2024.
