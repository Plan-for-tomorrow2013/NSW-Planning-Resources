# 8.1 Application of Part

**(1)**
This Part applies to land identified as "Burwood-Concord Precinct", "Homebush North Precinct" or "Kings Bay Precinct" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
A reference in this Part to a numbered Area is a reference to the Area with that number on the *Key Sites Map*.
