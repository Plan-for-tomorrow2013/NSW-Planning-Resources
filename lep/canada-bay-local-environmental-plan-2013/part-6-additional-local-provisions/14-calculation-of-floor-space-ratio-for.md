# 6.16 Calculation of floor space ratio for land at 17 George Street, North Strathfield

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified on the *Key Sites Map* as "Area 54".

**(2)**
In calculating a floor space ratio under clause 4.5 for a building on land to which this clause applies, floor area used for the following purposes may be excluded from the gross floor area of the building—
- (a) community facilities,
- (b) educational establishments,
- (c) information and education facilities.
