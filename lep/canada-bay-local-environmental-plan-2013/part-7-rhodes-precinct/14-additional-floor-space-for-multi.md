# 7.14 Additional floor space for multi dwelling housing (terraces) in Cavell Avenue Character Area

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in the Cavell Avenue Character Area.

**(2)**
Buildings resulting from the following development on land to which this clause applies may exceed the maximum floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* by 5%—
- (a) development for the purposes of multi dwelling housing (terraces),
- (b) mixed use development that includes development for the purposes of multi dwelling housing (terraces).
