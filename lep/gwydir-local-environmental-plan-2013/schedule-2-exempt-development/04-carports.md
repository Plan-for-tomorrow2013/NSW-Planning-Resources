# Schedule 2, Part 4 — Carports

## Part 4 Carports

### 4.1 Specified development

The construction or installation of a carport is development specified for this schedule if it is—
- (a) constructed or installed on land in Zone RU1, RU5, R5, E4, RE1 or RE2, and
- (b) not constructed or installed—
  - (i) on or in a heritage item or a draft heritage item, or
  - (ii) in a heritage conservation area or a draft heritage conservation area, or
  - (iii) on land in a foreshore area.

### 4.2 Development standards

**(1)**
The standards for development specified in clause 4.1 are that the development must—
- (a) have a roofed structure for the shelter of motor vehicles, and
- (b) have 2 or more sides open and at least one-third of its perimeter open, and
- (c) not have a floor area of more than—
  - (i) for a lot larger than 300m2 in a rural zone or Zone R5—85m2, or
  - (ii) for a lot larger than 300m2 in another zone—25m2, or
  - (iii) for a lot 300m2 or less—20m2, and
- (d) not be higher than—
  - (i) 3m above ground level (existing), or
  - (ii) if attached to an existing single-storey dwelling—the roof gutter line, and
- (e) be located at least 1m behind the building line of any road frontage, and
- (f) be located at a distance from each lot boundary of at least—
  - (i) for development carried out in Zone RU1 and R5—5m, or
  - (ii) for development carried out in another zone—900mm, and
- (g) have a roof located at least 500mm from each lot boundary, and
- (h) to the extent it is comprised of metal components—be constructed of low reflective, factory pre-coloured materials, and
- (i) not involve the construction of a new driveway or gutter crossing unless the consent of the relevant road authority for each opening of a public road required for the development has been obtained under the *Roads Act 1993*, and
- (j) be constructed or installed so that any roof water is disposed of—
  - (i) into the existing stormwater drainage system, or
  - (ii) if no stormwater drainage system exists—in a manner that does not discharge to an adjoining property, and
- (k) if it is connected to a fascia—be connected in accordance with a professional engineer's specifications, and
- (l) if it is located on bush fire prone land and is less than 5m from a dwelling—be constructed of non-combustible material, and
- (m) if it is constructed or installed in a heritage conservation area or a draft heritage conservation area—be located in the rear yard, and
- (n) be located in a way that does not reduce vehicular access to, or parking or loading or unloading on, or from, the lot.

**(2)**
There must not be more than one carport—
- (a) if there is a dwelling on the lot—per lot, or
- (b) otherwise—the greater of the following—
  - (i) per lot, or
  - (ii) per each separate occupation on the lot.
