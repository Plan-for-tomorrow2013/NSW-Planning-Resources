# Schedule 2, Part 3 — Cabanas, cubby houses, ferneries, garden sheds, gazebos and greenhouses

## Part 3 Cabanas, cubby houses, ferneries, garden sheds, gazebos and greenhouses

### 3.1 Specified development

The construction or installation of a cabana, cubby house, fernery, garden shed, gazebo or greenhouse is development specified for this schedule if it is—
- (a) constructed or installed on land in Zone RU1, RU5, R5, E4, RE1 or RE2, and
- (b) not constructed or installed—
  - (i) on or in a heritage item or a draft heritage item, or
  - (ii) in a heritage conservation area or a draft heritage conservation area, or
  - (iii) on land in a foreshore area, or
  - (iv) in an environmentally sensitive area.

### 3.2 Development standards

**(1)**
The standards for development specified in clause 3.1 are that the development must—
- (a) not have a floor area of more than—
  - (i) on land in Zone RU1 or R5—100m2, or
  - (ii) on land in another zone—50m2, and
- (b) not be higher than 3m above ground level (existing), and
- (c) be located at a distance from each lot boundary of at least—
  - (i) for development carried out in Zone RU1 or R5—5m, or
  - (ii) for development carried out in another zone—900mm, and
- (d) if it is not on land in Zone RU1—be located behind the building line of any road frontage, and
- (e) not be a shipping container, and
- (f) be constructed or installed so that roof water is disposed of—
  - (i) without causing a nuisance to adjoining owners, and
  - (ii) if no stormwater drainage system exists—in a manner that does not discharge to an adjoining property, and
- (g) to the extent it is comprised of metal components—be constructed of low reflective, factory pre-coloured materials if it is located on land in a residential zone, and
- (h) if it is located on bush fire prone land and is less than 5m from a dwelling—be constructed of non-combustible material, and
- (i) if it is constructed or installed in a heritage conservation area or a draft heritage conservation area—be located in the rear yard, and
- (j) if it is located adjacent to another building—be located so that it does not interfere with the entry to, or exit from, or the fire safety measures contained within, that building, and
- (k) be a class 10 building and not be habitable, and
- (l) be located at least 1m from any registered easement, and
- (m) in relation to a cabana—not be connected to water supply or sewerage services, and
- (n) not result in more than 2 structures to which clause 3.1 applies being located on the land.

**(2)**
In this clause—
*class 10 building* means a building classified as a class 10 building in the *Building Code of Australia*.
