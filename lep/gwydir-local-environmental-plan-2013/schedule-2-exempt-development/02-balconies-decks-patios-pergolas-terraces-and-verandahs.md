# Schedule 2, Part 2 — Balconies, decks, patios, pergolas, terraces and verandahs

## Part 2 Balconies, decks, patios, pergolas, terraces and verandahs

### 2.1 Specified development

The construction or installation of a balcony, deck, patio, pergola, terrace or verandah is development specified for this schedule if it is—
- (a) constructed or installed on land in Zone RU1, RU5, R5, E4 or RE2, and
- (b) not constructed or installed—
  - (i) on or in a heritage item or a draft heritage item, or
  - (ii) in a heritage conservation area or a draft heritage conservation area, or
  - (iii) on land in a foreshore area, and
- (c) for a replacement deck—the same height as the deck being replaced.

### 2.2 Development standards

**(1)**
The standards for development specified in clause 2.1 are that the development must—
- (a) not have an area of more than 25m2, and
- (b) not cause the total floor area of all such structures on the lot to be more than—
  - (i) for a lot larger than 300m2—15% of the ground floor area of the dwelling on the lot, or
  - (ii) for a lot that is 300m2 or less—25m2, and
- (c) not have an enclosing wall higher than 1.4m, and
- (d) be located—
  - (i) if carried out in connection with farm experience premises or farm gate premises—more than 50m from a road, or
  - (ii) otherwise—behind the building line of a road frontage, and
- (e) be located at a distance of 900mm from each lot boundary, and
- (f) to the extent it is comprised of metal components—be constructed of low reflective, factory pre-coloured materials, and
- (g) if it is a roofed structure—
  - (i) have a roof that does not overhang the structure by more than 600mm on each side, or
  - (ii) if it is attached to a dwelling—not extend above the roof gutter line of the dwelling, and
- (h) not be higher than 3m at its highest point above ground level (existing), and
- (i) if it is connected to a fascia—be connected in accordance with a professional engineer's specifications, and
- (j) be constructed or installed so that roof water is disposed of—
  - (i) into an existing stormwater drainage system, or
  - (ii) if no stormwater drainage system exists—in a manner that does not discharge to an adjoining property, and
- (k) not interfere with the functioning of existing drainage fixtures or flow paths, and
- (l) if it is located on bush fire prone land and is less than 5m from a dwelling—be constructed of non-combustible material.

**(2)**
Subclause (1)(f) does not apply to development carried out in connection with a building used for the following purposes—
- (a) farm experience premises,
- (b) farm gate premises,
- (c) farm stay accommodation.
