# 2.1 Land use zones

The land use zones under this Plan are as follows—
- Rural Zones
RU1 Primary Production
RU5 Village
- Residential Zones
R5 Large Lot Residential
- Employment Zones
E4 General Industrial
- Recreation Zones
RE1 Public Recreation
RE2 Private Recreation
- Conservation Zones
C1 National Parks and Nature Reserves
