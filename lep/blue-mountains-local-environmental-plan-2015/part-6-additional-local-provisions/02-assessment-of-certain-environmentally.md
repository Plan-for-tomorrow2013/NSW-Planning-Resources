# 6.2 Assessment of certain environmentally sensitive land

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to enable the consent authority to determine that certain land is not environmentally sensitive land.

> Note: Environmentally sensitive land is defined in the Dictionary to this Plan.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the following land—
- (a) land that is within 40 metres of the top of the bank of a watercourse,
- (b) land that is within 60 metres of any significant vegetation community,
- (c) land that is within 20 metres of any rare species of flora.

**(3)**
For the purposes of determining a development application, the consent authority may, after an assessment of the ecological or geomorphic functions of the land to which this clause applies, determine that any part of that land is not environmentally sensitive land for the purposes of this Plan.
