# 6.24 Use of certain land at Maroubra

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 4" on the *Special Provisions Area Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of serviced apartments unless the consent authority is satisfied the development is part of a mixed use development.
