# 6.26 Affordable housing contributions for Kensington and Kingsford town centres

**(1)**
This clause applies to development, other than excluded development, on land identified as "Area 1" on the *Special Provisions Area Map*.

**(2)**
When granting development consent to development to which this clause applies, the consent authority may impose a condition requiring an affordable housing contribution equivalent to 5% of the total floor area of the part of the building intended to be used for residential accommodation (the *contribution*).

**(3)**
A condition imposed under this clause must permit a person to satisfy the contribution by—
- (a) for development for the purposes of co-living housing or serviced apartments—a monetary contribution paid to the Council, or
- (b) for other development—
  - (i) a dedication, in favour of the Council, of land comprising 1 or more dwellings, each having a gross floor area of at least 50m2, and
  - (ii) if the dedication under subparagraph (i) does not meet the requirement under subclause (2)—a monetary contribution of the remainder paid to the Council.

**(4)**
A monetary contribution must be calculated in accordance with the *Kensington and Kingsford Town Centres - Affordable Housing Plan* adopted by the Council on 10 December 2019.
