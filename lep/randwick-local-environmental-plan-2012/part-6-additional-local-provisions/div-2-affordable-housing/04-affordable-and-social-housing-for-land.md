# 6.28 Affordable and social housing for land at 47-55 Bunnerong Road, Kingsford

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 433534, 47-55 Bunnerong Road, Kingsford.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 50% of the gross floor area of all buildings on the site that will be used for the purposes of residential accommodation will be used for one or more of the following purposes—
- (a) affordable housing provided by or on behalf of a social housing provider, within the meaning of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021*,
- (b) social housing premises, within the meaning of the *Residential Tenancies Act 2010*.
