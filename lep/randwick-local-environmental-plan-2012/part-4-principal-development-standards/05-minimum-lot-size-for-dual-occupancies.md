# 4.1C Minimum lot size for dual occupancies

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to provide for housing diversity and affordability in residential zones.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of dual occupancies on a lot in Zone R2 Low Density Residential unless the area of the lot is at least 550m2.
