# 4.1D Subdivision of dual occupancies in Zone R2

**(1)**
This clause applies to a dual occupancy on land in Zone R2 Low Density Residential for which development consent was granted before 6 July 2018.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision in this Plan, development consent may be granted for the subdivision of a dual occupancy to which this clause applies if the development meets the standards specified in the following provisions of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*—
- (a) for strata subdivision—clause 6.2, or
- (b) for Torrens title subdivision—clause 6.4.

> Note: Development consent may be granted under clause 4.6 for development that would contravene a standard specified in this clause.
