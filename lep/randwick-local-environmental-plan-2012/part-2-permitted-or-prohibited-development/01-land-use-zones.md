E1 Local Centre
E2 Commercial Centre
E4 General Industrial
- Special Purpose Zones
SP1 Special Activities
SP2 Infrastructure
- Recreation Zones
RE1 Public Recreation
RE2 Private Recreation
- Conservation Zones
C1 National Parks and Nature Reserves
C2 Environmental Conservation
