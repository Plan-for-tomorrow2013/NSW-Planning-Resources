# 8.1 Definitions

In this Part—
*Frenchs Forest Precinct* means the land identified on the *Precincts Map* as the Frenchs Forest Precinct.
*Site F* means the land identified on the *Key Sites Map* as Site F.
*Site G* means the land identified on the *Key Sites Map* as Site G.
*Site H* means the land identified on the *Key Sites Map* as Site H.
*Site I* means the land identified on the *Key Sites Map* as Site I.
