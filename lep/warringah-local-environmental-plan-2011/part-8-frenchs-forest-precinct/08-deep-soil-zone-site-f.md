# 8.8 Deep soil zone—Site F

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on Site F unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will not result in less than 5,500 square metres of deep soil zone in Site F.

**(2)**
In this clause—
*deep soil zone* means a landscaped area with no building above or below the ground, but does not include an area used or intended to be used for a driveway or parking.
