# 6.1 Definitions

In this Part—
*public utility infrastructure*, in relation to an urban release area, includes infrastructure for any of the following—
- (a) the supply of water,
- (b) the supply of electricity,
- (c) the disposal and management of sewage.
*urban release area* has the same meaning as in *State Environmental Planning Policy (Planning Systems) 2021*, section 4.42.
*Urban Release Area Map* has the same meaning as in *State Environmental Planning Policy (Planning Systems) 2021*, section 4.42.
