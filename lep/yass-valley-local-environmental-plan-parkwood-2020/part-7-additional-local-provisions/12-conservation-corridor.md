# 7.13 Conservation corridor

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to preserve and enhance the environmental values of land in Zone C2 Environmental Conservation along the Murrumbidgee River and Ginninderra Creek, and
- (b) to mitigate environmental hazards in response to urban development.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Zone R1 General Residential or Zone SP1 Special Activities.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the likely impact of the development on the management, protection and enhancement of the ecological, scenic and Aboriginal cultural heritage values of land in Zone C2 Environmental Conservation.
