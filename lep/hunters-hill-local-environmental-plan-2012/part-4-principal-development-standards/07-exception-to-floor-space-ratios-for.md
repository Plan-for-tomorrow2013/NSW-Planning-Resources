# 4.4A Exception to floor space ratios for certain land in the Hunters Hill Village

**(1)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on land identified as "Area 1" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* is—
- (a) for a site that has an area of at least 600 square metres but less than 900 square metres—1.75:1, and
- (b) for a site that has an area of 900 square metres or more—2:1.

**(2)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a residential flat building on land identified as "Area 2" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* that has a site area of 1,000 square metres or more is 0.8:1.
