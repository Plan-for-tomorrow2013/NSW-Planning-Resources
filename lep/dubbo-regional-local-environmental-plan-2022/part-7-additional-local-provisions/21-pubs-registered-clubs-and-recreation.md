# 7.21 Pubs, registered clubs and recreation facilities (indoor) in certain land in Zone E1

Development consent must not be granted to development for the following purposes on land identified as "Area F" on the *Land Zoning Map* if the gross floor area will exceed 500m2—
- (a) pubs,
- (b) registered clubs,
- (c) recreation facilities (indoor).
