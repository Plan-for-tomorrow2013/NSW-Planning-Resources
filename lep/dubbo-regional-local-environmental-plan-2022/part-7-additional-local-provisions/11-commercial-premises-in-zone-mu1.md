# 7.11 Commercial premises in Zone MU1

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of—
- (a) business premises or office premises if the gross floor area is greater than 200m2, or
- (b) retail premises or light industries if the gross floor area is greater than 150m2.

**(3)**
Subclause (2) does not apply to development involving a building that existed immediately before 11 November 2011.
