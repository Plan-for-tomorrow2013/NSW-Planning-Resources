# 5.1A Development on land intended to be acquired for public purposes

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to limit development on certain land intended to be acquired for a public purpose.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land—
- (a) shown on the *Land Reservation Acquisition Map* and specified in Column 1 of the table to this clause, and
- (b) that has not been acquired by the relevant authority of the State specified for the land in clause 5.1.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies other than development for a purpose specified for the land in Column 2.

| Land | Development |
|---|---|
| Zone R1 General Residential | Car parks |
| Zone C3 Environmental Management | Car parks |
