# 6.7 Location of sex services premises

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purposes of sex services premises if the premises will be located on land that adjoins, or that is separated only by a road from, land—
- (a) in Zone R1 General Residential, Zone R2 Low Density Residential, Zone R3 Medium Density Residential or Zone RE1 Public Recreation, or
- (b) used as a place of public worship or for community or school uses.

**(2)**
In deciding whether to grant consent to any such development, the consent authority must take into account the impact that the proposed development would have on children who use the land.
