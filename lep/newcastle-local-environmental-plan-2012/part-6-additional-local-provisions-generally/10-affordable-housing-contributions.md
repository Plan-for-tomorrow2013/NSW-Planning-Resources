# 6.10 Affordable housing contributions

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to capture increases in land value when changes to planning controls enable an increase in housing density,
- (b) to enable the imposition of conditions relating to the provision of affordable housing,
- (c) to deliver housing that promotes and retains a socially diverse population.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on land identified as the following on the *Affordable Housing Contributions Scheme Map*—
- (a) "Area A",
- (b) "Area B",
- (c) "Area C",
- (d) "Area D",
- (e) "Area E".

**(3)**
The consent authority may, when granting development consent to development to which this clause applies, impose a condition requiring an affordable housing contribution.

**(4)**
A condition imposed under this clause must provide for the affordable housing levy contribution to be satisfied by—
- (a) a dedication, in favour of the Council, of land, with any remainder paid as a monetary contribution to the Council, or
- (b) a dedication, in favour of the Council, of land comprising one or more dwellings, each having a gross floor area of at least 50m2, with any remainder paid as a monetary contribution to the Council, or
- (c) a monetary contribution paid to the Council.

**(5)**
The affordable housing levy contribution must be calculated in accordance with the Newcastle Affordable Housing Contributions Scheme.

**(6)**
In this clause—
*Newcastle Affordable Housing Contributions Scheme* means the *Newcastle Affordable Housing Contributions Scheme* adopted by the Council on 25 March 2025.
