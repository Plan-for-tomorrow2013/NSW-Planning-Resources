# 6.5 Public safety—licensed premises

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that development applications for licensed premises consider any impact on public safety.

**(2)**
Before granting consent for development involving the use of land as licensed premises, the consent authority must consider any impact of the proposed development on public safety.

**(3)**
In this clause—
*licensed premises* means a hotel within the meaning of the *Liquor Act 2007* or a registered club.
