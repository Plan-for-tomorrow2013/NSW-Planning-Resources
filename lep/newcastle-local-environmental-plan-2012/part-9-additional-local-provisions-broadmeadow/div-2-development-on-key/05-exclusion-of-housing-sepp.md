# 9.10 Exclusion of Housing SEPP

The following provisions of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021* do not apply in relation to development on a land identified as a key site under this division—
- (a) Chapter 2, Part 2, Divisions 1, 2, 3 and 5,
- (b) Chapter 3, Parts 3 and 4.
