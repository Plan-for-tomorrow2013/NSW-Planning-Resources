# 9.2 Application

**(1)**
This part applies to land identified as "Broadmeadow Precinct" on the *Broadmeadow Precinct Map*.

**(2)**
If there is an inconsistency between this part and another provision of this plan, whether made before or after the commencement of this part, this part prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.
