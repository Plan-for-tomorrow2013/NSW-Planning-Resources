# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of land at 301 Golf Links Road, Condobolin

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 301 Golf Links Road, Condobolin, being Lot 16, DP 914643, identified as "Item 1" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of an animal shelter is permitted.

## 2 Use of land at 48 Brownies Lane, Tottenham

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lots 68, 69 and 162, DP 753968, 48 Brownies Lane, Tottenham, identified as "Item 2" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of vehicle repair stations is permitted with development consent.
