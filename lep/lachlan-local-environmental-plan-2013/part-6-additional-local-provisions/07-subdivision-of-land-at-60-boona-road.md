# 6.8 Subdivision of land at 60 Boona Road, Condobolin

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 280, DP 752080, 60 Boona Road, Condobolin.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the subdivision of land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied each lot resulting from the subdivision will be connected to a water reticulation system and a sewage reticulation system.
