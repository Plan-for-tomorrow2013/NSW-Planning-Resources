# 6.5 Infrastructure—Pacific Highway access

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that access to the Pacific Highway is provided in a manner that does not impede the safe and efficient operation of the Pacific Highway as part of the national highway network.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for the subdivision of land in an urban release area unless arrangements have been made, to the satisfaction of the consent authority, for the provision of vehicular access from the urban release area to the Pacific Highway, including the closure or modification of any existing vehicular access from any land adjoining the Pacific Highway, if necessary.
