# 7.32 Maximum floor space that may be used for retail premises for certain land in Zone E1 at Hinchinbrook

For development of a site on land shown as being within Area 6 on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*, the maximum gross floor area that may be used for the purposes of retail premises on that land is 1,000 square metres.
