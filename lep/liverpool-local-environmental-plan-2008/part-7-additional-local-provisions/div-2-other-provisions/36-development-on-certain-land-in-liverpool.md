# 7.45 Development on certain land in Liverpool

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Liverpool identified as "Area H" on the *Land Zoning Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of business premises or retail premises unless the consent authority is satisfied —
- (a) the gross floor area of the business premises or retail premises will not exceed 1,200m2, and
- (b) each individual premises is not more than 150m2 other than not more than 2 individual premises that are greater than 150m2 but less than 300m2.
