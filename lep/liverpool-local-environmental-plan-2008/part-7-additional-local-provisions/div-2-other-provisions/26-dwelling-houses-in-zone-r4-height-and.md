# 7.33 Dwelling houses in Zone R4—height and floor space ratio controls

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone R4 High Density Residential.

**(2)**
Despite clauses 4.3(2) and 4.4(2), development consent must not be granted to development for the purpose of a dwelling house on land to which this clause applies unless—
- (a) the dwelling house will not exceed a height of 8.5 metres, and
- (b) the floor space ratio of the dwelling house will not exceed 0.6:1.
