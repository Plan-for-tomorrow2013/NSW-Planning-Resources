# 7.44 Use of certain land in Liverpool in Zone MU1

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Health Services Facility" on the *Key Sites Map* that is in Zone MU1 Mixed Use.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of health services facilities is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**
A building resulting from the development may have—
- (a) a height of up to 79m, and
- (b) a floor space ratio of up to 6.9:1.
