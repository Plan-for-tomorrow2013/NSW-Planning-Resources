# 7.43 Development of land at 2 Macarthur Drive, Holsworthy

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 5, DP 825745, 2 Macarthur Drive, Holsworthy.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development if the development will result in the gross floor area of retail premises on the land exceeding 9,000m2.
