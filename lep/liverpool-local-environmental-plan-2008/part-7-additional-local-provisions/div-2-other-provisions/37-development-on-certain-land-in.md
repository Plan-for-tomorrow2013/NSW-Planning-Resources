# 7.46 Development on certain land in Liverpool requiring preparation of development control plan

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Liverpool identified as "Area H" on the *Land Zoning Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of residential flat buildings or commercial premises unless the consent authority is satisfied a development control plan that provides for the matters specified in subclause (3) has been prepared for the land.

**(3)**
The development control plan must provide for the following matters—
- (a) through-site pedestrian links between Hoxton Park Road and Dale Avenue,
- (b) vehicular and pedestrian access and circulation,
- (c) appropriate parking,
- (d) built form controls, including the massing and modulation of buildings,
- (e) landscaping,
- (f) housing diversity.
