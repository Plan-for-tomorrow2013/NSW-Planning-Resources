# 4.1A Exceptions to minimum lot sizes for dwellings

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to encourage housing diversity without adversely impacting on residential amenity, and
- (b) to support affordable housing by allowing smaller lot sizes.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone R1 General Residential,
- (b) Zone R3 Medium Density Residential.

**(3)**
Development consent may be granted to a single development application for development on the land consisting of both of the following—
- (a) the subdivision of land into 2 or more lots,
- (b) either—
  - (i) if there is an existing dwelling on one of the lots resulting from the subdivision—the erection of a dwelling on the other lots resulting from the subdivision, or
  - (ii) otherwise—the erection of a dwelling on each lot resulting from the subdivision.
