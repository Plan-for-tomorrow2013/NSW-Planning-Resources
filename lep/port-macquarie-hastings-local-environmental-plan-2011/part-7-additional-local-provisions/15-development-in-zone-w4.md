# 7.16 Development in Zone W4

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that industrial activities proposed to be located in Zone W4 Working Waterfront require direct waterfront access or are associated with other industrial activities that require direct waterfront access.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for the purpose of industry on land in Zone W4 Working Waterfront unless the consent authority is satisfied that the relevant industrial activity requires direct waterfront access or is associated with another industrial activity that requires direct waterfront access.
