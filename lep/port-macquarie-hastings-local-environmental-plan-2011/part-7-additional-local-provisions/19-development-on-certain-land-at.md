# 7.20 Development on certain land at Integrity Circuit, Thrumster

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Integrity Circuit, Thrumster, identified as "Item 14" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of shop top housing is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**
Subclause (2) does not apply unless—
- (a) the consent authority has considered the *Thrumster Business Park Utilities Plan* published by the Council on 1 December 2025, and
- (b) the consent authority is satisfied the Thrumster Wastewater Treatment Plant and local sewer network have the capacity to service the proposed development, or—
  - (i) appropriate arrangements have been made to ensure the Thrumster Wastewater Treatment Plant and local sewer network will have capacity, and
  - (ii) the arrangements were carried out in accordance with the *Thrumster Business Park Utilities Plan*.
