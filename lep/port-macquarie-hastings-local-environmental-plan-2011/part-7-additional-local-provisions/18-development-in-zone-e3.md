# 7.19 Development in Zone E3

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to protect the primacy of the Port Macquarie Central Business District and the hierarchy of centres.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for the purposes of office and business premises on land in Zone E3 Productivity Support if the office and business premises have a total gross floor area of more than 300m2.
