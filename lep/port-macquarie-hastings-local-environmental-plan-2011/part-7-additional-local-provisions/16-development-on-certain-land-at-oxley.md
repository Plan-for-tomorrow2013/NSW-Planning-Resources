# 7.17 Development on certain land at Oxley Highway, Sancrox

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 1261690, Oxley Highway, Sancrox.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to subdivide the land to which this clause applies to create lots that are less than the minimum lot size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to the land if the consent authority is satisfied the following are on the land—
- (a) a highway service centre,
- (b) hotel or motel accommodation.
