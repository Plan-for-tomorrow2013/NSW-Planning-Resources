# 7.21 Visually sensitive land in Sancrox

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to protect visually and environmentally significant land in Sancrox.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land shown as "Visually Sensitive Land" on the *Visually Sensitive Land Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied the development—
- (a) will minimise visual impact, and
- (b) will be designed, set back and sited to complement the surrounding visual setting.
