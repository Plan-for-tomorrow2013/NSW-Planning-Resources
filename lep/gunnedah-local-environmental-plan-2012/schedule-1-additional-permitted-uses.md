# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at 107-109 Chandos Street, Gunnedah

This clause applies to land at Lots 15-17 and 19, Section 21, DP 758492, 107-109 Chandos Street, Gunnedah, shown as "Item 1" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

Development for the purposes of caravan parks is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at Conadilly Street, Gunnedah

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land identified as "Item 2" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*—
- (a) Lot A, DP 405072, 415-417 Conadilly Street,
- (b) Lot A, DP 360493 and Lot B, DP 372051, 419 Conadilly Street.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of hotel or motel accommodation is permitted with development consent.

## 3 Use of certain land at 127-141 Lochrey Road, Gunnedah

**(1)**
This clause applies to part of Lot 1, DP 841781, 127-141 Lochrey Road, Gunnedah, identified as "Item 3" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of highway service centres is permitted with development consent.
