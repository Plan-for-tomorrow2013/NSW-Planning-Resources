# 13A Size of display or sales area in shops on land within Zone No 3 (a)

**(1)**
The maximum area that may be used as a display or sales area in a shop that is on land within Zone No 3 (a) is 400 square metres.

**(2)**
In calculating the area used as a display or sales area, any part of the shop that is ancillary to the display or sales area or that is used for storage, office or staff convenience purposes is to be excluded.
