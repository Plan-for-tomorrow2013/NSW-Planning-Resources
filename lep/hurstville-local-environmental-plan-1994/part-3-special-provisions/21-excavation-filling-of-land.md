# 22 Excavation, filling of land

**(1)**
A person may excavate or fill any land to which this plan applies only with the consent of the council.

**(2)**
When considering an application for consent required by subclause (1), the council shall have particular regard to—
- (a) the likely disruption of, or detrimental effect on, existing drainage patterns and soil stability in the locality, and
- (b) the effect of the proposed works on the likely future use or redevelopment of the land.

**(3)**
Subclause (1) does not apply to any excavation or filling of land associated with minor landscaping or similar works.
