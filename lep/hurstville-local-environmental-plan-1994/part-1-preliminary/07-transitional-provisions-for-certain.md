# 6A Transitional provisions for certain development applications

**(1)**
The amendments to this plan made by *Hurstville Local Environmental Plan 1994 (Amendment No 43)* do not apply to development the subject of a development application that had been made, but had not been finally determined, before the commencement of those amendments.

**(2)**
The amendments to this plan made by *Hurstville Local Environmental Plan 1994 (Amendment No 70)* do not apply to development the subject of a development application that had been made, but had not been finally determined, before the commencement of those amendments.
