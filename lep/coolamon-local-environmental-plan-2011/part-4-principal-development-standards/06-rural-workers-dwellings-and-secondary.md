# 4.2C Rural workers' dwellings and secondary dwellings

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to ensure the provision of adequate accommodation for employees of existing agricultural or rural industries, and
- (b) to ensure that the erection of a rural worker's dwelling or secondary dwelling does not—
  - (i) have an adverse impact on the agricultural use of land, or
  - (ii) create an additional vehicular access to a public road.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
- (b) in relation to secondary dwellings—Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a rural worker's dwelling or a secondary dwelling on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the development will not impair the use of the land for agricultural or rural industries, and
- (b) the development will not require any additional vehicular access to a public road.
