# 50 Development in the vicinity of Elizabeth Bay House

**(1)**
This clause applies to development at 26, 28, 30A, 30B or 32 Billyard Avenue, Elizabeth Bay.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the following matters—
- (a) the impact of the proposed development on the historic and visual relationship between Port Jackson, the foreshore and Elizabeth Bay House,
- (b) the views to and from Elizabeth Bay House and the McElhone Reserve,
- (c) the impact of the bulk and height of, and the materials to be used in, the proposed development.
