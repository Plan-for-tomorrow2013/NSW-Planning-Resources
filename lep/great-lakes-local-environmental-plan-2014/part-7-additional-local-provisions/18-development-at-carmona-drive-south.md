# 7.19 Development at Carmona Drive, South Forster

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Development Area" on the *Carmona Drive Development Map*.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.1, the consent authority may grant development consent to the subdivision of land to which this clause applies to create lots of any size if—
- (a) the subdivision will be carried out under the *Community Land Development Act 1989*, and
- (b) the total number of lots proposed to be created for the purpose of a dwelling house will not exceed 27, and
- (c) the consent authority is satisfied that the lots referred to in paragraph (b) will be compatible with the existing subdivision pattern of the surrounding area.
