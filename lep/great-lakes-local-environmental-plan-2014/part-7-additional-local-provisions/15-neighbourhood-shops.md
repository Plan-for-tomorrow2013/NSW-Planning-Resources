# 7.16 Neighbourhood shops

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to permit development consent for the purpose of neighbourhood shops if certain conditions are met.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development for the purpose of one neighbourhood shop on any lot.

> Note: Clause 5.4 (7) specifies that a neighbourhood shop must not exceed 120 square metres of floor area.
