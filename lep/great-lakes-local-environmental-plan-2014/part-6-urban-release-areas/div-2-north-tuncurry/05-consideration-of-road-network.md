# 6.9 Consideration of road network

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure development will not have adverse impacts on the State road network.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone R1 General Residential,
- (b) Zone R3 Medium Density Residential,
- (c) Zone E1 Local Centre,
- (d) Zone E3 Productivity Support,
- (e) Zone E4 General Industrial.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the potential effects of the development on existing and proposed future road and public transport infrastructure in the locality.
