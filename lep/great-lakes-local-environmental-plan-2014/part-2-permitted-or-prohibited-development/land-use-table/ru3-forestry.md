# Zone RU3 Forestry

## 1 Objectives of zone

- To enable development for forestry purposes.
- To enable other development that is compatible with forestry land uses.

## 2 Permitted without consent

Home occupations; Uses authorised under the *Forestry Act 2012* or under Part 5B (Private native forestry) of the *Local Land Services Act 2013*

## 3 Permitted with consent

Aquaculture; Bed and breakfast accommodation; Camping grounds; Caravan parks; Eco-tourist facilities; Extractive industries; Farm stay accommodation; Flood mitigation works; Roads; Water reticulation systems; Wharf or boating facilities

## 4 Prohibited

Any development not specified in item 2 or 3
