# Dictionary — D

### depot

*depot* means a building or place used mainly for the storage of any plant, machinery, motor vehicles or stock of materials or spare parts used in the course of any one business or industrial undertaking or public utility undertaking, but does not include any part of the building or place used for sale by retail, wholesale or otherwise.

### dual occupancy

*dual occupancy* means 2 dwellings, whether attached or detached, on a single allotment.

### dwelling

*dwelling* means a room or suite of rooms occupied, or used (or so constructed or adapted as to be capable of being occupied or used), as a separate domicile, but does not include—
- (a) accommodation for seniors or people with a disability, or
- (b) tourist accommodation.
