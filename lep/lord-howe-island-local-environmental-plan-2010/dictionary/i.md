# Dictionary — I

### intensive agriculture

*intensive agriculture* means any form of agriculture involving the intensive housing, feeding (generally of a product that is not produced on the land on which the intensive agriculture is carried out) or slaughtering of animals and includes an abattoir, a cattle feedlot, an intensive piggery, a poultry farm or an intensive aquaculture farm.

### Island

*Island*—see clause 3.
