# 37A Additional permitted uses

**(1)**
Development on particular land described or referred to in Schedule 4 may, in accordance with any conditions specified in the Schedule in relation to the development, be carried out—
- (a) with development consent, or
- (b) if the Schedule provides—without development consent.

**(1A)**
This clause prevails over clauses 34 and 35.

**(2)**
This clause has effect despite anything to the contrary in Part 2 or the other provisions of this Part.
