# 29 Maximum height of buildings

**(1)**
Despite anything to the contrary in Part 2 or any other provisions of this Part, the erection of a building that has a height of more than 7.5 metres above natural ground level is prohibited.

**(2)**
Subclause (1) does not apply to the erection of—
- (a) a building for the purposes of public utility undertakings, and
- (b) aerials, masts or communication structures.
