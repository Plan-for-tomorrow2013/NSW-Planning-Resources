# 26 Limit on number of dwellings to which consent may be given

**(1)**
In any period, consent may be granted for the erection of no more than the total number of dwellings determined by the Board and approved by the Director-General in respect of that period for the purposes of this clause.

**(2)**
Regardless of the total number of dwellings determined for the purposes of subclause (1), consent may be granted for the erection of no more than a total of 25 dwellings during a period of 20 years commencing on 28 October 2005.

**(3)**
This clause does not apply in relation to the rebuilding of a lawfully erected dwelling.

**(4)**
A reference in subclauses (1) and (2) to the granting of consent to the erection of a dwelling includes a reference to the granting of consent to a new use referred to in clause 25.
