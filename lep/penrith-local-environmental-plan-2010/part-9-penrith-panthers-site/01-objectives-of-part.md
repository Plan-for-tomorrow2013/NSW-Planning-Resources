# 9.1 Objectives of Part

The objectives of this Part are as follows—
- (a) to ensure that development of land at Penrith Panthers occurs in a logical and cost-effective manner, in accordance with a staging plan and only after a development control plan that includes specific controls has been prepared for the land,
- (b) to limit the density of retail premises on the land,
- (c) (Repealed)
- (d) to manage the gross floor area of office premises on the land.
