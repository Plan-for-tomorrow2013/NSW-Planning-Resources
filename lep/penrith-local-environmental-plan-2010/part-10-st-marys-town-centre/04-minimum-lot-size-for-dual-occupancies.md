# 10.4 Minimum lot size for dual occupancies and residential flat buildings

Despite clause 4.1A, development consent must not be granted to development on a lot for the following purposes unless the lot is at least—
- (a) for dual occupancies (attached) on land identified as "Area 13" on the *Lot Size Map*—360m2,
- (b) for residential flat buildings on land in Zone R4 High Density Residential—1,100m2.
