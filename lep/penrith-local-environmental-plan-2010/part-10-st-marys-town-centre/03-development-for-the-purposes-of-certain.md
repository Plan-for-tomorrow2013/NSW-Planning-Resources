# 10.3 Development for the purposes of certain shops

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to incentivise retail development in the form of shops,
- (b) to encourage the early delivery of large floorplate retail premises to provide a key anchor use that activates the commercial core,
- (c) to enable a range of retail shops and services of various sizes,
- (d) to promote broader commercial development and the economic strength of St Marys Town Centre.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 1" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(3)**
Clause 10.2 does not apply to development for the purposes of a single shop with a gross floor area of at least 2,000m2.

**(4)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 31 December 2030.
