# 10.5 Development on land in Zone E3

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to promote development for the purposes of light industries on land in Zone E3 Productivity Support,
- (b) to provide limited residential development associated with development for the purposes of light industries,
- (c) to assist in revitalisation of employment land,
- (d) to encourage small scale live-work enterprises to support creative industries,
- (e) to provide a transition between adjoining land use zones.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Zone E3 Productivity Support identified as "49" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of dwellings unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the dwellings will be part of mixed use development that includes development for the purposes of light industries, and
- (b) no part of the ground floor of the building that fronts a street will be used for residential purposes, excluding access, car parking and waste storage, and
- (c) not less than 70% of the total gross floor area of the development will be used for the purposes of light industries, and
- (d) for development for the purposes of dwellings—the dwellings will be on the same lot of land as development for the purposes of light industries, including a lot in a strata plan or community title scheme.
