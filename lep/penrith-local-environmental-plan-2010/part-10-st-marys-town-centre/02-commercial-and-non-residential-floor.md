# 10.2 Commercial and non-residential floor space

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to protect and enhance the retail and commercial core of St Marys Town Centre,
- (b) to reinforce the status of St Marys Town Centre as a strategic centre and as a place of employment,
- (c) to promote active street frontages in the commercial core,
- (d) to enable a flexible approach to the delivery of commercial and non-residential floor space in areas close to the transport interchange,
- (e) to ensure the delivery and long-term protection of employment generating floor space on certain land in St Marys Town Centre.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 1" and "Area 2" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a building, or change of use of a building, on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) a minimum amount of gross floor area equivalent to a floor space ratio of 1.4:1 will be used for non-residential purposes after the erection or change of use, or
- (b) the ground floor and first floor of the building will be used for non-residential purposes after the erection or change of use.

**(4)**
The floor space ratio of buildings on land identified as "Area 1" on the *Clause Application Map* may be greater than the maximum floor space ratio for the land if the consent authority is satisfied that the additional floor space will be used for non-residential development.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*non-residential development* means development for purposes other than residential accommodation or car parks.
