# 8.2 Sun access

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to protect public open space from overshadowing.

**(2)** (Repealed)

**(3)**
Despite clauses 4.3, 5.6 and 8.4, development consent may not be granted to development on land to which this Part applies if the development would result in overshadowing of public open space to a greater degree than would result from adherence to the controls indicated for the land on the *Height of Buildings Map*.

**(4)**
This clause does not prohibit development that does not alter the exterior of any existing building.
