# 7.31 Affordable housing contributions

**(1)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on land identified as "Affordable Rental Housing Contribution Area" on the *Affordable Rental Housing Contribution Scheme Map*.

**(2)**
When granting development consent to development to which this clause applies, the consent authority may impose a condition requiring an affordable housing contribution (a *contribution*).

**(3)**
A condition imposed under this clause must require a person to satisfy the contribution by a monetary contribution paid to the Council.

**(4)**
The contribution must be calculated in accordance with the *Orchard Hills North, Glenmore Park (Stage 3) and St Marys Town Centre Affordable Rental Housing Contributions Scheme* adopted by the Council on 21 July 2025.

**(5)**
To avoid doubt, the demolition of a building, or a change in the use of land, does not give rise to a claim for a refund of a contribution.

**(6)**
In this clause—
*Affordable Rental Housing Contribution Scheme Map* means the *Penrith Local Environmental Plan 2010 Affordable Rental Housing Contribution Scheme Map*.
