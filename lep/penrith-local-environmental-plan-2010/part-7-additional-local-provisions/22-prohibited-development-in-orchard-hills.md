# 7.20C Prohibited development in Orchard Hills North

Development for the following purposes is prohibited on land in Zone C2 Environmental Conservation that is identified as "Orchard Hills North" on the *Clause Application Map*—
- (a) environmental facilities,
- (b) flood mitigation works,
- (c) oyster aquaculture,
- (d) recreation areas.
