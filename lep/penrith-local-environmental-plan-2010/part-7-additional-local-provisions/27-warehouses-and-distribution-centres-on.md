# 7.25 Warehouses and distribution centres on land zoned SP4 Enterprise

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to promote business activity within Zone SP4 Enterprise that provides for a range of higher order job opportunities in health, cultural and high technology industries.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as SP4 Enterprise within the Penrith Health and Education Precinct on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies for the purpose of a warehouse or distribution centre unless the warehouse or distribution centre—
- (a) is ancillary to a health, cultural or technology industry, and
- (b) occupies no more than 50% of the gross floor area of the development.
