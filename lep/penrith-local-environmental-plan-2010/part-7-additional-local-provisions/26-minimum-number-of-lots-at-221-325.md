# 7.24 Minimum number of lots at 221-325 Luddenham Road, Orchard Hills

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 221-325 Luddenham Road, Orchard Hills, identified as "Area 5" and "Area 6" on the *Lot Size Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for the subdivision of the land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) at least 40 lots will be created in "Area 5" on the *Lot Size Map*, and
- (b) at least 60 lots will be created in "Area 6" on the *Lot Size Map*.
