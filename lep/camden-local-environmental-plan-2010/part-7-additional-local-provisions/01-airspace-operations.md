# 7.2 Airspace operations

**(1)**
This clause applies in relation to a development application if—
- (a) the proposed development is on land shown on the *Obstacle Limitation Surface Map* for which an obstacle limitation surface is identified and the consent authority is satisfied the proposed development will penetrate the obstacle limitation surface, or
- (b) the proposed development is on land shown on the *Procedures for Air Navigation Services—Aircraft Operations Map* for which a PANS-OPS surface is identified and the consent authority is satisfied the proposed development will penetrate the PANS-OPS surface.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will not—
- (a) compromise the operation of the airport, or
- (b) cause undue risk to the community.

**(3)-(6)** (Repealed)

**(7)**
In this clause—
*Obstacle Limitation Surface Map* means the *Obstacle Limitation Surface Map* for the Camden Airport prepared by the operators of Camden Airport and approved by the relevant Commonwealth body or relevant Commonwealth Minister.
*Procedures for Air Navigation Services—Aircraft Operations Map* means the *Procedures for Air Navigation Services—Aircraft Operations Map* for the Camden Airport prepared by the operators of Camden Airport and approved by the relevant Commonwealth body or relevant Commonwealth Minister.
*relevant Commonwealth body* means the body responsible for development decisions relating to the Camden Airport under Commonwealth legislation.
*relevant Commonwealth Minister* means the Minister responsible for development decisions relating to the Camden Airport under Commonwealth legislation.
