# 7.12 Use of certain land in Little Street Industrial Precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area B" on *Land Zoning Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of industries on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the impacts of the development on adjacent residential areas, including in relation to noise, air quality and hours of operation.
