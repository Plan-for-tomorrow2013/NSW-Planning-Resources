# 7.32 Development on certain land in Zone C4

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 8" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of dual occupancies (attached) or dwelling houses if the site coverage would exceed 30% of the site area.
