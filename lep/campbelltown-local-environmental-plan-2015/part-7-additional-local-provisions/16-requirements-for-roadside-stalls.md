# 7.16 Requirements for roadside stalls

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that the operation of roadside stalls does not create vehicle or pedestrian hazards.

**(2)**
Each roadside stall must be located on private property and have safe access and adequate on-site parking and provision for vehicular manoeuvring.
