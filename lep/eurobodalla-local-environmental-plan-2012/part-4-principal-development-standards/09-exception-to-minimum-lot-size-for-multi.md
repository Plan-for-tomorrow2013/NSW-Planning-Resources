# 4.1G Exception to minimum lot size for multi dwelling housing and dual occupancies on certain land in Zone R2

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone R2 Low Density Residential for which the minimum lot size shown for the land on the *Lot Size Map* is 550m2.

**(2)**
The size of a lot resulting from a subdivision of land to which this clause applies may be less than 550m2 if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) development for the purposes of dual occupancies has been lawfully carried out on the land, and
- (b) there will be only one dwelling on each resulting lot, and
- (c) the area of each resulting lot will be at least 300m2.
