# 34 Zone No 1 (f) (Forestry Zone)

The following table provides general zoning controls for Zone No 1 (f)—
Table Objective of zone The objective is to allow forestry activities carried out by or on behalf of the Forestry Commission. Without development consent Development for the purpose of— agriculture any building, work, place or land use authorised under the *Forestry Act 1916* (including any ordinarily ancillary or incidental development) bush fire hazard reduction home occupations Exempt development Only with development consent Development for the purpose of— extractive industries mines utility installations Prohibited Development not included in item 34.2, 34.3 or 34.4.
