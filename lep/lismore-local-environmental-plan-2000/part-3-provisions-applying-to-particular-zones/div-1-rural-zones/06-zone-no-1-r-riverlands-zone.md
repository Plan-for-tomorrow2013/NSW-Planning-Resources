# 35 Zone No 1 (r) (Riverlands Zone)

The following table provides general zoning controls for Zone No 1 (r)—
Table Objectives of zone The objectives are— to encourage the use of the land for its optimum productive potential, and to permit a range of activities that support the agricultural industries being conducted on the land and limit development that may, in the opinion of the Council, reduce the agricultural production potential of the land, and to discourage the fragmentation of rural land, and to control development that may restrict the function of, or create a traffic hazard along, classified and other formed roads, and to limit the development of non-agricultural uses, except those which will not be adversely affected by flooding. Without development consent Development for the purpose of— agriculture (other than animal establishments) bush fire hazard reduction forestry home occupations Exempt development Only with development consent Development not included in item 35.2, 35.4 or 35.5. Prohibited Development for the purpose of— amusement parlours boarding houses brothels bulky goods showrooms bulk stores caravan parks car repair stations cemeteries and crematoriums clubs commercial premises drive-in theatres generating works goods transport terminals hospitals institutions licensed premises liquor outlets (other than those physically attached to and operated or to be operated as part of a convenience shop) markets materials recycling yards medical centres motels motor showrooms offensive or hazardous industries passenger transport terminals recreation facilities recreation vehicle areas residential flat buildings restricted premises shops storage sheds tourist facilities (except boatsheds and boat landing facilities, marinas, playgrounds, water sport facilities and houseboats) warehouses
