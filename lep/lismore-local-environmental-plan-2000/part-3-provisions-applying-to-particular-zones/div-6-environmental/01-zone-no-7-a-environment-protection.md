# 77 Zone No 7 (a) (Environment Protection (Natural Vegetation and Wetlands) Zone)

The following table provides general zoning controls for Zone No 7 (a)—
Table Objectives of zone The objectives are— to protect and preserve significant wetlands, and to retain significant areas of unique natural vegetation, such as rainforest remnants, and to prohibit development which could destroy or damage a wetlands ecosystem or rainforest. Without development consent Development for the purpose of— bush fire hazard reduction Exempt development Only with development consent Development for the purpose of— agriculture earthworks environmental facilities roads utility installations (other than gas holders or generating works) Prohibited Development not included in item 77.2 or 77.3.
