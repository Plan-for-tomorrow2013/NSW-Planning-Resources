# 66 Zone No 5 (b) (Special Uses (Technology Park) Zone)

The following table provides general zoning controls for Zone No 5 (b)—
Objectives of zone The objectives are— to provide a high quality workplace that is related to the discovery, research and development of natural products, and to encourage development of services and facilities that will transform research into commercial undertakings, and to provide facilities and services that will foster research and responsive technology, and to encourage facilities that will not adversely affect the natural environment, and to provide for other community facilities that will complement the technology park. Without development consent Development for the purpose of— agriculture bush fire hazard reduction environmental facilities home occupations works for the purpose of landscaping Exempt development Only with development consent Development not included in item 66.2, 66.4 or 66.5. Prohibited Development for the purpose of— abattoirs airfields airline terminals animal establishments brothels car repair stations helipads heliports junk yards liquid fuel depots mines motor showrooms restricted premises sawmills service stations stock and saleyards
