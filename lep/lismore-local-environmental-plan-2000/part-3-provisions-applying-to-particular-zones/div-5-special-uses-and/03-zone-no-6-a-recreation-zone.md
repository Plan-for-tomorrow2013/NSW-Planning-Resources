# 67 Zone No 6 (a) (Recreation Zone)

The following table provides general zoning controls for Zone No 6 (a)—
Table Objectives of zone The objectives are— to ensure access by the general public to adequate open space to meet the needs of all residents and provide opportunities to enhance the quality of the total environment of the City of Lismore, and to allocate land which will adequately provide both active and passive open space to service the present and future recreational needs of residents and visitors, and to manage flora and fauna on public open space. Without development consent Development for the purpose of— environmental facilities works for the purposes of gardening, landscaping or bush fire hazard reduction Exempt development Only with development consent Development for the purpose of— agriculture child care establishments community facilities recreation areas temporary events utility installations Prohibited Development not included in items 67.2, 67.3 or 67.4.
