# 72 Use of land pending acquisition

**(1)**
Land to which clause 70 applies, other than land zoned Special Uses (Main Road), may be developed for any purpose, with development consent granted, prior to its acquisition by the body concerned.

**(2)**
A person may, with development consent, carry out development on land zoned Special Uses (Main Road)—
- (a) for a purpose for which development may be carried out on land in an adjoining zone, or
- (b) for any other purpose that is compatible with development which may be carried out in an adjoining zone.

**(3)**
In deciding whether to grant consent to proposed development under this clause, the Council must take the following into consideration—
- (a) the need to carry out development on the land for the purpose for which the land is reserved,
- (b) the imminence of acquisition,
- (c) the likely additional cost resulting from the carrying out of the proposed development.
