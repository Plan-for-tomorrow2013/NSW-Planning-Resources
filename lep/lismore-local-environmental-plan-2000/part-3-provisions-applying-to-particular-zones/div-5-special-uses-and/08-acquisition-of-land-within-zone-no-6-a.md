# 71 Acquisition of land within Zone No 6 (a)—effect of rezoning

**(1)**
The Council need not take any action under clause 70 with respect to acquiring land within Zone No 6 (a) for 90 days (or for such other period as may be agreed between the owner and the Council before that period expires) after receiving a notice under that clause, if the Council, within 14 days after receiving the notice, notifies the owner that it is reviewing the zoning of the land.

**(2)**
The Council need not acquire the land pursuant to the notice if the land is rezoned, or the Council decides or has decided to prepare a local environmental plan to rezone the land, before the period of 90 days (or the agreed period) expires.
