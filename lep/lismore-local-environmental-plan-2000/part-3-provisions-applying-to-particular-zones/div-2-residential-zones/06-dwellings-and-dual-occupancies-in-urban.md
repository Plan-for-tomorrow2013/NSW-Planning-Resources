# 53 Dwellings and dual occupancies in urban areas

**(1)**
The clause applies to land within Zone No 2 (a) or 2 (v).

**(2)**
Consent may be granted for the erection of a dwelling-house on stable land only and not on land subject to erosion or landslip, unless the dwelling is suitably designed and the consent authority has considered a geotechnical report relating to the land, being a report considered adequate by the consent authority.

**(3)**
Development may be carried out on land to which this clause applies that results in 2 dwellings (whether or not attached) on the same allotment.
