# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at 72 Parker Street, Cootamundra

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 72 Parker Street, Cootamundra, being Lot 1, DP 663134.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of light industry, being a printing works, is permitted with development consent.
