# Schedule 3, Part 1 — Types of development

*(Clause 3.2)*

| Site area | Minimum landscaped area |
|---|---|
| Less than 850m2 | Site area × 0.5 |
| 850m2 or greater | Site area × [0.5 + (site area - 850)/6,500] |
