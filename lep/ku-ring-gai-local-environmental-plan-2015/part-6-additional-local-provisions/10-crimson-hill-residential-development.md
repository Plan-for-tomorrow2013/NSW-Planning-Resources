# 6.10 Crimson Hill Residential Development, Lindfield

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Crimson Hill" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purposes of retail premises on land to which this clause applies if the development would result in retail premises having a gross floor area of more than 80 square metres.

**(3)**
Development consent must not granted for the erection of a dwelling on land to which this clause applies if the number of dwellings on all of that land would exceed 345.
