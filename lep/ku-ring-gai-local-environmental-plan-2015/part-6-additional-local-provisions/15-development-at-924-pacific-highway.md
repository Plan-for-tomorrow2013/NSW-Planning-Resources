# 6.16 Development at 924 Pacific Highway, Gordon

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "non-residential floor space" on the *Non-Residential Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the development includes a minimum amount of gross floor area used for the purposes of non-residential development that is calculated using a floor space ratio of 1:1.

**(3)** (Repealed)
