# 6.15 Development in Gordon Town Centre

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 3" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to 109m.

**(3)**
The floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies may be up to 7.5:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will be at least 7,700m2, and
- (b) the buildings resulting from the development will have a minimum amount of gross floor area used for the purposes of non-residential development that is calculated using a floor space ratio of 1:1, and
- (c) the development will include—
  - (i) at least 3,000m2 of floor area used for the purposes of community facilities or public administration buildings, or
  - (ii) floor area, equal to at least 2% of the gross floor area used for the purposes of residential accommodation, used for the purposes of affordable housing.

**(5)** (Repealed)
