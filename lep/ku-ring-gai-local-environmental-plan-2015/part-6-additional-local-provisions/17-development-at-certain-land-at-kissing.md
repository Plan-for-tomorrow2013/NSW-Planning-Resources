# 6.18 Development at certain land at Kissing Point Road and Pacific Highway, Turramurra

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 7" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied the development includes a minimum amount of gross floor area used for the purposes of non-residential development that is calculated using a floor space ratio of 0.85:1.
