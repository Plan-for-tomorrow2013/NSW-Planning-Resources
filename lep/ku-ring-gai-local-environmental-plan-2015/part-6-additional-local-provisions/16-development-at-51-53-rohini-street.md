# 6.17 Development at 51-53 Rohini Street, Turramurra

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at 51-53 Rohini Street, Turramurra—
- (a) Lot 2, DP 302942,
- (b) Lot 21, DP 533032,
- (c) Lot 26, DP 585038.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of commercial premises or recreation facilities (indoor) unless the consent authority is satisfied the total gross floor area used for the purposes of commercial premises and recreation facilities (indoor) does not exceed 700m2.
