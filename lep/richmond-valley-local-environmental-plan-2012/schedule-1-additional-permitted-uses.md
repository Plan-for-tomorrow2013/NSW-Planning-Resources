# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Reynolds Road, Casino

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 2, DP 547143, Reynolds Road, Casino, identified as "Item 1" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a rail freight transport facility is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at 340 Woodburn-Coraki Road, Swan Bay

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 115365, 340 Woodburn-Coraki Road (corner Old Ferry Road), Swan Bay, known as "York Marine" and identified as "Item 2" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of boat building and repair facilities is permitted with development consent.

## 3 Use of certain land at 122 Centre Street, Casino

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 3, DP 337280, 122 Centre Street, Casino, identified as "Item 3" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a restaurant or cafe is permitted with development consent.

## 4 Use of certain land at Casino

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Item 4" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of intensive plant agriculture is permitted with development consent if the development comprises the cultivation of irrigated crops for commercial purposes, other than irrigated pasture or fodder crops.
