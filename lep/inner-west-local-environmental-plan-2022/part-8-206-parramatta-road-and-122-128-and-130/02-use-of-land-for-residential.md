# 8.2 Use of land for residential accommodation

**(1)**
Despite clause 4.3, a building on the land may have a height of up to 80m.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.4, the floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land may be up to 7.97:1.

**(3)**
Subclauses (1) and (2) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 10% of the gross floor area of all buildings on the site used for the purposes of residential accommodation will be used as affordable housing forat least 15 years from the date of the issue of the occupation certificate.

**(4)**
Clause 6.15 does not apply to development on land to which this part applies.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 18 September 2026.
