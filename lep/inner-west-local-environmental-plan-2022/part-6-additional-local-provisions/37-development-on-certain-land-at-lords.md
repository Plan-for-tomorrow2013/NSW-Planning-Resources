# 6.39 Development on certain land at Lords Road, Leichhardt

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land in Leichhardt—
- (a) Lot 1, DP 940543, 67-73 Lords Road,
- (b) Lot 1, DP 550608, 75 Lords Road.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will include a publicly accessible open space of at least 1,500m2.
