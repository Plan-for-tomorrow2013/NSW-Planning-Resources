# 6.35 Development of certain land at Parramatta Road and Pyrmont Bridge Road

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "N" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) at least 1,000m2 of the land will be publicly accessible open space, and
- (b) at least 50% of the publicly accessible open space will receive at least 2 hours of direct sunlight between 9am and 3pm on 21 June each year.
