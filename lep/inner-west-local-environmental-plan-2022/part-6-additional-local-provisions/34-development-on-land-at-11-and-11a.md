# 6.36 Development on land at 11 and 11A Edinburgh Road, Marrickville

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on the following land in Marrickville, identified as "Area 21" on the *Key Sites Map*—
- (a) Lot 67, DP 4991, 11 Edinburgh Road,
- (b) Lot 1, DP 607677, 11A Edinburgh Road.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may exceed the maximum floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* by up to 2.25:1 if the consent authority is satisfied the building will be used only for the purposes of self-storage units.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 7.5% of the site area of the development will provide for deep soil planting.
