# 6.37 Development on land at 67 Mary Street, St Peters

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 100, DP 1283113, 67 Mary Street, St Peters.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, a building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to 38m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies may be up to 2.42:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) at least 747m2 of the gross floor area of all buildings on the site will be used for the purposes of affordable housing for at least 15 years from the date of the issue of the occupation certificate, and
- (b) no more than 28,000m2 of the gross floor area of all buildings on the site will be used for the purposes of residential accommodation.

**(5)**
Clause 6.27 does not apply to development on land to which this clause applies.

**(6)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 30 June 2026.
