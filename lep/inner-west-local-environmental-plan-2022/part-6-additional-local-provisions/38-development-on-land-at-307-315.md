# 6.40 Development on land at 307-315 Parramatta Road, Leichhardt

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at 307-315 Parramatta Road, Leichhardt—
- (a) Lots A and B, DP 158098,
- (b) Lot 2, DP 303623,
- (c) Lot 1, DP 1243481.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, a building on the land may have a height of up to 50m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land may be up to 5:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 4% of the gross floor area of all buildings on the site used for the purposes of residential accommodation will be used as affordable housing for at least 15 years from the date of the issue of the occupation certificate.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 20 October 2026.
