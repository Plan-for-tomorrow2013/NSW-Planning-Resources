# 6.38 Affordable housing contributions for certain development at 67-73 Lords Road, Leichhardt

**(1)**
In this clause, the *Inner West Affordable Housing Principles* are as follows—
- (a) affordable housing must be provided and managed to accommodate a diverse residential population within areas representative of all income groups in the Inner West local government area,
- (b) affordable housing must be rented to tenants at rents that do not exceed 30% of actual household income,
- (c) dwellings provided for affordable housing must be managed to maintain their continued use as affordable housing,
- (d) the Council must use the following, received by or on behalf of the Council, to improve or replace, or provide additional, affordable housing in the Inner West local government area—
  - (i) rent from affordable housing, excluding landlords' expenses such as management and maintenance costs and rates and taxes payable in connection with the dwellings,
  - (ii) money from the disposal of affordable housing,
- (e) affordable housing must consist of dwellings constructed to a standard that, in the consent authority's opinion, is consistent with the same type of dwellings forming part of the proposed development that are not intended to be used as affordable housing, particularly in relation to solar access and natural ventilation.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the following development on Lot 1, DP 940543, 67-73 Lords Road, Leichhardt—
- (a) development used for the following purposes—
  - (i) co-living housing,
  - (ii) multi dwelling housing,
  - (iii) residential flat buildings,
  - (iv) seniors housing,
  - (v) shop top housing,
- (b) development used for a purpose referred to in paragraph (a) and forming part of mixed used development that includes one or more of the following—
  - (i) industrial retail outlets,
  - (ii) light industries,
  - (iii) recreation facilities (indoor).

**(3)**
This clause does not apply to development for the purposes of residential accommodation that is—
- (a) public housing, or
- (b) affordable housing managed by a registered community housing provider in perpetuity.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the Inner West Affordable Housing Principles.

**(5)**
The consent authority may impose a condition requiring an affordable housing contribution of 5% of the total gross floor area of the development.

**(6)**
A contribution imposed under this clause must permit a person to satisfy the condition by—
- (a) providing the following—
  - (i) a dedication, in favour of the Council, of land comprising one or more dwellings, each having a gross floor area of at least 50m2,
  - (ii) a monetary contribution paid to the Council of equivalent value to any remainder of the gross floor area specified in subclause (5), or
- (b) a monetary contribution paid to the Council of equivalent value to the gross floor area specified in subclause (5).

**(7)**
The rate at which a monetary contribution is taken to be equivalent to floor area for the purposes of this clause must be calculated by reference to the market value of dwellings of a similar size to the dwellings in the proposed development.

**(8)**
In this clause—
*community housing* has the same meaning as in the *Community Housing Providers National Law (NSW)*.
*public housing* has the same meaning as in the *Housing Act 2001*.
