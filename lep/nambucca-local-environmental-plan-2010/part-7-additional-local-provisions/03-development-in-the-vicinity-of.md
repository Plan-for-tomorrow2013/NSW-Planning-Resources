# 7.5 Development in the vicinity of identified mineral resource areas

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to identify the location of significant resources of minerals, petroleum or extractive materials for the purposes of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Resources and Energy) 2021*, section 2.19.

**(2)**
The land shown as "Mineral and Extractive Resource" on the *Mineral and Extractive Resources Map* is the land, for the purposes of this environmental planning instrument, to which *State Environmental Planning Policy (Resources and Energy) 2021*, section 2.19 applies.
