# 7.10 Residential development and subdivision prohibited on lots resulting from road closures

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted for the erection of a dwelling house on a lot resulting from the closure of part or all of a road, irrespective of when the road closure occurred, unless the lot is at least the minimum lot size shown on the *Lot Size Map*.

**(2)**
This clause extends to a lot resulting from the closure of part or all of a road if the boundaries of the lot have been realigned as a result of a subdivision of an adjoining lot that did not result from the closure of part or all of a road.

**(3)**
This clause does not apply to a lot created by the consolidation of a lot resulting from the closure of part or all of a road with an adjoining lot that did not result from the closure of part or all of a road.
