# Schedule 3 Complying development

*(Clause 3.2)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies complying development and the complying development conditions for that development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional complying development not specified in that Policy.

## Part 1 Types of development

### Building identification signs and business identification signs

**(1)**
Does not include signs that may be erected as exempt development under Schedule 2.

**(2)**
Must not cover mechanical ventilation inlet or outlet vent.

**(3)**
Must not incorporate flashing or moving components.

**(4)**
If erected on a building, must not project beyond any part of the building on which it is erected.

**(5)**
If erected over a public road (which includes a public footpath), the lowest point of the sign must be at least 2.6m above ground level (existing).

**(6)**
Must not be externally illuminated.

**(7)**
If on a heritage item, heritage conservation area or archaeological site, must comply with the following—
- (a) no part may cover windows or architectural projections or other decorative features of the premises upon which it will be displayed,
- (b) must be able to be removed without significantly altering or damaging the fabric of the heritage item, heritage conservation area or archaeological site.

**(8)**
Maximum advertising areas as follows—
- (a) projecting wall sign—2m2,
- (b) above awning sign—3m2,
- (c) pole or pylon sign—6m2.

**(9)**
Maximum height of pole or pylon sign—6m above ground level (existing).

**(10)**
Must relate to the lawfully established use and occupation of the land.

**(11)**
Must not be more than 3 signs displayed at the premises or place.

### Industrial development

**(1)**
Must be external alterations.

**(2)**
Must only be carried out in Zone E4.

**(3)**
Roofing must be of pre-coloured materials.

**(4)**
Must be face brick, pre-coloured metal cladding, textured finish or painted masonry and 'tilt-slab' concrete, or a combination of these materials.

**(5)**
Must not be constructed within 1m of any easements or sewer mains.

**(6)**
The floor space ratio (including any mezzanine areas) must not be more than 0.5:1.

**(7)**
Ground level (existing) must not be cut or filled so as to alter by more than 2.0m.

**(8)**
The front setback must be at least 6m.

**(9)**
Awnings or eaves must be at least 1m from the side and rear setbacks required by the Building Code of Australia for the intended use of the building.

**(10)**
A minimum 2m wide landscape area must be provided along all street frontages.

**(11)**
Parking spaces must be provided on the site at the rate of 1 space per 70m2 of gross floor area.

**(12)**
Parking spaces and turning areas must comply with AS 2890.1:2004, *Off-street car parking*.

**(13)**
Internal driveways must provide access to the building or separate occupancy to accommodate a rigid truck that is at least 11m long, in compliance with AS 2890.1:2004, *Off-street car parking*, sufficient to allow such trucks to enter and leave the site in a forward direction.

**(14)**
All internal roadways must be constructed to an all-weather bitumen or concrete surface in accordance with Council's Aus-Spec #1 Design and Construction Specifications.

**(15)**
Storage areas and waste bins must be screened from the street frontage and provided behind the building line.

## Part 2 Complying development certificate conditions

> Note: Complying development must comply with the requirements of the Act, the regulations under the Act and this Plan.

### General conditions

Any development specified in Part 1 is subject to the same conditions set out in Schedule 6 to *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*.

### Industrial development on land below the flood planning level

**(1)**
A floor level survey plan, prepared by a registered surveyor, must be submitted to the principal certifying authority before the construction of wall frames of a building located on land below the flood planning level.

**(2)**
The plan must show that the floor levels are above the flood planning level.
