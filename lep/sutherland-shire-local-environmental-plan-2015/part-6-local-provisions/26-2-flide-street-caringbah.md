# 6.27 2 Flide Street, Caringbah

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lots 57, 58, 59, 60 and 80, DP 8147, 2 Flide Street, Caringbah.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on the land is 50m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land is 4:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied the development is for the purposes of residential flat buildings.
