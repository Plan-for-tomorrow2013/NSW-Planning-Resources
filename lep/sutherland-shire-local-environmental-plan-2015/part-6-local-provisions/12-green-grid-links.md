# 6.13A Green grid links

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to provide for a network of tree-lined pathways for pedestrians and cyclists that run between and through open spaces and along waterways.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as a green grid link on the *Green Grid Map*.

**(3)**
In deciding whether to grant development consent to development on land to which this clause applies, the consent authority must consider the extent to which the development—
- (a) retains and enhances tree canopy cover, and
- (b) facilitates and prioritises pathways for pedestrians and cyclists that run between and through open spaces and along waterways.
