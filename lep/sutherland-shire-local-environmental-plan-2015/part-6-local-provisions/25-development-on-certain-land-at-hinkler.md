# 6.26 Development on certain land at Hinkler Avenue and Taren Point Road, Caringbah

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land in Caringbah—
- (a) Lots 36-41, DP 15573, 6-16 Hinkler Avenue,
- (b) Lots A and B, DP 35462, 18-20 Hinkler Avenue,
- (c) Lots 10-15, DP 15573, 319-329 Taren Point Road,
- (d) Lots 1 and 2, SP 70334, 331 Taren Point Road,
- (e) Lot D, DP 35462, 333 Taren Point Road.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on land to which this clause applies is 60m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of a building on land to which this clause applies is 3.9:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied at least 15% of the gross floor area of all buildings will be used for affordable housing for at least 15 years.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 30 June 2026.
