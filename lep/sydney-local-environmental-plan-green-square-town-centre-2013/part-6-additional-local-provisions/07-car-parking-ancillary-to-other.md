# 6.8 Car parking ancillary to other development

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to identify the maximum number of car parking spaces that may be provided to service particular uses of land, and
- (b) to minimise the amount of vehicular traffic generated by a development.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development that includes car parking spaces in connection with a use of land if the total number of car parking spaces (including existing car parking spaces) provided on the site would be greater than the maximum set out in this clause.

**(3)**
If the total number of car parking spaces under this clause is not a whole number, the total is to be rounded down to the next whole number.

**(4)**
The maximum number of car parking spaces is as follows—
- (a) for multi dwelling housing, residential flat buildings and dual occupancies—
  - (i) 0.5 spaces for each studio dwelling, and
  - (ii) 0.5 spaces for each 1 bedroom dwelling, and
  - (iii) 0.8 spaces for each 2 bedroom dwelling, and
  - (iv) 1.2 spaces for each 3 or more bedroom dwelling,
- (b) for dwelling houses, attached dwellings and semi-detached dwellings—2 spaces for each dwelling,
- (c) for retail premises, if the building has not more than 1,000 square metres of gross floor area used for the purposes of retail premises—1 space for every 50 square metres of gross floor area used for those purposes,
- (d) for retail premises being a supermarket—the number of spaces determined by survey-based assessment,
- (e) for business premises or office premises—1 space for every 125 square metres of gross floor area used for that purpose,
- (f) for health consulting rooms or medical centres—2 spaces for each consulting room,
- (g) for places of public worship or entertainment facilities—the greater of—
  - (i) 1 space for every 30 square metres of gross floor area used for that purpose, or
  - (ii) 1 space for every 10 fixed seats in the place of public worship or entertainment facility,
- (h) for centre-based child care facilities—1 space, plus an additional space for every 100 square metres used for that purpose,
- (i) for information and education facilities—1 space for every 200 square metres of gross floor area used for that purpose,
- (j) for warehouse or distribution centres—1 space for every 300 square metres of gross floor area used for that purpose,
- (k) for industries—1 space for every 100 square metres of gross floor area used for that purpose,
- (l) for serviced apartments or hotel and motel accommodation—
  - (i) if less than 101 bedrooms are used for that purpose—1 space for every 4 bedrooms, and
  - (ii) if 101 or more bedrooms are used for that purpose—1 space for every 5 bedrooms.

**(5)**
For multi dwelling housing, residential flat buildings and dual occupancies, the following number of visitor car parking spaces, in addition to the maximum number of car parking spaces under subclause (4), may be provided—
- (a) if the building contains less than 31 dwellings—1 space for every 5 dwellings,
- (b) if the building contains more than 30 dwellings, but less than 71 dwellings—6 spaces plus an additional 1 space for every 8 dwellings in excess of 30 dwellings,
- (c) if the building contains more than 70 dwellings—11 spaces, plus an additional 1 space for every 15 dwellings in excess of 70 dwellings.

**(6)**
In this clause—
*car parking space* means a space intended to be used for the parking of motor vehicles that is ancillary to another land use on the site, but does not include any of the following—
- (a) a place primarily used for the purpose of washing vehicles,
- (b) a place primarily used for the purpose of loading or unloading goods,
- (c) a place primarily used for the purpose of storing bicycles.
*visitor car parking space* means a car parking space intended to be used for the purposes of providing parking for visitors to the buildings to which those parking spaces relate and not for the purposes of providing parking for the residents of the buildings.
