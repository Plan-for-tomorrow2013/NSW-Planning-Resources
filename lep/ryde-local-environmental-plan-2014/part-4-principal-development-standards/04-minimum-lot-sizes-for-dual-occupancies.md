# 4.1B Minimum lot sizes for dual occupancies

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to achieve planned residential density in certain zones.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted for development on a lot in Zone R2 Low Density Residential for the purposes of dual occupancy if—
- (a) the area of the lot is equal to or greater than 580 square metres, and
- (b) the primary road frontage of the lot is equal to or greater than 15 metres.
