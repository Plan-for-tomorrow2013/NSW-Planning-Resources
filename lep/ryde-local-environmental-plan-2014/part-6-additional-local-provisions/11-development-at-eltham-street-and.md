# 6.15 Development at Eltham Street and Victoria Road, Gladesville

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on the following land in Gladesville—
- (a) Lot 1, DP 515535, 44 Eltham Street,
- (b) Lot 2, DP 734439, 46 Eltham Street,
- (c) Lot 8, DP 13865, 48 Eltham Street,
- (d) Lot 100, DP 1303371, 363 Victoria Road,
- (e) Lot 1, DP 734439, 365-369 Victoria Road,
- (f) Lots 1-3, DP 13865, 371 and 373 Victoria Road.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building resulting from the development is 47m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on the site of the development is 3.74:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply to the development unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) at least 6.8% of the total number of dwellings resulting from the development will be used for the purposes of affordable housing (the *affordable housing component*), and
- (b) all dwellings comprising the affordable housing component will be used for the purposes of affordable housing for at least 15 years from the date the occupation certificate is issued for the building containing the dwelling.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 25 October 2026.
