# 7.4 Effect on neighbouring sites

Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this part applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will not result in a neighbouring site becoming—
- (a) a site for which consolidation with an adjoining site is not—
  - (i) physically possible, or
  - (ii) reasonably feasible because of the nature of surrounding development, or
- (b) a site that has a reduced development potential because of its size, shape or location.
