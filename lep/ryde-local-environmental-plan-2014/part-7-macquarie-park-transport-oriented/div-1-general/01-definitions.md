# 7.1 Definitions

In this part—
*Macquarie Park Transport Oriented Development Precinct* means land to which this part applies.
*road infrastructure facilities* has the same meaning as in *State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021*, Part 2.3, Division 17, Subdivision 1.
