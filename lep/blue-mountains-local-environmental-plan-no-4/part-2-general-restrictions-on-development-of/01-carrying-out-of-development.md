# 9 Carrying out of development

Except as otherwise provided in this plan, the purposes—
- (a) for which development may be carried out without development consent,
- (b) for which development may be carried out—
  - (i) only with development consent, but where that consent cannot be refused, and
  - (ii) subject to such conditions as may be imposed under section 91 of the Act,
- (c) for which development may be carried out only with development consent, and
- (d) for which development is prohibited,
on land within each of the zones specified in Column 1 of the Table to this clause are respectively shown opposite thereto in Columns 2, 3, 4 and 5 of that Table.
