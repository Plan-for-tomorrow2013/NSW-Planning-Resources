# 67 Development of certain land at Blackheath

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 911334, Lot 1, DP 911760, DP 911652, Part Lot 1, DP 909231 and DP 942534, Corner Hat Hill Road and Wentworth Street, Blackheath, as shown edged heavy black on the map marked "*Blue Mountains Local Environmental Plan No 94*".

**(2)**
The council shall not grant consent to an application for the subdivision of the land to which this clause applies unless the two heritage buildings located on that land and identified in the council's heritage register deposited in the office of the council are retained within one allotment created by such subdivision.
