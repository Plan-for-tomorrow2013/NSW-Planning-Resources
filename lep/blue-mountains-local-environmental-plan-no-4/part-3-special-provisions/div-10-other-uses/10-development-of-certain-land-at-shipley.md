# 63 Development of certain land at Shipley Plateau

The Council shall not consent to the carrying out of development on that part of the land in Centennial Glen Road in DP 12669 or DP 29886 which is within Zone No 1 (c3) unless the area of the allotment on which the development is proposed to be carried out is not less than 1 000 square metres.
