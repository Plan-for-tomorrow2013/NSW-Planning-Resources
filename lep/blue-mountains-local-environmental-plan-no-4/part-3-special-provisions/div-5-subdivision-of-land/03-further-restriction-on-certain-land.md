# 34 Further restriction on certain land within Zone No 1 (a3), 1 (c1), 1 (c2), 1 (c3), 2 (a2) or 7 (e)

The council shall not consent to the subdivision of land within Zone No 1 (a3), 1 (c1), 1 (c2), 1 (c3), 2 (a2) or 7 (e).
