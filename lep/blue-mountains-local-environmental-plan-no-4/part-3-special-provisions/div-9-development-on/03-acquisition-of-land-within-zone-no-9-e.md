# 57A Acquisition of land within Zone No 9 (e) by corporation

**(1)**
The owner of any land within Zone No 9 (e) may, by notice in writing, require the corporation to acquire that land.

**(2)**
Upon receipt of a notice referred to in subclause (1), the corporation shall acquire the land to which the notice relates.
