# 57 Acquisition of land

**(1)**
The owner of any land within Zone No 6 (b), 9 (a), 9 (b), 9 (c) or 9 (d) upon which the erection of any building or the carrying out or alteration of any work of a permanent character or the making or alteration of any permanent excavation is prohibited, except for or incidental to a purpose for which the land is so zoned, and the owner of any land so zoned, in respect of which the council has refused its consent pursuant to clause 56 (3) may, by notice in writing, require the council to acquire that land.

**(2)**
Upon receipt of a notice referred to in subclause (1), the council shall acquire the land to which the notice relates.
