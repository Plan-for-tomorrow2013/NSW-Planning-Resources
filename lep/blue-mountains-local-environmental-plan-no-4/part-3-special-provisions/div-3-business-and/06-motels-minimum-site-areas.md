# 26 Motels—minimum site areas

A person shall not carry out development for the purpose of a motel on any land which has an area—
- (a) in the case of land within Zone No 1 (a1), 1 (a2), 1 (c1) or 1 (c2), of less than 4 hectares,
- (b) in the case of land within Zone No 2 (c1), 2 (c2) or 2 (d), of less than 1 400 square metres, or
- (c) in the case of land within Zone No 3 (a), of less than 1 000 square metres.
