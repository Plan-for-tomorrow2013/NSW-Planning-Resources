# 22 Commercial development in Zone No 2 (c1), 2 (c2) or 2 (d)

Notwithstanding the provisions of Part 2, the council shall not consent to the carrying out, on land within Zone No 2 (c1), 2 (c2) or 2 (d), of development for the purpose referred to in Schedule 1, if in the opinion of the council, the development will involve—
- (a) interference with the amenity of the locality by reason of the emission of noise, vibration, smell, fumes, smoke, vapour, steam, soot, ash, dust, waste water, waste products, grit, oil or otherwise,
- (b) the display of goods, whether in a window or otherwise,
- (c) the exhibition of any notice, advertisement or sign, other than a notice or sign not exceeding 0.8 square metre in size exhibited on a building to indicate the name and occupation of the occupier of that building, or
- (d) the employment of more than 3 persons.
