# 15 Residential flat buildings in Zone No 2 (b1) or 2 (b2)

A residential flat building Class 1 shall not be erected or used on land within Zone No 2 (b1) or 2 (b2) which has—
- (a) an area of less than 700 square metres,
- (b) a depth of less than 28 metres, and
- (c) a width of the front alignment of the building of less than 20 metres.
