# 16 Residential flat buildings in Zone No 2 (c1), 2 (c2) or 2 (d)

A residential flat building shall not be erected or used on land within Zone No 2 (c1), 2 (c2) or 2 (d) which has in the case of—
- (a) a single storey building—
  - (i) an area of less than 700 square metres,
  - (ii) a depth of less than 30.5 metres, and
  - (iii) a width at the front alignment of the building of less than 18.5 metres, or
- (b) a two-storey building—
  - (i) an area of less than 1 000 square metres,
  - (ii) a depth of less than 30.5 metres, and
  - (iii) a width at the front alignment of the building of less than 21.5 metres.
