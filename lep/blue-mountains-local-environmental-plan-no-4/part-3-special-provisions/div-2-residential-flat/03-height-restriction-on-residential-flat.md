# 17 Height restriction on residential flat buildings

A person shall not erect on land a residential flat building having a height of more than—
- (a) in the case of land within Zone No 2 (b1)—1 storey,
- (b) in the case of land within Zone No 2 (b2), 2 (c1), 2 (c2), or 2 (d)—2 storeys.
