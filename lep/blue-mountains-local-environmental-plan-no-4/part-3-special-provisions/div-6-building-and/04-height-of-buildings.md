# 39 Height of buildings

A person shall not erect a building on land within—
- (a) Zone No 2 (b1), if, at any point, that building contains more than 1 storey above ground level, or
- (b) a zone other than Zone No 2 (b1), if that building contains more than 2 storeys above ground level.
