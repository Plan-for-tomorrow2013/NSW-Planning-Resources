# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Stony Chute Road, Nimbin

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Stony Chute Road, Nimbin, being Lot 4, DP 1048778 and identified as "Item 1" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Subdivision of land to which this clause applies to create—
- (a) a maximum of 26 community development lots and a single neighbourhood lot under the *Community Land Development Act 1989*, and
- (b) a residue Torrens Title lot,
is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**
In this clause, *community development lot* and *neighbourhood lot* have the same meanings as they have in the *Community Land Development Act 1989*.

## 2 Use of certain land at High Street, Nimbin

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 13 and 13A High Street, Nimbin, being Lot 3, DP 708400 and Lot 1, DP 107523 and identified as "Item 2" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Subdivision of land to which this clause applies to create a maximum of 4 lots is permitted with development consent.

## 4 Use of certain land at Broadwater Road, Dungarubba

**(1)**
This clause applies to an approximately 2-hectare part of the south-eastern corner of the land at 354 Broadwater Road, Dungarubba, being part of Lot 2, DP 8877 and identified as "Item 4" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of industry (limited to the manufacture of concrete fence posts) is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**
The consent authority must not consent to development referred to in subclause (2) unless a condition is imposed that requires the development to cease no later than 5 years after the date on which development consent is first granted in respect of the development.

## 5 Use of certain land at Union Street, South Lismore

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 34 and 36 Union Street, South Lismore, being part of Lots 1 and 2, DP 608006 and identified as "Item 5" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the following purposes is permitted with development consent—
- (a) office premises,
- (b) information and education facilities (limited to art galleries),
- (c) entertainment facilities,
- (d) food and drink premises,
- (e) markets.

## 6 Use of certain land at 464 Broadwater Road, Dungarubba

**(2)**

## 7 Use of certain land at Invercauld Road, Goonellabah

## 8 Use of certain land at 1112 Bruxner Highway, McKees Hill
