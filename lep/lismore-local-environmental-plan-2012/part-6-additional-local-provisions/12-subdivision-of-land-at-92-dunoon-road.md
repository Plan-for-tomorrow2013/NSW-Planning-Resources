# 6.12 Subdivision of land at 92 Dunoon Road, North Lismore

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 11, DP 1096690, 92 Dunoon Road, North Lismore.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.1(3), the size of a lot resulting from subdivision of land to which this clause applies may be less than the minimum lot size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to the land if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the lot results from a subdivision of land into 2 lots, and
- (b) the subdivision will not significantly increase rural land use conflict in the locality, and
- (c) the subdivision is compatible with the following—
  - (i) the flood behaviour and flood function on the land,
  - (ii) the flood hazard of the land.
