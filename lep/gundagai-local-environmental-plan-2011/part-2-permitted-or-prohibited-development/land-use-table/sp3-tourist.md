# Zone SP3 Tourist

## 1 Objectives of zone

- To provide for a variety of tourist-oriented development and related uses.
- To recognise and promote the cultural significance of the "Dog on the Tuckerbox" installation at the Five Mile.

## 2 Permitted without consent

Nil

## 3 Permitted with consent

Amusement centres; Aquaculture; Camping grounds; Car parks; Caravan parks; Cellar door premises; Community facilities; Emergency services facilities; Entertainment facilities; Environmental facilities; Environmental protection works; Food and drink premises; Function centres; Garden centres; Highway service centres; Home businesses; Home occupations; Information and education facilities; Kiosks; Markets; Passenger transport facilities; Recreation areas; Recreation facilities (indoor); Recreation facilities (outdoor); Roads; Roadside stalls; Service stations; Sewerage systems; Signage; Shops; Tourist and visitor accommodation; Transport depots; Water supply systems

## 4 Prohibited

Local distribution premises; Any development not specified in item 2 or 3
