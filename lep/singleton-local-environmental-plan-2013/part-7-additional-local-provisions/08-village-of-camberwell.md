# 7.9 Village of Camberwell

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to restrict development for the purpose of residential accommodation on land at Camberwell that is in Zone R5 Large Lot Residential.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land at Camberwell that is in Zone R5 Large Lot Residential.

**(3)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development consent must not be granted to development for the purpose of residential accommodation on land to which this clause applies unless the development will be situated on a lot that is at least the minimum size shown for the land on the *Lot Size Map*.
