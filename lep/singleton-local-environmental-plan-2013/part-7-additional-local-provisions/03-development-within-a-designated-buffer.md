# 7.4 Development within a designated buffer area

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to protect the operational environment of waste or resource management facilities and the Singleton Military Area.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Singleton Military Area" and "Waste Management Facility" on the *Buffer Areas Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the following matters—
- (a) the impact that any noise and other emissions associated with existing land uses may have on the proposed development,
- (b) any proposed measures incorporated into the development that limit the impact of such noise and other emissions associated with the existing land use,
- (c) any opportunities to relocate the proposed development outside the land to which this clause applies,
- (d) whether the proposed development is likely to adversely affect the operational environment of any existing development on the land to which this clause applies.
