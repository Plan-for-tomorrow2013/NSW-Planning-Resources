# 7.7 Tourist and visitor accommodation in rural and conservation zones

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to permit tourist and visitor accommodation on land in rural and conservation zones if a dwelling house is also permitted on that land.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
- (b) Zone RU2 Rural Landscape,
- (c) Zone C3 Environmental Management.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for the purpose of tourist and visitor accommodation on land to which this clause applies unless a dual occupancy or dwelling house is permitted on that land under clause 4.2A.
