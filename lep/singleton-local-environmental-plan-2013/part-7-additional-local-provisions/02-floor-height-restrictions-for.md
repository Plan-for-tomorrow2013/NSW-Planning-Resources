# 7.3 Floor height restrictions for residential accommodation and tourist or visitor accommodation

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted for the erection of a single storey building used for the purpose of residential accommodation or tourist or visitor accommodation on flood liable land unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the foundations and structure of that building will mitigate any potential damage to the building caused by floodwaters, and
- (b) if the building is located on land identified on the *Floor Height Restriction Map*—the finished floor height of any habitable room in that building will not be less than the minimum height shown for the land on that map, and
- (c) if the building is located on other flood liable land—the finished floor height of any habitable room in that building will not be less than the flood planning level.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a two-storey building used for the purpose of residential accommodation or tourist or visitor accommodation on flood liable land unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the floor area of all habitable rooms on the ground floor will not exceed 50% of the total floor area of all habitable rooms in the building, and
- (b) all habitable rooms on the ground floor will be constructed using water-resistant materials, and
- (c) a flood-free building site is not located on natural ground level on the land, and
- (d) if the building is located on land identified on the *Floor Height Restriction Map*—the finished floor height of the first floor of the building will not be less than the height shown for the land on that map and will not exceed 4 metres above natural ground level, and
- (e) if the building is located on other flood liable land—the finished floor height of the first floor of the building will not be less than the flood planning level and will not exceed 4 metres above natural ground level.

**(3)**
This clause does not apply to the following development—
- (a) the extension of an existing dwelling house by no more than 50% of its internal floor area,
- (b) an addition to an existing dwelling house with an area of no more than 50% of the internal floor area of that dwelling to be used for the purpose of a dual occupancy.
