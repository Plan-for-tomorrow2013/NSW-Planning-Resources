# 1.2 Aims of Plan

**(1)**
This Plan aims to make local environmental planning provisions for land in Singleton in accordance with the relevant standard environmental planning instrument under section 3.20 of the Act.

**(2)**
The particular aims of this Plan are as follows—
- (aa) to protect and promote the use and development of land for arts and cultural activity, including music and other performance arts,
- (a) to provide a framework for regulating development within Singleton,
- (b) to promote the use of rural resources for agriculture and primary production, including forestry and open cut mining, and major infrastructure providers,
- (c) to encourage the sustainable management, development and conservation of natural resources,
- (d) to protect and conserve the environmental and cultural heritage of Singleton,
- (e) to ensure that a range of housing choice is provided for all residents of Singleton throughout all life stages,
- (f) to promote and coordinate the orderly and economic use and development of land in Singleton,
- (g) to provide adequate protection for, and minimise risk to, the community, as far as is practicable, from environmental hazards, including flooding and bush fire,
- (h) to protect and enhance watercourses, riparian habitats and water quality in Singleton.
