# 6.10 Development on land intended to be acquired for Barton Highway duplication

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to limit development on land intended to be acquired for the construction of the Barton Highway duplication.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Barton Highway Corridor" on the *Barton Highway Duplication Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for any development of a permanent nature (for example, a building) on land to which this clause applies unless the development is consistent with the purpose for which the land is intended to be acquired.

**(4)**
A condition of a development consent granted under subsection (3) may require the demolition or removal of the work for which consent is granted before the land is acquired for the purpose of the Barton Highway duplication.
