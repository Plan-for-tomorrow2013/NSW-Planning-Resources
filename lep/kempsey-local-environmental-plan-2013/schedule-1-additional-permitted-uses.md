# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Clybucca

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Clybucca, being Lots 1 and 2, DP 1159172, Lots 31 and 32, DP 752409, Lot 4, DP 255368, and Lot 331, DP 1125325, and identified as "Item 1" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*, if all of the land is held in the same ownership.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted for the subdivision of the land as a whole under the *Community Land Development Act 1989* and the *Community Land Management Act 1989* if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the land will be subdivided into 5 lots suitable for rural-residential use, clustered on the flood free land in the western part of the land, and a single residue lot owned in common, and
- (b) each of the 5 rural-residential lots will be not less than 1 hectare and not more than 2 hectares in area, and
- (c) development will be carried out on each of the rural-residential lots resulting in the erection of a single dwelling house and other ancillary and compatible structures, and the use of those lots for activities and works associated with residential living, and
- (d) the residue lot will continue to be a contiguous lot and will be used primarily for agricultural purposes consistent with the zoning of the land, and
- (e) appropriate environmental protection works will be undertaken for the protection of wetlands and Aboriginal places of heritage significance, and
- (f) appropriate rehabilitation works will be undertaken for those parts of the land that are affected by past sand extractions.

**(3)**
The single residue lot must not be further subdivided even if such a subdivision would be allowed under other provisions of this plan.

## 2 Use of certain land at Fishermans Reach Road, north of Fishermans Reach

**(1)**
This clause applies land at Fishermans Reach Road, north of Fishermans Reach, being Lots 21-23, DP 812489, and identified as "Item 2" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted for the subdivision of the land under the *Community Land Development Act 1989* if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the subdivision is for the purpose of tourist and visitor accommodation for which development consent has been granted before the commencement of this clause, and
- (b) any dwelling forming part of the tourist and visitor accommodation will not be permanently occupied (other than a dwelling on each of Lots 21-23, DP 812489 that may be permanently occupied by a manager of the tourist and visitor accommodation).
