# 4.2D Subdivision of certain land at Crescent Head

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 1" on the *Lot Size Map* that is in Zone C3 Environmental Management.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to subdivision of the land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the land will be adequately managed to ensure the ongoing viability of the natural ecosystems and native vegetation.
