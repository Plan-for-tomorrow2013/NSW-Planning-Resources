# 1.2 Aims of Plan

**(1)**
This Plan aims to make local environmental planning provisions for land in Urana in accordance with the relevant standard environmental planning instrument under section 3.20 of the Act.

**(2)**
The particular aims of this Plan are as follows—
- (aa) to protect and promote the use and development of land for arts and cultural activity, including music and other performance arts,
- (a) to encourage sustainable economic growth and development,
- (b) to protect, enhance and conserve agricultural land through the proper management, development and conservation of natural and man-made resources,
- (c) to identify, protect, conserve and enhance Urana's natural assets,
- (d) to allow for the equitable provision of social services and facilities for the community,
- (e) to conserve, protect and enhance the environmental and cultural heritage of Urana.
