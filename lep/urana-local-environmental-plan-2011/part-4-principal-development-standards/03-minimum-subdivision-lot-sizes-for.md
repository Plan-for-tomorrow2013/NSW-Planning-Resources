# 4.1A Minimum subdivision lot sizes for certain split zones

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to provide for the subdivision of lots that are within more than one zone but which cannot be subdivided under clause 4.1, and
- (b) to ensure that the subdivision occurs in a manner that promotes suitable land use and development.

**(2)**
This clause applies to each lot (an *original lot*) that contains—
- (a) land in Zone RU5 Village or Zone R5 Large Lot Residential, and
- (b) land in Zone RU1 Primary Production.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.1, development consent may be granted to subdivide an original lot to create other lots (the *resulting lots*) if—
- (a) one of the resulting lots will contain—
  - (i) land in Zone RU5 Village or Zone R5 Large Lot Residential that has an area that is not less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to that land, and
  - (ii) all of the land in Zone RU1 Primary Production that was in the original lot, and
- (b) all other resulting lots will contain land that has an area that is not less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to that land.
