# 17 Provision of essential infrastructure

Consent must not be granted for development on any land to which this plan applies unless the consent authority—
- (a) is satisfied that adequate arrangements have been made for the provision of any infrastructure that is essential for the proposed development, including the following—
  - (i) a supply of water,
  - (ii) provision of energy,
  - (iii) provision of telecommunications,
  - (iv) a system for the disposal and management of sewage, and
- (b) has considered the impacts of the provision of that infrastructure on the land to which the development application relates.
