# 28A Residential flat buildings and multiple dwelling housing in Zone 2 (2)

**(1)**
Consent must not be granted to the erection or creation of a residential flat building on—
- (a) an irregular or standard corner allotment in Zone 2 (2) unless the allotment has a minimum area of 1,500 square metres and a minimum width of 30 metres, or
- (b) a battle-axe allotment in Zone 2 (2) unless the allotment has a minimum area of 2,000 square metres and the battle-axe access handle has a minimum width of 8 metres, or
- (c) any other irregular allotment or standard allotment in Zone 2 (2) unless the allotment has a minimum area of 1,200 square metres and a minimum width of 30 metres.

**(2)**
Consent must not be granted to the erection or creation of multiple dwelling housing on—
- (a) a corner allotment in Zone 2 (2) unless the allotment has a minimum area of 1,200 square metres and a minimum width of 30 metres, or
- (b) a battle-axe allotment in Zone 2 (2) unless the allotment has a minimum area of 1,500 square metres and the battle-axe access handle has a minimum width of 8 metres, or
- (c) any other allotment in Zone 2 (2) unless the allotment has a minimum area of 900 square metres and a minimum width of 25 metres.

**(3)**
In this clause—
*width* means the distance of the perpendicular line between the side boundaries, as measured at the front building setback.
