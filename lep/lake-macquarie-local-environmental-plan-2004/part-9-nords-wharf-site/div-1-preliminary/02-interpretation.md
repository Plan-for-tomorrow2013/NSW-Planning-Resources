# 87 Interpretation

**(1)**
In this Part—
*Council* means the Council of the City of Lake Macquarie.
*Height of Buildings Map* means the *Lake Macquarie Local Environmental Plan 2004—Nords Wharf—Height of Buildings Map*.
*Land Application Map* means the *Lake Macquarie Local Environmental Plan 2004—Nords Wharf—Land Application Map*.
*Land Reservation Acquisition Map* means the *Lake Macquarie Local Environmental Plan 2004—Nords Wharf—Land Reservation Acquisition Map*.
*Land Zoning Map* means the *Lake Macquarie Local Environmental Plan 2004—Nords Wharf—Land Zoning Map*.

**(2)**
A word or expression used in this Part has the same meaning as it has in the standard instrument prescribed by the *Standard Instrument (Local Environmental Plans) Order 2006* unless it is otherwise defined in this Part.
