# 48 Development requiring notification

**(1)**
A development application proposing the following development requires notification in accordance with *Lake Macquarie Development Control Plan No 1—Principles of Development*—
- (a) the demolition of a heritage item,
- (b) the carrying out of any development allowed by clause 53 (Conservation incentives).

**(2)**
Subclause (1) does not apply to designated development.
