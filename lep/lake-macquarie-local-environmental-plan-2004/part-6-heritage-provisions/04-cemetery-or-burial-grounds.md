# 46 Cemetery or burial grounds

Development consent is not required for the following development in a cemetery or burial ground if there will be no disturbance to human remains, to relics in the form of grave goods or to a place of Aboriginal heritage significance—
- (a) the creation of a new grave or monument,
- (b) an excavation or disturbance of land for the purpose of carrying out conservation or repair of monuments or grave markers.
