# 132 Application of Part

**(1)**
This Part applies to the land identified on the *Land Application Map*, referred to in this Part as the *South Wallarah Peninsula site*.

**(2)**
No other provisions of this plan (other than clauses 4 and 6) apply to land within South Wallarah Peninsula site.
