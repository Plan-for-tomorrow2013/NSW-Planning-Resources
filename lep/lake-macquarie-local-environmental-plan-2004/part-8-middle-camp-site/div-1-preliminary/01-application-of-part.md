# 63 Application of Part

**(1)**
This Part applies to the land identified on the *Land Application Map*, referred to in this Part as the *Middle Camp site*.

**(2)**
No other provisions of this plan (other than clauses 4 and 6) apply to land within the Middle Camp site.
