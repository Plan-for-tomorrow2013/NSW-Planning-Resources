# 75 Neighbourhood shops in Zone R2 Low Density Residential

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to set a maximum retail floor area for neighbourhood shops in Zone R2 Low Density Residential.

**(2)**
The retail floor area of a neighbourhood shop must not exceed 125 square metres.
