# 125 Trees or vegetation not prescribed by development control plan

**(1)**
This clause applies to any tree or other vegetation that is not of a species or kind prescribed for the purposes of clause 124 by a development control plan made by the Council.

**(2)**
The ringbarking, cutting down, topping, lopping, removal, injuring or destruction of any tree or other vegetation to which this clause applies is permitted without development consent.
