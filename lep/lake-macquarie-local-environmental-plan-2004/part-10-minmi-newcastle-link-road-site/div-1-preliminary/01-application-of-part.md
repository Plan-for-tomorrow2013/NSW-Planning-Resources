# 106 Application of Part

**(1)**
This Part applies to the land identified on the *Land Application Map*, referred to in this Part as the *Minmi-Newcastle Link Road site*.

**(2)**
No other provision of this Plan (other than clauses 4 and 6) applies to land within the Minmi-Newcastle Link Road site.
