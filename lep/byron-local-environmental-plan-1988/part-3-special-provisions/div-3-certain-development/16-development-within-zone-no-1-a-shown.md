# 38 Development within Zone No 1 (a) shown hatched on the map

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within Zone No 1 (a) and shown hatched on the map.

**(2)**
The council shall not consent to the carrying out of development for any purpose on land to which this clause applies unless the council has made an assessment of the susceptibility of the land and the proposed development to flooding, landslip, bushfire hazard, soil erosion and the like.
