# 62 Savings

Nothing in this plan prohibits or requires consent for—
- (a) the carrying out of development of any description specified in Schedule 12, or
- (b) the use of existing buildings of the Crown by the Crown, or
- (c) home occupations carried on in dwelling-houses.
