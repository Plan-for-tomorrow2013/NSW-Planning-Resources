# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Barooga Road, Tocumwal

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Barooga Road, Tocumwal, being Lot 133, DP 773957.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of one dwelling house is permitted with development consent.
