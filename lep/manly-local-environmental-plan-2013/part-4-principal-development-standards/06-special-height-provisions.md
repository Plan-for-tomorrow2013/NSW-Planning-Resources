# 4.3A Special height provisions

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to maintain public views to Sydney Harbour from street level on local roads above steeply sloping sites on certain land.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3 (2), the height of a building on a lot identified as "Special height provisions" on the *Height of Buildings Map* must not exceed the height of the highest point of the road adjoining the centre point of the lot boundary that adjoins the road that is the frontage to that lot.
