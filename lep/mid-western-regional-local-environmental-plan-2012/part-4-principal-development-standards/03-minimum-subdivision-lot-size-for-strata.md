# 4.1AB Minimum subdivision lot size for strata subdivision in certain rural, residential zones and environmental zones

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that land to which this clause applies is not unduly fragmented by subdivisions.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones that is used, or is proposed to be used, for residential accommodation—
- (a) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
- (b) Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots,
- (c) Zone R5 Large Lot Residential,
- (d) Zone C3 Environmental Management.

**(3)**
A lot resulting from a subdivision of land to which this clause applies by the registration of a strata plan or strata plan of subdivision under the *Strata Schemes Development Act 2015* must not be less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* for the land.

**(4)**
Subclause (3) does not apply to a lot comprising common property within the meaning of the *Strata Schemes Development Act 2015*.

**(5)**
This clause applies despite clause 4.1.
