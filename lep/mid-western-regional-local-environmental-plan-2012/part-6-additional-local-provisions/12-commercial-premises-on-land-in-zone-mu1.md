# 6.13 Commercial premises on land in Zone MU1 and known as Caerleon

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to maintain the commercial hierarchy of Mudgee by encouraging retail development of an appropriate scale on land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use and known as Caerleon.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development for the purposes of retail premises on land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use and known as Caerleon if the gross floor area of each individual retail premises does not exceed 1,280 square metres.

> Note: A building may contain more than 1 retail premises as long as each retail premises individually does not have a gross floor area exceeding 1,280 square metres.

**(3)**
Before granting development consent to development referred to in subclause (2), the consent authority must consider the economic impact of the development and be satisfied that the development will not have an adverse impact on the commercial hierarchy of Mudgee.
