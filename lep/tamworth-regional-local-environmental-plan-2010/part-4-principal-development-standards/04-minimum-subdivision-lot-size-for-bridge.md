# 4.1AB Minimum subdivision lot size for Bridge Street Residential Precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Bridge Street Residential Precinct" on the *Precinct Areas Map*.

**(2)**
Despite clauses 4.1 and 4.1A, the size of a lot resulting from a subdivision of land to which this clause applies may be less than the minimum lot size shown on the *Lot Size Map* if—
- (a) there is existing residential accommodation on the lot, or
- (b) the consent authority is satisfied that residential accommodation will be erected on the lot.
