# 4.1AC Minimum subdivision lot size for Daruka Road and Piper Street

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Daruka Road and Piper Street" on the *Lot Size Map*.

**(2)**
Despite clauses 4.1 and 4.1A, development consent may be granted to the subdivision of the land if the consent authority is satisfied that each lot resulting from the subdivision—
- (a) will be at least 300m2, and
- (b) will be used for at least one of the following purposes—
  - (i) attached dwellings,
  - (ii) dual occupancies,
  - (iii) multi dwelling housing,
  - (iv) semi-detached dwellings.
