# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "1A" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a vehicle body repair workshop is permitted with development consent.
