# 7.15 Minimum building street frontage in Zone E2

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to ensure that, visually, buildings have an appropriate overall horizontal proportion compared to their vertical proportions,
- (b) to provide appropriate dimensions and spacing to ensure adequate privacy between any residential component and the adjoining land use,
- (c) to provide appropriate dimensions for the design of car park levels and ensure access is reasonably spaced along roads and lanes,
- (d) to encourage larger development of commercial office, business, residential and mixed use buildings provided for under this plan.

**(2)**
This clause applies to residential apartment development within the meaning of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021*, Chapter 4 on land in Zone E2 Commercial Centre.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for the erection of a building unless the building will have a street frontage of at least 25m.

**(4)**
Subclause (3) does not apply if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) it is not possible for the building to have a street frontage of at least 25m because of the physical constraints of the land or adjoining land, and
- (b) the development is still consistent with the aims and objectives of this clause and the other provisions of this plan.
