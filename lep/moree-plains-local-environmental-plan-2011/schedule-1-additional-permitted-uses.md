# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Bingara Road, Moree

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Bingara Road, Moree, known as "Longpoint", being Lot 6, DP 1072832.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a dwelling house and a self-storage unit is permitted with consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at 393 Frome Street, Moree

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 393 Frome Street, Moree, being Lot 496, DP 751780.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of signage is permitted with consent.

## 3 Use of certain land at 74 Drive In Road, Moree

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 74 Drive In Road, Moree, being Lot 319, DP 751780.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of concrete works is permitted with consent.
