# 6.5 Events permitted without development consent

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to provide for the temporary use of public reserves and public roads for exhibitions, meetings, concerts or events.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development for the purposes of an event (including the erection of any associated structures) may be carried out on a public reserve or public road without development consent for a maximum period of 52 days (whether or not consecutive days) in any period of 12 months.

> Note: Other approvals may be required, and in such cases must be obtained, under other Acts, including the *Local Government Act 1993*, the *Roads Act 1993* and the *Crown Land Management Act 2016*.

**(3)**
Nothing in this clause permits development for the purposes of overnight accommodation.

**(4)**
*State Environmental Planning Policy (Miscellaneous Consent Provisions) 2007* does not apply to development to which this clause applies.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*event* means an exhibition, meeting, concert or other event that is open to the public.
