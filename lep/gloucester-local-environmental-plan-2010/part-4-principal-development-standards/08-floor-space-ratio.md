# 4.4 Floor space ratio

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to restrict the overall scale of buildings and structures to prevent overshadowing and preserve privacy, rural amenity and the traditional town character,
- (b) to minimise any adverse impact of development, including any such impact on heritage conservation areas and heritage items.

**(2)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on any land is not to exceed the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.
