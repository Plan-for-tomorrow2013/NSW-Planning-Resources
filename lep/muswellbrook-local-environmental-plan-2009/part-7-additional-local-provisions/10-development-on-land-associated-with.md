# 7.11 Development on land associated with Liddell-Bayswater Power Station

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at New England Highway, Muswellbrook identified as "Liddell-Bayswater 1" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to facilitate the transition of activities at the Liddell-Bayswater Power Station site from coal fired power station operations to a broader range of employment generating activities, and
- (b) to ensure land uses involved in future development are appropriate in the context of the continued existence of Liddell-Bayswater Power Station near the land.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless—
- (a) the consent authority is satisfied that—
  - (i) there will be no significant land use conflicts between the development and land uses associated with the Liddell-Bayswater Power Station, and
  - (ii) the land uses involved in the development will otherwise be appropriate for a site that is proximate to Liddell-Bayswater Power Station, and
- (b) a development control plan that provides for the matters specified in subclause (4) has been prepared for the land.

**(4)**
The development control plan must provide for the following—
- (a) a staging plan for the timely and efficient provision of necessary infrastructure,
- (b) an overall transport movement hierarchy showing the major circulation routes and connections to achieve a simple and safe movement system for private vehicles, public transport, pedestrians and cyclists,
- (c) an overall landscaping and vegetation management strategy for the protection and enhancement of riparian areas and remnant vegetation, in particular visually prominent locations, and detailed landscaping requirements to manage views of the site from both the public and private domain,
- (d) the location of flora, fauna and vegetation corridors,
- (e) stormwater and water quality management controls,
- (f) amelioration of natural and environmental hazards, including bush fires, flooding and site contamination and, in relation to natural hazards, the safe occupation of, and the evacuation from, any land so affected,
- (g) detailed urban design controls for significant development sites.

**(5)**
Subclause (3)(b) does not apply to the following development—
- (a) subdivision for the purposes of a realignment of boundaries that does not create additional lots,
- (b) subdivision of land if any of the lots proposed to be created are to be reserved or dedicated for public open space, public roads or another public or environmental protection purpose,
- (c) development of a minor nature only, if, in the consent authority's opinion, the development is consistent with the objectives of the zone in which the development will be carried out.
