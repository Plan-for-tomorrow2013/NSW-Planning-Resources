# 7.18 Development of certain land in Zone R3

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that development on land to which this clause applies occurs—
- (a) in a logical and coordinated manner, and
- (b) only after a development control plan that includes specific controls has been prepared for the land.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Medium Density Site" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless a development control plan that complies with subclause (4) has been prepared for the land.

**(4)**
The development control plan must provide for the following—
- (a) a staging plan for the timely and efficient delivery of development, making provision for necessary infrastructure and sequencing,
- (b) compatibility with the height, bulk and scale of adjoining lower density development,
- (c) compatibility with the built form of surrounding heritage items and heritage conservation areas,
- (d) urban design quality, including consistency with the document titled *Urban Design Excellence - R3 Medium Density and E1 Local Centre Zones*, adopted by the Council in February 2024,
- (e) environmental impacts, including the following—
  - (i) overshadowing and solar access,
  - (ii) visual and acoustic privacy,
  - (iii) separation distance,
  - (iv) environmental sustainability,
- (f) noise mitigation,
- (g) landscaping of open space, including private and communal open space, and tree retention,
- (h) access, parking and traffic,
- (i) encouragement of sustainable transport, including the following—
  - (i) connectivity to, and increased use of, public transport,
  - (ii) safe and effective pedestrian access and cycling,
  - (iii) road access, the circulation network, car parking provision and integrated options to reduce car use.

**(5)**
Subclause (3) does not apply to the following development—
- (a) a subdivision for the purpose of a realignment of boundaries that does not create additional lots,
- (b) proposed development that is of a minor nature only, if the consent authority is of the opinion that the carrying out of the development would be consistent with the objectives of the zone in which the land is situated,
- (c) proposed development for the purposes of site preparation, including the following—
  - (i) the demolition of a building or work on the site,
  - (ii) the carrying out of remediation of, or earthworks on, the site,
  - (iii) development for the purpose of sewer, stormwater, water or telecommunications infrastructure for the site.
