# 7.14 Development on land in Zone RU5

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to reinforce the primary function of Zone RU5 Village as an area for land uses, services and facilities associated with a rural village.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land in Zone RU5 Village unless the consent authority has considered—
- (a) whether the development will reduce the amount of floor space used for commercial premises in the zone, and
- (b) if the development will reduce the amount of floor space used for commercial premises in the zone—whether the reduction is appropriate.
