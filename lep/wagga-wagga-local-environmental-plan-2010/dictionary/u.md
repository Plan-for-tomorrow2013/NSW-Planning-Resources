# Dictionary — U

### underground mining

*underground mining* means—
- (a) mining carried out beneath the earth's surface, including bord and pillar mining, longwall mining, top-level caving, sub-level caving and auger mining, and
- (b) shafts, drill holes, gas and water drainage works, surface rehabilitation works and access pits associated with that mining (whether carried out on or beneath the earth's surface),
but does not include open cut mining.

### urban release area

*urban release area* means an area of land identified as "Urban Release Area" on the *Urban Release Area Map*.

### Urban Release Area Map

*Urban Release Area Map* means the *Wagga Wagga Local Environmental Plan 2010 Urban Release Area Map*.
