# 6.5 Lloyd Quarry site development

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as the "Lloyd Quarry site" on the *Lloyd Quarry Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for the subdivision of land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) quarrying activities have ceased permanently, and
- (b) the land has been adequately rehabilitated for the purpose of urban development.
