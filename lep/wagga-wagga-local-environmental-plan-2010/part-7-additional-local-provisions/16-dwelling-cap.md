# 7.16 Dwelling cap

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 1" or "Area 2" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will not result in more than 220 dwellings.
