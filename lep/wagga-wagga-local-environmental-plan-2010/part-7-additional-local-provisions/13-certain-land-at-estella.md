# 7.13 Certain land at Estella

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that development of certain land at Estella provides for cycling and pedestrian pathways.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the following land at Estella—
- (a) Lot 199, DP 1206757,
- (b) Lot 2, DP 1270330.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for a subdivision of land unless the consent authority is satisfied that adequate provision is made for cycling and pedestrian pathways.
