# 7.7 Protected regrowth for Native Vegetation Act 2003

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to identify, for the purposes of the *Native Vegetation Act 2003*, protected regrowth on land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone RE1 Public Recreation,
- (b) Zone C2 Environmental Conservation,
- (c) Zone C4 Environmental Living.

**(2)**
Regrowth of native vegetation on the land marked "Regrowth protection area" on the *Protected Regrowth Map* is identified as protected regrowth for the purposes of section 10(1) of the *Native Vegetation Act 2003*.
