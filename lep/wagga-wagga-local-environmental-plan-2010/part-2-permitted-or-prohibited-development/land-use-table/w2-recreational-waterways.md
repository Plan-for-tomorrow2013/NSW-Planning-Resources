# Zone W2 Recreational Waterways

## 1 Objectives of zone

- To protect the ecological, scenic and recreation values of recreational waterways.
- To allow for water-based recreation and related uses.
- To provide for sustainable fishing industries and recreational fishing.

## 2 Permitted without consent

Nil

## 3 Permitted with consent

Aquaculture; Boat launching ramps; Boat sheds; Charter and tourism boating facilities; Environmental facilities; Environmental protection works; Information and education facilities; Jetties; Kiosks; Marinas; Mooring pens; Recreation areas; Recreation facilities (major); Recreation facilities (outdoor); Roads; Signage; Water recreation structures; Wharf or boating facilities

## 4 Prohibited

Industries; Local distribution premises; Multi dwelling housing; Residential flat buildings; Retail premises; Seniors housing; Warehouse or distribution centres; Any other development not specified in item 2 or 3
