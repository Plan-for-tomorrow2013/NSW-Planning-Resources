# 4.4 Floor space ratio

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to regulate the density of development and generation of vehicular and pedestrian traffic,
- (b) to facilitate development that contributes to the economic growth of Wagga Wagga city centre,
- (c) to ensure the bulk and scale of development does not have an unacceptable impact on the streetscape and character of the area in which the development is located.

**(2)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on any land is not to exceed the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.
