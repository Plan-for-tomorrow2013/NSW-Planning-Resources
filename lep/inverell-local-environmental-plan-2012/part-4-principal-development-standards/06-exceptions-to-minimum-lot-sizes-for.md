# 4.1D Exceptions to minimum lot sizes for certain residential development in Zone R1

Despite clause 4.1 (3), the size of any lot resulting from a subdivision of land in Zone R1 General Residential may be less than the minimum lot size shown on the Lot Size Map in relation to that land, but not less than 300 square metres, if development consent has been granted in respect of the subdivision for the purposes of any of the following—
- (a) dual occupancies,
- (b) multi dwelling housing,
- (c) residential flat buildings,
- (d) semi-detached dwellings.
