# 6.5 Development within a designated buffer area

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to protect the operational environment of a livestock processing industry, a stock and sale yard, a sewage treatment plant and a waste or resource management facility.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the land identified as "Livestock processing industry", "Stock and sale yards", "Sewage treatment plant" and "Waste or resource management facility" on the *Designated Buffer Map*.

**(3)**
Before granting development consent for development on land to which this clause applies, the consent authority must consider the following matters—
- (a) the impact that any noise and other emissions associated with existing land uses would have on the proposed development,
- (b) any proposed measures incorporated into the development that limit the impact of such noise and other emissions associated with the existing land use,
- (c) any opportunities to relocate the proposed development outside the land to which this clause applies,
- (d) whether the proposed development would adversely affect the operational environment of any existing development on the land to which this clause applies.
