# 2 Land to which plan applies

**(1)**
Except as provided by subclause (2), this plan applies to the land as shown edged heavy black on the map.

**(2)**
This plan does not apply to the land shown as "deferred matter" on the map.

**(3)**
This plan does not apply to land to which *South Sydney Local Environmental Plan 1998* applies.
