# 6 Adoption of Model Provisions

The *Environmental Planning and Assessment Model Provisions 1980* (except for the definitions of *boarding house*, *child care centre*, *dwelling house*, *floor*, *map* and *residential flat building* and clause 16, 17, 19, in its application to *car repair stations*, 28 and 35 (c) are adopted for the purposes of this plan.
