# 8 Zones indicated on the map

For the purposes of this plan, land to which this plan applies shall be within a zone specified hereunder if the land is shown on the map in the manner specified hereunder in relation to that zone—
- Zone No 2 (a) (Residential Zone)—edged heavy black and lettered "2 (a)"
- Zone No 2 (c) (Mixed Use [Residential] Zone)—edged heavy black and lettered "2 (c)"
- Zone No 2 (d) (Residential Renewal Zone)—edged heavy black and lettered "2 (d)"
- Zone No 3 (b) (Neighbourhood Business Zone)—edged heavy black and lettered "3 (b)"
- Zone No 4 (General Industrial Zone)—edged heavy black and marked "4"
- Zone No 4 (d) (Industrial [Interim Development] Zone)—edged heavy black and lettered "4 (d)"
- Zone No 5 (a) (Special Uses Zone)—edged heavy black with a particular land use indicated by lettering
- Zone No 6 (Recreation Existing Zone)—lightly dotted
- Zone No 9 (a) (Reservation [Arterial Road] Zone)—broken bands between black lines
- Zone No 10 (a) (Employment [Business Park] Zone)—edged heavy black and lettered "10 (a)"
