# 7.8 Villawood—height of buildings

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to allow development to be built to the maximum permissible building height if certain development standards are met.

**(2)**
This clause has effect despite clause 4.3.

**(3)**
The height of a building on land identified as "Villawood" on the *Town Centre Precinct Map* must not exceed 9 metres unless the site area of the building is at least the minimum site area shown for the land on the *Minimum Site Area Map*.
