# 7.12 Carramar—height of buildings

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Carramar Precinct" on the *Town Centre Precinct Map* that is in Zone E1 Local Centre.

**(2)**
The height of a building on the land must not exceed 9m if the site area is less than the minimum site area shown for the land on the *Minimum Site Area Map*.
