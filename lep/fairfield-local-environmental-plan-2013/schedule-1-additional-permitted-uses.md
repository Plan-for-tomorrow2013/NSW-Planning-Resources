# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Tarlington Parade, Wall Place and Shearing Place, Bonnyrigg

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "1" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of office premises and a plant nursery is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at 451 North Liverpool Road, Bonnyrigg Heights

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a service station and take away food and drink premises is permitted with development consent.

## 4 Use of certain land at 87 and 99 Cabramatta Road East, Cabramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "4" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of hardware and building supplies or a timber yard is permitted with development consent, but only if the maximum floor space ratio does not exceed 1.5:1.

## 5 Use of certain land at 401-403 Cabramatta Road West, Cabramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "5" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a service station and restaurant or cafe is permitted with development consent, but only if the maximum gross floor area does not exceed 800m2.

## 6 Use of certain land at 563-567 Cabramatta Road West, Cabramatta West

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "6" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a shop and associated car park is permitted with development consent, but only if—
- (a) the maximum gross floor area that is able to be leased is 750m2, and
- (b) the maximum building height is one storey for any non-residential use.

## 20 Use of certain land at 620 Elizabeth Drive, Bonnyrigg Heights

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "22" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a service station and take away food and drink premises is permitted with development consent, but only if—
- (a) the consent authority is satisfied that the maximum gross floor area of the site that is used for the purposes of a service station and take away food and drink premises does not exceed 600m2, excluding the floor space of a service station that comprises the driveway, petrol pumps and pump islands, and
- (b) not more than 1 take away food and drink premises is located on the site at any one time.

## 22 Use of certain land at 1187 The Horsley Drive, Wetherill Park

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "23" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the following purposes is permitted with development consent if the gross floor area of the premises does not exceed 500m2—
- (a) commercial premises within a ground floor,
- (b) business and office premises within a mezzanine level.

**(3)**
Adjoining premises that share a pedestrian access point or other direct internal links are taken to be a single premises.

## 23 Use of certain land at 17-23 Longfield Street, Cabramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "24" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a recreation facility (indoor) and take away food and drink premises is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**
Despite subclause (2), development for the purposes of neighbourhood shops and take away food and drink premises is permitted only if the combined gross floor area of the development does not exceed 160m2.
