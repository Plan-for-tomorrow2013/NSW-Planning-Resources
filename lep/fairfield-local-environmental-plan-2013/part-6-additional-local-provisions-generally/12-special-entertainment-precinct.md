# 6.14 Special entertainment precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Fairfield Special Entertainment Precinct" on the *Special Entertainment Precinct Map*.

**(2)**
For the *Local Government Act 1993*, section 202C(1)(a), a special entertainment precinct is established on the land to which this clause applies.
