# 4.1A Minimum lot size for dual occupancies

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to establish a minimum lot size for dual occupancies.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.1, development consent may be granted for development for the purpose of a dual occupancy if the development will be located on a lot that is at least the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size for Dual Occupancy Development Map* in relation to that land.
