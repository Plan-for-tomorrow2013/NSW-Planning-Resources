# 6.9 Subdivision of land at 9 Endeavour Drive, Balranald

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 126, DP 751170, 9 Endeavour Drive, Balranald (the *original lot*).

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the subdivision of land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) each lot resulting from the subdivision (a *resulting lot*) will be connected to a water reticulation system and a sewage reticulation system, and
- (b) one resulting lot (the *RU1 lot*) will contain all the land in Zone RU1 that was in the original lot, and
- (c) all resulting lots, other than the RU1 lot, will have an area that is not less than the minimum lot size shown for the land on the *Lot Size Map*.
