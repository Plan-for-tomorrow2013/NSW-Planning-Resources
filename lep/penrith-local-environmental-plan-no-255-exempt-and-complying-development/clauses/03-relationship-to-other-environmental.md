# 3 Relationship to other environmental planning instruments

**(1)**
In the event of an inconsistency between this plan and any other local environmental planning instrument or deemed environmental planning instrument, this plan shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency, subject to this clause and section 36 of the Act.

**(2)** (Repealed)

**(3)**
Nothing in this plan affects the application of *Penrith Local Environmental Plan 1991 (Environmental Heritage Conservation)* to land to which this plan applies.

**(4)**
This plan does not affect the application of the following environmental planning instruments to land to which this plan applies—
- (a) *State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021*, Chapter 2,
- (b) *State Environmental Planning Policy (Resources and Energy) 2021*, Chapter 3,
- (c) *State Environmental Planning Policy (Precincts—Western Parkland City) 2021*, Chapter 5,
- (d) *State Environmental Planning Policy (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021*, Chapter 6.
