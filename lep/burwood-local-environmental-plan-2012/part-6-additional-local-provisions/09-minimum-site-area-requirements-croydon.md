# 6.10 Minimum site area requirements—Croydon North Masterplan Precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to development for the following purposes on land in the Croydon North Masterplan Precinct—
- (a) multi dwelling housing,
- (b) residential flat buildings,
- (c) seniors housing,
- (d) serviced apartments,
- (e) shop top housing.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the development has a site area of at least 1,500m2.
