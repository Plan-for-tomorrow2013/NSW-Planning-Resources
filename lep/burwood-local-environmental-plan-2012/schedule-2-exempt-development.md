# Schedule 2 Exempt development

*(Clause 3.1)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies exempt development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional exempt development not specified in that Policy.

> Note: Exempt development may be carried out without the need for development consent under the Act. Such development is not exempt from any approval, licence, permit or authority that is required under any other Act and adjoining owners' property rights and the common law still apply.

## Signage—general

All signage must comply with the following—
- (a) must not cover any mechanical ventilation inlet or outlet vents,
- (b) if over a public road—must be at least 600mm from kerb or roadway edge,
- (c) must not be moving, flashing or running signage,
- (d) must not be located on land on which there is a heritage item or that is in a heritage conservation area.

## Signage—business identification signs

Business identification signage must comply with the following—
- (a) maximum of 1 sign per business,
- (b) area of sign must not exceed 0.8m2 per business,
- (c) if there is more than 1 business on a property, total area of all signs on the property must not exceed 1.6m2.

## Signage—real estate signs (advertising premises or land for sale or rent)

One sign per premises or land not exceeding 3.5m2 in area and displayed for no more than 40 days.

## Signage—existing under-awning signs

Must involve only the replacement of panels on an existing sign.

## Signage—non-illuminated signs behind glass frontage of shops

Must not cover more than 15% of the glass frontage area.

## Signage—temporary signs

Temporary signs must comply with the following—
- (a) must announce a local event of a religious, educational, cultural, social or recreational nature,
- (b) must not contain commercial advertising except the event's sponsor or sponsors,
- (c) must only be displayed for 28 days before, and 7 days after, the event,
- (d) if in the form of posted bills or the like—must not be applied to poles, posts or any other structure on land under the care or control of the Council.

## Signage—top hamper signs (non-illuminated)

Top hamper signs (non-illuminated) must comply with the following—
- (a) must not extend more than 200mm beyond building alignment,
- (b) must not extend below or beyond sides of door hamper, and must not project more than 600mm above top door frame to which sign is attached.
