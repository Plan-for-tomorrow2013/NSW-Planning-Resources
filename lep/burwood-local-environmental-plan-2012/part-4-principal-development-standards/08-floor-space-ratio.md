# 4.4 Floor space ratio

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to enable development density and intensity of land use to achieve an appropriate urban form,
- (b) to focus higher development density and intensity of land use in the inner part of the Burwood Town Centre and to provide a transition in development density and intensity of land use towards the edge of the Burwood Town Centre.

**(2)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on any land is not to exceed the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(2A)**
When calculating the floor space ratio, the following must be excluded from the gross floor area of a building—
- (a) a part of a building that—
  - (i) is used for public car parking, and
  - (ii) was owned or operated by or on behalf of the consent authority on 10 May 2010,
- (b) for buildings used for the purposes of residential flat buildings, shop top housing or commercial premises—a part of a building used for bicycle parking that is—
  - (i) on the ground floor of the building, and
  - (ii) accessible from the outside of the building.
