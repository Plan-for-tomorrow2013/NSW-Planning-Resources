# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at 5 Alexandra Avenue, Croydon

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 5 Alexandra Avenue, Croydon, being Lots 80 and 81, DP 11902.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of office premises is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at 9-11 and 13 Queen Street, Croydon

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 9-11 and 13 Queen Street, Croydon, being Lots 1 and 2, DP 206474 and Lot 3, DP 978926.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of restaurants or cafes and take away food and drink premises is permitted with development consent.
