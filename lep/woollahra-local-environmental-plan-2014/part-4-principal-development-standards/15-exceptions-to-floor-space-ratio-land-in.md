# 4.4E Exceptions to floor space ratio—land in Zone R2

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone R2 Low Density Residential, other than land in the Paddington, Watsons Bay or Woollahra heritage conservation areas identified on the *Heritage Map*.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land is—
- (a) for land identified as "Area 6" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*—0.75:1, or
- (b) for other land—
  - (i) for buildings on a site 400m2 or greater—the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*, or
  - (ii) for buildings on a site less than 400m2—the floor space ratio specified for the site area in the table to this subclause.
| Site area | Maximum floor space ratio |
|---|---|
| <150m2 | 1.05:1 |
| ≥150m2<200m2 | 0.95:1 |
| ≥200m2<250m2 | 0.85:1 |
| ≥250m2<300m2 | 0.75:1 |
| ≥300m2<350m2 | 0.65:1 |
| ≥350m2<400m2 | 0.55:1 |
