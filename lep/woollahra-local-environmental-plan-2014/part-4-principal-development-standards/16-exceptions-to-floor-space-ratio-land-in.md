# 4.4F Exceptions to floor space ratio—land in Zone R3

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone R3 Medium Density Residential.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings used for the purposes of dual occupancies, dwelling houses or semi-detached dwellings on a site on the land is——
- (a) for land identified as "Area 7" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*—0.75:1, or
- (b) for other land—the floor space ratio specified for the site area in the table to this subclause.
| Site area | Maximum floor space ratio |
|---|---|
| <150m2 | 1.05:1 |
| ≥150m2<200m2 | 0.95:1 |
| ≥200m2<250m2 | 0.85:1 |
| ≥250m2<300m2 | 0.75:1 |
| ≥300m2<350m2 | 0.65:1 |
| ≥350m2<400m2 | 0.55:1 |
| ≥400m2 | 0.5:1 |
