# 4.4G Exceptions to floor space ratio—heritage conservation areas

**(1)**
This clause applies to development that is—
- (a) for the following purposes—
  - (i) attached dwellings,
  - (ii) dual occupancies,
  - (iii) dwelling houses,
  - (iv) semi-detached dwellings, and
- (b) on land in the Paddington, Watsons Bay or Woollahra heritage conservation areas identified on the *Heritage Map*.

**(2)**
Clauses 4.4 and 4.4F do not apply to development to which this clause applies.
