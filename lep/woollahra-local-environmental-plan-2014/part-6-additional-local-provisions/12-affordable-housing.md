# 6.13 Affordable housing

**(1)**
In this clause, the *Woollahra Affordable Housing Principles* are as follows—
- (a) affordable housing must be provided and managed to accommodate a diverse residential population representative of all income groups in the Woollahra local government area,
- (b) affordable housing must be rented to tenants at rents that do not exceed a benchmark of 30% of actual household income,
- (c) dwellings provided for affordable housing must be managed to maintain their continued use as affordable housing,
- (d) the Council must use the following, received by or on behalf of the Council, to improve or replace, or provide additional affordable housing in the Woollahra local government area—
  - (i) rent from affordable housing, excluding landlord's expenses such as management and maintenance costs and rates and taxes payable in connection with the dwellings,
  - (ii) money from the disposal of affordable housing,
- (e) affordable housing must consist of dwellings constructed to a standard that, in the consent authority's opinion, is consistent with the same type of dwellings forming part of the proposed development that are not intended to be used as affordable housing, particularly in relation to solar access and natural ventilation.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on land identified as "Area 1" on the *Affordable Housing Map* resulting in—
- (a) the erection of one or more new buildings if the part of the resulting building or buildings used for the purposes of residential accommodation will have a gross floor area of at least 200m2, or
- (b) alterations to one or more existing buildings if the alterations will result in at least 200m2 of gross floor area used for the purposes of residential accommodation.

**(3)**
This clause does not apply to development for the purposes of boarding houses, community housing, group homes, hostels or public housing.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the Woollahra Affordable Housing Principles.

> Note: The matters set out in *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021*, section 15 may also apply to the development.

**(5)**
The consent authority may, when granting development consent to development to which this clause applies, impose a condition requiring an affordable housing contribution equivalent to the contribution specified in subclause (6).

**(6)**
The contribution for development is the amount of gross floor area equivalent to the percentage, shown for the land on the *Affordable Housing Map*, of the gross floor area of the residential component of the development.

**(7)**
A condition imposed under this clause must permit a person to satisfy the contribution by—
- (a) providing the following—
  - (i) a dedication, in favour of the Council, of land comprising one or more dwellings, each having a gross floor area of at least 50m2,
  - (ii) a monetary contribution paid to the Council of equivalent value to any remainder of the gross floor area specified in subclause (6), or
- (b) a monetary contribution paid to the Council of equivalent value to the gross floor area specified in subclause (6).

**(8)**
The rate at which a monetary contribution is taken to be equivalent to floor area for the purposes of this clause must be calculated by reference to the market value of dwellings of a similar size to the dwellings in the proposed development.

**(9)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height for a building on land to which this clause applies is RL 62.7m if the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the contribution required under this clause has been satisfied, and
- (b) the development will result in a mix of dwelling types in residential flat buildings.

**(10)**
Despite clause 4.3 and subclause (9), the maximum height for a building on land to which this clause applies is RL 91m if the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the contribution required under this clause has been satisfied, and
- (b) the development will result in—
  - (i) a mix of dwelling types in residential flat buildings, and
  - (ii) at least 106 dwellings.

**(11)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies is 3.7:1.

**(12)**
In this clause—
*community housing* has the same meaning as in the *Community Housing Providers National Law (NSW)*.
*public housing* has the same meaning as in the *Housing Act 2001*.
