# 6.5 Particular dual occupancy subdivisions must not be approved

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted for a subdivision that would create separate titles for each of the 2 dwellings resulting from a dual occupancy development.

**(2)**
This clause does not apply in relation to a subdivision under—
- (a) the *Community Land Development Act 1989*, or
- (b) the *Strata Schemes (Freehold Development) Act 1973*.
