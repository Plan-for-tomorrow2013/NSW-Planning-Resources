# 10.2 Development control plans for development on land in precinct

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless a development control plan that complies with subclause (2) has been prepared for, or applies to, the land.

**(2)**
The development control plan must provide for the following matters—
- (a) building envelopes and built form controls, including the following—
  - (i) bulk, massing and modulation of buildings,
  - (ii) setbacks to the ground floor and upper storeys,
  - (iii) specified building storeys,
- (b) the location of the development, having regard to the need to achieve an acceptable relationship with other buildings on the same site or on neighbouring sites in terms of separation, setbacks, outlook, orientation, amenity and urban form,
- (c) impact on, and improvements to, the public domain,
- (d) application of the principles of ecologically sustainable development,
- (e) pedestrian, cycle, vehicular and service access and circulation, including the permeability of pedestrian networks,
- (f) improvements to landscaped areas and open space, including tree retention and canopy cover,
- (g) vehicular access to, and design of, basement car parking.
