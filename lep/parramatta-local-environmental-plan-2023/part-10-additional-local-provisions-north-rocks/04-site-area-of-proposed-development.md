# 10.4 Site area of proposed development includes dedicated land

The site area of proposed development on land to which this part applies is, for the purpose of applying a floor space ratio under clause 4.5, taken to include land that—
- (a) is dedicated to the Council or a public authority for a public purpose, including roads, drainage or open space, and
- (b) would have been part of the site area if the land had not been dedicated.
