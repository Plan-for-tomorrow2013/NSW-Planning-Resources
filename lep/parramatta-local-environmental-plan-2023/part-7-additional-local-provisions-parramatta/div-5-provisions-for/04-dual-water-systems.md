# 7.24 Dual water systems

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure the security of water supply in Parramatta City Centre.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a building unless the consent authority is satisfied the building uses a dual water reticulation system containing pipes for potable water and recycled water for all inside and outside water uses.
