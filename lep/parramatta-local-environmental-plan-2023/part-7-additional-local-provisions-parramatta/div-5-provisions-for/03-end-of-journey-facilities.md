# 7.23 End of journey facilities

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to facilitate pedestrian and cycling access to commercial premises.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development involving the erection of a building—
- (a) used only for commercial premises, or
- (b) involving mixed use development with at least 600m2 of gross floor area used for commercial premises.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to the development unless the consent authority is satisfied the building includes all of the following facilities—
- (a) showers,
- (b) change rooms,
- (c) lockers,
- (d) bicycle storage areas.
