# 7.29 63 and 83 Church Street, Parramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at Church Street, Parramatta—
- (a) Lot 100, DP 1249271, 83 Church Street (*Site 1*),
- (b) Lot 102, DP 1249271, 63 Church Street (*Site 2*).

**(2)**
The objectives of this clause are to ensure that development on the land—
- (a) provides employment opportunities in the precinct by ensuring that a minimum proportion of the available floor space is provided for commercial purposes, and
- (b) the scale and bulk of the development does not adversely impact the amenity of the precinct.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development, including staged development, involving the erection of a building on Site 1 or Site 2 unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) at least 25% of the combined gross floor area of all buildings on Site 1 and Site 2 will be used for purposes other than residential accommodation or serviced apartments, and
- (b) (Repealed)
- (c) the gross floor area of each storey of a building on Site 1 above a height of 40m will not exceed 700m2.

**(4)**
Clause 7.14 does not apply to development carried out under this clause if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the development comprises alterations or additions to a building, and
- (b) the design of the building is the result of a design competition.

**(5)**
Clause 7.24 does not apply to development carried out under this clause if the consent authority is satisfied that the development comprises alterations or additions to a building.
