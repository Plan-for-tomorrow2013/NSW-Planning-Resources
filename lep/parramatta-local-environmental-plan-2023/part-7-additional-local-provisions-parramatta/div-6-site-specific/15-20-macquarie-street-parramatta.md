# 7.43 20 Macquarie Street, Parramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 18" on the *Special Provisions Area Map*.

**(2)**
A building on the land may have a floor space ratio of up to 10:1 if the consent authority is satisfied the additional GFA will—
- (a) be used only for hotel or motel accommodation, and
- (b) not be used for residential accommodation or serviced apartments.
