# 7.39 142-154 Macquarie Street, Parramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 12" on the *Special Provisions Area Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a building on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied at least 21,000m2 of the gross floor area of the building will not be used for residential accommodation.

**(3), (4)** (Repealed)
