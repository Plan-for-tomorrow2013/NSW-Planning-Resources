# 7.32 7 Charles Street and 116 Macquarie Street, Parramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at Parramatta—
- (a) Lots 3 and 4, DP 17466, 7 Charles Street,
- (b) Lot 12, DP 706694, 116 Macquarie Street.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a building on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied at least 6,000m2 of the floor space of the building will be used for commercial premises.
