# 7.40 10 Valentine Avenue, Parramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 9" on the *Special Provisions Area Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for the strata subdivision of part of a building on the land that is above ground level (existing) and used for car parks.

**(3)**
A building on the land may exceed the applicable FSR if the consent authority is satisfied the additional gross floor area will be used only for office premises.
