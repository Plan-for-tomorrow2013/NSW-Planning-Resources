# 7.13 Design excellence

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this Division applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the building resulting from the development exhibits design excellence.

**(2)**
In considering whether a building exhibits design excellence, the consent authority must consider the matters specified in clause 6.13(4)(a)-(d).
