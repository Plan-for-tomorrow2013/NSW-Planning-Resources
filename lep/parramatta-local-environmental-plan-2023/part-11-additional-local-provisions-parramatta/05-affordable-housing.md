# 11.5 Affordable housing

**(1)**
In this clause, the *Parramatta North Affordable Housing Principles* are as follows—
- (a) affordable housing must be provided and managed to accommodate a diverse residential population within areas representative of all income groups in the Parramatta local government area,
- (b) affordable housing must be rented to tenants at rents that do not exceed a benchmark of 30% of actual household income,
- (c) at least 5% of all dwellings in the Parramatta North Precinct should be affordable housing,
- (d) dwellings provided for affordable housing must be managed to maintain their continued use as affordable housing,
- (e) affordable housing must consist of dwellings constructed to a standard that, in the consent authority's opinion, is consistent with the same type of dwellings forming part of the proposed development that are not intended to be used as affordable housing, particularly in relation to internal fittings and finishes, solar access and privacy.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on land in the Parramatta North Precinct, other than the following—
- (a) boarding houses,
- (b) co-living housing,
- (c) group homes,
- (d) hostels,
- (e) seniors housing,
- (f) residential accommodation used for the following purposes—
  - (i) campus student accommodation within the meaning of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021*, Chapter 3,section 3.3,
  - (ii) community housing within the meaning of the *Community Housing Providers National Law (NSW)*,
  - (iii) public housing within the meaning of the *Housing Act 2001*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority—
- (a) has considered the Parramatta North Affordable Housing Principles, and
- (b) has considered whether the development should include dwellings used for affordable housing to give effect to the principle in subclause (1)(c), and
- (c) is satisfied that from the date of the issue of the occupation certificate—
  - (i) the dwellings proposed to be used for affordable housing will be used for affordable housing, and
  - (ii) all dwellings used for affordable housing will be managed by a registered community housing provider, and
- (d) is satisfied that a restriction under the *Conveyancing Act 1919*, section 88E will be registered on title before the issue of the occupation certificate to ensure the requirements in paragraph (c) are met.
