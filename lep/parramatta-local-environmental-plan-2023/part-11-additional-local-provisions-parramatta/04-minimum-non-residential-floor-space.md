# 11.4 Minimum non-residential floor space ratio for certain land

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to ensure that a suitable level of non-residential floor space is provided to promote employment in the Parramatta North Precinct, and
- (b) to encourage a mix of land uses which generate employment opportunities in the Parramatta North Precinct.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land identified on the *Additional Local Provisions Map* unless the floor area of non-residential development is at least the following—
- (a) for land identified as "Area 7"—29,450m2,
- (b) for land identified as "Area 8"—58,800m2,
- (c) for land identified as "Area 9"—38,350m2,
- (d) for land identified as "Area 10"—25,500m2,
- (e) for land identified as "Area 11"—10,000m2,
- (f) for land identified as "Area 12"—11,500m2.

**(3)**
Subclause (2)(a)-(d) does not apply to development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) a concept consent applies to the land that will result in at least 152,100m2 of floor area being used for the purposes of non-residential development on land to which subclause (2)(a)-(d) applies, or
- (b) the development is the subject of a concept development application that, if approved, would result in at least 152,100m2 of floor area being used for the purposes of non-residential development on land to which subclause (2)(a)-(d) applies.

**(4)**
In this clause—
*non-residential development* means development other than—
- (a) development for the purposes of residential accommodation, other than residential care facilities, and
- (b) development for the purposes of car parks, and
- (c) development for the purposes of campus student accommodation within the meaning of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021*, Chapter 3, section 3.3, and
- (d) a building that is a heritage item.
