# 11.3 Design excellence

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to ensure that development exhibits design excellence that contributes to the natural, cultural, visual, heritage and built character values of Parramatta North Precinct, and
- (b) to ensure that the development takes a Country-centred approach to respect and enhance Aboriginal cultural heritage and archaeology.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the following development in the Parramatta North Precinct—
- (a) the erection of a new building,
- (b) the erection of a bridge,
- (c) additions or external alterations to an existing building that, in the consent authority's opinion, are significant.

**(3)**
The consent authority must consider the following before determining whether external alterations or additions to an existing building are significant—
- (a) whether the development significantly increases the height or gross floor area of a building,
- (b) whether the development will cause significant adverse impacts on adjoining buildings or enjoyment of the public domain,
- (c) whether the development will significantly alter the external appearance of the building as viewed from a public place.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted for development to which this clause applies unless—
- (a) the consent authority is satisfied the development exhibits design excellence, and
- (b) if the development has not been subject to a competitive design process—
  - (i) a design advisory panel has reviewed the development, and
  - (ii) the consent authority considers the advice of the design advisory panel.

**(5)**
In considering whether the development exhibits design excellence, the consent authority must consider the following matters—
- (a) the matters specified in clause 6.13(4)(a)-(d),
- (b) how the development addresses—
  - (i) Aboriginal cultural heritage, and
  - (ii) the protection and enhancement of adjacent waterways.

**(6)**
Development consent must not be granted for the following development in the Parramatta North Precinct unless a competitive design process has also been held in relation to the proposed development—
- (a) development on land identified as "Area 23", "Area 24", "Area 25", "Area 26", "Area 27", "Area 28", "Area 29" and "Area 30" on the Special Provisions Area Map,
- (b) development—
  - (i) that involves a building that has or will have a building height of more than 55m above ground level (existing), or
  - (ii) that has an estimated development cost of more than $100 million, or
  - (iii) for which the applicant has chosen to have a competitive design process.

**(7)**
Subclause (6) does not apply if—
- (a) the consent authority certifies in writing that a competitive design process is not required, and
- (b) a design advisory panel reviews the development, and
- (c) the consent authority considers the advice of the design advisory panel.

**(8)**
Subclause (6) does not apply to development the subject of a concept development application for which development consent may be granted on the determination of the concept development application.

**(9)**
In deciding whether to grant development consent to development referred to in subclause (6), the consent authority must consider the results of the competitive design process.
