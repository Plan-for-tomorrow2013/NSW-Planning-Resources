# 11.6 Sun access controls

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are the following—
- (a) to maximise solar access to public open spaces within the Parramatta North Precinct to support vegetation growth, access to sunlight, and the enjoyment of the open spaces,
- (b) to protect open spaces from overshadowing, including Parramatta Park.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the Parramatta North Precinct.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land identified as "Area 7" on the *Sun Access Protection Map* unless the consent authority is satisfied the development—
- (a) will not result in any additional overshadowing to land identified as "Area 1" in the Greater Sydney Parklands Shadow Modelling Study, and
- (b) will result in only minor additional overshadowing to land identified as "Area 3" in the Greater Sydney Parklands Shadow Modelling Study between 2pm and 3pm on 21 June in any year.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land in the Parramatta North Precinct if the development will create overshadowing between 9:00am and 3:00pm on 21 June in any year—
- (a) for land identified as "Area 8" on the *Sun Access Protection Map*—on 50% of the land for more than 3 hours, and
- (b) for land identified as "Area 9" on the *Sun Access Protection Map*—on 50% of the land for more than 2 hours, and
- (c) for land identified as "Area 10" on the *Sun Access Protection Map*—on 40% of the land for more than 1 hour, and
- (d) for land identified as "Area 11" on the *Sun Access Protection Map*—on 30% of the land for more than 1 hour, and
- (e) for land identified as "Area 12" on the *Sun Access Protection Map*—on 30% of the land at any time.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*Greater Sydney Parklands Shadow Modelling Study* means the *Greater Sydney Parklands Shadow Modelling Study 2022* published in Government Gazette No 289 of 1 July 2022.
