# 11.7 Additional building height and floor space ratio for certain land

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 13", "Area 14", "Area 15", "Area 16", "Area 17", "Area 18", "Area 19", "Area 20" and "Area 21" on the *Additional Local Provisions Map*.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, a building on the land may have a height of up to—
- (a) for land identified as "Area 14", "Area 15" or "Area 16"—18m, or
- (b) for land identified as "Area 17"—32m, or
- (c) for land identified as "Area 18"—35m, or
- (d) for land identified as "Area 19"—43m, or
- (e) for land identified as "Area 20"—45m, or
- (f) for land identified as "Area 21"—51m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, a building on the land may have a maximum floor space ratio of up to—
- (a) for land identified as "Area 15", "Area 17" or "Area 18"—2.6:1, or
- (b) for land identified as "Area 14"—3:1, or
- (c) for land identified as "Area 16", "Area 19", "Area 20" or "Area 21"—3.7:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that 9,000m2 of the land identified as "Area 13" will be used for the purposes of open space that is accessible to the public.
