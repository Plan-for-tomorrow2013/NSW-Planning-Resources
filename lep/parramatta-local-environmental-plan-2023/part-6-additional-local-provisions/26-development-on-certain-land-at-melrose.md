# 6.27 Development on certain land at Melrose Park and Ermington

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use—
- (a) Lot A, DP 356298, 69 Hughes Avenue, Ermington,
- (b) Lot B, DP 356298, 71 Hughes Avenue, Ermington,
- (c) Lot D, DP 369480, 73 Hughes Avenue, Ermington,
- (d) Lot E, DP 369480, 75 Hughes Avenue, Ermington,
- (e) Lot F, DP 369480, 77 Hughes Avenue, Ermington,
- (f) Lot G, DP 369480, 19 Hope Street, Melrose Park.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on the land is 64m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land is 3.75:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) at least 1,100m2 of the gross floor area of all buildings on the land will not be used for residential accommodation, and
- (b) at least 72% of the total gross floor area of all buildings on the land used for the purposes of residential accommodation will be used for affordable housing for at least 25 years from the date of issue of the occupation certificate.

**(5)**
Clause 9.2(4A) does not apply to the land.

**(6)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 30 June 2026.
