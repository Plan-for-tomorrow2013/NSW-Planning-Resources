# 6.30 Development at Thomas Street, Parramatta

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land—
- (a) Lots 13, 15 and 16, DP 1239,
- (b) Lot 142, DP 537053.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to 30m.

**(3)**
The floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies may be up to 2.25:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) 5% of the sum of the gross floor area of all buildings on the land will be used for the purposes of affordable housing, and
- (b) the parts of the buildings used for the purposes of affordable housing will be used for that purpose for at least 15 years after the issue of the occupation certificate.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 19 June 2026.
