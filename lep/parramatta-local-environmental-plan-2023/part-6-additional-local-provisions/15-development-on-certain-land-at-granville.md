# 6.16 Development on certain land at Granville

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on land identified as "Area B" on the *Key Sites Map* involving the erection of a building.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the development unless the consent authority is satisfied the gross floor area of the part of the building not used for residential accommodation is less than 4,000m2.
