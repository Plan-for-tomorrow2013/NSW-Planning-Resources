# 6.15 Development on certain land at Westmead

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on land identified as "Area A" on the *Key Sites Map* involving a building with a frontage to Hawkesbury Road or Darcy Road, Westmead.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the development unless at least 30% of the gross floor area of the building is not used for residential accommodation.
