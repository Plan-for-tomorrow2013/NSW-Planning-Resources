## 28 Use of certain land at 59-59A Belmore Street East, Oatlands

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "28" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the following purposes is permitted with development consent—
- (a) business premises,
- (b) food and drink premises,
- (c) office premises,
- (d) recreation facilities (indoor).

**(3)**

- (a) for commercial premises, other than retail premises—850m2,
- (b) for food and drink premises—150m2,
- (c) for recreation facilities (indoor)—1,100m2.
