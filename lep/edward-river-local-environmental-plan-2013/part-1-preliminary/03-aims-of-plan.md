# 1.2 Aims of Plan

**(1)**
This Plan aims to make local environmental planning provisions for land in Edward River in accordance with the relevant standard environmental planning instrument under section 3.20 of the Act.

**(2)**
The particular aims of this plan are as follows—
- (aa) to protect and promote the use and development of land for arts and cultural activity, including music and other performance arts,
- (a) to preserve rural land for all forms of primary production through the proper management, development and conservation of natural and human-made resources,
- (b) to facilitate a range of residential and employment opportunities that—
  - (i) are in accordance with demand, and
  - (ii) contribute to the social, economic, and environmental resources of the area, and
  - (iii) support the economic viability of the local community,
- (c) to promote ecologically sustainable urban and rural development and sustainable economic growth,
- (d) to protect, conserve and enhance natural assets and areas of significance for nature conservation and scenic or recreational value,
- (e) to minimise land use conflicts and adverse environmental impacts,
- (f) to provide for the orderly development of tourist activities,
- (g) to minimise the cost to the community of fragmented and isolated development of rural land,
- (h) to protect and conserve places and buildings of archaeological or heritage significance, including Aboriginal objects and places,
- (i) to promote the efficient and equitable provision of public services, infrastructure and amenities.
