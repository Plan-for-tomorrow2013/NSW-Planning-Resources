# 6.10 Airspace operations

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to provide for the effective and ongoing operation of Deniliquin Airport by ensuring its operation is not compromised by development that penetrates the Limitation or Operations Surface for Deniliquin Airport,
- (b) to protect the community from undue risk from the operation.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development the consent authority is satisfied will penetrate the Limitation or Operations Surface.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will not—
- (a) compromise the operation of the airport, or
- (b) cause undue risk to the community.

**(4)** (Repealed)

**(5)**
In this clause—
*Limitation or Operations Surface* means the obstacle limitation surface as shown on the *Limitation or Operations Surface Map*.
