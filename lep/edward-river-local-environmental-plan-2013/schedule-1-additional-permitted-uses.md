# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of land at 321 Victoria Street, Deniliquin

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Lot 17, DP 237702, 321 Victoria Street, Deniliquin.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of residential accommodation is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of land at 227 Augustus Street, Deniliquin

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Lot 114, DP 756310, 227 Augustus Street, Deniliquin.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of general industries is permitted with development consent if the development is limited to the manufacture of precast concrete components.

## 3 Use of land at 137-143 Napier Street, Deniliquin

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Lots 2 and 3, DP 1135813, 137-143 Napier Street, Deniliquin.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of office premises is permitted with development consent.
