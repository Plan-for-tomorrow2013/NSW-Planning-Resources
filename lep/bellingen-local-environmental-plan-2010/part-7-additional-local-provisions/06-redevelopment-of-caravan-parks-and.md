# 7.7 Redevelopment of caravan parks and manufactured home estates

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that the redevelopment of caravan parks and manufactured home estates in Bellingen is carried out in a way that does not reduce the availability of affordable housing in Bellingen.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to an application for the redevelopment of a caravan park or manufactured home estate in Bellingen unless the consent authority has considered the following matters—
- (a) whether the redevelopment is likely to reduce the number, size and location of long-term sites allowed by any approval that applies to that park under Division 2 of Part 3 of the *Local Government (Manufactured Home Estates, Caravan Parks, Camping Grounds and Moveable Dwellings) Regulation 2005*,
- (b) whether the redevelopment is likely to reduce the availability of affordable housing in Bellingen,
- (c) whether there is sufficient comparable affordable housing in Bellingen to satisfy the demand for such housing,
- (d) whether the redevelopment is likely to cause adverse social and economic effects on the long term residents of the park or estate, or the general community.
