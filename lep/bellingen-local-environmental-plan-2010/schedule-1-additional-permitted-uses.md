**(3)**
Development consent under subclause (2) must not be granted after 5 years from the commencement of this Plan.

## 2 Use of certain land at Endeavour Drive, Bellingen

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Endeavour Drive, Bellingen, being Lots 72-79, DP 785443.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a community title subdivision of the land is permitted with consent but only if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) a geotechnical assessment prepared by a suitably qualified person indicates that the development, including the construction of access and the construction of dwellings is feasible having regard to the geotechnical constraints of the land, and
- (b) vehicular access will be along the common boundaries of lots in a community title arrangement with no less than 2 but no more than 3 lots per access way, and
- (c) no more than 3 lots will use shared access ways as their sole form of access to Endeavour Drive, and
- (d) access will be achieved to each lot in a safe and sustainable way, and
- (e) provision will be made for bin storage in the common land adjoining Endeavour Drive, and
- (f) any part of the land that is riparian land has been cleared of all weeds and regenerated in accordance with a plan prepared by a suitably qualified person, and
- (g) arrangements, satisfactory to the Council, have been made for the provision of water supply and drainage and the disposal of stormwater and sewage from the land.

**(3)**
Development consent under subclause (2) must not be granted after 5 years from the commencement of this Plan.
