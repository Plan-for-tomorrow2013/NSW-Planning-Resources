# 6.15 Development of certain land at Kurmond

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Kurmond identified as "A" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the impact of the proposed development on the following species will be adequately managed—
- (a) Cumberland Plain Land Snail,
- (b) Green and Golden Bell Frog.

**(3)** (Repealed)
