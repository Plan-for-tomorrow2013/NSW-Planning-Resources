# 93 Development at Merimbula Airport

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 549112, at Merimbula Airport, as shown on sheet 12 of the zoning map.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this plan, a person may carry out development for the purpose of commercial, light industrial or community uses on the land to which this clause applies, but only with development consent.

**(3)**
Consent may be granted to a commercial, light industrial or community use of the land to which this clause applies only if—
- (a) the use does not interfere with the effective operation of the airport, and
- (b) the use does not detract from the commercial viability of the Merimbula Central Business District.
