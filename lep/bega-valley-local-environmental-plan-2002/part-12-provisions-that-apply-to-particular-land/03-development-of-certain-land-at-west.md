# 92 Development of certain land at West Pambula

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within Zone 2 (f) at West Pambula, as shown on sheet 29 of the zoning map.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this plan, a person may carry out development for the purpose of a dwelling on land to which this clause applies, but only with development consent.

**(3)**
Consent may be granted to the erection of a dwelling on an allotment of land to which this clause applies only if—
- (a) in the case of an unsewered allotment—
  - (i) the allotment has an area of not less than 1,500 square metres and the consent authority is satisfied that sufficient area will be available for on-site treatment of septic waste, and
  - (ii) the siting of the dwelling will allow future re-subdivision of the land should a reticulated sewerage service become available, and
- (b) the consent authority is satisfied that adequate arrangements have been or will be made to service the development with roads, drainage and a reticulated water supply.
