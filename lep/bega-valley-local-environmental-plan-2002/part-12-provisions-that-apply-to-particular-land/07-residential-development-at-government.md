# 96 Residential development at Government Road, Eden

**(1)**
Notwithstanding clause 26, before consent is granted to any subdivision for residential purposes of the land as shown on sheet 14 of the zoning map, the consent authority must prepare and consider a traffic impact study for the entire proposed development.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of the plan, consent must not be granted to the creation of more than 150 residential lots from the land as shown on sheet 14 of the zoning map until the Council has adopted strategies for the provision of a water supply to, and for the disposal of sewage arising from use of, the proposed lots.
