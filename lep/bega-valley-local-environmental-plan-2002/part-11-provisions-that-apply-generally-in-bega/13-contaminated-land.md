# 76 Contaminated land

**(1)**
Consent must not be granted to the subdivision of land or the erection of a building on any land unless the consent authority has made an assessment of—
- (a) any contamination of the land resulting from previous use of the land, and
- (b) any measures to mitigate against any adverse impacts arising from the contamination of the land.

**(2)**
This clause does not affect any requirement made by the *State Environmental Planning Policy (Resilience and Hazards) 2021*, Chapter 4.
