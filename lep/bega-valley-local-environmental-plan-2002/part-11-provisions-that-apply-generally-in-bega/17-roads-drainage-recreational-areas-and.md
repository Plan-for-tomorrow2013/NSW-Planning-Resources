# 80 Roads, drainage, recreational areas and parking

Nothing in this plan prevents the Council or another public authority from carrying out, or requires the Council or another public authority to obtain consent to carry out, development on land within any zone for the purpose of roads, stormwater drainage, recreational areas, landscaping, gardening, bushfire hazard reduction, amenities buildings or parking.
