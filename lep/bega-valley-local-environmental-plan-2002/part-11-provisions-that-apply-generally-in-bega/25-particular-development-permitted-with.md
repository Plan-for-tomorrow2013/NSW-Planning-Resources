# 88 Particular development permitted with consent

**(1)**
Notwithstanding any other provision of this plan, a person may, with development consent or (if specified) without development consent carry out development on land referred to in Schedule 4 if the development is specified in relation to that land in that Schedule, but only subject to such conditions, if any, as are so specified.

**(2)**
Subclause (1) does not affect the application, to or in respect of development to which that subclause applies, of such of the provisions of this plan as are not inconsistent with that subclause or with a consent granted in respect of the development.
