# 6.19 Development at Hill Street and Forest Road, Hurstville

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land in Hurstville—
- (a) Lot A, DP 421265, 1A Hill Street,
- (b) Lot B, DP 421265, 105 Forest Road.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on land to which this clause applies is 75m.

**(3)**
Despite clauses 4.4 and 4.4B, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land is 6.4:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 1,674m2 of the total gross floor area of all buildings on the land will be used for the purposes of affordable housing for at least 15 years from the date of the issue of the occupation certificate.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 31 August 2026.
