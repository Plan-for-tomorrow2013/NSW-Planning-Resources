# 6.12 Gross floor area in Willoughby Market Gardens

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 1" on the *Special Provisions Area Map* and known as the Willoughby Market Gardens.

**(2)**
The sum of the gross floor area of all the dwellings on the land must not exceed 16,000m2.
