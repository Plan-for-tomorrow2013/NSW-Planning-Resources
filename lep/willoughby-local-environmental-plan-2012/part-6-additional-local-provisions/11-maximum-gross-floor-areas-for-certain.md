# 6.11 Maximum gross floor areas for certain dual occupancies

Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of dual occupancies on land identified as "Area 1" on the *Dual Occupancy Restriction Map* if the gross floor area of more than one of the dwellings exceeds 60m2.
