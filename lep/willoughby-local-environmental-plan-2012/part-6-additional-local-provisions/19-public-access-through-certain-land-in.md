# 6.19 Public access through certain land in St Leonards

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 2" on the *Special Provisions Area Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that there will be public access—
- (a) through the land linking Chandos Street to the Talus Street Reserve, and
- (b) from Evans Lane to Herbert Street.
