# 6.16 Minimum lot sizes for commercial and mixed use development in Chatswood CBD

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 8" on the *Special Provisions Area Map*.

**(2)**
The minimum lot size for development for the purposes of commercial premises on land to which this clause applies in Zone E2 Commercial Centre is 1,800m2.

**(3)**
The minimum lot size for development for the purposes of mixed use development on land to which this clause applies in Zone MU1 Mixed Use is 1,200m2.

**(4)**
Subclause (3) does not apply to the following land in Chatswood—
- (a) Lot 20, DP 1107551, 58 Anderson Street,
- (b) SP 2715, 3 Ellis Street,
- (c) Lots 9-14, DP 4138, 629 and 637-639 Pacific Highway.
