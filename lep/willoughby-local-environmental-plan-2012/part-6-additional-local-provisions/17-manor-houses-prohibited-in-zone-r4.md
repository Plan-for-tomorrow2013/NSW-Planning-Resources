# 6.17 Manor houses prohibited in Zone R4

**(1)**
Development for the purposes of manor houses is prohibited in Zone R4 High Density Residential.

**(2)**
In this clause—
*manor house* has the same meaning as in *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*.
