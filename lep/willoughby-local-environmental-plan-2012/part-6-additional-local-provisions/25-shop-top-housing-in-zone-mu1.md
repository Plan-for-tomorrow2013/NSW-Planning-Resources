# 6.25 Shop top housing in Zone MU1

**(1)**
This clause applies to land—
- (a) other than land identified as "Crows Nest Transport Oriented Development Precinct" on the *Key Sites Map*, and
- (b) in Zone MU1 Mixed Use.

**(2)**
Development consent for the purposes of shop top housing must not be granted unless the consent authority is satisfied at least 17% of the gross floor area of the building will be used for non-residential purposes.
