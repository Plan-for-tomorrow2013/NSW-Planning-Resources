# 6.27 Crows Nest Transport Oriented Development Precinct Design Guide

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Crows Nest Transport Oriented Development Precinct" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will be consistent with the *Crows Nest Transport Oriented Development Precinct Design Guide* published by the Department in November 2024.
