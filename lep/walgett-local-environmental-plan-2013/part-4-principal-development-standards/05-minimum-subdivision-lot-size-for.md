# 4.1C Minimum subdivision lot size for certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 57, DP 705026, being land at Wooloroo Road, Lightning Ridge.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.1, development consent may be granted to subdivide the land to which this clause applies to create 2 lots if each of the resulting lots will be at least 2,500 square metres.

**(3)**
Development consent may be granted to development for the purpose of a dwelling house on each lot created under this clause.
