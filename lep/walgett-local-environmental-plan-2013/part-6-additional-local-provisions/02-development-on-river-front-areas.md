# 6.3 Development on river front areas

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to protect and improve the bed and bank stability of the Barwon and Namoi Rivers,
- (b) to maintain and improve the water quality of the Barwon and Namoi Rivers,
- (c) to protect the amenity, scenic landscape values and cultural heritage of the Barwon and Namoi Rivers and to protect public access to their riverine corridors,
- (d) to conserve and protect the riverine corridors of the Barwon and Namoi Rivers, including wildlife habitat.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development consent may only be granted to development on land in a river front area for the following purposes—
- (a) boat building and repair facilities, boat launching ramps, boat sheds, charter and tourism boating facilities or marinas,
- (b) the extension or alteration of an existing building that is wholly or partly in the river front area, but only if the extension or alteration is to be located no closer to the river bank than the existing building,
- (c) environmental protection works,
- (d) environmental facilities, recreation areas and recreation facilities (outdoors),
- (e) water recreation structures,
- (f) intensive livestock agriculture and intensive plant agriculture.

**(3)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development for the purpose of extensive agriculture is permitted on land in a river front area without development consent.

**(4)**
Before granting development consent under subclause (2), the consent authority must be satisfied that—
- (a) the appearance of the development, from both the river concerned and the river front area, will be compatible with the surrounding area, and
- (b) the development is not likely to cause environmental harm, including (but not limited to) the following—
  - (i) pollution or siltation of the river concerned,
  - (ii) any adverse effect on surrounding uses, riverine habitat, wetland areas or flora or fauna habitats,
  - (iii) any adverse effect on drainage patterns, and
- (c) the development is likely to cause only minimal visual disturbance to the existing landscape, and
- (d) continuous public access, and opportunities to provide continuous public access, along the river front and to the river concerned are not likely to be compromised, and
- (e) any historic, scientific, cultural, social, archaeological, architectural, natural or aesthetic significance of the land on which the development is to be carried out and of surrounding land is to be maintained.

**(5)**
In this clause, *river front area* means land in Zone RU1 Primary Production that is within 100 metres of the top of the bank of the Barwon or Namoi Rivers.
