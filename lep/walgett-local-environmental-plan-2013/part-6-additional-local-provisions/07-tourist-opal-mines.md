# 6.8 Tourist opal mines

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to permit tourist opal mines on certain land.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Crown Reserve 1024168, being land identified as "Mining" on the *Land Zoning Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purpose of a tourist opal mine on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the development—
- (a) will have adequate vehicular access and car parking, and
- (b) will have above ground structures that do not exceed 200 square metres, and
- (c) will not cause significant conflict with adjoining opal mines.

**(4)**
In this clause, *tourist opal mine* means a place comprising rooms, access ways and associated structures that provides information or education to visitors, or that is located partly below the natural ground level, created by mining or excavation methods.
