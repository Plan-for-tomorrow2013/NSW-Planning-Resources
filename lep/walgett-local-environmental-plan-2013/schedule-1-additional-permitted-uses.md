# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2A" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of hardware and building supplies and light industries is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2B" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of backpackers' accommodation and a pub is permitted with development consent.

## 3 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2C" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a pub is permitted with development consent.

## 4 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2D", "2F", "2G", "2H", "2J", "2O", "2P", "2Q" and "2S" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of an information and education facility is permitted with development consent.

## 5 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2E" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a neighbourhood shop is permitted with development consent.

## 6 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2I" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a function centre and an information and education facility is permitted with development consent.

## 7 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2K" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a recreation facility (outdoor) and a registered club is permitted with development consent.

## 8 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2L" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a depot and office premises is permitted with development consent.

## 9 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2M" and "2N" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of an emergency services facility is permitted with development consent.

## 10 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2R" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a depot is permitted with development consent.

## 11 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2T" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of industrial training facilities is permitted with development consent.

## 12 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to the land coloured green on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a dwelling house on each residential mineral claim is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**
In this clause—
*residential mineral claim* means land to which—
- (a) a mining lease under Part 5 of the *Mining Act 1992* applies, or
- (b) a mineral claim under Part 9 of the *Mining Act 1992* applies, or
- (c) a Western lands lease within the meaning of Schedule 3 to the *Crown Land Management Act 2016* applies.

## 13 Use of certain land at Lightning Ridge

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "2U" and coloured red on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of an information and education facility (being an observatory) is permitted with development consent.
