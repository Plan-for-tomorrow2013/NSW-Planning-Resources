# 6.7 Dual occupancies (detached) in Zone RU1 and Zone RU4

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to permit dual occupancies (detached) within Zone RU1 Primary Production or Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots, provided they are within close proximity of an existing dwelling house, are on the same title and share the same vehicular access.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land within Zone RU1 Primary Production or Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a dual occupancy (detached) on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the dual occupancy (detached)—
- (a) is located within 200 metres of an existing dwelling house, and
- (b) is on the same title and is to remain on the same title as that existing dwelling house, and
- (c) shares the same vehicular access as that existing dwelling house, and
- (d) would not occupy land currently being used for agricultural purposes.
