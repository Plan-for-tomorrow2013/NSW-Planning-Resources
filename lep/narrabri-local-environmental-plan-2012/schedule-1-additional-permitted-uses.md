# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at 262 Caloola Road, Narrabri

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 262 Caloola Road, Narrabri, being Lots 102 and 106, DP 755475.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of hotel or motel accommodation is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at 96 Old Gunnedah Road, Narrabri

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 96 Old Gunnedah Road, Narrabri, being Lot 100, DP 1161967.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of hotel or motel accommodation is permitted with development consent.

## 3 Use of certain land at 1216 Braymont Road, Boggabri

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 1216 Braymont Road, Boggabri, being part of Lot 1, DP 1145592, shown as "Item 3" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of heavy industry is permitted with development consent.

## 4 Use of land at 11553 Newell Highway, Narrabri

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Lot 217, DP 41546, 11553 Newell Highway, Narrabri.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of highway service centres is permitted with development consent.
