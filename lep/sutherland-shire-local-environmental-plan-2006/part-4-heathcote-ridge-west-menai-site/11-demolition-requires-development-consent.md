# 69 Demolition requires development consent

The demolition of a building or work may be carried out only with development consent.

> Note: If the demolition of a building or work is identified in an applicable environmental planning instrument, such as this Part or *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*, as exempt development, the Act enables it to be carried out without development consent.
