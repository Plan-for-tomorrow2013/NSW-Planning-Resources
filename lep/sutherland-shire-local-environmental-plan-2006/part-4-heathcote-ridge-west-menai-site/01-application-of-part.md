# 59 Application of Part

**(1)**
This Part applies to the land identified on the *Land Application Map*, referred to in this Part as the *Heathcote Ridge West Menai site*.

**(2)**
No other provision of this plan (other than clauses 5 (2) and 6) applies to land within the Heathcote Ridge West Menai site.
