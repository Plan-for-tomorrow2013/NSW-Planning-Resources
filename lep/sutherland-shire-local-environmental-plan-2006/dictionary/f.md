# Dictionary — F

### fence

*fence* means a dividing structure of natural or other materials that extends along, or is setback from, a boundary, whether or not continuously, including components such as posts, panels, masonry, railings and any foundation necessary to support the structure.

### ferry operations

*ferry operations* means a regular, scheduled ferry service for conveying passengers, vehicles or animals across water.

### flood planning land

*flood planning land* means land that is shown as "Flood planning area" on the Flood Planning Map.

### Flood Planning Map

*Flood Planning Map* means the map marked "*Sutherland Shire Local Environmental Plan 2006 Flood Planning Map (Amendment No 12)*", as amended by the maps (or the specified sheets of maps) marked as follows—
> Note: The amending maps are not necessarily listed in the order of publication on the NSW legislation website. Information about the order of publication can be determined by referring to the Historical notes at the end of the plan.
- *Sutherland Shire Local Environmental Plan 2006 (Amendment No 14)—Flood Planning Map*

### floor space ratio

*floor space ratio*—see clause 35.

### food shop

*food shop* means a building or place having as its principal purpose the preparation and retail of food or refreshments for consumption off the premises and does not involve a drive-through service.

### foreshore building line

*foreshore building line*—see clause 17.

### foreshore land

*foreshore land* means land that is between a foreshore building line and any waterway or waterfront reserve in respect of which the building line is fixed.
