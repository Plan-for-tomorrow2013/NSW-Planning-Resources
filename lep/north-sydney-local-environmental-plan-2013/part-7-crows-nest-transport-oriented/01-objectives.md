# 7.1 Objectives

The objectives of this part are to—
- (a) increase housing supply in the Crows Nest Transport Oriented Development Precinct, and
- (b) enable a variety of land uses within walking distance of the St Leonards train and Crows Nest metro stations, and
- (c) deliver housing supported by public spaces, vibrancy and community amenity, and
- (d) increase the amount of affordable housing in the Precinct.
