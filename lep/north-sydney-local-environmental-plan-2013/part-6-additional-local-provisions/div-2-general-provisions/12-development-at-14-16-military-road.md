# 6.16 Development at 14-16 Military Road, Neutral Bay

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 14-16 Military Road, Neutral Bay, being Lot 1, DP 191827 and Lot F, DP 441071.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development for the purposes of a driveway, car parking, landscaping and an acoustic wall used in relation to a service station is permitted with development consent.
