# 6.19D Development at 270-272 Pacific Highway, Crows Nest

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 1" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a floor space ratio of up to 6.02:1 if the consent authority is satisfied that the gross floor area equal to the amount by which the floor space ratio of the building exceeds the maximum floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*—
- (a) will be located below ground level (finished), and
- (b) will not be used for the purposes of the following—
  - (i) residential accommodation,
  - (ii) retail premises, other than neighbourhood shops.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4A, the non-residential floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies must not be less than 1:1.

**(4)**
Subclause (3) ceases to have effect on 30 June 2026.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*non-residential floor space ratio* has the same meaning as in clause 4.4A.
