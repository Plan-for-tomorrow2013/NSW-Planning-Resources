# 6.21 Development at 153 and 157 Walker Street, North Sydney

**(1)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of shop top housing on the following land in North Sydney—
- (a) Lot 1, DP 89983, 153 Walker Street,
- (b) Lot 1, DP 84729, 157 Walker Street.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that 3% of the total number of dwellings resulting from the development will be used for affordable housing for at least 15 years after the date on which the occupation certificate is issued.

**(3)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 10 July 2026.
