# 6.17 Development at 200 Miller Street, North Sydney

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 200 Miller Street, North Sydney, being Lot 3, DP 1101874.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development for the purposes of a driveway used in relation to a car park, place of public worship and ancillary residential accommodation at 34 McLaren Street, North Sydney, is permitted with development consent.
