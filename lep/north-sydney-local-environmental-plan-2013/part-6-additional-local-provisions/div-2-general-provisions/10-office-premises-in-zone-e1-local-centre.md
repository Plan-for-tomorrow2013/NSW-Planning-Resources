# 6.14 Office premises in Zone E1 Local Centre

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to permit additional compatible uses within Zone E1 Local Centre but only if that use does not impact on the activation of street frontages.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purposes of office premises on land in Zone E1 Local Centre unless the consent authority is satisfied that the office premises will be located above ground floor level or away from the primary street frontage at ground level of the building in which the office premises are located.
