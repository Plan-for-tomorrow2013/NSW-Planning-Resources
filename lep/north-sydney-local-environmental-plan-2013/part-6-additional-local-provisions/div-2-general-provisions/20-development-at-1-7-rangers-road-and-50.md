# 6.20 Development at 1-7 Rangers Road and 50 Yeo Street, Neutral Bay

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lots 1-3, DP 1091373, 1-7 Rangers Road and 50 Yeo Street, Neutral Bay.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to—
- (a) for a building on land identified as "T2" on the Height of Buildings Map—31m, or
- (b) for a building on land identified as "U2" on the Height of Buildings Map—44m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4A, a building on land identified as "S4" on the Non-Residential Floor Space Ratio Map may have a non-residential floor space ratio of 1.5:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 5% of the sum of the gross floor area of all buildings used for the purposes of residential accommodation will be used as affordable housing for at least 15 years.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 29 May 2026.

**(6)**
In this clause—
*non-residential floor space ratio* has the same meaning as in clause 4.4A.
