# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at 25 Ranken Street, Cowra

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 25 Ranken Street, Cowra, being Lot 1, DP 872743, identified as "DP 872743" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a dwelling house is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at 31 Rankin Street, Woodstock

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 31 Rankin Street, Woodstock, being Lots 5 and 6, Section 10, DP 759112, identified as "DP 759112" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of an animal boarding or training establishment is permitted with development consent.
