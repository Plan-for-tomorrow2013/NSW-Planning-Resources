# 4.1D Subdivision of certain land at or near Millthorpe

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land—
- (a) land identified as "Area C" or "Area D" on the *Lot Size Map*,
- (b) Lot 135, DP 750360, 78 Clover Ridge Road, Millthorpe.

**(2)**
The minimum lot size for land identified as "Area C" or "Area D" on the *Lot Size Map* is 4,000m2 if the consent authority is satisfied the land is, or will be, serviced by a sewage reticulation system and a water reticulation system.

**(3)**
The maximum number of lots resulting from subdivision of the following land is—
- (a) for land identified as "Area D" on the *Lot Size Map*—6 lots, and
- (b) for Lot 135, DP 750360, 78 Clover Ridge Road, Millthorpe—5 lots.

**(4)**
A lot created under subclause (3) must not be further subdivided.
