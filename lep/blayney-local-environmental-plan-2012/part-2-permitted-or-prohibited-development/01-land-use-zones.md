RU5 Village
- Residential Zones
R1 General Residential
R5 Large Lot Residential
- Employment Zones
E1 Local Centre
E3 Productivity Support
E4 General Industrial
- Special Purpose Zones
SP2 Infrastructure
- Recreation Zones
RE1 Public Recreation
- Conservation Zones
C2 Environmental Conservation
