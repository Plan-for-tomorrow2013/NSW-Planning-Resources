# 4.2C Rural workers' dwellings and secondary dwellings

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to ensure the provision of adequate accommodation for employees of existing agricultural or rural industries, and
- (b) to ensure that the erection of rural workers' dwellings and secondary dwellings does not impair agricultural or rural uses of the land.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
- (b) Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of rural workers' dwellings or secondary dwellings on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will not impair the use of the land for agricultural or rural industries.
