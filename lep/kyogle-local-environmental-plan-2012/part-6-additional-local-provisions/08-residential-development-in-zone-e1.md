# 6.9 Residential development in Zone E1

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to minimise land use conflicts between residential development and commercial uses in the local centre.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development for the following purposes on land in Zone E1 Local Centre—
- (a) attached dwellings,
- (b) boarding houses,
- (c) multi dwelling housing,
- (d) residential flat buildings,
- (e) shop top housing.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied of the following—
- (a) the development will not adversely affect existing commercial premises,
- (b) the resulting residential accommodation will not be adversely affected by noise or other emissions associated with existing land uses on the site and surrounding land,
- (c) the development will not result in a decrease in the gross floor area of commercial premises in a building on the land on which the development is carried out.

**(4)**
Subclause (3)(c) does not apply to development for the purposes of shop top housing.
