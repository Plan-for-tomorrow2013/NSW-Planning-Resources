# Schedule 2 Exempt development

*(Clause 3.1)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies exempt development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional exempt development not specified in that Policy.

> Note: Exempt development may be carried out without the need for development consent under the Act. Such development is not exempt from any approval, licence, permit or authority that is required under any other Act and adjoining owners' property rights and the common law still apply.

## Signage

**(1) For the sale of motor vehicles, trailers, farm machinery and motor cycles**
- (a) Must be flush to the item for sale.
- (b) Maximum area—0.75m2.
- (c) The item must not be on public land for the purposes of displaying the sign.
- (d) Only 1 sign per premises.

**(2), (3)** (Repealed)

**(4) A-frame signs**
- (a) Must only be displayed in conjunction with a lawful commercial land use of the land during the normal hours of operation.
- (b) Must refer to the premises or goods and services that are available from the premises.
- (c) Maximum area—0.9m × 0.6m.
- (d) Maximum—1 sign per premises.
- (e) Must be non-reflective, non-iridescent and non-illuminated.

**(5), (6)** (Repealed)

## Temporary use for retail and exhibition of regional goods

**(1)**
Must be on land identified as "Active street frontage" on the *Active Street Frontages Map*.

**(2)**
Must be for the retail or exhibition of the following—
- (a) agricultural produce,
- (b) packaged food and drink products produced principally in the Kyogle local government area,
- (c) art and craft products,
- (d) community and tourism services.

**(3)**
Must be located within an existing building.

**(4)**
Must not involve building works.

**(5)**
Must only be open to the public between the following hours—
- (a) Monday to Friday—8am-6pm,
- (b) Saturday—8am-4pm,
- (c) Sunday—9am-1pm.

**(6)**
Must not be for more than 12 consecutive months.
