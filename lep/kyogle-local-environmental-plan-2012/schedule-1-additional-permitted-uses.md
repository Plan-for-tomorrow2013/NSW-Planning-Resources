# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Temporary workers' accommodation in Zones RU1 and RE2

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
- (b) (Repealed)
- (c) Zone RE2 Private Recreation.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of temporary workers' accommodation (within the meaning of clause 6.6) is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at 4-8 Craig Street, Kyogle

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 379688, 4-8 Craig Street, Kyogle.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of general industry (limited to the manufacture of pre-cast concrete products) is permitted with development consent.
