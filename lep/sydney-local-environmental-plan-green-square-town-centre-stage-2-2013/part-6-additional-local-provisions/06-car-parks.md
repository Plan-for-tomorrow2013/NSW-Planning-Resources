# 6.7 Car parks

Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of a car park unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the development will not encourage persons to travel in private vehicles between 7.00 am and 9.30 am or between 3.30 pm and 7.00 pm on weekdays, and
- (b) there will be restrictions on the hours of opening and the amount of time a vehicle may remain in the car park, and
- (c) the development will not have an adverse impact on road congestion, in particular the movement of public transport on roads, and
- (d) if located on land on which there is not an existing car park—the development will primarily serve nearby community facilities, entertainment facilities, information and education facilities, recreation facilities (major), recreation facilities (outdoor) or retail premises that will not be adequately serviced by public transport or existing public car parking.
