# 4.3A Exceptions to height of buildings

Despite clause 4.3, development consent may be granted to a building on land having a height exceeding the height shown for the land on the *Height of Buildings Map* by not more than 3 metres if the consent authority is satisfied that the additional building height—
- (a) is associated with at least one of the following—
  - (i) electricity generating works,
  - (ii) a public utility undertaking,
  - (iii) a resource recovery facility,
  - (iv) a waste or resource transfer station, and
- (b) will cause minimal overshadowing, and
- (c) does not include floor space area and is not reasonably capable of modification to include floor space area.
