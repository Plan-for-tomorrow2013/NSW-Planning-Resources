# 8.4 Affordable housing

Development consent must not be granted to development involving the erection of residential accommodation unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) at least 15% of the residential floor area of all buildings on the land will be used for the purposes of affordable housing, and
- (b) for at least 15 years after the issue of the occupation certificate—
  - (i) the accommodation proposed to be used for affordable housing will be used for affordable housing, and
  - (ii) the accommodation that is used for affordable housing will be managed by a registered community housing provider, and
- (c) before the date of the issue of the occupation certificate, a restriction will be registered against the title of the land in accordance with the *Conveyancing Act 1919*, section 88E that will ensure the requirements in paragraph (b) are met.
