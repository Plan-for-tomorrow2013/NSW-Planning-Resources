# 4.4B Exceptions to floor space ratio—town centres and village centres

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to encourage lot consolidation of smaller lots to—
  - (i) reduce the possibility of fragmented development, and
  - (ii) encourage shared facilities, and
  - (iii) manage traffic movements,
- (b) to facilitate development, where appropriate, that contributes to economic growth.

**(2)**
A building on land identified as "Canton Beach Local Centre", "The Entrance Local Centre", "Toukley Local Centre" or "Wyong Local Centre" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* with a site area specified in Column 1 of the table to this subclause may exceed the maximum floor space ratio shown on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* by the percentage shown in Column 2.
| Site area | Percentage of maximum FSR |
|---|---|
| 1,500m2 or more, but less than 2,000m2 | 7.5% |
| 2,000m2 or more, but less than 2,500m2 | 10% |
| 2,500m2 or more, but less than 3,000m2 | 12.5% |
| 3,000m2 or more, but less than 4,000m2 | 15% |
| 4,000m2 or more | 20% |

**(3)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on land identified as "Terrigal Village Centre" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* is 1.8:1 if the building has a street frontage of at least 20m as identified on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(4)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on land identified as "Woy Woy Town Centre" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*is—
- (a) 2.1:1 if—
  - (i) the site area is at least 1,000m2, but less than 1,500m2, and
  - (ii) the building has a street frontage of at least 20m as identified on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*, and
- (b) 2.3:1 if—
  - (i) the site area is at least 1,500m2, and
  - (ii) the building has a street frontage of at least 20m as identified on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(5)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on land identified as "Umina Village Centre" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*is 1.8:1 if—
- (a) the site area is at least 1,000m2, and
- (b) the building has a street frontage of at least 20m as identified on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(6)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on land identified as "Ettalong Village Centre" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* is 2:1 if—
- (a) the site area is at least 1,000m2, and
- (b) the building has a street frontage of at least 20m as identified on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(7)**
The maximum floor space ratio for a building on land identified as "Erina Town Centre" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*is 1.3:1 if—
- (a) the site area is at least 3,000m2, and
- (b) the building has a street frontage of at least 40m as identified on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.
