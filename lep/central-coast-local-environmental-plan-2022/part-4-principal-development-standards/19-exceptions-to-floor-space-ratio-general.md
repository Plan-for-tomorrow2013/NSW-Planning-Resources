# 4.4A Exceptions to floor space ratio—general

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to encourage the consolidation of smaller lots to—
  - (i) reduce the possibility of fragmented development, and
  - (ii) encourage shared facilities, and
  - (iii) manage traffic movements,
- (b) to provide incentives for the development of health-related facilities for land identified as "Kanwal Enterprise Zone" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*,
- (c) to promote the provision of affordable housing,
- (d) to encourage lot consolidation and new development forms in Zone R1 General Residential with car parking below ground level.

**(2)**
The floor space ratio for a building on any land may exceed the ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* by 0.1:1 if—
- (a) at least 75% of the dwellings in the building will be used for the purposes of affordable housing, and
- (b) the dwellings used for the purposes of affordable housing have a gross floor area of—
  - (i) for a studio—at least 35m2 but no more than 36.75m2, and
  - (ii) for a dwelling with 1 bedroom—at least 50m2 but no more than 52.5m2, and
  - (iii) for a dwelling with 2 bedrooms—at least 70m2 but no more than 73.5m2, and
  - (iv) for a dwelling with 3 or more bedrooms—at least 95m2 but no more than 99.75m2.

**(3)**
A maximum floor space ratio of 2:1 applies to buildings on land identified as "Kanwal Enterprise Zone" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* if—
- (a) the site area is at least 2,000m2, and
- (b) the building is used for—
  - (i) the purposes of health services facilities, or
  - (ii) another land use that is permissible in the zone if the consent authority is satisfied the land use is complementary to health services facilities.

**(4)**
The maximum floor space ratio of a building on land identified as "Area 1" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* is—
- (a) 0.7:1, if all of the following applies—
  - (i) the building is to be used for the purposes of a residential flat building or multi dwelling housing,
  - (ii) all on-site parking is located in the basement of the building,
  - (iii) the site area is at least 1,000m2, or
- (b) 0.6:1, if all of the following applies—
  - (i) the building is to be used for the purposes of a residential flat building or multi dwelling housing,
  - (ii) all on-site parking is located in the basement of the building,
  - (iii) the site area is less than 1,000m2.

**(5)**
The maximum floor space ratio of a building on land identified as "Area 2" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* is—
- (a) 0.85:1, if all of the following applies—
  - (i) the building is to be used for the purposes of a residential flat building or multi dwelling housing,
  - (ii) all on-site parking is located in the basement of the building,
  - (iii) the site area is at least 1,000m2, or
- (b) 0.7:1, if all of the following applies—
  - (i) the building is to be used for the purposes of a residential flat building or multi dwelling housing,
  - (ii) all on-site parking is located in the basement of the building,
  - (iii) the site area is less than 1,000m2, or
- (c) 0.6:1, if subclause (a) or (b) do not apply to the building.

**(6)**
The maximum floor space ratio of a building on land identified as "The Esplanade" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map* is 1.75:1 if the site area is at least 3,000m2.
