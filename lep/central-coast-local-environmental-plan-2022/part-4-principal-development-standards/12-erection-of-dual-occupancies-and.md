# 4.2B Erection of dual occupancies and dwelling houses on land in certain rural and conservation zones

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to enable development for the purposes of dwelling houses or dual occupancies—
- (a) on land on which the erection of a dwelling house or dual occupancy was permissible before the commencement of this Plan, and
- (b) in other specified circumstances.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
- (b) Zone RU2 Rural Landscape,
- (c) Zone RU6 Transition,
- (d) Zone C3 Environmental Management.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for the erection of a dwelling house or dual occupancy on land to which this clause applies unless the land—
- (a) is a lot created before this Plan commenced and on which the erection of a dwelling house or dual occupancy was permissible immediately before the commencement of this Plan, or
- (b) is a lot that is at least the minimum lot size shown on the *Lot Size Map*, or
- (c) is a lot otherwise created in accordance with this Plan, or
- (d) would have been a lot referred to in paragraph (b) had it not been affected by—
  - (i) a minor realignment of its boundaries that did not create an additional lot, or
  - (ii) a subdivision creating or widening a public road or public reserve or for another public purpose, or
- (e) is a lot to which the following applies—
  - (i) the lot is within an area identified as "Lot Amalgamation" on the *Lot Amalgamation Map*,
  - (ii) the lot has been amalgamated with all lots in the identified area,
  - (iii) development for the purposes of dual occupancies or dwelling houses was permissible on all the lots in the identified area immediately before this Plan commenced.
