# 4.3B Exceptions to height of buildings—Terrigal Village Centre

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to establish the maximum height limit for certain buildings in the Terrigal Village Centre.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the land identified as "Terrigal Village Centre" on the *Height of Buildings Map*.

**(3)**
The maximum height of a building on land to which this clause applies is 2.75m less than the maximum height shown for the land on the *Height of Buildings Map* if—
- (a) the site area is less than 2,000m2, and
- (b) the building has a street frontage as identified on the *Height of Buildings Map* of less than 20m.

**(4)**
The maximum height of a building on land to which this clause applies is 2.75m more than the maximum height shown for the land on the *Height of Buildings Map* if—
- (a) the site area is at least 2,000m2, and
- (b) the building has a street frontage as identified on the *Height of Buildings Map* of at least 20m, and
- (c) the consent authority is satisfied the development will include at least 1 publicly accessible outdoor space facing a street or lane that—
  - (i) strengthens retail activity and contributes to the accessibility of existing pedestrian spaces, and
  - (ii) includes—
    - (A) open alleyways that link existing laneways or streets, or
    - (B) open courtyards that receive satisfactory levels of mid-winter sunlight.

**(5)**
If subclause (4) applies to a building, the gross floor area of the part of the building that exceeds the maximum height shown on the *Height of Buildings Map* may increase the gross floor area of the building by a maximum of 25% of the site area.
