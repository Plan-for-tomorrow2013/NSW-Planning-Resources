# 7.19 Subdivision at Goldsmith Road and Tallara Road, Somersby

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at Somersby—
- (a) Lot 416, DP 1265173, Goldsmith Road, identified as "Goldsmith Road" on the *Key Sites Map*,
- (b) Lot 4, DP 261507, Tallara Road, identified as "Tallara Road" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Land to which this clause applies may be subdivided with development consent—
- (a) for land identified as "Goldsmith Road"—to create 3 or fewer lots, and
- (b) for land identified as "Tallara Road"—to create 2 lots.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted unless the consent authority is satisfied the development—
- (a) will retain, and be complementary to, the biodiversity values of the land, and
- (b) will not have a significant adverse effect on threatened species or endangered ecological communities or their habitats, and
- (c) will not involve, or will involve only minimal, clearing of native vegetation, and
- (d) will not result in other significant adverse environmental impacts on the land or adjoining land.

**(4)**
In this clause—
*biodiversity values* has the same meaning as in the *Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016*.
