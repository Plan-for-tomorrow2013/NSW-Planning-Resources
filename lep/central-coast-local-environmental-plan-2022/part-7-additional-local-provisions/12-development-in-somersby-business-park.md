# 7.12 Development in Somersby Business Park

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Somersby Business Park" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the development will maximise the opportunity for industrial development in Somersby Business Park to provide employment opportunities in the Central Coast, and
- (b) the ecological and Aboriginal heritage characteristics of land in Somersby Business Park will be protected and appropriately managed, and
- (c) the development is consistent with the *DRAFT Plan of Management— Somersby Industrial Park* dated June 2005 and published on the Council's website.

**(3)**
In deciding whether to grant development consent to the subdivision of land to which this clause applies, the consent authority must consider the following—
- (a) whether the proposed subdivision relates to land identified as "Ecologically Significant and Aboriginal Heritage Lands" on the *Key Sites Map*,
- (b) the potential for the lots created from the proposed subdivision (the *resulting lots*) to accommodate future development that will not have an adverse impact on the protection of the ecological and Aboriginal heritage characteristics of the locality, having regard to—
  - (i) the size of the resulting lots, and
  - (ii) the configuration of the resulting lots, and
  - (iii) the topography of the land of the resulting lots.
