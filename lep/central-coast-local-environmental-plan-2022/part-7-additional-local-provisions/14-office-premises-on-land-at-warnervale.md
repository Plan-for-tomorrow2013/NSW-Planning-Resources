# 7.14 Office premises on land at Warnervale

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to protect the viability of the Warnervale town centre, and
- (b) to encourage a mix of uses in the town centre.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Warnervale" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purposes of stand-alone office premises on land to which this clause applies unless the gross floor area of the building resulting from the development is not more than 30% of the total area of the land to which this clause applies.

**(4)**
In this clause—
*stand-alone office premises* means a building predominantly used for the purposes of office premises.
