# 7.15 Office premises and specialised retail premises on land at Kanwal

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to protect the viability of existing and planned town centres, and
- (b) to provide incentives for the development of health services facilities.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land at Kanwal identified as "Kanwal Enterprise Zone" on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purposes of stand-alone office premises on land to which this clause applies unless the gross floor area of the building resulting from the development is not more than 30% of the total area of the land to which this clause applies.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purposes of specialised retail premises on land to which this clause applies if the gross floor area of the building resulting from the development is more than 1,000m2.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*stand-alone office premises*—see clause 7.14(4).
