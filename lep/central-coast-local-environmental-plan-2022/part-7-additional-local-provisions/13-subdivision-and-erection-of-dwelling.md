# 7.13 Subdivision and erection of dwelling house on certain land at Wisemans Ferry Road, Somersby

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are to—
- (a) ensure that lot sizes and subdivision design conserve and protect the environmental, conservation and biodiversity values of land, and
- (b) allow for flexibility in the size and arrangement of lots to facilitate appropriate development and improved environmental outcomes.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the part of Lot 41, DP 1046841 in—
- (a) Zone C2 Environmental Conservation, or
- (b) Zone C3 Environmental Management.

**(3)**
Land to which this clause applies may be subdivided to create lots smaller than the minimum lot size shown on the *Lot Size Map* if the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the total number of lots created from the subdivision will not exceed 6, and
- (b) one of the lots created will include—
  - (i) all of the land in Zone C2 Environmental Conservation, and
  - (ii) part of the land in Zone C3 Environmental Management, and
- (c) the lots created from the subdivision will be used for the purposes of environmental conservation and protection of the environment, and
- (d) the lots created from the subdivision will retain, and are complementary to, the environmental conservation and biodiversity values of the land, and
- (e) the subdivision will not result in significant adverse environmental impacts on the land or on adjoining land, and
- (f) vehicular access to Peats Ridge Road from the land will not adverse affect endangered flora on the land or on adjoining land.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted to the subdivision of a lot created under this clause unless the subdivision is for one or more of the following purposes—
- (a) a minor realignment of its boundaries that does not create an additional lot,
- (b) a subdivision creating or widening a public road or public reserve or for another public purpose,
- (c) a consolidation of adjoining lots.

**(5)**
Development consent must not be granted for the erection of a dwelling house on a lot created by a subdivision under this clause unless the dwelling house will be erected on land solely in Zone C3 Environmental Management.
