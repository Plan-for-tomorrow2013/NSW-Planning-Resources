# 7.20 Subdivision at Marana Road, Springfield

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 3, DP 1273246, Marana Road, Springfield, identified as "Marana Road" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to the subdivision of land to which this clause applies if the subdivision would result in no more than 3 lots.
