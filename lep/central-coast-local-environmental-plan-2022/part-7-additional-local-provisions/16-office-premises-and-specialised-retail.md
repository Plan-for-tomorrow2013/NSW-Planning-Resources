# 7.16 Office premises and specialised retail premises at North Wyong Industrial Estate

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to protect the viability of existing and planned town centres.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "North Wyong Industrial Estate" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purposes of stand-alone office premises on land to which this clause applies unless—
- (a) the gross floor area of the building resulting from the development is not more than 30% of the total area of the land to which this clause applies, and
- (b) the gross floor area of the building resulting from the development is at least 200m2.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purposes of specialised retail premises on land to which this clause applies unless the gross floor space of the building resulting from the development is not greater than 2,000m2.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*stand-alone office premises*—see clause 7.14(4).
