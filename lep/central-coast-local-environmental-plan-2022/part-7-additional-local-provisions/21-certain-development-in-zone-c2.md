# 7.21 Certain development in Zone C2 Environmental Conservation

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone C2 Environmental Conservation—
- (a) identified as "Dwelling Opportunity" on the *Dwelling Opportunity Map*, and
- (b) on which development for the purposes of dwelling houses was permissible immediately before the commencement of this Plan.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development for the following purposes on land to which this clause applies—
- (a) dwelling houses,
- (b) home occupations,
- (c) bed and breakfast accommodation.

**(3)**
Clauses 4.1AA, 4.2A and 4.2C apply to land to which this clause applies.
