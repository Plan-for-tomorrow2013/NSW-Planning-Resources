# 7.17 Managed resort facilities at 50 Summerland Road, Summerland Point

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "50 Summerland Road—Part A" and "50 Summerland Road—Part B" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of a managed resort facility is permitted with development consent on land to which this clause applies.

**(3)**
Development consent may be granted to subdivision under the *Community Land Development Act 2021* to create lots of any size on land identified as "50 Summerland Road—Part A" on the *Key Sites Map* if—
- (a) the land is within a managed resort facility, and
- (b) no more than 75% of the dwellings in the managed resort facility are used as permanent residences, and
- (c) the consent authority is satisfied the use of the dwellings as permanent residences will not result in the dominant use of the land to which this clause applies being for a purpose other than a managed resort facility.

**(4)**
In this clause—
*managed resort facility* means a building or place used for development—
- (a) for the purposes of tourist and visitor accommodation and associated recreation facilities (indoor) and recreation facilities (outdoor), and
- (b) for at least 1 of the following purposes—
  - (i) boat launching ramps,
  - (ii) boat sheds,
  - (iii) entertainment facilities,
  - (iv) function centres,
  - (v) marinas,
  - (vi) registered clubs,
  - (vii) residential accommodation,
  - (viii) retail premises.
