# 5.1A Development on land intended to be acquired for public purposes

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to limit development on certain land intended to be acquired for a public purpose.

**(2)**
This clause applies to the following land at Manning Road, The Entrance that has not been acquired by the relevant authority of the State specified for the land in clause 5.1—
- (a) Lots 3, 6, 8 and 11, DP 14527,
- (b) Lot 12A, DP 408523.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies other than development for the purposes of car parks.
