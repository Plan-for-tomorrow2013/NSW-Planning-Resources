# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Macqueen Street, Aberdeen

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Macqueen Street, Aberdeen, being Lots 30-34, DP 738079, in Zone R1 General Residential.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of vehicle repair stations and vehicle sales or hire premises is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land at Bunnan Road, Scone

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Bunnan Road, Scone, being Lot 42, DP 846091, in Zone SP1 Infrastructure.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of subdivision and the erection of dwelling houses is permitted with development consent if—
- (a) each lot to be created has an area of at least 4,000m2, and
- (b) the consent authority is satisfied that the development—
  - (i) will not interfere with the effective operation of the Scone Memorial Aerodrome, and
  - (ii) will include a building to store aircraft and will provide access for aircraft stored in that building to the Scone Memorial Aerodrome runway, and
- (c) the consent authority is satisfied that each dwelling house (including any outbuilding) complies with AS 2021-2000—*Acoustics—Aircraft noise intrusion—Building siting and construction*.

## 3 Use of certain land at New England Highway, Scone in Zone R1

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at New England Highway, Scone, being Lots 2-4, DP 1041230, in Zone R1 General Residential.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of veterinary hospitals is permitted with development consent.
