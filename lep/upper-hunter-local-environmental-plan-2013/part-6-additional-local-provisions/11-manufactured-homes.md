# 6.12 Manufactured homes

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in zones where development for the purposes of a dwelling house is permitted with development consent.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of the installation or placement and use of a manufactured home may be carried out with development consent.

**(3)**
The provisions of this plan that apply to the subdivision or use of land for the purposes of the erection of a dwelling house apply to the installation or placement and use of a manufactured home under this clause.

**(4)**
Despite another provision of this plan, in this plan *caravan park* does not include land on which a manufactured home is installed or placed as a dwelling house under this clause.
