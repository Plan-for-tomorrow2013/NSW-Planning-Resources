# 4.2B Erection of residential accommodation on land in certain rural and conservation zones

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to minimise unplanned rural residential development,
- (b) to enable the replacement of lawfully erected residential accommodation in rural and conservation zones.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone RU1 Primary Production,
- (b) Zone RU4 Primary Production Small Lots,
- (c) Zone C3 Environmental Management.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted for development involving the erection of residential accommodation on land to which this clause applies unless the land—
- (a) is a lot that is at least the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to that land, or
- (b) is a lot created before this Plan commenced and on which development involving the erection of residential accommodation was permissible immediately before that commencement, or
- (c) is a lot created under one of the following provisions before this Plan commenced—
  - (i) clause 12 of the *Merriwa Local Environmental Plan 1992*,
  - (ii) clause 12 of the *Murrurundi Local Environmental Plan 1993*,
  - (iii) clause 10 of the *Scone Local Environmental Plan 1986*, or
- (d) is an existing holding, or
- (e) would have been a lot or a holding referred to in paragraph (a), (b), (c) or (d) had it not been affected by—
  - (i) a minor realignment of its boundaries that did not create an additional lot, or
  - (ii) a subdivision creating or widening a public road or public reserve or for another public purpose, or
  - (iii) a consolidation with an adjoining public road or public reserve or for another public purpose.

> Note: A dwelling cannot be erected on a lot created under clause 9 of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Rural Lands) 2008* or clause 4.2.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted under subclause (3) unless—
- (a) no residential accommodation has been erected on the land, and
- (b) if a development application has been made for development for the purposes of residential accommodation on the land—the application has been refused or it was withdrawn before it was determined, and
- (c) if development consent has been granted in relation to such an application—the consent has been surrendered or it has lapsed.

**(5)**
Development consent may be granted for development involving the erection of residential accommodation on land to which this clause applies if—
- (a) there is lawfully erected residential accommodation on the land, and
- (b) the residential accommodation to be erected is intended only to replace the existing residential accommodation.

**(6)**
In this clause—
*existing holding* means land that—
- (a) was a holding on the relevant date, and
- (b) is a holding at the time the application for development consent referred to in subclause (3) is lodged,
whether or not there has been a change in the ownership of the holding since the relevant date, and includes any other land adjoining that land acquired by the owner since the relevant date.
> Note: The owner in whose ownership all the land is at the time the application is lodged need not be the same person as the owner in whose ownership all the land was on the stated date.
*holding* means all adjoining land, even if separated by a road or railway, held by the same person or persons.
*relevant date* means—
- (a) in relation to land to which *Merriwa Local Environmental Plan 1992* applied immediately before the commencement of this Plan—18 April 1975, or
- (b) in relation to land to which *Murrurundi Local Environmental Plan 1993* applied immediately before the commencement of this Plan—31 December 1991, or
- (c) in relation to land to which *Scone Local Environmental Plan 1986* applied immediately before the commencement of this Plan—15 May 1970.
