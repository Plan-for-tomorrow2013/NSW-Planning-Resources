# 7.16 Affordable housing

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following development on land identified as "Riverstone Town Centre" on the *Key Sites Map*—
- (a) the erection of a building used for the purposes of residential accommodation,
- (b) the erection of a building with more than 1,000m2 of gross floor area used for the purposes of non-residential development.

**(2)**
Despite subclause (1), this clause does not apply to the following development—
- (a) alterations or additions to existing buildings,
- (b) development for the purposes of—
  - (i) a single dwelling house,
  - (ii) boarding houses,
  - (iii) community housing,
  - (iv) group homes,
  - (v) hostels,
  - (vi) seniors housing,
  - (vii) public housing.

**(3)**
The consent authority may, when granting development consent to development to which this clause applies, impose a condition requiring a monetary contribution paid to the Council of equivalent value to—
- (a) on land zoned MU1 Mixed Use—3% of the gross floor area of the residential component of the development, or
- (b) otherwise—1.5% of the gross floor area of the residential component of the development.

**(4)**
The rate at which a monetary contribution is taken to be equivalent to floor area for the purposes of this clause must be calculated by reference to the market value of dwellings of a similar size to the dwellings in the proposed development.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*community housing* has the same meaning as in the *Community Housing Providers National Law (NSW)*.
*public housing* has the same meaning as in the *Housing Act 2001*.
