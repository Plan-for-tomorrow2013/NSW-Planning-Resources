# 4.1A Minimum subdivision lot size for strata plan schemes

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that the land to which this clause applies is not fragmented by subdivision that would be inconsistent with the prevailing character of large floor plate development.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Zone E4 General Industrial in the Huntingwood Estate identified as "Area A" on the *Lot Size Map*.

**(3)**
The size of any lot resulting from a subdivision of land to which this clause applies under the *Strata Schemes Development Act 2015* is not to be less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* in relation to that land.

**(4)**
This clause applies despite clause 4.1(4)(a).
