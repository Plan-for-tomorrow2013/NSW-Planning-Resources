# 4.3B Other exceptions to height of buildings

**(1)**
The height of a building on land that is a corner lot in Zone R3 Medium Density Residential must not exceed 10 metres.

**(1A)**
Subclause (1) does not apply to land identified as "Riverstone Town Centre" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
The height of a secondary dwelling in Zone R3 Medium Density must not exceed 5.5 metres if that dwelling—
- (a) is attached to, or separate from, the principal dwelling, and
- (b) is the furthest dwelling from the street frontage of the lot.

**(3)**
If a dual occupancy is established and one dwelling (the *rear dwelling*) that is part of the dual occupancy is further back from the street frontage of the lot than the other dwelling, the height of the rear dwelling must not exceed 5.5 metres.
