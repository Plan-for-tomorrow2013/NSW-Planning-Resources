# 1.8B Amendment of SEPPs applying to land

**(1)**
*State Environmental Planning Policy No 29—Western Sydney Recreation Area* is amended by omitting clauses 5 and 6 and by inserting instead—
Plan does not apply to land to which Blacktown LEP 2015 applies This Plan does not apply to the land to which *Blacktown Local Environmental Plan 2015* applies.

**(2)**
*State Environmental Planning Policy No 59—Central Western Sydney Regional Open Space and Residential* is amended by inserting at the end of clause 4—
Despite subclause (1), this Plan does not apply to the land to which *Blacktown Local Environmental Plan 2015* applies.
