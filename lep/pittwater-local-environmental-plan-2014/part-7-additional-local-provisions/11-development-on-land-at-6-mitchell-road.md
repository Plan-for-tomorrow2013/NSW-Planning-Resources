# 7.13 Development on land at 6 Mitchell Road, Palm Beach

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 1, DP 1086858, 6 Mitchell Road, Palm Beach.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the building will not exceed RL 74.5m.
