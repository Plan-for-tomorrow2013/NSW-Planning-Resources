# Schedule 2 Exempt development

*(Clause 3.1)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies exempt development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional exempt development not specified in that Policy.

> Note: Exempt development may be carried out without the need for development consent under the Act. Such development is not exempt from any approval, licence, permit or authority that is required under any other Act and adjoining owners' property rights and the common law still apply.

## Advertisements—general requirements

**(1)**
Must be designed and installed in a manner unlikely to cause danger or injury to persons, structures or vehicles.

**(2)**
Must be designed, located and installed to maintain normal and expected sightlines for vehicular traffic and pedestrian movements.

**(3)**
Must use vandal-resistant materials and surfaces.

## Business identification signs

**(1)**
Does not apply to land under the care or control of the Council (for example, road reserves and public reserves).

**(2)**
Does not include business identification signs for shops.

**(3)**
Must be located wholly within the property it identifies.

**(4)**
Maximum 1 awning sign per business, property or lease.

**(5)**
Maximum size—2m2.

**(6)**
Maximum height of a freestanding sign above ground level—1.8m.

**(7)**
If on a commercial building with no awning—must not extend to more than 4.6m above ground level or the first floor, whichever is lower.

**(8)**
Must not exceed 50% of frontage.

**(9)**
Maximum 1 suspended under-awning sign or projecting wall sign with maximum dimensions 2.5m long × 0.5m high located between 2.7m and 4.6m above ground level.

**(10)**
Must not alter the size of the awning.

**(11)**
Contents, design, size and location of signs must provide equitable opportunities for similar businesses in close proximity.

**(12)**
Must not create signage and advertising clutter and individual message dilution or confusion.

**(13)**
Must use minimal wording, and innovative and recognisable symbols and colours.

## Motor vehicle signs

**(1)**
Must meet the general requirements for advertisements.

**(2)**
Must not be in or on a motor vehicle parked specifically for the purposes of advertising or promotion.

## Non-commercial events or commemorations

**(1)**
Must be a single event or ceremony (not including music concerts, dance parties or special entertainment not related to a specific fundraising event) at a temporary location.

**(2)**
Must be located on a public reserve, sports ground, showground or on Crown land, community land, church ground, car parks or the like.

**(3)**
Must not be on private land.

**(4)**
Must hold relevant approvals required under section 68 of the *Local Government Act 1993*.

## Public art

Must be installed on land under the care or control of the Council.

## Public notice signs

**(1)**
Must be displayed by a public authority.

**(2)**
Must clearly communicate information or direction about services provided.

## Real estate signs

**(1)**
Must only indicate that premises, land or business is for sale or lease.

**(2)**
Must be erected on the property or land for sale or lease.

**(3)**
Must be structurally sound for the specific location and instalment method.

**(4)**
Must not be displayed for more than 10 days after sale, lease or auction.

**(5)**
Maximum size for properties being sold or let by private treaty—1.25m long × 1m high.

**(6)**
Maximum size for properties being sold or let by auction and containing only the word "Auction" on the returns—1.85m long × 1.25m high with returns of 180mm.

**(7)**
Maximum size for properties being sold or let as commercial or industrial premises—2.45m long × 1.85m high.

## Signs within a site or building

Must be a sign not visible from the public domain.

## Temporary signs

**(1)**
Must be for public or community events, festivals, announcements, celebrations.

**(2)**
Must be attached to structures that are adequate for the specific location and instalment method.

**(3)**
Must not be located on a road verge or other public land.

**(4)**
Must not include commercial advertising other than the names of the event sponsors.

**(5)**
Must not be displayed earlier than 28 days before, or later than 14 days after, the event.

**(6)**
Must not be used in relation to recurring events.
