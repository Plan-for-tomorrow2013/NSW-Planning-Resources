# 4.1A Minimum lot size for dual occupancies, multi dwelling housing and residential flat buildings in certain rural and residential zones

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to achieve planned residential density in certain zones.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted to development on a lot in a zone shown in Column 2 of the table to this clause for a purpose shown in Column 1 of the table opposite that zone, if the area of the lot is equal to or greater than the area specified for that purpose and shown in Column 3 of the table—
| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
|---|---|---|
| dual occupancy (attached) | R1 General Residential R2 Low Density Residential RU5 Village | 700 square metres |
| dual occupancy (attached) | R5 Large Lot Residential | 3,000 square metres |
| dual occupancy (detached) | R1 General Residential R2 Low Density Residential RU5 Village | 850 square metres |
| dual occupancy (detached) | R5 Large Lot Residential | 13,500 square metres |
| multi dwelling housing | R1 General Residential RU5 Village | 1,050 square metres |
| residential flat building | R1 General Residential RU5 Village | 1,050 square metres |

**(3)**
Despite subclause (2), development consent must not be granted for the purpose of a dual occupancy on a lot that is not serviced by a sewage reticulation system unless the area of the lot will not be less than—
- (a) if the lot is in Zone RU5 Village—1,700 square metres, or
- (b) if the lot is in Zone R5 Large Lot Residential—2 hectares.

**(4)**
For the purposes of this clause, if a lot is a battle-axe lot or other lot with an access handle, the area of the access handle is not to be included in calculating the lot size.

**(5)**
This clause does not apply to a growth area under Part 8.
