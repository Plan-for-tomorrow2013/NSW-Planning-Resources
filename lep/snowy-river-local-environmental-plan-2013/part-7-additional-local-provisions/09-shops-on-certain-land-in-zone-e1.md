# 7.10 Shops on certain land in Zone E1

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone E1 identified as "Area A" on the *Land Zoning Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of shops on land to which this clause applies unless the gross floor area of each shop is no more than 2,500m2.
