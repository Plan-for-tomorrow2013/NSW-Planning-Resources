# 82 Exceptions

**(1)**
Nothing in this plan prevents a person, with development consent, from carrying out development on land referred to in Schedule 6 for a purpose specified in relation to that land in that Schedule, subject to such conditions, if any, as are so specified.

**(2)**
Subclause (1) does not affect the operation of any provision within this plan that is consistent with the consent to such development.
