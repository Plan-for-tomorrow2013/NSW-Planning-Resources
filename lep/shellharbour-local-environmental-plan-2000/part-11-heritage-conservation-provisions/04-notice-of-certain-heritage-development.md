# 75 Notice of certain heritage development applications

**(1)** (Repealed)

**(2)**
Before granting consent to the demolishing or damaging of a heritage item identified in Schedule 3 as being of State significance, the consent authority must be satisfied that any necessary excavation permit required by the *Heritage Act 1977* has been granted.
