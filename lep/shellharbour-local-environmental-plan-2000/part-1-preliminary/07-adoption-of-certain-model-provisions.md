# 7 Adoption of certain Model Provisions

This plan adopts Part 1 and clauses 5, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, 24, 27, 29 and 35 of, and Schedule 1 to, the *Environmental Planning and Assessment Model Provisions 1980*.
