# 6.83 Development at Waverley Street and Palmer Street, Belmore

**(1)**
This clause applies to certain land in the Belmore and Lakemba Precinct, identified as "Area 40" on the *Special Provisions Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to the building height shown for the land on the *Incentive Height of Buildings Map*.

**(3)**
The floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies may be up to the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Incentive Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land to which this clause applies, and
- (b) the development will include an access laneway at least 6m wide that traverses Lot 35, Section 2, DP 7211, 2 Waverley Street and Lot 2, DP 1196853, 17B Palmer Street and is between—
  - (i) Waverley Street, and
  - (ii) the eastern boundary of Lot 2, DP 1196853, 17B Palmer Street, adjoining Lot 34, Section 2, DP 7211, 1 Tudor Street.
