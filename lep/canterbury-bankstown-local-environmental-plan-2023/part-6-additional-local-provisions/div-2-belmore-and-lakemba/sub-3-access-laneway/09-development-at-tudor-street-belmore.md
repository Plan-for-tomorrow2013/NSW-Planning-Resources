# 6.84 Development at Tudor Street, Belmore

**(1)**
This clause applies to certain land in the Belmore and Lakemba Precinct, identified as "Area 41" on the *Special Provisions Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to the building height shown for the land on the *Incentive Height of Buildings Map*.

**(3)**
The floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies may be up to the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Incentive Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied —
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land to which this clause applies, and
- (b) the development will include an access laneway at least 6m wide between the following land—
  - (i) the western boundary of Lot 34, Section 2, DP 7211, 1 Tudor Street adjoining Lot 2, DP 1196853, 17B Palmer Street,
  - (ii) the eastern boundary of Lot 34, Section 2, DP 7211, 1 Tudor Street adjoining Tudor Street.
