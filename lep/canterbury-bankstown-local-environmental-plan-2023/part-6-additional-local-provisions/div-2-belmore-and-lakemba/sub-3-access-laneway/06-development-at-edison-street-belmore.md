# 6.81 Development at Edison Street, Belmore

**(1)**
This clause applies to certain land in the Belmore and Lakemba Precinct, identified as "Area 38" on the *Special Provisions Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to the building height shown for the land on the *Incentive Height of Buildings Map*.

**(3)**
The floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies may be up to the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Incentive Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land to which this clause applies, and
- (b) the development will extend the existing access laneway at Edison Lane to create an access laneway at least 6m wide that traverses all land to which this clause applies and is between—
  - (i) the eastern boundary of Lot 61, DP 10105, 1 Edison Street adjoining Lot 1, DP 10105, 1 Waverley Street, and
  - (ii) the western boundary of Lot 64, DP 10105, 7 Edison Street adjoining Edison Lane.
