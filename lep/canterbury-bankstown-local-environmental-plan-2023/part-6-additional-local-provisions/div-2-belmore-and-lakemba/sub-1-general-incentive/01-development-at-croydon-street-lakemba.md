# 6.53 Development at Croydon Street, Lakemba Street and Railway Parade, Lakemba

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Lakemba, identified as "Area 10" on the *Special Provisions Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to the building height shown for the land on the *Incentive Height of Buildings Map*.

**(3)**
The floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies may be up to the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Incentive Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land to which this clause applies, and
- (b) at least 450m2 of land at Lot 1, DP 432109, 56-57 Railway Parade, will be used for the purposes of a single publicly accessible open space, and
- (c) the development will include a pedestrian link at least 4m wide between Railway Parade and the northern boundary of Lot B, DP 357959, 5-7 Croydon Street, adjoining Lot 1, DP 601048, 214 Lakemba Street, and
- (d) the development will include a pedestrian link at least 4m wide between Croydon Street and the western boundary of Lot B, DP 357959, 5-7 Croydon Street, adjoining SP 1299, 216 Lakemba Street.
