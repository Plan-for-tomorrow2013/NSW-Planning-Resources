# 6.56 Development at 26-62 Bridge Road, Belmore

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 101, DP 879064, 26-62 Bridge Road, Belmore, identified as "Area 13" on the *Special Provisions Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a height of up to the building height shown for the land on the *Incentive Height of Buildings Map*.

**(3)**
The floor space ratio of buildings on a site on land to which this clause applies may be up to the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Incentive Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land to which this clause applies, and
- (b) at least 3,000m2 of the site area of the development will be used for the purposes of a single publicly accessible open space, and
- (c) the development will include a pedestrian link at least 4m wide that—
  - (i) is between Collins Street and Gladstone Street, and
  - (ii) traverses Lot 101, DP 879064, and
- (d) the development will include a pedestrian link at least 4m wide that—
  - (i) is between Collins Street and Bridge Road, and
  - (ii) traverses Lot 101, DP 879064, and
- (e) the development will include a pedestrian link at least 4m wide that—
  - (i) is between Gladstone Street and Paragon Lane, and
  - (ii) traverses Lot 101, DP 879064.
