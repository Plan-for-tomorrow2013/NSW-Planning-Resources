# 6.95 Incentivised development in Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct

In this division, if development on land is specified to be *incentivised development*—
- (a) despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on the land is the height shown for the land on the *Incentive Height of Buildings Map*, and
- (b) despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land is the floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Incentive Floor Space Ratio Map*.
