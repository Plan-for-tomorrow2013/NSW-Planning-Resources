# 6.94 Objectives of division

The objectives of this division are to improve pedestrian connectivity through key centres by encouraging—
- (a) pedestrian connectivity and open space on identified blocks, and
- (b) the connection of development with existing and approved through-site links on adjacent blocks to provide for a connected network, and
- (c) the efficient and orderly use of land.
