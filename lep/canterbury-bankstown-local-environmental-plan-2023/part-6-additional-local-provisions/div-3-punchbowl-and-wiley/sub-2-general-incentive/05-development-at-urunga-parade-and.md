# 6.100 Development at Urunga Parade and Punchbowl Road, Punchbowl

Development on land identified as "Area 54" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 54", and
- (b) the development will result in the conservation of the heritage item on Lot 1, DP 1175212, 748 Punchbowl Road.
