# 6.99 Development at Shadforth Street, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 53" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 53", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link to create a pedestrian link at least 6m wide that—
  - (i) traverses SP 7953, 1-3 Shadforth Street and Lot 1, DP 339938, 1A Shadforth Street, and
  - (ii) is between Shadforth Street and Wiley Lane.
