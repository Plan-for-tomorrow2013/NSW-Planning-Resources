# 6.96 Development at Lakemba Street and King Georges Road, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 50" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 50", and
- (b) at least 1,500m2 of the land will be used for the purposes of a single publicly accessible open space, and
- (c) the development will include a laneway at least 9m wide that traverses along the eastern boundary of Lot A, DP 962951, 280 Lakemba Street and is between—
  - (i) Lakemba Street, and
  - (ii) the northern boundary of Lot 1, DP 124641, 76 King Georges Road.
