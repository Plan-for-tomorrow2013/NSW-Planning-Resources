# 6.101 Development at Rossmore Avenue and Arthur Street, Punchbowl

Development on land identified as "Area 55" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 55", and
- (b) at least 250m2 of the land will be used for the purposes of a single publicly accessible open space.
