# 6.98 Development at 77-89 King Georges Road, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 52" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 52", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link at Lot 1, DP 25126, 89 King Georges Road to create a pedestrian link at least 6m wide between King Georges Road and Wiley Lane.
