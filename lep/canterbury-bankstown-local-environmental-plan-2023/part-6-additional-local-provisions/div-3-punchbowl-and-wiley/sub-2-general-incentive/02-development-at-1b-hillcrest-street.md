# 6.97 Development at 1B Hillcrest Street, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 51" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) at least 1,000m2 of the land will be used for the purposes of a single publicly accessible open space, and
- (b) the development will include a pedestrian link at least 4m wide that—
  - (i) is connected to the publicly accessible open space, and
  - (ii) is between The Boulevarde and Hillcrest Street.
