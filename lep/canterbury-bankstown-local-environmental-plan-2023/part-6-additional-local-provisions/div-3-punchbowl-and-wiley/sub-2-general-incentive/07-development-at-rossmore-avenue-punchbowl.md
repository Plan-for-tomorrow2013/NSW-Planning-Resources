# 6.102 Development at Rossmore Avenue, Punchbowl

Development on land identified as "Area 56" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 56", and
- (b) at least 500m2 of the land will be used for the purposes of a single publicly accessible open space.
