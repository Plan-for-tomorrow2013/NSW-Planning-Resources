# 6.108 Development at 97-101 King Georges Road, Wiley Park

**(1)**
Development on land identified as "Area 62" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development.

**(2)**
Subclause (1) does not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link at the southern boundary of Lot 1, DP 1103978, 97-101 King Georges Road to create a pedestrian link at least 7m wide between Denman Avenue and King Georges Road.
