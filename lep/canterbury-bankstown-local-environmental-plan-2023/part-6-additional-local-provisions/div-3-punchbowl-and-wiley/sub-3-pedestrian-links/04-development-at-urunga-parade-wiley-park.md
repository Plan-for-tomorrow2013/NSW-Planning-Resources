# 6.106 Development at Urunga Parade, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 60" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 60", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link at Lot 20, DP 167333, 4B Urunga Parade to create a pedestrian link at least 7m wide that traverses Lot 1, DP 19347, 5 Urunga Parade and is between—
  - (i) Urunga Parade, and
  - (ii) the northern boundary of Lot 20, DP 167333, 4B Urunga Parade.
