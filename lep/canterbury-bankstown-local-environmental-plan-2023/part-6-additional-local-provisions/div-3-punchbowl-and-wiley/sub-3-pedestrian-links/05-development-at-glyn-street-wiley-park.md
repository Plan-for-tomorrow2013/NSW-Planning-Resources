# 6.107 Development at Glyn Street, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 61" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 61", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link between Glyn Street and Urunga Parade to create a pedestrian link at least 7m wide that traverses Lot 12B, DP 399225, 24 Glyn Street and is between—
  - (i) the southern end of Glyn Street, and
  - (ii) the northern boundary of Lot 20, DP 167333, 4B Urunga Parade.
