# 6.113 Development at Cornelia Street, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 67" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 67", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link between Defoe Street and Cornelia Street to create a pedestrian link at least 9m wide that traverses Lot 17A, DP 317272, 28 Cornelia Street and is between—
  - (i) Cornelia Street, and
  - (ii) the western boundary of Lot 17A, DP 317272, 28 Cornelia Street.
