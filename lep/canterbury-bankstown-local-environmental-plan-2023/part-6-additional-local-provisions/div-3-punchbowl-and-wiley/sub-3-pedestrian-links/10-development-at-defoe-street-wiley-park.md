# 6.112 Development at Defoe Street, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 66" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 66", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link between Defoe Street and Cornelia Street to create a pedestrian link at least 9m wide that—
  - (i) traverses Lot 5, DP 449679 and Lot 40, DP 17062, 39 Defoe Street, and
  - (ii) is between Defoe Street and the eastern boundary of Lot 5, DP 449679 and Lot 40, DP 17062, 39 Defoe Street.
