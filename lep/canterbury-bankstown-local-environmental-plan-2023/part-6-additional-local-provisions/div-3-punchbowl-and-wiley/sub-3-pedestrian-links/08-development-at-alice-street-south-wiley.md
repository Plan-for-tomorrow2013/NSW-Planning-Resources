# 6.110 Development at Alice Street South, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 64" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 64", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link at Lot 145, DP 6471 to create a pedestrian link at least 6m wide that traverses Lot 43, DP 6471, 24 Alice Street South and is between—
  - (i) Alice Street South, and
  - (ii) the eastern boundary of Lot 43, DP 6471, 24 Alice Street South.
