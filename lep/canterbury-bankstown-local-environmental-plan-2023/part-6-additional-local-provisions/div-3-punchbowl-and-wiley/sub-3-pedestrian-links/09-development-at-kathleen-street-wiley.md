# 6.111 Development at Kathleen Street, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 65" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 65", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link at Lot 145, DP 6471 to create a pedestrian link at least 6m wide that traverses Lot 65, DP 6471, 19 Kathleen Street and is between—
  - (i) Kathleen Street, and
  - (ii) the western boundary of Lot 65, DP 6471, 19 Kathleen Street.
