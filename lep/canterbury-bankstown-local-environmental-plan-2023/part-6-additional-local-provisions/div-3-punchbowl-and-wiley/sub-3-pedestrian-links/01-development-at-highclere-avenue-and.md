# 6.103 Development at Highclere Avenue and Kelly Street, Punchbowl

Development on land identified as "Area 57" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 57", and
- (b) the development will include a pedestrian link at least 4m wide between Highclere Avenue and Kelly Street.
