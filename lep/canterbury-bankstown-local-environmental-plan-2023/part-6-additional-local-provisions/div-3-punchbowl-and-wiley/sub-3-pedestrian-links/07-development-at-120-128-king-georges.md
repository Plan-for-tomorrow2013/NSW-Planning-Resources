# 6.109 Development at 120-128 King Georges Road, Wiley Park

Development on land identified as "Area 63" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 63", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link to create a pedestrian link at least 6m wide that—
  - (i) traverses Lot 10, DP 604706, 120 King Georges Road, and
  - (ii) is between King Georges Road and Samuel Street.
