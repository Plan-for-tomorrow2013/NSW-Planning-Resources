# 6.105 Development at Highclere Avenue, Punchbowl

Development on land identified as "Area 59" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 59", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link at Lot 35, Section H, DP 5720, 84 Highclere Avenue to create a pedestrian link at least 6m wide that—
  - (i) traverses Lot B, DP 314278, 86 Highclere Avenue and Lot 1, DP 587839, 88A Highclere Avenue, and
  - (ii) is between Highclere Avenue and the western boundary of Lot 35, Section H, DP 5720, 84 Highclere Avenue.
