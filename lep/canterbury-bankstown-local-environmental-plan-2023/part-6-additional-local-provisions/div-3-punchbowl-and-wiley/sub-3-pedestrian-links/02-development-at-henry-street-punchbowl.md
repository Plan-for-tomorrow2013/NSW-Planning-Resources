# 6.104 Development at Henry Street, Punchbowl

Development on land identified as "Area 58" on the *Special Provisions Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site area of the development will include all land in "Area 58", and
- (b) the development will result in the widening of the existing pedestrian link at Lot 55, DP 8422 to create a pedestrian link at least 6m wide that traverses Lot 1, DP 15497, 25 Henry Street and is between—
  - (i) Henry Street, and
  - (ii) the eastern boundary of Lot 55, DP 8422.
