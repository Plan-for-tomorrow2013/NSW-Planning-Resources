# 6.31 Residential development near pipelines

**(1)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on land identified as "Area 2" on the *Activity Hazard Area Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied there are measures in place to adequately mitigate the impacts of exposure to a pipeline.
