# 6.8 Airspace operations

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to protect airspace around airports.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development that is a controlled activity within the meaning of the *Airports Act 1996* of the Commonwealth, Part 12, Division 4, unless the applicant has obtained approval for the controlled activity under regulations made for the purposes of that division.

> Note: *Controlled activities* include the construction or alteration of buildings or other structures that cause an intrusion into airspace around airports.
