# 6.45 Building height in Belmore and Lakemba Precinct, Canterbury Precinct and Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Belmore and Lakemba Precinct", "Canterbury Precinct" or "Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct" on the *Precinct Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies resulting from development for the purposes of residential flat buildings or shop top housing may exceed the maximum building height shown for the land on the *Height of Buildings Map* if the consent authority is satisfied that the part of the building that will exceed the maximum building height will be a rooftop garden that—
- (a) has an area of at least 50% of the total area of the roof, and
- (b) will include planted vegetation and landscaping, and
- (c) will be accessible to all residents of the building, and
- (d) is designed in a way that—
  - (i) minimises safety and security risks, visual and acoustic privacy impacts and overshadowing, and
  - (ii) provides protection from the effects of the sun and wind.
