# 6.13 Special provisions for centre-based child care facilities

Development consent must not be granted for the purposes of centre-based child care facilities on land identified as "Area 1" on the *Clause Application Map* if the vehicular access to the land is from—
- (a) a classified road, or
- (b) a cul-de-sac road or a road where the carriageway between kerbs is less than 10m.
