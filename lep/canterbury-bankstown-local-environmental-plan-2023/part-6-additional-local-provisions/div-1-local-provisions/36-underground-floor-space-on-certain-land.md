# 6.37A Underground floor space on certain land in Zone E1

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area B", "Area C", "Area D", "Area E" and "Area F" on the *Underground Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have additional gross floor area equal to a floor space ratio of 1:1 if—
- (a) the building is on a site with an area of at least 3000m2, and
- (b) the additional floor area is located below ground level, and
- (c) the additional floor area is located in a part of the building with a ceiling height of at least 3.7m, and
- (d) the additional floor area is used for the following purposes—
  - (i) community facilities,
  - (ii) entertainment facilities,
  - (iii) recreation facilities (indoor),
  - (iv) registered clubs,
  - (v) shops, and
- (e) no part of the additional floor area is used as a car park, and
- (f) the consent authority is satisfied that the development will not result in an increase in car parks at street level.
