# 6.52A Development at 1 Leicester Street, Chester Hill

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 452, DP 800063, 1 Leicester Street, Chester Hill.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) at least 8,300m2 of the land will be used for non-residential purposes, and
- (b) at least 2,000m2 of the land will be used for the purposes of a single publicly accessible open space, and
- (c) not more than 800m2 of the land will be used for the purposes of pathways for pedestrian and cyclist access and circulation, and
- (d) the total floor area of the land used for the purposes of retail premises will not exceed 12,400m2, and
- (e) a development control plan has been prepared for the land.

**(3)**
Subclause (2) does not apply to development of a minor nature only, including minor additions to a building or ancillary development.

**(4)**
In this clause—
*non-residential purposes* means purposes other than—
- (a) residential accommodation, or
- (b) car parks.
