# 6.28 Restriction on development on land in Area 2

Development consent must not be granted to development for the following purposes on land identified as "Area 2" on the *Special Provisions Map*—
- (a) dual occupancies,
- (b) centre-based child care facilities,
- (c) community facilities,
- (d) health consulting rooms,
- (e) places of public worship,
- (f) respite day care centres.
