# 6.24 Development at 7A and 15 Marco Avenue, Revesby

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at Revesby identified as "Area 1" on the *Special Provisions Map*—
- (a) Lots 15-21, DP 16816, 7A Marco Avenue,
- (b) Lots 12-15, DP 35611, 15 Marco Avenue.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a maximum height of 40m and a maximum floor space ratio of 3.5:1 if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the site of the proposed development will be consolidated into a single lot, and
- (b) the development will include the following—
  - (i) a central plaza connecting to Abel Reserve,
  - (ii) a pedestrian entrance to Revesby railway station,
  - (iii) adequate public car parking spaces.
