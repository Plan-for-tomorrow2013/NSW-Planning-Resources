# 6.36 Community facility floor space

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as an "Area" on the *Community Facility Floor Space Map*.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have additional gross floor area of up to 3000m2 if the consent authority is satisfied that the additional floor area will be used for the purposes of community facilities.
