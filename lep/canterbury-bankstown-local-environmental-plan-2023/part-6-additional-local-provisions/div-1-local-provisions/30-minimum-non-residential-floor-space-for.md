# 6.32 Minimum non-residential floor space for certain land in Greenacre

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 402, DP 631754, 167 Hume Highway, Greenacre.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 30% of the gross floor area of all buildings on the land will be used for purposes other than residential accommodation.
