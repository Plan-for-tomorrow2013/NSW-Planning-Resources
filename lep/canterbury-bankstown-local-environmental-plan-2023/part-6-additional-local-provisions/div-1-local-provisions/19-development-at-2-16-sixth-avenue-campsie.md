# 6.20 Development at 2-16 Sixth Avenue, Campsie

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at 2-16 Sixth Avenue, Campsie—
- (a) Lot 1, DP 125349,
- (b) Lot 1, DP 10514,
- (c) Lots A and B, DP 312268,
- (d) Lots A and B, DP 394878,
- (e) Lots A and B, DP 307066.

**(2)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may have a maximum height of 25m and a maximum floor space ratio of 2.9:1 if—
- (a) the site area is at least 3,000m2, and
- (b) the street frontage is at least 50m.
