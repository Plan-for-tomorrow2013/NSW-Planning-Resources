# 6.12 Special provisions for shop top housing

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to permit shop top housing as part of live-work enterprises,
- (b) to encourage home-based, small-scale businesses on the fringe of the Bankstown City Centre as a way to promote local job opportunities.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 6" on the *Special Provisions Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of shop top housing on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the commercial premises or health services facilities are connected internally to a dwelling.
