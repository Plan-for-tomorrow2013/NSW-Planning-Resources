# 6.21 Restrictions on development in Zones E1 and E3

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land—
- (a) land in Zone E1 Local Centre,
- (b) land in Zone E3 Productivity Support identified as "Area 9" on the *Special Provisions Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that the ground level of a building resulting from the development will not be used for residential accommodation.
