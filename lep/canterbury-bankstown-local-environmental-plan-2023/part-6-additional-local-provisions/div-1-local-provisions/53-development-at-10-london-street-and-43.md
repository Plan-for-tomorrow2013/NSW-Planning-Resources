# 6.52B Development at 10 London Street and 43 North Parade, Campsie

**(1)**
This clause applies to the following land at Campsie—
- (a) Lot 11, Section B, DP 4190, 10 London Street,
- (b) Lot 10, Section B, DP 4190 and Lot 1, DP 798655, 43 North Parade.

**(2)**
Despite clause 4.3, the maximum height of a building on the land is 114m.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.4, the maximum floor space ratio of buildings on a site on the land is 10.9:1.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) at least 10.5% of the total floor area on the land will be used for the purposes of affordable housing (the *affordable housing component*), and
- (b) all dwellings comprising the affordable housing component will be used for the purposes of affordable housing for at least 15 years from the date the occupation certificate is issued for the building containing the dwelling.

**(5)**
This clause ceases to have effect on 30 October 2026.
