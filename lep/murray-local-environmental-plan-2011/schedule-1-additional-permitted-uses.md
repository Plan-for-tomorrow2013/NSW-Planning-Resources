# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at 75 Meninya Street, Moama

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 75 Meninya Street, Moama, being Lot 1, DP 222761.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a vehicle body repair workshop is permitted with development consent.

## 2 Use of certain land within Crown Reserve R56146

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within Crown Reserve R56146 at Moama that is occupied by the mooring specified in commercial mooring licence CL 6224 and located opposite Lot 1, DP 903914, Forbes Street, Moama and Lots 3-5, section 58, DP 758686, Ward Street, Moama.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a restaurant is permitted with development consent.

## 3 Use of certain land at 52 Perricoota Road, Moama

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 52 Perricoota Road, Moama, being part of Lot 261, DP 728943 and identified as "3" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a public administration building is permitted with development consent.
