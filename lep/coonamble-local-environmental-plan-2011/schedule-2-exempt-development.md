# Schedule 2 Exempt development

*(Clause 3.1)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies exempt development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional exempt development not specified in that Policy.

> Note: Exempt development may be carried out without the need for development consent under the Act. Such development is not exempt from any approval, licence, permit or authority that is required under any other Act and adjoining owners' property rights and the common law still apply.

## Signage—real estate signs

**(1)**
Maximum area—4.0m2, or if on land in an conservation zone—2.5m2.

**(2)**
Maximum 2 signs per premises.

**(3)**
Must be removed within 14 days after the premises or land is sold or let.

**(4)**
Must be non-flashing.

**(5)**
Must not be illuminated.

**(6)**
Must be within the boundary of the advertised property.

## Signage—signs behind the glass line of a shop window

Must not be illuminated.

## Signage—signs relating to lawful use of premises

**(1)**
Must relate to a lawful use of, and carried out on, the land.

**(2)**
Maximum area—
- (a) if on land in Zone RU1, RU5, R1 or R5—0.75m2, or
- (b) if on land in Zone E4—5.0m2, or
- (c) if on land in Zone E1 or E3—15% of the front elevation of the building on which it is displayed, or
- (d) 2.5m2.

**(3)**
Must be behind the glass line of a shop or window.

**(4)**
If on land in a residential zone—must not be illuminated.

**(5)**
Maximum height—
- (a) 3m above ground level (existing) or if suspended from an awning on a public road, 2.7m above ground level (existing), or
- (b) the height of the underside of any awning, whichever is greater.
