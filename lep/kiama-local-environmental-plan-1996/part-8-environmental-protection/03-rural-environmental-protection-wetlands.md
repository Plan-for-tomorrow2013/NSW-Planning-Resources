# 29 Rural Environmental Protection (Wetlands Buffer) Zone—Development in the Spring Creek Catchment

Notwithstanding any other provision of this plan, the Council may grant consent to attached dwelling dual occupancy development and development for the purposes of dwelling-houses, home hosting or land clearing on land within Zone No 7 (b1) in the Spring Creek catchment at Kiama.
