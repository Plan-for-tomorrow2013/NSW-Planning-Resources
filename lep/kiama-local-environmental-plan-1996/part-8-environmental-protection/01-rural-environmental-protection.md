# 27 Rural Environmental Protection (Estuarine Wetlands) Zone—Subdivision

**(1)**
A person shall not subdivide land within Zone No 7 (b) unless each allotment of land created by the subdivision has an area of not less than 40 hectares.

**(2)**
The Council shall not grant consent to the subdivision of land within Zone No 7 (b) if the Council is satisfied the allotments are intended to be used for residential purposes.
