# 21 General Business, Special Business (Heritage), Neighbourhood Business and Business (Tourist Related) Zones—Floorspace Ratios

A person shall not erect a building on land within a zone specified in Column I of the Table to this clause if the ratio of the floorspace of the building to the site area of the land on which the building is to be erected exceeds the ratio specified opposite that zone in Column II of that Table.

| Column I | Column II |
|---|---|
| Zone No 3 (a) | 1.5:1 |
| Zone No 3 (b) | 0.6:1 |
| Zone No 3 (c) | 0.5:1 |
| Zone No 3 (d) | 1.5:1 |
