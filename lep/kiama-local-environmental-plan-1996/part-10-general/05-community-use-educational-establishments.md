# 51 Community use—educational establishments

Nothing in this plan shall prevent the use of educational establishments for the purpose of enabling—
- (a) the community use of the facilities and sites of schools, colleges and other educational institutions, or
- (b) the commercial operation of those facilities and sites, or
- (c) any person to carry out development for the purposes of community uses on land used for the purpose of schools, colleges or other educational institutions whether or not the development is ancillary to that purpose,
provided that the consent of the Council is obtained.
