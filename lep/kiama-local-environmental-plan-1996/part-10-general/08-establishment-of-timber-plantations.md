# 53A Establishment of timber plantations

**(1)**
This clause applies only to lawfully cleared land within Zone No 1 (a), 7 (d), 7 (e) or 7 (f).

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this plan, a person may, without development consent, establish tree plantations on land that is an accredited proposed timber plantation or an accredited timber plantation (within the meaning of the *Timber Plantations (Harvest Guarantee) Act 1995*) consisting of land to which this clause applies.
