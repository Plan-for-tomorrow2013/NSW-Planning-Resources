# 62 Classification of public land as community land and operational land

**(1)**
In this clause—
*community land* means land classified as community land within the meaning of the *Local Government Act 1993*.
*operational land* means land classified as operational land within the meaning of the *Local Government Act 1993*.

**(2)**
All public land within Zone No 6 (a) or 6 (c), owned or controlled by the Council, is classified as community land, except where it is listed in Schedule 3.

**(3)**
The public land referred to in Schedule 3 is reclassified from community land to operational land.
