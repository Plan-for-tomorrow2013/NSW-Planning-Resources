# 57 Tourist facility development in unserviced areas within the Rural "A" Zone

**(1)**
This clause applies to land within Zone No 1 (a).

**(2)**
The Council shall not grant consent to development on land to which this clause applies for tourist facility purposes where that development will require water supply and sewage disposal facilities unless—
- (a) the proposed buildings intended to be used for the purposes can be connected to reticulated water supply and reticulated sewerage systems acceptable to the Council, or
- (b) a reliable water supply capable of meeting the projected needs of the occupiers of the proposed building and a satisfactory alternative option for sewage treatment capable of achieving standards of treatment and effluent discharge quality can be provided.
