# 55 Traffic impact

The Council shall not grant consent to development in any residential, commercial or industrial zone unless it has given consideration to—
- (a) traffic impacts on the road system and on the amenity of residents, and
- (b) the safety of traffic access, and
- (c) the adequacy of car parking.
