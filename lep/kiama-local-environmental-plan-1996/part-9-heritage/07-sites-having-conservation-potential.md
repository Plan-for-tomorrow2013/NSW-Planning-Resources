# 47 Sites having conservation potential

Where the Council receives an application to carry out development on land which comprises a potential historical archaeological site, the Council must not grant consent unless—
- (a) it has considered a conservation plan which includes an assessment of how the proposed development would affect the conservation of the site, and
- (b) in the case of development involving the disturbance or excavation of the land, any excavation permit required under Division 9 of Part 6 of the *Heritage Act 1977* has been obtained.
