# 8.2 Objectives

The objectives of this part are to—
- (a) encourage additional residential development, including affordable housing, in the Hornsby Transport Oriented Development Precinct, while retaining commercial floor space, and
- (b) ensure that development in the Precinct occurs in accordance with the *Hornsby Precinct Design Guide*.
