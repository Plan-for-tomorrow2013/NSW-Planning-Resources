# 4.1D Minimum subdivision lot size for dual occupancies in Zone R2

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to establish a minimum subdivision lot size for dual occupancies in Zone R2,
- (b) to achieve planned residential density in Zone R2,
- (c) to ensure lots are large enough to accommodate dual occupancies that provide a high level of residential amenity,
- (d) to minimise any adverse impacts of development on the amenity of the area.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Zone R2 Low Density Residential.

**(3)**
Despite clauses 4.1 and 4.1A, development consent may be granted to the subdivision of land to which this clause applies if the consent authority is satisfied that there is an existing dual occupancy on the land that was lawfully erected.

**(4)**
Subclause (3) does not apply to land in a heritage conservation area unless each lot resulting from the subdivision will—
- (a) for dual occupancies (attached)—be at least 400m2, and
- (b) for dual occupancies (detached)—be at least 450m2, and
- (c) have only one dwelling located on the lot.

**(5)**
Subclause (3) does not apply to land that is not in a heritage conservation area unless each lot resulting from the subdivision will—
- (a) for dual occupancies (attached)—be at least 350m2, and
- (b) for dual occupancies (detached)—be at least 400m2, and
- (c) have only one dwelling located on the lot.

**(6)**
If the lot is a battle-axe lot or has an access handle, the area of the access handle is not included in calculating the size of the lot under subclauses (4) and (5).
