# 7.2 Objectives of part

The objectives of this part are—
- (a) to increase housing supply in the Cherrybrook Station Precinct within walking distance of Cherrybrook Metro Station, and
- (b) to deliver housing supported by vibrant public spaces and community amenity, and
- (c) to increase the amount of affordable housing in the Cherrybrook Station Precinct.
