# 7.13 Use of certain land in Cherrybrook Station Precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to the Cherrybrook Station Precinct, other than land identified as "Area 2" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of attached dwellings and multi dwelling housing is permitted with development consent.
