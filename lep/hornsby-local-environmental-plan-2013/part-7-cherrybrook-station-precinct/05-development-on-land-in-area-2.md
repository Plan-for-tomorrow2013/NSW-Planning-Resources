# 7.5 Development on land in Area 2

Development on land identified as "Area 2" on the *Key Sites Map* is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the development includes adequate provision for pedestrian and road infrastructure that is configured and located in a way that will allow a suitable level of connectivity within the Cherrybrook Station Precinct, and
- (b) at least 4,000m2 of land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use in Area 2 will be publicly accessible open space, and
- (c) at least 3,000m2 of the gross floor area of all buildings on land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use in Area 2 will be used for the purposes of community facilities, and
- (d) at least 10% of residential floor space in Area 2 will be used for the purposes of affordable housing (the *affordable housing component*), and
- (e) all dwellings comprising the affordable housing component will be—
  - (i) used for the purposes of affordable housing for at least 15 years from the date the occupation certificate is issued for the building containing the dwelling, and
  - (ii) managed by a registered community housing provider.
