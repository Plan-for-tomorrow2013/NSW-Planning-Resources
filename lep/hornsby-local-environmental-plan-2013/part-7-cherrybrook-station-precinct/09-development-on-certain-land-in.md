# 7.9 Development on certain land in Cherrybrook Station Precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 6", "Area 7", "Area 8", "Area 9", "Area 10", "Area 11", "Area 12", "Area 13", "Area 14", "Area 15", "Area 16", "Area 17", "Area 18", "Area 19", "Area 20", "Area 21", "Area 22", "Area 23", "Area 24" and "Area 25" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development on land in each area is incentivised development if the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) all lots comprising the area are amalgamated into a single lot, and
- (b) the development includes adequate provision for pedestrian and road infrastructure that is configured and located in a way that will allow a suitable level of connectivity within the Cherrybrook Station Precinct.
