# 7.12 Use of land in Zone R4 in Area 2

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 2" on the *Key Sites Map* that is in Zone R4 High Density Residential.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of food and drink premises is permitted with development consent.
