# 7.11 Use of land in Zone MU1 in Area 2

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 2" on the *Key Sites Map* that is in Zone MU1 Mixed Use.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of residential flat buildings is permitted with development consent.
