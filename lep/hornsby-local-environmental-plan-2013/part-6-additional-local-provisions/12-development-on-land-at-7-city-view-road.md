# 6.13 Development on land at 7 City View Road, Pennant Hills

**(1)**
This clause applies to Lot 3, DP 732565, 7 City View Road, Pennant Hills, identified as "Area 1" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of residential flat buildings and seniors housing is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**
A building on land to which this clause applies may exceed the maximum floor space ratio shown for the land on the *Floor Space Ratio Map*.

**(4)**
Subclauses (2) and (3) do not apply unless the consent authority is satisfied that a floor space ratio of at least 0.5:1 for the building will be used for purposes other than residential accommodation.
