# 6.12 Seniors housing on land at Ashley Street and Webb Avenue, Hornsby

**(1)**
This clause applies to development for the purposes of seniors housing on land identified as "Area 3" on the *Height of Buildings Map*.

**(2)**
The maximum height of a building resulting from development to which this clause applies is 20.5m.
