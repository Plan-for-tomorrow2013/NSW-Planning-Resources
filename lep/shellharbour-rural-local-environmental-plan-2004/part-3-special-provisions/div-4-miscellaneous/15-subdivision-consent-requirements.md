# 64B Subdivision—consent requirements

Land to which this plan applies may be subdivided, but only with development consent.

> Note: If a subdivision is specified as exempt development in an applicable environmental planning instrument, such as this plan or *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008*, the Act enables it to be carried out without development consent.
> Part 6 of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* provides that the strata subdivision of a building in certain circumstances is complying development.
