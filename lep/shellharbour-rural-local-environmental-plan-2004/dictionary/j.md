# Dictionary — J

### junk yard

*junk yard* means land used for the collection, storage, abandonment or sale of scrap metals, waste paper, rags, bottles or other scrap materials, waste material or goods, including collecting, dismantling, storing, salvaging or abandoning of automobiles or for the sale of their parts.
