# Dictionary — O

### offensive or hazardous industry

*offensive or hazardous industry* means an industry which by reason of the processes involved or the method of manufacture or the nature of the materials used or produced, requires isolation from other buildings.

### older people

*older people* means people aged 55 years or over.

### operational buffer

*operational buffer* means the area required to contain most of the adverse externalities associated with mineral extraction.
