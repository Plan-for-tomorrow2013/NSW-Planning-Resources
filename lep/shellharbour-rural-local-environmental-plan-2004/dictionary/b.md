# Dictionary — B

### bed and breakfast accommodation

*bed and breakfast accommodation* means a lawfully erected house occupied for permanent residential use in which a maximum of three rooms are made available by the residents for temporary holiday accommodation.

### biodiversity

*biodiversity* means the variety of life forms, the different plants, animals and micro-organisms, the genes they contain and the ecosystems they form.

### boarding house

*boarding house* includes a house let in lodgings or a hostel but does not include a motel.

### brothel

*brothel* means premises habitually used for the purposes of prostitution. Premises may constitute a brothel even though used by only one prostitute for the purposes of prostitution.

### bulky goods

*bulky goods* means large goods which are of such a size, weight or shape as to require a large area for handling, storage or display, but not food and grocery items.

### bush fire

*bush fire* means any unplanned fire in vegetation.

### bush food plantation

*bush food plantation* means the growing of native plants on cleared land and the erection of associated buildings to obtain bush foods for commercial gain.

### bush rock removal

*bush rock removal* means the disturbance, dislodgment or removal of bush rock from land.

### bushland

*bushland* means land on which there is vegetation which is either a remainder of the natural vegetation of the land, or, if that has been altered, is still representative of the structure and floristics of the natural vegetation.
