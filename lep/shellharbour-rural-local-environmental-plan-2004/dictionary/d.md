# Dictionary — D

### damage

*damage*, in relation to vegetation, includes lopping, topping, ring barking, poisoning, felling, digging up, pulling out, smothering or any other form of deliberate damage.

### demolish

*demolish*, in relation to a heritage item, or a building, work, relic, tree or place within a heritage conservation area, means total or partial destruction or dismantling of the heritage item, or the building, work, relic, tree or place.

### detached dual occupancy

*detached dual occupancy* means two houses on a single allotment of land.

### development

*development* has the same meaning as in the Act.

### drainage of land

*drainage of land* means the engineered flow of water from the land into natural or engineered channels or water bodies, or both.
