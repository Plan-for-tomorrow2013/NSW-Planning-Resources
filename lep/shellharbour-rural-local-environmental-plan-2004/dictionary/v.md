# Dictionary — V

### vegetation community

*vegetation community* means a group of organisms living together in a definable region or habitat defined by its vegetation.

### vulnerable species

*vulnerable species* has the same meaning as in the *Threatened Species Conservation Act 1995* and includes a vulnerable species within the meaning of the *Fisheries Management Act 1994*.
