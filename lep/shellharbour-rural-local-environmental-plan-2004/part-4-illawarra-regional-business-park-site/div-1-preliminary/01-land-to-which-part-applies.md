# 65 Land to which Part applies

**(1)**
This Part applies to the land identified on the *Land Application Map*, referred to in this Part as the *Illawarra Regional Business Park site*.

**(2)**
No other provision in this plan (except clauses 6, 11 and 64B-64E) applies to the land to which this Part applies.
