# 69 Land use zones

**(1)**
For the purposes of this Part, land within the Illawarra Regional Business Park site is in a zone as follows if the land is shown on the Land Zoning Map as being within that zone—
- (a) Zone IN2 Light Industrial,
- (b) Zone SP2 Infrastructure,
- (c) Zone E2 Environmental Conservation.

**(2)**
The consent authority must have regard to the objectives for development within a zone when determining a development application in respect of land within the zone.
