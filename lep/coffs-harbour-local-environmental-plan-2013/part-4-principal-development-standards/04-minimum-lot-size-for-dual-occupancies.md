# 4.1B Minimum lot size for dual occupancies in Zone R2

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to achieve planned residential density in Zone R2 Low Density Residential.

**(2)**
Development consent may be granted for development on a lot in Zone R2 Low Density Residential for the purpose of a dual occupancy if the area of the lot is at least 800 square metres.

**(3)**
If a lot is a battle-axe lot or other lot with an access handle, the area of the access handle is not to be included in calculating the lot size.
