# 4.2E Erection of dual occupancies (detached) on land in certain rural and residential zones

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to ensure development is of a scale and nature compatible with the permissible uses, character and environmental capabilities of the land,
- (b) to ensure development addresses issues relating to access, siting, land suitability and potential impacts of the development,
- (c) to ensure dual occupancies (detached) are located so as to share services and retain opportunities for agriculture on rural land.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in the following zones—
- (a) Zone RU2 Rural Landscape,
- (b) Zone R5 Large Lot Residential.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of dual occupancies (detached) on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the development will not impair the use of the land, or adjacent land, for agriculture or rural industries, and
- (b) the land is physically suitable for the development, and
- (c) the land can accommodate the on-site disposal and management of sewage for the development, and
- (d) the development will not have an adverse impact on the scenic amenity or character of the rural environment, and
- (e) the development will not have an adverse impact on the following on or near the land—
  - (i) native flora or fauna,
  - (ii) water quality.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of dual occupancies (detached) unless—
- (a) if the development is on land in Zone RU2 Rural Landscape—
  - (i) the consent authority is satisfied both dwellings will use the same vehicular access to and from a public road, and
  - (ii) development consent for the erection of dwelling houses on the land may be granted in accordance with clause 4.2B, or
- (b) if the development is on land in Zone R5 Large Lot Residential—the area of the lot is at least 8,000m2.
