## 20 Use of certain land at Hamilton Drive, Boambee East

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 1 Hamilton Drive, Boambee East, being Lot 1, DP 777555, shown as "20" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of landscaping material supplies is permitted with development consent.

**(3)**

- (a) the development is to cease no later than 30 years after the date on which development consent is first granted in respect of the development, and
- (b) the infrastructure and materials associated with landscaping material supplies are to be removed from that part of Zone C2 that lies outside of the land to which this clause applies in accordance with the Voluntary Planning Agreement and the Vegetation Management Plan that apply to Lot 1, DP 777555, 1 Hamilton Drive, Boambee East.

## 23 Use of land at 4/4A Bray Street, Coffs Harbour

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Lots 3 and 4, DP 825181, 4/4A Bray Street, Coffs Harbour.

**(2)**
Development for the following purposes is permitted with development consent—
- (a) for land at Lot 3, DP 825181—a single retail premises, if the consent authority is satisfied that the retail premises will be used for the purposes of a drive-through bottle shop,
- (b) for land at Lot 4, DP 825181—a single pub.

**(3)**
In this clause—
*bottle shop* means a licensed premises, within the meaning of the *Liquor Act 2007*, the principal purpose of which is the retail sale of liquor in sealed containers for consumption away from the premises.

## 24 Use of certain land in Zone RU2

**(1)**
This clause applies to land in Zone RU2 Rural Landscape identified on the *Artisan Food and Drink Industries and Rural Function Centres Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the following purposes is permitted with development consent—
- (a) artisan food and drink industries,
- (b) function centres, other than convention centres or exhibition centres.
