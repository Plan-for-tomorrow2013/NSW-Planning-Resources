# 7.16 Erection of dwelling houses on land in Zone E4

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to permit the erection of dwelling houses on land in Zone E4 General Industrial in certain circumstances.

**(2)**
Despite any other provision of this Plan, development consent may be granted to the erection of a dwelling house on a lot in Zone E4 General Industrial, on which there is currently no dwelling house, if the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the lot is being used for a purpose permitted in Zone E4 General Industrial, and
- (b) the gross floor area of the dwelling house will be no more than 120 square metres, and
- (c) the dwelling house will not require its own separate access.
