# 7.14 Commercial premises in Zones E3 and MU1

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to maintain the commercial hierarchy of Coffs Harbour by encouraging commercial development of an appropriate scale in Zone E3 Productivity Support and Zone MU1 Mixed Use.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of business, office or retail premises on land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use if the gross floor area of that development will exceed 750 square metres per premise.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of business or office premises on land in Zone E3 Productivity Support if the total gross floor area of the part of the development comprising business and office premises will exceed 600 square metres on each lot that existed as at 16 December 2010 on which the development will be located.
