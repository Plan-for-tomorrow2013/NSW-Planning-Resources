# 7.24 Affordable housing contributions—Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct

**(1)**
In this clause, the *Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct Affordable Housing Principles* are as follows—
- (a) affordable housing must be provided and managed to accommodate a diverse residential population in areas representative of all income groups in the City of Coffs Harbour local government area,
- (b) affordable housing must be rented to tenants at rents that do not exceed a benchmark of 30% of actual household income,
- (c) dwellings provided for affordable housing must be managed to maintain their continued use as affordable housing,
- (d) the Council must use the following, received by or on behalf of the Council, to improve or replace, or provide additional affordable housing, in the City of Coffs Harbour local government area—
  - (i) rent from affordable housing, excluding landlords' expenses such as management and maintenance costs and rates and taxes payable in connection with the dwellings,
  - (ii) money from the disposal of affordable housing,
- (e) affordable housing must consist of dwellings designed to a standard that, in the consent authority's opinion, is consistent with the same type of dwellings forming part of the development that are not intended to be used as affordable housing, particularly in relation to solar access and natural ventilation.

**(2)**
This clause applies to development on land in the Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct resulting in any of the following—
- (a) the erection of one or more new buildings if—
  - (i) the buildings will be used for the purposes of residential accommodation, and
  - (ii) the gross floor area of all resulting buildings will exceed 2,000m2,
- (b) alterations to one or more existing buildings if—
  - (i) the building is used for the purposes of residential accommodation, and
  - (ii) the additional gross floor area resulting from all alterations will exceed 2,000m2.

**(3)**
This clause does not apply to a building used for the purposes of residential accommodation that is—
- (a) public housing, or
- (b) affordable housing managed by a registered community housing provider in perpetuity.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority has considered the Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct Affordable Housing Principles.

**(5)**
The consent authority may impose a condition requiring an affordable housing contribution of 3% of the total gross floor area of the development.

**(6)**
A condition imposed under this clause must permit a person to satisfy the condition by—
- (a) a dedication, in favour of the Council, of land comprising one or more dwellings, each having a gross floor area of at least 50m2, and a monetary contribution paid to the Council of equivalent value to any remainder of the gross floor area specified in subclause (5), or
- (b) a monetary contribution paid to the Council of equivalent value to the gross floor area specified in subclause (5).

**(7)**
The rate at which a monetary contribution is taken to be equivalent to floor area for the purposes of this clause must be calculated by reference to the market value of dwellings of a similar size to the dwellings in the proposed development.

**(8)**
In this clause—
*public housing* has the same meaning as in the *Housing Act 2001*.
