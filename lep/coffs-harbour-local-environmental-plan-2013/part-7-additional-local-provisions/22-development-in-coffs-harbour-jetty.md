# 7.23 Development in Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct

**(1)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land in the Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct unless the consent authority—
- (a) has considered the Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct Design Guidelines, and
- (b) is satisfied the development is consistent with the Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct Design Guidelines, Part 4.3.

**(2)**
In this clause—
*Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct Design Guidelines* means the *Coffs Harbour Jetty Foreshore Precinct Design Guidelines* published by the Department in September 2026.
