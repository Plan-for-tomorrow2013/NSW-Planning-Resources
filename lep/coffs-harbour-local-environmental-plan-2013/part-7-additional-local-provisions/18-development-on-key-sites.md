# 7.19 Development on Key Sites

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure development—
- (a) creates a focal point and adds visual interest to the streetscape, and
- (b) provides for improved pedestrian links, connectivity and a safe public domain, and
- (c) is designed to minimise bulk, overshadowing and visual intrusion.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land identified as the following on the *Key Sites Map*—
- (a) "1",
- (b) "2",
- (c) "3",
- (d) "4",
- (e) "5".

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied the development will—
- (a) provide for coordinated and cohesive development of the land, and
- (b) feature a high standard of architectural design, materials and detailing appropriate to the building type and location, and
- (c) provide for a two-storey building height at street level with taller parts of the building set back to minimise bulk, overshadowing and visual intrusion, and
- (d) provide mid-block pedestrian connectivity in accordance with the Coffs Harbour Development Control Plan, and
- (e) provide for continuous awnings on active street frontages, and
- (f) ensure no blank or opaque walls are provided on any street elevation, and
- (g) ensure vehicle access and driveways are not provided from primary road frontages, and
- (h) not result in on-site parking areas that are visible from the primary street frontage, and
- (i) maintain and conserve the biodiversity values of the land and adjoining land.

**(4)**
Subclause (3) does not apply to development of a minor nature only, if the consent authority is satisfied the development—
- (a) is consistent with the objectives of the zone in which the development will be carried out, and
- (b) will not compromise the coordinated and cohesive development of the land.

**(5)**
In this clause—
*Coffs Harbour Development Control Plan* means the *Coffs Harbour Development Control Plan 2015* as in force on the commencement of *Coffs Harbour Local Environmental Plan 2013 (Amendment No 32)*.
