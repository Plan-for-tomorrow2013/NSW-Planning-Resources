# 7.22 Development on certain land in Zone RU2

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are—
- (a) to ensure development does not adversely impact the rural amenity, agricultural production, scenic, environmental or biodiversity values of the land and surrounding land, and
- (b) to ensure function centres are small-scale and low-impact.

**(2)**
This clause applies to land in Zone RU2 Rural Landscape identified on the *Artisan Food and Drink Industries and Rural Function Centres Map*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of artisan food and drink industries on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) if the development is part of mixed use development that includes a restaurant or cafe—the gross floor area of the mixed use development used as a dining area must not exceed 120m2, and
- (b) the development will be located to minimise visual intrusion and will not be visible above ridgelines, and
- (c) the development will not generate noise, traffic, parking or light spill that will have a significant adverse impact on neighbouring land and surrounding residential areas, and
- (d) the development will not have a significant adverse impact on the long-term agricultural production potential, biodiversity values or environmental characteristics of the site and the surrounding locality.

**(4)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of function centres on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the function centre will operate for no more than 30 days in a period of 12 months, and
- (b) no more than 150 guests will be permitted to attend an event at the function centre at a time, and
- (c) no more than one event will be held at the function centre per weekend, and
- (d) the development will not generate noise, traffic, parking or light spill that will have a significant adverse impact on surrounding residential areas, and
- (e) the development will not have a significant adverse impact on the long-term agricultural production potential, biodiversity values or environmental characteristics of the site and the surrounding locality.
