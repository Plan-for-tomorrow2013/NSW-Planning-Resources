# Schedule 2 Exempt development

*(Clause 3.1)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies exempt development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional exempt development not specified in that Policy.

> Note: Exempt development may be carried out without the need for development consent under the Act. Such development is not exempt from any approval, licence, permit or authority that is required under any other Act and adjoining owners' property rights and the common law still apply.

## Cemeteries or burial grounds that are not heritage items

**(1)**
Must only be—
- (a) the creation of a grave or monument in a cemetery or burial ground, or
- (b) an excavation or disturbance of land for the purpose of carrying out the conservation or repair of a monument or grave marker in an existing approved cemetery or burial ground.

**(2)**
Must not disturb human remains, relics in the form of grave goods or a place of Aboriginal heritage significance.

## Charity bins

Maximum bins in any single location—2.

## Farm dams in Zone RU2

**(1)**
Must only be used for water collection, storage or supply to support the carrying out of extensive agriculture or intensive plant agriculture on the land.

**(2)**
If used for the collection or harvesting of surface run-off—total storage volume of dams on the property must not exceed the maximum harvestable right volume for the property under the *Water Management Act 2000*.

**(3)**
If used for the collection or storage of irrigation run-off or tailwater—
- (a) must not be constructed in a watercourse, and
- (b) must be sealed to prevent groundwater intrusion, and
- (c) must have water quality treatment devices downstream of the outfall.

**(4)**
Minimum distance from a property boundary at closest point—
- (a) for a boundary fronting a road—10m, and
- (b) otherwise—20m.

**(5)**
Minimum distance from an on-site sewage management system—40m.

**(6)**
Must be, in relation to a stream determined under the Strahler system, within the meaning of the *Water Management (General) Regulation 2025*, to be a third or higher order stream—
- (a) outside a floodway of the stream, or
- (b) at least 100m from the stream.

**(7)**
Must be constructed without clearing, damaging or destroying native vegetation.

**(8)**
May only be constructed in a watercourse if constructed on a first or second order stream.

**(9)**
Must not be constructed on land identified as "Class 1", "Class 2" or "Class 3" on the *Acid Sulfate Soils Map*.

**(10)**
Must not be constructed on land with a slope greater than 10%.

**(11)**
Must have a spillway that—
- (a) directs excess water around the fill embankment to an outfall below the embankment, and
- (b) does not direct excess water onto adjoining properties, and
- (c) is adequate for the storage volume of the dam.

**(12)**
Minimum height difference between the embankment crest level and the spillway level—1m.

**(13)**
Maximum fill embankment height, measured from the current ground level immediately down slope of the embankment to the top of the embankment crest level—3m.

**(14)**
Maximum embankment fill batter grades—1:2.5 (vertical:horizontal).

**(15)**
Minimum fill embankment crest width—2.5m.

**(16)**
Must have erosion and sediment controls that are effectively maintained—
- (a) during construction, and
- (b) when bare soil or disturbed ground is present.

## Lighting (external)

**(1)**
Must not be for the lighting of tennis courts or sports fields.

**(2)**
Must not cause glare to adjoining properties or streets.

**(3)**
Must not result in adverse physical and visual impacts on heritage items.

## Signage

**(1)**
Business identification signs in a residential, rural or conservation zone must comply with the following—
- (a) Maximum—1 sign per premises,
- (b) maximum size in a residential zone—1m2,
- (c) maximum size in a rural or conservation zone—1.5m2,
- (d) must not be illuminated,
- (e) must be located wholly within property boundaries of the land to which the sign relates, or be flush mounted to the front fence or front wall of a building if the sign does not protrude beyond the physical limits of that fence or building,
- (f) must not be attached to a heritage item or be located in a heritage conservation area.

**(2)**
Business identification signs in Zone E1, E2, E3 or MU1, 1 sign per premises must not be attached to a heritage item or be in a heritage conservation area and must comply with the following requirements—
- (a) suspended under awning sign—
  - (i) maximum length—2.5m, and
  - (ii) maximum size—1.5m2, and
  - (iii) must be securely fixed by rigid metal supports, and
  - (iv) if over a public road—must be at least 2.6m above the ground or pavement level and at least 0.6m from the vertical projection of the kerb or roadway line,
- (b) vertical or horizontal projecting wall sign—
  - (i) maximum size—2.5m2, and
  - (ii) must be securely fixed by rigid metal supports, and
  - (iii) if over a public road—must be suspended at a height not less than 2.6m above the ground or pavement level and at least 0.6m from the vertical projection of the kerb or roadway line,
- (c) flush wall sign—
  - (i) maximum size—2.5m2, and
  - (ii) must be securely fixed by rigid metal supports, and
  - (iii) must not project above the top of the wall to which it is attached,
- (d) top hamper sign—
  - (i) maximum size—2.5m2, and
  - (ii) must be securely fixed by rigid metal supports, and
  - (iii) must not extend below the level of the head of the doorway or window above which it is attached, and
  - (iv) must not be more than 3.7m above the ground level (existing),
- (e) signs on building walls—
  - (i) maximum height—3m above ground level (existing) on front and side walls, and
  - (ii) maximum display area—50% of the area of the wall.

**(3)**
Business identification signs in Zone E4, E5 or W4 must comply with the following requirements—
- (a) maximum size—1m2 per metre of frontage, up to 10m2,
- (b) must be securely fixed by rigid metal supports.

**(4)**
Real estate signs advertising that the premises on which they are displayed are for sale or lease must comply with the following requirements—
- (a) maximum size in residential zones—3m2,
- (b) maximum size in employment or mixed use zones or Zone W4—4m2,
- (c) maximum—2 signs per premises,
- (d) must be located wholly within the property boundaries of the land to which the sign relates or, if on the footpath, be flush against the property boundary,
- (e) must not be displayed for more than 7 days after the commencement of the letting or settlement for the sale of the property is completed,
- (f) must not be erected on public land.

**(5)**
Building identification signs must comply with the following—
- (a) maximum—1 sign per premises,
- (b) maximum size—1m2 in a residential zone and 1.5m2 in all other zones,
- (c) maximum height—1.8m in a residential, rural or conservation zone,
- (d) must not be illuminated in a residential, rural or conservation zone,
- (e) must be located wholly within the property boundaries of the land to which the sign relates, or be flush mounted to the front fence or front wall of a building if the sign does not protrude beyond the physical limits of that fence or building,
- (f) must not be attached to a heritage item or be located in a heritage conservation area.

**(6)**
School signs must comply with the following—
- (a) maximum size—0.75m2,
- (b) minimum distance apart—3.5m,
- (c) maximum height to top of sign—1.5m above ground level (existing),
- (d) must relate to the school,
- (e) maximum—6 signs per street frontage,
- (f) must not be attached to a heritage item or be located in a heritage conservation area.

## Waste management facility (roofed bin storage/wash area)

**(1)**
Maximum area—10m2.

**(2)**
Must be located behind the building setback line.

**(3)**
Maximum—1 per property.

**(4)**
Roof water must be disposed of without causing a nuisance to adjoining properties.

**(5)**
Must not discharge into Council's stormwater drainage system.

**(6)**
Must not involve the clearing of bushland in Zone C1, C2 or C3.

**(7)**
Must not be attached to a heritage item.

**(8)**
Must not be located in a heritage conservation area.

**(9)**
Must not be located on vacant land.

**(10)**
Must not be located on bush fire prone land.

**(11)**
Must notify Council of its construction.
