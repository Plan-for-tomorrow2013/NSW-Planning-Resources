# 6.10 Development on land at 118 and 120 Niagara Street, Armidale

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at 118 and 120 Niagara Street, Armidale, being Lot 1, DP 799192 and Lot 19, DP 1076915, identified as "Item 3" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purpose of specialised retail premises or a shop unless the gross floor area of the premises or shop will not exceed 100 square metres.
