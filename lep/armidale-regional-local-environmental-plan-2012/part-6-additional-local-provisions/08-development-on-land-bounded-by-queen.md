# 6.9 Development on land bounded by Queen Elizabeth Drive, Golgotha Street, Niagara Street and Tancredi Street, Armidale

**(1)**
This clause applies to land bounded by Queen Elizabeth Drive, Golgotha Street, Niagara Street and Tancredi Street, Armidale that is in Zone MU1 Mixed Use, identified as "Item 2" on the *Clause Application Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purpose of specialised retail premises or a shop unless the gross floor area of the premises or shop will not exceed 1,500 square metres.
