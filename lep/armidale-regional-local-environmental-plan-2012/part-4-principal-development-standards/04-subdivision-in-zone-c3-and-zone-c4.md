# 4.1B Subdivision in Zone C3 and Zone C4

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that lot sizes and subdivision patterns in certain conservation zones allow buildings to be sited to better protect the environmental values of the area.

**(2)**
This clause applies to each lot (an *original lot*) that contains land in both Zone C3 Environmental Management and Zone C4 Environmental Living.

**(3)**
Despite clause 4.1, development consent may be granted to subdivide an original lot to create other lots (the *resulting lots*) that are less than the minimum size shown on the *Lot Size Map* for the land if—
- (a) the consent authority is satisfied that—
  - (i) no buildings will be erected on any part of a resulting lot that contains land in Zone C3 Environmental Management, and
  - (ii) the subdivision will not result in any significant adverse environmental impacts on the land being subdivided, and
- (b) the number of resulting lots will not exceed the number of lots that could be created by a subdivision of land under clause 4.1.
