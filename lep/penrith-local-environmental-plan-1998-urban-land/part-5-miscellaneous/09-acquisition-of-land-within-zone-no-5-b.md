# 22 Acquisition of land within Zone No 5 (b), 6 (b) or 7 (a)

**(1)**
The owner of any land within Zone No 5 (b), 6 (b) or 7 (a), may, by notice in writing, require the council to acquire the land.

**(2)**
On receipt of a notice referred to in subclause (1), except during the currency of a consent granted pursuant to clause 23, the council must acquire the land.
