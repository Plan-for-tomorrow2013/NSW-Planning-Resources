# 24 Acquisition of land within Zone No 6 (d) by the corporation

**(1)**
The owner of any land within Zone No 6 (d) (the Regional Open Space zone) may, by notice in writing, require the corporation to acquire the land.

**(2)**
Upon receipt of the notice, the corporation shall acquire the land to which the notice relates.
