# 29 Prohibited access

**(1)**
Except as provided in subclause (2), the creation of a vehicular crossing in, on or through the boundary of any land shown on the map with the notation "Vehicular access denied" and marked on the map with a series of heavy black dots is prohibited.

**(2)**
Despite subclause (1), the council may grant a consent for development that involves such a vehicular crossing where it is satisfied that carrying out the development would be impracticable unless direct vehicular access is provided.
