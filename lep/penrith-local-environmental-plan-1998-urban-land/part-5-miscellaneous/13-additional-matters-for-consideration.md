# 26 Additional matters for consideration for development in Zone No 6 (a), 6 (b) or 6 (d)

In determining an application for consent to carry out development on land within Zone No 6 (a), 6 (b) or 6 (d), the council must take the following matters into consideration—
- (a) the need for the proposed development on the land, and
- (b) the impact of the proposed development on the existing or likely future use and character of the land, and
- (c) whether any proposed building will be secondary and complementary to the existing or proposed use of the land as public open space, and
- (d) whether the proposal will substantially diminish public use of, and access to, open space, and
- (e) whether the proposal is compatible with adjacent uses in relation to its height, bulk and noise generation and any other aspects that might conflict with surrounding land uses, and
- (f) whether the proposal is consistent with a plan of management applying to the land under Division 2 of Part 2 of Chapter 6 of the *Local Government Act 1993*.
