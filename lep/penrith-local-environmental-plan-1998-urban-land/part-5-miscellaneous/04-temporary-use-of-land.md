# 18 Temporary use of land

Despite any other provision of this plan, a person may carry out development on land to which this plan applies with the consent of the council for any purpose for a maximum period of 28 days, whether consecutive or non-consecutive, in any one year, but only if, in the opinion of the council, the use of the land for that purpose contributes to the cultural well-being of the community.
