# 37 Land in the vicinity of Combewood

Despite any other provision of this plan, the land shown stippled on Sheet 1 of the map marked *Penrith Local Environmental Plan 1998 (Urban Land) (Amendment No 3) North Penrith* may not be used for any purpose other than landscaping.
