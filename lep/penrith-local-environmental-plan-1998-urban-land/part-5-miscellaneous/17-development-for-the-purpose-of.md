# 31 Development for the purpose of advertisements

Despite any other provisions of this plan, a person may carry out development for the purpose of any one or more of the following on any land to which this plan applies without the consent of the Council—
- (a) an internal window display,
- (b) any advertisement erected on land that is not visible from outside the land (but not an advertisement on a heritage item or on a site within a heritage conservation area),
- (c) a temporary advertisement, being one which is displayed for a period not exceeding 2 months in total in any one year,
- (d) a public notice in a public place,
- (e) a road safety or advisory sign,
- (f) a specific sign directing the travelling public to buildings or places of tourist interest.
