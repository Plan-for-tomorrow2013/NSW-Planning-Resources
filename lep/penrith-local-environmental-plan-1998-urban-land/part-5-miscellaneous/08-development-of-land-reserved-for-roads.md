# 21 Development of land reserved for roads

**(1)**
Despite clause 9, a person may, with the consent of the council, carry out development on land within Zone No 5 (c) if the development may be carried out on land within an adjoining zone created by this plan.

**(2)** (Repealed)

**(3)**
Land acquired under clause 20 may be developed, with the consent of the council, for the purpose of any land use permitted on land within an adjoining zone under this plan, until such time as it is required for the land use for which it was acquired.
