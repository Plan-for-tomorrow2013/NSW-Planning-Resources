# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Narraburra Street, Temora

**(1)**
This clause applies to all lots at Narraburra Street, Temora that are within Zone E4 General Industrial.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a dwelling house is permitted with consent, if—
- (a) the consent authority is satisfied that the design and siting of the proposed development is unlikely to interfere with the residential amenity of Narraburra Street, and
- (b) the dwelling house is on the same lot as an industrial use that is being carried out and that is permitted in the zone, and
- (c) the lot has frontage to Narraburra Street, and
- (d) the setback of the dwelling house does not exceed 50m.

## 2 Use of certain land at Schuberts Road, Trungley Hall

**(1)**
This clause applies to part of Lot 941, DP 130014, identified as "1" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purpose of a recreation facility (outdoor) is permitted with development consent.
