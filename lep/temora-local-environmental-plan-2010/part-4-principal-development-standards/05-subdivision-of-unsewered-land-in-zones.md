# 4.2B Subdivision of unsewered land in Zones RU1 and RU5

Development consent must not be granted for the subdivision of land in Zone RU1 Primary Production or Zone RU5 Village for the purpose of erecting a dwelling house unless the consent authority is satisfied that each lot to be created will have an area, shape, boundary dimensions and physical attributes that are adequate for the on-site disposal of sewage effluent in a manner that is not likely to detrimentally affect the health and safety of the occupants of the dwelling house on that lot or any adjoining lot.
