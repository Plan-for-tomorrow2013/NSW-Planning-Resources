# 1.2 Aims of Plan

**(1)**
This Plan aims to make local environmental planning provisions for land in Temora in accordance with the relevant standard environmental planning instrument under section 3.20 of the Act.

**(2)**
The particular aims of this Plan are as follows—
- (aa) to protect and promote the use and development of land for arts and cultural activity, including music and other performance arts,
- (a) to reinforce the strong "country town" character of urban Temora whilst moving towards a more sustainable environment,
- (b) to direct future urban expansion of Temora towards the north, but only within the catchment of the sewerage treatment works,
- (c) to protect Temora's crop and pasture lands from adverse environmental impacts whilst allowing for planned urban expansion on the urban fringe,
- (d) to protect environmentally sensitive land and conserve native vegetation habitats and threatened species,
- (e) to give priority to the protection, conservation and enhancement of indigenous and non-indigenous cultural heritage,
- (f) to encourage non-agricultural enterprises by permitting a wide range of urban land uses consistent with the imperative to support economic growth, employment creation and business opportunities,
- (g) to encourage further urban growth of Ariah Park and Springdale Village by providing for a variety of residential living opportunities,
- (h) to create and develop opportunities for strengthening the role of Temora Airport as a location for tourist attraction, centre for recreational aviation, commercial enterprises, aviation-related housing and associated supporting industries,
- (i) to maintain Hoskins Street and the adjoining streets as the primary zone for business, civic and cultural activities, whilst working towards the eventual exclusion of heavy vehicles from the town centre,
- (j) to encourage the renewal and upgrade of older residential precincts close to Temora Town Centre where there is spare infrastructure capacity.
