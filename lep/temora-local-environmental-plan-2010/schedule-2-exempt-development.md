# Schedule 2 Exempt development

*(Clause 3.1)*

> Note: *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies exempt development under that Policy. The Policy has State-wide application. This Schedule contains additional exempt development not specified in that Policy.

> Note: Exempt development may be carried out without the need for development consent under the Act. Such development is not exempt from any approval, licence, permit or authority that is required under any other Act and adjoining owners' property rights and the common law still apply.

## Signage

> Note: All types of signs are defined in the *Temora Shire Council Code No. 88—Signage Guidelines*.

**(1)**
Erection of an advertising structure and the display of an advertisement on it, or the display of an advertisement, in any of the following circumstances—
- (a) temporary community event signage,
- (b) real estate signage,
- (c) moveable signage.

**(2)**
Temporary community event signage must—
- (a) be located at—
  - (i) for Temora—the Caravan Park fence line (4 signs), the Lake Centenary Rest Stop (4 signs) and the Southern Side Stock Road—Road Reserve (4 signs), or
  - (ii) for Ariah Park—Burley Griffin Way turn off or entrance (4 signs) or Harper Park (4 signs), or
  - (iii) for Springdale—Springdale Rest Stop or Public Hall (4 signs), and
- (b) have an area of not more than 1.8m × 1.2m, and
- (c) be removed within 4 working days of the event's conclusion, and
- (d) not be erected on a heritage item.

**(3)**
Real estate signage must—
- (a) have an area of not more than 1.2m × 0.9m (if displayed on land in a residential zone) or 2.4m × 1.2m (if displayed on land in any other zone), and
- (b) have no more than 3 signs on any one property.

**(4)**
Moveable advertising signage must—
- (a) have an area of not more than 2m × 0.9m, and
- (b) be displayed while premises are open and operating, and
- (c) be securely weighted or anchored, and
- (d) not be illuminated or have moveable parts, and
- (e) have no more than 2 signs on any one property.
