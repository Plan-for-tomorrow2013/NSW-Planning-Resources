# Dictionary — M

### map

*map* means a map deposited in the office of the Council.

### master plan

*master plan* means a master plan adopted under Part 6 of Chapter 3.

### master plan area

*master plan area* means land in Ultimo-Pyrmont that is shown on the Ultimo-Pyrmont Site Identification Map as a master plan area.

### mixed-use development

*mixed-use development* means a building, or buildings, in which two or more uses are carried out.
