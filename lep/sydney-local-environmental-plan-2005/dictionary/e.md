# Dictionary — E

### ecologically sustainable development

*ecologically sustainable development* or *ESD* means development that uses, conserves and enhances the community's resources and energy so that the ecological processes on which life depends are maintained and the total quality of life now and in the future can be increased.

### educational establishment

*educational establishment* means a building used as a school, college, technical college, academy, lecture hall, gallery or museum, but does not include a building used wholly or principally as an institution or child care centre.

### existing height of a building

*existing height of a building*, in relation to a building within Central Sydney, means the height of the building at 27 December 1996.
