# Dictionary — D

### demolish

*demolish* a heritage item or any other building or structure means to damage, deface, destroy, pull down, dismantle or remove it in whole or part.

### development plan

*development plan* means a development plan adopted under Part 5 of Chapter 1.

### dredge

*dredge* means remove material from a harbour or river bed for the purpose of maintaining the previously established harbour or river depth, constructing a new or deeper navigational area or channel or re-opening a discontinued navigational area or channel.

### dwelling

*dwelling* in Central Sydney is a form of residential accommodation capable of being separately occupied.

### dwelling-house

*dwelling-house* means a building containing one but not more than one dwelling.
