# Dictionary — B

### backpacker accommodation

*backpacker accommodation* in Central Sydney means low cost tourist and visitor accommodation provided in a building or part of a building that may involve use of shared facilities, such as a communal bathroom, kitchen and laundry, and shared sleeping accommodation in which there are two or more persons in a room.

### boarding house

*boarding house* in Central Sydney means low cost residential accommodation provided in a building or part of a building that may involve use of shared facilities, such as a communal bathroom, kitchen and laundry, and shared sleeping accommodation. It includes a hostel and student accommodation.

### brothel

*brothel* means premises used for prostitution, whether or not used by only one prostitute.

### building envelope

*building envelope*, in relation to a heritage item in Central Sydney, means the volume, measured in cubic metres, of the existing building on the site of the item at the commencement of this plan.

### building in a heritage streetscape

*building in a heritage streetscape* in Central Sydney means a building with a frontage outlined in red on the Central Sydney Heritage Streetscape Map.

### business floor space

*business floor space* in Ultimo-Pyrmont means so much of the gross floor area of a building as is the subject of a business use.

### business use

*business use* in Ultimo-Pyrmont means any form of development carried out for a commercial or educational purpose, but does not include residential development or development for the purpose of a public utility undertaking.
