# Dictionary — A

### adjoining land

*adjoining land*, in relation to land in Central Sydney the subject of a development application, means any other land that abuts the land or that would abut the land if it were not separated from it by a public road.

### advertisement

*advertisement* has the same meaning as in the Act.

### affordable housing

*affordable housing* has the same meaning as in the Act.

### aids to navigation

*aids to navigation* means buoys, signs, lights or other structures, located on the waterway or on dry land, that are designed to assist the safe and efficient movement of vessels on the waterway.

### amusement centre

*amusement centre* means a building or place (not being a hotel or pub) used principally for playing—
- (a) billiards, pool or other like games, or
- (b) electrically or mechanically operated amusement devices, such as pinball machines, computer or video games and the like.
