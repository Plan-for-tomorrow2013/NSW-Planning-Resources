# 8.5 Affordable housing in Area 2

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Area 2" on the *Affordable Housing Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied that at least 30% of the gross floor area of all buildings in "Area 2" that will be used for the purposes of residential accommodation will be used for one or more of the following purposes for at least 15 years—
- (a) affordable housing provided by or on behalf of a social housing provider, within the meaning of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021*,
- (b) social housing premises, within the meaning of the *Residential Tenancies Act 2010*.
