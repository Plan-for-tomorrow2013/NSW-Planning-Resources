# 8.4 Affordable housing in Area 1

**(1)**
This clause applies to development on land identified as "Area 1" on the *Affordable Housing Map*—
- (a) for one of the following purposes—
  - (i) independent living units,
  - (ii) multi dwelling housing,
  - (iii) residential flat buildings,
  - (iv) shop top housing, and
- (b) resulting in the erection of at least 10 dwellings.

**(2)**
This clause does not apply to—
- (a) the making of alterations to, or the enlargement or extension of, a building, and
- (b) development used for the following purposes—
  - (i) affordable housing provided by or on behalf of a social housing provider, within the meaning of *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021*,
  - (ii) public housing, within the meaning of the *Housing Act 2001*,
  - (iii) social housing premises, within the meaning of the *Residential Tenancies Act 2010*.

**(3)**
Development consent must not be granted to development to which this clause applies unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) at least 2% of the total gross floor area of the residential component of the development will be—
  - (i) used for the purposes of affordable housing for at least 15 years, and
  - (ii) managed by a registered community housing provider, and
- (b) each dwelling will—
  - (i) have a minimum gross floor area of at least 50m2, and
  - (ii) be located within 400m of existing or proposed public transport infrastructure, retail premises or open space.
