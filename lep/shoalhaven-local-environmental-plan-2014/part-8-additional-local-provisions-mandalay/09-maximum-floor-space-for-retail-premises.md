# 8.9 Maximum floor space for retail premises in Hyam Street Sub-precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Cl 8.9" on the *Clauses Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development resulting in more than 100m2 of the gross floor area of all buildings on the land being used for the purposes of retail premises.
