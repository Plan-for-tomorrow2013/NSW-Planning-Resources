# 8.10 Minimum site area for certain buildings in Hyam Street Sub-precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Cl 8.10" on the *Clauses Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies that will result in the erection of a building with a height that is greater than 11m unless the consent authority is satisfied the site area of the land is at least 1,800m2.
