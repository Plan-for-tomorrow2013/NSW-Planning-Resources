# 8.3 Development on land in Mandalay and Nowra Riverfront Precincts

**(1)**
The objectives of this clause are as follows—
- (a) to ensure future development acknowledges and embeds Country,
- (b) to encourage development that demonstrates design excellence,
- (c) to create a neighbourhood which is vibrant and well-connected,
- (d) to build resilient infrastructure that enables long-term growth and development of the Mandalay and Nowra Riverfront Precincts,
- (e) to provide diverse and affordable housing in the Mandalay and Nowra Riverfront Precincts,
- (f) to support businesses that support community health and wellbeing and that complement the existing uses of land in the Mandalay and Nowra Riverfront Precincts, including the Shoalhaven Memorial Hospital,
- (g) to ensure small scale development will not compromise, restrict or otherwise adversely impact the future use of land in the Mandalay and Nowra Riverfront Precincts.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this part applies unless—
- (a) the consent authority is satisfied the development will result in an appropriate mix of residential uses, and
- (b) a development control plan that complies with subclause (3) has been prepared for, or applies to, the land.

**(3)**
The development control plan for land to which this clause applies must provide for the following—
- (a) the location of the development, having regard to the need to achieve an acceptable relationship with other buildings on the same site or on neighbouring sites in terms of separation, setbacks, outlook, orientation, amenity and urban form,
- (b) the bulk, massing and modulation of buildings,
- (c) distribution of the major land uses, including the function and landscaping of public, communal and private open spaces,
- (d) the mitigation of environmental impacts, including noise attenuation, flood mitigation, water and soil management, remediation of contaminated land, solar access, energy efficiency and environmental sustainability,
- (e) access, including public transport, pedestrian, cycle and road access and circulation networks, as well as local traffic and parking management,
- (f) the impact on, and proposed improvements to, the public domain,
- (g) appropriate interfaces at ground level between the building and the public domain,
- (h) the compatibility of the proposed development with the desired future character of the area, having regard to the future vision and objectives of Nowra City Centre,
- (i) amalgamation of lots and subdivision,
- (j) landscaping, including any tree retention or removal,
- (k) concept plans showing the indicative layout, form and scale of proposed buildings,
- (l) heritage conservation.

**(4)**
This clause does not apply to proposed development that is of a minor nature only, if the consent authority is of the opinion that the carrying out of the development would be consistent with the objectives of the zone in which the land is situated.
