# 8.11 Minimum site area for certain buildings in Hyam Street Sub-precinct

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "Cl 8.11" on the *Clauses Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development on land to which this clause applies that will result in the erection of a building with a height that is greater than 11m unless the consent authority is satisfied—
- (a) the site area of the land is at least 9,500m2, and
- (b) the development will include a publicly accessible vehicular and pedestrian link.
