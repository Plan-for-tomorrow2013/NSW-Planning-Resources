# 8.7 Shops in Zone MU1 Mixed Use

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to limit the size of shops in Zone MU1 Mixed Use to ensure that land within Zone E2 Commercial Centre remains the principal retail area.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted for development for the purpose of a shop on land in Zone MU1 Mixed Use if the gross floor area of the shop is to be more than 400 square metres.
