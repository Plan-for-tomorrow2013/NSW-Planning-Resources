# 8.4 Minimum building street frontage

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure that buildings have a minimum width to provide for the efficient development of land and design of buildings.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a building that does not have at least one street frontage of 20 metres or more on land within Zone E2 Commercial Centre, Zone E3 Productivity Support or Zone MU1 Mixed Use.

**(3)**
Despite subclause (2), the consent authority may grant consent to the erection of a building on land referred to in that subclause if it is of the opinion that it is not physically possible for the building to be erected with at least one street frontage of 20 metres or more.
