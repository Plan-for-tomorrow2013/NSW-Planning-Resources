# 8.8 Use of certain land at Crown Street and Church Street, Wollongong

**(1)**
This clause applies to land identified as "14" on the *Key Sites Map*.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to development for the purposes of hotel or motel accommodation on the land unless the consent authority is satisfied of the following matters—
- (a) for 163-171 Crown Street and the part of Globe Lane on land identified as "14" on the *Key Sites Map*—the development will not include—
  - (i) a building with a height exceeding 80m, and
  - (ii) less than 200 rooms or self-contained suites for the purposes of hotel or motel accommodation,
- (b) for 80-82 Church Street and the part of Globe Way on land identified as "14" on the *Key Sites Map*—the development will—
  - (i) not include a building with a height exceeding 30m at Burelli Street and increasing at an angle of 4.0/6.6 from the horizontal to a height plane of 45m, and
  - (ii) be used for the purposes of hotel or motel accommodation, office premises or registered clubs,
- (c) the development will include all land to which this clause applies.

**(3)**
The following do not apply to development under this clause—
- (a) clause 4.3,
- (b) clause 4.4,
- (c) clause 4.4A,
- (d) clause 4.6(8)(ca), to the extent it relates to clause 8.3,
- (e) clause 7.18(4)(d),
- (f) clause 8.3.
