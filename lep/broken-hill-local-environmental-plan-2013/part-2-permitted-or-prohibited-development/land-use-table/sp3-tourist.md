# Zone SP3 Tourist

## 1 Objectives of zone

- To provide for a variety of tourist-oriented development and related uses.
- To facilitate and recognise existing tourist facilities within the mining area of Broken Hill.
- To protect and enhance the natural environment for recreational, educational and conservation purposes.

## 2 Permitted without consent

Car parks; Environmental facilities; Environmental protection works; Places of public worship; Plant nurseries; Roads

## 3 Permitted with consent

Aquaculture; Community facilities; Entertainment facilities; Food and drink premises; Function centres; Information and education facilities; Shops; Tourist and visitor accommodation

## 4 Prohibited

Any development not specified in item 2 or 3
