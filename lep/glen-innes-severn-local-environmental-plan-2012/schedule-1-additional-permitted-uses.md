# Schedule 1 Additional permitted uses

*(Clause 2.5)*

## 1 Use of certain land at Glen Innes Showground, Glen Innes

**(1)**
This clause applies to land at Glen Innes Showground, Bourke Street, Glen Innes, being Lot 156, DP 753282, and Lot 1, DP 16643, shown as "1" on the *Additional Permitted Uses Map*.

**(2)**
Development for the purposes of camping grounds and caravan parks is permitted with development consent.
