# 4.2B Erection of rural workers' dwellings

**(1)**
The objective of this clause is to ensure the provision of adequate accommodation for employees of existing agricultural or rural industries.

**(2)**
Development consent must not be granted to the erection of a rural worker's dwelling on land in Zone RU1 Primary Production unless the consent authority is satisfied that—
- (a) the development will not impair the use of the land for agricultural and rural industries, and
- (b) the agricultural or rural industry being carried out on the land has a demonstrated capacity to support the ongoing employment or rural workers, and
- (c) the development is necessary considering the nature of the agricultural or rural industry land use lawfully occurring on the land or as a result of the remote or isolated location of the land.
