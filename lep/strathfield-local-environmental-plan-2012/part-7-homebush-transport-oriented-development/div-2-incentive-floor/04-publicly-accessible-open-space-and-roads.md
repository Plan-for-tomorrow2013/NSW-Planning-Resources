# 7.10 Publicly accessible open space and roads

**(1)**
Column 2 of the following table specifies, for each Area specified in Column 1, the minimum amount of the site area that must be used for 1 or more of the following purposes—
- (a) pedestrian links,
- (b) public open space,
- (c) roads.
| Area on *Key Sites Map* | Minimum area (m2) |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2,450 |
| 2 | 1,650 |
| 3 | 600 |
| 4 | 600 |
| 5 | 450 |
| 6 | 3,900 |
| 7 | 4,100 |
| 8 | 3,800 |
| 9 | 3,650 |
| 10 | 1,750 |
| 11 | 3,100 |
| 12 | 500 |

**(2)**
In this clause—
*pedestrian link* includes the following—
- (a) a footpath,
- (b) a shared cycle and pedestrian pathway,
- (c) a shared zone within the meaning of the *Road Rules 2014*.
