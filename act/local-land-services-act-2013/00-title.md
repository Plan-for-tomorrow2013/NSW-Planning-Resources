# Local Land Services Act 2013

**See also—**

*Local Land Services Amendment (Private Native Forestry) Bill 2026* [Non-government Bill— the Hon M J Banasiak, MLC]

*Veterinary Services Bill 2026*

**whole Act:** Am 2026 No 15, Sch 2.91[1] (“local authority” omitted wherever occurring, “local council” inserted instead).

An Act to establish Local Land Services and to repeal the *Rural Lands Protection Act 1998* and the *Catchment Management Authorities Act 2003*; and for other purposes.
