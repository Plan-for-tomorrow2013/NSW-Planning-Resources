# Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999

### Part 19A—Reconsideration of fees

#### 514Y Applications for reconsideration of fee

(1) This section applies if:

- (a) a fee is worked out by a person to whom a function or power is delegated under:
  - (i) section 515 or 515AAA of this Act; or
  - (ii) for a fee in relation to a function or power of the CEO under this Act—subsection 59(1) of the *National Environmental Protection Agency Act 2025*; and
- (b) a method prescribed by the regulations under subsection 520(4C) of this Act has been used to work out the fee; and
- (c) the person required to pay the fee is dissatisfied with the way the method was used.

(2) The person may apply to the following persons for reconsideration of the way the method was used to work out the fee:

- (a) for a fee that relates to a function or power of the CEO under this Act—the CEO;
- (b) for all other fees—the Secretary.

(3) The application must:

- (a) be in a form prescribed by the regulations; and
- (b) set out the reasons for the application.

(4) The application must be made within 30 business days after the applicant is informed of the fee.

(5) A person may apply only once in relation to a fee.

#### 514YA Reconsideration of fee

(1) Upon receiving an application for reconsideration of a fee, the CEO or Secretary, as applicable, must:

- (a) reconsider the way the method was used to work out the fee; and
- (b) either:
  - (i) confirm the fee; or
  - (ii) work out a new fee by using the method again.

(1A) The person who undertakes the reconsideration of a fee that relates to a function or power of the CEO under this Act must be:

- (a) the CEO; or
- (b) a member of the staff of NEPA who:
  - (i) was not involved in working out the fee; and
  - (ii) occupies a position that is senior to that occupied by any person involved in working out the fee.

(2) The person who undertakes the reconsideration of any other fee must be:

- (a) the Secretary; or
- (b) an employee in the Department who:
  - (i) was not involved in working out the fee; and
  - (ii) occupies a position that is senior to that occupied by any person involved in working out the fee.

(3) The CEO or Secretary, as applicable, must give to the applicant a written notice that:

- (a) states the outcome of the reconsideration; and
- (b) gives reasons for that outcome.

#### 514YB Deadline for reconsideration

The CEO or Secretary, as applicable, must undertake a reconsideration of a fee within 30 business days after receiving an application for reconsideration.
