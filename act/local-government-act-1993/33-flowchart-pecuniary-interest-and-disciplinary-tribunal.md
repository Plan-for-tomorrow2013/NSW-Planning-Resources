# Local Government Act 1993

## Flowchart: Pecuniary Interest and Disciplinary Tribunal process (ss 468–486)

*(Flowchart typed out from the picture in the Act, not the Act’s own wording. Read top to bottom; indented items follow from the item above.)*

- Report of the Director-General (s 468)
  - Pecuniary Interest and Disciplinary Tribunal
    - Consideration of report (s 469)
      - Decision not to conduct proceedings into complaint (s 469 (2))
        - Written statement of decision and reasons to complainant, person against whom complaint was made and Director-General (s 469 (2))
      - Decision to conduct proceedings into complaint (s 469 (1)), with or without a hearing (s 470)
        - Conduct proceedings, with or without a hearing (ss 471–481)
          - Finding that complaint is not proven
          - Finding that complaint is proven (s 482), with:
            - Councillor (s 482 (1)):
              - counsel
              - reprimand
              - suspend for up to 6 months
              - disqualify for up to 5 years
              - suspend right to be paid for up to 6 months
            - Council employee (s 482 (2)):
              - Reprimand
              - Counsel
              - Recommend to council:
                - disciplinary action
                - dismissal
          - After a finding that the complaint is proven, Appeal to Supreme Court (s 485 (1)), then Order of Supreme Court (s 485 (4))
      - Refer matter to another authority (s 486)
