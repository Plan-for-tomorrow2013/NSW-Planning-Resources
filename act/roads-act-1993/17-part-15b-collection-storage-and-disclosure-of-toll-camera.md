# Roads Act 1993

## Part 15B Collection, storage and disclosure of toll camera images

**pt 15B:** Ins 2026 No 37, Sch 8[2].

### 261C Definitions

In this part—

***approved toll camera*** has the same meaning as in section 250A.

***photograph*** has the same meaning as in section 250A.

***relevant information*** means information acquired in the exercise of functions in connection with the use or operation of an approved toll camera, including a photograph taken by an approved toll camera under section 261D.

***serious indictable offence*** has the same meaning as in the *Crimes Act 1900*.

***toll camera image*** means a photograph taken by an approved toll camera.

**s 261C:** Ins 2026 No 37, Sch 8[2].

### 261D Photographs approved toll camera is permitted to take

An approved toll camera is permitted to take a photograph of the following—

- (a) a vehicle,
- (b) the driver of a vehicle,
- (c) a passenger in a vehicle, including a passenger who is less than 16 years of age.

**s 261D:** Ins 2026 No 37, Sch 8[2].

### 261E Prohibition on collection, storage and disclosure of toll camera images except for certain purposes

A person who acquires relevant information must not directly or indirectly make a record of, or make use of or disclose, the relevant information except as follows—

- (a) in the exercise of functions in connection with—
  - (i) the payment, collection and enforcement of tolls, or
  - (ii) another function prescribed in relation to tolls,
- (b) as permitted under section 261F or 261G.

Maximum penalty—50 penalty units.

**s 261E:** Ins 2026 No 37, Sch 8[2].

### 261F Collection, storage and disclosure of toll camera images for purposes relating to NSW Police Force

(1) A person may collect, store and disclose relevant information—

- (a) for the investigation of serious indictable offences by the NSW Police Force and the prosecution of the serious indictable offences, or
- (b) if it is reasonably necessary to locate a person who the NSW Police Force has been notified is missing, or
- (c) to prevent or lessen a serious threat to the life, or health or safety, of a person, including a child, or
- (d) to support an investigation into a death, suspected death, fire or explosion for the purposes of the *Coroners Act 2009*.

(2) A person must, if asked by the Law Enforcement Conduct Commission, collect, store or disclose the following information for the purposes of the Law Enforcement Conduct Commission’s functions—

- (a) relevant information,
- (b) information about the collection, storage and disclosure of relevant information under subsection (1).

**s 261F:** Ins 2026 No 37, Sch 8[2].

### 261G Disclosure of toll camera images to other authorised agencies

(1) A person may disclose relevant information to an authorised agency if—

- (a) either or both of the following apply—
  - (i) the person is—
    - (A) a toll operator, or
    - (B) an officer of a toll operator, or
    - (C) a person exercising functions on behalf of or otherwise acting under the authority of a toll operator,
  - (ii) the disclosure of the information is in accordance with a protocol approved by the Privacy Commissioner, and
- (b) the relevant information was acquired in relation to a vehicle driven in contravention of a requirement to pay a relevant toll.

(2) In this section—

***authorised agency*** means the following persons or agencies—

- (a) the Australian Crime Commission,
- (b) the Independent Commission Against Corruption,
- (c) the New South Wales Crime Commission,
- (d) the NSW Police Force,
- (e) the Law Enforcement Conduct Commission,
- (f) the Ombudsman,
- (g) another person or government agency prescribed for the purpose of this paragraph.

**s 261G:** Ins 2026 No 37, Sch 8[2].
