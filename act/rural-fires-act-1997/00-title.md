# Rural Fires Act 1997

**Does not include amendments by—**

*Environmental Planning and Assessment Amendment (Planning System Reforms) Act 2025* No 71 (not commenced)

*Building (Approvals and Practitioners) Act 2026* No 26 (not commenced)

**See also—**

*Emergency Services Legislation Amendment Bill 2026*

*Rural Fires Amendment (Fire Fighting Fleet) Bill 2026*

**124C:** Ins 2002 No 67, Sch 4 [22].

An Act to establish the NSW Rural Fire Service and define its functions; to make provision for the prevention, mitigation and suppression of rural fires; to repeal the *Bush Fires Act 1949*; to amend certain other Acts; and for other purposes.
