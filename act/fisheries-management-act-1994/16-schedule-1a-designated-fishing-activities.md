# Fisheries Management Act 1994

## Schedule 1A Designated fishing activities

(Section 7B)

### 1 Share management fisheries

Fishing activities for commercial purposes in a share management fishery specified in Schedule 1.

> **Note.** On the enactment of this Schedule, the abalone fishery and the lobster fishery were share management fisheries. On the commencement of Schedule 3 to the *Fisheries Management and Environmental Assessment Legislation Amendment Act 2000*, the ocean prawn trawl fishery, the ocean fish trawl fishery, the ocean hauling fishery, the ocean trap and line fishery, the estuary general fishery and the estuary prawn trawl fishery become share management fisheries.
