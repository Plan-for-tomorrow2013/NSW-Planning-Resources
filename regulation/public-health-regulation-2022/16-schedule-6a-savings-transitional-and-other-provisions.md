# Public Health Regulation 2022

## Schedule 6A Savings, transitional and other provisions

### Part 1 Provision consequent on enactment of Public Health Amendment (Terramation) Act 2026

#### 1 Effect of Part 8, Division 4A

Part 8, Division 4A does not have effect before the day that is 2 years after the date of assent to the *Public Health Amendment (Terramation) Act 2026*.

**sch 6A:** Ins 2026 No 51, Sch 1.2[3].
