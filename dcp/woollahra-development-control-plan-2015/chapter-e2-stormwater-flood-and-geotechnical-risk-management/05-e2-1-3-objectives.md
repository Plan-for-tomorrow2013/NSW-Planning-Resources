#### E2.1.3 Objectives

The objectives of this chapter are:

O1 To encourage ecologically sustainable stormwater management and the use of water sensitive urban design.

O2 To maintain existing natural drainage patterns.

O3 To ensure that adequate provision has been made for the disposal of stormwater from land proposed to be developed.

O4 To ensure the controlled release of stormwater to public stormwater systems without adversely impacting on adjoining or downstream properties.

O5 To protect Sydney Harbour and its waterways from stormwater pollution.

O6 To minimise flood risk and damage to people and property by setting appropriate development controls.

O7 To ensure that flood levels are not increased by development.

O8 To minimise risks and impacts relating to excavation, subterranean buildings and dewatering works.
