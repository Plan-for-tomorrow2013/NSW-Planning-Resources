#### E2.1.1 Land and development to which this chapter applies

#### Stormwater drainage

This chapter applies to all land within the Woollahra Municipality.

#### Flood risk management

The flood risk management component of this chapter applies to all land within the Woollahra Municipality that is within a “flood risk precinct”.

#### Ground water and geotechnical risk management

The groundwater (hydrogeology) and geotechnical component of this chapter applies to all land within the Woollahra Municipality.
