#### 7 and 8. Combinations and interrelationships

It is the combination of, and interrelationships between, these various elements - the portentous entrance gates, the carriage drive and loop, the classically styled building, the billiard room, the tennis court, the swimming pool - that demonstrated the uses, activities and lifestyles of the successive upper-middle class occupants; in other words, that give the whole place its cultural meaning.  By themselves, some of these items are of little if any inherent significance (as the list demonstrates): it is the role that, cumulatively, they play in contributing to the overall use, understanding and interpretation of the place that gives them significance.
