## 11. Vegetation and its role

Numerous mature trees frame and screen the site, particularly on the south and west.  One – the Bunya pine - is listed on Council’s register of significant trees.  A few trees are mid to late 19th century plantings, and demonstrate the landscaping style and taste in plants prevalent in Woollahra at that time.  They also provide an indication of the attitudes and lifestyles of the occupants of the site, and contribute both aesthetic value and amenity.  As LandArc’s Landscape Heritage Assessment Report (1994) stated: “The sum total of all these trees adds enormously to the visual quality and asset value of the property”.  According to the Woollahra History and Heritage Society, “it is the largest remaining cluster of garden and trees on the Edgecliff escarpment”.  They should therefore be conserved. Note: Council consent is required for any tree works affecting trees or other vegetation that are prescribed under the Woollahra DCP.
