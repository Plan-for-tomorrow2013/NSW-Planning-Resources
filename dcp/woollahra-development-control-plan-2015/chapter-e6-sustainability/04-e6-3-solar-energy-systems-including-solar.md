#### E6.3 Solar energy systems (including solar panels, solar hot water systems and solar heating systems)

A solar energy system includes a photovoltaic electricity generating system, solar hot water system, or solar air heating system.

These solar energy systems are often exempt or complying development under the State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021.

However, a development application is required to be lodged for a solar energy system when the system does not meet the standards for exempt or complying development in the Transport and Infrastructure SEPP. The controls also provide guidelines for the design and location of solar energy systems that form part of other works that require the development consent.  For example, a development application for a dwelling house may include solar energy systems.  In these cases, the solar energy system is assessed as part of the development proposal for the new building.

Note: primary road in this clause refers to the road to which the front of a dwelling house, or a main building, on a lot faces or is proposed to face.
