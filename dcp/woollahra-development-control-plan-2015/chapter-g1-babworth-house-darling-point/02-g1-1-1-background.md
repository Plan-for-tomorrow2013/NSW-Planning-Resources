#### G1.1 Introduction

#### G1.1.1 Background

This chapter provides detailed controls for development on the Babworth House site and seeks to ensure the conservation of Babworth House, significant cultural landscape features and out-buildings.

Note: This chapter reflects the site specific development control plan adopted by Council on 15 June 1999, and which commenced on 19 November 1999.
