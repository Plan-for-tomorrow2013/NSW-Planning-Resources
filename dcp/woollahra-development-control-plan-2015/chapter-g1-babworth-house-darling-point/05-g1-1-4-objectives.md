#### G1.1.4 Objectives

The objectives of this chapter are:

O1 To maintain the cultural (heritage significance of Babworth House in the context of its cultural landscape setting.

O2 To retain the principal heritage and cultural landscape features of the site.

O3 To facilitate appropriate development of the site and uses within Babworth House.
