#### Chapter B3 General Development Controls

The general controls in Chapter B3 apply to all land where Chapter B1 applies.

Development is required to fulfil the relevant requirements of all the general controls. Unless otherwise indicated, where there is a disparity between the objectives and controls in Chapters B1 and B3, the precinct desired future character statements and objectives in this chapter takes precedence over the general objectives and controls.
