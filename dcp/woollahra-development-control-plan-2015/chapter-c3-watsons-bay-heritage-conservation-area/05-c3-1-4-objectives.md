#### C3.1.4 Objectives

The objectives of this chapter are:

O1 To facilitate the implementation of the objectives and provisions relating to heritage conservation in Woollahra LEP 2014.

O2 To acknowledge and conserve the unique built and natural heritage significance of Watsons Bay including places of importance for Aboriginal people.

O3 To require the retention and appropriate development of heritage items and contributory items.

O4 To ensure that proposed development is compatible with the significance of the Watsons Bay HCA and the character of its individual precincts.

O5 To provide controls that encourage contemporary design which responds appropriately to the character of Watsons Bay and the identified heritage values of the area.

O6 To encourage and promote public awareness, appreciation, understanding and knowledge of heritage conservation.

O7 To enhance amenity and heritage values within Watsons Bay.
