#### C3.3.10 Acoustic and visual privacy

Privacy is a major determinant of the ability of residents and neighbours to enjoy their homes. The acoustic and visual privacy needs of residents are to influence all aspects of design, including the location of new building works, building scale, the placement of windows, the location of main living rooms in a building and the type of materials and construction techniques.

#### Objectives

O1 To ensure an adequate degree of acoustic and visual privacy in building design.

O2 To minimise the impact of new development on the acoustic and visual privacy of existing development on neighbouring lands.
