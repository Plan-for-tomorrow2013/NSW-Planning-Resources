#### C3.1.2 Land where this chapter applies

This chapter applies to the land identified in Map 1 on the following page.

This predominantly comprises land identified as the Watsons Bay HCA in Woollahra Local Environmental Plan 2014 (Woollahra LEP 2014), but also includes other land, such as HMAS Watson.
