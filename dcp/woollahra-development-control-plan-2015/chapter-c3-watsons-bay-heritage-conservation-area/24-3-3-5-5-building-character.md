#### 3.3.5.5 Building character

Watsons Bay retains a significant built form that has the character of a low-scaled 19th and 20th century village. A variety of building forms and styles exist throughout the area. Distinct precincts are evident throughout the area and individual streetscapes are varied in terms of architectural style and character.

#### Objectives

O1 To ensure that the character of development enhances the streetscape by remaining in harmony with adjacent contributory items and minimising the visual impact upon the immediate area.

O2 To ensure that the design of development responds to the character and style of adjacent and surrounding contributory items.

O3 To promote sympathetic contemporary design that responds to the historic character of the area.
