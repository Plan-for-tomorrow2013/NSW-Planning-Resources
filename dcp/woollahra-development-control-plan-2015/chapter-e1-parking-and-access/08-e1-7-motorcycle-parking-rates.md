#### E1.7 Motorcycle parking rates

Motorcycles are defined as any powered two-wheel vehicle, including motorbikes, scooters and mopeds.

#### E1.7.1 Calculating required motorcycle parking

#### Change of use

Where there is an intensification of parking based on the parking rates of this chapter, the amount of parking required will equal the difference between the parking generated by the proposed development and the parking generated by the current use as calculated by the rates in this chapter.

#### Alterations and additions

For proposals involving additional floor space, required parking shall be calculated using the rate specified in this chapter.

#### New development

Where a building is to be totally demolished and replaced, parking will be provided at the rate specified in this chapter.  No parking credits will be allowed for the current building and its use.
