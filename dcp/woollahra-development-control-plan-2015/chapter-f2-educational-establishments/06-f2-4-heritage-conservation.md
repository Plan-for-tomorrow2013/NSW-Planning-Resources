#### F2.4 Heritage conservation

Established schools are a source of cultural and heritage significance. Woollahra LEP 2014 lists a number of schools as containing heritage items, and several are located within heritage conservation areas.  As part of a development application relating to, or in the vicinity of, a heritage item the Council may require the submission of a heritage impact statement or conservation management plan or both.
