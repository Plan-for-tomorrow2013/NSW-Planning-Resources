The heritage inventory sheets for the above heritage conservation areas should be used as guiding documents for development in these areas. Heritage inventory sheets are available on the Heritage NSW website for existing heritage conservation areas here: www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/heritage/search-heritage-databases/state-heritage-inventory

or on Council’s website for new and amended heritage conservation areas here: www.bmcc.nsw.gov.au/heritage/heritage-conservation-areas

C1. Development within these conservation areas is to be in accordance with the provisions of Parts D9 Development controls for heritage properties and D10 Specific building elements.
