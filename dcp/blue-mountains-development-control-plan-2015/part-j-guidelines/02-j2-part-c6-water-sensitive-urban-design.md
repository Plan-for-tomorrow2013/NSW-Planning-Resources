### J2 PART C6:  Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD)

### guidelines for large scale development

The WSUD strategy and stormwater management system is to be appropriately designed and sized in a manner consistent with the latest guidelines and current best management practices for WSUD The latest WSUD guidelines include but are not limited to:

(a) Using MUSIC in Sydney’s Drinking Water Catchment A WaterNSW Current Recommended Practice (2023) by WaterNSW (for use in Sydney’s Drinking Water Catchments) (www.waternsw.com.au/__data/ assets/pdf_file/0020/58250/Using-MUSIC-in-the-Sydney-Drinking- Water-Catchment.pdf)

(b) Concept Design Guidelines for WSUD by Water by Design SEQ Version 1.1 April 2010 (waterbydesign.com.au/download/concept-design-guidelines-for-water-sensitive-urban-design)

(c) Construction and Establishment Guidelines by Water by Design SEQ Version 1.1 April 2010 (waterbydesign.com.au/download/wsud-construction-guidelines)

(d) Water Sensitive Urban Design – Technical Design Guidelines for South East Queensland, by Water by Design SEQ 2006 (waterbydesign.com. au/download/water-sensitive-urban-design-technical-design-guidelines-forsouth-east-queensland)

(e) Maintaining Vegetated Stormwater Assets by Water by Design SEQ 2012 (waterbydesign.com.au/download/maintaining-vegetated-assets)

(f) Adoption Guidelines for Stormwater Biofiltration Systems (Version 2) by the Cooperative Research Centre for Water Sensitive Cities, 2015 (watersensitivecities.org.au/content/stormwater-biofilter-design/)

(g) Australian Runoff Quality - A Guide to Water Sensitive Urban Design by Engineers Australia, 2006 (www.engineersaustralia.org.au/sites/default/ files/Learned%20Society/Resources-Guidelines%26Practice%20notes/ Australian_Runoff_Quality-Guide_to_WSUD.pdf)

(h) WSUD Engineering Procedures Stormwater by Melbourne Water dated 2005 (www.publish.csiro.au/book/4974)

(i) Managing Urban Stormwater: Harvesting and Reuse by the NSW Department of Environment and Conservation, 2006 (www.environment. nsw.gov.au/publications/managing-urban-stormwater-harvesting-and-reuse)

(j) Developments in Sydney’s Drinking Water Catchment – Water Quality Information Requirements by WaterNSW (as relevant to the Sydney Drinking Water Catchment). (www.waternsw.com.au/__data/assets/ pdf_file/0008/134747/Developments-in-the-Sydney-Drinking-Water- Catchment.pdf)

(k) Water Sensitive Design Guide for Rural Residential Subdivisions, Water Sensitive Urban Design (www.waternsw.com.au/__data/assets/pdf_ file/0003/56478/Water-Sensitive-Design-Guide-for-Rural-Residential- Subdivisions.pdf);

(l) Section 10 and WSUD drawings in the Western Sydney Engineering Design Manual (www.bmcc.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/docs/ WesternSydneyEngineeringDesignManual_Oct2022.pdf)

Note: These documents provides relevant general technical guidance however may include stormwater treatment targets or other criteria that are different to targets required by Blue Mountains City Council).
