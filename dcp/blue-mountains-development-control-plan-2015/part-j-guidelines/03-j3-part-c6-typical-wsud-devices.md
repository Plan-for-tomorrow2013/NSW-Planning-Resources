### J3 PART C6: Typical WSUD devices

The WSUD strategy is to include a treatment train that is to comprise all necessary stormwater treatment devices, to provide the retention, filtering, infiltration and safe disposal necessary to meet the required stormwater treatment performance outcomes. The WSUD strategy is to include appropriate water quality and quantity treatment devices, such as:

(a) Rainwater tanks and reuse

(b) Grey water treatment and tanks for re-use

(c) Detention basins

(d) Sediment basins

(e) Gross pollutant capture devices

(f) Oil/grit/grease/water separators

(g) Vegetated filter strips

(h) Grass or vegetated swales

(i) Bio-retention swales or basins

(j) Sand filter beds

Part J: Guidelines

(k) Porous pavements

(l) Infiltration measures

(m) Constructed wetlands

Note - Adaptability to climate change should be considered in the planning, design and management of WSUD strategies and infrastructure, particularly with regard to long term stormwater harvesting schemes and landscaping species selection. Consider potential changes in the volume and frequency of rainfall, temperatures and other climatic conditions, bushfire and storm recurrence and intensity and ecological succession.
