### E2.3. Access

#### Explanation

The efficient functioning of the Blue Mountains relies upon safe, logical and appropriate access within and through the local government area, and between the public road network and private property. In this regard access is considered a fundamental requirement for all development. Issues such as access and egress in medical or bush fire emergencies, waste collection, convenience of users and visitors are important considerations.

#### Controls

C1. All properties, development and new lots are required to have a legally constituted physical access from a formed public road.

C2. Access is to be located, designed and constructed so as to minimise environmental impacts, and avoid environmentally sensitive land unless no other alternative is available.

C3. All access is to be safe, convenient and practical, and constructed in accordance with the relevant standard.
