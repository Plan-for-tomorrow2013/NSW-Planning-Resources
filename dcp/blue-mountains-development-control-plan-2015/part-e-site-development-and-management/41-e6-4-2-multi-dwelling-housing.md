### E6.4.2.	 Multi dwelling housing

Waste and recycling storage areas within multi dwelling housing developments can affect residential amenity and the safety of both residents and collection contractors. It is important that these areas are integrated within the built form of the development and designed to enhance the overall development. Note: Council does not support the use of private waste collection for this development type.

#### Controls

C1. This section applies to ‘multi dwelling housing’ and housing for seniors and people with a disability as defined in the Blue Mountains LEP 2015.

Part E6: Waste Management

C2. For developments comprising up to 5 dwellings, please refer to the controls applying to ‘low density residential development’ (E6.4.1) unless thecharacteristics of the site will restrict or impeded safe access for residents or collection contractors.

C3. MA communal on-site waste storage area must be provided for residential developments in the following circumstances:

(a) Contain 6 or more dwellings; or

(b) Include ‘mixed use developments’, defined in accordance with the Blue Mountains LEP 2015; or

(c) Where the number of bins would not fit comfortably along the development street frontage or where the placement on the nature strip would have a detrimental effect on the residential amenity; or

(d) Where the characteristics of the site inhibit the provision of a safe and efficient waste collection service; and

(e) In accordance with the provisions outlined within the Blue Mountains ‘Multi Dwelling Waste Management Guideline’ document.

C4. A communal on-site waste storage area to be provided and must be of sufficient size to accommodate the full bin allocation associated with the development. This is to be achieved through the provision of a designated communal on-site waste storage area which:

(a) Provides direct and accessible access for the occupants of the development, and

(b) Provides safe and unobstructed access to transfer bins from the storage area to the collection point, and

(c) Does not impact the amenity of the occupants within and adjoining the development in relation to visual, noise and odour, and

(d) Does not interfere with the car parking (on or off street), driveways and footpaths, and

(e) Councils standard waste collection vehicle can access the designated collection point if required to undertake on-site collection to permit a safe and efficient waste collection service, and

(f) Complies with the Blue Mountains ‘Multi Dwellings Waste Management Guideline’.

Part E6: Waste Management

C5. Where the communal on-site waste storage area is enclosed/secured, and Council is required to access bins under a wheel-out wheel-back arrangement, a locking mechanism to be provided in accordance with Councils specifications. The installation of the lock and provision of keys to be provided at the developer’s expense. Specifications of Councils lock system will be provided upon request to Councils Waste and Sustainability Department

C6. The development is to provide an integrated on-site bulky collection area within the development. This is to be achieved through the provision of a   designated communal on-site bulky waste storage area which:

(a) Provides direct and accessible access for the occupants of the development, and

(b) Provides safe and unobstructed access for the transfer of bulky items for residential dwellings to the collection area, and

(c) Is located close to the on-site waste storage area, and

(d) Does not impact the amenity of the occupants within and adjoining the development in relation to visual, noise and odour, and

(e) Designed in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Multi Dwellings Waste Management Guideline’ document.
