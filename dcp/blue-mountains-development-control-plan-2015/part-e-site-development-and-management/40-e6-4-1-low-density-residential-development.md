### E6.4. Operational waste management

### E6.4.1.	 Low density residential development

#### Explanation

Appropriate  design of waste and recyclable storage areas within residential development can provide for  ease of use and limited amenity loss associated with the movement and handling of waste for the life of the development. In the Blue Mountains, the majority of housing stock takes the form of low density development and it is therefore important that the design and operation of waste management within homes provides for optimal waste minimisation. Low density residential development includes single dwellings, secondary dwellings, semi-detached dwellings, dual occupancy and housing for seniors and people with a disability (for developments up to 5 dwellings). Note: Council does not support the use of private waste collection for this development type.

#### Controls

C1. The location and design of waste and recycling storage areas are to:

(a) be located behind the building line and not visible from the street, and

(b) avoid noise and odour impacts to maintain the amenity of the adjoining properties, and

(c) ensure the distance of travel between the storage location and the designated collection point is minimised and permits unobstructed accessible resident access to place bins kerbside, and

(d) be on a hardstand area that allows sufficient access to, and operation of, bins and can accommodate Councils full bin allocation, and

(e) ensure on-site infrastructure is provided in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Residential Waste Management Guideline (including Subdivisions)’ document.

C2. Provision to be made for sufficient space within the kitchen (or alternate location within the dwelling) for the interim storage of waste, organic material and recyclables.

C3. The provision of composting areas is encouraged. These are to be within designated areas that will not impact on the amenity of adjoining properties.

C4. The nominated kerbside collection point to not impede pedestrian traffic and safety and specifications demonstrated in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Residential Waste Management Guideline (including Subdivisions)’ document.
