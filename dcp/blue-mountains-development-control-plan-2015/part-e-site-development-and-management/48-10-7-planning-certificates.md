### 10.7 Planning Certificates

Under Section 10.7 of the EP&A Act a person may request a Planning Certificate from Council containing specific information about the land. A Section 10.7(2) Planning Certificate includes information on prescribed matters arising under the Contaminated Land Management Act 1997.  A Section 10.7(5) Planning Certificate will stipulate if Council records indicate if any activities listed in Table 1 of the Guidelines have occurred on the site, and if there is any site investigations held by Council, and/or any notification of remediation, and/or copies of any site audit statement held by Council.
