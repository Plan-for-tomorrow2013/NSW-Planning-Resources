### E6.4.3.	 Residential Flat Buildings

On-site waste collection areas integrated within residential flat buildings can enhance urban design principles, increase the developments amenity and facilitate the provision of a safe and efficient waste collection service for residents and collection contractors.

Note: Council does not support the use of private waste collection for this development type.

#### Controls

C1. This section applies to ‘residential flat building’ as defined in the Blue Mountains LEP 2015.

C2. The development to provide an integrated on-site waste storage

area. This is to be achieved through the provision of the following infrastructure:

Part E6: Waste Management

(a) The room to be integrated wholly within the buildings ground floor. Internal direct and accessible access is required for residents from the residential corridor, and

(b) The room to provide a separate unobstructed external access to permit scheduled servicing by the designated waste collection contractors. If a wheel-out wheel-back service is provided, the locking mechanism is to be provided in accordance with Councils specifications. The installation of the lock and provision of keys to be provided at the developer’s expense, and

(c) The room is to provide light, ventilation, wash facilities and drainage in accordance with the Building Code of Australia and Australian Standard provisions, and

(d) An unobstructed internal height clearance of 2.6m to be provided to permit unobstructed access for residents and collection contractors free from utilities and service infrastructure, and

(e) On-site infrastructure to be provided in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Residential Flat Building Waste Management Guideline’ document.

C3. The development is to provide an integrated on-site bulky collection room within the buildings ground floor in accordance with the provisions outline in E6.4.3 (C2). Additional provisions to be incorporated into the rooms design include:

(a) The room to be located adjacent to the integrated on-site waste storage room, and

(b) On-site Bulky Collection infrastructure to be provided in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Residential Flat Building Waste Management Guideline’ document.

C4. The following kerbside collection configuration criteria applies to buildings up to three storeys:

(a) Where Council determines that the site characteristics allow a kerbside collection, utilising Council’s standard waste collection may be proposed.

Part E6: Waste Management

(b) A loading zone restriction (no parking – waste and removalist vehicles excepted) is to be implemented along the developments frontage to permit the schedule loading of Councils standard waste collection vehicle. The restriction to be placed on the designated collection days determined by Council. This is to be approved by Councils Local Traffic Committee prior to implementation and installed at the developers’ expense.

(c) On-site infrastructure to be provided in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Residential Flat Building Waste Management Guideline’ document.

C5. The following collection configuration criteria applies to buildings of four of more storeys:

(a) Adequate and safe on-site access must be provided for Councils standard waste collection vehicle.

(b) A chute system to be installed in accordance with E6.4.3

(c) The route must be designed to permit Councils standard waste collection vehicle to enter and exit the site in a forward direction with limited or no need to reverse

(d) The route of travel, height clearances, ramp grades, swept path models and supporting on-site infrastructure to be 	d e s i g n e d and comply with AS2890.2 for a Heavy Rigid Vehicle (HRV).

(e) The designated waste collection loading bay to be clearly nominated on the developments submitted architectural plans. Access to be demonstrated by supporting swept path models with 500mm clearances provided from all on-site infrastructure during the vehicle’s ingress/egress maneouvres.

(f) Where on-site waste collection is proposed, the on-site waste storage area and bulky goods collection rooms to be located adjacent to the on-site loading bay.

(g) To permit a safe and efficient collection service, a Deed of Agreement (DoA) will need to be made with Council. Contact Council’s Waste and Sustainability Department. In preparation of the agreement, a hazard assessment will be undertaken by Council prior to formalisation of the agreement.

(h) On-site infrastructure to be provided in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Residential Flat Building Waste Management Guideline’.

C6. The development is to incorporate a single chute system for buildings of four of more storeys. The chute system and on-site collection configuration to incorporate the following:

Part E6: Waste Management

(a) A single chute inlet for Councils general waste stream to be provided on each residential level. The inlets to be located within close proximity to the central elevator core

(b) The chute inlets to be located within cupboards, with a maximum depth of 150mm and incorporate the following; self-closing sealed doors, mechanical ventilation, waterproofing and permit accessible resident access

(c) A waste chute room to be provided within the basement to collect the general waste from the single chute system. The room to provide light, ventilation, wash facilities and drainage in accordance with the Building Code of Australia and Australian Standard provisions.

(d) The single chute system be of sufficient size to accommodate a linear or circular carousel system and permit adequate services clearances. The chute single chute system, chute room and supporting infrastructure is to be designed in accordance with Councils ‘Residential Flat Buildings Waste Management Guideline’.

(e) A bin tug device to be provided to transport the bins from the waste chute room to the designated on-site waste loading bay/ collection point.

(f) Adjacent to the chute inlet on each residential level, a bin cupboard to be provided large enough to accommodate a minimum of 1x 240L recycling bin. The cupboard to incorporate; self-closing sealed doors, mechanical ventilation, waterproofing and permit accessible resident access

(g) A suitable system to be provided to transport the 240L recycling bins located within the bin cupboards on each residential level to the designated on-site waste loading bay/ collection point.

C7. Council may review alternative and innovative on-site waste management systems for high density developments which deliver sound urban design and environmental outcomes for the development and wider community. The applicant is encouraged to discuss the innovative solutions with Councils Waste and Sustainability Department and during scheduled Council Pre-lodgment meetings.

Part E6: Waste Management

(a) Alternative solutions to be reviewed in accordance with the provisions outlined within the Blue Mountains ‘Residential Flat Building Waste Management Guideline’ document.

C8. Agents of the owners corporation are to take responsibility for the management of waste and recyclable materials generated within the

site. Arrangements are to be in place in regards to the management, maintenance and cleaning of all waste/recycling management facilities.
