### E6.4.4.	 Industrial and Commercial

Industrial and commercial developments provide opportunities for innovative waste minimisation technologies, resource recovery practices and integrated infrastructure configurations.

Note: Council supports the use of private waste collection for this development type.

#### Controls

C1. The term ‘commercial’ is defined in the Blue Mountains LEP 2015.

C2. The term ‘industrial’ is defined in the Blue Mountains LEP 2015.

C3. The development is to provide designated on-site waste collection room/s integrated wholly within the developments built form to accommodate all waste streams generated for the site. The room/s and supporting infrastructure to be designed in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Industrial, Commercial and Mixed-Use Waste Management Guideline’ document provisions.

C4. Each tenancy to keep written records of the collection contractor engaged for the scheduled collection and disposal of the waste streams generated from the site. The collection contractor to be licensed for the safe and efficient collection of each waste stream.

C5. A Plan of Operations to be submitted accompanying the development proposal, outlining how waste will be managed on-site. The plan to be provided in accordance with Blue Mountains ‘Industrial, Commercial and Mixed-Use Waste Management Guideline’.

C6. The waste collection room to provide separate bin infrastructure for each waste stream produced. Standard and consistent signage to be clearly displayed outlining each waste steam on-site.

C7. Collection to be undertaken at times to minimise the adverse impacts upon residential amenity, pedestrian and vehicle movements.

Part E6: Waste Management

C8. Provide sufficient space for waste collection infrastructure within each individual tenancy, room, communal area and food preparation area within the development for the interim storage of all waste produced on-site.

C9. Premises that discharge trade waste are only permitted to do so following the attainment of a written agreement from Sydney Water. A copy of the agreement to be accompanied with the development application.

C10. Premises that generates putrescible waste (seafood, meat, poultry, vegetables and organic material etc) are to have the waste stream/s collected daily or items stored within a dedicated on-site refrigerated waste storage/collection area.

C11. The storage and disposal of liquid waste such as oils and chemicals to comply with the provisions outlined within the ‘Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997’.

C12. Vehicles proposed to service the development to incorporate the following:

(a) Adequate and safe on-site access must be provided for the proposed waste collection vehicle

(b) The route must be designed to permit the waste collection vehicle to enter and exit the site in a forward direction with limited or no need to reverse.

(c) The route of travel, height clearances, ramp grades, swept path models and supporting on-site infrastructure to be designed and comply with AS2890.2 Parking Facilities: Off Street Commercial Facilities for a Heavy Rigid Vehicle (HRV).

(d) The designated on-site waste collection loading bay to be clearly nominated on the developments submitted architectural plans. Access to be demonstrated by supporting swept path models with 500mm clearances provided from all on-site infrastructure during the vehicles ingress/egress maneouvres.

(e) The on-site waste storage/collection area/s to be located adjacent to the on-site loading bay.

Part E6: Waste Management

C13. Council may review alternative and innovative on-site waste management systems for industrial, commercial and mixed-use developments which deliver sound urban design and environmental outcomes for the development and wider community. The applicant is encouraged to discuss the innovative solutions with Councils Waste and Sustainability Department and during scheduled Council pre-lodgment meetings.

(a) Alternative solutions will be reviewed in accordance with the Blue Mountains ‘Industrial, Commercial and Mixed-Use Waste Management Guideline’ document provisions.
