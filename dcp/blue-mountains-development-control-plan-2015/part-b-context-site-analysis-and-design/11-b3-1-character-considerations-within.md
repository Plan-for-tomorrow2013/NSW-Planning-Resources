### B3.1. Character considerations within precincts

This part applies to development in town centres and surrounding areas identified as precincts areas on the LEP 2015 Built Character Map. The primary controls for these areas are within Part G Locality Management of this DCP. This section provides further guidelines to supplement the specific controls within Part G. This includes considerations for urban design, infill development, design of shopfront buildings and medium-density residential development.
