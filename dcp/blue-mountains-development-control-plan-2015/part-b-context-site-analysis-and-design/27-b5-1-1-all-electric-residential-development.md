### B5.1. Towards Net Zero

### B5.1.1.	 All-electric Residential Development

#### Explanation

The use of fossil fuels such as gas and coal are worsening climate change. Gas appliances - including stovetops, heaters and hot water systems - are also more expensive to run and have health implications, including a link to childhood asthma. Council is encouraging a transition away from natural gas and a move towards all-electric appliances, which can be powered by renewable energy.

#### Objectives

O1. Reduce the indoor air pollutants associated with the onsite combustion of gas to improve air quality for occupants.

O2. Maximise operational cost savings to occupants.

#### Controls

Part B5: Resilience and Health

C1. For development applications lodged after 1 October 2026, internal gas cooktops, gas ovens, gas internal space heating systems and gas hot water systems are not to be included in a new residential dwelling. Instead, electric systems should be installed and clearly marked on plans.

C2. Notwithstanding C1, a new secondary dwelling may include gas appliances where it is proposed in conjunction with an existing principal dwelling that has an existing gas connection.

C3. Swimming pool heating systems are recommended to be:

• Solar thermal only

• Solar thermal boosted with electric heat pump, or

• Electric heat pump

Note: Consideration of Control 1 is encouraged for alterations and additions.

Note: Where solar access is compromised by large trees on the subject site or an adjoining property, application of these controls will be subject to a merit assessment.
