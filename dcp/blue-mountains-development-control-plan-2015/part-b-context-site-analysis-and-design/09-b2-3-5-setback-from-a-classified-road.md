### B2.3.5.	 Setback from a Classified Road

C1. Where land adjoins a Classified Road, other than a Tourist Road, the setback of any buildings from the alignment or proposed alignment of the road is to comply with Part B2 - Table 1.

Note: Classified Road has the same meaning as in the Roads Act 1993 and includes the Great Western Highway, Hawkesbury Road, Darling Causeway and Bell’s Line of Road.

Part B2 - Table 1: Setback from Classified Roads.

| Zone | Setback |
|---|---|
| C3 Environmental Management | 30m |
| Any other zone | 18m |

Part B2: Building Envelope

C2. Council may consent to development that does not comply with C1 only if it is satisfied that the existing development on the land or the physical or functional circumstances of the land would warrant a lesser setback, and this would not result in the creation of a traffic hazard.
