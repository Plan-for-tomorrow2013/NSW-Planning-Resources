### B2.2. Floor Space Ratio

#### Explanation

The floor space ratio (FSR) of buildings is the ratio of the gross floor area of all buildings within a site to the site area. FSR controls seek to regulate bulk and scale of buildings and prevent over development of a site. FSR controls apply in all zones.

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure development is compatible with the bulk, scale and character of existing and future surrounding development and is appropriate to the site.

#### Controls

C1. The floor space ratio of a development is not to exceed the maximum floor space ratio set by LEP 2015 clause 4.4 (Floor space ratio) and the Floor Space Ratio Map.
