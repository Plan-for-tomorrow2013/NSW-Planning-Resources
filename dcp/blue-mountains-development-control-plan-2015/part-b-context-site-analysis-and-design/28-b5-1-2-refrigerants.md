### B5.1.2.	 Refrigerants

#### Explanation

Synthetic refrigerant gases commonly used in air conditioning systems often have a high Global Warming Potential (GWP). The GWP is the number of times the refrigerant is more harmful to the atmosphere than carbon. Some refrigerant gases with the highest GWP are being phased out in Australia, e.g. R22 which has a GWP of 1810, meaning it is 1810 more harmful than carbon. Priority should be given to using refrigerants with the lowest GWP commonly available. Leakage from air conditioning systems or the improper disposal of refrigerant can be a significant source of greenhouse gas emissions. Using refrigerants with a low GWP will reduce the impact of the emissions from air conditioning systems.

#### Objectives

O1. Reduce the greenhouse gas emissions associated with the leakage or improper disposal of synthetic refrigerant gases with high Global Warming Potential (GWP).

#### Controls

Part B5: Resilience and Health

C1. This part applies to any commercial development with a floor area over 1000m2.

All new air conditioning and refrigeration equipment are to use refrigerants with a GWP of less than 10;

(a) if the equipment can be supplied on similar terms to conventional systems, and

(b) at a cost of not more than 10% higher than the market rate for conventional systems.
