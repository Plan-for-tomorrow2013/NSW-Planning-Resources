### B4.1.1.	 PDA Setback Controls

#### Controls

C1. For land consisting of or including an area of greater than 4,000m2 that is zoned C3 Environmental Management or C4 Environmental Living (large lots) the PDA setback from any boundary is to be a minimum of 10 metres
