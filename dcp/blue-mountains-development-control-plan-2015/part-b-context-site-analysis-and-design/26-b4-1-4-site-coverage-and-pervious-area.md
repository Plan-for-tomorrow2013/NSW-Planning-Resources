### Introduction

As one of only two cities in the world within a World Heritage National Park, the Blue Mountains local government area is perfectly placed to be a leader in responding to climate change and striving to be a City that models sustainability and sustainable living.

In 2019, Blue Mountains City Council was the third council in NSW to declare a climate emergency, and Council has pledged to reach net zero emissions across the Blue Mountains LGA by the 2040s or sooner.

The residential sector is responsible for 75% of all community emissions across the Blue Mountains (Resilient Sydney emissions data 2021-2022).

Reducing fossil fuel consumption of households, through appliance selection and building design, is an important step in reaching net zero emissions across the City.
