### C5.3. Trees which are not prescribed (exempt)

#### Explanation

In accordance with the Biodiversity and Conservation SEPP, this part of the DCP describes trees which are not prescribed by this development control plan, and therefore do not require development consent or a permit granted by the Council to undertake any of the activities identified by the SEPP.

#### Controls

C1. Trees which are not prescribed (exempt) include:

(a) Any tree species listed in ‘Weeds of the Blue Mountains’ in Part

C2.3 of this DCP, except:

i. if it exceeds the nominated height limitation in Part C2.3, or

ii. if it is located on any land to which  Part D Heritage Man-agement of this DCP applies.

(b) Any non-indigenous species located on a Council owned and maintained bushland reserve, irrespective of height, where a Council directed bushland restoration program is operational.

Part C5: Tree and Vegetation Preservation

(c) Any tree which poses an imminent danger to human life, buildings or other property as a result of a bush fire burning in the vicinity of the land on which the tree is situated.

(d) Any tree that complies with the 10/50 Vegetation Clearing Code of Practice prepared by the Commissioner of the NSW Rural Fire Service.

(e) Trees that are dead or dying and are not required as the habitat of native animals or pose a risk to property or life (if not part of mapped biodiversity areas).

(f) Vegetation removal as part of traditional Aboriginal cultural activities.

Note: Where a tree is pruned or removed under the exemption provisions, evidence of the condition or characteristics of the tree is to be retained by the owner for a minimum of six months and presented to the Council upon request. That evidence may include:

• detailed photos of the tree; or

• where the photographs do not provide adequate or clear description of the circumstances, a declaration from a third party which corroborates the facts regarding the removal. Note: All exempt vegetation must meet the documentation requirements specified in State Environmental Planning Policy (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021
