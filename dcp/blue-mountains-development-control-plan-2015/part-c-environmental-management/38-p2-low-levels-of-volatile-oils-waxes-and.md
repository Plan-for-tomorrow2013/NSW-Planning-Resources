#### P2. Low levels of volatile oils, waxes and resins in leaves

The leaves of plants containing significant amounts of oils, waxes and resins will often have a strong scent when crushed. For example Rosemary, Lavender and Tea Trees have oil in their foliage and pine trees can have high resin content.
