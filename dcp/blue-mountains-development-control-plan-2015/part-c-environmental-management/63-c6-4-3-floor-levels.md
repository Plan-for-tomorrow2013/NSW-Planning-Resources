### C6.4.3.	 Floor levels

#### Objective

O1. To ensure that floor levels are set at an appropriate height to reduce the frequency of inundation of structures and floors to an acceptable probability. Note: Floor levels refer to the minimum required building floor levels. For development such as basements, the floor level refers to the lowest level at each access point.

#### Controls

C1. Building floor levels shall comply with the Part C6 - Table 2.

C2. For the purposes of this part of the DCP, non-habitable floors include areas such as laundries or sheds, but exclude garages. All other floor spaces are habitable areas.
