### C6.4.4.	 Building components

#### Objective

O1. To ensure the structure and construction of development is compatible with flooding up to the applicable floor level.

#### Controls

C1. All development shall have flood compatible building components below the floor levels identified in Part C6 - Table 2.

Part C6: Water Management

C2. All structures within the flood plain shall be constructed to withstand the forces of floodwater, debris and buoyancy up to and including the floor levels identified in Part C6 - Table 2.

C3. All development that is in ‘cut’ is to address the impacts of overland flow path from upstream catchments, and ensure that there is adequate provision for flows paths around the structure, stepping up at least 225mm from the finished ground level to the floor level of the structure.
