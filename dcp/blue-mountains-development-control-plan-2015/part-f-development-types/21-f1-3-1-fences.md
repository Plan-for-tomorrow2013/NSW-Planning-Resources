### F1.3.1.	 Fences

Note: When proposing a side or rear fence the proponent must also refer to the requirements of, and understand their obligations under, the Dividing Fences Act 1991.

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure that fences for residential development are appropriately scaled and allow for passive surveillance between public and private realms.

Part F1: Residential Development

O2. To ensure that rear and side fencing will assist in providing privacy to private open space areas.

O3. To ensure boundary fencing is of a high quality and does not detract from the streetscape.

#### Controls

#### Residential & Environmental Zones

C1. The maximum height of a fence is to comply with the height limits prescribed in Part F1 - Table 8.

Part F1 – Table 8: Maximum height of fence

| Location | Maximum height above ground level |
|---|---|
| Primary frontage | 1.2m |
| Secondary road | 1.2m between the front boundary and the |
| frontage | front building line 1.8m between the front building line and the rear boundary |
| Common boundary | 1.8m 1.2m between the front boundary tapering to 1.8m at the front building line 1.2m if masonry construction |

C2. Fences installed on a primary road frontage shall be open for at least 20% of the area of the fence that is more than 400mm above ground level, with any individual solid element of the fence above this height being no more than 350mm in width with a minimum aperture of 25mm.

Part F1: Residential Development

C3. If a lot has a frontage to a secondary road(s), C2 applies to 50% of contiguous secondary road boundaries, measured from the corner with the primary road boundary.

C4. Despite C1, if the fence is erected on a sloping site and stepped to accommodate the fall in the land:

(a) a fence that is required to be not more than 1.2m above ground level, must not be more than 1.5m above ground level at each step, and

(b) a fence that is required to be not more than 1.8m above ground level, must not be more than 2.2m above ground level at each step.

C5. Long sections of fencing on a secondary road frontage are to include recessed portions located at regular intervals along the length of the fence for the purpose of additional landscaping.

C6. Despite C1, where traffic from a classified road is likely to cause an adverse impact on the amenity of the residence, Council may consider a fence up to 1.8m in height of solid construction but only when its appearance is softened by:

(a) providing a setback from the property boundary by a distance equivalent to the height of the fence, and

(b) landscaped planting is provided between the fence and the property boundary, with a mature height of at least 1.5m, and

(c) incorporating openings, and a variation of colours, texture or materials, for visual interest.

#### Rural Zones

C7. The maximum height of a fence is to be no higher than 1.8m above ground level unless the fence is erected on a sloping site and is stepped to accommodate the fall in the land in which case it may not be more than 2.2m above ground level at each step.

C8. A fence shall not include any masonry construction that extends more than 3m from either side of the entrance to the property from the primary road.

C9. Fences are to be constructed using post and wire or post and rail.

C10. If the fence includes an entrance gate, the gate shall not open outwards.
