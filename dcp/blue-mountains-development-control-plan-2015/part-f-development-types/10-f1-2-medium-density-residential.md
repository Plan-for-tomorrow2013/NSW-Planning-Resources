### F1.2. Medium Density Residential

This part contains objectives and controls which apply to development for the purposes of medium density housing, including the following types of development defined in the LEP 2015:

• Multi dwelling housing

• Residential flat buildings

• Attached dwellings

• Boarding Houses

• Seniors Housing

Note: State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021 applies to the erection of a new residential flat building (RFB), the substantial redevelopment or refurbishment of an existing RFB or the conversion of an existing building to an RFB.

Note: Part F7 contains additional provisions for seniors housing.
