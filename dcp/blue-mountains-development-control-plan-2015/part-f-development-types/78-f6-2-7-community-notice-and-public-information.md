### F6.2.7.	 Community notice and public information signs

Community notice and public information signs are signs that provide information on, or advertise services or activities on a site for a public or community institution or organisation.

Note: Community notice and public information signs may be exempt pursuant to State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008 (Codes SEPP).

#### Controls

C1. Subject to C2, community notice and public information signs are to accord with each of the design requirements prescribed in Part F6 - Table 7:

Part F6 - Table 7: Design requirements for community notice and public information

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| Maximum No. of signs | One (1) per road frontage |
| Maximum area (per sign) | 3.5m2 |
| Maximum dimensions | 5m in height |
| Location | Wholly within site boundaries |
| Illumination | Must not be illuminated |

Part F6: Signage

C2. Community notice and public information signs that do not accord with the design requirements prescribed in C1 are unlikely to be supported however will be assessed on the merits of the proposal and are to demonstrate:

(a) compatibility with the existing or desired future character of an area, and

(b) appropriate positioning so as not to compromise vehicular or pedestrian safety by obscuring sightlines.
