### F5.2. Consolidation

#### Objectives

O1. To minimise the potential of integral parts of a development being sold separately.

O2. To promote the efficient use of land and to avoid the creation of sites that are isolated from established urban settlements.

#### Controls

C1. Where development extends over 2 or more allotments, the allotments are to be consolidated prior to the issue of any Occupation Certificate.

C2. Consolidation of allotments for multi-unit development is encouraged so as to avoid isolated single dwellings on small lots surrounded by high density development.

### Part F6

### SIGNAGE


### Introduction

Rational, well located and appropriately designed signage serves to effectively identify and promote buildings and businesses in the Blue Mountains and can positively contribute to streetscape character. Conversely, signs that are inappropriately located, poorly designed and excessive in number create visual and physical clutter and can significantly detract from streetscape character. The objectives and controls contained in this part of the DCP apply to proposals for, or incorporating, signage.
