### F7.4. Sex Services Premises

#### Explanation

Sex services premises is a permitted use within limited areas of the Blue Mountains. Within these areas, there are other restrictions with regard to the location of this land use, relative to other forms of development.

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure sites proposed for use as sex services premises are appropriately located and sufficiently distant from other incompatible land uses.

#### Controls

C1. Sex services premises are not to be located:

(a) Adjacent to, adjoining or within 100m of an educational establishment, a childcare centre, a recreational area, a place of worship, a hospital, or any other place regularly frequented by children for recreational or cultural activities, and

Part F7: Other Development

(b) Within 50m of the boundary of any zone in which a dwelling house is a permissible use, and

(c) On land fronting, or with direct vehicular access from, the Regional Transport Corridor.
