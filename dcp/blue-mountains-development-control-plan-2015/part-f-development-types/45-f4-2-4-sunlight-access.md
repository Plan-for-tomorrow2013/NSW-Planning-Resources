### F4.2.4.	 Sunlight Access

#### Controls

C1. For new construction for the purposes of bed and breakfast accommodation and farm stay accommodation, the development is to be designed and located so that between the hours of 9am and 3pm on 21 June:

Part F4: Tourist Development

(a) at least 1m2 of living room windows associated with neighbouring development receive a minimum of 3 hours of unobstructed sunlight, and

(b) at least 50% of private open space areas associated with development on adjoining allotments receive a minimum of 3 hours of unobstructed sunlight.

C2. Where pre-development sunlight access enjoyed by development on adjoining allotments is less than the outcomes prescribed in C1, new development is not to create any additional overshadowing for that development.
