### F4.2.5.	 Services

#### Controls

C1. For new dwellings to be used for bed and breakfast accommodation, the provision of electricity and gas services is to be provided underground.

C2. Provision is to be made for only one external television aerial to service the bed and breakfast accommodation.

C3. For all dwellings to be used for bed and breakfast, the following dedicated areas are to be provided on site:

(a) A common clothes drying area, and

(b) An area for storing garbage, recycling and composting bins, which is suitably located and screened.
