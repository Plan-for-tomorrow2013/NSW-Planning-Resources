#### R2 Low Density Residential, Zone C3 Environmental

Management and Zone C4 Environmental Living are to be able to accommodate a minimum 300m2 building envelope that:

(a) is 15m by 20m or 12m by 25m, and

(b) does not include any Environmentally Sensitive Land, and

(c) does not contain land steeper than 10% slope, and

(d) is wholly outside the 1% Annual Exceedance Probability (AEP) flood, and

(e) is not within the boundary setbacks prescribed by LEP 2015 or the DCP; and

(f) can be readily and safely accessed by a vehicle from a public road.
