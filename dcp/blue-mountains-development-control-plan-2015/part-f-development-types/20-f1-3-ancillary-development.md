### F1.3. Ancillary Development

#### Explanation

Development ancillary to a dwelling house commonly includes fences, studios, sheds and swimming pools/spas. Ancillary development should be visually compatible with the design of the subject building and sympathetic to the character of the locality.

Note: Some forms of ancillary development may be categorised as exempt or complying development pursuant to State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008 (Codes SEPP).
