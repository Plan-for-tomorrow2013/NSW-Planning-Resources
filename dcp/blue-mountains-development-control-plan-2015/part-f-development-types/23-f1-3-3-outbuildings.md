### F1.3.3.	 Outbuildings

#### Objectives

Part F1: Residential Development

O1. To ensure that outbuildings are of an appropriate scale and have regard to residential amenity.

O2. Ensure that the form, scale and design of outbuildings are compatible with the established character and setting of the locality.

O3. Manage the cumulative impact of outbuildings, particularly the visual impact when viewed from the public domain and neighbouring properties.

#### Controls

C1. Outbuildings are to be located at least 1m behind the building line of any road frontage.
