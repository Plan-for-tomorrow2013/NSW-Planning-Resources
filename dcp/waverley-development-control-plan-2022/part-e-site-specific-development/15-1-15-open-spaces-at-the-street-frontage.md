### 1.15 OPEN SPACES AT THE STREET FRONTAGE

#### Objectives

(a) To ensure private buildings are built to the street alignment.

(b) To retain a consistent alignment along streets.

(c) To retain and increase activity on the street front.

(d) To increase safety of the public domain and passive surveillance.

(e) To encourage public buildings to create forecourts that are well designed and enhance the public domain.

#### Controls

(a) New open spaces on the street front for private buildings are not suitable for Bondi Junction.

(b) New open spaces on the street front for public buildings may be considered if they meet the following controls:

(i) New open spaces require active frontages along all the built sides of the space.

(ii) Logical and functional pedestrian connections through and beyond the space are to be provided.

(iii) Clear sight lines into and throughout the space.

(iv) The space must be accessible and useable to the public.

(v) Public open spaces must not to be located on block and street corners, and must be a min. of 10m from a corner.
