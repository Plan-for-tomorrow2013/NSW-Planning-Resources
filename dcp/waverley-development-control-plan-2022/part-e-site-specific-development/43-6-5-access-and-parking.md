### 6.5 ACCESS AND PARKING

#### Objectives

a) To minimise the impacts of vehicles on pedestrian amenity and public spaces.

b) To encourage a safe and practical space for all transport modes.

c) To minimise any additional pressure on the existing parking in West Bondi Junction.

#### Controls

a) Osmond Lane is to be retained as a 7.0 metre local access lane with “No Parking” restrictions on both sides.

b) Access to and from Osmond Lane at Nelson Street is to be left-in left-out with enforcement by signs or a central raised median on Newland Street.

c) The pedestrian bridge over Syd Einfeld Drive and ramps/stairs is to be retained. The bridge should be easily accessible from the site and the pedestrian and cycleway through site links.

d) Resident parking should be provided on site

e) The 3.5m road widening dedication along Oxford Street is not required for traffic related uses and as such can be allocated for other purposes.

f) Resident parking for this development will not be included within area 22 of the Waverley Resident Planning Parking Scheme (RPPS).
