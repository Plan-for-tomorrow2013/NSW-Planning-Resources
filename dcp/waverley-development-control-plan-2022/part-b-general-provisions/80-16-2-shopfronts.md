### 16.2 Shopfronts

(a) Retain the original significant features including ingoes, signage, glazing pattern, location of doors, tiling and awnings.

(b) Respect the original form, scale and detailing of the building and not compromise the integrity and consistency of the streetscape.

(c) Aim to increase accessibility to the shopfront through permanent or temporary measures as deemed suitable in consultation with Council.
