### 1.4 RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHER PLANS, STANDARDS AND CODES

This DCP should be read in conjunction with WLEP. Where there is an inconsistency between this Plan and the WLEP, the LEP prevails. This DCP is also to be read in conjunction with the following: Environmental Planning & Assessment Act 1979; Environmental Planning & Assessment Regulation 2021; Local Government Act 1993; Roads Act 1993; Any relevant State Environmental Planning Policy (SEPP); Any relevant Land and Environment Court Planning Principle; National Construction Code and Building Code of Australia; Any relevant Australian Standard (identified or not in this Plan); Any policy or guideline adopted by Council including: o Waverley Local Strategic Planning Statement (LSPS). _ o Creative Lighting Strategy Parts 1 and 2 o Our Liveable Places Centres Strategy. o Local Housing Strategy. o Public Domain Technical Manual. o Development Contributions Plan. o Planning Agreement Policy. o Tree and Vegetation Vandalism Policy o Tree Management Policy. o Heritage Policy. o Public Art in the Private Domain. o Inter-War Factsheets.

o Water Management Technical Manual.

It is the responsibility of the applicant to identify all relevant legislative requirements. The NSW Legislation website should be regularly checked for the most up-to-date version of all legislation and can be accessed at: www.legislation.nsw.gov.au
