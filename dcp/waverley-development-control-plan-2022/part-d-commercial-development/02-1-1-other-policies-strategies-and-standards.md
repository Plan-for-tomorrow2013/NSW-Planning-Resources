### 1.1 OTHER POLICIES, STRATEGIES AND STANDARDS

Applicants are to ensure that the proposed development is in compliance with the relevant Australian Standards, including: The National Construction Code (NCC) Australian Standard AS/NZS 1158 3.1:2020 Pedestrian (P) Lighting The Food Act 2003 The Food Standards Code The Noise Guide for Local Government The Protection of the Environment and Operations Act 1997
