### 1.2 SETBACKS

#### Objectives

(a) To ensure that the bulk and appearance of the proposed development is appropriate to the streetscape.

(b) To set a rhythm and character to residential streets.

(c) To ensure the distance between buildings on adjacent properties allows adequate solar access, ventilation and privacy.

(d) To ensure that the amenity of rear yards, their function as private open space, and their visual and landscape contribution to the surrounding area is protected and enhanced.

(e) To accommodate flexibility in the siting of buildings, where appropriate.

(f) To ensure the front and rear setbacks of buildings are consistent with surrounding buildings and do not visually detract from the streetscape.

(g) To ensure significant views and view corridors available from the public domain and existing properties are considered as part of the local context of any development. Refer to Part C1.10 Views.

(h) To ensure buildings on corner lots are consistent with the predominant building lines of adjoining sites.
