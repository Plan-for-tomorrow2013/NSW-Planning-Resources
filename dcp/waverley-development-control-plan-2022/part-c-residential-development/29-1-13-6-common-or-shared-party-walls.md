#### 1.13.6 Common or shared party walls

a) Subject to appropriateness on heritage grounds, where a previously interior party wall with no cavity becomes exterior then an appropriate second skin, and damp-proof course and waterproofing treatment will be required at the expense of the applicant.

b) Where new common walls are constructed, they should be constructed as cavity walls with a damp-proof course.
