#### 1.13.3 Material Finishes and Detail for Semi-detached Dwellings

(a) Additions are to be cohesively integrated with the finishes and detail of the existing building.

(b) The style, pitch, profile and colour of roofs to proposed additions are to match and complement the existing roof form of the dwelling.

(c) Historic features of the existing roofscape are to be identified and where appropriate be incorporated into the proposed addition.

(d) Dormer roof forms are to be used in a manner characteristic of the original style of the subject dwelling.

(e) New roofing is to match the original roofing in material colour and profile. Where roofs of adjoining semi-detached dwellings are currently different to each other, new additions are to match the roofing of the adjacent semi-detached dwelling.

(f) Windows to first and ground floor additions are to be in scale and proportionate to the original windows in the semi-detached dwelling.

(g) Upper wall finishes are to reflect the style and character of the original building finishes.
