#### 1.8.5 Dimensions

(a) Hardstand spaces, carports and garages should have minimum dimensions of 5.4m x 2.4m per vehicle.

(b) All car spaces are to accommodate the vehicle within the site without the vehicle or vehicle appendages overhanging the public domain.

(c) Internal sliding or hinged gates are to be provided to hardstands/carports to ensure enclosure of the vehicle within the site.
