#### 1.2.2 Side Setbacks Controls

(a) Comply with the minimum setbacks as follows:

| Location of proposed works | Side setback (min.) |
|---|---|
| Ground Floor | 0.9m |
| First Floor | 0.9m |

| Second Floor | 1.5m |
|---|---|
| Third Floor | On merit |

Table 1 Minimum side setbacks

(b) The side setbacks may be reduced if the proposed dwelling or alteration adjoins another dwelling without a setback along the shared boundary. This applies only to that section of the boundary which the neighbouring dwelling is built.

(c) The ‘ground floor’ is considered the lowest floor on site when considering which side setback floor to apply. Where a site slopes, the ground floor should be taken from the lowest floor on the site from each end of the site.

(d) Greater side setbacks may be required to achieve compliance with Parts C1.3, C1.5, C1.6, C1.7 and C1.9 of this DCP.

(e) Side setbacks for components of existing buildings being retained do not need to be changed to comply with Table 1, however, new works proposed to an existing building do need to be changed to comply.

(f) Where a brand new three storey structure is proposed, all floors must be setback by 1.5m.
