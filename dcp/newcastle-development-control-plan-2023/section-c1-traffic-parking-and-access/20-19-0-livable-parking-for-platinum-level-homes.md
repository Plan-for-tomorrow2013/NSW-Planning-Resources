### 19.0 Livable parking for platinum level homes

#### Objectives

1\. Ensure adequate provision of platinum level car spaces to move around a vehicle.

2\. Enable home occupants to easily enter and exit the dwelling.

#### Controls (C)

C-1.Where dwellings are required to meet platinum level, car spaces and access are designed and constructed as per the Livable Housing Guideline, 2017.
