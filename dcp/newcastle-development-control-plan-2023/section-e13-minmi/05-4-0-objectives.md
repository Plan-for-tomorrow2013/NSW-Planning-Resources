### 4.0 Objectives

1\. Ensure that Minmi retains its existing village character.

2\. Develop as a westernmost local activity centre for the Western corridor.

3\. Ensure that the built heritage and character of Minmi is preserved.

4\. Contribute to tourist related development and strengthen the local employment base.
