### 8.0 Urban structure – biological diversity

#### Controls (C)

C-1. Revegetation restores the diversity of indigenous species originally present, which may have been lost C-2. New development does not adversely affect (and preferably benefits) downstream Hexham Wetlands, which is recognised as being of international significance as habitat for migratory birds.
