### 9.0 Car parking

#### Design of parking structures

#### Performance criteria

P1. Parking structures are integrated into new buildings and are not visually prominent from the public domain.

P2. The design and construction method of at grade and above ground car parking areas enable adaptable reuse in the future for residential or commercial uses.

P3. Car parking areas are able to be adapted in response to changing future transport mode or demands.

#### Acceptable solutions

A1. All parking is located within the building footprint, either in a basement or integrated into the building.

A2. Ground level or above ground parking areas are not visible from the public domain by:

a. being located behind other uses, or

b. using green walls and roofs, or

c. using architecturally designed façade treatment or artwork.

A3. Car parking is located on level flooring and has a minimum ceiling height of:

a. 4m where located on ground level, and

b. 3m where located on any upper levels.
