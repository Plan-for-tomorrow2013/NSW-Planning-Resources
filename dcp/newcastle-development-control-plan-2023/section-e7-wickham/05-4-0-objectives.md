### 4.0 Objectives

1\. Guide the evolution of Wickham from a semi-industrial area at the outer fringe of Newcastle City Centre into a mixed use urban neighbourhood supporting the emerging commercial core within Newcastle West.

2\. Ensure development is consistent with the intended future character of each urban precinct.
