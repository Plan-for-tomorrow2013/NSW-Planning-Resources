### 17.0 Ancillary development – carports and garages

#### Controls (C)

C-1.The carport or garage has a maximum floor area of 40m2. C-2.The carport or garage has a maximum height of 4.5m above existing ground level. C-3.The carport or garage complies with controls under sub-section 16.0 Car parking and vehicular access, of this section. C-4.The minimum front setback is 1m behind the building line or 5.5m from the property boundary of the road frontage, whichever is the greater. C-5.Side/rear setbacks:

a. the setback from side boundaries complies with the controls under sub-section 9.0 or sub-section 10

b. the minimum setback to the rear boundary is 900mm.

C-6.A carport has two or more sides open and not less than one-third of its perimeter open.
