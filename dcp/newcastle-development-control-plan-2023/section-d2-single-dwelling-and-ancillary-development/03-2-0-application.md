### 2.0 Application

> [Picture text on page 2 is stored in the PDF without readable letters (no character map), so it could not be copied; see the original PDF.]

This section applies to all development consisting of: single dwellings alterations and additions ancillary structures.

For development involving heritage items or heritage conservation areas identified under LEP 2012, a merit assessment is required to ensure the outcomes sought are balanced with heritage conservation outcomes.
