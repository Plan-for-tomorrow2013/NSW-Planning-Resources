### 18.0 Amenity – ceiling heights

#### Objectives

1\. Ensure ceiling height achieves sufficient natural ventilation and daylight access, and provides spatial quality.

#### Controls (C)

The following controls apply to all forms of residential development C-1.All residential flat buildings comply with the standards for 'Ceiling Height’ in the Apartment Design Guide. C-2.For all other residential development, the ceiling height measured between finished floor level and finished ceiling level are:

a. 2.7m to all ground floor habitable rooms

b. 2.7m to first floor living rooms

c. 2.4m to all first floor bedrooms.
