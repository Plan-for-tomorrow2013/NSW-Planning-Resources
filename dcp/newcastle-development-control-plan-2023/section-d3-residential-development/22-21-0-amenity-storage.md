### 21.0 Amenity – storage

#### Objectives

1\. Ensure each dwelling has adequate, well designed storage.

#### Controls (C)

The following controls apply to all forms of residential development C-1.Compliance with the standards for 'Storage' in the Apartment Design Guide, for all residential flat buildings required to comply with that standard. C-2.For all other residential development:

a. in addition to storage in kitchens, bathrooms and bedrooms, storage is provided as follows:

| Dwelling size | Private open space |
|---|---|
| 1 bedroom | 8m3 |
| 2 bedrooms | 12m3 |
| 3+ bedrooms | 16m3 |

b. at least 50% of the required storage is located in the dwelling

c. storage not located in a dwelling is secure and clearly allocated to specific dwellings if located in a common area.
