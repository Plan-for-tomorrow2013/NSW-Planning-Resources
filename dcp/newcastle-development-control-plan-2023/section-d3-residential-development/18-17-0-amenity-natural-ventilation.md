### 17.0 Amenity – natural ventilation

#### Objectives

1\. Ensure all habitable rooms are naturally ventilated.

#### Controls (C)

The following controls apply to all forms of residential development C-1.All residential flat buildings comply with the standards for 'Natural Ventilation’ in the Apartment Design Guide. C-2.For all other residential development:

a. each habitable room has natural ventilation

b. each dwelling is cross ventilated.
