### 22.0 Amenity – car and bicycle parking

#### Objectives

1\. Car and bicycle parking is appropriate for the scale of the development.

#### Controls (C)

#### Explanatory notes

The following controls apply to all forms of residential development C-1.Entries to a basement car park, garage or carport are set back at least 1m behind the front building line. Where Car and bicycle parking comply with Section C1 Traffic, the building line is less than 4.5m, the entry to the basement car park, garage or carport is setback at least of 5.5m parking and access. from the primary road.
