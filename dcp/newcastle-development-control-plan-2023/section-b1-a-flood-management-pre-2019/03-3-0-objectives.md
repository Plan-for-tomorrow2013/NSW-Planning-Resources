### 3.0 Objectives

1\. Guide the development of flood prone land, applying balanced strategies to economically, socially and environmentally manage risk to life and property.

2\. Set aside appropriate areas to convey and/or store flood waters.

3\. Ensure development, when considered both individually and as an instance of cumulative development trends, will not cause unreasonable adverse flooding impacts in other locations.

4\. Implement the principles of The NSW Government Floodplain Development Manual (2005) to development as applicable.
