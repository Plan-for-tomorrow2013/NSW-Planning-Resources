### 15.0 Access to sunlight

| Objectives 1. Ensure development retains reasonable levels of solar access to solar panels on neighbouring properties. |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Controls (C) | Acceptable solutions (AS) | Explanatory notes |
| C-1.Consideration must be given to neighbouring properties’ solar panels and the loss of sunlight to these panels as a result of any development proposal, having regard to the performance, efficiency, economic viability and reasonableness of their location. | AS-1.Where reasonably practicable sunlight to any existing solar panels should not be reduced to less than two hours between 9am and 3pm on 21 June. |  |
