### 14.0 Parking, vehicle access and movement

#### Objectives

Ensure adequate provision is made for on-site car parking and for employees and visitor's vehicles.

1.

2\. Create attractive landscaped car parking throughout the development.

3\. Maximise opportunities for walking and cycling and where possible.

#### Controls (C)

#### Explanatory notes

C-1.Car parking provided on site in accordance with the requirements of Section C1 Traffic, parking and access. C-2.Off-street parking is provided behind or at the side of the building area. C-3.Heavy and light traffic movements are to be separated. C-4.All vehicles are able to enter and leave the site in a forward direction.
