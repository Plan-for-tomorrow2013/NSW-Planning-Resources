### 1.0 Introduction

The Lingard Hospital Precinct (Hospital Precinct) is within the suburb of Merewether and is situated approximately three kilometres south-west from Newcastle CBD. The Hospital Precinct offers a comprehensive range of specialities and on-site cardiac, medical, surgical and allied health services.
