### 2.0 Application

This section applies to all development consisting of a new building or structure, extension or structural alteration, or subdivision located on land within a proclaimed mine subsidence district subject to the Coal Mine Subsidence Compensation Act 2017 (as amended or replaced).

Note: Certain minor construction works do not require approval from Subsidence Advisory NSW and are covered under the following exempt development list at: nsw.gov.au/subsidence-advisory/exempt-developments
