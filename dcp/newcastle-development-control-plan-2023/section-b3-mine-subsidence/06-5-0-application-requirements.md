### 5.0 Application requirements

| Development category | Application requirements | Explanatory notes |
|---|---|---|
| All development, or subdivision on land identified as being within a Mine Subsidence District require assessment from Subsidence Advisory NSW prior to approval. | Endorsed plans stamped by Subsidence Advisory NSW are to be submitted with the DA. | This is applicable for development proposed within mine subsidence districts where special consideration of the likely subsidence issues is required prior to approval or where Subsidence Advisory NSW requires plans to be submitted. |
