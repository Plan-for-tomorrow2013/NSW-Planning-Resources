### 2.0 Application

> [Picture text on page 2 is stored in the PDF without readable letters (no character map), so it could not be copied; see the original PDF.]

This section applies to all development consisting of: Early education and care facility Community facilities Correctional centres Educational establishments Health services facilities Information and education facilities Place of public worship Public administration building Research station.

General controls in sub-sections 8.0 to 16.0 are also applicable to land uses specified in sub-sections 17.0 and 18.0.

For development involving heritage items or heritage conservation areas identified under Newcastle Local Environmental Plan 2012 (LEP 2012), a merit assessment will be undertaken to ensure the outcomes sought are balanced with heritage conservation outcomes.
