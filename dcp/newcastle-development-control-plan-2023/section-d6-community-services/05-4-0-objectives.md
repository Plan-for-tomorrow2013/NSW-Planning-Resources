### 4.0 Objectives

1\. Maintain the streetscape, amenity and character of areas surrounding community services.

2\. Ensure community services are appropriately located and designed.

3\. Ensure residential development has a high level of privacy, comfort, security, amenity and liveability.

4\. Promote adoption of best practice water efficiency and energy efficiency measures to create healthy work environments.
