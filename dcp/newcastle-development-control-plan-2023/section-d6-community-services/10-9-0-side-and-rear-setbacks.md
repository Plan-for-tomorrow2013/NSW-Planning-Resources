### 9.0 Side and rear setbacks

| Objectives 1. Development is consistent with and complements the desired built form prevailing in the street and local area. 2. Setbacks maintain the amenity and privacy of public spaces and adjoining dwellings and their private open space. |  |
|---|---|
| Controls (C) | Explanatory notes |
| C-1.Design is to: a. ensure adequate natural light, ventilation and privacy between buildings b. protect public tree assets c. consider the impact on solar access and private open space of adjoining dwellings. | Side and rear setbacks are also specified for locality specific locations. Development undertaken off site requires approvals. This includes the use of public footpaths. |
| C-2.For a centre-based child care facility, the side setback to access doors from children's internal space is a minimum of 4m. |  |
