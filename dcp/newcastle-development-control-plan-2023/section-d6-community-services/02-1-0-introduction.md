### 1.0 Introduction

Community services are important gathering places for people to engage in activities, interact with one another, and build a sense of community. They provide important services to the community.

Community facilities are often distinctive buildings in the urban landscape and good design is important to ensure that they contribute to the streetscape. Good design is essential as these facilities and the activities within them can greatly impact the amenity of the surrounding area. Community facilities also offer opportunities to realise sustainability benefits including reducing energy consumption and increasing water efficiency.
