### 6.0 Application requirements

| Development category | Application requirements | Explanatory notes |
|---|---|---|
| All residential development to which this section applies. | Development application plans must provide sufficient detail indicating the universal design features included in the design. Dwellings must be clearly identified as either meeting Silver Level or Platinum Level. LHA Provisional Design Certificate issued by a registered LHA Assessor. LHA Final-as-Built Certification issued by a registered LHA Assessor. | LHA Assessors are registered with LHA and can be found on the Livable Housing Australia Website. All dwelling houses are encouraged to be designed and constructed to accord to Silver Level Livable Housing Design Standards. |
