### 12.0 Structure

DCP 2023 has seven parts each containing objectives and controls that apply to all development. Development applications need to consider each part and respond to relevant provisions. Table 1 illustrates this.

#### Table 1: DCP Structure

| Part | Summary |
|---|---|
| A: Introduction | Sets out aims, objectives, application and relationship with other planning instruments and structure. |
| B: Site planning controls | Sets out controls for all development. Part B contains objectives and controls that underpin orderly and sustainable development. |
| C: General development controls | Sets out objectives and controls that generally apply to all development. |
| D: Development controls by land use | Provides objectives and controls to guide specific development. |
| E: Place and precincts | Provides additional objectives and controls for development on specific sites, areas or precincts and in character and heritage areas. |
