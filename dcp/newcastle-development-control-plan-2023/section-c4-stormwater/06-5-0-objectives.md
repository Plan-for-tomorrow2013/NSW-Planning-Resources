### 5.0 Objectives

1\. Outline CN's requirement for stormwater management for development.

2\. Adopt a whole of water cycle approach to development.

3\. Promote sustainable practices in relation to the use of water resources for human activities.

4\. Ensure an appropriate quality and quantity of water enters waterways.

5\. Protect and enhance waterways, watercourses, wetlands and their riparian corridors.

6\. Promote soil infiltration and ensure stormwater is controlled and managed appropriately.

7\. Promote best practice and innovative water sensitive urban design solutions.
