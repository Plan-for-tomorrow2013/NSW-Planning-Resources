### 3.0 Objectives

1\. Protect and enhance the aesthetic quality provided by the natural topography and landform features of the area by controlling the land forming operations to appropriate levels.

2\. Prevent pollution to waterways from construction sediment and waste streams.

3\. Minimise the potential for landslip on sloping sites.

4\. Ensure that development does not disturb, expose or drain acid sulfate soils and cause environmental damage.
