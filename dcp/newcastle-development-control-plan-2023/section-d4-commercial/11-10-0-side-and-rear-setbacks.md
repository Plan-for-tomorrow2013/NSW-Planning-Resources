### 10.0 Side and rear setbacks

#### Objectives

1\. Development is consistent with and complements the desired built form prevailing in the street and local area.

2\. Setbacks maintain the amenity and privacy of public spaces and adjoining dwellings and their private open space.

| Controls (C) | Explanatory notes |
|---|---|
| C-1.Design is to: | Side and rear setbacks are also specified Part E – Places and |
| a. ensure adequate natural light, ventilation and privacy between buildings | Precincts. |
| b. protect public tree assets | Commercial enterprises undertaken off site require approvals. |
| c. consider the impact on solar access and private open space of adjoining dwellings. | This includes the use of public footpaths. |
