### 1.0 Introduction

Flood management is an essential aspect of land use planning and development. Flooding affects up to one third of all properties in the local government area (LGA), and can cause significant damage to property and infrastructure, disrupt the community and threaten public safety. Many old swamplands and floodplains are developed. New development needs to respond to the local environment as well as future intensifying flood risks associated with climate change.
