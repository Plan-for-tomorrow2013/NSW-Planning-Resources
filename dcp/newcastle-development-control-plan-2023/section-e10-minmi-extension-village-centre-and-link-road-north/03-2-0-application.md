### 2.0 Application

The guidelines relate to three precincts, as shown in Map 2 – Precincts of guidelines, being: Village Centre Precinct Link Road North Precinct (being that portion within the Newcastle local government area) Minmi Extension Precinct.

The guidelines apply to all development within these precincts requiring development consent.
