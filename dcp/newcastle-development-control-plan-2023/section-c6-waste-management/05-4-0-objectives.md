### 4.0 Objectives

1\. Manage waste in accordance with the waste hierarchy to:

a. avoid producing waste in the first place

b. minimise the amount of waste produced

c. re-use items as many times as possible to minimise waste

d. recycle once re-use options have been exhausted

e. dispose of what is left, as a last resort, in a responsible way to appropriate waste disposal facilities.

2\. Ensure waste management and mitigation at demolition, construction and operation stages are designed to provide satisfactory amenity for occupants.

3\. Ensure occupants are active and empowered participants in creating solutions and waste mitigation and minimisation.

4\. Ensure that development incorporates waste management systems that are efficient and capable of handling the forecasted waste generation.
