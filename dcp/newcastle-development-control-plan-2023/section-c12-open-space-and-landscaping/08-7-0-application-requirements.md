### 7.0 Application requirements

For the purpose of this section development proposals are grouped into three categories, which determine the level of information required with a development application (DA): Category 1 - small scale development with relatively little impact on surrounding development.  No landscape plan is required for Category 1 development. Category 2 - medium scale development with potential visual significance and impact on the amenity of the host neighbourhood. Category 3 - large scale development or development on prominent or ecologically sensitive sites with a high degree of visual significance and environmental impact. In general, proposals will be categorised according to the following schedule. CN reserves the right to make a final determination on the development category following consideration of a formal DA.
