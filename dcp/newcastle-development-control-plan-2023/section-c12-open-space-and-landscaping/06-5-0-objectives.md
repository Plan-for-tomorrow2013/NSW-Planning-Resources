### 5.0 Objectives

1\. Ensure landscaping is integrated into the design of the development.

2\. Ensure the character of the development and surrounding landscaping are complementary.

3\. Improve stormwater quality and reduce stormwater quantity.

4\. Ensure landscaping does not detract from the local natural environment and provides habitat connections for native plants and animals.

5\. Improve human wellbeing through the landscape design of private and public space to provide natural spaces, improve residential amenity, reduce urban heat and improve air quality.

6\. Design open space to improve serviceability, connectivity and access.

7\. Ensure landscaping is in scale with the built form and complements the built and natural features on the site and adjoining land.
