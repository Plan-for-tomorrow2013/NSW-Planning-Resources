#### Design excellence: Urban design review panel

The urban design review panel (UDRP) provides independent, expert advice to City of Newcastle (CN) and applicants about the quality of the urban design and amenity of developments. Some types of development must be referred to the UDRP. These include:

• Residential flat buildings

• Shop top housing

• Mixed-use development with residential accommodation component

• Strata subdivision of serviced apartments

Within the renewal corridors, other types of development may be referred to the UDRP due to their nature, location or scale, and likely impact upon the surrounding locality. These include:

• Boarding houses

• Education establishments

• Hospitals

• Multi dwelling and attached dwelling developments comprising ten or more dwellings,

• Place of public worship

• Seniors housings

• Serviced apartment

• Tourist and visitor accommodation

• Modification applications where the development consent to be modified was subject to UDRP advice and the modifications are not minor

• Any other development referred at the discretion of the Manager

The UDRP is an advisory panel only and the advice provided by the panel informs the assessment process. It is not the purpose of the UDRP to have any role in the determination of DAs, nor are its recommendations binding on CN’s determination of an application.
