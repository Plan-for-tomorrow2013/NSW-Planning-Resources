### 16.0 Subdivision construction

| Objectives |  |
|---|---|
| 1. Ensure subdivision works meet all necessary standards. |  |
| Controls (C) | Explanatory notes |
| C-1.All construction works associated with the subdivision are carried out in accordance with CN's adopted Engineering Specifications for Development Design and Construction. | Refer to CN's Subdivision, Stormwater and Urban Forest Technical Manual/s, as amended. |
| 17.0 Bonding of work |  |
| Objectives |  |
| 1. Ensure lots are serviced prior to titles being registered with NSW Land Registry Services. |  |

| Controls (C) | Explanatory notes |
|---|---|
| C-1.Bonding for completion of minor works to allow the issuing of the subdivision certificate is only accepted where it is demonstrated such works are not required to service proposed lots that may be transferred into private ownership. | Bonds must be either cash or guarantee by a recognised financial institution. |
| C-2.Bonding of work to be completed on land that will continue to be privately owned after subdivision is generally not supported. |  |
| C-3.Bonds may also be associated with defect liability periods or on-going maintenance works. |  |
