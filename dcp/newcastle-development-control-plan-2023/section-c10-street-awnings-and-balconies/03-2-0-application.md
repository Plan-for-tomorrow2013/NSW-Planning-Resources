### 2.0 Application

This section applies to all development that includes awnings or balconies over the road reserve.

For development involving heritage items or heritage conservations areas identified under Newcastle Local Environmental Plan 2012 (LEP 2012), a merit assessment will be undertaken to ensure the outcomes sought are balanced with heritage conservation outcomes.
