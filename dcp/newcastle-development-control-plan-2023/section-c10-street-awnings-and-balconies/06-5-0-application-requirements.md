### 5.0 Application requirements

| Development category | Application requirements | Explanatory notes |
|---|---|---|
| All development | A maintenance plan is to be developed and includes: a. annual inspection of structural components b. repainting every five years c. regular maintenance to guttering and downpipes d. regular cleaning and replacement of defective lighting, advertising or other deteriorated components of the awning e. regular cleaning of awning glazing, where installed. |  |
