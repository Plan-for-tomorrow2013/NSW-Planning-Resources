### 12.0 Design requirements for awnings and balconies – drainage

#### Objectives

1\. Ensure the appropriate drainage is provided for street awnings.

#### Controls (C)

C-1.Provision is made for the drainage of street awnings in a manner that does not interfere with pedestrian or vehicle traffic, nor create unsightliness. C-2.The awning roof drains towards the building so as to avoid gutters and downpipes at the kerb line. C-3.Gutters are constructed so as to be concealed from the footpath or as an integral component of the awning structure. C-4.Downpipes are recessed into the ground floor frontage below a height of 2.7m from footpath level in buildings.
