### 13.0 Design requirements for awnings and balconies – maintenance

#### Objectives

1\. Ensure structures are easily maintained.

#### Controls (C)

C-1.Provision is made for regular maintenance to ensure the continuing structural integrity and attractive appearance of the awning or balcony.
