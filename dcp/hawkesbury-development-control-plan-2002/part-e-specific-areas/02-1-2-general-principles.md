### 1.2 GENERAL PRINCIPLES

The general principles in relation to development within Kurrajong Heights Village are:

![Picture on page 9](images/part-e-specific-areas-page-9-3.gif)

> [Picture on page 9: a picture, saved as a picture (images/part-e-specific-areas-page-9-3.gif, 150 dpi).]

 to manage change in Kurrajong Heights Village via the implementation of objectives and guidelines that conserve and protect the character and significance of not only individual heritage items but that of the wider village;

 to conserve and enhance the visual and environmental amenity of Kurrajong Heights Village and its hinterland; and

 to ensure that alterations and extensions to existing buildings and new development in Kurrajong Heights Village are compatible in scale, form and character with the character and significance of the village.
