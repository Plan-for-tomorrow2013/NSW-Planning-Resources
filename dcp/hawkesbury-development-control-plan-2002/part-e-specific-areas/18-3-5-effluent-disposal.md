### 3.5 EFFLUENT DISPOSAL

Amendment 64 requires a geotechnical assessment to be undertaken to demonstrate the land is suitable for the disposal of effluent on site.

_______________________________________________________________________________________________________________GROSE WOLD

#### Objectives

¾ To minimise the impact of development on the water quality of the Grose River and its tributaries.

¾ To protect any vegetation located downstream of the development.

¾ To assess the cumulative impact of effluent disposal on vegetation in the locality.

#### Rules

(a) Preparation of a geotechnical assessment undertaken by a suitably qualified person.  The report will provide the following: à Site and soil characteristics determined from a site evaluation. à Impact on vegetation located downstream of the proposed development. à The cumulative effect on the proposed development and existing on site sewage facilities in the locality on the vegetation.

à Surface water drainage channels. à The land application area. à Proximity of the land application facilities to surface waters and surface water drainage channels in the locality.

à Separation distance between land application area, property boundaries, buildings.

à Retaining walls, embankments, swimming pools, etc. à Reserve application areas/wet weather storage. à Area required for dwelling and associated facilities. à Provisions of Sydney Regional Environmental Plan No. 20 - Hawkesbury Nepean River (2-1997).

(b) Design of an appropriate system based on the geotechnical assessment and in accordance with the Environment and Health Protection Guidelines – On Site Sewage Management for Single Households (prepared in 1998 by the Department of Local government et al).

Notes: Construction of the effluent disposal system may be required as part of the approved subdivision.
