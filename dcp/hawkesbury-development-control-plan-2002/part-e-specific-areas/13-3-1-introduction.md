### 3.1 INTRODUCTION

This chapter aims to provide guidelines and identify the requirements for the development of the Grose Wold area.  The chapter also aims to minimise the impact of development on the locality in relation to vegetation, scenic quality and water quality and manages the design and construction of subdivision in the locality.

This chapter applies to any parcel of land within the Grose Wold area that was rezoned to Rural 1(c1) under Amendment 64 to Hawkesbury Local Environmental Plan 1989.  Figure E3.1 Plan of the Area shows the land this chapter applies to.
