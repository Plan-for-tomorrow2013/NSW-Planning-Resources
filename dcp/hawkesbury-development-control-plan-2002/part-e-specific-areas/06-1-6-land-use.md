### 1.6 LAND USE

Land uses in Kurrajong Heights have not significantly changed since the evolution of the village.  There has always been a concentration of commercial activity in the village as evident today.  Past activity was directed towards the provision of accommodation, refreshment and stabling for travellers and drovers.

#### Aims

 To ensure that land uses in Kurrajong Heights Village are compatible with its special qualities (in particular character and heritage significance) and accordingly reflect the scale of the existing village.

#### Objectives

¾ "Infill" buildings accommodating new commercial activity must incorporate appropriate design principles such as: à relationship to adjoining buildings; à line of vision - skyline and setback; à roof form; à bulk and scale; à windows and doors; à verandas; à wall and roof finishes; à fences; à landscaping; à architectural detail; and à car accommodation.

#### Rules

(a) The retention of existing commercial activities located on lands with frontage to Warks Hill Road.
