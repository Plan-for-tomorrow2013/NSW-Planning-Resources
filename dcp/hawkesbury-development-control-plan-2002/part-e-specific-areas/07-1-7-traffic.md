### 1.7 TRAFFIC

In the past the village of Kurrajong Heights provided a place for drovers to stop and a place where travellers could obtain a meal and overnight accommodation.  The Bells Line of Road

___________________________________________________________________________________________________KURRAJONG HEIGHTS VILLAGE

around which the village lies now, is a main road with increasing volumes of traffic particularly heavy vehicles.  Noise, fumes and dust from the traffic intrude into the otherwise quiet village setting.  Traffic from the Bells Line of Road impinges on resident and tourist vehicular and pedestrian movement in the village.  A lack of provision of functional car parking for commercial activities, directional signage and visitor maps indicating major vantage points further compound movement problems in the village.

#### Aims

 To formulate and implement a coordinated system for vehicular and pedestrian movement in and around the village.

#### Objectives

¾ Existing and new commercial development shall provide functional car parking commensurate with increased development.

¾ Provision of directional signage and visitor map in convenient and safe locations.

#### Rules

(a) Assessed on merit.
