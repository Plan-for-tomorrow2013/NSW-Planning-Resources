### 9.2 AIMS AND OBJECTIVES

The aim of this chapter is to preserve the amenity, biodiversity, ecology and heritage of the area through the preservation of trees and vegetation.

The objectives of this chapter are to:

a) prescribe the trees and vegetation to which this chapter applies

b) clarify the assessment criteria under which applications to ringbark, cut down, top, lop, remove, injure, or wilfully destroy trees or vegetation will be determined

c) define the meaning of terms used within this chapter
