### 8.3 COMPLIANCE WITH WASTE MANAGEMENT PLANS

The person who generates the waste is responsible for it and must adhere to their Waste Management Plan. Failure to comply with a Waste Management Plan may result in a fine of up to $600. To ensure compliance, Council officers will carry out regular audits.  Some of the methods that can be used to assist with compliance include:

 Providing all builders with a copy of the Waste Management Plan

 Ensuring that contractors are aware of their obligations to minimise waste going to landfill  Keeping a Waste Data File to verify recycling and disposal
