### 1.1 LANDSCAPING OBJECTIVES

![Picture on page 7](images/part-c-general-guidelines-page-7-2.gif)

> [Picture on page 7: a picture, saved as a picture (images/part-c-general-guidelines-page-7-2.gif, 150 dpi).]

The objectives for this chapter are to:

¾ encourage the enhancement of the natural, cultural and built environment;

¾ outline the landscaping requirements relating to all forms of development;

¾ encourage the recognition of climatic influences and the incorporation of landscaping design features to enhance or modify the climatic factors relating to the site;

¾ encourage the design of low maintenance landscaping;

¾ integrate development into the landscape to minimise the impact on the natural environment;  and

¾ provide for landscaping which allows freedom of access and mobility.
