### 7.3 OBJECTIVES

The objectives of this chapter are:

¾ to set out the minimum requirements for applications requiring or relying the installation of an on-site sewage management facilities;

¾ to set out the limited circumstances where Council may agree to removal of sewage by pump out or tanker removal; and,

¾ to identify special provisions relating to connection to reticulated sewerage systems and development within the rural and environmental protection scenic zones.
