### 2.3 LANDSCAPING

Landscaping of parking areas is required to provide shade for cars and pedestrians, to lessen the visual impact of expansive paved areas, to enhance the overall appearance of the development and to screen the car park from adjacent development.  Refer to Part A Chapter 2 Section 2.6.4 and Part C Chapter 1 of this DCP for Council's detailed landscaping requirements.
