### 3.3 SIGNS ON HERITAGE ITEMS

#### Aim

 To ensure the design and location of signs on heritage items does not detract from the heritage significance of the building or streetscape.

#### Objectives

¾ All signs on heritage items are to be carefully designed and placed to ensure the heritage integrity of the building is not compromised.

#### Rules

(a) Above awning or projecting wall signs are unacceptable.
