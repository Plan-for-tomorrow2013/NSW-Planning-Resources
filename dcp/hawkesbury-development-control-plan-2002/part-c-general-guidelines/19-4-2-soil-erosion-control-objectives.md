### 4.2 SOIL EROSION CONTROL OBJECTIVES

Principles of erosion and sediment control are to:  investigate site features;

 prepare a SESCP;  save topsoil for reuse;

 control run-off onto, through and from the site;

 use erosion control measures to prevent on-site damage;  use sediment control measures to prevent off-site damage;

 rehabilitate disturbed areas quickly; and

 maintain erosion and sediment control measures.
