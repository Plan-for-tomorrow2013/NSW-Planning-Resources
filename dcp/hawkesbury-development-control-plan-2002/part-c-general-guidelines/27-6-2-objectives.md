### 6.2 OBJECTIVES

The objectives in relation to energy efficient homes are to: ¾ improve the quality and energy efficiency of dwellings;

¾ contribute positively to an overall reduction in greenhouse gas emissions;

¾ assist professionals, technicians and tradespersons by providing relevant information and resources;

¾ create homes that are comfortable and economical to live in;

¾ foster partnerships between Council, State Government and industry; and ¾ provide performance criteria and acceptable solutions to cover all aspects of energy efficient residential development in Hawkesbury.
