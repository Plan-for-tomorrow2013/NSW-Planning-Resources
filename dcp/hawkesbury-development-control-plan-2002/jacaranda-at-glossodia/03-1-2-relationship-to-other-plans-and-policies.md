### 1.2 Relationship to other plans and policies

The Hawksbury Development Control Plan (DCP) applies for all development within the Hawkesbury Local Government Area. This Section of the DCP applies in addition to the remainder of the Hawkesbury DCP and provides development controls for development within Jacaranda.

In the event of an inconsistency between other Parts of the Hawkesbury DCP this Chapter prevails.
