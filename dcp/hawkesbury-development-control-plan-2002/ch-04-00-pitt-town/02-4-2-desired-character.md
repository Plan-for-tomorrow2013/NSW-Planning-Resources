### 4.2 DESIRED CHARACTER

Pitt Town provides a relaxed and comfortable lifestyle with a semi rural village character. New development is to maintain a semi rural village character with generous and landscaped building setbacks and open streetscapes within a modified grid urban structure. New development will have building designs and materials compatible with the semi rural setting and traditional housing forms. The public domain is to reinforce the semi rural character of Pitt Town.
