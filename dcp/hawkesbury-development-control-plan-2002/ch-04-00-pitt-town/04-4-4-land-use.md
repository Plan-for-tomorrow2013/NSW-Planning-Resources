### 4.4 LAND USE

#### 4.4.1 Aims

y The proposed use of land does not cause unmanageable conflicts. y The land proposed for development is suitable for the intended use. y There is adequate land set aside within Pitt Town for public purposes to meet the needs of the local population.

#### 4.4.2 Rules

a) The use of land within Pitt Town must be substantially in accordance with the Pitt Town Development Plan presented as Figure E4.2.

b) A site contamination assessment must accompany development applications for subdivision to demonstrate there are no contamination constraints to future development.
