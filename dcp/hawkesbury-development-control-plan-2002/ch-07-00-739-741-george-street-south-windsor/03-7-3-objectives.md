### 7.3 OBJECTIVES

The primary objectives of this chapter are:

a) to ensure that any future development incorporates and reflects sound urban design principles and is compatible with neighbouring land uses and the desired future character of the area

b) to minimise the impact of the development on the Windsor District Baptist Church and its operation on the land

c) to retain and enhance the existing streetscape

d) to minimise any impact on existing flora and fauna on the site

e) provide appropriate framework to enable orderly development on the land
