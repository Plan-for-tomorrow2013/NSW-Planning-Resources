### 1.16 CABLES

#### Objectives

Electricity, telephone, television and other cables must be underground.  Where there is an existing connection of these cables directly from the service pole to the fascia of the front dwelling, these lines only, may remain above ground.

#### Rules

(a) The design, location and construction of utility services must satisfactorily meet the requirements of both the relevant servicing authority and Council.
