### 3.4 INFORMATION REQUIREMENTS FOR APPLICATIONS FOR COMPLYING DEVELOPMENT

An application for a CDC should contain the information, and be accompanied by the documents, specified in Part 2 of Schedule 1 of the Regulation (see www.legislation.nsw.gov.au for details).
