### 3.11 DURATION A COMPLYING DEVELOPMENT CERTIFICATE

A CDC becomes effective and operates from the date endorsed on the certificate.  The CDC lapses 5 years after the date endorsed on the certificate and no proceedings may be taken before a court or tribunal to extend this period.  However, a CDC does not lapse if the development to which it relates is physically commenced on the land within the period of 5 years after the date of endorsement of the certificate.
