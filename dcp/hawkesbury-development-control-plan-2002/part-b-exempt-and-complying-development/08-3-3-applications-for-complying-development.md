### 3.3 APPLICATIONS FOR COMPLYING DEVELOPMENT CERTIFICATES

An application for a CDC may be made by the owner of the land on which the development is proposed to be carried out, or by any other person, with the consent of the owner of that land.

Applications can be made to Council or an accredited certifier.
