### PLAN OF SITE

| APPLICANT TO TICK | REQUIREMENTS | OFFICE USE |  |
|---|---|---|---|
|  |  | Supplied | Required |

| ❏ | The location and use of any existing buildings on the land. | ❏ | ❏ |

| ❏ | The adequacy of the internal accessway design both in terms of amenity and function for the proposed use of the land. | ❏ | ❏ |
