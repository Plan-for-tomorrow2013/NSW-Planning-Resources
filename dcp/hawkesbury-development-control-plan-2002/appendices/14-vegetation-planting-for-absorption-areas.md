#### NOTE

Most ferns are also appropriate, however, Fishbone Fern (Nephrolepis cordifolia) should not be grown as it is a bushland weed.

In addition to these species, a large number of sedges, rushes and native grasses are available. Species include Scirpus, Cyperus, Juncus, Eleocharis, Microlaena, Entolasia, Oplismenus, and Typha.  These species can provide a very effective means of absorbing sewage nutrients and water whilst creating an attractive and low maintenance swamp or grassland feature.
