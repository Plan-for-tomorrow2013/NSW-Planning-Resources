### 10. 3. 3 Natural Heritage

Two thirds of the Hawkesbury LGA is located in National Parks, including the Wollemi, Cattai,

Scheyville, Yengo, Blue Mountains National Parks, and the Parr State Conservation Area,

The western section of the Hawkesbury LGA includes a part of the Greater Blue Mountains World

Heritage Area which contains a wide and balanced representation of eucalypt habitats as well as

localised swamps, wetlands, and grassland.
