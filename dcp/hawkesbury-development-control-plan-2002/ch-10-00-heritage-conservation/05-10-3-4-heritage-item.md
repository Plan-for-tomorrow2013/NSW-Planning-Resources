### 10. 3. 4 Heritage Item

The term ”Heritage Item” is defined in the LEP. All of the heritage items within the Hawkesbury LGA

are listed in Schedule 5 of the LEP and are shown on the Heritage Map of the LEP.

A heritage item may be a house, place, farm shed, a slab barn, church, school, cemetery, road,

bridge, public reserve, the remains of a previous structure, or even a tree which are considered to be

of heritage significance to the Hawkesbury, the Region or the State. A large number of State listed

heritage items are located within the Hawkesbury LGA.
