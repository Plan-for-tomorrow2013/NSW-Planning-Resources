### 8.2 OBJECTIVES

The primary objectives of this Chapter are:

(a) To enable the erection of farm buildings and outbuildings in a manner that complements the landscape character and any scenic qualities of the locality.

(b) To ensure farm buildings and outbuildings are designed and sited with regard to site planning principles and also the requirements specified in this Chapter to minimise the likely impact on the amenity of adjoining land uses, especially dwellings.

(c) To ensure farm buildings and outbuildings are sited to minimise unnecessary disturbances to the natural environment.
