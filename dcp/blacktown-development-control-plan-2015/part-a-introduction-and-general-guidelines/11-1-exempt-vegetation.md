### Appendix 1 Exempt vegetation

Council recognises that in some instances vegetation can be removed without seeking

approval from us. The situations below detail where vegetation exemptions may be applied.

#### A.1.1 Exempt vegetation work

A permit is not required for the following work to vegetation under this Development Control

Plan:

a. the pruning or removal of non-native vegetation, NOT including prescribed trees in this

Development Control Plan, for the maintenance of properties

b. removal of vegetation comprising any of the priority weed species as detailed within

Hawkesbury River County Council Priority Weed List that is not required for habitat

purposes

c. the clearing of non-native vegetation where deemed by Council in writing and shown by

recorded photographic evidence to be dead and is not required for habitat for native fauna

d. the clearing of non-native vegetation where deemed by Council in writing and shown by

recorded photographic evidence to be an imminent risk to human life or is likely to cause

substantial damage to property in the near future

e. native vegetation clearing approved under a Rural Fires Act permit for hazard reduction. You are advised to contact Council if you require clarification on the exempt vegetation

provisions.
