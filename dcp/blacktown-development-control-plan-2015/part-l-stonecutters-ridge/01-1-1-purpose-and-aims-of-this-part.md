## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose and aims of this Part

This Part is known as Blacktown Development Control Plan (DCP) 2015 Part L (Stonecutters Ridge). It has been prepared pursuant to the provisions of Section 72 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979. The purpose of this Part is to provide detailed controls to guide the future development of Stonecutters Ridge. The Plan supplements the site specific controls for the land set out in Blacktown Local Environmental Plan (LEP) 2015 Schedule 1 (Additional permitted uses). The specific aim of this Part is to provide planning and design objectives and controls for the subdivision of land and the subsequent establishment of quality public domain and built form that recognise and reflect the particular characteristics of a golf course oriented lifestyle community.
