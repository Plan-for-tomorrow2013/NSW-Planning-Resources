## 5 Services

Any development proposed within the RU4 Primary Production Small Lots Zone will be required to provide an appropriate level of services to match the intensity of the development. Council cannot grant development consent unless essential services are provided in accordance with Clause 7.5 of Blacktown LEP 2015 (Essential Services). Other requirements relating to the provision of services are contained in Part A of Blacktown DCP 2015.
