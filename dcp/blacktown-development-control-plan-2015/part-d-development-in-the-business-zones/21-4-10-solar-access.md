### 4.10 Solar Access

#### Solar Access

All development should be designed so as to minimise overshadowing and

maximise solar access to adjoining public spaces or adjoining residential land.

Council may require the submission of shadow diagrams to assess the effect of

the proposed development on the surrounding sites, particularly public areas and

residentially zoned land.
