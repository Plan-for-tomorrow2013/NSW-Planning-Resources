### 3.0 BLOCK BY BLOCK CONTROLS

The Quakers Hill Urban Village contains three defined blocks – the Railway

Road Block, the Nirimba Drive Block and the Douglas Road Block.  The

following section sets out the key development outcomes that are sought for

each Block.  These are to be read in conjunction with the detailed design

guidelines contained at Section 4.0 of this section.

The building envelopes indicated within this section of this Part of the DCP do

not infer that automatic approval would be granted for the removal of significant

areas of existing vegetation. Where possible existing vegetation is to be retained

and each Development Application is to be accompanied by a detailed landscape

plan and arborist's report.
