### 4.3 Building Setbacks

#### Building Setbacks

In assessing any application for business development Council will take into

consideration whether a building setback is required for aesthetic purposes or

streetscape design (e.g. the reduction of bulk and scale of buildings by

incorporating boundary setbacks and terracing of storeys) or to enable adequate

sight distances for traffic using adjacent roads.  In some cases a zero building

setback may be acceptable.
