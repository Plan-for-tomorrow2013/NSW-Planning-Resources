### 4.7 Service Laneways

#### Service Laneways

Service laneways are often required, usually in local centres, to provide rear

loading access for delivery vehicles and access to rear car parking where the

retail/commercial development has a zero lot line or minor setback only to the

fronting street.

Where a service laneway is indicated on the DCP map relevant to a business

centre, Council will require the dedication and/or construction of part of the

laneway which is contained within the development site where it is considered

that the proposed development creates a demand for the laneway.
