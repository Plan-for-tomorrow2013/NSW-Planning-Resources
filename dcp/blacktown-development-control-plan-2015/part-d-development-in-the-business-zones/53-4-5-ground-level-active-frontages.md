### 4.5 Ground Level Active Frontages

Objectives

Controls

#### Ground Level Active Frontages

#### Objectives

(1) To ensure adequate non-residential land uses are provides within the village.

(2) To enhance the vitality, activity and safety of the pubic domain.

#### Controls

(1) Active uses are to be provided in accordance with Figures 2 – 4.

(2) ‘Active uses’ include non-residential uses such retail shops, community

facilities and the like.  Lobbies for residential uses above are permitted.

Outdoor eating / seating is encouraged along mandatory active use

frontages.

(3) Commercial uses are preferred at first floor level.

(4) Basement car park entries, service areas, blank walls and the like are to be

avoided on mandatory active use frontages.

(5) Where active uses are mandatory, shopfronts should be generally glazed to

enhance pedestrian interaction.
