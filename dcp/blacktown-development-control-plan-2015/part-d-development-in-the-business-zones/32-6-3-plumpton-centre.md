### 6.3 Plumpton Centre

#### Plumpton Centre

Potential exists within the Plumpton district centre to increase retail floorspace

commensurate with residential growth in the locality.  Where new development

is proposed, the general guidelines in this Part of the DCP should be followed,

with particular emphasis on integration of built form with the design of the

existing centre.
