### 4.12 Signage and Outdoor Advertising

Objectives

Controls

#### Signage and Outdoor Advertising

#### Objectives

(1) To ensure that signage and advertising structures are unobtrusive and co-

ordinated.

#### Controls

(1) All signage is to be consistent with the provisions of SEPP 64 (Advertising

and Signage).

(2) Signage should be integrated with the design of the development by

responding to scale, proportions and architectural detailing.

(3) Provide clear and legible ‘way-finding’ for residents and visitors.
