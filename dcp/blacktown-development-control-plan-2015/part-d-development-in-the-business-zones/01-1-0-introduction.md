1.1 Provisions of Blacktown Local Environmental Plan 1988

#### Provisions of Blacktown Local Environmental Plan 1988

One of the major aims of Blacktown Local Environmental Plan (LEP) 1988 is to

simplify and modernise the development controls applying to the business

centres in the City of Blacktown.  The LEP contains 3 business zones.  These

zones are briefly explained in section 2 of this Part of Blacktown Development

Control Plan (DCP) 2006.
