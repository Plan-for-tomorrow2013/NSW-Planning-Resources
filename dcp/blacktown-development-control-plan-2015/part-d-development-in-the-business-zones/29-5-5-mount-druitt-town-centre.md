### 5.5 Mount Druitt Town Centre

### SUB-REGIONAL

### CENTRES

#### Mount Druitt Town Centre

The Mount Druitt Town Centre is also a sub-regional centre, although secondary

to the Blacktown CBD.

Approximately half the land within the Town Centre is still available for

commercial and/or retail development.  Any new development in the Centre

should relate in function to the existing uses within the Centre.

Intending developers should discuss their proposals with Council prior to

finalising their plans.
