### 2.3 The 3(c) Entertainment and Tourism Zone

#### The 3(c) Entertainment and Tourism Zone

This zone applies only to that land occupied by “Australia’s Wonderland “ at

Eastern Creek, in recognition of the development’s uniqueness.  This zone is

intended to promote the site as a tourist, recreational and employment focus for

Blacktown.  Clause 29 of Blacktown LEP 1988 outlines additional requirements

for this zone.

Residential development within this zone should be limited to development

which is ancillary or complementary to amusement parks, entertainment

facilities, recreational facilities or tourist facilities.
