## 4 Justification

In considering any variation from the guidelines of this Part of Blacktown Development Control Plan 2015, Council will have regard to the following environmental factors:

(a) The location, design and siting of the facility

(b) The existing and proposed use of the land and adjoining properties

(c) The need for the proposal and alternatives considered by the proponent

(d) The proximity of the proposed facility to residential and community facility land uses

(e) Any guidelines, advice or supporting information submitted by the proponent or other authorities which may be relevant

(f) The visual impact of the proposal and measures proposed to ameliorate this impact

(g) Site access, security and landscaping proposals

(h) Any submissions received from the public.
