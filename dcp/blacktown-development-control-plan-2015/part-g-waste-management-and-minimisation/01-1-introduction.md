## 1. Introduction

This part of the Blacktown Development Control Plan 2015 (DCP 2015) sets out the requirements for waste management and minimisation for new developments and should be read in conjunction with the Blacktown City guideline for waste management in new developments. Both documents will be used to assess any development application. Note, reference to truck access in this document relates to waste collection vehicles only. General use loading bays, including those proposing to co-locate with waste servicing loading bays, must comply with requirements from Council’s Access and Transport Management section.

### 1.1 Aims of this Part

a. Ensure all new developments consider the design of disposal and management of waste generated onsite throughout all stages of the development.

b. Increase opportunities for reuse and recycling of building, household, industrial and commercial waste.

c. Establish the framework for the delivery of Australian and NSW Government waste reduction targets.

d. Provide waste management objectives and performance standards to assist in designing developments with suitable waste management systems for ongoing use.

e. Help reduce the overall negative environmental effects of waste.

### 1.2 Objectives of this Part

a. Decrease the amount of waste being sent to landfill and maximise opportunities for reuse and recycling.

b. Improve separation of waste types at the source.

c. Provide design standards that will assist waste collections.

d. Maintain the amenity and character of the neighbourhood.

e. Support the circular economy by considering the whole life impact of the development.

f. Guide the preparation of waste management plans.

### 1.3 Relationship to other documents

This part has been prepared to meet the objectives of the Waste Avoidance and Resource Recovery Act 2001. It supplements the provisions contained within the Blacktown Local Environment Plan 2015 and should be read with all relevant Parts of the Blacktown DCP 2015 and any other relevant Blacktown City Council policy, standard or guideline.

### 1.4 When does this Part apply?

This part applies to all development and to any activity that requires development consent under the provisions of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979.

### 1.5 How to read this Part

For developments identified in Part 5 Other development types, refer to the matrix at Appendix 1. You will need to identify your development type and address as part of your submission to Council, the requirements identified with a tick.
