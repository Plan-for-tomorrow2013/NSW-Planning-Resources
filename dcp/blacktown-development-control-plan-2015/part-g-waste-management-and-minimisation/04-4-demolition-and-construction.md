## 4. Demolition and construction

### 4.1 Objective

The objective of this section is to reduce waste generation and maximise reuse and recycling during any building demolition and construction stage of the development.

### 4.2 Development controls

#### 4.2.1 Preparing a waste management plan

A waste management plan is to be submitted with every development application for demolition and construction and must demonstrate how reuse and recycling will be maximised and waste going to landfill reduced. The waste management plan must indicate how waste reduction at the demolition and construction stages will be achieved allowing for reuse of solid waste either on or offsite through:

• staging of works

• appropriate onsite storage

• source separation. The waste management plan must:

• identify volumes of waste materials likely to be generated from the demolition and construction activity

• identify the materials and their expected volumes that can be recycled or reused either on or offsite

• detail the methods chosen to ensure the separation of materials for reuse, recycling and disposal. Consideration must be given to environmental factors such as slope, drainage, location of waterways, stormwater outlets, vegetation, and access and handling requirements

• outline the arrangements that will be made with contractors to ensure materials that cannot be reused or recycled are safely separated and disposed of at licensed landfills. Receipts for all disposed materials must be kept and made available for inspection by regulatory authorities

• outline how asbestos containing material, hazardous and/or intractable wastes will be disposed of in line with WorkSafe NSW and Environment Protection Authority requirements. Refer to our policy on Asbestos Management in Blacktown City for more specific information on how to deal with asbestos containing materials

• outline measures for onsite litter management, including the methods to reduce airborne litter and litter entering the stormwater system

• detail methods used to reduce site disturbance and eliminate unnecessary excavation

• identify the condition of materials and any possible contamination from hazardous materials such as asbestos containing material

• comply with the required information as outlined in the waste management plan template in the Blacktown City guideline for waste management in new developments.

#### 4.2.2 Preparing scaled site and floor plans

The waste management plan must be supported by scaled site and floor plans that clearly identify the:

• nominated area onsite for separate collection bins for residual waste. Bin and storage areas must be clearly signposted to identify their intended contents and purpose

• storage requirements for goods and materials onsite including the provision of a temporary storage area for materials generated by demolition and construction activity that can be used at a later stage.
