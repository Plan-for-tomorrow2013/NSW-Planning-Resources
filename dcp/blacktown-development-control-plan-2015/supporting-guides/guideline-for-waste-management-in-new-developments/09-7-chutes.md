## 7. Chutes

Chutes are required for any residential or mixed use development of 3 storeys or more with a lift.

### 7.1 Design

Chutes must:

• be for garbage only. Dual chutes and e-diverters for waste and recyclables are not supported.

• be a maximum 30 metres from any dwelling on any residential floor

• be contained within a waste chute room on each residential level with storage for a 240 litre recycling bin to be rotated by the building manager

• be designed to reduce noise nuisance

• be completely enclosed and fire rated to comply with the National Construction Code

• discharge directly into 1100 litre bulk waste bins in the waste room or chute discharge room

• be caged at the discharge point if located inside the main waste room(s)

• comply with manufacturer specifications and operational limitations

### 7.2 Chute contingency

Where chutes are proposed, applicants must demonstrate that the waste chute rooms can accommodate a 240 litre bin for waste and a 240 litre bin for recycling in case the chutes fail or malfunction. The building manager must rotate these bins as required.

#### 7.2.1 Bin tippers

As part of this contingency plan, a bin tipper must also be provided for the site. Bin tippers will allow the contents of the 240 litre waste bins to be transferred to the larger 1100 litre waste bins in the waste rooms. Consideration must be given to the required clearance heights for the bin tipper in the location where it will be operating. The location of the bin tipper must be identified on the architectural plans. The waste management plan must include the specification sheet, details on how this equipment will be used onsite and where it will be stored.
