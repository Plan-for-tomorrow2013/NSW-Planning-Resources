## 8. Bin movement

Select bin movement aids by considering the distance that the bins need to be moved across a site to get them from the interim waste room(s) to the waste room(s) and collection point(s). Maximum bin travel distances are outlined in Part G Waste management and minimisation.

### 8.1 Bin movement aids

Designs must ensure that bins and bulky waste do not need to be manually moved from the storage area to the collection point through high traffic areas such as driveways or at the base of ramps.
