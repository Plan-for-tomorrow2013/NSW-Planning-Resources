## 10. Private contractors

Developments must be designed to accommodate a waste service to the requirements outlined by Part G Waste management and minimisation. This ensures residents are always able to access waste services provided by Council, including household clean ups.

A private waste and recycling service may be elected for a development. If private contractors are selected, you must consult with the contractor for service requirements in the early stages of development.

Alternative methods of waste collection may be required for selected sites as determined by Council during the assessment process. Truck access and design where the use of small rigid vehicles is the only option (see Section 11.7), design must be in accordance with AS2890.2.

Where it is demonstrated that a site cannot accommodate a residential waste service to Council’s requirements or if the applicant chooses to engage a private waste and recycling contractor, details of the collection arrangement must be outlined in the waste management plan.

### 10.1 Selecting private contractors

The private waste and recycling contractors must be experienced in collecting and managing waste in Western Sydney. Applicants must provide confirmation on company letterhead from 2 established private waste and recycling contractors that they can access and service the site with the proposed vehicle. The Owners Corporation/Body Corporate must fund a private service.

### 10.2 Section 88E for private contractors

For residential developments serviced by private contractors, your residents will not have access to Council’s waste, recycling and household clean-up services for furniture and whitegoods.

A Section 88E will be imposed on any consent requiring a private contractor to indemnify Council in respect of any claim regarding the non-provision of bin and bulky waste services by Blacktown City Council.
