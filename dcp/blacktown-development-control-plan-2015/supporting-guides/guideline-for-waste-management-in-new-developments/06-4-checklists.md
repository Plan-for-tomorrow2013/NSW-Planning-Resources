## 4. Checklists

Checklists in Appendix 4 have been developed for demolition, construction, residential, mixed use and commercial and industrial developments. As part of your waste management plan, the relevant checklist must be completed. For waste management plans prepared by an experienced waste consultant, the checklist should be included as an appendix to your submission.

### 4.1 Demolition

The demolition checklist located in the waste management plan template (Appendix 4) will assist in preparing a waste management plan with demolition activity. Table 1 provides some options for reuse and recycling of building material.

Table 1: Opportunities to reuse and recycle building materials

| Waste material | Reuse and recycling opportunities |
|---|---|
| Concrete | reused for filling, levelling or road base |
| Bricks and pavers | cleaned for reuse, rendered over or crushed for landscaping or driveways |
| Roof tiles | cleaned and reused in landscaping or driveways |
| Untreated timber | reused as floorboards, fencing, furniture, mulched or sent to second-hand timber suppliers |
| Treated timber | reused as formwork, bridging, blocking and propping or sent to second-hand timber suppliers |
| Doors, windows, fittings | sent to second-hand timber suppliers |
| Glass | reused as glazing or aggregate for concrete production |
| Metals | removed for recycling |
| Synthetic rubber | reprocessed for reuse in safety devices and speed humps |
| Significant trees | relocated either on or off-site |
| Garden waste | mulched or composted |
| Plasterboard | removed for recycling or returned to the supplier |

### 4.2 Construction

The demolition checklist located in the waste management plan template (Appendix 4) will assist in preparing a waste management plan with construction activity. Table 2 contains some ideas on how to reduce waste through the demolition and construction stages of development.

Table 2: Ways to reduce waste through the demolition and construction stages

| Stage | Ways to reduce waste |
|---|---|
| Initial planning | • identify and communicate roles with all stakeholders to gain relevant information • identify how you can maximise retaining materials for reuse • identify all waste streams and estimate volumes • set waste reduction targets • research sustainable building material alternatives • research disposal costs. |
| Design | • design using standard material sizes • implement a purchasing policy. |
| Off-site activities | • use prefabricated materials • choose suppliers who will take back excess stock and resources • inform stakeholders of the waste management plan requirements • communicate with sub-contractors • implement a delivery plan for the project. |
| Onsite activities | • secure the site to reduce illegal dumping • identify storage requirements • select resources and materials with less packaging • separate, reuse and recycle • ensure bins and storage areas are clearly labelled with adequate signage • communicate with all staff and contractors, hold toolbox talks • establish a monitoring system during onsite construction activity • undertake safe and lawful disposal of unavoidable waste • keep necessary documentation regarding material recycling and disposal at licenced waste facilities. |
