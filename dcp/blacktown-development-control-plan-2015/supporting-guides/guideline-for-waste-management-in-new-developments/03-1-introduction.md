## 1. Introduction

Part G – Waste management and minimisation the Blacktown Development Control Plan 2015 (DCP 2015) sets out the requirements for waste management in new developments. This guideline must be read in conjunction with Part G Waste management and minimisation as both documents will be used to assess a development application.
