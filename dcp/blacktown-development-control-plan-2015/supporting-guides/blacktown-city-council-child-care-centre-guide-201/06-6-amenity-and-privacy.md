## 6. Amenity and Privacy

### 6.1. Ventilation and lighting

Child care centres must be designed to provide maximum natural light and proper ventilation, with natural airflow for sufficient cooling and heating. All natural ventilation must comply with the Building Code of Australia All heating and cooling units must be inaccessible to children. Space heating equipment must not include:

• open fires

• combustible liquids

• exposed flame heaters

• exposed floor electric heaters. External lighting must be provided for the safety of users of the site.

### 6.2. Privacy

Child care centres must not overlook the living areas or private open space areas of adjoining residential properties. To help preserve privacy of adjoining residential properties the side and rear setbacks should be planted with a 1 m wide landscape buffer. The lighting design must ensure that external lights do not cause nuisance to adjoining properties. The acoustic report must address how the design minimises potential noise transmission within play areas and on adjoining residential properties. Matters to be considered may include:

• ceiling form, soft surfaces or acoustic wall panelling in play rooms

• locating driveways, car parking, pedestrian walkways and ramps away from playrooms and sleeping rooms within the centre, and the habitable rooms and private open space of surrounding dwellings

• locating air conditioning systems and plant equipment so that they do not cause ‘offensive noise.’
