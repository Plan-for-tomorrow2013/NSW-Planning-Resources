## 3. Building design

### 3.1. General considerations

The design and layout of child care centres must create a stimulating, attractive and engaging environment, with a range of flexible spaces for children to learn and play. We recommend that materials and colours that are attractive and inviting to children are incorporated into the building design. The built form must be compatible with the established streetscape in terms of height, bulk, scale and external finishes. It is recommended that free-standing child care centres in residential area are designed to look like a dwelling house when viewed from the street. The building design must incorporate eaves for shade, weather protection and climate control. Stairways must be fitted with child proof barriers that will prevent any child entering the stairway without direct adult supervision
