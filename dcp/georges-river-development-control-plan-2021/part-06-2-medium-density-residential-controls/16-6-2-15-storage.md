#### 6.2.15 Storage

The inclusion of provision for storage augments the quality of accommodation by ensuring that residents have flexibility in accommodating items such as sporting equipment and seasonal equipment.

#### Objectives

a) Adequate, well designed storage is provided in each dwelling.

#### Controls

1\. In addition to storage in kitchens, and bedrooms, the following storage volumes with a minimum dimension of 500mm is to be provided for each dwelling:

i. One (1) bed dwelling 6m³

ii. Two (2) bed dwelling 8m³

iii. Three (3)+ bed dwelling 10m³
