## 6. Fencing

#### Objectives

a) To minimise the impact of fencing when viewed from the water.

#### Controls

1\. Fencing, between the Mean High Water Mark (MHWM) and the Foreshore Building Line (FBL), including boundary fencing should be no higher than 1200mm above the natural or finished ground level.

2\. Fences, between the Mean High Water Mark (MHWM) and the Foreshore Building Line (FBL), are to be constructed of open weave materials to enable vines, creepers and hedges to provide natural cover.
