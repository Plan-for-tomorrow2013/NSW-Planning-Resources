## 2. Residential Waterfront Structures

#### Objectives

a) To ensure sufficient open space between residential waterfront structures to ensure they do not visually dominate the waterfront.

#### Controls

1\. Residential waterfront structures are not developed on:

i. Land which does not have frontage to the waterway (this includes allotments which only have a right of way to the waterway).

ii. Existing allotments having a water frontage of less than 9 metres.
