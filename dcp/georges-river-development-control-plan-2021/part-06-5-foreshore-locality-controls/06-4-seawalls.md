## 4. Seawalls

Note: where the foreshore is in its natural state, seawalls will generally not be permitted.

#### Objectives

a) To protect the land from the waterbody or to stop accelerated erosion of the shoreline, avoiding flooding or for necessary retention works.

b) To ensure that seawalls are sympathetic to the natural character of the foreshore.

c) To encourage environmentally friendly seawalls.

#### Controls

1\. Seawalls must be located wholly within private land above MHWM. Council may permit the replacement of a seawall below MHWM where there is an existing seawall and the reclamation has been authorised under a permissive occupancy.

2\. The height of the seawall must be flush with the retained ground level or the reclamation level located behind it.

3\. Vertical seawalls are discouraged as they offer little aquatic habitat. A sloping seawall with nooks and crannies for fish and invertebrates to hide in is preferred.

4\. Natural sandstone blocks or sandstone facing over concrete walls are preferred.

5\. Environmentally friendly seawalls are designed to consider:

i. Establishing estuarine vegetation such as mangroves directly in front of seawalls.

ii. Providing a native riparian vegetation buffer landward of the seawall.

iii. Providing artificial reef habitat immediately in front of seawalls.

iv. Providing variation of texture and form on the seawall surface.
