#### 8.1.1 Background

The strategic centre of Kogarah is one of two strategic centres in the Georges River LGA and is comprised of the Kogarah Town Centre and the surrounding medical, allied health and education uses. The Greater Sydney Commission (GSC) identifies Kogarah as a health and education precinct due to the high concentration of medical facilities and schools in addition to the presence of a mix of retail and community activities.

Kogarah is also highlighted as a Collaboration Area where a place-based and multi-stakeholder process commenced in March 2019 to deliver regionally significant outcomes in terms of liveability, productivity, connectivity and sustainability. This work culminated in the development of the “Collaboration Area Kogarah Place Strategy” by the GSC in December

2019\. The Collaboration Area (refer to Figure 1) covers a wider area when compared to the Kogarah Town Centre.

The Kogarah Town Centre is bounded by Gladstone Street, the T4 Eastern Suburbs & Illawarra train line, the Princes Highway and Gray Street. The town centre performs a primary civic and population serving role for the Collaboration Area and supports the residents of the area, and the workers and visitors to the health and education core.
