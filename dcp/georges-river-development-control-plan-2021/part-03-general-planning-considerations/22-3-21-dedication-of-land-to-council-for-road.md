### 3.21 Dedication of Land to Council for Road/Lane Widening

#### Objectives

a) Extend existing land and street frontage alignments to improve public amenity and access.

b) Provide clear lines of sight between existing roads in designated locations to allow for improved footpaths, vehicle movement and parking.

#### Controls

1\. Approval for development in the locations listed in the table below will be subject to dedication of land (for road/lane widening) without cost to Council. A method for the dedication of land is by planning agreement under s 7.4 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979. Council’s planning agreements policy is available on Council’s website.

| Suburb | Location | Land Dedication |
|---|---|---|
| Beverly Hills | Land fronting McCready Lane • 7 Edgbaston Road • 10 Melvin Street • 18 Melvin Street • 24 Melvin Street • 32 Melvin Street • 44 Melvin Street | Any redevelopment of lots is to include a 3m land strip to be dedicated to Council to enable the widening of McCready Lane. |
| Hurstville | Land fronting Woniora Road • 36 Woniora Road • 64 Woniora Road • 66 Woniora Road | Any redevelopment of lots is to include a 3m land strip to be dedicated to Council to enable the widening of Woniora Road. |
| Hurstville | Land fronting Finney Lane • 15 West Street • 17 West Street • 19 West Street | Any redevelopment of lots is to include a 3m land strip at the rear to be dedicated to Council to enable the widening of Finney Lane. |

2\. The area of the land to be dedicated can be included in the site area when calculating the maximum permissible gross floor area of the development.
