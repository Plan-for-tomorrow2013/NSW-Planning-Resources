## 2. Relationships to other documents and planning policies

Where relevant, these provisions should be read in conjunction with the following:

• The NSW Government’s Apartment Design Guide;

• State Environmental Planning Policy Housing (Housing SEPP); and

• Georges River Local Environmental Plan 2021.

This section also needs to be read in conjunction with the following parts of this DCP:

• Part 1 – Introduction

• Part 2 – Application Process for Georges River DCP 2021

• Part 3 – General Planning Considerations

• Part 4 – General Land Use

• Part 6 – Residential Controls

• Appendices

Development within the Site must comply with all other applicable parts of the DCP. If there is a discrepancy between Section 10.3 and other parts of the DCP, the controls in Section 10.3 will always prevail.
