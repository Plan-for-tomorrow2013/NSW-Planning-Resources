### 10.2 53A-59A Gloucester Road, Hurstville

#### 10.2.1 Introduction

This section contains built form and design provisions to guide the future residential care facility at 53A-59A Gloucester Road, Hurstville as permitted in accordance with clause 6.17 of the Georges River Local Environmental Plan 2021.

Under Clause 6.17 the maximum heights and FSR for the purposes of a residential care facility at 53A-59A Gloucester Road Hurstville 12m, 14m and 16.9m and 1.6:1 respectively.  Refer to Part 10.2.2 of this DCP for the location of the range of heights over the Site.
