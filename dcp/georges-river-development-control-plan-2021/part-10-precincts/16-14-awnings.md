## 14. Awnings

#### Objective

(a) Provide pedestrian amenity and streetscape continuity.

#### Controls

1\. Awnings are to be sized to adequately accommodate street trees. In addition, ground floor street frontages must be recessed into the building to provide an ample undercover passage without impacting street tree planting.

2\. Provide under awning lighting to enhance safety.
