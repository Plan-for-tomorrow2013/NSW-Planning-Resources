## 16. Solar Access to Public Domain

(a) Ensure development retains reasonable levels of solar access to the current and future planned open space in the public domain.

#### Controls

1\. Maintain solar access to public open space.

2\. New development shall maintain solar access to open space, including the open space (including the area currently used for car parking) bounded by Victoria Street, Gladstone Street, Victor Street and Palmerston Street.  This open space is identified by Council as being a future Town Common, for shared use by the school and residents.
