#### Part 7.0 – Business Precincts

This Part establishes provisions for development within all Business-zoned land, being E1 Local Centre, E2 Commercial Centre and MU1 Mixed Use.

All development proposals within these business precincts must refer to the provisions within this Part that relate to the type of development being proposed.

In the event of a discrepancy between the controls specified in this Part of the DCP and Part 8.0 Strategic Centres, the specific requirements identified within Part 8.0 of the DCP shall prevail.
