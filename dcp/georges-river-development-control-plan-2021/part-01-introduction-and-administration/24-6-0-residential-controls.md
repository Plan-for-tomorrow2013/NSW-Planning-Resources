#### Part 6.0 – Residential Controls

This Part establishes provisions for certain types of developments including single dwellings, terraces and dual occupancies; residential flat buildings and ancillary structures. This Part also includes Foreshore Locality provisions.

All development proposals must refer to the provisions that best describes that type of development being proposed.
