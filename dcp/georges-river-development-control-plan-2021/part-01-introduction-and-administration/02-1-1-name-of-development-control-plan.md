### 1.1 Name of Development Control Plan

This Development Control Plan is called the Georges River Development Control Plan (DCP) 2021. This DCP supports the provisions of the Georges River Local Environmental Plan 2021 (LEP) by providing additional objectives and development controls to guide and enhance development within Georges River.
