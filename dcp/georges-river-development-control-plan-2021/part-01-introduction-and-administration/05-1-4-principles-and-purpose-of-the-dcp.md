### 1.4 Principles and Purpose of the DCP

The aims of this DCP are to:

• Have a single document that supports the Georges River LEP 2021;

• Provide objectives and development controls that establish clear guidelines for development;

• Develop a high quality urban environment and built form character in the Georges River LGA;

• Ensure development contributes to the prosperity of the Georges River LGA; and

• Ensure development protects and enhances the natural environment.
