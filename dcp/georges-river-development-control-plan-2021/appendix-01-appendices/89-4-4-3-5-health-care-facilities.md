#### 4.4.3.5 Health Care Facilities

a) Waste storage and handling must be in accordance with requirements of NSW Health and relevant Waste Management Guidelines for Health Care Facilities.

b) Waste storage facilities must allow for physically separated storage of:

i. Clinical, hazardous and related waste

ii. Garbage and recycling

c) Clinical and hazardous waste must be clearly colour-coded and labelled as per NSW Health requirements.

d) All waste generated from Health Care facilities to be managed in accordance with relevant NSW Health and EPA regulations.
