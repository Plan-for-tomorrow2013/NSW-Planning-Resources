#### 2.2.2 Nos. 211-219 Rocky Point Road, Ramsgate

#### Objective

(a) Minimise the impact of development on the adjoining heritage building

#### Controls

1\. Development adjacent to the heritage listed buildings at Nos. 211-219 Rocky Point Road, Ramsgate is to step down to a maximum of two (2) levels to reduce the impact on the heritage listed building.
