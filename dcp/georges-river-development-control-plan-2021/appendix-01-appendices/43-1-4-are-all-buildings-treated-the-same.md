### 1.4 Are All Buildings Treated the Same?

Council recognises that conservation areas are not museum pieces and stresses that the important principle is to respect their essential character by conserving significant elements and adding new sympathetically designed elements. Buildings in the area generally fall into one of 3 categories.

1\. Contributory Items

2\. Neutral Items

3\. Intrusive Items
