#### 4.4.3.2 Retail

f) For premises with high volumes of cardboard waste or if the development includes more than 2,000 m2 of retail space, an area for a cardboard baler/compactor is to be provided within or in close proximity to the waste and recycling storage area.

g) Additional space or reduction systems for handling and storing plastic shrink-wrap should be allocated where applicable.

h) Additional space for handling and storage for pallets and reusable crates should be allocated where applicable.
