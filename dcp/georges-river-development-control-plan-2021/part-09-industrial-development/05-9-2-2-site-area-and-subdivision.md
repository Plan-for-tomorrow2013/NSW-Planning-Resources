#### 9.2.2 Site Area and Subdivision

#### Objectives

(a) Ensure sites are of sufficient size to accommodate a range of industrial, warehouse and related uses; and

(b) Ensure sites are large enough to achieve appropriate streetscape presentation and adequate vehicle access, vehicle manoeuvrability and parking.

#### Controls

1\. Development is to comply with the minimum lot size standards for land zoned E4 General Industrial as prescribed in Clause 4.1 of the Georges River LEP 2021 and associated maps.
