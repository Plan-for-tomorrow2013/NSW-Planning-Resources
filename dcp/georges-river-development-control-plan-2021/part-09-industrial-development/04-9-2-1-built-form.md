### 9.2 General Provisions

#### 9.2.1 Built Form

#### Objectives

(a) Ensure that the form, scale, design and nature of development maintains and enhances the visual quality of industrial areas;

(b) Ensure that the scale of any new industrial development is compatible with surrounding development; and

(c) Floor space is distributed on the site to ensure that the height, bulk and scale of development is in context with the surrounding development.

#### Controls

1\. Development is to comply with the maximum Height of Building Standard for land zoned E4 General Industrial as prescribed in Clause 4.3 and associated maps of the Georges River LEP 2021.

2\. Development is to comply with the maximum Floor Space Ratio Standard for land zoned E4 General Industrial as prescribed in Clause 4.4 and associated maps of the Georges River LEP 2021.
