#### 9.1.2 General Objectives

(a) Encourage employment generating uses;

(b) Guide the nature, scale and quality of development in the industrial areas;

(c) Ensure that the design of future development within the industrial areas enhances the amenity, visual quality and character of the area;

(d) Encourage the development of well-landscaped industrial zoned with well-maintained industrial/commercial buildings and sites;

(e) Ensure development has minimal impacts on adjacent sensitive land uses;

(f) Ensure that development incorporates safe, effective, adequate and convenient provision for servicing, parking, pedestrian and vehicular access and movements;

(g) Ensure that the effects of development upon drainage, water quality and stormwater management are considered; and

(h) Encourage ecologically sustainable development.
