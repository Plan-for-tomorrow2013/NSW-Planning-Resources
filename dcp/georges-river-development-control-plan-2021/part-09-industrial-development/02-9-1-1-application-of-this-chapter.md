### 9.1 Introduction

#### 9.1.1 Application of this chapter

This part applies to all land zoned E4 General Industrial under the Georges River LEP 2021.

Existing dwelling houses in the E4 zone and alterations to such dwellings are to comply with relevant provisions within Georges River LEP 2021 and Part 3 – General Planning Considerations and Part 6.0 – Residential Controls of this DCP.
