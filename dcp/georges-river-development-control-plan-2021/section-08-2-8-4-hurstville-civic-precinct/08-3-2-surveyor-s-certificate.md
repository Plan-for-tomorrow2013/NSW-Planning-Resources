### 3.2 Surveyor’s Certificate

#### Objectives

a) To ensure employment generating and community oriented land uses continue to be developed on the Site.

#### Controls

1\. A Development Application for the Site must be accompanied by a Surveyor’s Certificate, including gross floor area diagrams to scale of each and every proposed level that indicates the breakup of floor area by residential, non-residential and community uses and facilities consistent with the requirements of Clause 6.18 of the GRLEP 2021 to provide a minimum of 45% of the Site for non-residential land uses and a minimum of 25% of the Site for community uses and facilities; for the purpose of calculating gross floor area.

Note: This is to be accompanied by a properly scaled surveyor diagram.
