## 12. Materials, Colour Schemes and Details

#### Objectives

a) To ensure that the choice of external materials, colour schemes and building details in new development and existing houses where visible from a public place, reinforces and enhances any identifiable visual cohesiveness or special qualities evident in the street and the locality.

#### Controls

1\. No large expansive surfaces of predominantly white, light or primary colours would dominate the streetscape or other vista should be used.

2\. New development should incorporate colour schemes that have a hue and tonal relationship with the predominant colour schemes found in the street.

3\. Matching buildings in a row should be finished in the same colour or have a tonal relationship.

4\. All materials and finishes utilised should have low reflectivity.
