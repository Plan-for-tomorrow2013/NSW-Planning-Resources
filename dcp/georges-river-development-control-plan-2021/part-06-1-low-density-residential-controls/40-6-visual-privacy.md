## 6. Visual Privacy

#### Objectives

a) Development minimises direct overlooking between main living areas and areas of principal private open space within the site and adjoining sites.

1\. The main windows and balconies of a dwelling are directed toward the front and rear of a site.

2\. Windows are not located directly opposite each other.

3\. Where windows and balconies cannot be off-set, they are separated by sufficient distance, screened or contain frosted glass or other suitable material.

4\. Dormer windows are no wider than 1.5m and are proportional to the roof-scape.

5\. First floor balconies located at the rear of dwellings incorporate fin walls or privacy screens on the side.

6\. Where privacy screens are used, they are not to be higher than 1.8m and are compatible with the building design.
