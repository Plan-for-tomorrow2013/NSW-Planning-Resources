## 4. Parking / Access

#### Objectives

a) Encourage parking provision relative to lot sizes.

#### Controls

1\. One (1) garage space must be provided. A reduced rate of parking is permitted to reflect the smaller site areas and encourage a lower rate of car usage.

2\. Where there is no lane to the rear, garaging must be incorporated within the dwelling (see Figure 12).

3\. If more than one (1) space is required, the space must be tandem with only a single garage door presenting to the street.

4\. The front setback must allow for a car to stand in the driveway so as not to force parking onto the street (minimum 5.5m).

5\. Where properties adjoin Burraneer Close to the rear, vehicular access must be via the laneway. Garaging of this nature must be detached from the dwelling and have a maximum floor to ceiling height of 3m.
