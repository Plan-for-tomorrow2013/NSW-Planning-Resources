## 9. Subdivision

Subdivision of dual occupancy development is permitted with consent. The suitability of the dual occupancy development and its impacts upon streetscape and general amenity will guide the suitability of the underlying lots to be created.

The minimum lot sizes that will apply to subdivision of a dual occupancy is contained within the GRLEP 2021 as referenced at Clauses 4.1A Minimum subdivision lot size for dual occupancies.
