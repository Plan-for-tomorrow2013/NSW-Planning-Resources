## 4. Facades

#### Objectives

Facades:

a) Reduce the appearance of building scale and bulk.

b) Facilitate engagement with and activation of open space in the street.

c) Achieve a high level of design and architectural quality.

#### Controls

1\. The dwelling house has a front door or window to a habitable room facing the primary street frontage.

2\. The dwelling house incorporates at least two of the following building elements facing any street frontage:

i. Entry feature or portico.

ii. Awnings or other features over windows.

iii. Eaves and sun shading.

iv. Window Planter box treatment.

v. Bay windows or similar features.

vi. Wall offsets, balconies, verandas, pergolas and the like.
