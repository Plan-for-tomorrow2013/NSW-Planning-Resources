## 1. Building Scale

#### Objectives

a) Provide buildings that are proportionate to the existing subdivision pattern and ensure appropriate bulk and scale.

#### Controls

1\. The maximum number of dwellings that may be attached (nil setbacks) is two (2) so as to break up development and minimise bulk.
