#### 7.1.5 Shopping Trolley Management Plan

1\. A Shopping Trolley Management Plan is to be submitted with any Development Application that seeks approval for a supermarket.

2\. The Shopping Trolley Management Plan is to outline in detail how shopping trolleys will be managed on the site.

3\. The Plan is to include all aspects of trolley management and should specifically address the following:

a. How the operator will restrict the movement of shopping trolleys outside the development;

b. How trolleys will be returned to the trolley bays; and

c. How it is proposed to manage any shopping trolleys that are taken off site.
