#### 7.1.6 Plant Rooms

#### Objectives

a) Minimise the massing and visual impact of the development.

#### Controls

1\. Plant rooms/ devices, where possible, should be located in the basement or integrated into the building design, or where located on the roof, any plant room should be well setback from all frontages.
