b) Residential Flat Buildings are defined in accordance with Fairfield Local Environmental Plan 2013.

c) Model Waste and Recycling storage and collection infrastructure configurations are outlined in Appendix H Model Waste Infrastructure section 3.

d) Developments to be designed to permit integrated on-site waste collection by Councils standard 10,800mm heavy rigid waste collection vehicle. A detailed swept path analysis supported by scaled architectural plans to be submitted to Council to permit a detailed assessment. The swept path model to:

i. Demonstrate compliance with Australian Standards (AS) 2890.2 for a heavy rigid waste collection vehicle

ii. The preparation of the swept path models to be in accordance with the Austroads Design Vehicles and Turning Path Templates (latest edition)

iii. On-street parking to be provided on both sides of the road to demonstrate a business-as-usual model responsive to local traffic and parking availabilities

iv. Councils standard heavy vehicle waste collection vehicle to:

1\) Enter and exit the site ins a forward direction

2\) Can perform all required access and collection manoeuvres without impeding vehicle, pedestrian, cyclist, and user safety

3\) Have unobstructed minimum height clearances, maximum ramp grades, maximum rates of change, and unobstructed operational

clearances throughout all required manoeuvres (ingress, egress, loading bay access) to permit safe and efficient scheduled collections

4\) A minimum 500mm clearance to be provided from all obstructions throughout all required vehicle manoeuvres to perform scheduled collections

e) Specifications of Councils standard 10,800mm heavy rigid waste collection vehicle are outlined in Appendix H Model Waste Infrastructure section 3.14 – 3.17.

f) To support the provision of safe and efficient waste collection service, the integrated on-site multi use loading area to:

i. Be fully enclosed, walled and accessed via a restricted opening to provide physical separation from vehicles, pedestrian, cyclists, and other users

ii. Mechanically ventilated

iii. Incorporate acoustic attenuation and supporting infrastructure detailed within a report prepared by an acoustic consultant to support use of a heavy rigid collection vehicle at high revs

iv. Provide a 2,000mm unobstructed clearance at the rear of the vehicle to support the collection of Councils waste streams

g) Multi use loading areas outlined in section f) above that propose the use of turn tables are, to incorporate the following infrastructure and specifications:

i. Hydraulic override or similar assisted override system to permit the turn table to rotate in the event of a power outage or systems failure

ii. Control panel locked and keyed to Councils bi-lock key system

h) The kerbside presentation of Councils waste and recycling streams is not an acceptable solution, including pull-in bays for Councils standard waste collection vehicles.
