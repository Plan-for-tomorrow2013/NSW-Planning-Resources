#### 7.7.1.3 Site Restricted Developments

There may be lot characteristics or constraints that restrict the opportunity for the development to accommodate an integrated on-site loading arrangement for Councils standard vehicle. To explore alternative solutions for site restricted lots determined by Council, the onus is on the applicant to demonstrate that:

a) The adjacent lot/s cannot be acquired to support an integrated on-site site waste outcome

b) An improved operational waste outcome can be achieved for Council, the residents and the wider community that:

i. Does not compromise safety

ii. Supports an enhanced outcome for:

1\) Urban Design

2\) Streetscape

3\) Deep Soil

4\) Tree Canopies

iii. Maintains existing on-street parking allocations

c) A heightened amenity outcome is achieved for the development and the surrounding community

d) The arrangement inhibits the permeation of odour and the opportunity for vermin

e) Permits collections in accordance with Councils waste and recycling collection contract provisions
