## Chapter 7 – Residential Flat Buildings

i. Room large enough to accommodate the entire bin allocation with 200mm clearance between each bin for manoeuvrability

ii. Bunded bin wash bay to permit scheduled cleaning of Councils assigned bin infrastructure

iii. Accessed via dual, 180-degree, outwards opening, self-closing sealed doors with a minimum opening of 1,800mm

iv. Room enclosed, walled, and not permit through access to other on-site infrastructure

v. Doors locked through Councils bi-lock system. Councils’ key specifications will be provided upon request

vi. Path of travel from the room to the multi-use loading bay to not pass through an active vehicle carriageway

vii. Located within 10000mm of the integrated on-site multi use loading area

h) A bulky waste collection room to be located within close proximity to the integrated on-site waste loading bay. The room to accommodate the following infrastructure:

i. Minimum unobstructed internal area in accordance with the following calculation:

1\) Bulky waste area (m2) = total dwellings x 0.25m2

\*Minimum area of 10m2 to be provided to accommodate Councils waste stream

ii. Accessed via dual, 180-degree, outwards opening, self-closing sealed doors with a minimum opening of 1,800mm

iii. Minimum unobstructed internal width of 1,800mm

iv. Room enclosed, walled, and not permit through access to other on-site infrastructure

v. Doors locked through Councils bi-lock system. Councils’ key specifications will be provided upon request

vi. Path of travel from the room to the multi-use loading bay to not pass through an active vehicle carriageway

vii. Located within 10000mm of the integrated on-site multi use loading area

i) A bin tug device and trailer to be provided to facilitate the safe and efficient transport of Councils bin infrastructure within the development. The room to accommodate the following infrastructure:

i. Accessed via dual, 180-degree, outwards opening, self-closing sealed doors with a minimum opening of 1,800mm

ii. Sufficient size to accommodate the tug device and trailer responsive to the sites anticipated waste volumes

iii. Room enclosed, walled, and not permit through access to other on-site infrastructure

iv. Electrical charge capabilities to permit scheduled charging

v. Adequate lock infrastructure to restrict resident access

j) All integrated on-site waste and recycling storage/collection infrastructure outlined in section 7.7.1.1, subsections c) - h) above to incorporate the following infrastructure, including but not limited to:

i. Integrated wholly within the developments-built form to support an enhanced urban design outcome

ii. Rooms to be free of pylons, obstructions and other external services

iii. Permit safe, efficient, and unobstructed work practices for assigned users and waste collection staff

iv. Mechanical ventilation

v. Automated lighting

vi. Hot and cold-water taps

vii. Drainage connected to the sewer

viii. Minimum unobstructed internal height clearance of 2,700mm

ix. Water proofing throughout all intersections and extend 1,200mm high on walls

x. Accessed by a 1,800mm wide unobstructed access corridor

k) Separate, enclosed, and designated waste and recycling storage and collection rooms to be provided from non-residential tenancies proposed within the development in accordance with section 8.5.3 of the Fairfield City Wide Development Control Plan 2024
