## Chapter 7 – Residential Flat Buildings

Relevant controls, performance criteria and where the policy applies can be found in Chapter 4 of the Stormwater Management Policy – September 2017.

#### 7.2.6 Lifts

Passenger lift access is required for any residential flat building either 4 levels above ground with no basement parking or 3 levels above ground and including basement parking.
