## Chapter 7 – Residential Flat Buildings

i. Demonstrate Councils waste and recycling streams can be provided and are accessible to all residential dwellings

ii. Identify the waste streams and anticipated volumes expected to be generated throughout construction and in perpetuity when occupied by residents

iii. Demonstrate integrated on-site loading and supporting infrastructure is provided to permit safe and efficient schedule collections by Councils standard 10,800mm heavy rigid collection vehicle

iv. Outline how the waste generated on-site will be managed within the development

v. Identify the path of travel from individual units to:

1\) The waste chute system inlet on each level

2\) The communal organics waste storage room

3\) The bulky waste collection room

vi. Identify the path of travel from the multi-use loading area to:

1\) The waste collection room

2\) The bulky waste collection room

vii. Identify the path of travel from waste chute room/s in the basement to:

1\) The waste collection room

viii. Outline how and where Councils waste streams will be stored, transported and collected for each component of the development

ix. Provide detailed architectural plans to demonstrate compliance with sections i-viii outline above

x. Be prepared by a suitably qualified consultant experienced in the management of waste and recycling streams

#### 7.7.1.1 Waste and Recycling Storage and Collection Infrastructure

a) Councils waste and recycling streams to be allocated to the site in accordance with Councils waste generation rates:

i. Garbage = 120L per dwelling/week (collected weekly)

ii. Recycling = 80L per dwelling/week (collected fortnightly)

iii. Organics = 40L per dwelling/week (collected alternate fortnightly) *The bin allocation for each individual stream to be rounded up to the next whole number

b) Councils standard bin infrastructure to be accommodated within the development using the following minimum dimensions:

i. 240L bins = 600mm width, 740mm depth and 1,100mm height

ii. 660L bins = 1,260mm width, 800mm depth and 1,400mm height
