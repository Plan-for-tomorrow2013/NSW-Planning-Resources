## Chapter 7 – Residential Flat Buildings

i. A separate waste chute is to be provided for Councils assigned waste stream

ii. Inlets are located within a chute cupboard, accessed via 180-degree, outwards opening, self-closing sealed doors

iii. Cupboard to have a maximum depth of 200mm from the door to the chute inlet

iv. Cupboard is mechanically ventilated

v. Located within close proximity to the central elevator core

vi. The infrastructure to provide safe, efficient, and accessible access for all users

vii. The path of travel from individual dwellings to the chute inlet to not exceed a maximum distance of 20,000mm, accessed directly from the residential corridor, and not require residents to travel to other levels

viii. Acoustic attenuation to adjacent dwellings on each residential level and a report detailing the infrastructure to be compiled by a qualified acoustic consultant

ix. Doors to be unlocked to permit unobstructed resident access

d) A designated bin cupboard to be provided adjacent to the chute cupboard/s on each residential level. To accommodate Councils assigned waste and recycling streams the bin cupboard to incorporate the following infrastructure:

i. Minimum internal dimensions of 1,000mm width by 1,000mm depth per 240L bin proposed or dimensioned as prescribed by Council

ii. Accessed via 180-degree, outwards opening, self-closing, sealed doors

iii. Bin path of travel from the cupboard to the central elevator core to be tiled to permit scheduled cleaning

iv. Doors to be unlocked to permit unobstructed resident access

e) A communal organics waste storage room to be provided adjacent to the bin cupboard on ground floor within each building. To accommodate Councils organics waste stream the room to incorporate the following infrastructure:

i. Minimum unobstructed internal dimensions to be provided using one of the following calculations:

1\) Single Sided = (2,200mm) width by (Bin Allocation x 600mm) depth

2\) Double Sided = (3,200mm) width by (Bin Allocation x 300mm) depth

ii. Accommodate the buildings anticipated waste volumes

iii. Minimum 1,200mm wide doorway opening

iv. Bin path of travel from the room to central elevator core to be tiled to permit scheduled cleaning

f) A chute room to be provided within the basement to accommodate Councils assigned bin infrastructure below each of the respective chute outlet/s. The room to accommodate the following infrastructure:

i. A circular carousel system under each of the chute outlets large enough to accommodate 4x 660L bins or 2 days worths of anticipated waste volumes, whichever is the greater

ii. A chute offset and dampener to be provided prior to material discharging into Councils bin infrastructure

iii. Accessed via dual, 180-degree, outwards opening, self-closing sealed doors with a minimum opening of 1,800mm

iv. Room enclosed, walled, and not permit through access to other on-site infrastructure

v. Adequate lock infrastructure to restrict resident access
