#### 4A.3 Landscaping

#### Overview

All new developments in the rural zone are required to provide soft soil zones to complement existing vegetation and the neighbouring area.

#### Objectives

a) To encourage native plantings within the rural area.

## Chapter 4A – Development in the Rural Zones

b) To soften the built form of rural sheds and other large buildings.

#### Controls

A Landscaping Plan will need to be prepared by a Landscape Architect and provided in accordance with Appendix F.
