#### 4A.0  Introduction

This chapter applies to all development in those areas zoned:

a) RU1 Primary Production

b) RU2 Rural Landscape

c) RU4 Primary Production Small Lots

d) RU5 Village

under Fairfield Local Environmental Plan 2013.

The objectives of this chapter are to:

a) set out all the necessary information applicants must consider in their proposed development

b) ensure development proposals make a quality contribution to the vision for the City’s rural area

c) ensure development proposals maintain environmental and quality of life standards for present and future residents in the rural area.

For properties identified as heritage items within Fairfield City, refer to Chapter 3B Local and Aboriginal Development.

For properties near heritage items, contact Council on how your proposal must minimise potential impacts.
