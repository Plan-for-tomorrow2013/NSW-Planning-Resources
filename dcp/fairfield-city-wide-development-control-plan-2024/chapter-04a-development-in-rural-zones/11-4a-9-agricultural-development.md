#### 4A.9 Agricultural Development

#### Overview

Describes Council's position on the extent and nature of agricultural activity which is appropriate given the rural residential character of the area.

#### Objectives

a) To facilitate agriculture such as horticulture, flower cultivation and those activities, which could, in the opinion of Council, qualify as a hobby farm.

b) To ensure the use of chemical sprays and pesticides are carried out without health affects to the community.

c) To ensure that agricultural activities do not pollute neighbouring properties.

#### Controls

That any development proposal for agricultural development be assessed against the development objectives set out above.
