#### Controls

Child Care Centre proposed on or in close proximity to Bushfire prone land must be compliant with Clause 3.9 Bushfire of Chapter 3A Environmental Management Controls of this Development Control Plan.

#### 13.1.19 Flood Risk Management

Sites that are affected by flooding will need to refer to Chapter 11 Flood Risk Management in the City Wide DCP for more information. This chapter identifies different controls applicable to specific land uses based on the level of flood inundation and hazard. According to 13.1.3 Location Requirements, childcare centres will not be permitted in medium and high flood risk zones.

Note: A Section10.7(2) & (5) Planning Certificate will need to be purchased in order to obtain the relevant flooding affectation as well as any other environmental and planning constraints for the site.
