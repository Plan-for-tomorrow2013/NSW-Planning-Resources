## Chapter 13 – Child Care Centres

Note: Artificial ventilation control measures may be required in some areas where natural ventilation is not feasible.

#### 13.1.22 Waste and Recycling Service

a) Childcare Facility is defined in accordance with Fairfield Local Environmental Plan 2013

b) To support the provision of a safe and efficient waste collection service within Childcare Facilities, designated waste and recycling storage and collection areas to be provided in accordance with section 8.5.3 of the Fairfield City Wide Development Control Plan 2024.

c) The scheduled collection of waste and recycling streams to be conducted outside of hours of operation.

d) The kerbside presentation of waste and recycling streams is not an acceptable solution
