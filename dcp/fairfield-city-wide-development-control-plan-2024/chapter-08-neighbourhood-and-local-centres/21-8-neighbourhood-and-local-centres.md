2\) Can perform all required access and collection manoeuvres without impeding vehicle, pedestrian, cyclist, and user safety

3\) Have unobstructed minimum height clearances, maximum ramp grades, maximum rates of change, and unobstructed operational clearances throughout all required manoeuvres (ingress, egress, loading bay access) to permit safe and efficient scheduled collections

c) The kerbside presentation of waste and recycling streams is not an acceptable solution

d) Applicant to demonstrate the proposed service arrangements can meet the waste and recycling volumes anticipated for the development in accordance with the commercial generation rates outline within the EPA Better practice guide for resource recovery in residential developments

e) To support the provision of safe and efficient waste collection service, the integrated on-site multi-use loading area to:

i. Be fully enclosed, walled and accessed via a restricted opening to provide physical separation from vehicles, pedestrian, cyclists, and other users

ii. Mechanically ventilated

iii. Incorporate acoustic attenuation and supporting infrastructure detailed within a report prepared by an acoustic consultant to support use of a heavy rigid collection vehicle at high revs

iv. Provide a 2,000mm unobstructed clearance at the rear of the vehicle to support the collection of waste streams

v. The collection of materials to be conducted in accordance with the NSW Environment Protection Authority, Industrial Noise Policy 2000

f) A detailed waste management plan to be submitted to Council. The plan must:

i. Demonstrate waste and recycling streams can be provided and accessible to all users

ii. Identify the waste streams and anticipated volumes expected to be generated by the development throughout construction and in perpetuity during operation

iii. Demonstrate integrated on-site loading and supporting infrastructure is provided to permit safe and efficient schedule collections by a standard waste collection vehicle

iv. Outline how the waste generated on-site will be managed to support unobstructed access to each user and full suite of waste and recycling streams

v. Identify the path of travel from disposal point to the designated collection infrastructure to permit scheduled collection/s

vi. Outline how and where waste will be collected, transported, stored and managed for each component of the development

vii. Provide detailed architectural plans to demonstrate compliance with sections i-vi outline above

viii. Be prepared by a suitably qualified consultant experienced in the management of waste and recycling streams
