### 8.3 Amenity

Amenity is an important aspect of any development adjacent to residential dwellings. Ventilation, visual and acoustic privacy, solar access and access to private open space all contribute to the overall liveability of dwellings within and adjacent to the development.

#### 8.3.1 Laneways

If a laneway separates commercial and residential zones, a pedestrian footpath must be provided, as part of the new development to encourage safe pedestrian access.
