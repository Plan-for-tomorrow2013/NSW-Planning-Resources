### 8.1 Public Domain

#### 8.1.1 Active Street Frontages

Active street frontages are critical to the viability and vitality of local centres as direct, easy access from the footpath draws people from the street into the shop. Active street frontages also add to the safety and security of a street by enabling passive / casual surveillance.
