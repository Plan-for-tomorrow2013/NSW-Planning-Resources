#### 3A.2.3 Tree Work Permit – Fees

Inspection fees apply residents who apply for a Tree Work Permit. The fee has been introduced in an attempt to encourage residents to think seriously about the issue of tree preservation, and to perhaps reconsider hasty decisions to remove trees. Contact Council for the fee amount.
