#### 3A.1  Statutory Provisions

#### 3.1.1 Fairfield Local Environmental Plan 2013 – Environmental Protection Zones and Waterway Zone

Under Fairfield LEP 2013 the Standard LEP Environmental Protection Zones have been applied to a number of areas of the City due to the conservation and/or management of environmental values or constraints relevant to these areas.  A Recreation Waterway zone also applies to waterways comprising Chipping Norton Lakes and Georges River which aims to protect the ecological, scenic and recreation values of this area.
