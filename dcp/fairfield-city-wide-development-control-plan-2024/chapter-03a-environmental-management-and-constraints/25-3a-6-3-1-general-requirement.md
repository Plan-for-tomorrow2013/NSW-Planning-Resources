#### 3A.6.3.1 General Requirement

All development applications (except for change of use and occupation DA’s) must submit information within the statement of environmental effects (as detailed in Chapter 2) detailing whether there is evidence to suggest that the site of the proposed development may be contaminated. In order to prepare this statement reporting on the sites history including historical records of land use, land title searches, aerial photographs and the results of a site investigation will be necessary.
