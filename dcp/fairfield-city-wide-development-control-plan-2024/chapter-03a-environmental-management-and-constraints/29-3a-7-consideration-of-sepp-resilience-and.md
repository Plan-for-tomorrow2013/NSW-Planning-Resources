#### 3A.7 Consideration of SEPP (Resilience and Hazards) 2021

#### Objectives

To ensure applicants adequately consider SEPP (Resilience and Hazards) 2021 and NSW Hazardous Industry Planning Advisory Paper No.10 – Land Use Safety Planning, 2011.when preparing development applications.

#### Controls

Applicants are required to consider and address SEPP (Resilience and Hazards) 2021 and NSW Hazardous Industry Planning Advisory Paper No.10 – Land Use Safety Planning when proposing a residential use adjoining a Hazardous or Potentially Hazardous or Offensive or Potentially Offensive Development.
