#### 3A.2.2 Special LEP Provisions in relation to Heritage Items – Trees

Please refer to SEPP (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021 for specific requirements relating to permits that require the clearing of vegetation.

Notes: As a consequence of this subclause, the activities concerned will require development consent. The heritage provisions of clause 5.10 of Fairfield LEP 2013 will be applicable to any such consent. Heritage Items are listed under Schedule 5 of Fairfield LEP 2013.
