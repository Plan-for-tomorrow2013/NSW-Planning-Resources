#### 5A.7  Site Servicing

#### Objectives

a) Ensure that developments are serviced and provide for facilities.

b) Ensure front façades are free of utility services and structures.

c) Ensure dwellings are able to be identified by street number.

#### Controls

#### 5A.7.1 Water and Sewerage

a) Water and sewerage connections must meet the requirements of Sydney Water.

b) Rainwater tanks, collecting rainwater from all site roof areas (including separate garages/sheds etc) and used for toilet flushing, laundry, and garden irrigation are encouraged.

#### 5A.7.2 Electricity

a) Internal/on-site power poles must be located at the intersection of the front and side boundaries. They must be black or grey in colour.

b) Electrical services must satisfy the requirements of electricity distribution authority.

c) Meter boxes are to be placed in positions acceptable to the electricity distribution authority but may not face the street.

## Chapter 5A Single Dwelling House

#### 5A.7.3 Telephone and Internet

Telephone line installation must be in accordance with the requirements of Telstra.

#### 5A.7.4 Dwelling Street Number

a) Contact Council to request the allocation of house number if required .

b) Position and secure clearly visible street numbers near property entrances such as the front fence, wall or letterbox.

c) Street numbers should be positioned at a minimum height at 500 millimetres above ground level (existing).

#### 5A.7.5 Postal Services

a) The size of a letterbox must meet the Australia Post approved size. Australia Post recommends a separate box or tube to accommodate newspaper and non-mail articles.

b) A letterbox must clearly mark the street number of the dwelling that it serves.

c) A letterbox must be located on the boundary of the property with the footpath or road or at the junction of the driveway with the road. The opening of the mailbox must face the footpath or road.

#### 5A.7.6 Waste and Recycling Service

a) Councils waste and recycling service to be provided to all residential dwellings in accordance with section 496 of the Local Government Act 1993.

b) Dwelling Houses are defined in accordance with Fairfield Local Environmental Plan 2013.

c) Waste and Recycling storage and collection infrastructure configurations are outlined in - Appendix H Model Waste Infrastructure, section 1.

d) A designated waste and recycling hard stand storage area to be provided for each dwelling and large enough to accommodate Councils residential waste and recycling streams. The area to be:

i. Of sufficient size with minimum dimensions of 2,200mm width by 1,000mm depth

ii. Located within each dwelling’s private open space

iii. Behind the building line

iv. Located free from windows or doors

v. Support an enhanced amenity outcome

vi. Integrated wholly within the built form

e) To support the provision of a safe and efficient waste collection service, a designated hard stand kerbside waste collection area to be:

i. Provided for each dwelling

ii. Large enough to accommodate Councils residential waste and recycling streams with a minimum width of 3,300mm

iii. Located wholly within the lot’s frontage

iv. Clearly displayed on architectural plans

v. Free from driveways, on-street parking, footpaths, streets trees and service utilities

f) The path of travel from the waste and recycling storage area to the nominated kerbside waste collection area to:

i. Not pass through the residential dwelling, garage or through adjacent lots

ii. Be clearly displayed on architectural plans

iii. Provide a 900mm unobstructed width throughout all manoeuvres

iv. Not permit the movement of Councils bins over stairs, obstructions, uneven, and vegetated areas

g) Specifications of Councils standard 10,800mm heavy rigid waste collection vehicle are outlined in - Appendix H Model Waste Infrastructure, sections 1.7 – 1.9. All infrastructure is to be designed to permit unobstructed access for Councils largest collection vehicle required to service Councils waste streams.

#### 5A.7.7 TV Antennas

The antenna must be located at the rear of the site to reduce visibility from the street.
