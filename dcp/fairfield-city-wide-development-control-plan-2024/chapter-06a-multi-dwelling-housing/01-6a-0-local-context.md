#### 6A.0  Local Context

#### 6A.0.1 Zones and development to which this Chapter applies

This Chapter generally applies to multi dwelling housing development permitted within the following residential zones under Fairfield Local Environmental Plan (LEP) 2013:

a) R1 General Residential

b) R3 Medium Density Residential

c) R4 High Density Residential

#### 6A.0.2 Purpose of this Chapter

This chapter applies to all development applications for the purposes of multi dwelling housing development. Multi dwelling means three or more dwellings (whether attached or detached) on one lot of land where each dwelling has ground floor access to private open space.

The site controls, development controls, setbacks, building appearance, car parking and access, fencing, private open space, landscaping, overshadowing, privacy, drainage, utilities and services are regulated to establish a high standard of residential development.

The general objectives of the Chapter are to:

a) provide for the housing needs of the community within a medium density residential environment, meeting the needs of families and households that require smaller dwelling units and more affordable housing choices.
