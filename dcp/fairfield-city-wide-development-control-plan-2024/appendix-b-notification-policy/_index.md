### Notifications Policy

Please refer to Part Three – Community Participation Plan of Council’s Community Engagement Strategy 2020.

The Community Participation Plan has been developed to ensure the community has the opportunity to participate in planning decisions and outcomes. It sets out how and when the community can participate in the planning system, Council’s function and different types of proposals. The Community Participation Plan is delivered in conjunction and must be used with Council’s Community Engagement Strategy, which details the community participation objectives, principles and types of engagement

methods.
