### 11.2  How does the plan relate to other legislation and regulations?

This Plan should be read in conjunction with the relevant provisions of the following:

• NSW Government Flood Prone Lands Policy and Flood Risk Management Manual (FRMM 2023);

• the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act, 1979, and Regulations thereto;

• applicable Environmental Planning Instruments (in particular Fairfield Local Environmental Plan (LEP) 2013;

• State Environmental Planning Policy (Biodiversity & Conservation) 2021; and

• other relevant controls of this DCP and policies adopted by Council.
