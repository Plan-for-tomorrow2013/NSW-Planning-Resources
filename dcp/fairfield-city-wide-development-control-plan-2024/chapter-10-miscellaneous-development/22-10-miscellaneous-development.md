g) Loading and Unloading - All loading and unloading is to be undertaken on-site and shall be serviced by vehicles no larger than small ridged vehicles.

h) Waste and Recycling Service - To support the provision of a safe and efficient waste collection service, designated waste and recycling storage and collection areas to be provided in accordance with section 8.5.3 of the Fairfield City Wide Development Control Plan 2024.

#### 10.8.2 Industrial Zones

A neighbourhood shop can serve the daily needs of the local industrial workforce. This section applies to neighbourhood shops in the E4 General Industrial zone.

#### Objectives

a) To maximise convenience for local workforce while protecting the vitality of town centres.

b) To provide an active frontage and level of surveillance to the street

c) To ensure an identifiable entrance point to the neighbourhood shop
