#### 10.1.7 Fencing and Screening

#### Overview

Fencing and screening must be visually acceptable and in character with other development in the locality. The streetscape should not be negatively affected by the fencing of a development.

#### Objective

a) Reinforce the intrinsic character of a locality;

b) Ensure consistency in the building design by avoiding fencing design that interfere or obstruct resident’s vision onto adjoining premises and public spaces;

c) Ensure that the design and materials used are consistent and complement the existing streetscape;

d) Fences must have adequate footings, be self-supporting and able to withstand loads; and

e) Fences must not stop or redirect surface waters so as to cause a nuisance

#### Controls

a) Boundary fences to public roads are to be visually acceptable and in character with other development in the locality.

b) Where a development has frontage to two or more streets there may be the need to screen rear storage, servicing or parking areas from public view.

c) Timber or masonry materials are to be used in the construction of any boundary
