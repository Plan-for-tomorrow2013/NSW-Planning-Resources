#### 10.7.11 Group Homes

Group homes provide temporary or permanent accommodation for people with a disability or people that are socially disadvantaged. Refer to Fairfield LEP dictionary for the definition of a group home.

Group home applications lodged as a development application with Council will need to comply with the State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021 Chapter 3 Diverse Housing, Part 2 Group Homes to ensure group home developments are built for purpose.
