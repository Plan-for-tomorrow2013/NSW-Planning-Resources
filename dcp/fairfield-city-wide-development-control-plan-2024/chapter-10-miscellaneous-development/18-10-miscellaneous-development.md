#### 10.7.8 Visual and Acoustic Amenity Impacts

a) Boarding houses and co-living developments are to be designed to minimise and mitigate any impacts on the visual and acoustic privacy of neighbouring buildings by locating:

i. the centralised entry point at the front of the site, away from side boundary areas near adjoining properties;

ii. communal areas and bedroom windows away from the main living area or bedroom windows of any adjacent buildings;

iii. screen fencing, plantings, and acoustic barriers in appropriate locations.
