#### Open space Objectives

a) To ensure areas of open space are provided for communal use by residents, for child care occupants as well as visitors and the local community.

b) To provide open space that is functional, safe, usable, accessible and practical.

c) To provide areas for deep soil and landscaping which will add to the amenity of the site and the public domain.
