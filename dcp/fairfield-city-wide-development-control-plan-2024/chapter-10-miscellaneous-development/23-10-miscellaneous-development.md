### 10.9  Religious buildings used for the storage of cremated remains of deceased persons

The erection or use of a religious building within a residential zone for the storage of cremated remains of deceased persons is prohibited.

The reason for prohibition is that such buildings are not considered to be in character with the amenity of a residential area nor meet the objectives of the residential zones.
