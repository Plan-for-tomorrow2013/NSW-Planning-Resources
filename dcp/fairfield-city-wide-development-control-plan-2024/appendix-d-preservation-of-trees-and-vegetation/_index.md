### Preservation of Trees and Vegetation

Note: Specific requirements relating to the preservation of trees and vegetation and an explanation of the Tree Permit requirements are contained within Chapter 3 of this DCP.

Appendix D presently does not contain any content. In the future Appendix D will contain general horticultural advice and best practice examples relating to tree and vegetation care and management.
