#### 4B.8  Residential Development near Extractive Industries

There are 4 extractive industries located within Horsley Park and Cecil Park. If your property is located within 500 metres of an extractive industry boundary, certain development controls may apply to your land.

Refer to Chapter 4 of the City Wide DCP 2024 for details in respect to Controls for residential development near extractive Industries.
