## Chapter 9  - Industrial Development

a) Site boundaries that immediately adjoin residentially zoned land must include a continuous landscaped strip planted with vegetation of sufficient height and dimensions to soften the development as viewed from the adjoining residential properties.

b) The depth and width of the required landscaped area shall allow for growth and maintenance of tree species endemic to the locality. Such landscaping strips are to be suitably maintained so as to provide ongoing benefit to adjoining residences.
