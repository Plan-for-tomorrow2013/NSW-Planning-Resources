### 9.6 Development Guidelines for Specific Activities and Uses

There are some industrial activities, which require more specific development controls to ensure they are managed effectively and reinforce the objectives of this chapter. The developments identified are those activities, which are more likely to have a greater impact on the environment. Council has prepared these controls to minimise their impacts and ensure they are integrated more sympathetically into the streetscape.

#### 9.6.1 Storage Premises

#### Objectives

a) To ensure storage areas are kept in a clean and tidy state.
