### Schedule 2 – Solar Access & Energy Efficiency


## 1. Land Subdivision

This section applies to all applications for the subdivision of land with a site area of 1.0 hectare or greater, or the subdivision of land involving the development of 5 or more building allotments.

This section should also be taken into consideration when designing developments proposed for future subdivision of any kind.
