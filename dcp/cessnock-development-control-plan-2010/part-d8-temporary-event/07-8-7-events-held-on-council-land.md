### 8.7 EVENTS HELD ON COUNCIL LAND

Events held on Council land (eg. sports events, weddings, fun days etc.,) are often minor events and generally do not require development consent, but require Council’s written permission in the form of a letter.

Such events may attract a fee for the use of the land and shall comply with any usage conditions and ‘Plan of Management’ pertaining to the land.

To obtain permission for the usage of Council land, a letter outlining the specific location of the event, the proposed date and times, the applicant, insurance details and a full summary of proposed activities are to be submitted four months prior to the event.

For information on using Council land for an event or function contact Council on Ph: (02) 4993- 4100.
