#### 4.2.4 Encroaching Development to Provide Buffer

Where an application is received which is likely to result in a conflict with existing or likely future adjoining land uses, it will be the responsibility of the ’encroaching development’ to provide the recommended buffer areas or satisfactorily reduce or remove the conflict through some other approved method.

It will be generally required that where a physical separation is required it will be located on land in the ownership or under the control of the owner or operator of the encroaching development.
