#### 4.1.2 Purpose

To provide consistent development guidelines for residents, developers, the Council and assessment staff when considering applications for development which:

may conflict with existing developments or environmentally sensitive areas because of emission of an odour, noise, vibration, visual impact or other nuisance and may therefore require a separation or other means of reducing the conflict to an acceptable level; is proposed in a location where there is an existing development which adversely affect it and may therefore need to provide its own separation or other means of reducing or removing the conflict in order to minimise land use conflicts between potentially incompatible land uses.
