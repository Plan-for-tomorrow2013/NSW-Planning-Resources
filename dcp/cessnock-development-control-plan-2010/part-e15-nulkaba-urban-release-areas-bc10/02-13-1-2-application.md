#### 13.1.2 Application

This Plan is called Nulkaba Urban Release Areas, and forms part of the Cessnock City Wide Development Control Plan 2010. The Plan consists of the written statement and plans referred to in the document. Figure 1 details the land to which the Plan applies (shown edged heavy black). The land is proposed to be subdivided to permit the development of dwellings. This Plan (Chapter) was adopted by Council on 20 August 2014
