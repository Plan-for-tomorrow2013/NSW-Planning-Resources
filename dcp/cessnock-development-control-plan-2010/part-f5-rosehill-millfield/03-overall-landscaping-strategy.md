## 3 Overall Landscaping Strategy

A landscape plan shall be submitted with development applications for subdivision.

Landscaping should enhance the retained vegetation.  Any future landscaping should aim to utilise locally occurring species.

The subdivision design is to provide for lot frontages addressing streets, and drainage reserves.

Boundary fencing within the precinct must be of a rural character (post and wire or post and rail fence).
