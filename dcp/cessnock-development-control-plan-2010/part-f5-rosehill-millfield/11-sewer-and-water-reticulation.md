## 11 Sewer and Water Reticulation

All lots within the Rosehill Urban Release Area must be connected to reticulated sewer and water.

Up to 100 lots can be serviced by an Alternate Sewer System in accordance with the ‘Hunter Water Corporation - Water and Sewer Design Manual, Section 6: Alternative Sewer Systems’.
