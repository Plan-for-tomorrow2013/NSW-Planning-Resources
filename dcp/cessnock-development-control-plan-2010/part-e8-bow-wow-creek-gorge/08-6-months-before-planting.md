#### 6 months before planting

Apply a knockdown herbicide along rip lines or cultivate several times to reduce weeds. Fallow to build up moisture. Continue to allow grazing to reduce pasture. Fence the site, leaving space between outside rows and the fence of about 3.0 metres to restrict stock from grazing your growing plants from over or through the fence. Also leave space for machinery to get in and out of the site.
