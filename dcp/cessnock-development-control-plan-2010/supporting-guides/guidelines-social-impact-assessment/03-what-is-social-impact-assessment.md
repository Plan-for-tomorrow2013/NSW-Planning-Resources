## 3. WHAT IS SOCIAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT?

Social impacts are the consequences of actions on groups of people and on society. Social impacts can be positive or negative. Social impact assessment is the identification and appraisal of likely social impacts, whether intended or unintended, short or long term, of a proposed event such as a projects, a development, or a policy1.
