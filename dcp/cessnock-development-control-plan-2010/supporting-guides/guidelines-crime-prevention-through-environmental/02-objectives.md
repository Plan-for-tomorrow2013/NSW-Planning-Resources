## 2. OBJECTIVES

To identify and evaluate potential crimes risks and detail treatments for developments that will minimise or mitigate crime impacts. To promote design features within developments which will enhance community safety by reducing opportunities for crime. To provide well designed, maintained and managed buildings, facilities and public spaces that lead to a reduction in the incidence and fear of crime.
