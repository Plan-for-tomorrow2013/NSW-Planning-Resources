## 3 Overall Landscaping Strategy

A landscape plan must be submitted with development applications for subdivision. Any future landscaping should aim to utilise locally occurring species. The subdivision design is to provide for lot frontages addressing streets, and drainage reserves.

Residential boundary fencing must comprise consistent materials and colour. Boundary fencing in and adjoining the R5 - Large Lot Residential zone must have an open style rural character. This may include post and rail/wire fencing.
