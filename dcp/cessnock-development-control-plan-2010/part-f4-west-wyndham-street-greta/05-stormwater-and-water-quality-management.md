## 5 Stormwater and Water Quality Management Controls

The Indicative Layout Plan identifies details of stormwater management facilities. Adequate stormwater management must be provided at all times during the staged release of land. Stormwater management facilities are to include appropriate landscaping and are to be designed to Councils standards.

All development applications are required to demonstrate that there will be no detrimental impact on downstream waterways, wetland environments or agricultural productivity as a result of new development. Details are to be submitted with the development applications or at each stage for the subdivision.
