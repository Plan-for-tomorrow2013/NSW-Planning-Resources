## 12 Voluntary Planning Agreements

Any future development must have regard for the Voluntary Planning Agreements applying to the land: Local Infrastructure - Voluntary Planning Agreement Adopted by Council dated 6 August 2014; and On-site and Off-site Biodiversity Offsets - Voluntary Planning Agreement Adopted by Council 1 October 2014.
