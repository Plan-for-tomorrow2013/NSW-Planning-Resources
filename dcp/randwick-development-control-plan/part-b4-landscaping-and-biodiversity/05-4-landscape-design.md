## 4. Landscape design

Explanation The design of landscaped areas and deep soil planting constitutes an integral component of the overall site planning for any development. Accordingly, controls relating to the location, extent and design of landscaped areas and deep soil zones are provided within the relevant sections of this Development Control Plan (DCP) applicable for different development types, including:

• C1 Low density residential

• C2 Medium density residential

• Part D4 Local centres

• C10 Industrial uses

• Part D15 Specific sites
