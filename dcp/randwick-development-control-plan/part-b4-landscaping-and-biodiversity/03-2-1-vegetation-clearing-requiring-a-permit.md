### 2.1. Vegetation Clearing Requiring a Permit

A permit from Council is required where:

• the clearing is not for a purpose that requires development consent

• the trees to be cleared are covered by the Council’s Development Control Plan Preservation of Trees B5

• the clearing is below the NSW Biodiversity Offsets Scheme threshold.

Clearing below the NSW Biodiversity Offsets Scheme threshold The NSW Biodiversity Offsets Scheme threshold (BOS threshold) identifies when proposed vegetation clearing must be assessed under the under the Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016 (NSW),

rather than under Council’s local tree and vegetation management controls.

Where proposed vegetation clearing does not exceed the BOS threshold, the clearing may be assessed

under this DCP and Council’s relevant controls, subject to compliance with all other

applicable legislation , instead of just through council’s tree/vegetation controls . Where proposed clearing exceeds the BOS threshold, the Biodiversity Offsets Scheme applies and:

a) the application must be accompanied by a Biodiversity Development Assessment Report (BDAR) prepared in accordance with the Biodiversity Assessment Method (BAM); and

b) approval must be obtained in accordance with the Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016 (NSW).

Applicants should note that clearing above the BOS threshold is not assessed solely under Council controls and requires determination under the State biodiversity assessment framework.

Table 1 - clearing limits for lot size Biodiversity Offset Scheme threshold

| Minimum Lot size | Area of clearing |
|---|---|
| Less than 1 hectare | 0.25 hectare or more |
| Less than 40 hectares but not less than 1 hectare | 0.5 hectare or more |
| Less than 1,000 hectares but not less than 40 hectares | 1 hectare or more |
| 1,000 hectares or more | 2 hectares or more |

Threshold limits as specified under Section 7.2 of the Biodiversity Conservation Regulation 2017

Controls

a) In accordance with the State Environmental Planning Policy (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021, development consent is required for clearing of vegetation that does not exceed the Biodiversity Offsets Scheme (BOS) threshold where identified by this DCP

b)  The following as vegetation is declared by Council to require development consent for clearing:

i. vegetation on land identified as ‘Biodiversity’ on the Terrestrial Biodiversity Map in the RLEP

ii. vegetation on land zoned C2 Environmental Conservations C2 under the RLEP

iii. vegetation on l and identified as ‘Biodiversity Habitat Corridor’ in the SSROC Connected Corridors for Biodiversity map

c) This control does not apply to the removal of undesirable or environmental weed species as specified in the Appendix B4-1 in this DCP

d) This control does not apply to:

i. Land zoned a C1 National Park under the Randwick LEP

ii. land under the ownership or care, control and management of Randwick City Council where vegetation management works are undertaken by or on behalf of Council

e) A permit cannot apply to the following unless Council is satisfied that the clearing is minor in nature, is required for maintenance purposes and will not result in adverse impacts on:

i. a heritage item

ii. a heritage conservation area,

iii. forms part of an Aboriginal object or

iv. an Aboriginal place.

Note Vegetation clearing permit Once received by Council a permit must be determined within 28 days after the date on which the application was duly made. If not determined is taken to have been refused after the expiration of that 28-day period Development consent (via a Development Application) is required for clearing of vegetation (where development requires consent under Part 4 of the EP&A Act).
