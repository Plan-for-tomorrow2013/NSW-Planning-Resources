### 5.25. Paving in the public domain interface

Controls Paving and driveways in the areas visible from streets, parks and the public domain area of the site should be consistent and within the following limited colour/material range:

a) Permitted materials include:

i. Reconstituted stone, such as Haddonstone or Urbanstone

ii. Eco Concepts concrete pavers or equal

iii. Granite or bluestone porphyry sets

iv. Sandstone

v. Dry laid (mortarless) brick paving

vi. Concrete.

b) Colours should suit the architectural palette

c) Discouraged materials include:

i. Stencilled concrete

ii. Coloured concrete

iii. Granite or ceramic tiles.
