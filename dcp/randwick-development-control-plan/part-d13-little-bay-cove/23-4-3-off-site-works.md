### 4.3. Off-site works

Note All development relating to offsite works shall refer to and comply with the technical details and specifications outlined in Appendix C of this DCP including:

- Intersections

- Paving and kerbing

- Bioretention swales

- Parking lanes

- Ramps

- Vehicle crossings

- Retaining walls

- Elevated pathways, lookouts and structures

- Street lighting

- Street furniture
