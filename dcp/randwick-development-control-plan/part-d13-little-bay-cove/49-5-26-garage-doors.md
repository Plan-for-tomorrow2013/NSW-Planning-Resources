### 5.26. Garage doors

Controls

a) Permitted materials include horizontal slim line and panel lift doors such as:

i. B&D Panel Masta Contemporary

ii. Slim Master

iii. Panelift SevilleTurino

iv. Horizontal timber panelled or battened doors.

b) Permitted colours include:

i. Birch

ii. Sand bar

iii. Merino

iv. Stone

v. Gull grey

vi. Jasmin brown

vii. Slate grey

viii. Cedar brown

ix. Sarawak red

x. For timber refer to Section 5.23.

c) Discouraged materials include:

i. Traditional imprint and patterned doors

ii. Roller doors
