### 4.4. Parking for adaptable housing

Explanation Where adaptable housing units are provided in accordance with part C3 Universal, adaptable and accessible housing of this DCP, the car parking rate will be equivalent to that required for residential flat buildings. Objectives The objectives for parking for adaptable housing are to:

1\. Ensure that the design of parking areas is safe and accessible with best practice standards for people with a disability

Provide enough designated car parking spaces for vehicles used by people with a disability. Controls

a) Parking must comply with the minimum requirements of AS 4299 Adaptable Housing regarding car parking or alternatively with the access and dimensional standards for car spaces for people with a disability as specified in the NCC and the Australian Standard AS 2890.6

b) Accessible parking spaces for people with a disability must be located close to building entrances, lifts or access points and connected by a continuous accessible path of travel

c) The location of accessible parking spaces must be clearly signposted at the entrance to the car park

d) Accessible parking spaces must be well lit and clearly line marked

e) Signage and directional information within parking areas must be legible, well-lit, and designed for ease of use and safety

f) Where lifts are used to access parking areas, they must include audio cues and tactile control panels to support universal access

g) Accessible car parking spaces allocated to adaptable units must form part of the strata lot within the strata scheme and must not to be designated as utility lots or common property
