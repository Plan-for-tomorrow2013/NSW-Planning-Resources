### 4.3. Accessible parking requirements

Explanation The Federal Government’s Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (DDA) sets the legal framework to ensure equal access for people with a disability.  The National Construction Code (NCC) and Disability (Access to Premises

—Buildings) Standards establish the minimum requirements for

accessible car parking. Objective

1\. All development is to provide accessible car parking spaces for people with a disability as set out in the NCC and the relevant (and most current) Australian Standard.

Control

a) The dimensions for car spaces, including headroom and access requirements for people with a disability, are set out in AS 2890.6 2022.
