### 3.3. Green Travel Plan

Explanation A Green Travel Plan (GTP) is a workplace plan developed to make it easier for employees to get to and from work and reduce reliance on private vehicles and parking spaces.  Such a plan typically includes support for walking, cycling, car-pooling and public transport use. It is an important part of managing the transport demand generated by a development. Objectives The objectives of a Green Travel Plan are to

1\. Ensure developments are supported by effective, site-specific transport planning.

2\. Provide appropriate end-of-trip facilities to encourage walking and cycling.

3\. Improve accessibility and awareness of sustainable transport options for occupants.

4\. Establish clear implementation, monitoring and review mechanisms to support long-term travel behaviour change.

Controls

a) A GTP is to be based on the findings of the TIA

b) The GTP must:

i. Be prepared by a suitably qualified transport or traffic consultant, in consultation with Transport for NSW where necessary

ii. Include clear objectives and mode share targets aimed at reducing private car use and increasing public and active transport. Targets must be measurable, achievable, and linked to defined implementation timeframes.

iii. Identify specific tools, actions and measures to achieve the stated objectives and mode share targets, including any carparking management strategies required to support sustainable transport outcomes.

iv. Outline implementation responsibilities, including roles and responsibilities of relevant staff and procedures for ongoing management of the Plan

v. A monitoring and review program to assess travel behaviour and the effectiveness of the Plan.

c) End of trip facilities must be provided proportionate to the scale and nature of the development

d) End of trip facilities must be provided in developments in accordance with Tables 3 and 5 at the rates specified. Refer also to the Transport for NSW, Walking and Cycling Program Guidelines (2018-19) and the NSW Bike Plan (May 2010) Active Transport Strategy (TfNSW) and Council’s Active Transport Plan - Walking and Cycling.

e) The GTP should include a Travel Access Guide (TAG) incorporating the following information:

i. A comprehensive map showing all modes of public and active transport, including buses (private and public), trains, walking and cycling routes, as well as times for these public transport options.

ii. Information advising that service routes, timetables for buses, trains, light rail and ferries and walking routes are available on the Transport for NSW website

iii. Number and location of End of Trip facilities and bicycle parking (eg bicycle racks, showers, lockers, change rooms)

iv. Location of any carpooling/share car spaces where available.

Note Refer to the TfNSW Guide to Transport Impact Assessment 2024.
