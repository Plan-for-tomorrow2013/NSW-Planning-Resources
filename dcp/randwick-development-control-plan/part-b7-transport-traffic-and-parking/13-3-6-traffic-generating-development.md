### 3.6. Traffic generating development

Explanation Under the State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021, Council is required to refer certain types of DAs to Transport for New South Wales (TfNSW). Objective

1\. Ensure traffic-generating development is appropriately assessed and coordinated with relevant authorities to minimise impacts on the transport network and surrounding community Controls

a) The specific development types subject to referral are outlined in the SEPP

b) Any temporary signage or line marking required on Council-managed roads must be clearly justified and may be subject to community consultation and referral to the Local Transport Forum.
