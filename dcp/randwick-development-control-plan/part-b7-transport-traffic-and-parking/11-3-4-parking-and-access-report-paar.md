### 3.4. Parking And Access Report (PAAR)

Explanation A PAAR may be required, should the development result in a parking shortfall. Objective

1\. Ensure adequate assessment of on-street parking availability to minimise parking impacts from development Control

a) The PAAR should incorporate a survey of available on-street parking within 100m walking distance from the site demonstrating sufficient availability to mitigate any impacts. Surveys should reflect typical conditions and must not be undertaken during school holidays
