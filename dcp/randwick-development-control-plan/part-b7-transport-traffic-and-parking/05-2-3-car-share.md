### 2.3. Car share

Explanation Car share schemes provide an economical alternative to car ownership for residents and businesses. Several commercial schemes operate in Sydney providing on-line booking of vehicles linked to dedicated parking spots. These schemes are more viable in locations where private car ownership is discouraged or where available on street parking is limited. Membership of a car share scheme provides access to a network of new cars parked locally - without the expense of owning a car. Car sharing also helps to reduce the number of cars on the road and alleviate problems associated with traffic congestion. Council has also resolved to establish preferential parking for car-share and hybrid vehicles. For large development, there may be an opportunity to provide dedicated on-site parking spaces for car share vehicles.  This would be particularly effective for development on sites such as the University of NSW and Randwick Hospitals Campus, key strategic sites and within or adjoining commercial centres. Car share schemes are effective on sites, or in areas of higher density with ready access to good public transport and services. To operate successfully car share vehicles need to be highly visible, easily and safely accessed at any time (whether on, or off the street) by residents and business operators on the site, as well as those in the surrounding precinct. Car share spaces can also be dedicated for the exclusive use of building occupiers, if desired. In this case, the cost of the car share can be met through strata levies and must be organised by the developer. Where car parking rates are being considered, Council will look more favourably on proposed reductions within close walking distance to areas of high public transport provision, including strategic bus corridors, and the light rail network and/or where a carshare scheme is provided on site.

Note Any development can consider car share, however it is larger developments where a car share space/s are most encouraged by Council.

Objectives The objectives for car share are to:

1\. Encourage sustainable transport options and reduce private vehicle dependency by incentivising the provision of car share spaces in areas of high public transport accessibility

2\. Increase car share usage as a means of reducing reliance on private vehicle ownership

3\. Promote social inclusion by providing access to vehicles for households without private cars.

Controls

a) Car share spaces must be provided in residential, mixed use, shop top housing, co-living and/or commercial development, where public transport accessibility is high and/or where a Transport Assessment Study is required

b) Co-living developments, may be eligible for a parking credit equivalent to five standard parking spaces for each carshare space provided

c) The maximum number of carshare spaces within a co-living development shall generally not exceed one carshare space for every 50 rooms

d) For other residential or mixed-use developments, carshare credits will be considered by Council on a case-by-case basis, subject to demonstrated transport benefits

e) Car share spaces must be in convenient, accessible and secure areas with good visibility

f) Where located in a basement, carshare spaces are to be positioned near entry/exit points to enable easy access for non-residents of the building

g) Carshare spaces provided by a commercial operator must offer 24-hour public access, unless otherwise approved by Council

h) Car share spaces must be clearly marked with appropriate signage and pavement markings, in accordance with Transport for NSW (TfNSW) standards, indicating exclusive use by car share vehicles

i) Carshare schemes must be operational upon completion or occupation of the development to ensure immediate availability and uptake

j) Carshare spaces located on private property must be retained as common property under the management of the Owners Corporation of the site

k) Carshare spaces must not be converted to general parking spaces without prior approval from Council

l) A legal agreement or evidence of partnership with a recognised car share provider must be submitted with the DA.
