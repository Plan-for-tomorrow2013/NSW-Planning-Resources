## 4. Vehicle, motorcycle and scooter requirements

### 4.1. Relationship to other standards and policy

DAs requiring car parking will need to consider the following documents:

1\. Australian Standards (AS)

• AS 1428 Design for access and mobility

• AS 2890 Parking Facilities series:

i. AS 2890.1 Off-street car parking

ii. AS 2890.2 Off-street commercial vehicle facilities

iii. AS 2890.5 On-street parking

iv. AS 2890.6 Off-street parking for people with a disability

2\. State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021

3\. TfNSW Guide to Transport Impact Assessment 2024 (GTIA)

4\. Austroads guides
