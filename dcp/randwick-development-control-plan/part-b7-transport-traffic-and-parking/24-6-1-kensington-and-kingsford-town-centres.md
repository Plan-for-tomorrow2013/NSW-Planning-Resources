### 6.1. Kensington and Kingsford Town Centres (K2K)

Controls

a) Vehicle parking within the K2K town centres is to be provided in accordance with the rates outlined in Table 2: K2K, RJTC and WLHAs carparking rates. For land uses in K2K, RJTC and WLHAs, not addressed in Table 2, rates in Table 1 apply

b) Vehicle access points off Anzac Parade and Gardeners Road must be avoided

c) Where practical, parking access and/or loading is to be provided from secondary streets (rather than directly off Anzac Parade or Gardeners Road), set back at least 6m from the intersection of the rear lane

d) Parking is to be accommodated underground where possible

e) Basement carpark access must comply with the requirements of this DCP part B8 Water management

f) Parking access and/or loading areas are to be designed as recessive components of the elevation to minimise the visual impact

g) Sub-basement car parking is to extend no more than 1.2m above existing ground level

h) Provide flexible hardstand areas for the purpose of bicycle maintenance and repairs

i) Where a variation to the DCP car parking rates is sought, the proponent shall respond directly to control Section 8 Exceptions to parking rates of this DCP

j) A Green Travel Plan (GTP) is required to accompany all DAs for new buildings and substantial alterations to existing buildings. The GTP is to set out:

i. Future travel mode share targets, specifically a reduction in car driver mode share

ii. Travel demand management strategies to encourage sustainable travel

iii. Initiatives to implement and monitor travel measures such as car share and bike share

iv. Alignment with 8 Exceptions to parking rates of this DCP.

k) Car share spaces are to be provided in accordance with 2.3 Car share of this DCP
