## 9. Service and delivery vehicles

### 9.1. Service and delivery vehicle requirements and rates

Explanation The number of service bays required for a development depends on the size and servicing requirements of the type of development. The following rates are based on the TfNSW Guideline. Major developments should quantify their service vehicle requirements through new surveys of similar developments. Development Applications must demonstrate that servicing, loading and waste management arrangements are designed in accordance with the principles and requirements of TfNSW including The Last Mile Toolkit and relevant TfNSW policies. This includes the provision of adequate on-site servicing capacity, accommodating the anticipated freight and waste task, ensuring safe and efficient vehicle access and manoeuvring and minimising adverse impacts on the surrounding road network, kerbside operations and public domain amenity.

Note Development applications must include plans that clearly show how vehicles will enter, exit, and move within the site safely and smoothly, while minimising impacts on nearby roads, kerbside use, and public spaces

Objectives

1\. Ensure developments provide adequate on-site servicing, loading and delivery facilities that meet the operational needs of the development.

2\. Minimise impacts of servicing and delivery activities on the surrounding road network, kerbside operations, pedestrian amenity and the public domain.

Controls

a) Development must comply with the minimum requirements for the parking of service and delivery vehicles as set in Table 6: Service and delivery vehicle rates.

b)  Service vehicle dimensions, layout and service/loading bays must comply with Australian Standard AS 2890.1 (off street parking) and AS 2890.2 (off street commercial vehicle facilities) as applicable.

c) Should on site waste collection by Council be required, loading bays must be designed to accommodate Council’s waste collection vehicles, with the bin presentation area immediately adjacent.  Technical specifications of Councils current waste vehicle fleet can be obtained from Council’s Engineers

d) Driveways must be minimised along primary streets and along active frontages with no additional vehicle crossings permitted unless it can be demonstrated that there are no feasible alternative access arrangements.

e) All accessways, driveways, and servicing areas must accommodate the nominated vehicle in accordance with:

i. AS 2890.1

– Parking facilities (Off-streetcar parking)

ii. AS 2890.2

– Off-street commercial vehicle facilities

iii. AS 2890.6

– Off-street parking for people with disabilities

f) Driveways and crossovers must prioritise pedestrian safety and amenity.

g) Development applications must include swept path analysis (based on the appropriate service and delivery vehicle) prepared by a suitably qualified traffic engineer demonstrating satisfactory ingress and egress.

Table 6: Service and delivery vehicle rates

| Development type | Minimum requirements |
|---|---|
| Commercial premises | 1 space per 4,000m² GFA up to 20,000m² GFA plus 1 space per 8,000m² thereafter (50% of spaces adequate for trucks) |
| Department stores | 1 space per 1,500m² GFA up to 6,000m² GFA plus 1 space per 3,000m² thereafter (all spaces adequate for trucks) |
| Supermarkets, shops and restaurants | 1 space per 400m² GFA up to 2,000m² GFA plus one space per 1,000m² thereafter (all spaces adequate for trucks) |
| Warehouse, industrial | 1 space per 800m² GFA up to 8,000m² GFA plus 1 space per 1,000m² thereafter (all spaces adequate for trucks) |
| Hotels and motels | 1 space per 50 bedrooms or bedroom suites up to 200 plus one per 100 thereafter plus one space per 1,000m² of public area set aside for bar tavern, lounge and restaurant, (50% of space adequate for trucks) |
| Residential flat buildings | 1 space per 50 units up to 200, plus 1 space per 100 units thereafter. PLUS 1 space per 1,000m² of public area set aside for bar, tavern, lounge and restaurant. |
| Other uses | 1 space per 2,000m² GFA (50% of spaces adequate for trucks) |

Source: Guide to Traffic Generating Developments (GTGD)
