### 1.1. Objectives

The overarching transport objectives are to:

1\. Develop a transport network that prioritises active and public transport use as the preferred transport mode, by enhancing accessibility, connectivity and user amenity

2\. Promote low-emission and shared transport options in both new and existing developments to reduce environmental impact and strengthen climate resilience

3\. Restrict off-street parking provision in areas well-served by high-frequency public and active transport infrastructure to discourage private car dependency and reduce congestion

4\. Encourage multimodal transport options for development, particularly along key transport corridors, within commercial centres and at strategic/key sites to improve accessibility, flexibility and user amenity

5\. Support the integration of car share schemes, bicycle and scooter parking, and connections to public transport to enhance mobility and reduce congestion

6\. Ensure car parking facilities, service and delivery areas, and access points are designed to complement and enhance the streetscape character and protect pedestrian amenity and safety

7\. Limit vehicle access points along major transport corridors and regional roads to preserve traffic flow and enhance road safety.
