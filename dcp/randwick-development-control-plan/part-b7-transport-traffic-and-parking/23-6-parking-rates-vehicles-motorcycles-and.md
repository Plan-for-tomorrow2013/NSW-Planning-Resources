## 6. Parking rates – vehicles, motorcycles and scooters for K2K, RJTC, WLHA

This section of the DCP provides the applicable car parking rates for vehicles, motorcycles and scooters for new development and significant alterations in the following locations:

• Kensington and Kingsford Town Centres (K2K),

• Randwick Junction Town Centre (RJTC) and

• five Well Located Housing Areas (WLHA) (formally known as HIAs).
