### 4.6. Parking layout, configuration and dimensions

Explanation The specific requirements for parking layout and dimensions (for car spaces, aisles, disabled, grades, etc.) are provided in the relevant Australian Standard and the TfNSW guidelines. All development must comply with these standards as a minimum level of provision to ensure car parking facilities are efficient, adequate and safe.

In residential areas, smaller car spaces are sometimes sought for dwelling houses or semi-detached dwellings on narrow lots with access from the primary street frontage. These carports or hard stand spaces may only be suitable where they are able to accommodate medium sized cars as this avoids overhanging the footpath and creating a potential sight and physical hazard to pedestrians and other road users. Off street parking often also involves the loss of valuable on-streetcar parking spaces and disrupts the continuity and safety of footpaths. Objectives

1\. Provide parking spaces and access arrangements that accommodate a range of users and vehicle types

2\. Minimise impacts on pedestrian safety, footpath continuity and on-street parking Controls

a) The design and minimum dimensions of off-street car parking spaces must be in accordance with the requirements of AS 2890.1 Parking Facilities

b) A reduction in car space length to a maximum of 5m may be considered where site constraints

—such as limited lot width or reduced front setbacks —are characteristic of

small lots, semi-detached, or attached dwellings. In such scenarios the applicant must:

i. Demonstrate site specific design constraints

ii. Maintain safe vehicle access and egress

iii. Comply with relevant standards for small car spaces

iv. Provide a swept path analysis where applicable.

c) Motorcycle parking spaces must be a minimum 2.5m by 1.2m and clearly marked

d) Motorcycle spaces are to be designed and located so they are not vulnerable to being struck by manoeuvring vehicles

e) Motorcycle spaces must be located on flat and even surfaces as they rely on side-stands to park

f) In all development except dwelling houses, semi-detached dwellings or attached dwellings, all vehicles must enter and exit in a forward direction.

g) For development located on a main or arterial road, all vehicles must enter and exit in a forward direction

h) Unless otherwise stated, development is to comply with the relevant Australian Standard and the TfNSW guidelines for car parking layout, dimensions, aisle widths, grades, access requirements for different uses and users (e.g. those with disabilities), driveway widths, service and delivery needs.

i) Tandem parking spaces are generally discouraged unless where it can be demonstrated that:

i. the operation of the tandem parking arrangement will not adversely impact the adjacent road or footpath area

ii. the proposal is located within a low traffic area

iii. all vehicle movements associated with the tandem arrangement will occur wholly within the site and would not require vehicles to queue, wait, or undertake manoeuvres within the public road or footway.

iv. The parking layout will be designed to ensure safe, efficient and practical operation for all users, including consideration of peak period vehicle movements and the management of potential conflicts between vehicles parked in tandem

Refer also to the relevant parts of this DCP for car parking details relating to specific land uses such as residential, commercial, industrial and specific locations such as UNSW.
