## 3. Development Application (DA) requirements

### 3.1. Traffic and parking study requirements

Explanation To effectively manage transport demand generated by new development, applicants must provide appropriate assessments that demonstrate how transport impacts will be addressed. These assessments help Council ensure that development does not unreasonably increase congestion or parking demand, and that sustainable transport options are considered and encouraged. Objectives

1\. Ensure development proposals are supported by appropriate and proportionate transport and traffic studies.

2\. Minimise congestion and parking pressures generated by new development.

3\. Promote sustainable transport choices, including public transport, walking and cycling. Controls

a) Depending on the nature and scale of the development, one or more supporting studies may be required to be submitted with the development application:

i. Transport Impact Assessment (TIA)

ii. Green Travel Plan (GTP)

iii. Parking and Access Study

iv. Delivery and Servicing Plan

v. Construction Traffic Management Plan (CTMP)

Note Table 1: Vehicle parking rates, sets out where one or more of these studies is required, depending on the proposed type/s of development.

b) The TIA must be prepared by a qualified traffic engineer. The study/report must be signed by the author and include their professional qualifications and membership number with the Institute of Engineers, Australia. This is to ensure that new developments do not unreasonably increase congestion and parking demands in busy areas, and to encourage developers to adopt sustainable transport options and new residents to use public transport, car share, walking and cycling.
