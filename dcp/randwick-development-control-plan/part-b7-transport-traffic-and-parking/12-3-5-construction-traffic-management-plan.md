### 3.5. Construction Traffic Management Plan (CTMP)

Explanation A CTMP is a practical response to ensuring that demolition and construction works do not adversely impact on the public domain – on pedestrian, cyclist and vehicular movements, and on-street parking in an area. Objective

1\. Ensure construction and site activities are managed to minimise disruption to traffic, public transport operations, and pedestrian movement. Control

a) A CMTP is to detail how proposed development located on busy roads, bus or light rail stops or on difficult sites will be able to undertake loading and unloading, demolition and construction including the manoeuvring of trucks in and out of a site with minimum disruption to vehicular and pedestrian traffic or transportation networks.
