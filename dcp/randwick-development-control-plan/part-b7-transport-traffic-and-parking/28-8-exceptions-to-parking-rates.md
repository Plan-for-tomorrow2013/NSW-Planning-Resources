## 8. Exceptions to parking rates

Explanation The DCP sets appropriate parking rates for most development types and conditions, however it is also recognised that variations may be appropriate where developments incorporate suitable and sustainable transport alternatives. In some cases, it may not be physically feasible, or aesthetically appropriate, to provide on-site parking - for example where off street parking would impact the frontage of a heritage item or compromise areas of significant streetscape value. It is the responsibility of the applicant through the development assessment processes, to demonstrate that parking provision is adequate, or that the overall planning benefits of the proposed outweigh any shortfall in parking. Objectives:

1\. Ensure any variation to parking rates does not result in unacceptable impacts on local traffic, on-street parking, or safety.

2\. Support developments that incorporate sustainable and integrated transport options, reducing reliance on private vehicles.

3\. Require a robust, evidence-based justification demonstrating that any shortfall in parking is appropriate and will not adversely impact the surrounding area.

Controls

a) Any proposed variation to the prescribed parking rates must address the following considerations relevant to the development:

i. The type and scale of the development and its potential impact on local traffic and parking conditions

ii. Comparative analysis of parking provision in similar recent developments

iii. Existing on-street parking provision available prior to the proposed development

iv. Site specific building constraints that limit parking provision

v. Heritage and urban design considerations including significant streetscape elements such as sandstone retaining walls, significant mature trees and any other valued features

vi. Availability of on street and public parking in the vicinity, and proximity to public transport services

vii. Accessibility to local services, employment hubs, retail centres and recreational facilities

viii. Safety considerations for vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists

ix. Provision of integrated and sustainable transport options within the development such as bicycle parking, car share and EV Charging.
