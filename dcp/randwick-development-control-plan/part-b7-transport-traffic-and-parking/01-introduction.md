## 1. Introduction

Explanation Randwick City aspires to becoming a well-connected, climate-resilient community where transport infrastructure supports road safety, sustainable growth, equitable access, and vibrant urban life. Over the next decade, the Local Government Area (LGA) will experience continued growth and development in its commercial centres and residential areas, necessitating the progressive improvement of the public transport network to further connect and support mode share options, and to facilitate the realisation of the 30-minute city concept

– where residents live

within convenient walking distance of employment, public transport, essential services and recreational opportunities. The following provisions are designed to complement the objectives of the Randwick Local Environmental Plan (LEP), with a focus on fostering a well-connected, sustainable and inclusive urban environment. These provisions aim to:

• Encourage an efficient and appropriate mix of land uses, vibrant centres, and seamless integration between land use and transport

• Promote sustainable travel by prioritising safe walking, public transport and cycling use in line with Council’s Transport Hierarchy

• Ensure equitable access to transport infrastructure for people of all ages and abilities

• Enhance urban liveability through safe, healthy and inclusive public spaces connected by active transport networks

• Support diverse housing and employment opportunities through effective land use and transport connectivity

• Align development aligns with climate resilience goals, including the reduction of transport-related emissions

• As travel behaviour shifts towards low-emission modes, Randwick City will focus on delivering safe, legible, and integrated networks that connect people to places, strengthen local centres, and reflect the community’s aspirations for a liveable, green and future-ready city.

This Part of the DCP incorporates a range of initiatives that apply to all development in Randwick City. They outline objectives and controls that Development Applications (DA) must address through thoughtful design, and well managed access and parking arrangements. These controls encourage the adoption of alternative transport modes and supports a sustainable transport approach. It also specifies the types of supporting studies that may be required when lodging a development application. This section of the DCP should be read in conjunction with:
