### 4.2. Vehicle parking rates

Explanation Vehicle parking rates apply to all forms of development, including new development, alterations and additions to existing development and changes of use. The Provision of motorcycle and scooter parking is encouraged recognising these modes as efficient and lower-emission transport alternatives. Service delivery vehicle parking rates are provided in Table 6 of this Part of the DCP. Bicycle parking requirements are set out in Tables 3, 4 and 5. Objectives

1\. Ensure an appropriate and efficient provision of off-street vehicle parking that supports the operational needs and accessibility of development.

2\. Minimise reliance on private vehicle use by aligning parking provision with access to public transport, centres and active transport networks.

3\. Encourage a shift towards more sustainable transport modes, including walking, cycling and public transport, by avoiding the over‑provision of car parking.

4\. Support the creation of high-quality, people-focused urban environments by balancing parking provision with urban design, public domain and land use outcomes.

5\. Facilitate flexible and context-responsive parking outcomes that reflect the nature, scale and location of development, including proximity to public transport and local services.

Controls

a) Development must comply with the vehicle parking rates as detailed in Table 1: Vehicle parking rates. Any parking in excess of the prescribed parking rates will be included in the calculation of Gross Floor Area (GFA).

b) Parking must be provided for service and delivery vehicles, bicycles and persons with a disability in accordance with the relevant provisions of this DCP having regard to the nature and scale of the development.

c) Where a parking rate is not specified in Table 1, parking provision for the proposed development must be determined having regard to the Transport for NSW Transport

Impact Assessment Guidelines.  Alternatively, a Transport Assessment Study may be submitted for consideration to justify an appropriate parking provision.

d) For mixed use development the allocation and distribution of car spaces between different uses must be clearly identified on the DA plans.
