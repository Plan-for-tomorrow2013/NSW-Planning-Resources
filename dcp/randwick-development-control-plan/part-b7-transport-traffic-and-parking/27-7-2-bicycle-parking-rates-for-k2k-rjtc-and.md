### 7.2. Bicycle parking rates for K2K, RJTC and WLHA

This section of the DCP provides the applicable rates for bicycles, motorcycles and end of trip facilities for new development and significant alterations in Kensington and Kingsford Town

Centres (K2K), the Randwick Junction Town Centre (RJTC) and the five Well Located Housing Areas (WLHA) (formally known as HIAs). Control

a) Bicycle and motorcycle parking rates in the K2K town centres are to comply with Table 4: K2K bicycle and motorcycle parking rates

b) Bicycle parking rates and end-of-trip facilities are to be provided in RJTC and the WLHAs in accordance with Table 5: RJTC and WLHA - bicycle parking rates and end-of-trip facilities.

Table 4: K2K bicycle and motorcycle parking rates

\* Council is willing to consider variations from the applicable motorcycle parking rate for co-living/boarding house development, on merit, if sufficient justification based on parking demand, in comparable recent development, can be provided by the Applicant

| Bicycle and motorcycle rates |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Use | Bicycle | Motorcycle |
| Residential |  |  |
| Residents | 1 per unit | 1 per 12 carparking spaces |
| Co-living/ boarding houses | 1 per 5 bedrooms | 1 space per 10 rooms |
| Visitor | 0.1 per unit | 1 per 10 motorbike spaces |
| Commercial |  |  |
| Commercial premises | 1 per 1,000m2 | 1 per 12 carparking spaces |

Table 5: RJTC and WLHA - bicycle parking rates and end-of-trip facilities

| Land use | Residents / Employees | Visitors / Customers | End-of-trip facilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-dwelling housing / residential flat building | 1 bike space per unit / dwelling | 1 bike space per 10 units / dwelling | n/a |
| Commercial | 1 bike space per 2 car parking spaces | 1 bike space per 2 car parking spaces | Showers/change cubicles: 1 for up to 10 bike parking spaces, 2 for 11-20 bike parking spaces, 2 additional shower and change cubicles for each additional 20 bike parking spaces. |

Source: Local Transport Study, Randwick Junction WLHAs Final Report, January 2022
