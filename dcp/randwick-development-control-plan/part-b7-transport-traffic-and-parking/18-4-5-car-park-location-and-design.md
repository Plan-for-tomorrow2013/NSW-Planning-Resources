### 4.5. Car park location and design

Explanation The Australian Standards and TfNSW guidelines provide the base requirements for parking location and design. Car park location and design need to be carefully considered to ensure pedestrian safety, clear sight lines and to maintain streetscape character and amenity. In older established areas, with uniform streetscapes and heritage conservation areas, the provision of car parking needs to maintain the character of the area and the significance of the item or conservation area. Objectives

1\. Ensure car parking is located and designed to support safe, functional, and efficient vehicle, pedestrian, and cyclist movement.

2\. Minimise impacts on on-street parking, pedestrian amenity, and streetscape continuity

3\. Maintain and enhance streetscape character, particularly in established and heritage areas.

4\. Reduce the visual prominence of parking, vehicle access and loading areas within the public domain

Controls

a) Minimise loss of existing on-street parking supply by:

i. Careful location of crossings and laybacks

ii. Tapering the driveway at the property boundary

iii. Amalgamating driveway crossings with adjoining property where possible

iv. Considering the overall streetscape, continuity of footpaths and the need for safe pedestrian movement.

b) Ensure pedestrian and cycling safety is maintained or improved

c) Where feasible, vehicle access and/or loading are to be provided from secondary streets or rear lanes and set back a minimum of 6m from intersections to improve safety and reduce visual impact

d) Basement carpark access must comply with the requirements of B8 Water management

e) Vehicle access points and loading areas are to be designed as visually recessive elements within the building elevation to minimise their prominence

f) Parking is to be accommodated underground wherever possible to preserve streetscape character and reduce surface-level congestion

g) Sub-basement car parking structures must not exceed 1.2m above existing ground level Refer to other relevant parts of this DCP, for example sections that address residential or heritage considerations for detail on car parking design, provision and location particularly for older areas or areas with heritage value.

Note In some cases, it may not be possible to provide off-street car spaces.
