### 2.5. Resident parking schemes

Explanation Resident parking permits are intended to exempt eligible residents from certain kerbside time restrictions in areas where on-street parking demand is high due to shoppers, commuters and visitors. These circumstances typically occur in and around business or neighbourhood shopping centres, and major activity generators such as the Randwick Education and Health Specialised Centre, public swimming pools, sports fields and beaches. To support sustainable transport outcomes and reduce reliance on private vehicles, resident parking permits will not be issued to occupants of new residential developments that include on-site parking approved under this DCP. This restriction will be imposed as a condition of development consent, recorded on Section 10.7 Planning Certificates, and must be disclosed to all prospective purchasers and tenants. Objectives

1\. Improve the amenity for residents who do not have access to off-street parking and who live in areas with time-limited on-street parking, in accordance with the NSW Resident Parking Scheme.

2\. Encourage sustainable transport and reduce car dependency. Controls

a) No resident parking permits will be issued for new residential development, co-living, boarding houses or for significant alterations and additions to mixed use, shop top housing, or residential flat buildings, that result in a net increase in dwellings. This restriction must be communicated to all prospective owners and tenants.

b) A notice must be displayed in the foyer or common area of all affected buildings advising occupants that the building is not eligible for on-street resident parking permit exemptions.
