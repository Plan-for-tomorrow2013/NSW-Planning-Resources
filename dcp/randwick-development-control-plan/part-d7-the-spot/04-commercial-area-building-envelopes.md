## 4. Commercial area – building envelopes

Objectives The objectives for commercial building envelopes are to:

1\. Ensure that any new development respects the scale and form, siting and setbacks of surrounding buildings

2\. Ensure that any new development reinforces existing urban form and streetscape character

3\. Minimise the potential of buildings within the local centre to adversely affect the amenity of adjoining residential land.
