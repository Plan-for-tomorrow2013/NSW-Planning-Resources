### 3.9. Remediation

Objectives The objective for remediation is to:

1\. Ensure that any area of the site that is contaminated does not pose a risk to public health.

Controls

a) Any remediation needs are to be identified with specific development proposals.

b) Where necessary, the site is to be remediated to accepted standards. Remediation works, when required, are to be carried out in accordance with Randwick City Council's Contaminated Land Policy and State Environmental Planning Policy (Resilience and Hazards) 2021. A Site Audit Statement by an accredited EPA Site Auditor may be required

c) When relevant, appropriate environmental monitoring of any excavated materials is to occur during infrastructure works and construction.
