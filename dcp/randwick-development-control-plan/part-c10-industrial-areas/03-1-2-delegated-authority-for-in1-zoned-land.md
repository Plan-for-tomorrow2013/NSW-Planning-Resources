### 1.2. Delegated Authority for IN1 zoned Land

On 9 May 2025, the Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure notified the Council that it had approved the delegation of the Department’s planning authority functions relating to Development Control Plans (DCPs) to Council in accordance with Division 3.6 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979.

This delegation applies to land within Randwick City Council that is subject to Chapter 5 of the State Environmental Planning Policy Transport and Infrastructure 2021 (old Three Ports SEPP). This delegated land is zoned General Industrial IN1 under the SEPP (purple shaded area in Figure 1 below). The delegation does not apply to the Lease Area, Special Activities Zone SP1 (shaded in yellow). This General Industrial IN1 zone within the Randwick LGA comprises 68.94 hectares. By comparison the area of General Industrial E4 zoned area under the Randwick Local Environment Plan 2012 is 12.78 hectares. Council is the consent authority for land zoned General Industrial (IN1) zone under the Randwick LEP. While the IN1 zone facilitates a range of industrial and employment-generating uses, it is essential that new development is carefully designed and managed to minimise adverse impacts on surrounding land uses, particularly where industrial areas interface with residential zones and the public domain. The inclusion of Part C10 of the DCP allows Council to apply more detailed non-statutory controls to complement the provisions of the LEP and relevant State Environmental Planning Policies.  These controls provide a framework to ensure development applications appropriately address, assess and mitigate potential environmental impacts.  In particular development within industrial zones must consider impacts relating to amenity, acoustic, traffic generation, access, parking, loading and unloading, light spill, water and air quality and stormwater management.
