## 9. Waste management

Objectives The objectives for waste management are to:

1\. Minimise waste generation and to promote the principles of ecological sustainable development (ESD) including waste avoidance, reuse and recycling

2\. Facilitate efficient source separation and ensure waste management systems are appropriately designed to integrate with Council and private waste collection and management services

3\. Ensure waste storage, handling and collection arrangements do not adversely impact the amenity, health or safety of occupants, adjoining properties or the public domain.

Controls

a) Development involving demolition, construction or ongoing use must be accompanied by a Waste Management Plan prepared in accordance with Council requirements

b) Development must provide sufficient space for the purposes of on-site separation, storage and handling of recyclables. Waste storage areas must be designated to minimise impacts particularly on nearby residential areas

c) For multi-use and industrial units, allocated areas for waste storage and recycling must be provided for each individual industrial tenancy

d) The waste storage and recycling area must be easily accessible for occupants, designed to allow safe and efficient access for collection vehicles and be clearly signposted

e) Trade/commercial waste materials must not be disposed via Council’s domestic waste collection service and must be managed by an appropriate licensed waste contractor

f) Waste collection and servicing must be undertaken in a manner that does not adversely impact traffic flow, pedestrian safety, or the amenity of adjoining properties.

Notes Waste management and recycling processes of commercial and industrial facilities should be guided by the Waste Management and Recycling in Commercial and Industrial Facilities - Better Practice Guidelines (EPA, 2012). This guide provides advice on how to incorporate better waste management practice into the design, establishment, operation and ongoing management of waste services in commercial and industrial development https://www.epa.nsw.gov.au/publications/managewaste/120960-comm-ind
