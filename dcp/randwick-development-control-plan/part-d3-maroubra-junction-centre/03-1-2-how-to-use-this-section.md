### 1.2.  How to use this section

This subsection contains the purpose and introduction to the DCP. Section 2 of this DCP contains analysis of the study area, opportunities and constraints, vision statement, and a proposed urban strategy for the centre. It sets out the overall design principles underlying the controls. After considering the relationship between the development site and its context, refer to the building envelope and bock-by-block controls to determine the specific built form controls for your site.

#### 1.2.1. Development controls

This Section outlines the primary development controls that apply generally to all sites within the centre, and provides detailed building envelope controls for each block within the centre. Maroubra Junction centre is divided into 12 blocks, with specific controls for each. To establish the building envelope for a specific site:

• Identify the site’s block number using the map s in Section 3.2

• Review the primary development controls which apply to all sites within the precinct. These controls include amalgamation, subdivision, building height, building depth, building separation, articulation zone, street setbacks, side and rear setbacks and site access

• Identify the building envelope for the subject site, comprising: building height, building use, building zone/ depth, front setback, side setback, rear setback and deep soil zone location

• Use Section 4 of this DCP to guide the detailed design of the development proposal.

#### 1.2.2.  Design controls

This includes guidelines and controls for best practice urban and building design, including:

• Site configuration

• Site amenity

• Site access

• Building configuration

• Building amenity

• Building form

• Building performance

• Heritage and conservation areas Following a review of the detailed design guidelines, prepare a site analysis and develop the design proposal.
