### 1.3. Alignment with other planning instruments

This section should be read in conjunction with the provisions of the Environmental Planning & Assessment Act 1979 (EP&A Act 1979), Randwick Local Environmental Plan 2012 (RLEP 2012). In addition to the RLEP, several State Environmental Planning Policies (SEPP) apply to certain types of development within the Maroubra Junction Town Centre, depending on the nature of the proposal. The key SEPPs are:

• Housing SEPP 2021

• Transport and Infrastructure SEPP 2021

• BASIX SEPP 2004 (Building Sustainability Index) In the event of an inconsistency between this DCP and a relevant SEPP, the SEPP prevails to the extent of the inconsistency. This Part of the DCP should be read in conjunction with the overall Randwick DCP sections:

• Part A

– Introduction

• Part B - General Controls of the DCP

• Other sections of the DCP for specific development types, sites, or locations, as relevant to the Development Application (DA)

Note Apartment development must comply with the latest Apartment Design Guidelines (ADG).
