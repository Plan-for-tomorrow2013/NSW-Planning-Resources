## 1. Introduction

This DCP section applies to telecommunications and radiocommunications facilities and their supporting infrastructure and ancillary development under the following legislation and industry instruments:

• Telecommunications Act 1997

• Radiocommunications Act 1992

• Telecommunications Code of Practice 2021

• Telecommunications (Low-impact Facilities) Determination 2018 Under the Commonwealth regulatory framework, certain telecommunications infrastructure is classified as “low -

impact facilities” and is exempt from State and Territory planning and

environmental legislation.  Accordingly, Council does not have regulatory control over “development that falls within the scope of the Commonwealth Low-impact Facilities Determination (LIF Determination). A range of telecommunications infrastructure may be permissible without development consent under NSW planning legislation including the State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008 and State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021. This section of the DCP provides objectives, controls and guidelines for the siting, design and installation of telecommunications and radiocommunications facilities that require development consent. It is informed by the NSW Telecommunications Facilities Guideline, Including Broadband 2022 and the Industry Code for the Mobile Phone Base Station Deployment (C564:2020). While this section does not apply to low impact facilities, it may be used as a guide to encourage best practice siting and installation considerations and design outcomes for all telecommunications infrastructure.

Note The current version of the LIF determination can be accessed at: Federal Register of Legislation - Telecommunications (Low-impact Facilities) Determination 2018

Objectives

1\. Ensure the effective, efficient, and suitable provision of telecommunications and radiocommunications infrastructure so that it achieves social, environmental and economic sustainability, having regard to:

Social

2\. Apply a precautionary approach to the site selection, design, and operation of telecommunications and radio-communications infrastructure

3\. Minimise Electromagnetic Radiation (EMR) exposure to the public

4\. Locate infrastructure to avoid or minimise adverse impacts on sensitive land uses including aged care facilities , schools, childcare centres, children’s playgrounds , and residential land uses

5\. Ensure that the public and local communities have equitable access to telecommunications services

6\. Balance the needs of carriers, landowners, the community and other stakeholders in the provision of telecommunications infrastructure

7\. Ensure the infrastructure is clearly identifiable and all responsible carriers and operators can be readily determined

8\. Provide a transparent planning and assessment that enables the community to be appropriately informed and to participate in the planning/decision-making process.

Environmental

9\. Promote high quality urban design outcomes in the siting and design of telecommunications and radiocommunications infrastructure

10\. Ensure infrastructure is sited and designed to be visually compatible with surrounding streetscape character, built form and locality/visual context with particular regard to heritage buildings/areas and cultural icons

11\. Minimise visual clutter and the cumulative impact of multiple facilities, including through co-location, infrastructure sharing and integrated design solutions

12\. Minimise adverse impacts on the natural environment

13\. Ensure sites are rehabilitated and restored following the decommissioning or removal of infrastructure.

Economic

14\. Identify appropriate land use zones and locations suitable for telecommunications infrastructure in the Local Government Area (LGA) having regard to land use compatibility

15\. Facilitate the timely delivery and upgrade of telecommunications infrastructure to support emerging technologies

16\. Ensure proposed infrastructure is compatible with surrounding and permissible land uses

17\. Provide for efficient and reliable access to telecommunications services

18\. Provide certainty, consistency and transparency in the assessment and delivery of telecommunications infrastructure.

### 1.1. Definitions

Electromagnetic radiation (EMR) means the radiation in the microwave and radiofrequency band of the electromagnetic spectrum. Low-impact telecommunication facility (LIF) means a low-impact facility within the meaning of the Telecommunications (Low-impact Facilities) Determination 2018 of the Commonwealth made under the Telecommunications Act 1997 of the Commonwealth. Non-Low impact facility means a telecommunications facility that is NOT exempted from State and Council planning control under the Telecommunications (Low-impact Facilities) Determination 2018. Radiocommunications facility means a base station or radiocommunications link, satellite-based facility or radiocommunications transmitter.

Note The terms used in this DCP Part have the meanings described above. The definitions included here are for the purpose of clarification only and do not replace the definitions in relevant legislation and regulations.
