### 4.1. Built form and Design

Explanation Co-living developments should be designed to support long-term adaptability and building re-

use. This includes providing floorplate dimensions, structural systems and façade configuration that enable future conversion to self-contained residential apartments. Designing for adaptability ensures the development remains viable over time and responsive to changing housing needs, without requiring substantial structural works. Controls

a) Building design must incorporate adaptable floorplates, structural layouts and façade arrangements that enable future conversion to residential units compliant with the Apartment Design Guide (ADG)

b) Conversion to apartment use must be achievable through:

• internal reconfiguration, and

• minor external alterations only without the need for significant structural modification to the building

c) Development must demonstrate, at development application stage, how the building layout is capable of achieving ADG-compliant apartments in the future, including consideration of:

• structural grid and spans

• floor-to-floor heights

• façade rhythm and window placement

• provision for private open space and services.
