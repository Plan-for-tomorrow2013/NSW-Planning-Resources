### 2.13. Access and mobility

Explanation Heritage places should be accessible to all members of the community including people with disabilities, older persons, and families with young children. Developments should support inclusive access while respecting the heritage significance of the place. Access solutions should be tailored to the specific characteristics of each heritage building to minimise impact on significant fabric. Objectives

1\. Ensure that development facilitating access, adaptable dwellings or universal housing does not adversely affect the heritage fabric or significance of heritage items or Heritage Conservation Areas.

Controls All development

a) Modifications and alterations to improve access and mobility must be sympathetic to the heritage significance and heritage fabric

b) Alterations and additions to facilitate access and mobility must be reversible, so that future restoration of heritage elements is possible

c) Where compromises are necessary, priority must be given to preserving heritage items and elements of heritage significance.

Heritage items and contributory buildings No additional controls apply beyond those set above. Non-contributory buildings No additional controls apply beyond those set above.
