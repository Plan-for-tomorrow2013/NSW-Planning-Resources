### 2.14. Commercial properties

Explanation Randwick City contains a number of heritage-listed commercial buildings, as well as Heritage Conservation Areas that include traditional commercial buildings, such as corner stores. These building types reflect historic land use patterns and contribute to diversity and character of the built environment. Objectives

1\. Ensure that original form, detailing, and character of traditional/historic neighbourhood and retail/commercial buildings are retained and enhanced.

Controls All development No additional controls apply beyond those set out in this section.

Heritage items and contributory buildings

a) Original forms, architectural detailing, materials, and finishes must be retained or re-instated where feasible. This includes original shopfronts, original suspended awnings and open first floor balconies

b) Where the property forms part of a single larger building or unified composition, changes to ground level shopfronts and upper-level facades must not compromise the integrity and collective value of the group.

Non-contributory buildings No additional apply beyond those set out in this section.
