### 1.2. Heritage places in Randwick City

This DCP section applies to the environmental heritage within Randwick City as identified in the RLEP:

• Heritage items (local, state, and national)

• Heritage conservation areas

• Archaeological sites

• Aboriginal heritage (places and objects) The requirements, objectives and controls in this section apply in addition to the heritage conservation requirements, principles and practices contained in the Burra Charter, RLEP Clause 5.10, policy guidelines contained in the Heritage Inventory Assessment Report (State Heritage Inventory Report available online at the Department of Environment’s heritage website), development requirements of other relevant parts of this DCP, and relevant conservation management plans.

Heritage items, HCAs some archaeological sites and Aboriginal places and significant landscape elements and geological sites are listed in Schedule 5 of the RLEP.
