### 1.3. Heritage advice

Prior to lodging a Development Application (DA) or undertaking maintenance works to a heritage item or a property located within an HCA, or if works are likely to affect an Aboriginal object, Aboriginal place of cultural significance or historical archaeological site, applicants are advised to discuss their proposal with Council’s specialist Heritage Officer. Where major work is proposed, applicants are strongly advised to obtain professional assistance from a recognised expert in heritage conservation.
