### 2.2. Demolition and excavation

Demolition of a heritage item or contributory building in a heritage conservation area is generally not supported, unless there are overriding reasons such as structural damage. The demolition of a non-contributory building and replacement by an appropriately designed infill building can be supported where carefully demonstrated (refer to section 2.1 above). In assessing a DA for the demolition of a heritage item or a contributory building, Council will consider:

• The heritage significance of the item or building

• The structural condition

• Comparative analysis of all options; and

• The contribution the item or building makes to the streetscape.

Council may require the submission of a report by a structural engineer with heritage experience to determine whether the building is, or is not, structurally capable of reasonable and economic use. Where demolition of a heritage item or a contributory building within a heritage conservation area is approved it will generally be conditional upon the submission of a photographic archival recording using either film or digital capture to provide a stable and long-term record. A photographic plan sheet of the building should be used to show the location and direction of all photographs and the sequence in which they were taken. The Heritage Branch guidelines include requirements for cameras, film and digital image storage.  Applications for demolition of a heritage item or buildings in a heritage conservation area are required to provide details on the replacement development. Excavation beneath, adjacent to, or in front of early buildings has the potential to compromise their structural integrity is generally not supported. To assess the impact of excavation applicants must provide detailed information on:

• The nature and method of construction

• The structural condition of all buildings on the site

• The potential impacts on neighbouring heritage-listed buildings and HCA properties

• The ground conditions of the site and adjoining properties

#### General controls

a) Demolition of heritage items and contributory buildings is not permitted

b) Excavation will not be permitted where: o it occurs under a heritage item or common walls and footings, freestanding boundary walls, or under any other part of a heritage item o it occurs beneath or forward of the front façade of a heritage item

c) Where excavation is proposed beneath and/or within 1.5m of a heritage item, adjacent heritage items or buildings within an HCA, a Geotechnical Report and Structural Report must be prepared by a professional with heritage experience to demonstrate that the works will not have any adverse effect on the structural integrity of those items.
