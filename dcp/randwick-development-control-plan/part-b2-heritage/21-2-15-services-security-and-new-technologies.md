### 2.15. Services, security, and new technologies

Explanation Council supports the installation of technologies that improve water conservation and energy efficiency. For heritage items and buildings in Heritage Conservation Areas such technologies (including solar energy systems and telecommunications infrastructure) must be discreetly located and designed to avoid visual intrusion when viewed from the public domain or neighbouring properties. Their siting and appearance must respect the heritage significance of the building and its setting. Objectives

1\. Minimise the visual impact of new building services and technical equipment on heritage items and Heritage Conservation Areas.

Controls All development

a) Air exhausts, ventilation systems, skylights, air conditioning systems, solar energy panels, TV antennae, surveillance systems, and satellite dishes must not be located on principal elevations or be visible from the public domain. They must not attached be to chimneys. These elements are to be located to the rear, within the roof spaces, or integrated/flush with the roof cladding, and must match the pitch of the roof. All equipment must be modest in scale/size and not visually prominent from the public domain/street

b) Skylights, solar panels, and other roof mounted structures must be located away from visually prominent roof planes and front elevations

c) Where solar energy panels are proposed on the rear principal roof plane of a heritage item or contributory building in a HCA, they must be reversible/removable, aligned parallel to the pitch of roof, and preferably integrated with the roof. The panels must make minimal intrusive change to significant roof fabric

d) Alterations to accommodate electrical, telecommunications, plumbing, or other services must be limited to what is essential for the proposed use and must minimise impacts on heritage fabric

e) Rainwater tanks must be located at the rear or side of the dwelling and suitably screened so as not to be visible from the public domain/street.

Heritage items and contributory buildings No additional apply beyond those set out in this section. Non-contributory buildings No additional apply beyond those set out in this section.
