### 2.3. Transport Links and Access

Anzac Parade links the DCP area to the remainder of Randwick City via various vehicular access points.

The DCP area is currently served by State Transit Authority bus routes that run along Anzac Parade. Figure 1A shows an indicative bus route and bus stops through the DCP area.

It is proposed to make provision for a cycleway along Anzac Parade, connecting the DCP area to its surrounds, including the national park to the south. As shown on Figure 1A, the cycle way will run along Jennifer Street / Harvey Street within the DCP area and along the buffer strip between the DCP area and the golf course, providing a dedicated shared cycle/pedestrian link from Anzac Parade to the eastern end of Pine Avenue. Roads within the DCP area will have a low speed environment and will also be suitable for cycling.

The DCP area has a connective and convenient street layout for both vehicles and pedestrians. In addition, there are several pedestrian paths providing further links (Figure 1A).

Key principles include:

• To promote the use of alternative modes of transport to the car, including walking, cycling and public transport (bus)

• To promote safe and convenient movement throughout the DCP area
