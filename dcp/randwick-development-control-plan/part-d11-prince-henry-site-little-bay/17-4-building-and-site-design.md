## 4. Building and Site Design

This subsection contains objectives and controls for building and site design. These apply to all development on the Prince Henry site.

Building height, density (FSR) and landscaped open space requirements are contained in the Built Form Control Table (Figures 6 and 7). These requirements are explained in more detail in the following sections, as well as other general requirements.

Subsection 6 contains detailed objectives and controls that apply to specific precincts within the site, in addition to these general controls.
