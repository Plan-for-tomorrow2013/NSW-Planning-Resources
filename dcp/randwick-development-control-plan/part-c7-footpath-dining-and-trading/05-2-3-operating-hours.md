### 2.3. Operating hours

Controls The following standard hours of operation apply for all applications:

a) Outdoor dining areas in employment zones:

i. E1 Local Centre: between 7:00 am and 10:00 pm Mondays to Saturdays and 7:00am to 9:30 pm Sundays

ii. E2 Commercial Centre: between 7:00 am to 11:00 pm Mondays to Saturdays and 7:00 am to 10:00 pm Sundays

Note

Extended hours of operation may be considered for footway dining areas associated with a café, restaurant or takeaway food and drink premises.

b) Premises may be permitted a 30-minute period after the above-mentioned hours in which to vacate the outdoor dining area, provided this is within the approved hours of operation of the indoor premises

c) Variations to the above hours may be permissible only based on:

i. the merits of the proposal

ii. existing hours of operation of the indoor premises and/or

iii. proximity of residences.
