### 2.5. Goods displays

Goods displays on public footpaths must be carefully managed to ensure they do not obstruct pedestrian movement, compromise safety, or detract from the visual quality of the streetscape. The following controls apply: Controls

a) All goods display structures must be portable and removed outside of trading hours

b) Goods displays are only permitted on footpaths with a minimum clear width of 3.0m. Displays must abut the shopfront of the associated business and may only be permitted where similar footpath trading activities exist adjacent to other shopfronts

c) Goods displays must be located directly in front of the associated premises and must not extend beyond the shopfront

d) Goods displays must be used solely for the orderly presentation of goods sold and not include advertising and signage or food items

e) A maximum of one stand per commercial business is permitted including multiple occupancy premises

f) Maximum display width of 1.0m, measured perpendicular from the front of the premises and have a maximum length of 50% of the shopfront

g) Displays must not obstruct or restrict access to public utilities, building entries, pedestrian movement or accessibility.
