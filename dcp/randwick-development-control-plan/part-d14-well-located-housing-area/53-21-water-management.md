## 21. Water management

Explanation All development within the WLHAs will be required to promote the sustainable use of water including minimising potable water consumption, collecting and reusing rainwater, recycling water and improving the quality of stormwater. All development must address Part B8

– Water

Management of the Randwick DCP in relation to water conservation, groundwater and flooding, overland flow paths, on-site detention and Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD). WSUD is an approach that provides increased rates of water retention and detention and water efficiency. It also can assist in mitigating localised flooding and improve water quality and visual amenity.

Note For controls relating to water management, please refer to Part B8 - Water Management of the Randwick DCP.
