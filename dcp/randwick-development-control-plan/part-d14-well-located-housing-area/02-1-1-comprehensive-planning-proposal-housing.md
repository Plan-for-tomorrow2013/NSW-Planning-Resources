### 1.1. Comprehensive Planning Proposal – Housing Investigation Areas

The Randwick LEP 2012 (Amendment 9), known as the Comprehensive Planning Proposal (CPP) came into effect on 1 September 2023. The CPP included changes to the zoning, maximum Height of Building (HoB) and Floor Space Ratio (FSR) and sets the overall planning parameters for the WLHAs, within which these DCP controls provide more detailed design guidance.
