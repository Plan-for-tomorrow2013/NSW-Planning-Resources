### 4.6. Water efficiency

Objective

1\. Encourage water sensitive practices in building and landscape design.

Controls

a) Water smart and Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD) practices are to be implemented. As a minimum the following are required:

i. Areas that do not drain to the wetland are to be incorporated into the overall stormwater strategy

ii. Overflow from the rainwater storage must be directed wherever possible to an on-site infiltration system/trench. When site conditions do not permit on-site infiltration, the overflow must be connected to the public stormwater drainage system

iii. Stormwater is to be drained to a silt arrestor pit prior to discharging to either an infiltration area or the public stormwater drainage system

iv. Rainwater storage and supporting structure must be visually screened and integrated into the building design

v. Rainwater storage must be mosquito proofed

vi. Rainwater storage water supply taps and any other fixtures must be marked “Rainwater -

Not for human consumption”

b) In-sink food and waste disposal systems are not to be installed.
