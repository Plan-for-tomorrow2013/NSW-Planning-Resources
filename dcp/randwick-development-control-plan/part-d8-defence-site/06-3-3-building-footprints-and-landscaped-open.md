### 3.3. Building footprints and landscaped open space

Street frontage setbacks relate to the character of the streetscape and are discussed in Section

3.4 of this DCP.

Objectives

1\. Locate buildings so that the provision and use of outdoor areas is maximised

2\. Provide a degree of consistency in building alignments, heights and garden areas so that a neighbourhood character can be established, while allowing sufficient diversity and variety in housing types and design

3\. Ensure building setbacks from the public street are generally consistent with those of adjoining development and relate to streetscape components such as buildings, street trees, the width of the road reserve, park frontages and street character

4\. Provide a built form that optimises solar access and cross ventilation.

5\. Minimise the impact of development on adjoining land

6\. Provide adequate space for landscaping, visual and acoustic privacy, sunlight penetration and private open space

7\. Provide equity and certainty with building locations

8\. Provide side and rear setbacks that are related to the nature of dwellings proposed and ensure adequate separation between buildings

9\. Encourage rear landscaped open space that contributes to contiguous garden zones at the rear of lots

10\. Limit the length of side boundary walls consistent with neighbours.

Controls

a) The percentage of a site area that is to be landscaped open space must not be less than the percentage indicated in the table at Figure 3

for the site’s density zone

b) Front building setbacks and rear garden zones must be no less than the dimensions shown on Figures 3, 5 and 7 to 15. Building setback is measured from the property boundary to any part of a building including parts of a building within the articulation zone

c) At least 50% of the front building setback is to be soft landscaping

d) Building articulation is to be provided within the articulation zones indicated on Figures 3 and Figures 7 to 15 or within the primary building zone. It may include GFA where that GFA does not exceed 30% of the area of the articulation zone of any floor on any façade. Bay windows are not suitable in an articulation zone if the setback is less than 3m from a side or rear boundary. The ground floor area of an articulation zone can be occupied by building where indicated on Figures 3 and 7 to 15

e) The primary building zone is to have a depth of typically 12m as indicated in Figures 3 and 7 to 15

f) Walls are acceptable on side boundaries behind the primary building zone where they:

i. Contain no windows overlooking other properties

ii. Have an average height of no more than 3m, a maximum height of 3.5m, and a length no greater than 55% of the distance between the primary building zone and the rear boundary, unless they:

- Are a higher existing or simultaneously constructed wall; and

- Are in accordance with an approved building envelope plan submitted as part of a subdivision application.
