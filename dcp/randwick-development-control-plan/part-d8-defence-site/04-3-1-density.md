### 3.1. Density

Objectives

1\. Control the bulk and scale of development

2\. Ensure building bulk is compatible with the surrounding built form and minimises the impact of building bulk on existing buildings in the locality, open spaces and the streetscape

3\. Set appropriate density controls that reflect the desired future character of the area

4\. Encourage a mix of dwelling sizes and types.

Control

a) The maximum FSR for a residential building in any density zone must not exceed the FSR indicated for that density zone in Figure 2.
