### 5.2. Driveways and manoeuvring areas

Objectives

1\. Provide adequate space for efficiently moving vehicles within a site

2\. Minimise potential conflict between vehicles and pedestrians

3\. Integrate driveway and manoeuvring areas with landscape features

4\. Minimise the detrimental visual impacts of parking and driveway areas

5\. Design driveway gradients for vehicle and pedestrian safety

6\. Ensure site planning and building layout minimise the area designated for driveways and manoeuvring areas

7\. Limit the width of kerb crossings to maintain on street parking and minimise impacts on pedestrian amenity.

Controls

a) Vehicles are to be able to enter and leave the site of residential flat buildings and multi-dwelling housing development in a forward direction at all times

b) Surface materials and external finishes are to be consistent and compatible with those used throughout the development.

Note Shared driveways between attached dwellings are encouraged to minimise kerb cuts and avoid impacts on street trees.
