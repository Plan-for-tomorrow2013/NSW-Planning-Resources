## 2. Land subdivision – lot size and orientation

Objectives

1\. Provide a range and mix of lot sizes with areas and dimensions suitable for the permitted land uses and a variety of building types

2\. Enable lot sizes that facilitate housing diversity and choice

3\. Promote and facilitate ecologically sustainable development and microclimate management by providing lots that are appropriately oriented

4\. Ensure that all lots have a primary street frontage

5\. Arrange lots in a manner that facilitates personal and property safety and security

6\. Ensure lots have total areas and dimensions that allow dwellings, ancillary buildings, private outdoor open space; landscaped open space and vehicle access and parking to be located and constructed appropriately.

Controls

a) Lots with direct vehicle access to car parking areas from a public road are to have a minimum width of 9m (this control does not apply if parking access is not on the primary street frontage)

b) Lots with rear boundaries adjoining Holmes Street properties are to respect the subdivision pattern of lots on Holmes Street within the same block

c) Corner lots are to have a size and shape that can accommodate development that results in a positive response to the prominent position of the corner and its frontage to two streets

d) All lots are to provide frontages oriented to streets and public open spaces to provide a clear address and so that personal and property security, deterrence of crime and vandalism, and surveillance of footpaths and public open space is facilitated

e) Lots are to be oriented so that dwellings can take advantage of microclimatic benefits and can have dimensions that allow adequate on-site solar access and access to breezes

f) Lots are to be designed to maximise efficiency in house plans and useable external areas by having a regular shape

g) Subdivision must not include battle axe blocks.
