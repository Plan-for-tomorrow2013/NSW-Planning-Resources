### 4.7. Safety and security

Objectives

1\. Promote community safety through appropriate design

2\. Enable children, the elderly and frail persons to enjoy residential living that minimises threats from assault or burglary

3\. Discourage crime in residential areas.

Controls

a) Footpaths, landscaped open space, and driveways must provide opportunities for surveillance and allow safe movement of residents around the site

b) Dwellings which face the street must allow for casual surveillance of footpaths and driveways which is important for the safety of residents and passing pedestrians, and for the security of the neighbourhood

c) High walls around residential buildings and parking structures which obstruct views into the development are to be avoided

d) Dwelling and building entries are to be visible from the street

e) The demarcation between public, communal and private areas in a development is to be clearly recognisable

f) Medium density housing developments (i.e. residential flat buildings, multi-dwelling housing and attached dwellings) must have adequate lighting in common and access areas

g) Signage is to be clear and easy to understand

h) Shared entries should serve a limited number of dwellings and be able to be locked.

i) Dwelling houses are to be designed to allow residents to see who approaches their dwelling without the need to open the front door

j) Secure car parking is provided in medium density housing developments (i.e. residential flat buildings, multi-dwelling housing and attached dwellings)

k) Splay corners are to be provided to the street corners of lots along the proposed bus route in accordance with Council guidelines.
