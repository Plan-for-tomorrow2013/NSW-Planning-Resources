### 3.5. Activity strip

Objectives

1\. Enable certain non-residential uses permitted by RLEP, such as neighbourhood shops, medical centres or restaurants / cafes on land marked as an activity strip within Figure 2

2\. Encourage neighbourhood convenience type retail use with active frontages

3\. Ensure non-residential use of land does not have an adverse effect on residential amenity

4\. Provide for small scale businesses and services which primarily serve the local community.

Controls

a) The non-residential use is limited to the ground floor area of a building on a site marked with an activity strip on Figure 2

b) The build to line is to be observed consistently along the street frontage

c) All ground floor units along an activity strip are to have fronts addressing the street and predominantly glass shop fronts

d) Designs are to provide easy conversion between residential uses and non-residential uses

e) Awnings over a public footway are to be:

i. A minimum clear height of 3m above the footpath

ii. A depth of 2m where non-residential uses adjoin

iii. Not less than 600mm from the edge of the road/kerb

f) Adequate provision is to be made for natural lighting, ventilation, internal storage needs, waste storage, collection and servicing.
