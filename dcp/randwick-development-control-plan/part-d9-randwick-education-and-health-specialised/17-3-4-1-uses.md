### 3.4. Development and design controls

#### 3.4.1. Uses

Explanation The complex and evolving range of uses, organisations and functional relationships within the Campus requires a clear yet flexible guidance on the location of future development.

Objectives The objectives for the development and design controls uses are to:

1\. Ensure that uses are appropriately sited in relation to each other and within an appropriate functional area

2\. Provide clear and legible campus entry points, with active edges and public uses fronting primary street fronts

3\. Incorporate flexibility to accommodate changing needs, and maximise opportunities to improve functional relationships through development.

Controls

a) Locate and concentrate high public interface activities and high throughput uses (e.g.: outpatients, community and allied health services) in the Clinical Core -High Street Interface and Heritage Precinct

b) Encourage suitable adaptive reuse for the Heritage Precinct that will enhance the public interface with adjacent streets

c) Any significant redevelopment within the Opportunity Precinct should be based on a detailed study into current and possible future uses, including liaison with current occupants in order to:

i. identify opportunities to improve functional relationships across campus

ii. consider its potential to decant existing uses with possible future redevelopment of the Clinical Core or mental health facilities as part of a long term staged campus development strategy

iii. identify demands and options for on-site patient, staff and carer accommodation.
