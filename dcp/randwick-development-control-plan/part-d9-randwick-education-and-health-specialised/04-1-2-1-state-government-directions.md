### 1.2. Planning context

#### 1.2.1. State Government directions

The Centre is identified as a Collaboration Area in the Greater Sydney Region Metropolitan Plan (2018). The Collaboration Area contributes to internationally competitive economic sectors and precincts which support changing technologies, grown knowledge intensive jobs and assist in the creation of a 30-minute city. Key Directions for the Collaboration Areas in the Plan are to:

• Grow and invest in health and education precincts and the Innovation Corridor

• Create conditions for the continued co-location of health and education facilities and services to support the precinct and growth

• Have high levels of accessibility

• Attract associated businesses, industries and commercialisation of research.
