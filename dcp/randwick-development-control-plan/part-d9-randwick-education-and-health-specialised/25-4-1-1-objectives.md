#### 4.1.1. Objectives

The aims of this subsection are to provide planning and design objectives and provisions which will optimise:

1\. The physical, social, educational and environmental quality of the UNSW Kensington Campus

2\. The role and environmental ‘fit’ of the campus within its Randwick City context and its compatibility with the evolving character of adjoining lands

3\. The Campus Experience.
