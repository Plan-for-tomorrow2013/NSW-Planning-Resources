#### 1.2.2. Local Government directions

Community Strategic Plan The City’s 10 year strategic community plan supports economic growth and strengthening of the important health and education roles of the Specialised Centre.

Precinct Plan A Precinct Plan was prepared in 2011 to provide more detailed strategic directions and actions for the Centre, involving input and feedback from key stakeholders in the Centre, state government agencies, and the community. The key strategies from this Precinct Plan form planning principles for this section of the DCP, applying to all sites identified in the Specialised Centre. In addition, input from the major institutions has informed the site-specific components of this section.
