#### 3.4.4. Landscape and open space

Explanation Well designed and located landscaped areas and open spaces can contribute to a healing environment and provide pleasure and visual interest.

Objectives

1\. To employ landscape and open space to contribute to a healing environment

2\. To provide a variety of different landscaped spaces as an integral component of development across the campus

3\. To maximise opportunities to connect landscaped areas and open spaces to integrate with the wider campus circulation framework

4\. To protect and enhance existing landscaped spaces, elements and features.

Controls

a) Maintain and enhance the existing formal landscaped frontage to Avoca Street

b) Incorporate landscaped areas into all new development on the campus, to provide views of and/or access to gardens or open space from:

i. public areas, such as foyers and major circulation areas

ii. patient accommodation and waiting areas

c) Ensure landscaped areas provide easy and safe access for patients, staff and visitors

d) Maintain and enhance views to key landscaped areas and open space.
