#### 3.4.7. Movement and circulation

Explanation This section refers to internal circulation and movement of people, goods and vehicles within the campus boundaries. Refer also to the Transport, Traffic, Access and Parking section in Part B of the DCP for requirements for car and cycle parking provision, end of trip facilities and requirements for traffic studies and travel plans. Objectives

1\. To achieve a campus-wide circulation framework to provide for safe, legible and efficient movement of people, material and vehicles

2\. To ensure minor and incremental development does not limit the potential to improve the wider campus circulation network

3\. To provide a high level of amenity in the public pedestrian circulation system

4\. To encourage use of sustainable and active transport modes to and within the campus. Controls

a) Retain the principal public vehicular access to the campus from Barker Street/Easy Street

b) Avoid public vehicular access to the campus from Avoca Street and High Street

c) Employ technology/simplified procedures to substitute the need for people and materials movement where possible

d) Consider a modern industrial goods handling and distribution system for any major new development

e) Incremental campus development shall be sited and designed to facilitate the long term circulation network

f) Major redevelopment of any part of the campus shall incorporate pedestrian and cycle network improvements.
