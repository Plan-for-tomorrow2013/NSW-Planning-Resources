### 4.2. Building depth

#### Explanation

Building depth is the horizontal distance between the front and rear elevations, or between the side elevations of a building, as measured from window line to window line. It represents the sectional dimension of a building and is a key factor influencing residential amenity. In general, buildings with a narrow sectional depth have greater potential for dual aspect apartments that facilitate natural ventilation and daylight access to the interior space. This control aims to ensure that building depth is appropriately managed to achieve well-designed dwellings that provide a high level of amenity for occupants and contribute to environmentally sustainable development outcomes.

#### Objectives

The objectives for building depth are to:

1\. Facilitate the provision of dwellings with multiple aspects to maximise access to natural daylight and cross-ventilation

2\. Ensure building depth supports a high level of residential amenity, including adequate solar access, natural ventilation and internal environmental quality
