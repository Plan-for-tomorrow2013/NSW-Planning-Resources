## 4. Building envelope

A building envelope is a three-dimensional representation that defines the permitted extent of development on a site. It establishes the outer limits of a building in terms of height, floor space ratio (FSR), depth and setbacks from site boundaries, and assists in illustrating an appropriate built form outcome. The Randwick Local Environmental Plan (LEP) sets the statutory height and FSR controls for land zoned R1 General Residential and R3 Medium Density Residential across the LGA. The provisions in this DCP provide further guidance on the application of these controls to ensure development achieves appropriate scale, built form and amenity outcomes consistent with Council’s planning objectives.
