### 1.2. Low and Mid Rise Housing

State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021, Chapter 6 Low and Mid Rise Housing describes an area within the Randwick Local Government Area (LGA) known as the Low and Mid Rise Housing Area defined by the 400m (Inner Area) and 800m (Outer Area) walking catchment from two designated commercial centres (Randwick Junction and Maroubra Junction) and three Light Rail Stations (Kensington, UNSW Anzac Parade and Juniors Kingsford). The Low and Mid Rise (LMR) legislation permits alternative development density, building height and car parking rates, subject to certain minimum design criteria and exemptions. Details of the planning controls are available on the NSW Government legislation webpage: https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/

#### Designing within transition areas

The desired future character, scale and streetscape of these transition areas will emerge progressively over time, as sites are redeveloped. Where new development proposes  significant

change in scale from such as a new apartment building of 6 to 8 storey height, the interface must be carefully managed to provide appropriate building and upper level setbacks to reduce perceived bulk and scale, incorporate  deep soil zones with screen planting to soften built form and enhance privacy, and ensure  careful placement  of windows, balconies and openings to maintain reasonable levels of visual and acoustic privacy for adjoining properties along the side of the building to preserve visual and acoustic privacy and minimise overshadowing and visual dominance when viewed from neighbouring dwellings and the public domain.

#### Low and Mid Rise Area – Additional DA Requirements

Randwick DCP part A1 Welcome identifies the submission requirements for all Development Applications (DA) in the Randwick LGA. Additional justification is required for new development in the Low and Mid Rise (LMR) areas, and for any development proposing increased density under State planning controls, including the Infill Affordable Housing Bonus under the Housing SEPP. The following additional DA submission studies and documentation are required:

• A detailed urban design analysis, illustrating how the proposed height and built form can be accommodated without adverse visual, bulk, or overshadowing impacts on the surrounding lower scale residences habitable / primary living spaces and outdoor private open spaces

• Provide a statement demonstrating compliance with Randwick DCP part B1 Local character areas including built form, architectural character and materiality requirements, including analysis of predominant street character, and where relevant, alignment with future desired character as identified in part B1

• Shadow diagrams that clearly illustrate the extent of overshadowing at Solstice (mid-winter) hourly from 10am to 4pm. Diagrams are to illustrate and distinguish by colour, the permitted building envelope shadow, and any proposed exceedance shadows

• Site cross sections that include adjoining properties, residences and streetscape, in particular focussing on the building-to-building interface, setbacks, deep soil provision and screen landscaping

• Comprehensive 3D computer modelling of the proposed building massing of the surrounding existing and potential future built form outcomes, to understand appropriate built form and design response, in the existing and future built context

• A physical model of the proposal (as per standard DA requirements), including the existing surrounding lower scale residential properties

• Street eye level photomontages from the key vantage points that illustrate the proposal in the urban/suburban context

• View and setting analysis identifying any significant existing vistas or landmarks that are protected/enhanced

• Landscape analysis and proposal details, identifying any significant trees or other vegetation that is to be retained, new planting plans and planting list

• Information on any proposed planting on structure and the proposed soil depth and volume in accordance with the ADG requirement for the plant material specified

• Details of any proposed green walls, including the long term maintenance provisions and commitment to irrigation and plant replacement over time

• Clear calculations and plan diagrams illustrating compliance with: o Landscape Area percentage o Deep soil area percentage o Tree canopy cover percentage

• Building material and finishes palette schedules and typical illustrations.
