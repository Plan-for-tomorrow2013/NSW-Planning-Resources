### 8.9. Street addressing policy

Under the Local Government Act 1993 and the NSW Address Policy, Council is the designated authority responsible for allocating property addresses. All addressing must comply with the standards set out in the NSW Address Policy and User Manual. Council will allocate and, where necessary, amend street numbers in a manner that is consistent, logical, and fit for purpose, in accordance with statutory requirements, public interest, and relevant information provided by applicants. The objective is to maintain a uniform addressing system that supports emergency services, public safety, efficient service delivery, and ease of navigation. Further provision relating to street addressing for residential land uses are contained in Council’s Street addressing policy.
