### 8.2. General

#### Controls

a) Fences are constructed with durable materials that are suitable for their purpose and can properly withstand wear and tear and natural weathering

b) Sandstone fencing must not be rendered and painted

c) The following materials must not be used in fences:

i. Steel post and chain wire

ii. Barbed wire or other dangerous materials

d) Expansive surfaces of blank rendered masonry to street frontages must be avoided.
