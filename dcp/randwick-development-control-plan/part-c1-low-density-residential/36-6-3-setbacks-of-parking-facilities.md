### 6.3. Setbacks of parking facilities

Controls

a) Garages and carports must comply with the side setback requirements stipulated in subsection 3.3

b) Entry to garages and carports off the rear laneway must be setback a minimum of 1m from the laneway boundary

c) Garages and carports built to the side boundary may be considered where:

i. The adjoining property has its parking facilities or outbuildings constructed to the common boundary

ii. The location of car parking is compatible with the streetscape character; and

iii. Appropriate sightlines will be maintained for drivers and pedestrians; and

iv. Development seeks to amalgamate the driveway crossing with that of the adjoining property.
