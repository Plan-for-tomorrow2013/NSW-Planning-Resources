### 4.8. Voids

Objectives

1\. To ensure voids contribute towards increased natural light and ventilation within the permissible building setback and height controls

2\. To ensure the infilling of existing or approved voids does reduce internal amenity

3\. To ensure that voids do not contribute to excessive external building bulk and streetscape presentation

Controls

a) Any proposed void must not require additional building mass outside of the permissible building envelope

b) The infilling of an existing or approved void must not reduce the provision of natural light, ventilation and visual connection between building floors.
