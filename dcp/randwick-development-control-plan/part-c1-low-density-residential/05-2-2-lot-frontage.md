### 2.2. Lot frontage

Explanation The minimum lot frontage controls in this DCP supplement the RLEP provisions on lot size and aim to maintain the established and desired future character of low-density neighbourhoods occupied by dwelling houses, semi-detached dwellings, dual occupancies or a mixture of these housing types. The frontage control serves to ensure suitable subdivision configuration, which will in turn enable dwellings of adequate dimensions, configuration, and amenity. It also functions to ensure that suitable space for open space and visually acceptable and efficient parking and access arrangements can be achieved. Objectives

1\. To ensure land subdivision respects the predominant existing or planned future subdivision and development pattern of the locality

2\. To ensure land subdivision creates allotments that have adequate width and configuration, to deliver suitable building design and to maintain the amenity of the neighbouring properties

3\. To ensure dwellings have suitable scale and built form proportional to their allotment to complement the streetscape

4\. To ensure dual occupancy dwellings do not result in unreasonable impacts on the surrounding properties in terms of visual amenity, solar access and privacy.

Controls

a) The minimum frontage widths for subdivision of lots must meet the following:

| Minimum lot primary street frontage widths for subdivision |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low density housing type | Zone | Parent lot | Resulting lot following subdivision |
| Dwelling houses | R2 | 24m | 12m per dwelling |
| Dwelling houses | R3 | 18m | 9m per dwelling |
| Semi-detached dwellings | R2, R3 and where permissible | 15m | 7.5m per dwelling |

b) The minimum frontage widths for development of lots where dual occupancy development is proposed must meet the following:

| Minimum lot primary street frontage widths for dual occupancy development |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Low density housing type Dual occupancy (attached) Dual occupancy (detached), where allotment has frontages lane or access | Zone R2, R3 and where permissible the dual R3 and where with either rear permissible secondary street (corner lot) Dual occupancy (detached), where dwellings R3 and where front primary street in a permissible side-by-side arrangement | Parent lot 15m 9m 18m |

c) Any subdivision of land must not create battle-axe or hatchet shaped allotments for the purposes of dwelling houses, semi-detached dwellings or dual occupancies (attached).
