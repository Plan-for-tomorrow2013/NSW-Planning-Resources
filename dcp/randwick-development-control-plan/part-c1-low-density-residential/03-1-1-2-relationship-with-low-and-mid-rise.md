#### 1.1.2. Relationship with low and mid rise housing provisions

As released in February 2025, the low and mid rise housing reforms contained within the Housing SEPP override certain development standards of the Randwick Local Environmental Plan and Randwick Development Control Plan. To assist applicants in determining the applicable development standard, the following provides a summary, arranged based on the structure of this DCP chapter, of the applicable development standard based on dwelling type and location.
