### 7.1. General fencing

Controls

a) Construct fences with durable materials that are suitable for their purpose and can properly withstand wear and tear and natural weathering

b) Sandstone fencing must not be rendered or painted

c) The following materials must not be used in fence design:

i. Steel post and chain wire

ii. Barbed wire or other dangerous materials

d) Expansive surfaces of blank rendered masonry to street frontages must be avoided.
