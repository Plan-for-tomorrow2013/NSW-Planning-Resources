### 6.4. Driveway configuration

Controls

a) The maximum width of a driveway is as follows:

i. Single driveway – 3m

ii. Double driveway

– 5m

b) In addition, the width of a driveway should be tapered towards the property boundary and preferably form a single width at that boundary.
