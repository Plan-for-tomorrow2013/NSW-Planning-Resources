### 8.3. Communications dishes and aerial antennae

Controls

a) Provide a maximum of one communication dish and one antenna per dwelling

b) Communication dishes, TV antennae and ancillary facilities must be positioned to minimise visibility from the adjoining dwellings and the public domain, and must be:

i. Located behind the front façade alignment

ii. Setback a minimum of 900mm from the side and rear boundaries

iii. Located below the ridge of the roof

iv. Not located on the roof plane facing the primary and any secondary streets

v. Positioned to avoid intrusion into significant views or outlook currently enjoyed by the adjoining dwellings.

c) The topmost point of freestanding communication dishes must be no higher than 2.7m above existing ground level.
