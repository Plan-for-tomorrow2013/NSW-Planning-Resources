### 6.7. Hardstand car space configuration

Controls

a) Hardstand car spaces should include permeable materials, such as porous paving units. Gravel or landscaping over deep soil may be provided in between concrete wheel strips

b) A hardstand car space must have minimum dimensions of 2.4m (wide) x 5.4m (deep).
