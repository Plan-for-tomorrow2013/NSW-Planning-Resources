## 15. Size and configuration of retail spaces

Explanation The scale and proportion of the existing fine-grain (narrow lots) shopfronts on McKeon Street are distinctive and provide local character. The retention of these dimensions is necessary to preserve this character and to allow for the establishment of a variety of small businesses in the area thereby contributing to the ongoing economic viability of the commercial centre.

Objectives

1\. Retail/commercial uses are to reinforce and reflect the existing fine-grain strip retail frontages at street level along McKeon Street, surrounding the proposed public open space fronting McKeon Street and along Marine Parade.

Controls

a) Retail/commercial premises must be no greater than 5 metres in width in accordance with Figures 17 and 18

b) The supermarket must be located behind tenancies on McKeon Street with access to be provided from McKeon Street.
