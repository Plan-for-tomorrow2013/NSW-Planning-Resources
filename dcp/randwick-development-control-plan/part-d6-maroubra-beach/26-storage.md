## 26. Storage

Explanation The provision of adequate storage within easy access for each residential apartment is a necessity for all residents. Storage areas need to be suitable for the needs of residents including being able to accommodate larger items.

Objectives

1\. Provide storage in a convenient location for all apartments

2\. Provide adequate storage for everyday household items

3\. Provide storage for sporting leisure, fitness and hobby equipment.

Controls

a) Storage (in addition to kitchen cupboards and bedroom wardrobes) must be provided at the following rates:

i. One-bedroom and studio apartments 6sqm

ii. Two-bedroom apartments

8sqm

iii. Three plus bedroom apartments

10sqm

b) At least 50% of the required storage must be located within each apartment and is to be accessible from either the hall or the living area

c) Dedicated storage areas must be provided within basement car park areas.
