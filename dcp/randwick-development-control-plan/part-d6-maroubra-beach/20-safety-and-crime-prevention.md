## 20.  Safety and crime prevention

Explanation New development must be designed and constructed so that it is safe and secure for residents and visitors. The design of new development must also ensure that the safety of the public domain is maintained and enhanced. Objectives

1\. Ensure that all development is safe and secure for residents and visitors alike

2\. Ensure that the safety of the public domain is enhanced and maintained. Control

a) Best practice Crime Prevention through Environmental Design (CPTED) principles must be utilised in building design

b) Development must optimise the visibility, functionality and safety of building entrances

c) The design of new buildings must maximise opportunities for casual surveillance over public and communal spaces

d) Opportunities for concealment must be minimised

e) Boundaries between public and private open space must be clearly defined.
