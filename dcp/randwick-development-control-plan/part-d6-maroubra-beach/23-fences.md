## 23. Fences

Explanation Fences contribute positively to the public domain. They also protect privacy and enhance security. Objectives

1\. Enhance the public domain

2\. Provide security and privacy. Controls

a) Solid fences facing the street must be no higher than 1.2 metres. This may be increased to 1.8 metres where the fence has openings that make it at least 50% transparent

b) Fences must be designed to integrate with the development on the subject property and the neighbourhood.
