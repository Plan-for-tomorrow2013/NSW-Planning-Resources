## 13. Dwelling layout, sizes and mix

Explanation A mixture of apartment sizes provides housing choice and ensures that new residential development meets the needs of the community. Objectives

1\. Ensure apartment layouts provide high standards of residential amenity

2\. Ensure the spatial arrangement of apartments is functional and well organised

3\. Maximise the environmental performance of apartments

4\. A

ccommodate a variety of household sizes, activities and occupants’ needs .

Controls

a) Main living areas must be located adjacent to private open space

b) Opportunities for natural ventilation and daylight must be maximised by providing:

i. Corner apartments

ii. Cross-over or cross-through apartments

iii. Split-level or maisonette apartments

iv. Shallow, single-aspect apartments.

c) A variety of apartment types must be provided at the following ratio:

i. 50% studio or one bedroom

ii. 40% two bedroom

iii. 10% three bedroom.
