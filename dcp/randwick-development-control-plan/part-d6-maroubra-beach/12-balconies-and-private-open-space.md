## 12. Balconies and private open space

Explanation It is desirable for each residential unit to have access to private open space at ground level or a balcony if the residential unit is located at a higher level, in order to provide a useable area for outdoor recreation. It is necessary to ensure balconies and areas of private open space are functional and respond to the environment. Balconies should be integrated into the overall architectural form of a building. Objectives

1\. Provide each apartment with a functional area for outdoor recreation

2\. Provide reasonable levels of amenity for residents

3\. Ensure balconies are integrated into the design of a development. Controls

a) Each apartment must have a balcony or area of private open space with a minimum area of 8 square metres and a minimum dimension of 2 metres

b) Balconies and areas of private open space must be adjacent to the main living areas

c) Balconies and areas of private open space must be facing predominantly north, east or west to provide adequate solar access

d) Balconies must be integrated into the design and form of the building. e)
