## 24. Public domain interface

Explanation The design of the public domain is critical in ensuring that the design of the new public square, the through-site link and the Mckeon Street upgrade are of high standard and incorporate expected levels of public safety. It is also critical that these new or improved areas of public domain integrate well with the existing public domain. Objectives

1\. Improve the amenity of and enhance the overall appearance of the commercial centre

2\. Ensure that the public domain is designed so that it is accessible to everyone. Control

a) The public domain interface must be designed in accordance with the requirements of the public domain plan.
