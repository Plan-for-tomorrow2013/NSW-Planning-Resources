### 4.6. Safety and evacuation

Objective

1\. To ensure the structure and construction of development is compatible with flooding up to the applicable floor level

2\. To ensure that building occupants are capable of seeking safe refuge during a flood event.

Controls

a) Include a description of the safety and evacuation methodology with all DAs, including the:

i. Provision of reliable and safe egress for inhabitants from the lowest building level to a publicly accessible location above the PMF level.

ii. Provision that any egress must not rely on electricity (including lifts) nor rely on a person or mechanism to unlock doors and consider accessibility for persons with limited mobility

iii. Method of access for emergency personnel.

Note For some developments a condition of consent may be imposed to require the placement of a Safety and Evacuation Plan for all building occupants in a visible location.
