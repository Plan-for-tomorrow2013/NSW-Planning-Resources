### 4.2. Flood effects

Objectives

1\. To ensure that development, either individually or cumulatively, minimises adverse impacts on flooding, conveyance of floodwaters and floodplain storage volume

2\. To ensure that floodways and overland flow paths are not obstructed by development.

Controls

a) The development shall not increase flood effects elsewhere, having regard to loss of flood storage, changes in flood levels and velocities and the cumulative impact of multiple potential developments, for floods up to and including the 1% AEP flood

b) Floodways and overland flow paths must not be obstructed or diverted onto additional and adjoining properties

c) Areas identified as flood storage areas must not be filled unless compensatory excavation is provided to ensure that there will be no net loss of floodplain storage volume below the 1% AEP flood.
