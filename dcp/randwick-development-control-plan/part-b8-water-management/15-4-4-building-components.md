### 4.4. Building components

Objective

1\. To ensure that building components and materials used in development is compatible with flooding up to the applicable floor level.

Controls

a) All development shall utilise flood compatible building components below the floor levels identified in Table 1

b) All structures shall be constructed to withstand the forces of floodwater, debris and buoyancy up to and including the floor levels identified in Table 1.
