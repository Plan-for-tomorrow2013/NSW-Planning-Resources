## 2. Vision, principles and indicative concept

### 2.1. Vision

Newmarket Green will be a high quality, socially cohesive and sustainable development that celebrates the unique landscape and built heritage of the site, in a manner that supports the growth of Randwick’s Education and Health Specialised Centre, through t he delivery of open space, diverse housing and complementary uses.
