### 1.4. Relationship to other Plans

This DCP supplements the Randwick LEP 2012 by providing specific development guidance for the Newmarket Green site. Development within the site will need to have regard to this DCP as well as relevant provisions in the Randwick DCP 2013 as noted above. This Part should be read in conjunction with:

• Part A

– Introduction,

• Part B - General Controls, and

• Other Parts of the DCP for specific development types, locations or sites, if relevant to the application.
