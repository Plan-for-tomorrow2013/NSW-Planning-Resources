## 5. Environmental management and sustainability

### 5.1. Contamination

Objectives The objectives for contamination controls are to:

1\. Ensure adequate procedures and controls for the identification and assessment of contamination that may increase risk to human health on the Newmarket Site and to minimise potential impacts on the environment

2\. Ensure that if such contamination is identified, proper precautions for managing the risk to human health and the environment from the contamination are implemented prior to development so that the land to be developed is suitable for the proposed use.

Note Applicants are advised of the following documents were prepared by the proponent as part of the planning proposal for the site:

• Preliminary (Phase 1) site investigations by E3 Consulting Australia for Precincts A, B and C, dated December 2010 and January 2011.

• Peer review Site Audit Report and non-statutory Site Audit Statement (no. 0503-1010) prepared by JBS, dated May 2011.

Controls

a) DAs shall adequately address and investigate site contamination including groundwater contamination and demonstrate compliance with the following:

i. State Environmental Planning Policy (Resilience and Hazards) 2021

ii. Randwick Council’s Contaminated Land Policy 1999

iii. Contaminated Land Management Act 1997 (under NSW EPA)

b) Development applications shall be accompanied by preliminary site investigation(s), detailed site investigation(s) and details of remediation as may be necessary to demonstrate the land is, or can be made, suitable for the intended use. (Applicants are advised a Phase 1 preliminary site investigation has been carried out for the whole Newmarket Green site)

c) The land subject to the DA shall be remediated, validated and certified in accordance with the documents listed in control

d) Remediation of the land, the subject of the DA must be completed to the satisfaction of the consent authority prior to the carrying out of any development on the land.
