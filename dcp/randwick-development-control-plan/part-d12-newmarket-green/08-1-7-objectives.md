### 1.7. Objectives

The objectives of this Part are to ensure that future development:

1\. Will result in an environmentally sustainable precinct

2\. Exhibits design excellence and incorporates high quality materials and finishes

3\. Provides for high quality publicly accessible open space that responds to the heritage values of the site

4\. Responds to the heritage context of the site and the surrounding fine grain neighbourhood

5\. Provides for housing types that meet the needs of key workers and students, including affordable housing

6\. Respects the existing low scale residential character, adjoining schools and public open space

7\. Incorporates adequate internal carriageway widths to provide a safe pedestrian and cycle network.
