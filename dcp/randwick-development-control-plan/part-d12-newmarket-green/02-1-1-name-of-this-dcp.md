### 1.1. Name of this DCP

This Development Control Plan (DCP) has been prepared in accordance with the provisions of division 3.6 section 3.43 (previously 74C) of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (the Act). The DCP was adopted by the Secretary of the Department of Planning and Environment on 30 March 2015.
