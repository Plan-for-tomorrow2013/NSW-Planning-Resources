### 2.1. Floor Space Ratio

Explanation Floor Space Ratio (FSR) is a measure of development density that assists in controlling the mass and bulk of a development. FSR operates in conjunction with building height, wall height and setback controls to define the three-dimensional space within which a development may occur, that is, the building envelope. FSR is expressed as a ratio of the permissible Gross Floor Area (GFA) to the site area. The maximum permissible FSR for any development is prescribed and defined in the Randwick Local Environmental Plan 2012 (RLEP 2012) Floor Space Ratio Map.
