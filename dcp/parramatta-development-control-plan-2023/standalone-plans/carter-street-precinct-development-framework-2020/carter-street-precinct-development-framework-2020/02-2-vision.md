## 2. Vision

### 2.1. Vision

Urban renewal of the Precinct will support Sydney Olympic Park and its role as a Strategic

Centre, delivering a mix of housing, employment and retail services with easy access to public

transport, the regional road network and high-quality public open spaces, entertainment and

recreational facilities.
