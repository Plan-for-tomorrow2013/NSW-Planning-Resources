## 4. Structure Plan

The Structure Plan demonstrates how the vision, development principles and key elements for

the Precinct may be achieved. Where variations are proposed, development is to demonstrate

how the vision, development principles, key elements for the Precinct and relevant specific

objectives are to be achieved.

#### Figure 3: Carter Street Precinct Structure Plan

### 4.1. Objectives

To support Sydney Olympic Park as a Strategic Centre; To ensure that development occurs in a coordinated manner consistent with the prescribed vision and development principles for the Precinct; To ensure applicable key elements are delivered (refer to Table 1); To locate residential uses close to Sydney Olympic Park; To develop a village centre around Uhrig Road to support the future population, focused around ‘main street’ retail with laneways and a village plaza; To develop a secondary active frontage along Haslams Creek, providing local small-scale services, retail and opportunities for outdoor dining; To locate employment land uses at the south of the Precinct, providing good access to Parramatta Road and the M4 Motorway, whilst forming a buffer for the adjacent nearby residential uses; To accommodate any future proposed Parramatta Light Rail (Stage 2) stop into the Precinct; To contribute to the regional active transport infrastructure consisting of connected and dedicated footpaths, shared paths and access to public transport stops; To ensure key regional infrastructure upgrades, such as the Hill Road upgrade, can be implemented and provide for high quality public domain outcomes; and To enhance existing views and provide new view corridors and vistas along streets.

### 4.2. Controls

Development shall be delivered so that it is consistent with the relevant key elements in Table 1 and the Structure Plan at Figure 3. Development shall provide a connected public street network with the Precinct and to the surrounding area; A subdivision plan is required for all development that confirms the street network, individual development lots, proposed setbacks and floor space allocations; Subdivision applications should address the following matters:

i. Follow the identified street, pedestrian and cycleway network;

ii. The delivery of key elements as relevant (refer to Table 1);

iii. Identify individual development lots, and lots for public open space or other public purposes;

iv. Confirm how development will be distributed across the Precinct and is consistent with the floor space ratio controls identified in the Auburn Local Environmental Plan 2010 (Auburn LEP 2010), by allocating a maximum

allowable gross floor area to each development lot distributed into individual building envelopes where proposed;

v. Indicate setbacks and active street frontages; and

vi. Include a stormwater management strategy.
