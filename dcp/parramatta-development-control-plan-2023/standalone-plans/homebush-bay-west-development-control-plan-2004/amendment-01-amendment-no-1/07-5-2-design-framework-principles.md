### 5.2 Design Framework Principles

Additional floor area for residential development is required as the funding

mechanism under the Voluntary Planning Agreement for the provision of the

Homebush Bay Bridge. In order to accommodate this additional floor area, a different building typology

in the form of towers is required to be introduced to complement the hierarchy

of street defining buildings employed in the Plan. The introduction of tower forms should not compromise the overarching Design

Framework for Wentworth Point.  It should also ensure visual and shadowing

impacts are minimised and the overall aesthetic effect is enhanced wherever

possible.
