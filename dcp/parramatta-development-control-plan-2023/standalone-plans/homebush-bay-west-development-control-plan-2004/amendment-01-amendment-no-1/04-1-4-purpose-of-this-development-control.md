### 1.4 Purpose of this Development Control

#### Plan

The purpose of this DCP is to provide amended and

additional planning and design controls to govern

development on land to which this plan applies that is

additional to that permitted under Part 3 and in accordance

with the VPA. In particular, it provides a revised rationale for building

height and massing in section 2.2.4 in order to

accommodate additional floor space and tower forms

and as a result, sets out revised controls for:

• Land use and density controls;

• Building heights;

• Building separation; and

• Street setbacks.
