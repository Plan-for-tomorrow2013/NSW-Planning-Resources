### 1.9 PRE-DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

For applications to the consent authority, a list of all material recommended for submission at pre-DA stage is given below:

- orientation and overshadowing of the site and adjoining properties by neighbouring structures and trees

- geotechnical characteristics of the site and suitability of development
