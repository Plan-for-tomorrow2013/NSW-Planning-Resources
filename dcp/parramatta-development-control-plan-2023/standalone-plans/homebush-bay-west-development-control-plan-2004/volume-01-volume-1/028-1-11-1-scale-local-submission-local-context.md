#### 1.11.1  Scale - Local Submission - Local context sketch plan - 1:5000

showing:

- any proposed site amalgamation of subdivision.

- the site to be developed

#### Submission - Shadow diagrams

showing:

- significant local features, parks and open space, heritage items and buildings

- existing buildings on site

- traffic and road patterns, pedestrian routes, bus stops and train stations

- solar access to the site and adjacent properties at summer solstice (Dec 21), winter solstice (June 21) and the equinox (March and September 21) at 9.00am, 12.00 midday, 3.00pm and 6.00pm.

- shopping and employment areas and community facilities

- shadows across key elevations

- shadows cast by approved and/or existing development.

- significant natural features and water courses, conservation areas, sensitive

- natural areas and their setbacks

#### Submission - Landscape plan - 1:200 or 1:500

accurately showing:

- building footprint of the proposal

- proposed site entries

#### Submission - Streetscape elevations

- Drawings or photomontage for at least 50m in both directions, or the adjacent three properties in both directions, whichever is more appropriate.

- ramps, stairs and retaining wall levels

- lines of fencing, security and access points

- For sites with multiple street addresses, photographs should be prepared for each separate address.

- built elements (pergolas, walls, planters, water features)

- Properties opposite the site should also be documented.

- details of public, communal and  private open space

#### Submission - Aerial photograph - 1:1000 or 1:2000

- Aerial photographs of site and context, in colour.

- trees to remain and proposed trees/planting including species and size. Trees to be removed shown dotted

- deep soil zones and/or adequate soil depth for planting on structures
