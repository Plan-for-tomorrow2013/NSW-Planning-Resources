### 1.10 DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION PROCESS

The Applicant is encouraged to have pre-lodgement meetings with the consent authority prior to lodgement of the DA [Development Application].Once the required number of pre-lodgment meetings have been conducted, the Applicant can proceed with the lodgement of the DA (The application fee is to be confirmed with the consent authority prior to DA lodgement).

- any proposed site amalgamation or subdivision.

The following will be considered by the consent authority when determining  Development Applications:

- Section 68 of the Local Government Act 1993 and Clause 12 of the Local Government [Approvals] Regulation;
