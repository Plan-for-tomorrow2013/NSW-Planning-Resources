#### 3.2.4  MAJOR NORTH-SOUTH STREET – SOUTH OF BURROWAY ROAD

There is a unique opportunity to create a link through the precinct north to Parramatta River, connecting the land parcels on each side of Burroway Road to each and to the water both visually and physically. This new street will also connect the maritime precinct with residential neighbourhoods within Homebush Bay West. South of Burroway Road, there is less requirement for on-street parking.  This allows for a generous median which functions as a linear park. The location and extent of the major north-south street is not ‘fixed’ except through the above mentioned sites.  It is indicated to continue as far as Baywater Drive to reflect the potential future continuous alignment.  South of Baywater Drive there is potential to link to the approved street structure and continue further south into the precinct.
