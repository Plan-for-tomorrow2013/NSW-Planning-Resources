#### 2.2.5  EXISTING SITE FEATURES

> [Picture on page 17, not reproduced as text; see the original PDF.]

The site is dominated visually by constructed rather than ‘natural’ features: the Millenium Marker, the transmission line, the radio masts (particularly at Wentworth Point), and most of all the 1.2 kilometre straight built edge to the Bay. West of the ferry wharf the built edge is softened by mangroves as the precinct gives way to the Wanngal Wetlands of Sydney Olympic Parklands.  The subdivision pattern which is an important component of the cultural history of the site is reflected in the few existing streets.  There are few significant trees.   The intended continuous foreshore promenade and cycleway exists in part.
