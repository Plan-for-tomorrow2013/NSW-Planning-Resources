#### 1.9.1 Scale - Local Submission - Local context sketch plan - 1:5000

showing:

- the site to be developed

- pedestrian and vehicular access points (existing and proposed)

- significant local features such as water courses, heritage items, buildings and construction areas

- existing buildings, shopping and employment areas

- location of utility services, including electricity poles, stormwater drainage lines, natural drainage, kerb crossings and easements

-   significant noise sources on and in the vicinity of the site,

- traffic and road patterns, pedestrian routes and public transport nodes

particularly  significant noise, odour or pollution sources.

- parks, community facilities and open space

-   assessment of site contamination, proposed remediation

- existing development controls

strategy and a statement from a recognised expert that the site can be remediated and made suitable for the proposed uses

#### Submission - Streetscape elevations - 1:200 or 1:500

- photographs for at least 50m in both directions, or the three adjacent properties in both directions, whichever is the lesser

- street frontage features including poles, trees, kerb crossovers, bus stops and other services

- for sites with multiple street addresses, photographs should be prepared for each separate address

- characteristics of, and distance to any nearby public open space

- properties opposite the site should also be documented in the same manner

- information on any nearby bushland or environmentally sensitive land

#### Submission - Aerial photograph - 1:1000 or 1:2000

- Aerial photographs of site and context, in colour
