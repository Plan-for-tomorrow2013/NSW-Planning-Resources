### 1.13 THE CONSENT AUTHORITY

Subject to the nature and scale of development, consent may be either by Auburn Council or by the Minister for Infrastructure and Planning. Applicants are advised to refer to the relevant environmental planning instruments at the time of lodgment to confirm the appropriate authority.

#### Submission - Architectural Models - 1:100 or 1:200

Architectural models are required:

1\. In residential development where the proposed development has a value of work exceeding $600,000.00 or a minimum of 6 dwellings (single storey developments excluded).
