#### 1.6.1 Understanding the background/urban structure [Part- 2 of this DCP]

Part 2 of this DCP contains a summary analysis of the study area, including opportunities and constraints, guiding principles and proposed urban strategies.  It is intended to show how the primary controls in Part 3, and the street-based controls in Part 4, are underpinned.
