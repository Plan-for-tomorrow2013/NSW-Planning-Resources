#### 1.6.3 Using the detailed design guidelines [Part 4 of this DCP]

This part provides detailed information and guidance on best practice urban design criteria and how they can be applied to buildings. Use the information in this part to guide building design in relation to the following:

- Site configuration

- Site amenity

- Site access
