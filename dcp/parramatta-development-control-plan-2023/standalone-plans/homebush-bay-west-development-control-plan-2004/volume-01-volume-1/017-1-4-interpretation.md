### 1.4  INTERPRETATION

Terms in this DCP generally have the meaning ascribed to them in the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 and the Sydney Regional Environmental Plan No. 24 – Homebush Bay Area.  Where the meaning of terms differ, definitions are included in the Glossary.
