### 1.1 Name of this DCP

This DCP is called the Wentworth Point Precinct Development Control Plan. The DCP has been prepared pursuant to the provisions of Section 74C of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (the Act). The DCP was adopted by the Secretary of the Department of Planning and Environment (the Secretary) on 7 August 2014 and came into force on 7 August 2014.

The DCP was subsequently amended on 26 October 2021 by City of Parramatta Council.
