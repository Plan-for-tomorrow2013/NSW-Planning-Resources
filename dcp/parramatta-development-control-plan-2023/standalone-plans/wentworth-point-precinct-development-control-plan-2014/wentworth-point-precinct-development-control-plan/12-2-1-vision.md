### 2.0 Vision, Principles, and Indicative Structure

### 2.1 Vision

Wentworth Point is a vibrant urban community that forms a key part of the broader Sydney Olympic Park Specialised Precinct, makes a significant contribution to providing high quality housing for Sydney’s diverse and growing population in an environment that embraces its location adjoining Homebush Bay, the Parramatta River and Sydney Olympic Park, Parklands and represents contemporary, high density sustainable living.
