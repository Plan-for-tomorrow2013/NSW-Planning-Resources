### 3.0 Public Domain

A Public Domain Plan (PDP) is to be prepared for all precincts as a part of future detailed development applications. It

is to show street sections; parking; access to basement car parking; tree planting; street layout; intersections; street

furniture including lighting; paving. The PDP is to be prepared in conjunction with Council’s requirements and reflect the

Parramatta Public Domain Guidelines (PDG).
