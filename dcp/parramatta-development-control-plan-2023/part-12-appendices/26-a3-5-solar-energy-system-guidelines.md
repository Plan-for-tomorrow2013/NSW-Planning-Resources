#### A3.5 SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEM GUIDELINES

This guideline document provides information of the solar energy system requirements for new Development Applications lodged with the City of Parramatta Council. The requirements set out in this guide support the solar energy system provisions in Part 7 – Heritage and Archaeology of Parramatta Development Control Plan (DCP) 2023 and current best practice solar energy system recommendations.
