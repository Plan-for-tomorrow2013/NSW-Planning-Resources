#### A2.1 WASTE MANAGEMENT GUIDELINES FOR NEW DEVELOPMENT

#### APPLICATIONS

This guidelines document provides information of the waste management requirements for new Development Applications lodged with the City of Parramatta Council. The requirements set out in this guide are based on the objectives of Section 5.5.9 – Waste Management of Parramatta Development Control Plan (DCP) 2023 and current best practice waste management recommendations.
