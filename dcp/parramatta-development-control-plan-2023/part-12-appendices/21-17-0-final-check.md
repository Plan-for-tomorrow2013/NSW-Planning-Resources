### 17.0 FINAL CHECK

Please read and tick the box to ensure all required information has been provided

Have you checked the waste requirements for the proposed type of development in Council’s document ‘Waste Management Guidelines for New Development Applications’ and provided all of the required information?

Have you completed the relevant sections to your application of the above Waste Management Plan template or provided an alternative Waste Management Plan addressing the required information?

Have you shown use of site waste storage areas, garbage chutes, bin pulls and compaction equipment on plans accompanying this application?

Have you shown the location of temporary waste storage areas, soil stock piles and vehicle entry/exit points during construction and demolition on the plans accompanying this application?

Have you shown the waste collection vehicle access to the collection point on-site (if applicable) on the plans accompanying this application?

Have you shown the pathway taken to move the bins to and from the on street collection point and the location of the on street collection point on the plans accompanying this application?
