### 2.12 AMENITIES IN BUILDINGS AVAILABLE TO THE PUBLIC

High-quality and well-located public amenities improve pedestrian comfort and the liveability of the City of Parramatta’s (the City) shared spaces. The provision of adequate amenities which cater to the needs of the public in a manner that is equitable, safe, and accessible is an integral part of any community infrastructure network.

Objectives

O.01 Encourage a high standard of women’s facilities, and amenities for parents in both women’s and men’s toilets in buildings available to the public.

Controls

C.01 The number of women facilities and amenities for parents in women’s and men’s toilets are encouraged to be of a higher rate and standard than that prescribed in the Building Code of Australia.
