### 1.9 REQUIREMENTS FOR SUBMITTING A DEVELOPMENT

### APPLICATION

For advice on the information required to be submitted with a Development Application, please contact Council’s Development Services Unit. More information can be found using Council’s Development Application information webpage.

Council’s Community Engagement Strategy 2022-24 contains information on Council’s procedures for the notification of Development Applications (refer to Appendix 1).
