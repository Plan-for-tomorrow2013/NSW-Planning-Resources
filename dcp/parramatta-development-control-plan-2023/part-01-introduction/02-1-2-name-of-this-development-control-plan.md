### 1.2 NAME OF THIS DEVELOPMENT CONTROL PLAN

This plan is known as Parramatta Development Control Plan (DCP) 2023.

The DCP was adopted by Council on 28 August 2023 and came into effect on 18 September 2023.

For Development Applications lodged prior to 18 September 2023, refer to Section 1.4 – Savings and Transitional Arrangements of this DCP.
