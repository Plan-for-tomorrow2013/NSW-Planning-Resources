### 1.5 PURPOSE OF THIS DEVELOPMENT CONTROL PLAN

The purpose of this DCP is to supplement the Parramatta LEP 2023 and provide more detailed provisions to guide development.

Under Section 4.15 of the EP&A Act, Council is required to take into consideration the relevant provisions of this DCP when determining an application for development. However, compliance with the provisions of this DCP does not guarantee that development consent will be granted.

Section 4.15 of the EP&A Act contains other matters that must be considered in determining a development application.
