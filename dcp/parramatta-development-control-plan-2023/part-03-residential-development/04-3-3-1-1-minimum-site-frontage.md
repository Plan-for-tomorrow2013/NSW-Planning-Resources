#### 3.3.1.1 MINIMUM SITE FRONTAGE

Objectives

O.01 Ensure sites are of sufficient width to achieve:

a) the necessary standard of amenity in relation to privacy, solar access, and private open space,

b) a sense of street address, and

c) safe and efficient pedestrian and vehicular access.

Controls

C.01 For any new subdivisions, a development lot must have a minimum site frontage width of 15 metres.
