#### 8.5.12.30 AWNINGS

Objectives

O.01 Ensure awnings are designed to improve user comfort, providing shelter from the sun and reduced solar heat at the street level.

Controls

C.01 All awnings and shading devices should have non-reflective surfaces. C.02 Transparent awnings are not encouraged on buildings. If transparent awnings are used, the awning must have a maximum solar transmittance of 50.
