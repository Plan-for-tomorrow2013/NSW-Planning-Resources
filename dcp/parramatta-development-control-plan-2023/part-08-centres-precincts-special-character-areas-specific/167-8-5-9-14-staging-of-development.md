#### 8.5.9.14 STAGING OF DEVELOPMENT

Controls

C.01 Development approval for the 27-33 North Rocks Road, North Rocks Target Site must have appropriate regard to the amenity of the adjoining residential development known as Target Site 7. Accordingly, any Development Application for the residential development of Lot 2 DP 1158967 shall have regard to the access requirements for development of the site.

C.02 Staging should be organised to follow a logical sequence and minimise disruption to surrounding development.
