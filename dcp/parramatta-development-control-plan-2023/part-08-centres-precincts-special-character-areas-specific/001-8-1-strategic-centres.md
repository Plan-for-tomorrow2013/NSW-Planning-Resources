### 8.1 STRATEGIC CENTRES

This Section contains development controls for Strategic Centres within the City of Parramatta (the City) as identified in Council's Local Strategic Planning Statement 2036. Strategic Centres co-locate a wide mix of land uses (commercial and residential), provide access to major public transportation, have high levels of amenity, walkability, and are cycle friendly. They have distinct commercial functions, and are prioritised for housing and employment growth with a focus on high-rise development to support this growth.
