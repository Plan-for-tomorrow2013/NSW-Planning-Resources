#### 8.5.7.4 FLOOR SPACE RATIO

Objectives O.01 Ensure that the resulting population density is appropriate for the characteristics of the site, its immediate surrounds and LGA.

Controls

C.01 The area of the public pedestrian pathway is not included as part of the site area for the purposes of calculating FSR and the provision of FSR is 1:1 on residentially zoned land.
