#### 8.5.13.1 DESIRED FUTURE CHARACTER

The site will deliver a Housing Diversity Precinct that will incorporate a mix of housing typologies including detached dwellings, terrace housing, seniors housing, aged care and residential flat buildings. Future medium density development at the North Rocks site must consider the arrangement and composition of new built form on a whole-of-site basis to create a central composition of higher density development that appropriately transitions to lower scale development responding to existing peripheral low density residential settings to the east and west. New buildings should achieve a high level of architectural design quality and resolution, using high quality and robust materials.

The site comprises existing significant landscape assets that can be retained as features of the new place, benefiting the amenity and experience of the new residential community. The generous proportions of the Primary Street and consistent building setbacks offer the opportunity to augment the existing tree canopy and consolidate a landscape setting for new buildings. The street layout and through site links provide permeability and connectivity. Community and public open spaces

General Objectives

O.01 Create a street network which provides safe and accessible vehicular and pedestrian access and movement through the site, which is compatible with the surrounding streets.

O.02 Provide for well-designed public open spaces and streets to optimise liveability, amenity, useability and walkability for the local community.

O.03 Provide a mixture of compatible non-residential uses and community uses, activating public open spaces and streets, improving the character of the locality.

O.04 Deliver a diversity of housing typologies, housing mix and affordability that are compatible with the scale of the locality.

O.05 Include provision for a well-located, accessible and prominent community facility, oval and sports facilities.

O.06 Development is designed to respond carefully to the topography, streetscape, existing trees and natural environment as key features.
