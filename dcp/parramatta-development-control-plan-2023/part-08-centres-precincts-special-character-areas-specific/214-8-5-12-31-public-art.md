#### 8.5.12.31 PUBLIC ART

Objectives

O.01 Enhance the sense of place through the provision of public art. O.02 Use public art to enhance and define the character of the site and locality.

263-281 PENNANT HILLS ROAD, CARLINGFORD

Controls

C.01 Public art is encouraged within the central public open space or through-site links, independent of building forms.

C.02 Public Art is to have a value of up to $50,000 and is to be integrated into the public open space.
