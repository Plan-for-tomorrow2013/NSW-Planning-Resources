#### 8.5.4.4 COMMUNAL OPEN SPACE

Objectives

O.01 Ensure appropriate provision of communal open space.

Controls

C.01 Provide communal open space on the podium accessible off the lift core on the western edge. C.02 Accommodate an undercover communal facility within the tower footprint adjacent to the open to the sky communal open space.
