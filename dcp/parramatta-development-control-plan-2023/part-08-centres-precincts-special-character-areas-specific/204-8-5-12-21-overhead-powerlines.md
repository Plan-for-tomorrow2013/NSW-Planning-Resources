#### 8.5.12.21 OVERHEAD POWERLINES

Objectives

O.01 Ensure the appropriate location of all power lines to provide an aesthetic appeal and necessary function.

263-281 PENNANT HILLS ROAD, CARLINGFORD

Controls

C.01 All new power lines and powerlines within the Shirley Street frontage of the site are to be undergrounded.

C.02 Undergrounding should be constructed in accordance with the Parramatta Public Domain Guidelines 2017.
