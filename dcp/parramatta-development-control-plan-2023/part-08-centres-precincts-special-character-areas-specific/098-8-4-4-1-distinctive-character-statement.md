#### 8.4.4.1 DISTINCTIVE CHARACTER STATEMENT

This area was auctioned as the Parramatta Heights Estate on 2 May 1925, by real estate agents, Peach Brothers. Construction of housing commenced in the 1930’s. In May 1939 the area was covered by a residential district proclamation that required the external walls of houses to be of brick construction.

This is a high-quality residential area at the edge of the nineteenth century development of Parramatta. Its value as a residential area, and an important part of the history of Parramatta, will become more obvious as time goes by and development of this period becomes more widely appreciated.
