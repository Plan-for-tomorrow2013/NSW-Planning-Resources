#### 8.4.4.2 DISTINCTIVE CHARACTERISTICS

• Undulating terrain

• Streets and some houses with views and glimpses of parramatta to the south

• Includes a small park enclosed by houses, with laneway access from pennant hills road and sutherland road

CENTRES, PRECINCTS, SPECIAL CHARACTER AREAS & SPECIFIC SITES

SPECIAL CHARACTER AREAS

• Houses date from the late 1920s to the 1950s; mostly single storey brick, with marseilles- tiled roofs including some distinctive skillion-roofed houses; a few original two-storey houses

• Consistency in the siting, scale, and character of houses

• Face brick

• Roads have grass verges, without footpaths, but with continuous street tree planting forming an avenue

• Low brick fences

• Gardens with open lawns and feature planting including mature trees and views

• Several large eucalypts in front and rear gardens add interest to the street scene

Controls

Development consistent with the existing character of the area: C.01 Additions in brick matching the house, designed to minimise impacts on the original character of the house, and to protect the views and amenity of neighbouring properties.

C.02 Rear garden placement of garages and carports. C.03 High fences only in Pennant Hills Road, behind the original fences. C.04 Recladding of roofs in similar materials. C.05 Additions should be designed to retain the original façade and to minimise impacts on it. C.06 Impacts on the amenity and views of adjoining properties should be minimised. Development not consistent with the existing character of the area: C.07 Major changes to the façade that alter its architectural character. C.08 Garage or carport beside the house and which block driveway space to back garden. C.09 Recladding, painting or rendering of exterior walls of brick houses and brick fences. C.10 Demolition of low brick fences. C.11 Fences higher than 1 metre. C.12 Buildings other than garages or other utility buildings within 6 metres of the rear of properties adjoining the park.
