#### 8.5.9.12 CONTAMINATION

The subject site is to be fully remediated and a validation report submitted to Council’s satisfaction prior to the issue of a Construction Certificate for residential development on the site.

Submission requirement

• A validation report indicating that the site has been made free from contamination.
