#### 8.5.9.2 HUNTER PIPELINE EASEMENT & AGL GAS MAIN

The applicant is required to consult with Caltex Australia Petroleum Pty Ltd and AGL Energy with respect to the location of any proposed structure or building on or in the vicinity of the pipeline or gas main.

Evidence of consultation and the concurrence of Caltex Australia Petroleum Pty Ltd and AGL Energy is to be submitted with any Development Application.
