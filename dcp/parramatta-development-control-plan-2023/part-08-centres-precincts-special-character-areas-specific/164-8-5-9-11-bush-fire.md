#### 8.5.9.11 BUSH FIRE

Development consent will not be granted for subject site unless the consent authority:

• is satisfied that the development conforms to the specifications and requirements of Planning for Bush Fire Protection 2006, prepared by NSW Rural Fire Service in co-operation with the Department of Planning; or

• has been provided with a certificate prepared by a qualified consultant in bush fire risk who is recognised by the NSW Rural Fire Service stating that the development conforms to the relevant specification and requirements
