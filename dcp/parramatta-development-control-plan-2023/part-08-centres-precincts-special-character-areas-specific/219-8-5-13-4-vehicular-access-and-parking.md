#### 8.5.13.4 VEHICULAR ACCESS AND PARKING

#### 8.5.13.4.1 VEHICULAR ACCESS TO DEVELOPMENT LOTS

Objectives

O.01 The amount, location and design of car parking cater for the needs of residents, workers and visitors.

O.02 The impact of vehicle access points and driveways on the streetscape, pedestrian safety and quality of the public domain is minimised.

Controls

C.01 Where practicable, there must be only one vehicular entry point per block. C.02 Vehicular access must be provided at the lowest point of the street frontage. C.03 Where practicable, basement access must be located on local secondary streets. C.04 Locate vehicular driveways where they minimise the need for steep ramping from the street, so that the visual impact of driveways is minimised.

C.05 Vehicular access doors must be fitted behind the façade and finished of a material that will integrate into the building.

C.06 Vehicle access must be designed to minimise the visual impact to the street.

NORTH ROCKS VILLAGE PRECINCT

C.07 Vehicle access must be located a minimum of 10 metres from the perpendicular of any intersection of any two roads.

C.08 All vehicles must enter and exit the site in a forward direction. C.09 Vehicle and pedestrian access must be appropriately separated to remove conflict. C.10 Loading dock and waste collection must be incorporated within the building envelopes and must be located within the basement with one entry where possible.

#### 8.5.13.4.2 ON-SITE DEVELOPMENT PARKING

Objectives

O.01 Provide an appropriate amount of parking for the development. O.02 Minimise the negative impact of underground car park basement entries. O.03 Ensure adequate on-site car parking is provided for the facility that can be separately and easily managed and operated by Council.

Controls C.01 Adequate landscaped area must be maintained around the basement footprints. C.02 Car parking for non-residential uses are to be provided as set-out below.

| Facility | Minimum car parking provision |
|---|---|
| Oval | 30 spaces |
| Community Facility | 15 spaces or 1 space per 100sqm |

C.03 A traffic impact assessment must demonstrate that the proposed parking is sufficient to support the oval and community facility uses.

C.04 On-Street car parking within the immediate street adjoining the oval and community facility maybe used to satsify parking demand for the oval and community facility only.

C.05 Car parking for all other uses are to be provided in accordance with Part 6 of the Parramatta Development Control Plan (refer to Table 6.2.1).

C.06 Car parking rates must be rounded-up to the nearest whole number.
