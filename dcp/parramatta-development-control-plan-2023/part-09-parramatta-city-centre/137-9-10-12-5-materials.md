#### 9.10.12.5 MATERIALS

Objectives

O.01 Ensure the development does not compromise the amenity or safety of the public domain and surrounding building occupants.

Controls

C.01 Development is to comply with the controls relating to Building Exteriors and Section 9.8 – Environmental Sustainability. In particular, materials selection is to minimise reflectivity and glare impacts.
