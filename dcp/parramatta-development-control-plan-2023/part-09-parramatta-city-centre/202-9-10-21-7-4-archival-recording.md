#### 9.10.21.7.4 ARCHIVAL RECORDING

Objective

O.01 Undertake an archival recording of the site and buildings to ensure that a record of the whole of the place is undertaken prior to any works commencing.

Control

C.01 Prepare an archival recording of the site.
