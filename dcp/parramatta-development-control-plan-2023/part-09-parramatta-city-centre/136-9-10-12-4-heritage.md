#### 9.10.12.4 HERITAGE

Objectives

O.01 Ensure development demonstrates an appropriate transition to any heritage items or heritage conservation areas.

2 O’CONNELL STREET, PARRAMATTA

Controls

C.01 Development is to provide a transition in building height from the St Johns Anglican Cemetery to the tower structure through the use of podiums, awnings and other design features. A podium and awning that complies with control C0.7 under the heading “Building Envelopes” is considered to provide an appropriate transition for the purpose of this control.
