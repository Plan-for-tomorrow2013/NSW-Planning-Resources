#### 9.10.21.2 DESIRED FUTURE CHARACTER

St John’s Anglican Cathedral occupies a prominent site in the heart of the City Centre. It is the oldest Church site and continuous place of Christian worship in Australia dating from 1803. Development of the site is congruent with the Cathedral’s State heritage significance. This DCP supports the on-going role and heritage significance of St John’s Anglican Cathedral, as a landmark site in the City Centre.

Development of the site must enhance the heritage values associated with the St John’s Anglican Cathedral, and its immediate surrounds. The site extends the public spaces created by Parramatta Square and Centenary Square, framed by built form around the Cathedral with buildings positioned to maintain solar access to the key public open spaces. St John’s Anglican Cathedral is reinforced as the focal point for key views following the alignment of Church Street and Parramatta Square. The design of new open space accommodates the use of the St John’s Anglican Cathedral as a place of Anglican worship and provides for general movement and public uses.

Site Objectives

O.01 Provide a setting for St John’s Anglican Cathedral and the associated public domain that is sensitive to the heritage significance of the site and its significant elements.

O.02 Deliver employment uses within the City Centre that contribute to Parramatta’s employment objectives.

O.03 Deliver a civic response through the provision of public access, additional open space and public domain upgrades.

O.04 Facilitate the continued use of the site for Anglican worship supported by contemporary spaces for the Parish of Parramatta to support their ongoing use of the place.

O.05 Balance active street frontages and flood mitigation requirements. O.06 Enhance the heritage significance of the Warden Cottage (Verger’s Cottage), St John’s Building and the Bicentennial Square heritage listing, to communicate the historical evolution of the landmark site between St John’s Anglican Cathedral and contemporary development.
