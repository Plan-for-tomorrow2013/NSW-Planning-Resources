#### 9.10.21.7.1 HERITAGE LISTINGS

State heritage listing

• The subject site contains a State heritage item known as St John’s Anglican Cathedral (SHR ID 01805)

OPTION B - ST JOHN'S PARISH HALL PARTIALLY RETAINED

Local heritage listing

• St John’s Anglican Cathedral (item no. I465)

• St John’s Parish Hall (item no.I466)

• Warden’s Cottage (Verger’s Cottage) (item no.I467)

• Bicentennial Square and adjoining buildings (item no.I461)

• St John’s Building at 38 Hunter Street (included in the Bicentennial Square heritage listing) The subject site is in the vicinity of several state and locally listed heritage items, including:

• Centenary Square and adjoining buildings (item no.I461).

• Murray’s Building (and potential archaeological site) (item no.I464)

• Centennial Memorial Clock (item no.I468)

• Parramatta Town Hall (and potential archaeological site) (item no.I463)

• Shop (and potential archaeological site) (item no.I469)

• Two-storey residence, No. 41 Hunter Street (item no.I573)

The Parramatta LEP 2023 sets out the controls for development within the vicinity of heritage items.

General Objectives

O.01 Ensure that any new development of the land owned by the Anglican Church is congruent with the heritage values of the Cathedral complex and appropriate in the context of the heritage items located within the vicinity of the site.

O.02 Retain the Cathedral complex’s preeminent status in the City Centre. New development does not reduce the importance of the St John’s Anglican Cathedral complex in its immediate context, or the landmark quality of the Cathedral.

O.03 Ensure that new development retains the relationships between the St John’s Anglican Cathedral complex and the historic buildings of Centenary Square as well as broader relationships within the City Centre.

O.04 Provide an appropriate setting for St John’s Anglican Cathedral, St John’s Parish Hall, the former Warden’s Cottage (Verger’s Cottage), and the St John’s Building in any new development. This includes the retention of existing significant trees, an enhancement of the existing setting by the removal of at-grade car parking, and the usage of the land around the Cathedral complex for pedestrian access into the public domain of Centenary Square.

O.05 Ensure new development responds appropriately to its historic context, including the historic street pattern.
