#### 9.10.14.7 LAND USES

Objectives

O.01 To provide for useable and functional floor space that can support the desired use, achieve internal spaces appropriate to their function and support the Parramatta City Centre.

Controls

C.01 The ground floor street frontage is used for active commercial uses. C.02 Commercial/retail tenancies are of a sufficient size and layout to cater for their desired use and function.
