#### 9.10.2.2 SITE OBJECTIVES

Objectives

O.01 Create an urban environment that provides a mix of uses including high density residential, commercial, retail and community facilities.

O.02 Ensure built form articulation and an attractive composition of building elements with a strong relationship between buildings and streetscape.

O.03 Provide appropriate public domain elements, including internal pedestrian walkways, footpaths, open space for the benefit of the existing and future community.

O.04 Ensure building height is distributed across the site having regard for orientation, overshadowing, and views and vistas suitable for this gateway to Parramatta.

O.05 Provide opportunity for future car showroom functions on the ground level. O.06 Provide local amenities for existing and new residents with a variety of activities, services, and functions to attract people and places for them to meet and stay.

O.07 Provide an appropriate level of active ground floor uses to increase safety, pedestrian activity and use of public domain areas.

O.08 Provide a visual and physical connection throughout the site for a high level of surveillance and safety.

O.09 Accommodate generated traffic, and to mitigate traffic effects. O.10 Include stormwater management measures which appropriately address the level of flood affectation on the site and immediate surrounds.
