#### C2.11  Demolition

Full demolition of heritage items is generally not permissible. Partial demolition of heritage items is possible subject to the merits of the proposal.

Outbuildings that relate to heritage items can be demolished if the demolition does not impact on the significance of the heritage item.

#### Objective

O1. To retain buildings that are of heritage significance or components of the site that contributes to the significance of a heritage item.

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Buildings that are listed as heritage items, or components of the site of a heritage item that contribute to the significance of a heritage item, must not be demolished. |
| C2. | Partial demolition of a heritage item or of its significant components is only acceptable, where there is no impact on the heritage significance, or when the condition of fabric is such that its failure is imminent. In the latter case, the reconstruction of demolished fabric may be required. |
| C3. | Where demolition is proposed due to the condition of a heritage item, a supporting report from a suitably qualified and experienced heritage engineer is required. |

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C7. | Services such as television aerials and satellite dishes are to be located on rear facing roof slopes so the elements have limited visual impact and will not be visible from the public domain. |
| C8. | Services, including solar panels, must not be located on slate roofs. |
| C9. | Solar panels must not be located on roofs and walls facing primary roads, and must be set close to and parallel with the roof plane below. |
| C10. | Services should not be higher than the main ridge line of a building that is or is part of a heritage item and shall be located so that they are not visible from the public realm. |
| C11. | Lifts, if required, must be installed where existing building fabric and spaces have little or no significance. Lifts must not be visually intrusive. |
| C12. | Fire services must respect existing building fabric and details, including minimising changes and being appropriately located. |
| C13. | New service must not interrupt significant spaces and building fabric. |
