#### C3.1  General

#### Objectives

O1. To provide an appropriate visual setting for heritage items and heritage conservation areas, including through appropriate landscaping, fencing and car parking.

O2. To ensure the setting of heritage items and heritage conservation areas is not compromised by new development.

O3. To ensure that new development respects the contribution of heritage items and heritage conservation areas to the streetscape and/or townscape.

O4. To ensure that new development in the vicinity of a heritage item does not detract from the importance of the heritage item in the streetscape.
