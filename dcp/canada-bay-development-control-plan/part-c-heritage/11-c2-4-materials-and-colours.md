#### C2.4  Materials and colours

The appropriate selection of materials and colours is very important to ensuring that the aesthetic qualities of a heritage item are conserved. Development that includes changing roof materials, re-skinnning of brickwork, rendering or painting of face brickwork, and inappropriate textured finishes can degrade the character of a heritage item.

New buildings on the site of a heritage item, and additions and alterations should take into consideration the original materials and colours of the heritage item. While it is not always necessary to exactly match the materials and colours of the original building, new materials and colours must be carefully selected to ensure they complement the original building and ensure that the new work blends in rather than stands out.

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure that original materials that contribute to the significance of heritage items are not obscured.

O2. To ensure that colours of paintwork on heritage items are consistent with the significance of the heritage item.

O3. To ensure that external materials and colours of new development relate well to the materials and colours of the heritage item.

| C5. | Materials for additions and alterations to heritage items should be harmonious with the original materials of the heritage item. Generally, materials and colours must match the original. |
|---|---|
| C6. | Colour schemes for heritage items should have a hue and tonal relationship with traditional colour schemes for the period and style of the heritage item. |
| C7. | The use of fluorescent paint and primary colours on heritage items is not permitted. |

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Original materials of heritage items must not be replaced with different materials or materials of different colour. |
| C2. | Non-original materials of heritage items that are being replaced shall be replaced with material that matches the original material as closely as possible. |
| C3. | Painting or rendering original face brick walls and sandstone is not permitted. New weatherboards for timber houses must match the material and profile of the original. Re-roofing should use materials that match the original. |
| C4. | The detail and texture of original rendered finishes should not be changed. |
