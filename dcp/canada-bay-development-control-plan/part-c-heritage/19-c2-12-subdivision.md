#### C2.12  Subdivision

The site on which a heritage item is located usually contributes to the heritage significance of a heritage item, and may include structures and landscaping strongly associated with eh heritage item. The grounds also provide a  setting for a heritage item and can also ensure that important views to or from a heritage item are available. Subdivision can result in the loss of historic values, and adversely impact the setting of a heritage item.

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure that subdivision of a heritage item does not result in a loss of appropriate curtilage for the heritage item.

O2. To ensure that subdivision of a heritage item does not potentially result in development that would obscure important views to or from the heritage item.

O3. To ensure that subdivision does not degrade the heritage values of a heritage item.

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Subdivision of the site of a heritage item must ensure that an appropriate curtilage is retained for the heritage item. |
| C2. | The relationship between key elements that are important to the heritage item must be maintained. |
| C3. | Subdivision of land that includes a heritage item is not allowed unless it can be established that proposed or future development on the created allotments will not adversely impact on the setting of a heritage item or important views to or from the heritage item. |
| C4. | Subdivision must not be undertaken where a heritage item is located on an allotment that contributes to heritage values of the item. |
