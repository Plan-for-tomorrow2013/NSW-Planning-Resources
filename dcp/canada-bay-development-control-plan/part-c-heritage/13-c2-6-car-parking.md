#### C2.6  Car parking

Garages and carports can have a detrimental impact on the aesthetic qualities of heritage items. Garages and carports in front of the building line obscure views of the buildings and disrupt its contribution to the streetscape. The proportions of garage doors do not relate to the smaller and more vertical proportions of windows and doors that are usually found on heritage items.

#### Objective

O1. To ensure garages and carports do not detract from the setting of a heritage item. O2. To ensure garages and carports do not interrupt views to heritage items.

O3. To ensure garages and carports retain the heritage values of a place and have a good relationship with existing significant buildings. O4. To encourage the retention of original garages.

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Original or significant garages should be retained and conserved. |
| C2. | Garages and carports must be detached and located as far behind the front building alignment as possible. The minimum setback is 3 metres from the main wall (not a verandah wall). |
| C3. | Garages must not be incorporated into the main body of the heritage item or the front façade of a heritage item. |
| C4. | Where a new garage or carport is on the same side of a building as a side verandah, the garage or carport must be detached and located entirely behind the end of the verandah by at least 1m. |
| C5. | New car parking structures must be visually recessive. |
| C6. | Double garages, unless located in the backyard behind the main building, are not acceptable. |
| C7. | Basement parking is not permitted. |

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C3. | Roller shutters, security bars and grilles are not permitted on window and door openings that have a frontage to the street or that are adjacent to public open space. |
| C4. | Additions to external doors, including security screens and grilles, must not obscure or distort the original form of doors or the character and significance of the building. |
| C5. | New windows and doors must be: a) carefully located to retain the original relationship of solids and voids, and the pattern of openings; and b) of proportions, materials and details similar to existing windows and door openings in the building. |
| C6. | New dormer and roof windows of a house should be located on rear roof slopes in preference to roof slopes visible from the street. |
| C7. | New dormer windows visible from the street are only be possible on houses of style and period appropriate for dormer windows. These dormers must be: a) located to complement the original design of the building; and b) of proportions and details to complement the original character of the building. |
| C8. | Extensive areas of glazing are not permitted unless this feature was a feature of the original design of the building visible from the public realm. |
| C9. | New skylights are not permitted in roof slopes visible from the public realm. |

#### Controls

C8. Garages and carports must be single storey. Additional storeys above a garage are not acceptable. An attic may be possible for a garage located on a rear lane provided:

a) there is no adverse impact on the setting of the heritage item and views to the heritage item; and

b) the attic is wholly contained within the roof space; and

c) the garage has a maximum height of

5.4 metres; and

d) the roof slope is between 25⁰ and 35⁰; and

e) the garage does not extent boundary to boundary but has a minimum side boundary setback of 1.5 metres; and

f) if the lot is less than 13 metres wide the maximum garage width is 7 metres, or if the lot is greater than 13 metres has a maximum width of 9 metres.

C9. Original existing building fabric or garden elements of a heritage item must not to be demolished or altered to provide a carparking structure or hard stand area.

C10. Garages, carports and hard stand areas must not adversely affect the setting of a heritage item. They must not be located in front gardens or require the removal of trees.
