#### C1.1  Statement of heritage impact

A statement of heritage impact (also called a heritage impact statement) is an assessment report that analyses and describes how proposed development would affect the heritage significance of a heritage item, place in a conservation area, or development in the vicinity of a heritage item or conservation area. A statement of heritage impact can also propose measures to conserve the significance of a place for example, by stabilisation or repair and the reconstruction or reinstatement of original features.

A statement of heritage impact must be undertaken in accordance with the Department of Planning and Environment guideline Guidelines for preparing a statement of heritage impact, 2023.

A statement of heritage impact must also address relevant heritage provisions of the City of Canada Bay Development Control Plan.

A statement of heritage impact for a heritage item must include an assessment of significance undertaken in accordance with the Department of Planning and Environment guideline Assessing heritage significance,

2023\. The existing heritage inventory sheet for a property is in most cases too brief to properly understand the potential impacts of proposed development on the heritage significance of a place.
