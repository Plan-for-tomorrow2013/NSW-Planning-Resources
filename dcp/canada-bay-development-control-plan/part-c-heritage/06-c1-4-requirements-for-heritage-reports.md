#### C1.4  Requirements for heritage reports

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure heritage reports assist Council to determine the impact of a proposal on the heritage significance of a place.

#### Controls

C1. A statement of heritage impact must be submitted with a development application for proposed development to a heritage item or to a place within a heritage conservation area. A statement of heritage impact must also be prepared for developments over two storeys in height where the subject site is in the vicinity of a heritage item or heritage conservation

area. The statement of heritage impact must be prepared in accordance with Department of Planning and Environment guideline Guidelines for preparing a statement of heritage impact, 2023. The statement of heritage impact must also address relevant heritage provisions of the City of Canada Bay Development Control Plan.

C2. A statement of heritage impact for a heritage item must include an assessment of significance undertaken in accordance with the Department of Planning and Environment guideline Assessing heritage significance, 2023.

C3. A statement of heritage impact for development located within the vicinity of a heritage item or heritage conservation area must address the controls in Part C3 Development in the vicinity of a heritage item or a heritage conservation area of the DCP.

C4. A development application for proposed development associated with heritage item numbers I21 and I541 within the Bakehouse Quarter, North Strathfield, must be accompanied by a conservation management plan for any individual building concerned.

| C6. | A conservation management plan may be required by Council to be submitted for development to a heritage item other than those stated in controls C4 and C5. The applicant should seek advice from Council’s Heritage Coordinator. |
| C7. | A conservation management plan must be prepared in accordance with the Heritage Council of NSW guideline Guidance on developing a conservation management plan, 2021. (Further information in relation to conservation management plans is available on the Heritage NSW website.) |
