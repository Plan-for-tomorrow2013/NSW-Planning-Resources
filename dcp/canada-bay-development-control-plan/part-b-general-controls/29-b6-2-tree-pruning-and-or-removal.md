#### B6.2  Tree pruning and/or removal

This part explains when approval from Council is required to carry out tree or vegetation works, pursuant to State Environmental Planning Policy (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021, Chapter 2, Vegetation in Non-Rural Areas.

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure the safety of the community, private property and public infrastructure assets. O2. To protect trees within and adjacent to development sites and to ensure that all new development provides an opportunity for existing and new trees to grow.

O3. To protect and manage the urban landscape so trees continue to make a significant contribution to its quality, character and amenity.

#### General Controls

C1. A person must not ringbark, cut down, top, lop, prune, remove, injure or wilfully destroy any protected tree, or other vegetation to which this development control plan applies without an approval granted by the Council, unless an exemption applies.

#### Development application Controls

C2. Development consent is required for the pruning and/or removal of any vegetation that forms part of a Heritage Item or is within a Heritage Conservation area.

#### Tree pruning and removal permit Controls

C3. A permit is required for the pruning and/ or removal of a Protected Tree that is not associated with a Heritage Item or within a Heritage Conservation Area, unless a permit exemption applies.

#### Permit exemptions for trees and vegetation works

A permit is not required in the following circumstances:

a) Pruning and/or removal of any tree that is not a Protected Tree.

b) Works limited to the removal of dead branches that do not have hollows and do not provide habitat for native fauna.

c) Pruning and/or removal of a tree that is listed as a State or Regional Priority Weed under the Biosecurity Act 2015.

d) Pruning that amounts to no greater than 10% of a tree’s total canopy being removed in a calendar year, provided that the works are carried out by an Arborist of Australian Qualification Framework (AQF) level 3 and above, in accordance with the Australian Standards for the Pruning of Amenity Trees AS4373-2007.

e) Pruning of branches that are overhanging from an adjoining property in accordance with permit exemption (d) and ‘AS4373—Pruning of amenity trees’.

f) Works to trees owned by, or under the care, control and management of Council and undertaken by delegated Council staff or their authorised contractors.

g) The tree is an exempt species listed in Table B-U.

h) Works limited to the maintenance of a minimum clearance of five hundred (500) millimetres from domestic service leads as specified by Energy Australia, provided that the works were carried out by an Arborist of Australian Qualification Framework (AQF) level 3 and above, in accordance with the Australian Standards for the Pruning of Amenity Trees AS4373-2007. Notes:

1\) The exemptions listed above do not apply to land identified as a Heritage Item or within a Heritage Conservation Area.

2\) The owner of the tree must provide written consent before any approval can be granted and before any exempt tree works described in a-g commence.

3\) Photo evidence of proposed exempt tree works must be available as evidence and provided to Council upon request by Council Officer.

4\) Council does not mediate exempt works to trees on an adjoining private property.

5\) Permits are issued in accordance with SEPP (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021, Part 2.3.
