### B10  Energy Infrastructure

#### B10.1  Energy efficiency

#### Objectives

O1. To encourage designs that make provision for current or future installation of energy efficient technologies.

#### Controls

C1. Roof forms shall be designed to allow for current or future installation of solar panels without adverse impacts on the amenity of neighbours or the streetscape.

C2. Space should be provided within the building for the current or future installation of battery storage.
