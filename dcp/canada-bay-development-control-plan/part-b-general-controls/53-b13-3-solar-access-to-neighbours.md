#### B13.3  Solar access to neighbours

#### Objectives

O1. To minimise the amount of overshadowing of neighbouring developments and outdoor spaces to maintain their amenity.

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Direct sunlight to all north facing windows of habitable rooms of adjacent dwellings should not be reduced to less than 3 hours between 9.00am and 3.00pm on 21 June (mid-winter). |
| C2. | Direct sunlight to 50% of the principal private open space of adjacent properties should not be reduced to less than 3 hours between 9.00am and 3.00pm on 21 June (mid-winter). |
