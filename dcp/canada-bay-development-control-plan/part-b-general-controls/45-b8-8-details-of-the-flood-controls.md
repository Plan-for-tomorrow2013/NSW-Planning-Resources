#### B8.8  Details of the Flood Controls

(Flood Planning Matrix see Table B-Y)

#### Floor Level

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Floor levels to be equal to or greater than the 1% Annual Exceedance Probability (AEP) flood level. |
| C2. | Habitable floor levels to be no lower than the 1%AEP flood level plus freeboard. Non-habitable floor levels to be equal to or greater than the 1%AEP flood level plus freeboard. Freeboard requirement referring to the clause, Minimum Freeboard Requirement for Flood Prone Land in DCP, Appendix 2 – Engineering Specification or specific in Council’s adopted flood study. |
| C3. | All floor levels to be equal to or greater than the Probable Maximum Flood (PMF) level. |
| C4. | Floor levels to be equal to or greater than the 1%AEP flood level plus freeboard. Where this is not practical due to compatibility with the height of adjacent buildings, or compatibility with the floor level of existing buildings, or the need for access for persons with disabilities, a lower floor level may be considered. In these circumstances, the floor level is to be as high as practical, and, when undertaking alternations or additions, no lower than the existing floor level. |
| C5. | A restriction is to be placed on the title of the land, pursuant to S.88B of the Conveyancing Act, where the lowest habitable floor area is elevated more than 1.5m above finished ground level, confirming that the subfloor space is not to be enclosed. |
| C6. | Because of the site conditions/particular catchment characteristics of the Concord West Precinct and Rhodes Precinct, additional requirement is for habitable floor levels to be at a minimum of RL 3.0m AHD. |

#### Building Components and Method

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | All structures to have flood compatible building components below the 1%AEP flood level plus freeboard. |
| C2. | All structures to have flood compatible building components below the PMF. |

#### Structural Soundness

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | A professional Engineer’s report is required to certify that the structure can withstand the forces of floodwater, debris and buoyancy up to and including a 1%AEP flood level plus freeboard. However, if shelter-in-place is to be the flood evacuation strategy, the structure must be designed to meet Structural Soundness – C2 below. |
| C2. | A professional Engineer’s report is required to certify that the structure can withstand the forces of floodwater, debris and buoyancy up to and including a PMF level. |

#### Flood Affectation

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | A professional Engineer’s report is required to demonstrate how and certify that the development will not increase flood affectation elsewhere, having regard to: a) loss of flood storage; b) changes in flood levels, flows and velocities caused by alterations to flood flows; and c) the cumulate impact of multiple existing/ potential developments in the vicinity. |
| C2. | The impact of the development on flooding elsewhere to be considered having regard to the three factors listed in C1 above. |

#### Management and Design

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Applicant is to demonstrate that potential development as a consequence of a subdivision proposal can be undertaken in accordance with this clause, and any applicable flood study, FRMS and FRMP. |
| C2. | i) A Flood Safe and Action Plan for home and business to address safety measures for preparing and responding to flood events during and greater than the 1%AEP event. ii) Site Emergency Response Flood Plan required where the site floor levels are below the 1%AEP flood level. The plan shall be prepared and certified by a suitably qualified engineer. |
| C3. | Applicant is to demonstrate that area is available to store goods above the 1%AEP flood level plus freeboard. |
| C4. | No storage of materials which may cause pollution or be potentially hazardous during any flood is permitted below the 1%AEP flood level. |
