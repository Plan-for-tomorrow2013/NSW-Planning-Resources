#### B10.2  Substations

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure substations are incorporated into the design of the development.

#### Controls

C1. All applications for medium or high density developments, that involve the construction of more than two dwellings, must include the results of a Preliminary Enquiry from Ausgrid.

C2. If an Ausgrid Preliminary Enquiry identifies the need for a substation then the substation and any associated infrastructure/works must be included in the development application.

The substation must be incorporated into the building design so it does not have a negative impact on the streetscape or future occupants of the building and it must also satisfy Ausgrid requirements.
