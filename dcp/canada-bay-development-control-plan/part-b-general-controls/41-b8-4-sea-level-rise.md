#### B8.4  Sea level rise

#### Table B-V Adopted 2100 Sea Level Rise Tailwater

#### Conditions.

| Design Event (AEP) | 2100 Sea Level Rise Tailwater Level (m AHD) |
|---|---|
| 1%AEP Envelope | 1% Harbour Level +0.9m 1.44m AHD + 0.9m = 2.34m AHD |
| 0.5% |  |
| 0.2% |  |
| PMF |  |

In the absence of updated advice on sea level rise due to climate change, a 2100 level of 0.9 m has been adopted in accordance with the “NSW Sea Level Rise Policy Statement” (October 2009). Council’s adopted Exile Bay Catchment Flood Study (December 2020) Table 26, or Table B-V also incorporates the 2100 Sea Level Rise scenario.

The minimum habitable floor level and basement floor flood protection for the property adjacent to natural watercourse or bays is the 1% AEP Envelope with freeboard, set at 2.84m AHD. In the Concord West and Rhodes Precinct areas, the minimum habitable floor level and the basement floor flood protection shall be no lower than 3m AHD.
