#### B2.1  To what facilities does this Part

#### apply?

This Part of the DCP applies to any fixed transmitter, its supporting infrastructure and ancillary development under the following legislation:

• Telecommunications (Low-impact Facilities) Determination 2018 (LIF Determination);

• Telecommunications Act 1997, and

• Radiocommunications Act 1992. The DCP does not apply to temporary emergency services.
