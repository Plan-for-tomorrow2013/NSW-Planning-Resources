#### B13.4  Solar access to dwellings within

#### the development

#### Objectives

O1. To maximise solar access to living areas and private open space in order to improve residential amenity.

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | The proposed development shall receive a minimum of 3 hours of direct sunlight to all north facing living room windows between 9.00am and 3.00pm on 21 June (mid-winter). |
| C2. | Direct sunlight to 50% of the principal private open space should not be reduced to less than 3 hours between 9.00am and 3.00pm on 21 June (mid-winter). |
