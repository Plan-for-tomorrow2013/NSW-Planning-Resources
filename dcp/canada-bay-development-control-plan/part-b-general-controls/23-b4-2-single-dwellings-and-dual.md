#### B4.2  Single Dwellings and Dual

#### Occupancies

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Residential developments are to be designed to provide each individual dwelling with waste storage cupboard and space within the kitchen to hold two days volume of waste for three waste streams (general waste, recycling and food organics) in accordance with the following: Allocated 1x 120L Waste Bin (1 per dwelling), 1 x 240L Recycling Bin (1 per dwelling collected fortnightly) and 1 x 240L Garden Organics (Food Organics / Garden Organics bin)* (1 per dwelling). *future proofing |
| C2. | Space must be allocated within each property boundary behind the building line for storing Council specified waste and recycling bins. |
| C3. | The waste/recycling storage area shall be constructed of brick or other approved masonry material, have a concrete floor at appropriate level approved by Council and be suitably graded to allow drainage. |
