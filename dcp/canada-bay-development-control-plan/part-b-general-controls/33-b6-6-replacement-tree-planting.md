#### B6.6  Replacement tree planting

Replanting requirements for trees removed from private and public land.

All development proposals must be designed to maintain or improve the urban forest, amenity and biodiversity values of the site by minimising the impact on trees. Proposals must include replacement planting if trees are proposed for removal. Replacement trees are to attain a similar mature size and habit of those proposed for removal.

This requirement applies to trees on public and private land (within the property of proposed development, including Council owned and managed trees, trees on adjacent property and trees on adjoining land). All such development must comply with the provisions of

AS4970 Protection of Trees on Development Sites.

#### Objectives

O1. To limit the loss of canopy resulting from tree removal.

O2. To limit the loss of plantable space resulting from tree removal.

O3. To expand the tree canopy.

#### Controls

C1. Replacement plantings for trees removed on private property are to be planted on the property, unless otherwise specified in a permit or development consent issued by Council.

C2. Replacement plantings from trees removed from public property are to be planted in a public or private location as specified in a permit or development consent issued by Council.

C3. If a tree (regardless of health or species) is proposed to be removed, a replanting ratio of 2:1 will generally apply, requiring the planting of two trees for every tree removed.

Trees from the list of Canada Bay tree species provided in this DCP are to be used.

C4. Replacement trees are to be of a similar size and habit to those removed, unless otherwise specified in a permit or development consent issued by Council.

C5. A 4:1 replanting ratio will apply where the tree to be removed is on land that is:

a) In an C2 Environmental Conservation zone; or

b) Environmentally Sensitive Land; or

c) Identified as containing Biodiversity; or

d) Within a Biodiversity Corridor. Locally native species (including canopy trees, shrubs and groundcovers) must be used (refer to list of Plants suitable for corridors and restoration planting).

C6. Trees are to be planted a minimum of 2 metres from an approved dwelling or approved retaining wall >1m in height or approved commercial structure (excluding detached garages, carports and other buildings ancillary to a dwelling house), unless otherwise specified in a permit or development consent issued by Council.

C7. Trees are to be planted a minimum of 2 metres distance from any other tree or shrub and trees should not be grown as or form part of a hedge.

C8. A permit or development consent issued by Council may specify planting locations.

C9. Photographic evidence of replacement planting must be provided to council within 3 months of issue of tree pruning and removal permit or final occupation certificate.
