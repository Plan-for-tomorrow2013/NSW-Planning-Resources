#### B3.15  Car Share

C3.

#### Objectives

O1. To provide car sharing facilities to meet current and future needs.

O2. To reduce car usage and reliance.

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Car sharing facilities must be provided in Rhodes East in accordance with Table B-L. |
| C2. | Car share spaces are to be for the exclusive use of car share scheme vehicles. |

C4.

C5.

#### Table B-L Car sharing facilities

Car share parking spaces are to be:

a) Exclusive of visitor car parking;

b) Retained as common property of the Owners Corporation of the site and not sold or leased to an individual owner/ occupier at any time;

c) Made available for use by operators of car share schemes;

d) Grouped together in the most convenient locations relative to car parking entrances and pedestrian lifts or access points;

e) Located in well-lit places that allow for casual surveillance; and

f) Signposted for use only by car share vehicles and made known to building occupants and car share members through appropriate signage which indicate the availability of the scheme and promotes its use as an alternative mode of transport.

Development Applications are to demonstrate how the car share parking space(s) is to be accessed, including where access is through a security gate. A covenant is to be registered with the strata plan advising of any car share parking space. The covenant is to include provisions that the car share parking space(s) cannot be revoked or modified without prior approval of Council.

| Type of development | Car share facilities required within 400m or less walking distance to station. | Car share facilities required if greater than 400m walking distance to station. |
|---|---|---|
| Multi dwelling housing, Residential flat building, Shop top housing | 1 per 20 dwellings | 1 per 40 dwellings |
| Car share rate to reduce car parking provision | N/A | 1 car share space in lieu of 3 private car parking spaces |
