#### B3.13  Electric Vehicles

#### Objectives

O1. To provide facilities for charging of electric vehicles to meet current and future needs. O2. To accommodate hybrid and electric vehicles by ensuring that adequate charging points for these vehicles are provided in off-street private and public car parking areas.

#### Controls

C1. Level 1 electric vehicle charging facilities must be provided for all new developments and substantial alterations to existing developments in accordance with Table B-I.

C2. Level 2 electric vehicle charging facilities must be provided for all new developments and substantial alterations to existing developments in accordance with Table B-J.

Note: If Level 2 facilities are required they are to be provided in addition to any Level 1 requirements.

C3. Shared EV facilities must be accessible to anyone who has legal access to the building and are to be shared by all users of the building. The facility must be provided in an area that is of an appropriate size (refer to minimum parking dimensions) to safely park a vehicle during charging. Shared EV facilities are not to be provided in parking spaces required by this DCP.

C4. All electric charging points in common property or visitor parking areas are to have clear signage identifying:

a) Location; and

b) Fees and charges, if any.

#### Controls

C5. Electric circuitry to accommodate ‘Level 2’ electric vehicle charging points must be integrated into all off-street car parking of new residential and non-residential development to ensure that all car spaces can install electric vehicle charging points in the future. This must include:

a) Ensuring adequate electrical capacity and infrastructure (cable size, distribution board size etc.) for the electric vehicle charging point system; and

b) Providing either buried cables underground or cable trays sufficient to accommodate electric circuitry to each car space; and

c) Slow, single phase 7kW (minimum) electrical circuitry for private car spaces; and

d) Fast, three-phase 11kW – 25kW (minimum) electrical circuitry for non-private, visitor and shared EV spaces; and

e) Electricity meters must be installed to enable separate billing for each dwelling.

Note: Circuitry requirements for all off-street parking spaces are in addition to minimum charging point requirements in Table B-I and Table B-J.

C6. Shared EV facilities shall include a billing mechanism for all users of the facility. The developer must not lock in a vendor and must provide a clear pathway for the owners corporation to change vendors without a punitive exit clause.

C7. The installation of a Level 2 electric vehicle charging points is encouraged for new dwelling houses, dual occupancies and semi-detached dwellings.

C8. An EV charging unit must not:

a) Obstruct the following

i) vehicular, cyclist or pedestrian access to or from, or entry into, a building,

ii) cyclist or pedestrian movement along a cycleway or footpath, and

b) Obstruct access to, or interfere with, a structure, device, fixture or equipment used for firefighting or fire protection, including a fire hydrant.

C9. An EV charging unit for a residential premises that is proposed to be installed on public land must also:

a) Have approval from Council for the use of the land, and

b) Have development approval from Council, and

c) Be installed adjacent to an area where a motor vehicle may lawfully be parked, and

d) Be for the non-commercial use of an owner or occupier of the adjacent residential premises, and

e) Be connected to the electricity meter of the owner or occupier, and

f) Locate all cables providing electrical power to the unit underground, and

g) Be able to be fully retracted underground when not in use, and

h) Be installed in accordance with specifications or instructions issued by the manufacturer of the unit, and

i) Not include advertising, and

j) Be installed in accordance with Council’s EV Charging Infrastructure Policy.

k) Comply with relevant provisions of State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021. Note: Installation of a charging facility on public land adjacent to a property does not grant ownership or dedicated use of the associated public land.

C10. A statement prepared by a suitably qualified person must be provided with a development application which specifies that the proposal will be able to meet the electric vehicle requirements of this Development Control Plan and the National Construction Code.

Note: See Part L for definitions.

#### Minimum Level 1 electric vehicle charging facility requirements

#### Table B-I

| Type of development | Type of charging facility installed (EV-Ready) | Minimum number of charging points/ facilities/stations |
|---|---|---|
| Dwelling houses, dual occupancies, semi-detached dwellings | Level 1 | 1 per parking space |
| Secondary dwelling | Level 1 | 1 per parking space (if parking is provided) |
| All other residential development such as Boarding houses, Manor houses, Multi-dwelling housing, Multi-dwelling housing (terraces), Residential flat building, Shop top housing (residential component) | Level 1 | 1 per parking space 1 per five bicycle parking spaces (a dedicated space and charging point for electric bicycles and mobility scooters to be charged must be provided for every five bicycle parking spaces) |
| Non-residential | Level 1 | 1 per parking space 1 per five bicycle parking spaces (a dedicated space and charging point for electric bicycles and mobility scooters to be charged must be provided for every five bicycle parking spaces) |

#### Table B-J

| Type of development | Type of charging facility installed (EV-Installed) | Minimum number of charging points/ facilities/stations |
|---|---|---|
| All other residential development such as Boarding houses, Manor houses, Multi-dwelling housing, Multi-dwelling housing (terraces), Residential flat building, Shop top housing (residential component) | Level 2 | Private spaces • A charging point is to be provided in 10% of all car parking spaces. If this results in less than one, then a minimum of one charging point must be provided Shared EV facilities • 1 Shared EV facility for developments with 5-10 dwellings • 1 additional Shared EV facility for every additional 10 dwellings or part thereof • To be provided in a location accessible to all users of the building (in addition to required parking) |

Note: Numbers are to be rounded up if the fraction of the total calculation is equal or more than half (0.5 of a space), unless otherwise specified.
