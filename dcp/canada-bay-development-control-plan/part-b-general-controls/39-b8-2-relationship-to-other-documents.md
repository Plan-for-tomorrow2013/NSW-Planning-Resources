#### B8.2  Relationship to other documents

In areas where Council has adopted a Flood Study or Floodplain Risk Management Study or Floodplain Risk Management Plan that sets a flood planning area and freeboards, these will take precedence over the following DCP controls where there is any inconsistency.

Reference should also be made to the Canada Bay Local Environmental Plan for requirements in relation to flood planning and considerations.
