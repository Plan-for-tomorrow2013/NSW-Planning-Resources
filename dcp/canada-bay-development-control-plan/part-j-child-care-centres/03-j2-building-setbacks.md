### J2  Building setbacks

#### Controls

#### Objectives

C1. For Child Care Centres in residential zones, advertising should be limited to not more than one sign per Child Care Centre which

a) Has a maximum area of 0.5m2; and

b) Serves only to identify the name and

O1. To ensure the height and scale of a child care centre relates to site conditions, complements the prevailing character of the streetscape and minimises any adverse amenity impacts upon the surrounding properties.

phone number of the Child Care Centre and the hours of operation.

O2. To ensure the appearance of the development enhances the streetscape.

#### Controls

C2. For Child Care Centres in all other zones, compliance should be achieved with Council’s signage requirements.

C1. The Child Care Centre should comply with the relevant setback controls as stipulated in the Canada Bay Development Control Plan as follows:

• Within a residential zone, setbacks for dwelling houses; and

• Within a commercial / industrial zone – setbacks will be considered on a merit basis.
