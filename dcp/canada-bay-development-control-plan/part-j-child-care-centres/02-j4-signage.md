### J4  Signage

O2. Integrate into existing residential environments and are unobtrusive in terms of size, bulk and height;

#### Objectives

O3. Are appropriate for the surrounding built form and natural landscape;

O1. Complement and enhance the predominant character of the locality;

O4. Will have minimum impact on surrounding land uses; and

O2. Not obscure the view of attractive landscapes, streetscapes, or significant buildings; and

O5. To ensure the health, safety and wellbeing of children and staff in Child Care Centres.

O3. Not adversely affect the safety of traffic or pedestrians.
