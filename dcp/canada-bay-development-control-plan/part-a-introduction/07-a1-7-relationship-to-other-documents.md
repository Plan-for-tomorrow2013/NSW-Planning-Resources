#### A1.7  Relationship to other documents

#### State environmental planning policies

State environmental planning policies (SEPPs) may apply to the land to which this DCP applies. Where this occurs, the statutory provisions of those SEPPs prevail over this DCP.

The DCP does include controls for development to which SEPPs are applicable.  In this regard the DCP aims to complement and provide guidance on such developments.

#### Community Participation Plan

Division 2.6 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (EP&A Act) sets out the mandatory community participation requirements with respect to the exercise of relevant planning functions.

The City of Canada Bay Community Participation Plan sets out how and when Council will undertake community participation when exercising relevant planning functions.  This includes notification of development applications and applications to modify a development consent.

#### Local infrastructure contributions

Under Sections 7.11 and 7.12 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979, councils are able to levy contributions from developers for the provision of public facilities and infrastructure required as a consequence of development.

These contributions are used for the upgrade and development of community facilities, recreation facilities, infrastructure, roads and traffic management and town centre improvements.

The City of Canada Bay Local Infrastructure Contribution Plan specifies the contributions that may need to be paid for development approvals.

#### Affordable housing contributions scheme

Under Section 7.32(1)(c) of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 and State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021, councils are able to develop affordable housing contributions schemes and levy contributions from developers for the provision of affordable housing as part of a development approval.

The City of Canada Bay Affordable Housing Contributions Scheme specifies the contributions that may need to be paid for development approvals.

#### Apartment Design Guide

The Design Guide provides planning and design standards for residential flat buildings that are three or more storeys high and that comprise at least four dwellings.

#### Rhodes Place Strategy

The Strategy creates a 20-year vision, and a planning and infrastructure delivery framework and complements the Rhodes East and Rhodes West planning controls in the Special Precinct section of this DCP.

#### Homebush Transport Oriented Development

#### Precinct Design Guide

The Design Guide applies to land within the Homebush TOD Precinct.

#### Public Domain Plans/Street Design Guides

The public domain plans and street design guides provide detailed guidance on the development of specific streets and open space.
