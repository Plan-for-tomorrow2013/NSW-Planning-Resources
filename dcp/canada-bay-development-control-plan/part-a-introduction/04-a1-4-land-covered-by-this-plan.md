#### A1.4  Land covered by this Plan

This Plan applies to all land within the Canada Bay Local Government Area except for the sites included in:

• Strathfield Triangle Development Control Plan It should be noted that in certain circumstances site and precinct specific DCPs adopt some of the provisions of this DCP in accordance with Section 3.43 (3) of the Act.
