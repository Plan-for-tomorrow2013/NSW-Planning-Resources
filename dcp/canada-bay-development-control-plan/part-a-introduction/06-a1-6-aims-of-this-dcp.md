#### A1.6  Aims of this DCP

1\. Encourage development that responds to its context and is compatible with the existing built environment and public domain;

2\. Recognise and reinforce the distinctive characteristics of Canada Bay’s neighbourhood and centres;

3\. Build upon the detailed objectives and controls under Canada Bay LEP 2013;

4\. Protect and enhance the public domain;

5\. Encourage design that maintains and enhances the character and heritage significance of heritage items and heritage conservation areas; and

6\. Encourage ecologically sustainable development and reduce the impacts of development on the environment.
