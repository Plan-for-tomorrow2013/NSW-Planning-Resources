## 63 Blaxland Road

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C37. | The former school building is to be retained in-situ for community use or compatible change of use. |
| C38. | Existing trees, particularly those to the north, are to be retained. |
| C39. | The paved areas can be re-used or landscaped for pedestrian amenity. |
| C40. | The historic core of the school building is to be conserved and revealed by the demolition of the later enclosure. |
| C41. | No vertical additions will be permitted to the retained school building. |
