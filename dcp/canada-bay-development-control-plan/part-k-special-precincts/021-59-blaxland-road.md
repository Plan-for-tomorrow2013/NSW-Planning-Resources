## 59 Blaxland Road

| Controls |  |
|---|---|
| C31. | The existing Federation period house to remain in situ and be retained as residential or incorporate a complimentary change of use. |
| C32. | The two palm trees in the front garden are to be either retained or relocated within the current site. |
