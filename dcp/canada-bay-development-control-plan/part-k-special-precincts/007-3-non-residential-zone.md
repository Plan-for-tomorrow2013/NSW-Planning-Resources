## 3. Non Residential Zone

This land includes areas which may contain low level residual contamination, It includes the area above the 'containment cell stratum'.

The anticipated uses in this zone include open space, playing fields, commercial or industrial, roads and infrastructure. Limited residential development could be permitted in this zone subject to certification by the site auditor.
