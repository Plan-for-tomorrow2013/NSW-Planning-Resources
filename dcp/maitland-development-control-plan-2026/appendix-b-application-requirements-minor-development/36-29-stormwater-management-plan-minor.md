## 29. STORMWATER MANAGEMENT PLAN (MINOR)

If you are proposing urban development or subdivision, consultation with Council’s Development Engineers is advised. Any subdivision over 3,000m2 and/or impacting on the public stormwater system will require a full Stormwater Management Plan in accordance with Item 54 of Appendix A.
