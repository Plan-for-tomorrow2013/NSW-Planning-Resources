## 17. FLORA AND FAUNA ASSESSMENT (FFA)

The purpose of the Flora and Fauna Assessment (FFA) is to assess potential impacts on biodiversity and ensure compliance with relevant environmental legislation and policies. An FFA is required when the Biodiversity Offset Scheme is not triggered but impacts to biodiversity still require evaluation.
