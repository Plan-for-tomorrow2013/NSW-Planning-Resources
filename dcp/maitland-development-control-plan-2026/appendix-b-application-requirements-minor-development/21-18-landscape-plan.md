## 18. LANDSCAPE PLAN

A plan or document outlining the extent, type and location of hard and soft landscape works proposed for a development. Drawings of the proposed landscape area must be submitted, including species selected and materials to be used, presented in the context of the proposed building or buildings, and the surrounding development and its context.
