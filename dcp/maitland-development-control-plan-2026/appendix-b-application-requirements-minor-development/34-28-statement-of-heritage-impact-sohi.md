## 28. STATEMENT OF HERITAGE IMPACT (SOHI)

The heritage impact statement demonstrates how the proposed development conserves and mitigates for the protection of the identified heritage significance, based on the following principles:

a. Development is consistent with the statement of heritage significance for that item.

b. Development protects the setting of the heritage item.

c. Development retains the significant internal and external spaces and is to recycle, re-purpose

and re-use fabric and building elements.

d. Development avoids facadism by using all of the components of the building including, but not

limited to, the structure, floor, roof, floor and wall framing, fittings and finishes, fabric and

e. Development removes alterations and additions that are unsympathetic to the heritage

significance of the heritage item.

f. Reinstates missing building elements and details.

g. Uses materials, finishes and colours that are appropriate to the architecture, stylistic period of

h. Reinforces the dimensions, pattern, scale and style of the original windows, door openings and

features of the heritage item.

i. Maintains and repairs building elements in order to retain the heritage item in a serviceable

condition commensurate with the statement of heritage significance. Reference to the Heritage Act 1977 is required where potential to yield highly significantly archaeological items and relics are discovered and there is likely to be disturbance, damage or an item destroyed by excavation. The preparation of heritage reports is to be undertaken by a suitably qualified consultant who has experience in heritage conservation matters and is registered on the NSW Heritage’s Consultants
