## 7. CIVIL PLANS

Civil Engineering Plans encompass all information relating to the in-ground services of a site. This includes storm water connections, pumps, pits, and drains. Civil plans can include section views, which are detailed cross sections, providing further clarification on the height positions of elements. In conjunctions with the plan views, these are necessary for an accurate positioning of the many components and their relation to each other.
