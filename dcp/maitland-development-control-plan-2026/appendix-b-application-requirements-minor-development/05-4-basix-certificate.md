## 4. BASIX CERTIFICATE

Note: Applicants can generate the BASIX Certificate at the BASIX website:

http://www.basix.nsw.gov.au or contact the BASIX Help line on 1300 650 908.

> Words printed on the picture (title and legend, copied from the PDF):
> • Setout, such as ground levels (existing and finished)
> • Clearing vegetation
> • Removing topsoil (cut and fill)
> • Removing and replacing unsuitable material
> • Cuttings and embankment construction
> • Spoil or borrow activities
> • Processing selected material
> • Total cubic metres of fill import
> • Total cubic metres of fill export
