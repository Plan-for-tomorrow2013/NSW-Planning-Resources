## 26. SOIL AND WATER MANAGEMENT PLAN (SWMP)

A soil and water management plan is a set of specific site plans or drawings that detail sediment and erosion control measures on building and construction sites. The Soil and Water Management Plan (SWMP) shows the type, location, design, installation and maintenance schedule for all these measures and should be considered as the blueprint for controlling all anticipated erosion and for preventing sediment from leaving a site. A SWMP is to be prepared in accordance with Council’s MOES and Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure’s Guidelines for erosion and sediment control on building sites, and Landcom’s The Blue Book – Managing Urban Stormwater; Soils and Construction/Planning for Erosion and Sediment Control on Single Residential Allotments.
