#### 4.6.8. Infill development

Infill development involves the addition of new buildings within HCAs. New buildings should be sympathetic in design to the HCA and should not impact the streetscape character.

#### Objectives

O.72 To ensure that infill development enhances the character of heritage items and contributory buildings.

O.73 To ensure that infill development is sympathetic to the HCA in terms of scale, form, architectural style, details and materials.

O.74 To ensure that siting of new buildings respects the significance and character of the HCA.

| PC.2 • O.74 | New buildings are to be setback or sited behind the façade of heritage items and contributory buildings and are to maintain the established historically significant pattern of setbacks in the streetscape. |
