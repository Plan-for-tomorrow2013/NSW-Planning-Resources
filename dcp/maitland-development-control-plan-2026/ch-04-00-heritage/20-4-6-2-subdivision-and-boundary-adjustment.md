#### 4.6.2. Subdivision and boundary adjustment

#### Objectives

O.46 To retain the original historic subdivision patterns. O.47 To protect the significance of heritage conservation areas, including contributory buildings within them.

O.48 To ensure that HCAs are properly considered in the design of new subdivisions.
