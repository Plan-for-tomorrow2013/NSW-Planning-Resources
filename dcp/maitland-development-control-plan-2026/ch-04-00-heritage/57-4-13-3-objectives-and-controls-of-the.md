#### 4.13.3. Objectives and Controls of the Aberglasslyn House HCA

Note: The controls within Chapter 10, Section 10.3.10.15 and Section 10.3.10.22 are to be directly

considered alongside this section.

#### Objectives

O.139 To retain and conserve Aberglasslyn House, its principal garden and grounds. O.140 To retain and conserve the pastoral landscape of the HCA and setting of Aberglasslyn House. O.141 To retain the views to and from the house and broader HCA. O.142 To retain mature trees and contributory landscape fabric and items that positively contribute to the character of the HCA.

O.143 To discourage new development within the HCA that does not relate to the historic character or use of Aberglasslyn House.
