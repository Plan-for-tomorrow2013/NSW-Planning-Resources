#### 4.6.1. Demolition

#### Objectives

O.44 To ensure that contributory buildings and elements that contribute to the character and significance of heritage conservation areas are retained and conserved.

O.45 To ensure that the original form and features of buildings that are contributory are retained and improved.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.44 • O.45 | Demolition of contributory buildings is to be avoided wherever possible. A Structural Assessment Report prepared in accordance with Appendix A: Application Requirements – Other Development or Appendix B: Application Requirements – Minor Development, whichever is applicable, is to be provided, with associated costing to make the building safe. Following this, an Estimated Cost of Development Report in accordance with Appendix A: Application Requirements – Other Development or Appendix B: Application Requirements – Minor Development, whichever is applicable, is to be provided for sympathetic alterations and additions. |

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
|  | If demolition is the only option after this is undertaken, a SoHI must verify that all alternative options to demolition have been fully investigated, and demonstrate the replacement building’s compatibility with the physical context. The SoHI must include details of the: a. Structural condition. b. Overall extent of the remaining fabric. c. Potential retention and adaptive reuse. d. Comparative costings (i.e. retention, reuse, adaption costings), e. All other requirements for a SoHI under Appendix A: Application Requirements – Other Development or Appendix B: Application Requirements – Minor Development, whichever is applicable. |
| PC.2 | Later alterations and additions that detract from the contribution of a building to the character and significance of the HCA area may be removed. |
| • O.44 |  |
| • O.45 |  |
| AS.1 • O.45 | Non-contributory buildings and/or later additions to contributory buildings may be demolished and replaced with buildings or elements that complement the character of the HCA. |
| PC.3 • O.45 | The demolition of severely deteriorated sheds, garages, and outbuildings will be considered where the building or structure has no heritage value and the replacement building is of a suitable design. |
