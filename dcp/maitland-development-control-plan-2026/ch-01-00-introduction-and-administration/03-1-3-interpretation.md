### 1.3. Interpretation

This DCP adopts the terms and definitions of the Standard Instrument – Principal Environmental Plan, unless otherwise defined in the DCP. A reference to any Australian Standard, legislation, or supporting documentation includes a reference to any amendment or replacement as made.
