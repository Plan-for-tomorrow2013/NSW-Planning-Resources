### 1.5. Aims of this Plan

The Maitland Development Control Plan 2026 aims to:

1\. Ensure that development is economically, socially and environmentally sustainable.

2\. Ensure that development positively responds and contributes to the site and its context.

3\. Facilitate development consistent with the established vision, adopted plans, strategies and policies

including Council’s Local Strategic Planning Statement (LSPS).
