### 1.10. Relationship to Other Plans, Policies and Legislation

This plan was prepared in accordance with the provision of the EP&A Act 1979 and the Environmental Planning and Assessment Regulation 2021 (EP&A Regulation 2021). This DCP is to be read and applied in conjunction with the MLEP 2011 and the following:

• Environmental Planning & Assessment Act 1979

• Environmental Planning & Assessment Regulation 2021

• Any relevant SEPP

• Local Government Act 1993

• National Construction Code (NCC)

• Relevant Australian Standards

• Land and Environment Court Planning Principles

• Maitland City Council’s Manual of Engineering Standards (MOES)

• Any other relevant documents endorsed by MCC such as strategies, guidelines, plans, technical

manuals, studies, standard drawings, or the like.
