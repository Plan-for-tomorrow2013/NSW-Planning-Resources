### 1.1. Adoption and Commencement

The Maitland Development Control Plan 2026 was adopted by Council on 17 March 2026 and came into effect on 14 April 2026.

To assist with managing the Development Application process during the transition between the Maitland Development Control Plan 2011 and the Maitland Development Control Plan 2026, the following Savings and Transitional Arrangements are in place:

Any Development Application lodged before the Maitland Development Control Plan 2026 came into effect on 14 April 2026 must be determined as if the Maitland Development Control Plan 2026 had not been commenced.
