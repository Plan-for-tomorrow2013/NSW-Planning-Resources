### 1.2. Disclaimer

The contents of this Development Control Plan (DCP) are subject to periodic review and change. Applicants must ensure that they have obtained the latest version. The DCP is not necessarily an exhaustive list of requirements for a particular development application. Pre-application discussion with Council staff is essential to ensure all matters are considered. Council will accept no responsibility for reader interpretation of this DCP. Applicants should consult with Council staff to ensure the relevant parts of the DCP have been addressed and are understood.
