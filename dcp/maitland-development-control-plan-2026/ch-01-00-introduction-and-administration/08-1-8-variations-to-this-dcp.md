### 1.8. Variations to this DCP

If an alternative is proposed to a Prescriptive Control or Acceptable Solution (defined in Section 1.7), then the Objectives must be adequately addressed to the satisfaction of Council. Variations may be considered where a proposed variation meets the relevant objectives and results in a same or better outcome than would have been achieved by complying with the PC/AS. Any application seeking a variation to a control must be in writing and should:

1\. Identify the specific PC/AS which is proposed to be varied, and the extent of the proposed

variation,

2\. Outline the reasons for the variation to the PC/AS and how it satisfies the applicable

objectives, including through imagery, plans, and specialist assessment where necessary, and

3\. Identify any potential adverse impacts of the proposed variation on the proposed

development, surrounding properties, and how these potential impacts have been mitigated.

This variation should be justified in the Statement of Environmental Effects accompanying the development application.
