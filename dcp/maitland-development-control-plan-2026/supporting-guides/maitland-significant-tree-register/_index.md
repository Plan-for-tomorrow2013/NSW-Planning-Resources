### Maitland Significant Tree Register

Last updated: 27th March 2026

Version: 1.0

This register provides a list of identified Significant Trees within the Maitland Local Government Area. The register supports Section 2.3. Vegetation Management of the Maitland Development Control Plan 2026.

Trees within this register require a Council-issued Tree Permit if not present within a Heritage Conservation or a Heritage Item for removal. If they are present within a Heritage Conservation Area

or are part of a Heritage Item a Development Application is required to be lodged for their removal.
