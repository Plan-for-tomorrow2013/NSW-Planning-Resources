## 3. Canopy Cover Targets by Land Use

The following tables set out recommended canopy cover targets across key land types, including

streets, parks, residential lots, and business parks. They are advisory only and designed to guide

design processes for applicants. Specific controls related to tree provision are found throughout the Maitland DCP. These targets are to be read alongside the accompanying technical guidance, including planting specifications provided within Council’s MoES, the Technical Guideline – Street Trees and Standard Drawings. Additional reference is to also be made to Council’s Tree Species List, which provides details as to canopy areas and sizes.

#### Table 1: Public Land - Roads

| STREET CATEGORY | POWER INFRASTRUCTURE | TYPICAL RESERVE WIDTH | CANOPY COVER TARGET |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existing Residential | Overhead Underground | 12–30m 12–30m | 40% 50% |
| Existing Industrial | Overhead Underground | 20–25m 20–25m | 35% 45% |
| New Residential | Underground | 12–35m | 50% |
| New Industrial | Underground | 20–35m | 50% |

#### Table 2: Public Open Space (Community Land)

| PARK TYPE | DESCRIPTION | CANOPY COVER TARGET |
|---|---|---|
| Without Active Use E.g. Reserve | - | ≥ 45% of total site area |
