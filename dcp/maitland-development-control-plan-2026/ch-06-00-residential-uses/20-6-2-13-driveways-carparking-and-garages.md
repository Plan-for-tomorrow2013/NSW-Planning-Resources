#### 6.2.13. Driveways, carparking and garages

This section is to be read in conjunction with Chapter 3: Site Requirements, Section 3.4.4. Parking Provision for parking rate requirements.

#### Objectives

O.43 To provide convenient, accessible and safe parking to meet the needs of residents and visitors in a manner which does not dominate the streetscape or cause congestion in nearby streets.

O.44 To encourage the proper integration of driveway access, parking and landscaped areas as part of the overall landscape design.

O.45 To provide garage designs that are complementary to dwellings and surrounding character. O.46 To ensure driveways are located outside of areas prohibited by Council’s MoES. O.47 To improve canopy cover outcomes within public street reserves.
