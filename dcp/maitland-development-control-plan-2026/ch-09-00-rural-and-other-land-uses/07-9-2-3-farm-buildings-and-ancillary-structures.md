#### 9.2.3. Farm Buildings and Ancillary Structures

#### Objectives

O.8 To minimise the visual impacts on rural and scenic landscape character of Maitland LGA. O.9 To ensure that farm buildings & ancillary structures are of a size, height & bulk that is suited to the site area.

O.10 To ensure that farm buildings are located to have minimum adverse impact on the environment and on the potential use of the land for agriculture.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.8 | All farm buildings must be ancillary to an agricultural use being undertaken on the land on which it is situated. |
| AS.1 • O.10 | Farm buildings should be sited in clusters in order to minimise the amount of land occupied by development. Adequate evidence to Council’s satisfaction is to be provided regarding operational need for any farm building provided away from existing buildings. |
| PC.2 | The minimum setback for farm buildings from any road is 20 metres. |
| • O.10 |  |
| PC.3 | The minimum side and rear boundary setback for farm buildings is 10 metres. |
| • O.10 |  |
| PC.4 | Cut and fill must be kept to a minimum and slope of batter is not to exceed 10%. The toe of the batter is to not be within 10 metres of the setback. |
