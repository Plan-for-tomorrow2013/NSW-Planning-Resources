### 9.1. Introduction

#### 9.1.1. Application

Section 9.2 of this Chapter primarily relates to land uses within the rural context, being the RU1 Primary Production zone and RU2 Rural Landscape zone. Section 9.3 specifically relates to the land uses of farm stay accommodation and eco-tourist facilities. Section 9.4 generally applies to all land to which the named land uses are permissible with consent under the Maitland Local Environmental Plan (MLEP) 2011.
