#### 9.4.5. Places of Public Worship

#### Objectives

O.48 Ensure that places of public worship have a scale and intensity that is suitable to the site and consistent with the neighbourhood character.

O.49 To ensure that the bulk, scale and character of places of public worship are compatible with development of the surrounding area.

O.50 To minimise the impact of parking on local streets. O.51 To encourage places of public worship to locate in areas with good public transport.
