## 39. PHOTOMONTAGE

Photomontages are to show the key contextual streetscape and neighbourhood settings of the proposed development and other relevant images, such as impacts on critical/sensitive views from both the public and private domain.

> Words printed on the picture (title and legend, copied from the PDF):
> • Hazard analysis
> • Consequence analysis
> • Frequency analysis
> • Risk assessment and reduction
