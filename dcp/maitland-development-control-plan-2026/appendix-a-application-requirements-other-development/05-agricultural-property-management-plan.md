## 5. AGRICULTURAL PROPERTY MANAGEMENT PLAN

A plan prepared for agricultural uses that includes the following information:

a. Existing development within the property boundary.

b. Any machinery to be used during the use of the land for the proposed agricultural purpose.

c. Any handling, processing, and storage facilities.

d. Any internal fencing.

e. Adequacy of road access to and within the lot.

f. The location of any existing and/or proposed dwelling.

g. The economic impact and any benefits of the proposal.

h. Annual water demand for the proposed use, the preferred water supply, and any alternative

i. Irrigation system and how any impact on waterways and ground water will be managed.

j. Measures proposed to mitigate adverse impacts on ground water aquifers.

k. Any proposed drainage works and measures to mitigate pollutants entering waterways and/or

l. The impact of bushfire, flooding and any other hazards.

m. The location of any ground water, dam, river, creek, or other water source.

n. Potential impact on ground water levels and water quality and methods to mitigate impacts.

o. Details of potential odours, vibration and noise.

p. Possible exclusion zones around chemical or flammable liquid storage.

q. Safety measures for spill containment and backup provisions.
