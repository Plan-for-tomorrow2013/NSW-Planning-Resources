## 41. REMEDIATION ACTION PLAN (RAP)

For developments on sites that require contaminated land to be remediated. A report which details a strategy to remediate contaminated land in accordance with State Environmental Planning Policy (Resilience and Hazards) 2021 and Council’s Contaminated Lands Policy.
