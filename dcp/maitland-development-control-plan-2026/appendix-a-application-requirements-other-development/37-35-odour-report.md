## 35. ODOUR REPORT

For developments on sites in areas with odour producing sources including poultry farms, piggeries and horticulture. A report which assesses the odour impact to the development in accordance with the Technical Notes and Technical Framework - Assessment and Management of Odour from Stationary Sources in NSW.
