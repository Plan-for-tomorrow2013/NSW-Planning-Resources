## 26. EROSION AND SEDIMENT CONTROL PLAN (ESCP)

The following additional information should be provided for large development sites:

> Words printed on the picture (title and legend, copied from the PDF):
> • Evacuation route and emergency relief.
> • Area of highest ground where it is safe to move vehicles and/or equipment.
> • Hazardous substances management.
> • Power, water and gas.
> • Emergency kits.
