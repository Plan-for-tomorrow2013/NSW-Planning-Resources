## 42. RIPARIAN LAND ASSESSMENT

A Riparian Land Assessment is to be prepared by a suitably qualified and experienced person and assess the following matters:

a. Impacts on water quality and stream flow

b. Impacts on aquatic and riparian species, habitats and ecosystems

c. Impacts on stream bed and stream bank stability

d. Impacts on Key Fish Habitat, including the free passage of fish and other aquatic organisms

e. Any required future rehabilitation of the watercourse and riparian areas

f. Impacts on water extraction from the watercourse

g. Proposed measures to avoid, minimise or mitigate the above impacts.
