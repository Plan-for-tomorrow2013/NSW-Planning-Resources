## 55. STRUCTURAL ASSESSMENT REPORT

A structural assessment report is a document that evaluates a building or structure's condition and safety. The report is to be prepared by a qualified structural engineer who examines the building's structural elements, including the foundation, walls, roof, and floors. The report also includes the building's history, such as any past renovations. A structural assessment report's purpose is to identify any potential safety hazards or structural deficiencies.
