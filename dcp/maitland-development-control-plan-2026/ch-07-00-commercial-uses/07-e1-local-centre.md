#### E1 Local Centre

Aberglasslyn, Gillieston Heights, Lochinvar, Lawes Street, Lorn, Metford, Morpeth, Rutherford, Telarah, Tenambit, Thornton, Woodberry The E1 Local Centre Zone is intended to provide a range of retail, business and community uses that serve the needs of people who live, work or visit the area.
