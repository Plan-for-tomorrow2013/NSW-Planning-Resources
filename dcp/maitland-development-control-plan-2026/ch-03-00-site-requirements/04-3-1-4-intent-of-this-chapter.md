#### 3.1.4. Intent of this Chapter

The intent of this DCP Chapter is to:

• To provide a set of controls and acceptable solutions that guide general items that need to be considered across the construction and operation of all development.

• To ensure any potential adverse impacts on the environment and adjoining properties is adequately managed.

• To help integrate sustainable practices into development by establishing principles around aspects of the circular economy.

• To ensure that development considers existing and future infrastructure provision in cases where there is an intensification of use.

• To assist in making the Maitland LGA be more resilient to adverse impacts from new development.
