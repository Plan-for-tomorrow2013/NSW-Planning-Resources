#### 3.3.3. Erosion and Sediment Control

#### Objectives

O.15 To prevent erosion and sediment laden run off to occur during site preparation, construction, and operation of any use of land.

O.16 To prevent the pollution of waters from unmanaged and untreated runoff. O.17 To ensure that a ‘treatment train’ approach is an integral part of design solutions for controlling stormwater runoff.

O.18 To ensure adequate vegetation cover is achieved across the site.

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

#### PC.1

• O.15

All erosion and sediment (ERSED) measures proposed are to be prepared in accordance with the ‘Blue Book’ (Managing Urban Stormwater Soils and Construction) – by Landcom, Fourth Edition (2004)’.

• O.16
