#### 10.4.5.39. Residential Densities

#### Objectives

O.249 To encourage higher density living around transport, open space and service nodes.

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

| AS.1 | Any dual occupancy, medium density or integrated housing developments within the |
|---|---|
| • O.249 | precinct are encouraged to be located and designed around areas of high amenity, being sites adjacent to open space, water bodies and bus routes. |
