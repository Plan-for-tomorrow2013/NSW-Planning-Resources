#### 10.4.4.5. Amelioration of Natural and Environmental Hazards

#### Objectives

O.159 Amelioration of natural and environmental hazards, including bushﬁre, ﬂooding and site

contamination and, in relation to natural hazards, the safe occupation of, and the evacuation from, any land so affected is achieved.
