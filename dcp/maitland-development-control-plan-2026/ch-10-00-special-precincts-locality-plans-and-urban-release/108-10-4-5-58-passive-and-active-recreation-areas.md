#### 10.4.5.58. Passive and Active Recreation Areas

#### Objectives

O.356 Neighbourhoods must be conveniently located near open space areas that offer a range of

recreational opportunities for residents, accessible within walking distance from each residence.

O.357 To provide a safe and appropriate level of pedestrian and cycleway access linking new development

with established urban areas, parks and public transport, including a mix of on-road and off-road cycle routes.

O.358 To formalise connections and passive open space as provided for under the Thornton North

Contributions Plan.
