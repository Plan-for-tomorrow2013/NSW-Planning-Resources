#### 10.4.8.3. Overall Landscaping Strategy

#### Objectives

O.408 To soften the visual impact of all built elements, creating attractive and consistent streetscapes

when viewed by passing traffic and pedestrians.

O.409 To ensure key environmental areas such as waterways, vegetation, land resources, and areas of

cultural signiﬁcance and scenic value are protected.

O.410 To provide landscaping appropriate to the nature and scale of development that enhances the local

character and streetscape, supports retention and regeneration of ecological corridors and provides visual interest and a suitable backdrop to the built form.


#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS PC.1

Each Development Application is to include an overall landscaping strategy that meets the requirements for the protection and enhancement of the public and private realm. This includes, but is not limited to, riparian areas, remnant vegetation and noise sensitive areas. A series of residential neighbourhoods are to be designed throughout the Farley URA to create a sense of identity, through distinct landscape and built form elements.

#### PC.2 PC.3

The overall landscaping strategy must be to be based on the visual management principles identified in the Visual Impact Assessment prepared by ADW Johnson (which informed the rezoning of the Farley URA).
