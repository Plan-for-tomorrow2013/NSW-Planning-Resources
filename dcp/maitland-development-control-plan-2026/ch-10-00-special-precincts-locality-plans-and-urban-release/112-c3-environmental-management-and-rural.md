#### C3 Environmental Management and Rural Landscape Zoned Land Objectives

O.367  To ensure that detailed urban design controls are provided for signiﬁcant development sites.

O.368 To provide for the management and enhancement of vegetation, habitat and associated fauna.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.367 | A perimeter road (with development on one side only) is to be provided around the edge of the precinct where it adjoins C3 Environmental Management or RU2 zoned land. |
| PC.2 • O.367 • O.368 | Batters for perimeter roads adjoining the C3 Environmental Management zoned land are to be contained entirely within the residential zoned portion of the land. Additional verge width may be required to accommodate grades in such circumstances. |
| PC.3 | Retaining walls are not permitted within road reserve. |
| • O.367 |  |
| PC.4 • O.367 • O.368 | A vegetation management plan is to be developed by a suitably qualified person for any applicable C3 Environmental Management zone affected by development, detailing maintenance and enhancement of the existing vegetation community on site prior to the issue of a Subdivision Works Certificate or Construction Certificate. Such a plan is to incorporate mechanisms to support and improve the squirrel glider population of the area in association with any use of the land. |
| PC.5 • O.367 • O.368 | Subdivision design is to ensure that Asset Protection Zones (APZs) are contained wholly within the boundaries of residential allotments (and perimeter roads), and do not extend into the C3 Environmental Management zone where clearing would be required. |
