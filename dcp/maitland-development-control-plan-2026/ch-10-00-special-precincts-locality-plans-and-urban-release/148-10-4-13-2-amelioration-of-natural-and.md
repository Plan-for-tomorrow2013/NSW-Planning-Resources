#### 10.4.13.2. Amelioration of Natural and Environmental Hazards

#### Objectives

O.446 To minimise the ﬂood risk to life and property.

O.447 To minimize the visual impacts on the State signiﬁcance heritage item Anambah House and its rural

landscape.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 | Each allotment to be created by subdivision is to provide road access that is trafficable in a flood. |
| • O.446 |  |
| PC.2 | Flood free land is to be provided for each building envelope. |
| • O.446 |  |
| PC.3 • O.447 | For residential properties immediately adjoining the C4 Environmental Living zone, rear boundary fencing is to be of an open style, rural type fencing, constructed of post and wire or post and rail or similar. |
