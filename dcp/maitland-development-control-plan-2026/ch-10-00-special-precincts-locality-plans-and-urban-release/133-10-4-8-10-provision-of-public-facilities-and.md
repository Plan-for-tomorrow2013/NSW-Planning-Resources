#### 10.4.8.10. Provision of Public Facilities and Services

#### Objectives

O.422 To ensure that suitably located public facilities and services are provided, including provision for

appropriate traffic management facilities and parking.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 | Provision of community facilities and open space areas will be in accordance with the Farley Contributions Plan 2015. |
| • O.422 |  |
| PC.2 • O.422 | Development Applications are to consider the proximity of the nominated community facilities and recreation areas identified within this DCP chapter and the Farley Contributions Plan 2015 when designing future subdivisions layouts and movement linkages to adjoining sites. |
| PC.3 • O.422 | Medium and Major Subdivision Development Applications are to include suitably located public facilities and services, including provision for appropriate traffic management facilities and parking. |
