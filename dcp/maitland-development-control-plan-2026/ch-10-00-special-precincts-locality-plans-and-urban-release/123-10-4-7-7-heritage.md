#### 10.4.7.7. Heritage

#### Objectives

O.396 To ensure that heritage items, buildings with heritage signiﬁcance and Heritage Conservation Areas

are protected.

O.397 To ensure that matters relating to Aboriginal heritage are adequately assessed and, where

necessary, protected.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.397 | Development Applications for Medium and Major Subdivisions are to be supported by appropriate Aboriginal heritage assessment in the form of an Aboriginal Heritage Due Diligence Assessment or, if necessary, an Aboriginal Cultural Heritage Assessments (ACHA) to determine the presence and locations of any Aboriginal artefacts or sites of significance, including methods for providing any necessary buffers within the site. When preparing an ACHA, reference is to be made to the recommendations of the Aboriginal Heritage Assessment and Management Plan by Mary Dallas Consulting Archaeologist Pty Ltd (August 2010), with specific attention paid to any Potential Archaeological Deposits identified in that assessment. Note: Other development may require an Aboriginal Heritage Due Diligence Assessment and/or ACHA to be prepared, subject to relevant provisions under the National Parks and Wildlife Act 1974. |
| AS.1 • O.397 | In cases where development has triggered PC.1, Development Applications are to include documented evidence of consultation with Local Aboriginal Land Councils and relevant government agencies. |
| PC.2 • O.396 | Development Applications are to consider Heritage Items identified in the Lochinvar Structure Plan, and the visual significance of these items. Identified items include (but are not limited to): • Victoria House, Cantwell Road, • Police Station, • St Helena Cottage, • Clifton, Station Lane, and • Holy Trinity Church, • Government railway. • Catholic Cemetery, |
| PC.3 • O.396 | Where required due to proximity to a Heritage Item or as required by Heritage NSW, any Statement of Heritage Impact prepared for development is to incorporate an assessment of curtilage (including a map showing curtilage) for identified items and must include recommendations for the recognition and protection of any other items, should any be exposed as a result of further planning or construction processes. |
