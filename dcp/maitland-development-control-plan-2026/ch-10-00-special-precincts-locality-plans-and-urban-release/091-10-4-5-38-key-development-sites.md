#### 10.4.5.38. Key Development Sites

#### Flood Fringe/Rural and Environmental Zoned Land Interface Objectives

O.248 To ensure a detailed urban design controls are provided for signiﬁcant development sites.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.248 | A perimeter road (with development on one side only) is to be provided around the edge of the precinct where it adjoins flood prone land or rural land. |
| PC.2 • O.248 | A 3m wide off-road shared pedestrian/cycle path is to be provided on the lower side of the perimeter road to create a continuous pathway linking with existing and proposed networks and public open space. |
| PC.3 • O.248 | Additional landscaping to the perimeter of development fronting the floodplain/rural land and to the proposed water management basins are to be provided to filter views of the new development across the floodplain. Details are to be provided in a landscape plan with any relevant development applications. |
| PC.4 • O.248 | Any fencing of allotments forward of the front building line is to be of an open style of consistent design, material and colour so as to not dominate the landscape and minimise visual impact. |

#### Clay Quarry Site Interface

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.5 • O.248 | An impact assessment is to be undertaken by a suitably qualified consultant to assess the impact of the quarry and associated activities on the precinct. Any mitigation measures necessary to address noise, vibration and dust impacts on the precinct must be incorporated in any development application and apply to affected development. |
