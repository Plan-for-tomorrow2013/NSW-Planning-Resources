#### 10.4.17.10. Key Development Sites

#### Objectives

O.610 To ensure that key development sites are properly planned for within Precinct Plans.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.610 | The following key development sites are to be included and appropriately addressed in the relevant precinct plans which are to include concept designs, site plans and development guidelines: • Commercial/shopping precinct and Community facilities • Windella Estate surrounds • C4 Environmental Living zone • Management of TEC’s located within C4 zoned private land • School • Quarry site buffer (north) • Composting facility buffer (C4 Environmental Living zoned land) • Gateway site (Western Link Road Entrance) • Small lot housing • Recreational Sports Fields |
