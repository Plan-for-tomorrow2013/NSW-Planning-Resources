#### 10.4.7.10. Provision of Public Facilities and Services

#### Objectives

O.401 To ensure that suitably located public facilities and services are provided, including provisioning for

appropriate traffic management facilities and parking.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.401 | Each Development Application for Medium or Major Subdivision is to include suitably located public facilities and services, including provision for appropriate traffic management facilities and parking (see Key Development Sites above), as required by Council. |
| AS.1 • O.401 | Public transport should be addressed in Development Applications, with consideration made for overall network connectivity and access to bus stops and the Lochinvar Railway Station. |
| PC.2 • O.401 | Development Applications that include land in the Southern extent of the Lochinvar URA are to account for future expansion of the Lochinvar Railway Station and the potential for a transport interchange in this part of the site. |
| PC.3 • O.401 | Development Applications are to incorporate indicative road networks (Figure 10-79), stormwater detention areas, active and passive recreation areas and evidence of satisfactory arrangements for essential services. |
| PC.4 | Provision of community facilities and open space areas will be in accordance with the associated Lochinvar Contributions Plan. |
