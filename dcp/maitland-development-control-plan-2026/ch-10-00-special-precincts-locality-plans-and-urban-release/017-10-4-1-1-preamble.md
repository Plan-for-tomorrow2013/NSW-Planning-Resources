### 10.4. Urban Release Areas

#### 10.4.1. General Requirements

#### 10.4.1.1. Preamble

The Maitland Local Housing Strategy (LHS) provides the broad direction for future growth in the Maitland LGA. The LHS identifies a number of investigation areas for residential expansion, as well as low density residential areas in more constrained localities and areas to support employment growth. The process to develop investigation areas as identified in the LHS is as follows:

• Preparation of a Structure Plan (if not identified as a current Urban Release Area or Investigation

Area)

• Scoping Proposal

• Planning Proposal

• Infrastructure Contributions Plan

• Site-Specific Development Control Plan (this Section)
