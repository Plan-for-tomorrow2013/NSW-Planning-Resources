#### 10.4.5.59. Stormwater and Water Quality

#### Objectives

O.359 To provide for an integrated and sustainable approach to the design and provision of open space

and urban water management.

O.360 To protect and enhance the water quality, water quantity and habitat value of downstream

waterways and environment.

O.361 To prevent erosion and run-off during site preparation, construction and the ongoing use of the land

to minimise cumulative impact on receiving waterways.

O.362 To provide a coordinated stormwater management system for the whole of the precinct.
