#### 10.1.4. Intent of this Chapter

The intent of this DCP Chapter is:

a. To provide controls for the various areas of the Maitland LGA that require additional considerations

for their specific contexts.

b. To ensure that historic Locality Plans maintain their character and appropriately consider site-

specific natural and man-made hazards.

c. To ensure that the Maitland DCP has adequate accommodations for Proponents or Council to

satisfy Part 6 of the MLEP 2011.

d. To create a degree of place-making and character through site-specific sections of the DCP.
