#### 10.4.5.41. Staging Plan

#### Objectives

O.251 To provide for the timely and efficient release of urban land making provision for necessary

infrastructure and sequencing.

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

All development applications for subdivisions are to include a staged construction plan, where the development is intended to be constructed in stages.

#### PC.1

• O.251

#### Objectives

O.252 To achieve a simple and safe movement system for private vehicles, public transport, pedestrians

and cyclists.

O.253 To provide walkable neighbourhoods with convenient access to neighbourhood shops, community

facilities and other services, with less dependence on cars for travel

O.254 To provide for access generally by way of an interconnected network of streets and paths which

facilitate safe, efficient and pleasant walking, cycling and driving.

O.255 To facilitate new development which supports the efficiency of public transport systems, and

provides safe, direct access to the system for residents.
