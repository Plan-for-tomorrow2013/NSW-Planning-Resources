#### 10.4.6.4. Amelioration of Natural and Environmental Hazards

#### Objectives

O.377 Amelioration of natural and environmental hazards, including bushﬁre, ﬂooding and site

contamination and, in relation to natural hazards, the safe occupation of, and the evacuation from, any land so affected is achieved.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 | Constructed subdivision roads are to provide suitable flood free access to residential allotments. |
| • O.377 |  |
| PC.2 | Each lot is to provide development envelopes that enable all habitable floor levels to be constructed to the minimum flood planning level. |
| • O.377 |  |
| PC.3 • O.377 | A bulk earthworks plan is to be submitted with any development application where bulk earthworks are proposed showing the location, extent and tonnage of proposed fill in accordance with Figure 10-71 and Figure 10-72. |
| PC.4 • O.377 | Only ‘virgin excavated natural material’ (VENM), ‘excavated natural material’ (ENM) or waste derived material the subject of a resource recovery exemption (includes ENM) within the meaning of the Protection of the Environment Operations Act or Regulations should be used for the purposes of filling of the land. |
| AS.1 • O.377 | Adequate provision should be made for implementation of measures during subdivision construction to ensure that the landform is stabilised, and erosion controlled to prevent sedimentation runoff and protect the Anambah Lagoon. |
| PC.5 | All development applications shall demonstrate compliance with the requirements of the State Environmental Planning Policy (Resilience and Hazards) 2021. |
