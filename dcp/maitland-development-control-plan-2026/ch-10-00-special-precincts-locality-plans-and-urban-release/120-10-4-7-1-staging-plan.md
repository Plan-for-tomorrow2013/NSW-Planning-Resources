#### 10.4.7.1. Staging Plan

#### Objectives

O.379 To provide for the timely and efficient release of urban land making provision for necessary

infrastructure and sequencing.

O.380 To provide for the logical development of the URA based on the cost-effective provision and

availability of infrastructure and servicing arrangements.
