#### 10.4.11.2. Key Development Sites

#### Objectives

O.441 To maintain the established streetscape character of the Mount Harris URA, ensuring generous front

setbacks and cohesive visual amenity.

O.442 To maintain the large-lot, low-density character of the Mount Harris URA by ensuring that new

development respects the precinct’s established character.

O.443 To ensure fencing of dwellings maintains the existing rural residential character.
