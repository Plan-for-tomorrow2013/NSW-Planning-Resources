### 10.1. Introduction

#### 10.1.1. Application

The objectives and controls contained within this Chapter apply to various specific precincts, localities and Urban Release Areas located throughout the Maitland Local Government Area. As such, this Chapter is to be read in conjunction with the general controls and acceptable solutions provided under Chapters 2–9 of this DCP. Where there are inconsistencies between this Chapter and controls under Chapters 2-9, this Chapter will prevail.
