#### 10.4.14.28. Archaeological Heritage

#### Objectives

O.512 Heritage items, buildings with heritage signiﬁcance and Conservation Areas are protected.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.512 | Staff, contractors and construction and maintenance people involved in the development of the site are to be made aware of statutory requirements pertaining to archaeological sites and artefacts. |
| PC.2 | If site LP1 and/or LP2 will be impacted by construction works a Section 90 permit under the National Parks and Wildlife Act 1974 (CTD-salvage) is required prior to any works. |
| • O.512 |  |
| PC.4 • O.512 | If PAD’s 1, 2 or 3 will be impacted by any construction works a Section 87 (test excavation) is required prior to any works. |
