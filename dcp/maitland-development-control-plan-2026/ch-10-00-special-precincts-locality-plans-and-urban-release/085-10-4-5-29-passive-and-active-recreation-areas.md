#### 10.4.5.29. Passive and Active Recreation Areas

#### Objectives

O.227 Neighbourhoods are conveniently located open space areas that offer a range of recreational

opportunities for residents, accessible within walking distance from each residence.

O.228 To provide a safe and appropriate level of pedestrian and cycleway access linking new development

with established urban areas, parks and public transport, including a mix of on-road and off-road cycle routes.

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

A precinct sportsground at 4.2ha, a precinct playground at 1.8ha and a local playground at 0.5ha are to be provided within this Precinct in accordance with the Thornton North Section 7.11 Contributions Plan and the general locations identified in the Precinct Plan.
