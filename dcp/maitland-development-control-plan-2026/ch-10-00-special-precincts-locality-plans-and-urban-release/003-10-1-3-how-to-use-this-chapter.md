#### 10.1.3. How to use this Chapter

This Chapter is structured into three sections, as follows:

#### Special Precincts

This section seeks to identify areas within the Maitland LGA that are of a non-residential character and require a degree of environmental and built considerations in their immediate context. At present, only two airports have been included, being Maitland Airport and Williamtown Airport. As such, the Special Precincts section is expected to expand over time as Council conducts more detailed work into key infrastructure across the LGA in the future.

#### Locality Plans

The Locality Plans section provides a framework that enables development potential to be realised for a specific residential locality in the Maitland LGA, while also recognising the need to integrate the development of these sites within existing urban areas, or within rural landscapes. As such, they are limited in scope and generally aim to maintain existing character, as a number of them are now fully developed. The Locality Plans are as follows:

| Bolwarra Heights | West Bolwarra Heights |
| Greta (Orient Street) | Thornton (Radburn Estate) |

#### Urban Release Areas

The Urban Release Areas (URA) section of Chapter 10 provides controls for the areas indicated as Urban Release Areas within the Maitland Local Environmental Plan 2011. The content of these each of these URAs address Part 6 of the Maitland Local Environmental Plan 2011 and are required for each area in order for development to occur. They are as follows:
