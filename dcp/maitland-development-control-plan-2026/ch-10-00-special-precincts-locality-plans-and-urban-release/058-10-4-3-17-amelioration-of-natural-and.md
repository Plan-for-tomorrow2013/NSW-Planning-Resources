#### 10.4.3.17. Amelioration of Natural and Environmental Hazards

#### Objectives

O.137 To minimise the potential impact of ﬂooding on development.

O.138 To ensure ﬂood free access to the site.

O.139 To achieve consistency with the aims and objectives of the document titled Planning for Bushﬁre

Protection 2019, which seek to mitigate the impacts of bushﬁre on development.

O.140  To minimise risk to human health and the environment from the development of potentially

contaminated land.

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

Development Applications for subdivision are to provide consideration to the general provisions of Chapter 2 of the Maitland Development Control Plan for flooding.
