#### 10.4.14.25. Stormwater and Water Quality

#### Objectives

O.506 To provide for an integrated and sustainable approach to the design and provision of open space

and urban water management.

O.507 To protect and enhance the water quality, water quantity and habitat value of downstream

waterways and environment.

O.508 To prevent erosion and run-off during site preparation, construction and the ongoing use of the land

to minimise cumulative impact on receiving waterways.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 | Applications for subdivision are to include a Stormwater Management Plan (SWMP) for the precinct consistent with the principles for Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD). |
| • O.506 |  |
| PC.2 | The number and location of WSUD elements is to be determined by modelling to develop the SWMP for the site and be integrated with the overall design. |
| • O.506 |  |
| • O.507 |  |
| PC.3 | Long term maintenance costs are to be identified in the design of the WSUD elements and are to be submitted to Council for consideration prior to acceptance of the SWMP. |
| • O.508 |  |
| AS.1 | Swales may be acceptable where it can be demonstrated that they will meet Council’s performance and maintenance objectives and facilitate safe and effective movement of pedestrians and vehicles. |
| • O.507 |  |
| • O.508 |  |
| PC.4 | Flow control measures are to be used where grades in swales exceed 4%. |
| • O.507 |  |
| • O.508 |  |
| AS.2 • O.508 | Wherever possible, existing natural drainage gullies should form part of a stormwater and runoff drainage system incorporating detention basins and/ or wetlands to alleviate stormwater peak and retain pollutants. |
| AS.3 | Slopes surrounding wetlands should be gentle and offer convenient tractor-mowing access. |
| • O.508 |  |

#### Objectives

O.509 Amelioration of natural and environmental hazards, including bushﬁre, ﬂooding and site

contamination and, in relation to natural hazards, the safe occupation of, and the evacuation from, any land so affected is achieved.

O.510 To ensure that future residential development is not adversely affected by any noise and vibration

from Louth Park and Mount Vincent Road.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.509 | Any subdivision applications may require approval from the NSW Rural Fire Service and applicable measures addressed, such as Asset Protection Zones (APZ), in accordance with the NSW RFS Planning for Bushfire Protection 2019 guidelines. |
| PC.2 | Building envelopes are not to encroach upon the identified 40m Bushfire Protection Area as detailed in the southern precinct plan in Figure 10-114. |
| • O.509 |  |
| PC.3 • O.510 | Subdivision design and lot layout must identify and ensure that any future residential lots and housing will not be adversely affected by noise or vibration from traffic along Louth Park Road or Mount Vincent Road. |
| PC.4 | Development involving new dwellings are to undertake a noise and vibration assessment report to determine any impacts due to the nearby mining activities at Bloomfield Colliery. |
| • O.510 |  |
| PC.5 • O.510 | New dwellings are to be setback a minimum of 15m from Louth Park Road and 50m from Mount Vincent Road, and a landscaping buffer 5m wide (which may include existing vegetation) is to be provided adjacent to both roads to physically separate passing traffic. |
| PC.6 • O.509 • O.510 | The Southern Precinct is partly affected by shallow mine workings, and accordingly detailed geotechnical investigations will be required to inform the future subdivision layout, allotment sizes, building design controls and any designated building envelopes. Any development application in the affected areas will require the approval of Subsidence Advisory NSW. |
| PC.7 • O.509 • O.510 | Geotechnical investigations are to include details of the depth of coal seam, height of workings, floor conditions and thickness of competent rock over mine workings to the satisfaction of Subsidence Advisory NSW. At the completion of any geotechnical remediation works, a qualified geotechnical engineer is to certify that the remediation of the mine workings has been achieved. |
| PC.8 | No development over mine shafts or entry tunnels will be supported. Shafts and tunnels are to be filled and capped to comply with guidelines provided by NSW Trade and Investment – Division of Resources and Energy. |
