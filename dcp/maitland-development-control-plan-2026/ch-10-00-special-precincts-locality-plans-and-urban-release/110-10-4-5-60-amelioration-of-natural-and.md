#### 10.4.5.60. Amelioration of Natural and Environmental Hazards

#### Objectives

O.363 Amelioration of natural and environmental hazards, including bushﬁre, ﬂooding and site

contamination and, in relation to natural hazards, the safe occupation of, and the evacuation from, any land so affected is achieved.

O.364 To ensure that future residential development is not adversely affected by any noise and vibration

from road traffic associated with Raymond Terrace Road.
