#### 10.4.5.2. Transport Movement Hierarchy

#### Objectives

O.162 To achieve a simple and safe movement system for private vehicles, public transport, pedestrians

and cyclists.

O.163 To provide walkable neighbourhoods with convenient access to neighbourhood shops, community

facilities and other services, with less dependence on cars for travel

O.164 To provide for access generally by way of an interconnected network of streets and paths which

facilitate safe, efficient and pleasant walking, cycling and driving.

O.165 To facilitate new development which supports the efficiency of public transport systems, and

provides safe, direct access to the system for residents.
