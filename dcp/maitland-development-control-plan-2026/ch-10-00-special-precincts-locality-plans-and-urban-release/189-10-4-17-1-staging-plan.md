#### 10.4.17.1. Staging Plan

#### Objectives

O.565 To provide for the timely and efficient release of urban land, ensuring necessary infrastructure is in

place.

O.566 To ensure that infrastructure is sequenced and provided in a logical and cost-effective manner.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.565 • O.566 | Development should be generally in accordance with the Staging Plan in Figure 10-132. Where development is proposed that is inconsistent with the Staging Plan, detailed justification must be provided demonstrating how the proposed staging maintains orderly and economic development and infrastructure provision of water, sewer, electricity and roads. |
| AS.1 • O.565 • O.566 | Development must demonstrate that the provision and sequencing of essential infrastructure adequately services the proposed development and are strategically designed to support the continued growth and development of the Anambah Urban Release Area. |
