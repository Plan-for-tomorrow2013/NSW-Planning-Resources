#### 10.4.17.13. Staging Plan and Infrastructure Delivery

#### Objectives

O.629 To provide for the timely and orderly efficient release of Precinct 1, ensuring necessary infrastructure

is in place.

O.630 To ensure that infrastructure for Precinct 1 is sequenced and provided in a logical and cost-effective

manner.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| PC.1 • O.629 | Precinct 1 comprises two sub-precincts identified in Figure 10-138. The development of each sub-precinct (and staging within those) must provide essential infrastructure such as utility, road and drainage infrastructure connection. |
| PC.2 | Where development is for the purpose of subdivision that is intended to be constructed and released in stages, a staging plan for delivery is to be provided. |
| • O.630 |  |
| PC.3 • O.630 | Staging and infrastructure delivery is to demonstrate logical and timely connection with adjoining precincts. Applications for subdivision are to clearly show staging and connection points. |
