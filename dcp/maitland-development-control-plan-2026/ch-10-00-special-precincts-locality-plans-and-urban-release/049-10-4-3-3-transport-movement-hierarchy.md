#### 10.4.3.3. Transport Movement Hierarchy

#### Objectives

O.98 To achieve a simple and safe movement system for private vehicles, public transport, pedestrians

and cyclists.

O.99 To provide walkable neighbourhoods with convenient access to neighbourhood shops, community

facilities and other services, with less dependence on cars for travel

O.100 To provide for access generally by way of an interconnected network of streets and paths which

facilitate safe, efficient and pleasant walking, cycling and driving.

O.101 To facilitate new development which supports the efficiency of public transport systems, and

provides safe, direct access to the system for residents.

#### Objectives

O.102 To soften the visual impact of all built elements, creating attractive and consistent streetscapes

when viewed by passing traffic and pedestrians.

O.103 To ensure key environmental areas such as waterways, vegetation, land resources, and areas of

cultural signiﬁcance and scenic value are protected.

O.104 To provide landscaping appropriate to the nature and scale of development that enhances the local

character and streetscape, supports retention and regeneration of ecological corridors and provides visual interest and a suitable backdrop to the built form.
