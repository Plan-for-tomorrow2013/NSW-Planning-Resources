#### 10.4.8.1. Staging Plan

#### Objectives

O.402 To provide for the timely and efficient release of urban land making provision for necessary

infrastructure and sequencing.

O.403 To provide for the logical development of the URA based on the cost-effective provision and

availability of infrastructure and servicing arrangements.

| PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS |  |
|---|---|
| AS.1 | Staging of the urban release area should be generally in accordance with Figure 10-86. |
| • O.402 |  |
| PC.1 | The Farley URA Staging Plan is to be read in conjunction with the relevant Contributions Plan applying to land within the Farley URA. |
| • O.403 |  |
| PC.2 • O.403 | Development Applications for subdivision are to incorporate road networks, stormwater detention areas, active and passive recreation areas, consistent with the overall staging and intended development outcomes for the Farley URA. |
| PC.3 • O.403 | Land is to be developed in such a way that allows for walkable distances of up to 400m to a bus route, pedestrian network and local park to promote the establishment of sustainable communities. . |
