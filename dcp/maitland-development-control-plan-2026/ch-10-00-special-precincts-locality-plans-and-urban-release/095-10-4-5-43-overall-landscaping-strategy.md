#### 10.4.5.43. Overall Landscaping Strategy

#### Objectives

O.256 To soften the visual impact of all built elements, creating attractive and consistent streetscapes

when viewed by passing traffic and pedestrians.

O.257 To establish an attractive visual appearance to the development by street tree planting and

providing additional landscaping in public areas.

O.258 To ensure key environmental areas such as waterways, vegetation, land resources, and areas of

cultural signiﬁcance and scenic value are protected.

O.259 To provide landscaping appropriate to the nature and scale of development that enhances the local

character and streetscape, supports retention and regeneration of ecological corridors and provides visual interest and a suitable backdrop to the built form.

O.260 To provide landscaped buffers around the site perimeter adjoining major roads.

O.261 To provide a quality landscaped gateway to this part of the Thornton North Release Area.

O.262 To retain appropriate riparian corridors and integrate them with open space.
