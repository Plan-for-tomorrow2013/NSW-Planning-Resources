### 10.2. Special Precincts

This section seeks to identify areas within the Maitland LGA that are of a non-residential character and require a degree of environmental and built considerations in their immediate context. At present, only two airports have been included, being Maitland Airport and Williamtown Airport. As such, the Special Precincts section is expected to expand over time as Council conducts more detailed work into

key infrastructure across the LGA in the future.
