#### 10.4.17.7. Passive and Active Recreation Areas

#### Objectives

O.590 To ensure that neighbourhoods are conveniently located close to open space areas that offer a

range of recreational opportunities for residents.

O.591 To provide an equitable distribution of public open space and recreation opportunities.

O.592 To ensure good quality of design and embellishment of the public domain.

O.593 To ensure that passive and active recreation areas are linked through an adequate active transport

network.

O.594 To establish linear parks within riparian corridors that provide connected, high-quality passive

recreation opportunities integrated with the natural landscape.

O.595 To ensure that linear parks are designed and landscaped to enhance biodiversity, support ﬂood

resilience, and bushﬁre threat management and contribute to tree canopy to complement their public purposes for active movement, open space and recreation.
