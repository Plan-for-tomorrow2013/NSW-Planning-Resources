#### 10.4.2.13. Stormwater and Water Quality

#### Objectives

O.61 To provide for an integrated and sustainable approach to the design and provision of open space

and urban water management.

O.62 To protect and enhance the water quality, water quantity and habitat value of downstream

waterways and environment.

O.63 To prevent erosion and run-off during site preparation, construction and the ongoing use of the land

to minimise cumulative impact on receiving waterways.
