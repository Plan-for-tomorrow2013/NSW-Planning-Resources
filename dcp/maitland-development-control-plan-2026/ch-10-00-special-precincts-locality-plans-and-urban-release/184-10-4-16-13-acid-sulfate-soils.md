#### 10.4.16.13. Acid Sulfate Soils

#### Objectives

O.560 To manage and mitigate the impacts of land development in relation to acid sulfate soils, where

present in the landscape.

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

Development Applications will need to investigate soil salinity levels, soil structure/stability and Acid Sulfate Soils as part of geotechnical investigations associated with the site.
