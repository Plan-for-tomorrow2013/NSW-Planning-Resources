#### 10.4.5.51. Passive and Active Recreation Areas

#### Objectives

O.285 To establish the neighbourhood centre as the focal point for the community.

> Words printed on the picture (title and legend, copied from the PDF):
> c. Outdoor dining,

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

An urban design masterplan is to be submitted with the development at Stage 1 of centre. The masterplan is to provide opportunities for:

#### PC.1

a. An informal meeting place that can be used by local residents and parents of children

attending nearby local schools and child care centres,

b. Formal and informal outdoor seating areas,

d. A space for activities which support the local community such as charity fundraising

stalls, donation tables etc, and

e. Landscaping and design features which encourages use and activity throughout the

day and into the evening, including safe areas for children to play whilst being supervised from adjacent outdoor seating areas.
