#### 10.4.2.19. Passive and Active Recreation Areas

#### Objectives

O.75 Neighbourhoods are conveniently located open space areas that offer a range of recreational

opportunities for residents, accessible within walking distance from each residence.

O.76 To provide a safe and appropriate level of pedestrian and cycleway access linking new development

with established urban areas, parks and public transport, including a mix of on-road and off-road cycle routes.

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

Passive and active recreational space shall be provided generally in accordance with Figure 10-21.
