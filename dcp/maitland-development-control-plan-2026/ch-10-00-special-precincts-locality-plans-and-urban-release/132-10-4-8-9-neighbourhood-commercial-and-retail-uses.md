#### 10.4.8.9. Neighbourhood Commercial and Retail Uses

#### Objectives

O.420 To accommodate and control appropriate neighbourhood commercial and retail uses.

O.421 To foster a sense of community and strong local identity and sense of place in neighbourhoods.

#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS PC.1

• O.420

• O.421

Development Application(s) for a future neighbourhood centre will need to be undertaken in accordance with Chapter 7 Commercial Uses of the Maitland Development Control Plan 2011.
