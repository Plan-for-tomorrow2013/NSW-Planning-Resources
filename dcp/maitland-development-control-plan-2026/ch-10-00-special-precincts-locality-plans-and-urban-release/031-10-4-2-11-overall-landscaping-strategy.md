#### 10.4.2.11. Overall Landscaping Strategy

#### Objectives

O.57 To soften the visual impact of all built elements, creating attractive and consistent streetscapes

when viewed by passing traffic and pedestrians.

O.58 To ensure key environmental areas such as waterways, vegetation, land resources, and areas of

cultural signiﬁcance and scenic value are protected.
