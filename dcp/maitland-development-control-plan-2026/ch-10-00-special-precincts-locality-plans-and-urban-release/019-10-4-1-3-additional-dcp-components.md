#### 10.4.1.3. Additional DCP Components

#### Area Plan

An Area Plan may be required by Council to further coordinate the detailed strategic planning of large or complex sites where site constraints, infrastructure provision, existing development and land ownership patterns result in the identification of distinct precincts. More detailed Precinct Plans may then identify additional specific objectives and requirements for individual areas. Area Plans are not DCPs for the purpose of interpreting Part 6 in the LEP.

#### Precinct Plan

A plan that is prepared to include the matters outlined in Part 6 of the LEP will be interpreted as a DCP for the purposes of Part 6, Section 6.2 and 6.3. In most cases, this plan will be a Precinct Plan. Additional Precinct Plans or other plans that satisfy Part 6 of the LEP will be prepared as development occurs in accordance with the LHS. These Plans will be included in future amendments to the Maitland DCP 2026.
