#### 10.4.5.6. Amelioration of Natural and Environmental Hazards

#### Objectives

O.174 Amelioration of natural and environmental hazards, including bushﬁre, ﬂooding and site

contamination and, in relation to natural hazards, the safe occupation of, and the evacuation from, any land so affected is achieved.

O.175 To ensure that future residential development is not adversely affected by any noise and vibration

from incompatible land uses, including road and rail corridors and extractive industries.


#### PRESCRIPTIVE CONTROLS AND ACCEPTABLE SOLUTIONS

Subdivision design and lot layout must ensure that any future residential housing will not be adversely affected by noise or vibrations, particularly from quarry operations, the railway line and traffic along Raymond Terrace Road and Government Road.
