### 1.14. Statement of Heritage Impact

A Statement of Heritage Impact (SoHI) is required as part of a DA that affects the following:

• a heritage item identified under Schedule 5 of the MLEP 2011

• an item subject to an interim heritage order under the Heritage Act 1977

• external works in the vicinity or view of a heritage item identified under Schedule 5 of the MLEP 2011. A Statement of Heritage Impact (SoHI) may be required as part of a DA that affects the following:

• a property within a heritage conservation area identified under Schedule 5 of the MLEP 2011 The SoHI must be prepared in accordance with the Guidelines for Preparing a Statement of Heritage Impact and include:

• a cover page

• background information about the heritage item or site, including a site description, a summary

history and physical analysis

• an assessment of significance using the seven criteria, including a statement of significance

• an outline of the proposed works

• an impact assessment detailing how the works will impact the heritage significance of the item or

area and the measures implemented to minimise or mitigate any adverse impacts

• a summary of the impacts and recommendations.
