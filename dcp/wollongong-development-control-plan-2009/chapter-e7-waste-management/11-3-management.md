## 3. Management

(a) Garbage chutes are not to be used for the disposal of recyclable materials. Signage to this effect should be displayed near service openings.

(b) Arrangements must be in place for the regular maintenance and cleaning of garbage chutes and any associated service rooms, service openings and charging devices.

(c) Arrangements must be place for the regular transferral of recyclable materials (which are stored in service rooms) to the main waste / recycling storage room.
