## 2. Garbage Chute Service Room Design

(a) The service opening on each floor of the building must be located in a dedicated service room.

(b) The charging device for each service opening must be self-closing and must not project into the main chute.

(c) Each service room must include containers for the storage of recyclable materials. Signage regarding the materials that can be recycled should be displayed near these containers.

(d) Each service room must be located for convenient access by users and must be well ventilated and well lit.

(e) Branches connecting service openings to the main chute are to be no more than 1 metre long.

(f) The floors, walls and ceilings of service rooms must be finished with smooth, durable materials that are capable of being easily cleaned.

(g) Service rooms must include signage that clearly describes the types of materials that can be deposited into the garbage chute and the types of materials which should be deposited into recycling bins.
