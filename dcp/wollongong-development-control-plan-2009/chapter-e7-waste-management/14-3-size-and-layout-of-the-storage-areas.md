## 3. Size and Layout of the Storage Areas

(a) Waste and recycling storage areas must be of adequate size to comfortably accommodate all waste and recycling bins associated with the development.

(b) Waste and recycling storage areas must be able to accommodate separate general waste bins and recycling bins which are of sufficient volume to contain the quantity of waste generated between collections.

(c) The gradient of waste and recycling storage area floors and the gradient of any associated access ramps must be sufficiently level so that access for the purpose emptying containers can occur in accordance with relevant Occupational Health and Safety legislation.

(d) Within waste and recycling storage areas, containers used for the storage of recyclable materials should be kept separate from general waste containers, in order to minimise the potential for contamination of recyclable materials.
