### 3.2 Lilyvale

#### Existing Character

This locality lies mostly within the Royal National Park, with some small holiday/ weekend cottages shacks existing in the area around South Era and Burning Palms.  The village of Lilyvale, adjacent to the railway, is privately owned and contains one dwelling.

#### Desired Future Character

It is not anticipated that the character of this area will change or be subject to any significant new development.
