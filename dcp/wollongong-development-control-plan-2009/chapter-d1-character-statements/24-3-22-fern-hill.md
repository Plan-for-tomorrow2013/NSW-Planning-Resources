### 3.22 Fern Hill

#### Existing Character

Fern Hill is a relatively small and compact residential suburb located to the west of Towradgi and to the east of Balgownie. It has a low density residential character and comprises predominantly of single storey weatherboard and brick dwellings with pitched rooflines.

#### Desired Future Character

Fern Hill will generally remain a low density residential area. Fern Hill is however likely to experience some replacement of older dwelling stock with new two storey dwelling-houses as well as the amalgamation of some lots for medium density villa housing, especially in proximity to the Princes Highway.
