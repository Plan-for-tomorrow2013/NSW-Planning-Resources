## 10 PUBLIC TRANSPORT

### 10.1 General

1\. Public transport access for the local community and tourists will be integrated with the Village Centre to provide linkages to buses, trains and taxis that service the area.

### 10.2 Development Controls

a) Provide bus stops as indicated in Figures 7 and 789.

b) Bus shelters must be located at every bus stop.
