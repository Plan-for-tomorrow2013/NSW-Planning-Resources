## 23 BUILDING ENTRANCES

### 23.1 General

1\. Building entrances provide an interface between the public and private domain. Entrances give a building an identity and define the entry to the street.

### 23.2 Objectives

a) To provide an identifiable and desirable street address to each building.

### 23.3 Development Controls

a) Entrances are to be visible from the street and well lit.

b) Entrances must accommodate the movement of furniture.
