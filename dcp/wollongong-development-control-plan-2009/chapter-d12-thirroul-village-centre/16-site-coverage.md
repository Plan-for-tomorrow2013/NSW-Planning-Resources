## 16 SITE COVERAGE

### 16.1 General

1\. Site coverage controls refer to the percentage of the building footprint in relation to the site area. The remainder of the land should have a minimum percentage of pervious surfaces.

### 16.2 Development Controls

1\. Mixed Use Buildings with Ground Floor Retail

a) The maximum site coverage for mixed-use buildings is 80% of the site.
