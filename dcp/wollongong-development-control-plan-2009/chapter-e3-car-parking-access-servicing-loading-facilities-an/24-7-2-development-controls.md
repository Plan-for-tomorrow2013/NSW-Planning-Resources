### 7.2 Development Controls

1\. Refer to the Stormwater Management chapter contained in Part E of this DCP for stormwater

drainage and on-site stormwater detention requirements for off-street car parking and access areas.

2\. For certain developments, the Water Sensitive Urban Design treatment measures may also be required for car parking and access areas in accordance with the requirements of the Water Sensitive Urban Design chapter in Part E of the DCP.

3\. A Stormwater Management Plan is to be submitted with any Development Application detailing water sensitive urban design and utilisation of landscaped features to disperse, filter and infiltrate car park runoff.
