### 3.9 Emergency Vehicles

#### 3.9.1 Objectives

a) Ensure best practice design and layout of car parking to facilitate access for emergency vehicles, such as fire service, ambulance and police vehicles.

#### 3.9.2 Development Controls

1\. The location of car parking must not impede access for emergency vehicles.

2\. Emergency vehicles must have unimpeded access to water and gas systems.

Note: refer to “Access for fire brigade vehicles and firefighters (2019).
