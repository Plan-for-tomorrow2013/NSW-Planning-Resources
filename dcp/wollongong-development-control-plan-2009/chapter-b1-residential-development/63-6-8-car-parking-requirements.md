### 6.8 Car Parking Requirements

#### 6.8.1 Objectives

(a) To provide an adequate level of on site car parking based upon anticipated occupancy rates.

(b) To ensure that residential developments are designed to be accessible for pedestrians, cyclists and motorists.

(c) To ensure integrated design of car parking facilities to minimise visual impacts.

(d) To provide underground parking, wherever feasible.

(e) To ensure the provision of facilities such as bike racks, which encourage the use of alternative methods of transport.

#### 6.8.2 Development Controls

Refer to E3 Car Parking, Access, Servicing/Loading Facilities and Traffic Management.
