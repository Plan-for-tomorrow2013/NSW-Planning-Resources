## 5 ATTACHED DWELLINGS AND MULTI - DWELLING HOUSING

This section provides additional controls to those in Section 4 (excluding 4.1 to 4.11 and 4.19 to 4.22) of this chapter that must also be taken into consideration when preparing a development application for attached dwellings and multi-dwelling housing.
