### 4.22 Additional Controls for Dual Occupancy’s - Building Character and Form

#### 4.22.1  Development Controls

1\. On corner allotments, the dual occupancy development must address the street on both frontages. The garage and / or carport for each dwelling must be placed on each street frontage, at the furthest point of the site, from the intersection.

2\. Where garages are proposed on the front elevation they must be articulated from the front façade of the dual occupancy dwelling(s).

3\. Any external alterations and additions to a dual occupancy dwelling must be compatible in design, roof configuration and building materials with the other adjoining dwelling in the original dual occupancy development.

4\. Existing garages and outbuildings can not be used as a dual occupancy - second dwelling unless it can be demonstrated that the structure complies with the relevant provisions of the Building Code of Australia.
