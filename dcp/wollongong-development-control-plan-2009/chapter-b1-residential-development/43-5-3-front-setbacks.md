### 5.3 Front Setbacks

#### 5.3.1 Objectives

(a) To reinforce the existing character of the street by acknowledging building setbacks.

(b) To promote compatibility in front setbacks to provide for unity in the building line.

#### 5.3.2 Development Controls

1\. A 6m setback requirements applies from the front property boundary to the front façade of the building.

2\. On corner allotments a minimum setback of 3m to the secondary street frontage from the dwelling façade must be provided.

3\. Balconies, front courtyard fences and other building extrusions may be set back up to 900mm closer than the required front or secondary setback.

4\. An increase in setbacks may be required to retain existing trees or respect adjacent heritage items.
