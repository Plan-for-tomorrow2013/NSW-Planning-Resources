### 8 RELATIONSHIP TO CLAUSE 7.4 OF WOLLONGONG LOCAL ENVIRONMENTAL PLAN 2009

The objective of clause 7.4 Riparian Lands of Wollongong Local Environmental Plan 2009 is to ensure that development does not adversely impact upon riparian lands.  The requirements of this Chapter are intended to ensure that developments meet this objective. Clause 7.4 of Wollongong Local Environmental Plan 2009 applies to “riparian land” shown on the Wollongong Local Environmental Plan 2009 Riparian Land Map (www.wollongong.nsw.gov.au/about/maps). The watercourses mapped as “riparian land” under Wollongong Local Environmental Plan 2009 have a corresponding mapped category under this DCP Chapter as described in section 9.1 below.
