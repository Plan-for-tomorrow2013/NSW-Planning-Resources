## 1 INTRODUCTION

1\. This chapter of Wollongong Development Control Plan (DCP 2009) has been prepared to provide objectives and detailed controls for rural development and rural land uses, in addition to the planning controls contained in Wollongong Local Environmental Plan 2009.

2\. This chapter of the DCP applies to land zoned RU1 Primary Production, RU2 Rural Landscape and RU4 Rural Small Holdings, under Wollongong Local Environmental Plan 2009.

3\. This chapter of the DCP is to be read in conjunction with Part A (Introduction and Character Statements) of the DCP which outlines Council’s general requirements for all developments and provides advice on the lodgement requirements for a Development Application; Part C Specific Landuses; and Part D Locality Based DCP’s/ Precinct Plans; and Part E (General City Wide Planning Controls) which provides the general planning controls pertaining to all developments.
