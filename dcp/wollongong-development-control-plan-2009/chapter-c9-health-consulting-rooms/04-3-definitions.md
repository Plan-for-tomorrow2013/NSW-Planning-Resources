## 3 DEFINITIONS

“Health consulting rooms” are defined as a “medical centre that comprises one or more rooms within (or within the curtileage of) a dwelling-house used by not more than 3 health care professionals who practise in partnership (if there is more than one such professional) who provide professional health care services to members of the public.
