## 5. RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHER PLANS AND POLICIES

This chapter reflects recent developments in federal and state government policies, as well as the strategic direction of Wollongong City Council. This chapter must be read in conjunction with other relevant DCP chapters. ESD principles and objectives are considered in each update of the Wollongong DCP.
