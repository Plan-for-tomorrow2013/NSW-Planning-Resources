## 19 DISABLED PERSONS REQUIREMENTS

#### Objectives

a) To provide best practice in the design of access provisions and facilities for people with a disability.

#### Performance Standards

a) Site and building development must demonstrate compliance with the requirements of the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (DDA), Building Code of Australia and all the referenced Australian Standards.
