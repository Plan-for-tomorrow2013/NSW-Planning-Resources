## 4. Private Open Space Requirements

(a) All private open space is to be designed to ensure maximum useability, eg as entry courts, outdoor living spaces, or for service use as appropriate.

(b) One main open space of no less than 24m2 is required with an average dimension of 4 metres.  This space is not to include clothes drying facilities.  Where it occurs on street frontages, it is to be partly screened by a court wall complementary to the building and/or mature landscape planting.
