## 3 DEFINITIONS

“Bed and Breakfast accommodation” means tourist and visitor accommodation comprising of a dwelling (and any ancillary buildings and parking) where the accommodation is provided by the permanent residents of the dwelling and:

(a) Meals are provided for guests only, and

(b) Cooking facilities for the preparation of meals are not provided within guest’s rooms, and

(c) Dormitory-style accommodation is not provided.
