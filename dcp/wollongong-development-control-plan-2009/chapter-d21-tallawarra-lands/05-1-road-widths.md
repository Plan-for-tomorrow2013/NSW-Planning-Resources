## 1. Road widths

The proposed undergrounding of the (current) above ground HV power lines through the northern precinct and the resulting need to achieve buffer distances to the underground electrical feeders has necessitated a need to vary the road width requirements specified in Chapter B2 of Councils DCP. The proposed controls are specified in 6.7 of this chapter and provide for a slightly wider road pavement and a reduced verge width. A footpath width of 1.5m is maintained together with space for tree planting of 1.5m, which aligns with the MP concept plan approval and provides improved outcomes for future development. This variation is proposed to two (2) of the road types contained in the Northern Precinct.
