## 2. Residential Building Controls

Consistent with the MP 09_0131 approval (as modified), this DCP proposes specific objectives and controls for residential development in the Central and Northern Precincts. These include two- and three-dimensional buildings envelopes for development on future lots adjoining Carlyle Close in the Central Precinct.  These objectives and controls relate to siting, setbacks, stepping of finished floor levels and height, and areas specific to views of the ridgeline and minimising view loss from Carlyle Close. In addition, specific urban design, materials and density controls are specified to future dwelling development in the Northern Precinct, particularly in relation to dwellings oriented to the public domain, Lake Illawarra foreshore, and lots affected by noise contours associated with the EA power station operations.
