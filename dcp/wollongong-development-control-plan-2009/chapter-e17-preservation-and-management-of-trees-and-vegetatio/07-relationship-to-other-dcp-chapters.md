## 7 RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER DCP CHAPTERS

This Chapter should be read in conjunction with Chapter E18: Native Biodiversity Impact Assessment, which relates to biodiversity and includes consideration of impacts to both flora and fauna and their interaction with landscape elements that sustain a diverse ecosystem.
