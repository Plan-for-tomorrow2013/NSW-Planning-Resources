## 1 INTRODUCTION

Trees and vegetation play an important role in the urban landscape, delivering significant long term benefits for our City and community wellbeing. Effective management depends upon appropriately regulated pruning and removal of trees and vegetation, as well as the long term retention, protection and growth of existing vegetation and new plantings.
