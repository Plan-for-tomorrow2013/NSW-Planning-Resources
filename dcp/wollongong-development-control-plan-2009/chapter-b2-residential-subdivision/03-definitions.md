## 3 DEFINITIONS

Corner Allotment Is a lot which has frontage to two roads on adjacent boundaries.

Irregular shaped allotment means an allotment which is not regular in shape.

Regular shaped allotment means either:

(a) Allotment which is either square or rectangular in shape; or

(b) Allotment of another shape where a square or rectangular shape equivalent in area to the

minimum lot size area for the allotment type could be contained within the boundaries of the

allotment and includes a battle-axe shaped allotment and a corner allotment where the only

deviation from the above requirements is the access handle (ie battle axe lot) or the splay corner

(ie corner lot).
