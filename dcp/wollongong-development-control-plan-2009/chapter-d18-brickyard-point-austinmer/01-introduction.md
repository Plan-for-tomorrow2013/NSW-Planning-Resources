## 1 INTRODUCTION

This Locality Chapter is intended to supplement the land use planning controls applied by Part B of the Wollongong Development Control Plan 2009 (DCP) to guide any future development at the site at Brickyard Point – Headlands Hotel, Austinmer, comprising of 9 separate allotments. This Chapter recognises the unique characteristics of the Brickyard Point site, and the ability to deliver a range of development types (excluding residential) in a headland setting. This Chapter reinforces and aims to guide the future development as per the Draft Vision June 2011 – Brickyard Point, Headlands hotel, Austinmer. This chapter of the DCP should be read in conjunction with Part A, B, C, D and E of the Wollongong Development Control Plan 2009 and the relevant Local Environmental Plan. The built form controls in this Chapter supplement, and in some cases prevail over Part B. In the event that the provisions contained in this precinct plan are inconsistent with the provisions of any other part of the DCP, the provisions of this precinct plan shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
