## 2 OBJECTIVES

1\. The objectives of this chapter of the DCP are:

(a) To conserve the environmental heritage of the City in accordance with the principles contained in the Burra Charter;

(b) To consider the potential heritage significance of all properties identified on the Wollongong LEP 2009 Heritage Map and other applications as a matter to be taken into account in the assessment of Development Applications affecting those properties;

(c) To integrate conservation issues and management into the planning and development control process;

(d) To ensure that any development with respect to a heritage site is undertaken in a manner that is sympathetic to, and does not detract from the identified significance of the site;

(e) To encourage innovative approaches to the conservation of Wollongong’s heritage and to provide incentives for good management practice;

(f) To promote Wollongong’s cultural heritage as a valuable resource that must be conserved for future generations.

(g) To encourage and guide recording and interpretation of significant heritage throughout the development process.
