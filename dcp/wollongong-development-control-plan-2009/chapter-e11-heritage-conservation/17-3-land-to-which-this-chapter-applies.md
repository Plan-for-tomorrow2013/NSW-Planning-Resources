## 3 LAND TO WHICH THIS CHAPTER APPLIES

This Chapter of the DCP applies to any land within Wollongong Local Government Area where:

(i) An item of environmental heritage as listed under Schedule 5 of the Wollongong Local Environmental Plan 2009 is contained; or

(ii) The land is located within one of the Heritage Conservation Areas as contained in Schedule 5 of Wollongong Local Environmental Plan 2009; or

(iii) The land is located adjacent to or within the vicinity of a heritage item or heritage conservation area (or within the visual catchment of a heritage site).
