## 1 INTRODUCTION

1\. This chapter of the DCP provides objectives and performance criteria for outdoor restaurants and outdoor trading activities on or over public road reserve areas including footpaths, malls and other public domain areas.

2\. This chapter of the DCP should also be read in conjunction with the relevant LEP applying to the site.
