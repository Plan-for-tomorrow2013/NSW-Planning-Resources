## 3 AIMS AND OBJECTIVES

1\. The key aims and objectives of this precinct plan are:

(a) To reduce the visual impact of the proposed development and maintain the scenic qualities and rural character of Mount Kembla and approaches to it.

(b) To promote a low density and quality of design of residential development which complement the rural setting.
