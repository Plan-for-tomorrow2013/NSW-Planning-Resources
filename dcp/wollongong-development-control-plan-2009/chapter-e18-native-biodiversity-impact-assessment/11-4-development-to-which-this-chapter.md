## 4 DEVELOPMENT TO WHICH THIS CHAPTER APPLIES

This Chapter applies to development, activities and actions that may impact on biodiversity including both development requiring consent under Part 4 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979, and tree/vegetation clearing for which a permit is sought under the NSW State Environmental Planning Policy (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021.  It does not apply to activities under Part 5 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979. This Chapter also provides guidance on biodiversity assessment associated with Planning Proposals under Part 3 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979.
