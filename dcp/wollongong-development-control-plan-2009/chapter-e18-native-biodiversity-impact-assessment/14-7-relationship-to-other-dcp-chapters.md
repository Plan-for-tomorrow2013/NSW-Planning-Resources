## 7 RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER DCP CHAPTERS

This Chapter should be read in conjunction with Chapter E17: Preservation and Management of Trees and Vegetation. Chapter E18 relates to biodiversity and includes consideration of impacts to both flora and fauna and their interaction with landscape elements that sustain a diverse ecosystem, in contrast to Chapter E17, which relates only to the preservation of trees and other vegetation.
