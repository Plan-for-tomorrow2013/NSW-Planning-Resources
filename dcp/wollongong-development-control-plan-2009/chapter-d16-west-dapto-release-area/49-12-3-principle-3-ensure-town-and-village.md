### 12.3 Principle 3 – Ensure Town and Village Centre Employment Outcomes are Prioritised

a) Town and village centres are to ensure planning decisions (such as master plans and spatial arrangements) support and prioritise employment outcomes.

b) Encourage professional service type jobs and roles that are beyond the normal retail type jobs normally expected in new urban release areas.
