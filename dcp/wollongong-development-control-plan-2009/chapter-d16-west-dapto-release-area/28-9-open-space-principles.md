## 9. OPEN SPACE PRINCIPLES

This section establishes the open space principles for the West Dapto Urban Release Area. This section should also be read in conjunction with Community and Education Facilities principles and at a landscape structure level, will contribute to delivering against Council’s Urban Greening Strategy. An overarching framework with four inter-related principles is designed to achieve the open space objectives for the West Dapto Urban Release Area. Open spaces need to be considered places that are designed responding to principles to ensure they provide for a resilient community.

There are more details regarding Council’s specific requirements for each open space facility and subdivision requirements provided in the West Dapto Open Space Design Manual and the West

#### Dapto Open Space Technical Manual.
