### 12.1 Principle 1 – Support Local Sustainable and Accessible Employment

a) Support a variety of employment opportunities accessible to the whole community.

b) Employment containment to reduce commuting out of the release area and region.

c) Local access to higher order (career generating) employment opportunities.

d) Encourage high density employment opportunities within walking distance of existing or proposed public transport services.

e) Encourage employment area developments adjoining the structural road network to take advantage of accessibility and exposure.
