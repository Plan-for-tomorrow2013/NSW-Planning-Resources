### 12.4 Principle 4 – Protect Existing Employment Land

a) Maintain existing zoned employment land within the release area to ensure a supply is maintained over time and is available for employment generating opportunities.

b) Create a strategy to enable appropriate interim uses of employment areas that also allows for gradual intensification over time.

c) Support the preservation of large lots and clusters of light and heavy industrial land and ensure business parks are not located within light industrial zones.
