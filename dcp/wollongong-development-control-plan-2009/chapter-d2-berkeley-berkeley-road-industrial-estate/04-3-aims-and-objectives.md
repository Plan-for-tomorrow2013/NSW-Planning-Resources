## 3 AIMS AND OBJECTIVES

1\. The aims and objectives of this plan are to:

(a) Facilitate industrial development, which has regard to the scenic and environmental attributes of the Berkeley Hills;

(b) Encourage the efficient utilisation of industrial zoned land and to restrict development of land identified for environmental protection purposes;

(c) Provide for an effective transition between the industrial land and existing residential uses along Berkeley Road;

(d) Promote the provision of buffers between potentially conflicting land uses;

(e) Promote the retention of significant trees and vegetation on the site and to encourage the revegetation of cleared land;

(f) Encourage the preservation of the existing riparian environment on the site and to ensure the retention of downstream water quality.
