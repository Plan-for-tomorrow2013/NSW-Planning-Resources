## 7 RIPARIAN CORRIDOR WIDTHS

1\. Any industrial development shall provide minimum 10 metre setback from the top of banks on both sides of the natural watercourse, to ensure that development does not impact upon or alter the existing riparian environment.

2\. The applicant shall consult with the Department of Environment, Climate Change and Water, prior to the submission of a Development Application, to determine whether additional setbacks are required for development within 40 metres of the watercourse.
