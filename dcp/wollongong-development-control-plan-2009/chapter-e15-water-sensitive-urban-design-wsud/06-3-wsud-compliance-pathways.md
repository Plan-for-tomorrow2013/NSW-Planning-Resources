## 3. WSUD COMPLIANCE PATHWAYS

WSUD must be integrated at the Neighbourhood Plan, Development Application (DA), and Construction Certificate (CC) stages. To meet Council’s WSUD requirements, a WSUD Strategy must accompany an applicant’s Water Cycle Management Plan and demonstrate compliance with:

1\) This Chapter (E15 Water Sensitive Urban Design).

2\) Other applicable guidelines and regulations, such as:

i) Council’s WSUD Guidebook for Developers 2025 (as amended or superseded),

ii) Council’s WSUD Engineering Series 2025 (as amended or superseded), and

iii) any other relevant local and state legislation or technical standards.

Notes:

A pre-lodgement meeting with Council is highly recommended at an early stage in the application process to discuss and agree on the overall design approach before a detailed WSUD Strategy is completed. In cases where a pre-lodgement meeting is not held upfront on a proposed WSUD Strategy, or the parameters determined by Council are not met in the WSUD Strategy, Council may either require a major redesign of the WSUD design at the development application stage or may decline to accept the dedication of the WSUD asset to Council. Comprehensive guidance for preparing a compliant Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD) Strategy, along with supplementary resources to support WSUD integration in development planning and development applications, is provided in the latest edition of Council’s WSUD Guidebook for Developers, accessible via Council’s website. All electronic files related to or included as part of a WSUD Strategy must be submitted alongside the corresponding documentation, hereinafter referred to as a ‘WSUD Strategy.’
