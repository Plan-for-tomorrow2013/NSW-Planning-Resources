### 1.2 Development to which this Chapter applies

This Chapter applies to any development requiring development consent under Part 4 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 in the Wollongong Local Government Area (LGA). Applicants must comply with, and address Council’s WSUD requirements, including the Development Controls in this Wollongong DCP Chapter, or other Wollongong DCP Chapters such as A2, E13, E14 and E23.

The Development Controls in this Chapter apply to the following forms of development:

1\) Residential land subdivisions involving 20 or more proposed lots.

2\) Medium density housing involving 20 or more dwellings.

3\) Mixed use apartment buildings involving 20 or more residential apartments, or a gross floor area of 3,000 square metres or more.

4\) Serviced apartments, motels or hotel developments with 20 rooms or more.

5\) Industrial developments including major alterations and additions to existing industrial buildings involving a gross floor area of 4,000 square metres or more.

6\) Industrial subdivisions involving five (5) or more proposed lots, or a total footprint of 4,000 square metres or more.

7\) Development within a Peripheral Sales Precinct involving a total gross floor area of 10,000 square metres or more.

8\) Commercial office developments involving a total gross floor area of 10,000 square metres or more.

9\) All Industrial and business developments adjacent to riparian Category 1 or 2 watercourses.
