### 4.3 Stormwater harvesting, reuse and efficiency

1\) The percentage of proposed roof, or impervious surfaces directed to a rainwater tank must be maximised to increase the effectiveness and reliability of any stormwater reuse, or harvesting systems. The following water conservation measures must be incorporated unless a higher standard is achieved under the State Environmental Planning Policy (Sustainable Buildings) 2022. Alternative approaches may be considered by Council on merit:

i) Water Fixtures: All water fixtures (e.g. taps, toilets, urinals, showerheads) must have a minimum 3-star WELS rating.

ii) All water-using appliances (e.g. dishwashers, washing machines) must  have a minimum 3-star WELS rating. Where appliances are not installed at the time of construction, applicants must demonstrate how compliance will be achieved post occupancy.

iii) Landscaping must incorporate water-efficient or locally indigenous plant  species, consistent with Council’s recommended planting guidelines.

2\) Developments that are not subject to BASIX must still meet water efficiency requirements and achieve at a minimum, a 3-star WELS rating.

3\) Industrial, commercial, and business developments must supply 80% of their non-potable demand using non-potable water sources. Rainwater shall be the primary source and only supplemented by potable, or recycled water when rainwater cannot meet the 80% target of the demand. Where site constraints prevent full compliance, applicants must demonstrate best-practice water efficiency and provide justification for alternative approaches, to be assessed by Council.

Note: Council supports the use of water harvesting and reuse systems as part of stormwater management measures to reduce runoff volumes, conserve water, and promote sustainable development. For high-density residential developments in the city centre, spatial constraints may limit the feasibility of implementing the stormwater reuse measures. Where such limitations exist, developers are encouraged to consult with Council to explore alternative approaches that align with local planning controls and best practice stormwater management principles.
