### 1.3 Aims of this Chapter

The determining authority will take the provisions of this Chapter into consideration in determining applications within the Wollongong LGA. Development applications must demonstrate conformity with the aims, objectives and controls of this and other relevant chapters of the Wollongong DCP.

The aims, of this Chapter are to:

a) Minimise any adverse impacts of stormwater runoff and diffuse stormwater pollution on receiving environments.

b) Ensure that developments minimise the impact of stormwater runoff on adjoining properties, native bushland, riparian land, key fish habitats, the Sydney Drinking Water Catchment Area , receiving waterways, groundwater dependent ecosystems, and groundwater systems.

c) Integrate stormwater management systems into the landscape in a manner that provides multiple benefits, including environmental and water quality protection, the enhancement of ecological processes, habitat and biodiversity, flood mitigation, recreational value, visual amenity, and urban heat mitigation.

d) Integrate environmentally sensitive WSUD and stormwater management solutions early in the development and design processes to ensure that WSUD treatment measures are incorporated and that both stormwater and floodplain management issues are adequately addressed.

e) Maintain the health of Lake Illawarra, the ocean and all receiving waterways.

f) Ensure the sustainable and appropriate development of the floodplains, the West Dapto Urban Release Area (WDURA), and the Lake Illawarra catchment area.

g) Reduce the demand for potable water through water efficient fittings, rainwater harvesting and reuse.

h) Improve liveability and community amenity by managing water in a holistic, environmentally sensitive, and integrated way.

i) Apply a treatment-train approach matched to pollutant types and sources.

j) Keep WSUD assets as simple and cost-effective as possible, without compromising performance.

k) Encourage co-benefits for example, detention basins that function as open space when dry, or shaded paths adjacent to wetlands and educational signage.
