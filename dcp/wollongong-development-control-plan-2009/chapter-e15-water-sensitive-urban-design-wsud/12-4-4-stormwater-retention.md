### 4.4 Stormwater retention

1\) All WSUD measures must be designed to retain, treat, and gradually release all stormwater runoff up to the four (4) month Exceedances per year (EY) design storm (equivalent to a 98.17% AEP, as defined in Australian Rainfall and Runoff 2019 terminology). This design standard ensures that around 90% of the long-term stormwater runoff volume is treated, with only ten per cent (10%) bypassing, or overflowing the WSUD measure during larger rainfall events. For higher flows, such as those associated with up to a one per cent (1%) AEP event, additional dedicated detention storage may be required to significantly reduce peak flow rates and mitigate downstream impacts. the impact of stormwater from the development.

#### Notes:

For Council’s requirements on on-site stormwater detention (OSD), refer to Wollongong DCP 2009 Chapter E14 Stormwater Management. Where required by Council, a “no worsening” criterion may apply to other design events (e.g. 5% AEP or 20% AEP) to manage erosion and protect waterway health. Compliance with Council’s stormwater targets must be demonstrated through hydrological modelling that compares pre- and post-development scenarios at relevant discharge points. This evidence should be included in the applicant’s WSUD Strategy.
