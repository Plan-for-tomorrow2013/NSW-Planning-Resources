### 1.1 Purpose of this Chapter

The Wollongong Development Control Plan (DCP) 2009 – Chapter E15 Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD) outlines the objectives, controls and design guidance for protecting and improving water quality. The Chapter also outlines Council’s requirements for stormwater quality, reuse and retention. This Chapter supports the objectives contained in the Wollongong Local Environmental Plan (LEP) 2009. This Chapter is informed by the following Council policies and strategy: Our Wollongong Our Future Community Strategic Plan 2035 This Chapter includes specific references to recognised best practice design standards and guidelines, where appropriate.
