## 1 INTRODUCTION

1\. This chapter of the DCP outlines Council’s general requirements for the proposed establishment and operation of a retail market upon any land within Wollongong Local Government Area.

2\. This chapter of the DCP should be read in conjunction with.the relevant LEP applying to the site, to determine the zoning of the subject land and the permissibility for the establishment of a retail market.
