## 7 SALE OF GOODS

1\. In the event that the retail market application is ultimately approved, the market organiser will be responsible for the checking that all stall holders are appropriately licensed. For example, a second hand dealer’s license may be required to be obtained from the Office of Fair Trading (NSW Department of Commerce) for stall holders involved in the purchasing and subsequent sale of certain second hand items or goods.
