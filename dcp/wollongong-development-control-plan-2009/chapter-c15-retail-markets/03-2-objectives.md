## 2 OBJECTIVES

1\. The objectives of this part of the DCP are:

(a) To control the type, location and impact of retail markets within the Wollongong Local Government Area; and

(b) To reinforce Council’s retail hierarchy strategy whilst providing retail market opportunities in appropriate locations for the benefit of residents in Wollongong Local Government Area and the wider Illawarra Region.
