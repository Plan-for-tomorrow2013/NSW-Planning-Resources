## 4 HOURS OF OPERATION

1\. The operation of retail markets will generally be limited to either Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays and / or Public Holidays between the hours of 8.00 am to 6.00 pm. The setting up of any market stall may take place from 7.00 am on the day of the market, except where the land to which the proposed market, either directly adjoins or is opposite any residential zoned land, in which case stall holders will only be allowed to set up from 8.00 am onwards.
