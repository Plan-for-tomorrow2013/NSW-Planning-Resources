## 3 AIMS AND OBJECTIVES

1\. The key aims and objectives of this precinct plan are:

(a) To adopt certain development standards and environmental safeguards to facilitate the control of development on land to which this plan applies;

(b) To restore and safeguard indigenous vegetation cover to those parts of the landscape which have been disturbed previously by human settlement;

(c) To reduce the risk to life and property which may be affected by natural environmental hazards; and

(d) To protect and improve the scenic and environmental quality of the area.
