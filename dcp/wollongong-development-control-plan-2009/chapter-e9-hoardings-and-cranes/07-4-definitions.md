## 4 DEFINITIONS

“Hoardings” are structures or fences erected on or adjacent to a property to form barrier between demolition and construction sites and the public domain. Hoarding structures may consist of fencing, scaffolding and / or overhead structures as either individual elements or integrated together to form a uniform hoarding.

A “Type A Hoarding” is a hoarding comprising of a fence.

A “Type B Hoarding” is an overhead structure situated over footpaths.

A “Type C Hoarding” is a full- face scaffold.
