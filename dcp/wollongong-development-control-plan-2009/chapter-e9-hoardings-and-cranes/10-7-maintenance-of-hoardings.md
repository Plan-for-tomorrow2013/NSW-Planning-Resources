## 7 MAINTENANCE OF HOARDINGS

1\. Periodic inspection of the hoarding structure is required to be undertaken by the site supervisor. The hoarding structure is to be maintained in an operational state in accordance with relevant Australian Standards and the WorkCover Authority Code of Practice – Overhead Protective Structures, until the hoarding is removed after all the construction phases of the development are complete.
