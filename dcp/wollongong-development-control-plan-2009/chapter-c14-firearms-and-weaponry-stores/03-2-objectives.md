## 2 OBJECTIVES

1\. The key objectives of this chapter in the DCP are to:

(a) Ensure any firearms or weaponry store is located at a reasonable distance away from sensitive land uses.

(b) Ensure any firearms or weaponry store will not cause any adverse amenity impact upon the surrounding locality.

(c) Ensure any firearms or weaponry store takes into account Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design measures, to minimise crime and vandalism.
