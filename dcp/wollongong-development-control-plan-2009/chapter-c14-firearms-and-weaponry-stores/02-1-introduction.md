## 1 INTRODUCTION

1\. This chapter of the DCP outlines Council’s general requirements for the establishment and conduct of a firearms or weaponry store.

2\. This chapter of the DCP should also be read in conjunction with the provisions of the relevant LEP applying to the site, to determine the zoning of the subject land and the permissibility of the proposed development.
