## 1. NAME AND COMMENCEMENT OF THIS DEVELOPMENT CONTROL PLAN

This document may be cited as Wollongong Development Control Plan 2009 (DCP 2009). DCP 2009 was adopted by Council on 15 December 2009 and came into effect on 3 March 2010. Some individual Chapters, including this Chapter, have since been amended.

This DCP 2009 has been prepared in accordance with Section 3.43 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (EP&A Act) and Section 16 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Regulation 2021.
