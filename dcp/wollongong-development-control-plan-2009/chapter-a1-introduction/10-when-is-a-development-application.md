## 10. WHEN IS A DEVELOPMENT APPLICATION REQUIRED

The lodgement of a Development Application is required for any proposed development where the Wollongong LEP or any other environmental planning instrument specifies that a proposed development may only be carried out with development consent upon the land to which the instrument applies.

Certain proposed developments may be classified as either “designated development” or “integrated development” under EP&A Act or the Environmental Planning and Assessment Regulation 2021.

Designated development requires the preparation of an EIS which must be undertaken in accordance with the requirements of the Director-General of the NSW Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure.

Integrated development requires general terms of approval from the relevant authority, which if granted consent, forms part of the conditions of development consent. Separately, Concurrence may be required whereby agreement from a referral authority must be obtained before the Council can determine a Development Application.
