## 6 FUTURE EXPANSION OR ENLARGEMENT OF A HOME

### BUSINESS OR INDUSTRY IN RESIDENTIAL OR RURAL AREAS

1\. Home businesses and industries must not affect the amenity of any surrounding residential or rural locality.

2\. In cases where a home business or industry activity in the future requires expansion or enlargement (ie due to additional floor area required in excess of 25% of the total floor area of the building and / or additional employees being required), the relocation of the business to a suitable business zone will be necessary.
