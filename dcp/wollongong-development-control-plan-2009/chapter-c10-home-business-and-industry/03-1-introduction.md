## 1 INTRODUCTION

1\. This chapter of the DCP outlines Council’s requirements for the establishment and operation of a home business and home industry. Sections 2-6 applies of this chapter apply to both home business and home industry. Section 7 contains controls specific to home industry.

2\. This chapter should be read in conjunction with the provisions of the relevant LEP applying to the site and State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008.

3\. Under State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008, a “home business” and “home industry” is generally “exempt development” except where it involves a change of building use. Under the Building Code of Australia (BCA), change of building use involves a floor area greater than 10% of the total floor area of the building.
