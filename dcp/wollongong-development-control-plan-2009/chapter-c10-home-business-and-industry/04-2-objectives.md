## 2 OBJECTIVES

1\. The main objectives of this policy are:

(a) To allow for low scale home businesses and home industry which are conducted within a dwelling or a building ancillary to the dwelling; and

(b) To ensure home businesses and home industry are compatible in terms of amenity with surrounding land uses, especially in rural, residential and mixed use zones.
