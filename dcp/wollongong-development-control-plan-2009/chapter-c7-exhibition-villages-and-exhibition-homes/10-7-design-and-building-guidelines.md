## 7 DESIGN AND BUILDING GUIDELINES ENCOURAGING

### ECOLOGICALLY SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT & ENERGY EFFICIENCY PRINCIPLES

1\. As part of any exhibition village, the proponent is required to provide best practice ‘next generation’ design and building construction guidelines for participating building companies to incorporate into their dwelling designs. The proposed design and building guidelines must also incorporate ESD and energy efficiency / smart housing requirements.

2\. The design and building guidelines are to be prepared by the applicant and submitted with the Development Application. Should Council ultimately grant development consent for the exhibition village, a condition of consent will be imposed requiring the implementation of the approved design and building guidelines for each exhibition home within the exhibition village.
