## 10 BUSINESS IDENTIFICATION SIGNAGE FOR EXHIBITION

### VILLAGES AND EXHIBITION HOMES

1\. A maximum of one (1) corporate business identification pylon sign will be permitted at the main entry to an exhibition village or a series of exhibition homes under the same control of one (1) building company. The business identification sign may include corporate signage of all building companies within the exhibition village. No other advertising structure or pylon sign will be permitted within an exhibition village.

2\. A maximum of one (1) business identification sign will be permitted for each exhibition home.
