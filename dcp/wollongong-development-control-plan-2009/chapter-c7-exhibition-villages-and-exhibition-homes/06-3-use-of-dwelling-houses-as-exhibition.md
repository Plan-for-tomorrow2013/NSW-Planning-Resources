## 3 USE OF DWELLING HOUSES AS EXHIBITION HOMES

1\. The use of dwelling houses as exhibition homes will only be permitted in residential areas where the amenity and privacy of neighbouring residential properties will be maintained.

2\. All ancillary sales and marketing programs of a building company shall be contained within the cartilage of the exhibition home, at all times.
