## 8 HOURS OF OPERATION

1\. The hours of operation for an exhibition home or an exhibition village (ie access by the general public) shall be restricted to between 10.00 am to 5.00 pm, seven days per week. Any development consent granted for an exhibition home or exhibition village will be subject to an appropriate condition restricting the development to these hours of operation.
