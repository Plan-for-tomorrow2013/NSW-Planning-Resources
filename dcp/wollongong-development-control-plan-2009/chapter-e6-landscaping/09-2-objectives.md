## 2 OBJECTIVES

(a) To define the landscaping provisions necessary for the Development Application process.

(b) To ensure that landscaping is appropriate to characteristics of its locality, preserving and contributing to its natural, cultural, heritage and visual character.

(c) Minimise the impacts from development on natural site features in particular retaining existing trees where feasible.

(d) Facilitate long term improvements to the landscape of the Wollongong LGA.
