## 3 HOUSE RULES

House rules are to be clearly displayed throughout the boarding house. The rules are to address the following:

(a) visitor/guests policy

(b) activities and noise control

(c) operating hours for communal areas and outdoor spaces

(d) use of communal areas

(e) maximum room occupation

(f) resident and guest behaviour standards

(g) accessing rooms for inspection

(h) cooking and dining

(i) waste disposal

(j) damage/breakages/loss of keys/emergency contacts

(k) fire safety

(l) smoking, alcohol and zero tolerance policy on illegal drugs

(m) keeping of pets
