## 1. General

(a) The Kemblawarra peripheral sales (bulky goods retailing) precinct is located along King Street and Shellharbour Road, approximately 300 metres to the south of the Warrawong sub-regional shopping centre. The role and function of the Kemblawarra retail precinct is centred on bulky goods or peripheral sales.

(b) The Kemblawarra bulky goods retailing precinct is characterised by a large scale floor space, high level of accessibility with large car parking areas and high exposure to passing vehicular traffic which cannot be found within the traditional areas of retail activity.

(c) This precinct represents the most significant concentration of peripheral sales (bulky goods) in the Illawarra Region and its trade area extends throughout the majority of the Wollongong Local Government Area and the northern part of the Shellharbour Local Government Area.
