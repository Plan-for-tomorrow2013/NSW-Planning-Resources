## 2. Development Controls

(a) Any new retail development within this precinct shall be limited to bulky goods retailing showrooms or take away food or drink premises only.

(b) Any new bulky goods retailing will require the redevelopment of lands within the existing bulky goods retailing precinct which is bounded by King Street and the western side of Shellharbour Road.
