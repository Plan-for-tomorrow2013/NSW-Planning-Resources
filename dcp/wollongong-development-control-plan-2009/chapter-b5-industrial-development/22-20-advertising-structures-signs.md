## 20 ADVERTISING STRUCTURES / SIGNS

### 20.1 Development Controls

1\. All advertising signage or structures for industrial developments shall be in accordance with the requirements of State Environmental Planning Policy No. 64 – Advertising and Signage (SEPP

64\) and Chapter C1 Advertising Signage and Structures of this DCP.
