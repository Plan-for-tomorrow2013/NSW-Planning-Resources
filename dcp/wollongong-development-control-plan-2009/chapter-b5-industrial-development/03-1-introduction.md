## 1 INTRODUCTION

1\. This chapter of the DCP provides the objectives and detailed controls for the subdivision of industrial land and industrial development and should be read in conjunction with the relevant LEP applying to the subject site..

2\. This chapter of the DCP applies to land zoned either: IN1 General Industry, IN2 Light Industrial,

IN3 Heavy Industrial and IN4 Working Waterfront under the relevant LEP.

3\. This chapter of the DCP also applies to any light industry or warehouse or distribution centre proposed upon land zoned B6 Enterprise Corridor.

4\. It is also recommended that prospective applicant’s also refer to the Subdivision Code which provides the detailed design and construction requirements for roads, stormwater drainage and other infrastructure works.
