## 6 SAFETY AND SECURITY

### 6.1 Objectives

(a) To encourage appropriate natural survelliance and sightlines, in order to minimise potential crime risks within sites and the surrounding industrial areas.

(b) To provide unimpeded sight lines, particularly along pedestrian pathways from public roads and car parking areas within the site.

(c) To provide adequate lighting throughout the development site including lighting between building entrance points and car parking areas in accordance with Crime Prevention through Environmental Design (CPTED) principles.

### 6.2 Development Controls

#### 6.2.1 Entrances and natural surveillance

1\. The front door to a building should face the road, wherever possible.

2\. Any administration offices or showrooms must be located at the front of the building with windows facing the public road.

3\. The street number of the building must be visible from the road to allow visitors and emergency service vehicles to easily identify the building.

4\. Lighting (including bollard lighting) should be provided to the external entry path and the car parking area using vandal resistant light fixtures.

5\. Lighting design should address the principles of Crime Prevention through Environmental Design (CPTED).

6\. Compliance with the requirements of Chapter E2: Crime Prevention through Environmental Design (CPTED) in this DCP.
