1\. In Australia, there is a range of federal and state legislation and policies in place governing the

rights of people with disability. The key legislation, standards and codes utilised to enforce,

control and guide access are detailed below:

(a) Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Federal)

(c) The Disability (Access to Premises – building) Standards (2010)

(d) The Building Code of Australia under the National Construction Code
