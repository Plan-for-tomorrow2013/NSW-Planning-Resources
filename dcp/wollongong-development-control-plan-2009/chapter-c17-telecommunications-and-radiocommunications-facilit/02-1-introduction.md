## 1 INTRODUCTION

Telecommunications and radiocommunications facilities are vital infrastructure components of modern communication systems. Telecommunications and radiocommunications facilities include telecommunications towers, masts and antennas, base stations, satellite-based facilities, radiocommunications transmitters and their supporting infrastructure and ancillary development. It is important for these facilities to be well sited and designed to avoid adverse visual impacts, comply with electromagnetic energy emission exposure limits and minimise environmental and amenity impacts. Council is the consent authority for telecommunications and radiocommunications facilities that require development consent under the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979.  These are facilities not identified in the Telecommunications (Low Impact Facilities) Determination 2018 as ‘low impact facilities’.
