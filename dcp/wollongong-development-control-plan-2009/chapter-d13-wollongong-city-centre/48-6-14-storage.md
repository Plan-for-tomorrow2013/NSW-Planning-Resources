### 6.14 Storage

#### 6.14.1 Objective

a) Provide accessible storage for larger household items which cannot be readily accommodated within dwellings.

#### 6.14.2 Development Controls

a) For residential apartment buildings provide a secure space to be set aside exclusively for storage as part of the basement.  The storage area must comply with the following requirements:

| Dwelling | Storage Area | Storage Volume |
|---|---|---|
| One bedroom apartments | 3m2 | 3m3 |
| Two bedroom apartments | 4m2 | 8m3 |
| Three or more bedroom apartments | 5m2 | 10m3 |
