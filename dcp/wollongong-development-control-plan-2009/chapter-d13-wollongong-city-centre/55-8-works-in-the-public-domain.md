## 8 WORKS IN THE PUBLIC DOMAIN

Any development requiring works to be carried out within the public domain in the Wollongong City Centre will be subject to compliance with the requirements of the Wollongong City Centre Public Domain Technical Manual at Appendix 2 to this DCP and any other specific Council requirements.
