### 1.6 Aims and objectives

The aims and objectives of this DCP are:

#### 1

To identify expectations and requirements for development within Gosford City Centre

and build upon the GCC SEPP by providing detailed objectives and controls for development;

#### 2

To ensure that all development aligns with the vision, recommendations and place

character in the Urban Design Framework prepared by the NSW Government Architect;

#### 3

To identify approaches and techniques which promote design excellence resulting

in quality urban design and architectural outcomes in Gosford City Centre; and

#### 4

To promote best practice and quality environmental outcomes.
