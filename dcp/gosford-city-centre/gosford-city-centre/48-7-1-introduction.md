### 7.1 Introduction

This chapter contains detailed objectives and controls on pedestrian access, vehicular access, on-site parking and site facilities, including refuse collection and removal.

The controls in this chapter aim to:

–– Facilitate the development of building design excellence appropriate to a regional city.

–– Require parking and servicing provisions to be contained within development sites to an amount and rate adequate for the economic and sustainable growth of the city centre.

–– Provide for safe and secure access.

–– Minimise impacts on city amenity, the public domain and streetscape.

–– Ensure that access is provided for the disabled and mobility impaired.
