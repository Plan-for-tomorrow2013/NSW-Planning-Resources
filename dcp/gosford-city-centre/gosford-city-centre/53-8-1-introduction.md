### 8.1 Introduction

This chapter discusses energy efficiency requirements of buildings, water use and conservation, wind and solar impacts and waste management. The controls in this chapter aim to:

–– Facilitate the development of building design excellence appropriate to a regional city.

–– Ensure the environmental impact of new development is managed in a sustainable and economical way.

–– Ensure a healthy environment.

–– Provide an adequate and renewable supply of

–– Ensure application, where appropriate, of the State Environmental Planning Policy (Building Sustainability Index: BASIX) 2004 or Australian Greenhouse Ratings (AGR) certification systems. Note — Additional controls for key sites (in this DCP) and medium and large sites (using clause 8.4(2) and

(3) of GCC SEPP) please refer to Chapter 5 Built form Section 5.2.8.
