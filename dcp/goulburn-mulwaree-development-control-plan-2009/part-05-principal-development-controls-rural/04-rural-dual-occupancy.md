### Rural dual occupancy

#### Objective

Preserve the amenity of rural areas where dual occupancies are permissible.

#### Controls

A dual occupancy (attached or detached) shall only be erected if it can be demonstrated that adequate arrangements have been made for the provision of a potable water supply and the disposal of sewerage and stormwater from each dwelling.
