### 4.4 Dual Occupancies and Secondary Dwellings

### 4.4 Dual Occupancies and Secondary Dwellings

#### 4.4.1 Introduction

This BDCP section applies to all applications for development consent involving a dual occupancy or a secondary dwelling under the provisions of the BLEP 2012.

A reference to dual occupancy in this section is to be taken to also apply to a secondary dwelling, unless stated otherwise, or separate controls are provided.
