### 6.6 Landscaping for Development

### 6.6 Landscaping for Development

Council has adopted a separate Landscaping Code that aims to provide guidelines for the preparation of Landscape Plans as an integral component of new development in Burwood.

The Landscaping Code has been prepared to raise awareness of the aesthetic, functional and environmental benefits of landscaping. It aims to maintain and enhance existing themes and elements of the BTC and streetscapes of Burwood. The guidelines in the Code provide scope for creative design solutions for landscaping consistent with the intent of this Code.

The Landscaping Code does not form part of this DCP. It is available on Council’s website.
