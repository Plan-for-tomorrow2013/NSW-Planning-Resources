#### P5 Plans and reports on transport, traffic and parking to support Development

Applications: To provide adequate information for the assessment of proposals, Development Applications are to be supported by one of the following reports, depending on the scale and impact of the proposal.

1\. Transport, Traffic and Parking Impact Report and Management Plans
