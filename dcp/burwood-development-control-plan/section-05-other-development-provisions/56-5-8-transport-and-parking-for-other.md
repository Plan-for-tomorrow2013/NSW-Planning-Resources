### 5.8 Transport and Parking for Other Development

- Enable wheels and frame to be locked to the device without damaging the vehicle.

- Be well located and lit for security purposes.

- Be in a convenient and accessible location outside pedestrian and vehicle movement paths.

- Be protected from motor vehicle movements and useable without damaging other bicycles.

- Be protected from the weather.

- Designed to fit in with the development.
