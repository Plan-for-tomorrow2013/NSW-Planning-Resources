### 5.2 Health Consulting Rooms

### 5.2 Health Consulting Rooms

Health consulting rooms are permitted with consent in all residential and business zones under the Burwood Local Environmental Plan (BLEP) 2012. The BLEP defines health consulting rooms as “premises comprising one or more rooms within (or within the curtilage of) a dwelling house used by not more than three health care professionals at any one time”.

The following control applies:

The portion of a dwelling house used as a health consulting room must not exceed 50% of the floor area.
