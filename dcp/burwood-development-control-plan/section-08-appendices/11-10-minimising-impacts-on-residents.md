## 10 Minimising Impacts On Residents

So as to minimise impacts upon the residents of adjoining premises as well as residents of the building the following rules are to apply:

a. No loud music or television noise is permitted after 10.00pm.

b. No parties or gatherings are permitted upon the premises after 10.00pm.

c. No visitors other than residents of the property are permitted after 10.00pm.

d. No use of the outdoor areas is permitted after 10.00pm.

e. No smoking in areas which may affect the amenity of other residents on site or of residents of neighbouring properties.

Note: More specific references may be required to define the terms such as “loud” and “noise”, maximum number of persons after 10pm, etc depending on the scale of development proposed.
