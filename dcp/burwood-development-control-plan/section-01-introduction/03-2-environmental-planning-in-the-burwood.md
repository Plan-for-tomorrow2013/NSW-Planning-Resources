## Part 2  Environmental Planning in the Burwood LGA

Burwood DCP – Chapter 1 Introduction

Chapter 1 Introduction

This Part outlines Burwood’s broad planning context and Council’s approach to environmental planning that underpins the BDCP. The general requirements for analysis, consideration and management of site requirements, and the locational context of development are established in this Part.
