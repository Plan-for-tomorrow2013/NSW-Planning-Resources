### 3.10 Heritage in Centres and Corridors

P23 Exterior stonework and face brickwork of a heritage property shall not be painted or otherwise obscured. The cement rendering of masonry exteriors is generally not supported on heritage grounds.

P24 Original and early landscaping that includes plantings with direct links or association to the heritage property must be retained.

P25 New colour schemes should reflect the most significant design period of the heritage property. Corporate colours will not be permitted to dominate the architectural elements of a building or obscure building features.

P26 New colour schemes for a building in the vicinity of a heritage property must not detract from the setting of the heritage property.
