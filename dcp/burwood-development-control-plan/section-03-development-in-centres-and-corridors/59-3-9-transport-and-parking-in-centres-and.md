### 3.9 Transport and Parking in Centres and Corridors

| Service stations | 6 spaces per work bay plus 1 spaces per 20 sqm or part thereof of shop or food and drink premises included on site. |  |
|---|---|---|
| Sex services premises | 2 spaces per sex worker and 1 space per non-sex worker (e.g. manager and security). |  |
| Veterinary hospitals | 4 spaces per veterinary care professional being 1 space for the health care professional, 1 space for an employee and 2 spaces for attendee animal owners. |  |
| Shops amusement centres industrial retail outlets other retail premises not defined | On land zoned B4 Mixed Use 1 space for the first 400 sqm or part thereof, plus 1 space per 40 sqm or part thereof additional to the first 400 sqm. In all other business zones: 1 space per 40 sqm or part thereof. | The provisions of P2 in Section 3.9.2 in relation to Visitors Parking in the B4 Mixed Use zone may apply to these land uses. |
| Neighbourhood shops | 1 space per 50 sqm in all zones. Except in the B6 Enterprise Corridor zone – no parking required. |  |
| Vehicle hire or sales premises | As for Business premises in zone. |  |
| Entertainment facilities function centres | As determined by Council having regard to a Transport, Traffic and Parking Impact Report and Management Plan, or a Transport, Traffic and Parking Impact Report, as applicable. | The provisions of P2 in Section 3.9.2 in relation to Visitors Parking in the B4 Mixed Use zone may apply to these land uses. |

| Warehouse or distribution centres | 1 space per 100 sqm or 1 space per 2 staff, whichever is the greater. |  |
