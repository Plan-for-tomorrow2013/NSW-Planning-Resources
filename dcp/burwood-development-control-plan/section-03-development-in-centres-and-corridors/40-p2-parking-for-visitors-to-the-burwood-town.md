#### P2 Parking for visitors to the Burwood Town Centre and the Strathfield Town

Centre: Notwithstanding P1, in the B4 Mixed Use zone, Council may consider and accept an offer from an applicant of a monetary contribution for Visitors Parking in lieu of the provision on-site of all or part of the parking requirement. Such an offer is voluntary and subject to Council’s agreement at its discretion. If approved, it will be administered through a Voluntary Planning Agreement under the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979.
