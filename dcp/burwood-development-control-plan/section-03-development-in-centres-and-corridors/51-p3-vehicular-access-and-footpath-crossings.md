#### P3 Vehicular Access and Footpath Crossings

All vehicles must be able to leave on-site parking and service areas in a forward direction. Vehicular access for development must be provided from lanes and minor or secondary streets where available, rather than major streets or Classified Roads.

In particular, new vehicular access to land in the B4 Mixed Use zone from Burwood Road or The Boulevarde, or to land in the B2 Local centre zone from Liverpool Road will not be approved.

Vehicular access and footpath crossings must be minimised where provided the safety of pedestrians and cyclists must be maintained and there should be no more impacts on bus operations.

Where loading and servicing facilities are provided, there must be sufficient space to enable a nine metre rigid vehicle to queue off the street without impacting on footpath crossings.

Vehicular access must have a nominal width of 2.7 metres over the footpath, and be perpendicular to the kerb alignment. In exceptional circumstances, a double crossing with a nominal width of 5.4 metres may be permitted for safety reasons.

Loading dock and other service functions must not interfere with vehicular access to parking areas.

Openings in buildings that provide vehicular access must have automatic closing doors to conceal the opening from any public place.

Doorways in buildings at vehicular access points must incorporate design details that create visual interest.
