### 1.3 WHERE DOES THIS DEVELOPMENT CONTROL PLAN APPLY?

This Development Control Plan applies to all land within the Hawkesbury Local Government Area  and zoned under Hawkesbury Local Environmental Plan 2012. The Development Control Plan does not apply to land zoned under State Environmental Planning Policy (Sydney Region Growth Centres) 2006, unless referred to within the respective Development Control Plans. (At the time of adoption of this Development Control Plan the exclusion covers Stage 1 of the Vineyard Precinct).
