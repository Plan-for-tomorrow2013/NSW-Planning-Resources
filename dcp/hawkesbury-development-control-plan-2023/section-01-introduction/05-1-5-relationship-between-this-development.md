### 1.5 RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THIS DEVELOPMENT CONTROL PLAN AND HAWKESBURY

#### LOCAL ENVIRONMENTAL PLAN 2012

This Development Control Plan is to be read in conjunction with Hawkesbury Local Environmental

Plan. In the event of an inconsistency between the provisions of the two documents, the provisions of

Hawkesbury Local Environmental Plan 2012 will prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
