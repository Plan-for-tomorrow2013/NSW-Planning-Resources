#### 1.7 REVOCATION OF HAWKESBURY DEVELOPMENT CONTROL PLAN 2002

Pursuant to Section 3.43(4) of Environmental Planning and Assessment Act, the Hawkesbury

Development Control Plan 2023 revokes the following Chapters of Hawkesbury Development Control

Plan 2002:

Part C

• Car Parking and Access

• Effluent Disposal

• Heritage Conservation

Appendix E – Civil Works Specification (Part 1 Design Specification)

which covered land  for which this Development Control Plan now applies.
