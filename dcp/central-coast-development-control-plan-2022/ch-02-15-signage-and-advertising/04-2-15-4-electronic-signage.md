#### 2.15.4 ELECTRONIC SIGNAGE

Requirements for electronic signage are dealt with under the Transport Corridor Outdoor Advertising and Signage Guideline (2017).

In instances where proposed electronic signage relates to signage that is not an advertisement such as school signs or electronic business and building identification signs, the standards for electronic signage under the Transport Corridor Outdoor Advertising and Signage Guideline (2017) will be applied
