#### 5.2.5 PEDESTRIAN NETWORK

#### 5.2.5.1 Objective

To create a pedestrian pathway network that provides safe access to dwellings, open space areas and locations external to the precinct

#### 5.2.5.2 Controls

a Development Applications are to contain provision for the construction of pedestrian pathways for the frontage to the subject property in accordance with the accompanying plan and Figure 2 and 3 of section 5.2.4.
