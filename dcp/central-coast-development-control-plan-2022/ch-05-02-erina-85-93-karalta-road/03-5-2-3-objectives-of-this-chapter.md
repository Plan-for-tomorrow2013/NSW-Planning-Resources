#### 5.2.3  OBJECTIVES OF THIS CHAPTER

▪ To guide development of the land to support it's rezoning for residential development, primarily residential flat buildings

▪ To ensure coordinated infrastructure provision

▪ To provide owners with guidance as to the matters that need to be considered in the development of their properties
