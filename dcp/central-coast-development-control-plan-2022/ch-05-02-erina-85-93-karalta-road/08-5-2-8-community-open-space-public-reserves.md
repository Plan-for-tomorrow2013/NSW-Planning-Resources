#### 5.2.8  COMMUNITY OPEN SPACE (PUBLIC RESERVES)

#### 5.2.8.1 Objectives

▪ To provide a level of community open space that will serve the needs of the local residents

▪ To create community open space areas that contribute to the amenity of the precinct and provide link to Kincumber Mountain Reserve

▪ To create community focal points for the residents of the precinct

#### 5.2.8.2 Controls

a Land on Lot 13 DP 550325 and/or Lot 7 DP 1071636 Karalta Road as identified on the accompanying plan will be required for open space purposes. Council will compensate the owner for open space land provided in excess of requirements under CP 42 for the development of any lots resulting from the subdivision/development of Lot 13 DP 550325 and/or Lot 7 DP 1071636.
