#### 1.2.3 OTHER MATTERS

#### 1.2.3.1 Complying Development Certificates

Cl.161 of the EP&AR 2021 prescribes the public notification requirements to confirm the validity of a Complying Development Certificate pursuant to s.4.59 of the EP&A Act 1979. When Council issues a Complying Development Certificate, it will notify in accordance with cl.161 of the EP&AR 2021.
