## Section 12 -  Street and Public Place Lighting General Requirements

Lighting shall be designed and constructed for:

• Subdivision or development roads.

• Intersections, roundabouts and mid-block traffic calming devices.

• Public areas where nominated in the Development Consent.

• Pedestrian underpasses, overpasses, bridges, ramps and paths.

• Bicycle parking facilities.

• Car parks.

• Cul-de-sacs, permanent turning heads and temporary turning facilities.

• Potential vehicle conflict area and pedestrian and cyclist road crossing locations.

Lighting for local and collector roads shall be designed in accordance with AS/NZS 1158.3.1 Lighting for roads and public spaces, and where applicable, consideration of AS 4282 Control of the obtrusive effects of outdoor lighting, allowing for the following:

• Lighting design generally shall be sub-category PR1 to PR5. An alternative sub-category may be recommended by the Service Provider for Council Representative’s approval.

• Outdoor off-street car park designs shall be based on lighting sub-category PC1 to PC3.

• A minimum of 20-year design life for all lighting components, except supporting structures where a 40-year design life shall be provided.

• Best practice energy efficient lighting.

• Minimal impact of glare and light spill into adjacent properties.

• Consideration for cost, while endeavouring to meet the set lighting standards.

• Solar lights may be considered for installation in car park areas, alongside pathways and at specific roadside facilities, especially at locations where there is no suitable nearby connection to a low-voltage power supply and where it is considered the most economical way to provide lighting. All new solar light poles shall have a preferred pole height of 6m unless otherwise approved by Council’s Representative.

Lighting for distributor roads shall be designed in accordance with AS/NZS 1158.1.1 Lighting for roads and public spaces, sub-category V4 or V5. An alternative sub-category may be recommended by the Service Provider for Council Representative’s approval.

Only galvanised finished light poles and associated fittings will be accepted by Council unless otherwise negotiated with Council’s Representative. Decorative light poles shall not be permitted unless specifically approved by Council’s Representative.

Street lighting requirements on rural roads shall be discussed with Council’s Representative.

Best practice energy efficient lighting must be specified in the design and the use of LED as the lighting technology is preferred.

It is the Service Provider's responsibility to meet all costs associated with the design, supply and installation of street lighting where required by a Development Consent.
