## Section 14 -  Cold Milling of Asphalt and Base Course

### 14.1 General

This section covers the requirements for the removal of pavement materials from road pavements by cold milling, including the treatment of the milled vertical surfaces and the cleaning up, removal and disposal of the milled materials.

For traffic safety reasons, the Service Provider shall ensure that all milling or profiling work is not carried out any sooner than 48 hours prior to new asphalt being placed on the milled surface.

All traffic control will be undertaken in accordance with Section 4 - Traffic Management of this Specification.
