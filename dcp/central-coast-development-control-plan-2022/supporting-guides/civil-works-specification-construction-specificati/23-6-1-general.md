## Section 6 -  Stormwater and Subsurface Drainage Construction

### 6.1 General

Works covered by this section of the Specification include construction of all piped and box culvert stormwater drainage and subsurface drainage. The construction of open earth drains shall be in accordance with the requirements for earthworks in Section 3 - Site clearing and bulk earthworks.
