### 15.3 Service Provider’s Responsibility for Site Security

The Service Provider is responsible for the theft or vandalism of any surface treatment up to the following milestones:

• Final inspection where the surface treatment is located on existing public land, road reserve or other Council maintained land; and/or

• Registration of the linen plan in the case of land being dedicated to Council.
