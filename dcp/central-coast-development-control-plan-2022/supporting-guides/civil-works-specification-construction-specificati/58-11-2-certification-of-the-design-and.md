### 11.2 Certification of the Design and Construction

Certification of the design stating compliance with relevant Australian Standards will be required prior to construction. Inspections and certification of the construction confirming compliance with the design specifications shall be required as construction progresses. Certification at the completion of the structure shall be required to confirm that the structure has been constructed in accordance with the design and specification, is structurally adequate and shall meet the serviceability requirements intended by the design.

These certifications shall be provided by a suitably qualified Civil/Structural and Geotechnical Engineer(s). Copies of all certifications shall be provided to Council’s Representative (refer to
