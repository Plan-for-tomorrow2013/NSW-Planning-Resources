### 15.2 Compliance

#### 15.2.1 Compliance Inspections

Compliance with this part of the Specification will be confirmed upon site observation by Council’s Representative.

#### 15.2.2 Non-Complying Work

Where surface restoration and finishing of surface works are observed by Council’s Representative to not comply with this or any section of the Specification, the Service Provider will be instructed verbally or in writing of the non-compliance. The non-complying item(s) must be rectified prior to the final inspection.
