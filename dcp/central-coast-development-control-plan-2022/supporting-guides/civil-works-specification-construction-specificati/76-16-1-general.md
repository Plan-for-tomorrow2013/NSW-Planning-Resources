## Section 16 -  Traffic Signs, Pavement Marking and Road Safety

### Barriers

### 16.1 General

This section of the Specification covers the supply and installation of traffic signs, pavement markings and other pavement delineation, permanent traffic and pedestrian control devices and permanent safety barriers. This section of the Specification does not include traffic signals. Traffic signals are subject to TfNSW approvals, specifications and installation compliance.
