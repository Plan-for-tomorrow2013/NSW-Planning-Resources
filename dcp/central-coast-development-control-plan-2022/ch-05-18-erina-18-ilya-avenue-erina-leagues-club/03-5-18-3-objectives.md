#### 5.18.3 OBJECTIVES

The objectives of this chapter are:

▪ To encourage the orderly development of the site for the Erina Rugby League Football Club and complementary business land uses

▪ To facilitate traffic management

▪ To make provision for environmental protection

▪ To make provision for bushfire protection

▪ To control development in flood liable areas to minimise the effects on surrounding properties

▪ To identify site contamination issues
