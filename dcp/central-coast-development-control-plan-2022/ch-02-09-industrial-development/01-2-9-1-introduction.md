#### 2.9.1 INTRODUCTION

The purpose of this chapter is to provide appropriate and relevant requirements for the orderly development of industry, and specifies Council’s requirements relating to individual industrial sites.  It applies to all industrially zoned land within the Central Coast Local Government Area.

#### 2.9.1.1 Relationship to other Chapters and Policies

This Chapter should be read in conjunction with other relevant Chapters of this Development Control Plan and other Policy Documents of Council including, but not limited to:

▪ Chapter 2.4 – Subdivision

▪ Chapter 2.13– Transport and Parking

▪ Chapter 2.14 – Waste Management

▪ Chapter 2.15 – Signage and Advertising

▪ Chapter 3.1 – Floodplain Management

▪ Chapter 3.5 – Tree and Vegetation Management

▪ Parts 4 & 5 – Location Specific Development Controls (where relevant)

▪ Council’s Civil Works Specification

In certain circumstances, this chapter may also need to be read in conjunction with other documents referenced within the text, and with applicable State Environmental Planning Policies (SEPPs) and other legislation.

#### 2.9.1.2 Objectives of this Chapter

▪ To encourage employment generating developments

▪ To ensure the efficient use of urban land by maximising the development potential of land and infrastructure

▪ To promote quality industrial development by identifying Council's expectations and requirements relating to design and construction

▪ To control environmental impacts arising from industrial development
