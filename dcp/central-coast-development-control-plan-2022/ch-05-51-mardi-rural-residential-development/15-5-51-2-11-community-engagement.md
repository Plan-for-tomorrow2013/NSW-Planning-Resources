#### 5.51.2.11 Community Engagement

#### OBJECTIVE

▪ To assist in the management of enquiries and enable mitigation of issues during the development of the locality.

#### REQUIREMENTS

a During planning and construction, the applicant and/or developer of the locality should maintain contact with adjoining landholders to relay information and enable identification and mitigation of potential issues (noise, construction traffic etc.) as they arise.
