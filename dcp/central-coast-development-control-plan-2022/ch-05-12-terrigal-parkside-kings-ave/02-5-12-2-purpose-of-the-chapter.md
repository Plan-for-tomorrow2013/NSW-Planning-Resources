#### 5.12.2 PURPOSE OF THE CHAPTER

The purpose of this chapter is to provide more detailed guidelines for the subdivision and development of the land to which this chapter applies including providing for the opportunity for the creation of a Home Based Business Estate and associated facilities in a community title subdivision.
