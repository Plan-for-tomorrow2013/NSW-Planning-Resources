#### 5.48.2 PUBLIC ART DEVELOPMENT REQUIREMENTS

Public art provides scope for integration between major developments and the community, culture and environment in which they are constructed, as well as for major developments to enhance their distinctiveness through a unique and quality design interface. The procedure below sets out the application process and the information required by Council for the public art component of major development.
