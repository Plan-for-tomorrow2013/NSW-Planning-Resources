#### 4.5.1.2 Land to which this Chapter Applies

This chapter applies to all land within the Wyong Town Centre as shown in Figure 1 and covers a wide variety of land uses such as retail, commercial, mixed-use and residential. The Chapter should be read in conjunction with the provisions of the Central Coast Local Environmental Plan (LEP) 2022.
