#### 4.5.5 MIXED-USE AND COMMERCIAL DEVELOPMENT

This section provides provisions for mixed-use and commercial development in the Wyong Town Centre. Commercial development is development that is used for business or commercial purposes. Mixed-use development is development that has a mix of uses on the one site and generally involves some combination of commercial space combined with residential units in an urban area. Mixed-use development can range from smaller scale “shop-top housing” to larger scale developments with shopping centres and apartment towers. For residential only development refer to the relevant Chapters within this DCP.
