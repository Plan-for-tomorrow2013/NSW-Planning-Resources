#### 4.6.4  DESIGN GUIDELINES

#### OBJECTIVES

▪ To stimulate the highest-possible levels of outdoor pedestrian activity around all properties where mixed development is permitted

▪ To contribute to the successful operation of centres and community-based recreation facilities or clubs by stimulating the highest-possible levels of pedestrian activity

▪ To identify civic spaces and public infrastructure that would enhance desired levels of pedestrian activity

▪ To nominate locations for facilities or building elements which have the potential to disrupt or compromise desired levels of pedestrian activity
