#### 4.6.3.1 Building Envelope Provisions:  Residential Development

Numeric controls for residential development are provided within other DCP Chapters, including Chapters 2.1, 2.3 and 2.4, together with the Apartment Design Guide under State Environmental Planning Policy No 65 Design Quality of Residential Apartment Development (SEPP No.65) and Central Coast LEP 2022.
