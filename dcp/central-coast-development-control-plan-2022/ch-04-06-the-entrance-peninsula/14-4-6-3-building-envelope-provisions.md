#### 4.6.3 BUILDING ENVELOPE PROVISIONS

Envelope controls apply to specific facades or frontages.  They supplement “objectives” provided by the desired character statements (Section 2.0).

The Height of Building Map, read in conjunction with Clause 4.3 and 4.3A of the Central Coast LEP 2022, provides detail of height restrictions for development on The Entrance Peninsula.
