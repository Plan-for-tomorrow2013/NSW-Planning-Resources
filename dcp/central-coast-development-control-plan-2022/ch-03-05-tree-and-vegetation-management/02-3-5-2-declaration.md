#### 3.5.2 DECLARATION

All vegetation is declared to be vegetation to which Part 2.3 of State Environmental Planning Policy

(Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021 applies, subject to the exemptions set out in Section 3 of this Chapter.

Accordingly a Permit must be obtained from the Council under Part 2.3 of  State Environmental Planning Policy

(Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021 to remove such vegetation, that is to do any of the following:

a cut down, fell, uproot, kill, poison, ringbark, burn or otherwise destroy the vegetation, or

b prune, lop or otherwise remove a substantial part of the vegetation.
