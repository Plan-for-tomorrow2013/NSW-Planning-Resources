#### 5.39.2 OPEN SPACE BUFFER

#### OBJECTIVE

To provide a buffer between industrial and residential zoned land

#### REQUIREMENT

Landscaping to a design satisfactory to Council shall be required in areas zoned RE1 – Public Recreation and shown in this Chapter as open space buffer areas.
