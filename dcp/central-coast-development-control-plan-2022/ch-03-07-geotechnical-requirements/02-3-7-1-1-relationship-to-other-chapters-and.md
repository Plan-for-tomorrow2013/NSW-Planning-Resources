#### 3.7.1.1 Relationship to other Chapters and Policies

This Chapter must be read in conjunction with the relevant development provisions, environmental controls and location specific development controls of the Development Control Plan. Geotechnical requirements relating to coast processes, coastal cliff and bluffs as identified in Chapter 3.2 Coastal Hazard Management must be considered where relevant.
