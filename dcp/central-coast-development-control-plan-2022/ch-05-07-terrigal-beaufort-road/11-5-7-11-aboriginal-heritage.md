#### 5.7.11 ABORIGINAL HERITAGE

#### OBJECTIVE

To ensure development is planned in consideration of any Aboriginal heritage sites or values.

#### DEVELOPMENT CONTROLS

a An Aboriginal cultural heritage assessment is to be carried to ascertain whether any Aboriginal heritage values or sites are located on the site.
