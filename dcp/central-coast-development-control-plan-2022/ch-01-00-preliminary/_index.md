This Development Control Plan (DCP) may be amended from time to time by Council.

Proposed amendments are required to be advertised and exhibited in draft form and any

submissions received must be considered by Council before the amended plan is adopted.

People using this DCP should ensure that they have the current copy of the plan, including

any amendments. If in doubt, please check with Council’s Customer Service Centre.
