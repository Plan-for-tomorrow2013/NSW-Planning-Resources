#### 2.7.4 SHORT TERM RENTAL ACCOMMODATION

Controls for short term rental accommodation (STRA) are identified under State Environmental Planning Policy (SEPP) Housing. With respect to the Central Coast Local Government Area, the controls provided under the SEPP will not lead to an instance where a development application would be lodged for this land use (i.e. the proposal would either be exempt or prohibited development). In addition to the provision of the SEPP other considerations for STRA include but are not limited to:

• NSW Fair Trading’s Code of Conduct for the Short Term Rental Accommodation Industry

• Strata by-laws if relevant and in place

• Environmental Planning and Assessment Regulation regarding minimum fire safety standards for dwellings used for STRA;

• The STRA Register - administered by the NSW State Government Planning Portal
