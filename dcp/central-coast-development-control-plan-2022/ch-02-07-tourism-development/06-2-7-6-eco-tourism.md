#### 2.7.6 ECO-TOURISM

Eco-Tourism is defined in Central Coast LEP 2022. The LEP identifies where Eco - Tourism is permissible with consent.

#### REQUIREMENTS

a Clause 5.13 of Central Coast LEP 2022 provides requirements in relation to the granting of development consent for eco-tourist facilities
