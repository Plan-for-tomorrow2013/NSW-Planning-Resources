#### 5.10.12 PRIVACY

#### OBJECTIVE

To ensure development is sited and designed to minimise the potential for overlooking neighbours’

habitable rooms and recreational areas and provide a reasonable level of privacy.

#### REQUIREMENT

a Where the objective is not fully achievable due to site or design constraints, and overlooking is

unavoidable, sympathetic screening is required.
