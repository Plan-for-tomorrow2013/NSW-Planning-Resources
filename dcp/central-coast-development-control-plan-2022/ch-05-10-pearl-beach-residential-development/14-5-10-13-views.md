#### 5.10.13 VIEWS

#### OBJECTIVE

To maintain, within reason, the views and outlook of existing buildings

#### REQUIREMENTS

a Design and site development to adopt the principle of shared view corridors and within reason protect

views enjoyed by existing buildings.

b Particular regard shall be given to roof design to minimise environmental impact.
