#### 5.10.6 TREE COVER

#### OBJECTIVE

To ensure any development proposal will retain or replace existing tree cover to ensure the predominant

landscape quality of the locality is maintained.

#### REQUIREMENTS

a Development on a site should be located so as to retain as many existing trees on the site as

practicable.

b Where an application for development involves removal of a tree, consideration should be given to the

relocation or redesign of such proposals on the development site or neighbouring lot to ensure the

preservation of trees. In this regard, it should be noted that threats to feeder root system of large trees

will require an impact report from an arborist.

c Where trees are removed by building works, replacement planting of indigenous species with similar

growth potential will be required.
