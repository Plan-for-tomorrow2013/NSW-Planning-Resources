#### 5.10.14 SOLAR ACCESS

#### OBJECTIVE

Development should not unreasonably reduce solar access to living and recreational areas on adjacent

properties.

#### REQUIREMENTS

a Development shall maintain a reasonable level of sunlight to neighbours’ living and recreational space

between 9.00am and 3.00pm during the winter solstice, 22 June.

b Site design shall consider building orientation of living and recreational areas and the use of solar

energy collectors.
