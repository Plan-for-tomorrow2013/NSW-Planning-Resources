#### 5.10.16 CONSTRUCTION CONTROLS

#### OBJECTIVE

To minimise disturbance to neighbours and the environment during construction

#### REQUIREMENTS

a Prepare sites for development in an orderly and environmentally sensitive manner. Refer to Council’s

Tree and Vegetation Management DCP Chapter with regard to vegetation removal.

b Avoid the compaction of tree surrounds on site during construction.

c Provide protection to existing trees retained to avoid construction damage.
