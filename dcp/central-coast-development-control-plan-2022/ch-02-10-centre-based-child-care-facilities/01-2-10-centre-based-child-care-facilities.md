#### Chapter 2.10 Centre Based Child Care Facilities

### CHAPTER 2.10 CENTRE BASED CHILD CARE

### FACILITIES

#### 2.10.1 INTRODUCTION

This DCP Chapter is to be read in conjunction with State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021 and the NSW Government’s Child Care Planning Guideline.

Clause 3.27 of State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021 states the following:

(1) A provision of a development control plan that specifies a requirement, standard or control in relation to any of the following matters (including by reference to ages, age ratios, groupings, numbers or the like, of children) does not apply to development for the purpose of a centre-based child care facility:

(a) operational or management plans or arrangements (including hours of operation),

(b) demonstrated need or demand for child care services,

(c) proximity of facility to other early education and care facilities,

(d) any matter relating to development for the purpose of a centre-based child care facility contained in:

(i) the design principles set out in Part 2 of the Child Care Planning Guideline, or

(ii) the matters for consideration set out in Part 3 or the regulatory requirements set out in Part 4 of that Guideline (other than those concerning building height, side and rear setbacks or car parking rates).

This DCP Chapter only provides provisions for certain matters relating to development that would not be overridden by State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021 or the Child Care Planning Guideline

#### 2.10.2 GENERAL

#### OBJECTIVES

▪ To supplement the provisions of State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021 and the Child Care Planning Guideline

▪ To ensure the operation of childcare centres meet the required standards and operate with minimal impact on the community and environment

#### REQUIREMENTS

a Development Applications for child care centre are to address:

i the relevant provisions of Central Coast Local Environmental Plan 2022;

ii the design principles, considerations and regulatory requirements of Child Care Planning Guideline;

iii the additional issues identified below.
