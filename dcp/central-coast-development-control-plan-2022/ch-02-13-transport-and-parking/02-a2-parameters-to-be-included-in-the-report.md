### A2 Parameters to be included in the Report

a Study area is to be identified. It should be large enough to include all significant impacts of the proposed development. At a minimum, the area should include the adjacent controlled intersections.

b Time frame - Projected traffic demands should be based on fully occupied conditions. For multi-staged developments, traffic demands at the end of each stage should be determined.

c Any analysis is to include a background traffic growth extrapolated for 10 years and is to include pedestrian movements, school traffic, programmed traffic/transport improvements, future developments and other miscellaneous factors should be considered where appropriate.

d Road safety audit.

e Any other traffic related issues or concerns identified by Council staff.
