#### 5.44.1.3 Aims and Objectives

▪ To ensure the development accommodates a mix of residential, commercial, and retail use.

▪ To ensure that the development shall have an appropriate scale, texture and materiality that is sympathetic to the character of Toukley

▪ To ensure the iconic development of the site improves the public domain function and local amenity and connectivity.

▪ To ensure that the development provide a strong entry /arrival node for the Toukley Town Centre.

▪ To ensure that the development exhibits design excellence, including a high standard of architectural design, materials, façade treatments, interior design and landscape treatments.
