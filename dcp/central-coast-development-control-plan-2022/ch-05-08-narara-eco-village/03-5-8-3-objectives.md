#### 5.8.3 OBJECTIVES

The objectives of this chapter are:

▪ To encourage the orderly development of the site for residential and environmental related purposes including adaptive reuse of existing facilities;

▪ To protect threatened species and their habitats on the site;

▪ To facilitate small scale retail uses that support the community who live on site

▪ To ensure traffic and transport associated with the redevelopment is appropriately managed;

▪ To ensure effective bushfire protection and management occurs on site;

▪ To restrict development in flood liable areas and consider flooding and drainage issues and to ensure there is no increase in downstream flooding;

▪ To protect items of environmental heritage;

▪ To ensure the development is carried out in accordance with best practice management for site development; and,

▪ To ensure on-site contamination is addressed.
