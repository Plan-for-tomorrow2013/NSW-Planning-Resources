#### 5.8.2 PURPOSE OF CHAPTER

The purpose of this chapter is to provide detailed planning and design guidelines to support the development and use of the land for a residential subdivision, specifically an eco-village within the area zoned R2 Low Density Residential. The site was formerly used as Gosford Horticultural Research and Advisory Station before being acquired by a co-operative for an eco-village. Since that time the site has been known as ‘Narara Eco-Village’ and provides opportunities for eco-friendly living and related community ventures. The eco-village is a community title subdivision and operates under a Community Management Statement.
