#### 2.5.7 SERVICES

Servicing of commercial buildings is essential for the delivery of goods and the removal of waste as well as providing discrete access for employees and service personnel. How the design of a building incorporates servicing can influence its ease of public and private use and have a significant impact upon public amenity and safety.

#### REQUIREMENTS

a Waste areas and loading or servicing bays should be grouped and located underneath or behind buildings. Ideally, they should not be visible from public streets, parks or other public spaces, and should either be enclosed or screened with building elements or landscaping.

b Access to service areas shall be routed away from main streets to protect pedestrian amenity and safety, and reduce friction from vehicular traffic.

c The location and design of service areas must have regard for neighbours in terms of noise, light spill, odour, hours of operation and deliveries, especially when adjacent to residential development.

d Design for waste collection and recycling is to be in accordance with Chapter 2.14: Waste Management.

e Building services, such as drainage pipes, should be integrated and coordinated with the overall façade and balcony design.
