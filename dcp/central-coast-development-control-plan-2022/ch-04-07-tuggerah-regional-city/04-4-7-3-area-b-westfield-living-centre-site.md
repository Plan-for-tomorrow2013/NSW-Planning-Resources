#### 4.7.3 AREA B – WESTFIELD LIVING CENTRE SITE

Any proposal to significantly expand and/or redevelop the existing Westfield Living Centre Site will require the preparation of a site master plan, as part of any future development application, that demonstrates to the satisfaction of Council appropriate physical, built form, public domain connections and integration between Areas A and C.
