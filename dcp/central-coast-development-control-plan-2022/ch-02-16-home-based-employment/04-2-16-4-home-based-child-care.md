#### 2.16.4 HOME-BASED CHILD CARE

Home-based child care is considered exempt or complying development under State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021 - Ch 3. Educational Establishments and Child Care Facilities.

Note: The provisions regarding the number and age of children form part of the definition of home based child care and cannot be exceeded.
