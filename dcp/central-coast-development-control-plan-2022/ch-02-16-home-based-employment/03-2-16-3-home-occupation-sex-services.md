#### 2.16.3 HOME OCCUPATION (SEX SERVICES)

A ‘home occupation (sex services)’ requires development consent from Council and must comply with the requirements outlined in Chapter 2.11: Restricted and Sex Services Premises. Please refer to this chapter of the DCP for requirements.
