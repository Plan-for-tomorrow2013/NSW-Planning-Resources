#### S4.4.8 Outbuildings

1\. Any outbuilding in excess of 10m2 must be of the same architectural form as the main dwelling and be constructed of the same material. Such outbuildings must be contained within the building envelope.

2\. Temporary structures are not permitted in front of the building facade and are not to be visible from the abutting street.
