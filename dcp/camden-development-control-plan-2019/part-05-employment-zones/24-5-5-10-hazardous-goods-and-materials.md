#### 5.5.10 Hazardous Goods and Materials

#### Controls

1\. Where a development involves the storage and/or use of dangerous goods, full details of the quantities and types of goods and chemicals are to be submitted with the development application, together with the storage locations, mediums and the use intended for the goods and chemicals.

NOTE:  The requirements of SEPP No. 33 must be complied with. Based on the types and quantities of hazardous goods and of materials used/stored in a development, Council may require an assessment in accordance with SEPP No.33.
