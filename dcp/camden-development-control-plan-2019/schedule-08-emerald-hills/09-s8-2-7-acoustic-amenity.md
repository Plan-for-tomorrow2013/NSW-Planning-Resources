#### S8.2.7 Acoustic Amenity

#### Objectives

a. Establish an urban character which protects and enhances short and long views within the landscape, whilst allowing for the development of individual lots.

b. Mitigate noise effects from Camden Valley Way and Raby Road to ensure internal areas are not adversely affected by noise.

#### Controls

1\. Lots immediately adjacent to Camden Valley Way must adopt the indicative layout shown in Figure S8-1 which will help achieve the external noise criteria.
