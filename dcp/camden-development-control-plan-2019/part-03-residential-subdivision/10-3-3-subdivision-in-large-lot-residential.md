### 3.3 Subdivision in Large Lot Residential Areas

The purpose of the Large Lot Residential area (R5 zones) is to provide low density rural residential subdivisions free from commercial and industrial type uses. Objectives for this zoning can be found in the CLEP 2010.

Site specific controls for the subdivision of land within these areas are located within Schedules towards the end of this DCP. The Schedules specifically apply to:

• Catherine Field Village (Schedule 9)

• Yamba (Schedule 10)

• Grasmere (Schedule 11)

• 121 Raby Road (Schedule 12)
