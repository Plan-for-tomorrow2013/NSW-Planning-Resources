### S13.3 Parks and Open Space

In addition to general controls in the DCP, the following objectives and controls for the parks and open space apply to Gledswood Hills.

#### Objectives

a. Provide usable open space in conjunction with the gas easement.

#### Controls

1\. Parks and open space must be delivered in accordance with the ILP.

2\. The gas easement must be utilised to provide a linear open space area.
