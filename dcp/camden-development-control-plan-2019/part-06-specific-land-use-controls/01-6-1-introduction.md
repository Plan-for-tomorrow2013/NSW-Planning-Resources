### 6.1 Introduction

This Part applies to land zoned for rural or industrial uses. This plan also applies to site specific uses such as:

• Child Care Centres

• Restricted Premises

• Sex Service Premises

• Exhibition Homes and Villages

• Home Business & Home Industry

• Domestic Solid Fuel Burning Appliances (Wood Fired Heaters)
