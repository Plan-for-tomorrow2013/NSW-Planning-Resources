#### 4.1.2 How to use this part?

Part 4 establishes the objectives and controls that guide residential development, including dwelling houses, secondary dwellings, dual occupancies and semi-detached dwellings, attached dwellings and multi-dwelling housing, residential flat buildings and shop top housing. Part 4 also covers residential amenity controls such as streetscape, safety, privacy and fencing.
