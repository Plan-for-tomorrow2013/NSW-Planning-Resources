### 4.1 Introduction

#### 4.1.1 Background

There are a range of zones in the Camden LGA to provide for a variety of residential accommodation types and densities, both within existing urban areas and urban release areas. This chapter establishes the objectives and controls which will guide the design of residential development in the Camden LGA zoned under the CLEP 2010. This excludes land zoned under State Environmental Planning Policy (Precincts – Western Parkland City) 2021 where separate DCPs apply.
