### 6.8 Architectural diversity and articulation

#### Objectives

O1 To ensure the scale, modulation and façade articulation of development responds to its context.

O2 To achieve architectural diversity and visual interest in development on the site.

#### Development controls

C1 The building length should not exceed 45m.

C2 If the building length exceeds 45m, it should be broken into two or more components, so no length is longer than 45m, before which a minimum 3x3m inset is to be provided at all levels and be treated to provide visual relief.

C3 Each component of a building should have a different architectural character to the street or public domain and be articulated into smaller components at a scale or grain that reflects the interior and exterior uses and circulation of the building and site.
