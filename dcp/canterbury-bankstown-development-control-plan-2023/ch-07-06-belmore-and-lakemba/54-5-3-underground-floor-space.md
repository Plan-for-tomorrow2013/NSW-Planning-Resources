### 5.3	 Underground floor space

#### Objectives

O1. To facilitate large floorplate uses

and permeability at street level.

#### Principles

P1. Uses permitted for underground floor space include uses that generally require large floor areas, including neighbourhood

premises over 1000m² GFA, gym and

P2. Larger sites that can accommodate the above mentioned uses and demonstrate improved streetscape and public domain

P3. The underground uses should not go beyond the building footprint to ensure opportunities for deep soil planting and open space in setback areas are maintained.

P4. Entries to the underground floor space should have an appropriate, active, ground
