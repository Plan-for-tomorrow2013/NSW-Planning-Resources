#### 5.5.4 Basement flood risk management

#### Objectives

O1. To ensure for the utilisation of design

measures including elevated driveways,

overland flow redirection, or waterproofing

design for best practice flood management.

O2. To ensure basements in new developments

on flood prone land affected lots are

designed to preserve the safety of the occupants during a flood prone land event.

#### Controls

C1. Flood waters should be prevented from

entering basements by using the Flood

Planning Level, (1% AEP + 500 mm), or the

flood prone land level, whichever is greater

to set the apex level.

C2. Hydraulic analysis and/or water surface level

checks for all proposed basement entries in

flood-affected areas are required.
