#### 5.5.3 Shelter-in-Place  and Emergency

#### Response Design

#### Principles

P1. For flood prone land affected lots shown on Figure 54, evacuation may not be reliably achieved due to flash flooding. Development should include provision for SIP.

#### Controls

C1. To ensure SIP spaces in new developments

are designed appropriately, the NSW

Government Shelter-in-place  Guideline

(2025) sets out 10 design criteria as listed in

Table 11. Any DA that proposes Shelter-in-

place must demonstrate compliance with

Design Criteria 1-3. Design Criteria 4-10 must

be considered by Council when setting

conditions of consent.

C2. Council will not support the reliance on SIP

for sites that are subject to long-duration of

flash flooding which is greater than 12 hours.
