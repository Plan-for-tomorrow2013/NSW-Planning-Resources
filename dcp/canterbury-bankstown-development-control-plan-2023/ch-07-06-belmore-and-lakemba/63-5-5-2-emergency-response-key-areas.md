#### 5.5.2 Emergency Response Key Areas

#### Controls

C1. Applicants seeking to redevelop within

Spot 1, Spot 5 and Spot 8 must hold a

pre-lodgement meeting with Council to

demonstrate how the future residents on

these sites can safely SIP.

C2. Ensure proposed development at Spot 1

and Spot 5 demonstrates robust design

measures and integration with emergency

response planning that includes

consultation with the NSW State Emergency

Service prior to the lodgment of a

Development Application(DA) with Council.

Note: Emergency Response Key Areas

are locations where development may be

significantly constrained due to:

(a) Lack of viable evacuation routes.

(b) Long-duration flooding of

evacuation route (>12 hours).

(c) High flood prone land hazard (H6

hazard affected sites).
