#### C2 Southern Commercial Core

The Southern Commercial Core is a compact mixed use place with two anchor destinations: the community hub (Ray McCormack Reserve) and the Revesby Workers Club. The community hub will be a welcoming and successful public and cultural destination where people choose to come together for events and social activities.

The streets will be a place where cars travel slowly, making it easier to cross the street and a pleasant place to walk, sit and talk. Active street frontages along the main street (Revesby Place) will combine with the community hub to create a vibrant streetscape.

Variety is also the key to economic resilience and this place will be home to a diversity of building forms, with the tallest buildings next to the railway station. It is recognised the development of the local centre will occur over time, resulting in a rich mixture of old and new buildings with contrasting building heights and architectural styles.
