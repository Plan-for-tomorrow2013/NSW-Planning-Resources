#### C1 Gateway

The accessible railway station is the focal point for commuting, a great public space which connects both sides of the railway line. A distinct place that creates a memorable arrival to the small village centre. The gateway is also a central place of leisure, a place where people can play, relax and socialise in Avalon Reserve and surrounding urban spaces.
