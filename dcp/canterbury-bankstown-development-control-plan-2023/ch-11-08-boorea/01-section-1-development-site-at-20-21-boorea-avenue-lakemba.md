## SECTION 1–DEVELOPMENT SITE AT 20–21 BOOREA AVENUE, LAKEMBA

#### Explanation

The Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 and Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 combine to regulate effective and orderly development, consistent with Connective City 2036.

The Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 is Council's principal planning document. It provides objectives, zones and development standards such as lot sizes, floor space ratios and building heights.

The Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 supports the LEP by providing additional objectives and development controls for any residential accommodation purpose including residential care facilities at 20–21 Boorea Avenue, Lakemba. Note: If applicable to an application, the development controls of Chapter 11.8 of this DCP will prevail if there is an inconsistency with any other development controls in this DCP.

#### Objectives

O1 To protect the amenity of the adjacent residential properties.

O2 To ensure that that the development does not cause overshadowing to adjoining residential properties.

O3 To ensure that the development does not cause loss of acoustic or visual privacy on adjoining residential properties.

O4 To minimise the bulk of development through high quality design and landscaping.

#### Development controls

#### Built form and solar access

1.1 The maximum number of storeys of any building is five storeys (not including any basement).

1.2 The building setback to the southern boundary with Lot 26 DP 13586 is 14m. The area within the setback to this boundary may be used for access and egress, vehicle circulation and landscaping.

1.3 The building setback for the top storey must be a further 6m along the southern boundary with Lot 26 DP 13586.

1.4 Proposed development must retain a minimum of three hours of sunlight between 8.00am and 4.00pm on 21 June for existing primary living areas and to 50% of the principal private open space.

1.5 If a neighbouring dwelling currently receives less than three hours of sunlight, then the proposed development must not reduce the existing level of solar access to that property.

#### Design

1.6 Fencing on the frontage to Boorea Avenue is at least 50% transparent and does not exceed 1.8m in height.

1.7 No fencing is to exceed 1.8m in height around the site.

1.8 Lighting provided on the site should not unreasonably affect the amenity of the adjacent residences.

1.9 Any noise generated from use of the site should not unreasonably affect the adjacent and nearby residents.

1.10 An outdoor roof top terrace is permitted on level 4 within the building setback area. Screening must be used to protect adjoining residential properties from privacy impacts. Landscaping as a secondary privacy measure must also be incorporated on any outdoor roof top terrace.

1.11 Development must address the design principles of the State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021 (Chapter 4).

#### Traffic and parking

1.12 In addition to the car parking and traffic controls relating to nursing homes and residential care facilities in Chapter 3.2 of this DCP, employee and resident parking on Boorea Avenue is to be prevented. All parking requirements must be met on-site.

#### Landscaping

1.13 In addition to the landscaping objectives and controls in Chapter 3.7 of this DCP, a landscape plan by a qualified landscape architect is required.

1.14 Visual impacts of the building as viewed from Boorea Avenue and Wangee Road must be minimised through tree selection of an appropriate height and canopy.

1.15 The Landscape Plan must consider appropriate plant selection and address Council’s Australian White Ibis Management Plan (June 2018).

1.16 Deep soil landscaping capable of accommodating tall tree canopies must be provided along all setbacks.

#### Building services

1.17 Integrate systems, services and utility areas (such as plant rooms, hydrants, other fire related equipment, air conditioners and the like) with the design of the whole development. Development must coordinate materials with those of the building and integrate with landscaping.

1.18 Facilities should not be visually obtrusive. The location and design of substations must be shown on the plans. Substations should be located underground. Where not possible, substations are to be integrated into the building design and concealed from public view.

1.19 Substations must not be located forward of the front building line.
