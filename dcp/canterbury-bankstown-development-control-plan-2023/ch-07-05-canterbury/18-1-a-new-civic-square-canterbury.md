#### 1.	 A new civic square, Canterbury

Square, is proposed at the heart of

the Canterbury Core. The square will accommodate community life and invite people to experience the Local Centre. The square will be edged with active uses to engage pedestrians while having spaces for repose and for active play. Solar access will be balance with shade and a tree canopy requirement will ensure for mature tree growth. Pierson Lane is extended North to free the new civic square from traffic.
