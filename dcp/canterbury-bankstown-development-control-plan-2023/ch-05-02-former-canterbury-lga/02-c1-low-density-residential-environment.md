#### C1 Low density residential environment

To have a low density residential environment in Zone R2 where the typical features are dwelling houses and dual occupancies within a generous landscaped setting. The site cover and building form of development must be compatible with the prevailing suburban character and amenity of this zone. This zone is also the most restrictive in terms of other permitted uses that are considered suitable. These are generally restricted to facilities and services that meet the day-to-day needs of residents.
