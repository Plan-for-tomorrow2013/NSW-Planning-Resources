#### 2. Map of exposure levels

An applicant must submit a 360 degree prediction map of exposure levels within a 300m radius of the proposed site and measuring 1.5m above ground level (existing).

The map must also provide:

(a) information as to the carrier’s existing infrastructure in the area;

(b) an EMR assessment as prescribed by the CA Code; and

(c) compliance evidence or professional certification that the exposure details contained in the application are true and accurate.
