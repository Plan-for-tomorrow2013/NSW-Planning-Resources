### 2.2 Development consent is required to remove any tree:

(a) located on a site listed as a heritage item in Schedule 5 of the Canterbury- Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023; or

(b) located on land included on the Biodiversity Map under the Canterbury- Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023.
