## SECTION 5–LANDSCAPING

#### Objectives

O1 To ensure the provision of adequate deep soil area to support mature tree planting.

O2 To provide a pleasant outlook and contribute to visual privacy between buildings.

O3 To reinforce the desired ‘green and leafy character’ of the precinct.

O4 To assist in heat reduction and provide habitat for fauna.

#### Development controls

#### Deep soil zones

5.1 Deep soil areas should be provided along Croydon Street, Railway Parade and the new links/laneway.

5.2 Street trees with high canopies and a mature height of minimum 5m are to be provided along the length of the new links/laneway to frame views.

5.3 Basements are to be contained within the building footprint.

5.4 High quality landscape and canopy trees shall be provided within deep soil area and within street setbacks.

5.5 Street trees in accordance with Council’s street tree policy/public domain plan are to be provided to Lakemba Street, Railway Parade, Croydon Street and the new laneway.

#### Communal open space

5.6 Communal open spaces are to be provided either in the rear setbacks or the wider open space areas.

5.7 An area of central communal open space with minimum dimensions of 28m  28m comprising a minimum of 900m2 of contiguous communal open space is to be provided in the R4 zone.

#### View corridors

5.8 The built form shall not obscure the view corridors along the new links.

5.9 The landscape within view corridors should frame views to public open spaces and should not block eye line level views.
