## SECTION 3–BUILDING ENVELOPES

#### Objectives

O1 To encourage lively business centres capable of accommodating a mix of retail, commercial and residential uses.

O2 To achieve an appropriate distribution of height across the precinct within the LEP height controls that achieve transition, appropriate scale and reasonable amenity.

O3 To reduce the apparent bulk and scale of buildings by modulation of form and articulation of facades.

O4 To achieve high quality spatial and amenity outcomes.

O5 To allow for view sharing.

O6 To maximise the opportunities for sufficient and high quality landscape outcomes.

#### Development controls

#### Building envelope

3.1 Future developments should be consistent with ADG recommedations for building separation. Note: the achievement of the maximum FSR is dependent on satisfying the other objectives and controls in this DCP.

#### Building depth

3.2 The maximum overall building depth for residential uses is 18m glass line to glass line or 22m balcony edge to balcony edge.

#### Building length

3.3 The maximum building length is to be 40m with articulation provided through indentations every 10–15m. The proportions of indentations should comply with ADG objective (4B-2) where width to depth ratio should be a minimum of 2:1.

#### Building height

3.4 The maximum height allowable under the LEP is intended to accommodate all built form including plant, lift and stair access and rooftop communal open space and structures.

3.5 The floor to ceiling height of retail/commercial floors is to be a minimum of 3.3m with floor-to-floor height of minimum 3.7m.

3.6 The floor to ceiling height of all residential floors is to be a minimum of 2.7m with floor-to-floor height of minimum 3.1m.

#### Building density

3.7 The maximum floor space ratio shall comply with the Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environment Plan 2023.

3.8 The maximum floor space ratio may not be achievable if adverse visual, acoustic or privacy amenity or overshadowing impacts occur to adjacent dwellings, the open space or streetscape in the area.

#### Street setbacks and street wall heights

3.9 The minimum ground level setbacks are to be in accordance with Figure 3b setbacks and upper level setbacks.

3.10 Buildings with commercial and retail uses on the ground floor level may build to the boundary line with nil setbacks to both Lakemba and Croydon Streets.

3.11 Residential ground floor uses should accommodate front terrace areas and landscape gardens and provide reasonable amenity.

3.12 The street setback area is to be free from any projections or encroachments from any part of new buildings where possible.

3.13 Street wall heights shall be relative to the building height in storeys as per Figure 3a. Otherwise, the street wall height shall be the same as the building height.

#### Upper level setbacks

3.14 The minimum upper levels setbacks are to be 3m as per Figure 3b Setbacks and Upper Levels Setbacks.

3.15 Upper level setbacks must be free of any projections or encroachments from any part of the building.

3.16 The setback area is to be used for private open space where appropriate. The edge is to be created by landscaped planters.

3.17 All plant rooms and lift overruns are to be positioned to minimise their visibility. The preference is for all plant to be located within the building envelope or basements rather than the roof.

#### Setbacks and upper level setbacks to the overland flow path – Zone E1

3.18 Setbacks to the overland flow path edge for developments facing the north-western boundary of the precinct should be consistent with the overland flow path objectives and controls in Chapter 11.4 of this DCP.

3.19 A 6m setback from the overland flow path edge is required to the ground floor level to allow for the provision of an elevated walkway with adequate public interface in line with Figures 8a and 8b.

3.20 A 3m setback from the overland flow path edge is required for all floors above ground floor level to allow for adequate solar access to the elevated walkway.

3.21 An additional 3m setback from the building edge is to be provided above the street wall height as per Figure 3a: Street Wall Heights.

#### Separation

3.22 Provide separation distances between building forms in accordance with the ADG recommendations for building separation.

3.23 Deep soil zones shall be provided within the separation distances between the residential flat buildings within R4 zone.

3.24 When it is not possible to achieve deep soil requirements as suggested by the ADG objectives, possible alternative forms of planting can be provided on top of podium/structures.
