### 6.2 Outdoor play areas must:

(a) locate on a land gradient that is predominantly flat;

(b) provide access to shade, particularly between 9.30am and 3.00pm during summer months. This may be in the form of a shade structure or natural shade from trees;

(c) consider the surface treatment in accordance with best practice guidelines in early childhood environments.
