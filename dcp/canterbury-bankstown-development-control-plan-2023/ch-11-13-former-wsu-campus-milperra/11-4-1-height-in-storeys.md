### 4.1 Height in storeys

#### Objectives

O1 To ensure consistency in character with the surrounding residential areas and avoid poor amenity outcomes.

#### Development controls

C1 The maximum number of storeys is as follows:

(a) 9m LEP height – 2 storeys

(b) 11m LEP height – 3 storeys.
