### 10.8 The minimum setback to the secondary street frontage is:

(a) 3m for a building wall; and

(b) 5.5m for a garage or carport that is attached to the building wall.

10.9 For the portion of the building wall that has a wall height less than or equal to 7m, the minimum setback to the side boundary of the site is 0.9m.

10.10 For the portion of the building wall that has a wall height greater than 7m, the minimum setback to the side boundary of the site is 1.5m.

#### Setbacks in Zone R3

10.11 The minimum setback for a building wall to the primary street frontage is 6m.

10.12 The minimum setback for a building wall to the secondary street frontage is 6m.

10.13 For a single or two storey building, the minimum setback to the side and rear boundaries of the site is 0.6 multiplied by the wall height.

10.14 For a building with three or more storeys, the minimum setback to the side and rear boundaries of the site is 4.5m provided the average setback is 0.6 multiplied by the wall height.

10.15 The minimum setback for a basement level to the side and rear boundaries of the site is 2m.

10.16 The minimum setback for a driveway to the side and rear boundaries of the site is 1m.
