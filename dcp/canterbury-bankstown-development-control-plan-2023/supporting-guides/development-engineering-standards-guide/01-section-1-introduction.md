## SECTION 1–INTRODUCTION

#### Explanation

The Development Engineering Standards Guide supports the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 by providing additional requirements to protect the integrity of Council's infrastructure in situations where it is impacted upon by building and subdivision development.

#### Objectives

O1 To ensure that development provides a satisfactory level of engineering infrastructure.

O2 To minimise the impact of development on surrounding sites, roads and existing stormwater systems.

O3 To protect and construct the interface, under Council supervision and to Council's satisfaction, between development and Council's assets including footways, roadways and drainage systems.
