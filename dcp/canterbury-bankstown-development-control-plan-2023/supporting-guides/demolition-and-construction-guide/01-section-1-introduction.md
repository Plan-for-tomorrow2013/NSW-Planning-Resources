## SECTION 1–INTRODUCTION

#### Explanation

The Demolition and Construction Guide supports the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 by providing additional development controls that development must incorporate to ensure the demolition and construction of buildings or work are safe and do not adversely impact Canterbury-Bankstown.

This Guide is to be read in conjunction with State legislation, policies and guidelines including but not limited to:

• Contaminated Land Management Act 1997

• Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997.

#### Objectives

O1 To ensure that demolition is carried out in accordance with the relevant legislation and guidelines.

O2 To ensure that demolition procedures are safe and environmentally efficient.

O3 To ensure that demolition does not have an adverse impact on surrounding residents, land, buildings, or footpaths and roadways.

O4 To preserve significant trees and heritage items.

O5 To encourage the recycling and reuse of building materials.

O6 To ensure the use of licenced contractors to undertake demolition work and asbestos removal work.

O7 To ensure the maximum amount of waste materials resulting from demolition and construction are reused and/or recycled.
