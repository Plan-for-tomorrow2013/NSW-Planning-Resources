## 6. Glossary of Terms

### 6.1	 Development Types

| Type | Definition | Commonly Known As |
|---|---|---|
| Multi Dwelling Housing | Means three or more dwellings (whether attached or detached) on one lot of land, each with access at ground level, but does not include a residential flat building | Villas, Townhouses, Terrace House, Manor Houses |

### 6.2	 Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Bulk bins | Large bins which have four swivel wheels so can be moved in any direction. Usually greater than 660L bins. |
| Bulky waste | Large household items such as furniture, white goods and mattresses. |
| Classified Road | A high capacity urban road, as defined by NSW Roads and Martime Services. |
| Collect and return service | Service where council (or its contractors) access the bin storage area or temporary bin collection area and cart bins to the kerbside to be serviced. Bins are then returned to the bin storage area (or temporary collection area). The collection vehicle needs a safe parking spot on the kerb. |
| Communal bin storage area | Bin storage area(s) which stores allocated bins for the entire development and can be accessed by all residents and occupants. |
| HRV | Heavy Rigid Vehicle as per Australia Standard 2890.2 Parking Facilities: Off Street Commercial Vehicle Facilities. |
| Indemnity or Positive covenant or Section 88B certificate | A legal agreement ensuring that a party providing services to a particular property will not be held responsible for any loss or damage to such property as a result of the routine provision of the service. |
| Internal waste storage and separation of waste, recycling and compost | This means the separate recycling and garbage (2x 20L) bins for the dwelling’s kitchen. This is where the residents dispose / store the waste and recycling before taking it to the larger communal bins. There should also be sufficient space for a kitchen caddy to store food waste within the kitchen. |
| Kerbside collection | All allocated bins are presented kerbside by individual residents for collection. |
| Layback | The section of kerb that has been removed and replaced in concrete to allow easier access to the kerbside. Also known as a gutter crossing. |
| Mobile garbage bins (MGB’s) | Small bins which have two wheels so can only be moved forwards and backwards (not sideways). |
| Nominated collection point | The nominated point where waste and recycling are collected from by the service vehicle. |

| Term On-site collection | Definition Collection occurs within the development site’s boundary in a nominated collecting area. |
|---|---|
| Route of travel | The travel path for the waste collection vehicle when entering the site to access the nominated collection point and leaving the site after the waste has been collected. |
| Source Separation | The separation, by residents, of different recyclable items into separate bins or cages. |
| Temporary bin collection area | Area where bins are transferred to be temporarily stored for collection. Bins are required to be transferred back to the bin storage area as soon as possible after collection occurs. This bin transfer is undertaken by a caretaker. |
| Vehicular Crossing | The concrete vehicular crossing providing access across the Council controlled nature strip, consisting of a crossing and a layback. |
