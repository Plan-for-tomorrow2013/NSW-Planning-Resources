#### 13 Access for Inspections

The parties are to permit access to their property by the other party and the legal representatives and expert advisors of the other party, the local council or the Heritage Council of NSW, provided that access is on reasonable notice (given to the party’s legal representative or, if the party does not have a legal representative, direct to the party) and at a reasonable

time. The parties are permitted to supervise any such access.
