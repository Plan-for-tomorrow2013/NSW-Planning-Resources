## SECTION 2–GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

#### Kerbside collections

Kerbside collections occur along the street frontage of a property. This is not Council’s preference as bins on the kerbside detract of the overall amenity of our neighbourhoods and reduce accessibility along streets. Kerbside collections are only accepted when all other options have been exhausted.

#### Collect and return service

A collect and return service involves Council staff manoeuvring bins from an onsite storage area to the truck for collection and then returning the bins onsite. The following requirements of the collect and return service are to be considered and incorporated into the site:

• The use of 660Lor 1100L bins for general waste and recycling;

• All allocated bins are to be stored in a communal area at ground level;

• Bins are collected directly from the communal bin storage area or temporary collection area (if required).

• A safe and convenient bin-carting route is to be provided (see specifications in 5.2.3);

• Garden organics bins are required to be presented to the kerbside by residents or a caretaker/property manager for collection; and

• Bulky waste storage area is desirable, with material collected from the kerbside or directly from the storage area if located less than 5m from the truck.

Plans accompanying the development application are to identify the following, with dimensions:

• Communal bin storage area (see Section 5.2.1);

• Temporary collection area (if required, see Section 5.3.2);

• Bin carting route (see section 5.3.3), including collection vehicle parking location; and

• Bulky waste room (optional, see 5.2.4).
