## 1. Collect and Return Service

For low-rise developments, the following requirements of the collect and return service are to be considered and incorporated into the site planning of the development:

•	 The use of 660L or 1,110L bulk bins for general waste and recycling;

•	 All allocated bins are to be stored in a communal area at ground level;

•	 Bins would be collected directly from the communal bin storage area or temporary collection area (if required);

•	 A safe and convenient bin-carting route is to be provided;

•	 Garden organics bins would be required to be presented to the kerbside by residents or a caretaker/property manager for collection; and

•	 Bulky waste storage area is required, with material collected from the kerbside or directly from the storage area.

Plans accompanying the development application are to identify the following, with dimensions:

•	 Communal bin storage area (see

Section 5.3.1);

•	 Temporary collection area (if required, see Section 5.3.2); and

•	 Bin carting route (see section 5.3.3), including collection vehicle parking location which is not blocking driveways and traffic.
