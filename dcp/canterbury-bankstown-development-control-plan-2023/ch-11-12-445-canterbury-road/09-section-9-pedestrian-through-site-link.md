## SECTION 9–PEDESTRIAN THROUGH SITE LINK

#### Development controls

9.1 The pedestrian through site link shall be a minimum width of 6m and dedicated to Council, in accordance with the planning agreement.

9.2 Landscaping shall reinforce the entry to this midblock link from Canterbury Road and the rear laneway. Careful consideration must be given to landscape selection and lighting to ensure surveillance is maintain from the street and building.

9.3 The design of the link must carefully consider the 2.5m change in topography of the site and ensure that materials and finishes selection are of high quality and durable. The inclusion of a lift, use of tactile surfaces for visually impaired persons and other accessibility treatments must be considered in the design.

9.4 The pedestrian through site link is to include provision of public art in accordance with a Public Art Strategy submitted for the site as part of a future application for a new hospital development.
