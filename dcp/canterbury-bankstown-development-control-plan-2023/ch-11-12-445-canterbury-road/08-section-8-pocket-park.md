## SECTION 8–POCKET PARK

#### Development controls

8.1 The pocket park will be a minimum area of 205m2 and provide a place of repose, be safe and easy to access and have high amenity using planting, street furniture, public art and lighting. Planting must be selected carefully to ensure that surveillance is ensured.
