## SECTION 10–ACCESS AND MOVEMENT

#### Objectives

O1 To provide a functional, safe and easy to navigate movement network throughout the site and hospital where pedestrian and cyclists are given priority, with due regard to emergency vehicle movement, during the operating hours of the hospital.

O2 To provide functional and clear access and set down area for emergency vehicles that minimises potential conflict with pedestrians and other vehicles on the site.

O3 To ensure safety for pedestrians crossing to the pocket park from vehicles entering the sites, particularly emergency vehicles.

O4 To provide for vehicle and pedestrian access from Stanley Street that will facilitate movements, servicing, parking and appropriate addressing of the buildings

O5 To ensure for universal design within the building and site, signage devices and wayfinding elements.

#### Development controls

10.1 To deliver a rear two-way laneway of a minimum 9 metre width from Stanley Street through to the eastern boundary in anticipation of future connection to Una Street. This public road will be dedicated to Council in accordance with the planning agreement (see Figure 7).

10.2 The design of the laneway shall incorporate manoeuvring paths to accommodate the largest vehicles that will use the site including emergency, waste collection and delivery vehicles.

10.3 The entry to the basement car parking off the laneway shall be located to prioritise pedestrian safety.

### 10.4 The building entry from Canterbury Road should:

(a) be at the same general level as the footpath and accessible directly from the street, and

(b) provide a positive street address in the form of entries, lobbies and clear glazing, which positively contribute to street activity and promote passive surveillance.
