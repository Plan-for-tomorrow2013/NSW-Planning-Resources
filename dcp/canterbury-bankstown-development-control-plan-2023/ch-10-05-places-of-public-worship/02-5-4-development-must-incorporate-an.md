### 5.4 Development must incorporate an innovative roof design that:

(a) achieves a unique and contemporary architectural appearance; and

(b) combines high quality materials and finishes.

#### Front fences

5.5 The maximum fence height for a front fence is 1.8m.

5.6 The external appearance of a front fence along the street boundary of the site must ensure:

(a) the section of the front fence that comprises solid construction (not including solid piers) does not exceed a fence height of 1m above ground level (existing); and

(b) the remaining height of the front fence comprises open style construction such as spaced timber pickets or wrought iron that enhance and unify the building design.
