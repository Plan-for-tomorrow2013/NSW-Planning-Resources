### 3.1 Minimum frontage

#### Objectives

O1 To ensure efficient vehicular access to parking and servicing and reduce driveway crossings.

O2 To facilitate efficient building envelopes that achieve optimum density.

#### Development controls

C1 Where redevelopment is proposed in the E1 zone of the LEP, a minimum frontage of at least 18m shall be provided.

C2 Where redevelopment is proposed in the E3 zone identified as ‘Area 9’ on the Special Provisions Map, the minimum site frontage is 30m.
