## Section 2 UNDERSTANDING PLACE

This section establishes the unique social,

#### spatial and urban context that reflects

#### the Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct.

#### Proposed development must contribute

to delivering the Vision for Punchbowl and

#### Wiley Park by adhering to the Structure

#### Plan and demonstrating how a site's

#### development contributes to the character

areas and has regard for Connecting with

#### Country principles.
