## SECTION 4–PARKING

#### Consideration

C1 A matter of consideration is whether sufficient off-street parking is provided.

#### Development controls

4.1 The minimum number of car parking spaces required for sex services premises is 1.5 car space per service room.

4.2 Stacked parking is not acceptable. Parking areas must be located, designed and lit to maximise safety of workers and clients.
