#### 19E		 Secondary Dwellings within HCAs and Heritage

#### Items

19E.1	 	Secondary Dwellings within HCAs and Heritage Items

### SECONDARY DWELLINGS IN THE VICINITY OF HERITAGE ITEMS OR HCAS

#### Objectives

1	 To ensure new secondary

dwellings respect the

established streetscape,

and the historical patterns

of development within

the HCA, including

characteristic rhythm and

built form spacing. 2	 To ensure secondary

dwellings do not dominate

the Heritage Item and

are designed to be

complementary to the

Item, including its setting.
