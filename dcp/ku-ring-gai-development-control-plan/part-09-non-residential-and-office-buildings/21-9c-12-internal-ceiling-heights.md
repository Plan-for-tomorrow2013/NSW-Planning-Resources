### 9C.12  INTERNAL CEILING HEIGHTS

#### Objectives

#### Controls Offices

1	 To ensure internal ceiling

of use in the future.

1 All office developments are to comply with the following minimum

ceiling heights, measured from finished floor level (FFL) to finished

ceiling level (FCL):

i) 3.5m for ground floor / street level retail or commercial uses;

2	 To ensure internal ceiling

ii)	 3m for all other floors for commercial use. 2 Internal ceiling heights and slab levels are to be coordinated with

external height requirements and key datum lines. External building

elements requiring coordination include:

i) heights, datum and parapet lines set by the context or structure

ii)	 cornices and string courses of adjacent heritage buildings;

iii)	 exterior awning levels or colonnade heights.
