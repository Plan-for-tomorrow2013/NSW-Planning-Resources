### 24D.1  GENERAL

### D

#### Objectives

#### Controls

Controls 1 - 3 below are only relevant where it is proposed to undertake

development adjacent to or over an existing drainage system (including a

natural waterbody). In such situations the following controls apply: 1 Development is to be kept clear of floodways.

1	 To ensure existing

stormwater flow paths

and drainage systems

are preserved during all

rainfall events.

2 Development is not to impede overland flows. 3 Development in the vicinity of drainage systems are not to result in:

i) increased incidences of flooding;

2	 To ensure natural

watercourses and

floodplain processes are

maintained.

ii)	 damage to property and belongings;

iii)	 risk to life;

iv)	 loss of environmental amenity and integrity; or

3	 To ensure flows maintain

or mimic natural or

pre-development

conditions.

v)	 difficulty in maintaining or upgrading an associated drainage

system.

4	 To enhance the

environmental function of

urban creeks and riparian

lands.

Note: There may also be shallow overland flow paths and depressions

that may not be formally identified in the flood planning area that are to be

considered in design.

5	 To preserve the integrity of

existing open waterbodies. 6	 To minimise the

detrimental effects on

neighbouring properties. 7	 To ensure accessibility

to existing and future

underground piped

drainage systems is

preserved for maintenance

and construction

purposes. 8	 The impact of flood events

is not increased. 9	 To protect new

development from

inundation or flood

damage.
