### 24D.5  TENNIS COURTS AND OTHER SPORTING

### SURFACES

#### Controls

Tennis courts will not generally be permitted over drainage systems,

however, in certain limited circumstances, Council may consider such a

proposal acceptable. A tennis court in such a location is to comply with

the controls set out below. 1 Tennis courts are not to be constructed within the riparian land

associated with any watercourse (see ‘Natural Resources - Riparian

Lands Map’ in the KLEP).
