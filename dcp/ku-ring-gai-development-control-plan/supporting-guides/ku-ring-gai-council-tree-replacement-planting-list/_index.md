### Ku-ring-gai Council Tree Replacement Planting List

Trees and vegetation contribute to local amenity and help make our urban environment liveable by ameliorating climatic extremes, improving air quality, providing habitat, reducing erosion and salinity.

The following species of trees have been selected to help residents select a replacement tree to plant within your property as part of the conditions of your permit or in preparing a development application.

This list is to provide an indicative species palette suitable for planting in private property within Ku-ring-gai Council (LGA). Appropriate species are not limited to this list and should an alternative species be identified, the species must be suitable for the intended location and be approved by Council Officers.

Bush Fire Prone Land: provides guidance of suitability for planting in Bush Fire Prone Lands (BFPL). ✓ = suitable for BFPL; x = not suitable for BFPL. Please note that this guidance is based on for low-moderate intensity fires and ALL planting in BFPL should be undertaken in accordance with the NSW RFS  Planning for Bushfire Protection Guidelines, Appendix 4.

Origin: Australian Native = native to Australia, not naturally found in the Ku-ring-gai area; Locally indigenous = species naturally occurring in the Ku-ring-gai area. For Locally indigenous species, the relevant Threatened Ecological Community has been noted help guide appropriate planting in areas identified in Council’s GreenWeb mapping. BGHF = Blue Gum High Forest; STIF = Sydney Turpentine Ironbark Forest; DUFF = Duffy's Forest

Size at maturity: Representing tree size at maturity. Size is approximate to Sydney conditions. Various factors can influence the tree height and form.

| Size | Height |
|---|---|
| Small | Up to 6metres |
| Medium | 7 – 12metres |
| Large | > 12metres |
