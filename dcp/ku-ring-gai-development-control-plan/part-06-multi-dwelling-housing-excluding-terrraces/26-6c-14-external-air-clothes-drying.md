### 6C.14  EXTERNAL AIR CLOTHES DRYING

### FACILITIES

#### Controls Private drying facilities

Provide one external air clothes drying area for each dwelling.

1

Note: Clothes drying areas do not form part of the required 25sqm private

open space.

The external air clothes drying area is not to be located at the street

2

frontage and is to be screened from all public domain areas and

common areas.

#### Shared drying facilities

Where shared air clothes drying lines are provided, they are to

3

be located within common areas, but are not to form part of any

communal open space and is not to be visible from any public

domain.
