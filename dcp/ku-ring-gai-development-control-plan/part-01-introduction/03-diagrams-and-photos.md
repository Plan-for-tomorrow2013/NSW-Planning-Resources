#### 3. Diagrams and Photos

Diagrams and photos are used to illustrate particular elements sought by

the controls. There may be other elements within the photos or diagrams

that are not consistent with other controls in the DCP as they are drawn

from a variety of locations and development types, not always consistent

with the objectives for Ku-ring-gai. The photos and diagrams should

therefore only be used for guidance about the particular elements.
