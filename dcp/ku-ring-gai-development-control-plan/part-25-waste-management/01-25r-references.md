### WASTE MANAGEMENT

### INTRODUCTION

This Part guides development in meeting the aims and objectives within

the KLEP. This Part applies to all development types, unless stated

otherwise.

This Part seeks to outline how developments are to manage waste in Ku-

ring-gai. This includes, but is not limited to;

• waste storage;

• waste collection;

• layout of waste and recycling rooms;

• waste management for particular developments; and

• management of bulky goods waste.
