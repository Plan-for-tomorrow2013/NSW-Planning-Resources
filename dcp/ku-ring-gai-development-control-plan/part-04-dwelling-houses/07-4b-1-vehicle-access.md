#### Vehicular Access

1 Wherever possible, driveways must be located so that driver and

pedestrian sight lines are clear. 2 The driveway must be designed so that vehicles may exit the

property in a forward direction where:

i) the access is located on a major roadway; or

ii)	 the property is a battle-axe allotment; or

iii)	 sight lines are restricted (such as at curves or crests).
