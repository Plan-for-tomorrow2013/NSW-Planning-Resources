## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Land to which this Part applies

This Part applies to development in industrial zones under Cumberland Local Environmental Plan 2021.

### 1.2 Purpose of this Part

This Part is intended to provide design requirements and guide the assessment of the range of uses permitted in the industrial zones.
