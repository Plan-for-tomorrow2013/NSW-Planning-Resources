### 4.3 WHICH DEVELOPMENTS ARE ADVERTISED?

The following types of Development Applications are required to be advertised in the local Dungog Newspaper:

Subdivisions Boundary adjustments Tourist Developments New Commercial Developments Development  of  Listed  Heritage  Item  or  significant  development  within  a  heritage conservation area. Medium Density Development Dual Occupancy Farm Gate Sales Applications that are considered to be contentious or of public interest
