## 12. THE KEEPING OF DOGS FOR COMMERCIAL PURPOSES

This plan, which may be cited as “Dungog Development Control Plan No. 1” – Development Control Plan for the Keeping of Dogs for Commercial Purposes, constitutes a development control plan as provided for by Section 72 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act, 1979.
