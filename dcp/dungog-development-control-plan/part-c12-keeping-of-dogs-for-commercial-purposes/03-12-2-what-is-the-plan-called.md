### 12.2. WHAT IS THE PLAN CALLED?

The Plan may be cited as Development Control Plan (DCP) No.28 – Keeping of Dogs for Commercial Purposes.

The DCP was adopted by Council on 19 February 2002 and became effective on 20 February 2002.

The DCP has been prepared in accordance with the provisions of Dungog Shire Council Local Environmental Plan 2006 and the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979.
