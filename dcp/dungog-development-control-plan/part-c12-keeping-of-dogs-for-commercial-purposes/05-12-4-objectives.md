### 12.4. OBJECTIVES

The objectives of the Plan are:

• Provide a standard for the establishment of Commercial dog keeping premises.

• Through the provision of high quality premises, address the impact of commercial dog keeping on surrounding properties and occupants.

• To enable the provision of consistent and prompt advice from Council to assist persons in the planning, design and management of premises to be used for the commercial keeping of dogs.
