### 1.11 Introduced species means a translocated or alien  species

occurring at a place outside its historically known natural range as a result of intentional or accidental dispersal by human activities.

Notes

In this Charter, words for which a definition is provided are printed in italics

1.21. Inclusion of the concept of natural disturbance is problematical, but it is necessary because conservation decisions are often needed after natural extreme ‘catastrophic’ events. Human modification of the natural environment often contributes to the ‘catastrophic’ effects.

The appropriate use of Conservation processes is described in Part C. The term ‘rehabilitation’ has not been used in this Charter because it is widely used in other land management contexts which are not necessarily connected with natural heritage conservation.
