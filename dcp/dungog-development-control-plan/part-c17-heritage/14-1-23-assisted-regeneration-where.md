### 1.23. Assisted regeneration, where

there is some assistance by human intervention to accelerate the process of recovery, e.g., by removing threatening processes, may be justified under the same principles as those for restoration.

1.24 and 1.26.The time frame that would apply to the past state as reference for restoration and reinstatement is not specified; this should be determined for each situation through the conservation policy.
