### 1.29 Protection means taking care

of a place by maintenance and by managing impacts to ensure that natural significance is retained.

1.30 Maintenance means the continuous protective care of the

communities, including interactions with the non-living environment, that result in existing ecosystems and bring about changes in ecosystems over time.

1.17 Earth processes means the interactions, changes and evolutionary development of geodiversity over time.

1.18 Evolutionary processes means genetically-based processes by which life forms change and develop over generations
