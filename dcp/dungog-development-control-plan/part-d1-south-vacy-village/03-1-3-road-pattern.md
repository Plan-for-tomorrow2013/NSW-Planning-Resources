### 1.3. ROAD PATTERN

(a) Future subdivisions are to demonstrate a road layout generally in the position shown on the DCP map.  Where it is proposed to substantially deviate from the location shown, justification supporting the change, based upon detailed site survey, is to be submitted to Council for consideration.

(b) Roads are to be provided by the Developer in such a manner as to ensure the objectives off this development Control Plan are achieved.
