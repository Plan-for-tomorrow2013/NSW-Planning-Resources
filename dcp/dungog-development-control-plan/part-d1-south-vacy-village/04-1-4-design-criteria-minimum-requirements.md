### 1.4 DESIGN CRITERIA – (MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS)

(a) Roads must be 6.5m bitumen seal with 1m gravel shoulders on each side to enable vehicles to pass in opposite directions.

(b) The capacity of sealed roads, bridges & culverts must be sufficient to carry a fully loaded fire fighting vehicle (approximately 28 tonnes or 9 tonnes per axle).  Emergency paths must have a design capacity of 14 tonnes or 4.5 tonnes per axle. Culverts and creek crossings to nominate load rating.

(c) Curves must have a minimum 6m inner radius.

(d) The minimum distance between inner and outer curves should be 6m.

(e) Roads must be clearly signposted and buildings clearly numbered.
