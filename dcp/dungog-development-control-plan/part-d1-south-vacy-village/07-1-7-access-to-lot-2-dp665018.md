### 1.7 ACCESS TO LOT 2 DP665018

(a) The proponent of the subdivision of Lot 8 DP 1009184 is to provide an allotment of land 20m in width to Council generally in the location shown on the DCP Map (labelled A) which is approximately 190m in length, at no cost to the Council.

(b) The proponent of the subdivision of Lot 2 DP 665018 will be required, as a condition of development consent, to construct a road to Council’s standards (as provided for in this DCP) so as to provide access to the subject created allotments, at no cost to the Council, over that parcel of land identified in 9(a) above.
