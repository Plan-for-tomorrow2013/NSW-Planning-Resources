### 1.8 BUILDING ENVELOPES AND SETBACKS

Applications for subdivision of land require the identification of building envelopes which shall comply with the following minimum criteria:

a) 60 metre setback from Gresford Road

b) 30 metre setback from all other roads

c) 25 metre setback from an internal property boundary

d) 40 metre setback from a watercourse, and

e) have a minimum area of 2500m2.
