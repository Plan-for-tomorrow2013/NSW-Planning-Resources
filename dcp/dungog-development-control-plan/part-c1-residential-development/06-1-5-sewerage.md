### 1.5  SEWERAGE

Where that land is serviced by Council's reticulated sewer, the development is to be connected to the service at the applicant’s expense. Note headworks and water demand charges may apply.

Where the land is not serviced by reticulated sewer, the development is to be serviced by an approved on-site sewerage management facility. Council's Department of Environmental Services should be contacted regarding on-site effluent disposal requirements. Applications

for sewerage management facilities must be lodged prior to or in conjunction with residential development applications.
