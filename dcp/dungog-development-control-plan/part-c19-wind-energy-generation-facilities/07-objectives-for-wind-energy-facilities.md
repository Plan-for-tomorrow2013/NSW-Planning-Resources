#### 7.        OBJECTIVES FOR WIND ENERGY FACILITIES:

• To provide the opportunity for the development of suitably sized and located wind energy generation facilities within Dungog Shire.

• To provide for development that will not impact significantly on non participating residences and key vantage points in relation to visual amenity, noise, blade glint or flicker and overshadowing.

• To ensure the development of wind energy facilities that minimise the negative impacts and maximise the positive benefits for the natural and human environment and the local economy.

• To achieve outcomes from wind energy generation development that reflect the objectives of the Dungog Shire Council Local Environment Plan 2006.
