#### 8. NEIGHBOUR NOTIFICATION

All applications for wind energy facilities covered by this plan will require the written notification of landowners within a 3 kilometre radius and advertising in the local newspaper for a minimum of 14 days. Where the proposal is identified as integrated development, advertising will be for a minimum of 28 days.
