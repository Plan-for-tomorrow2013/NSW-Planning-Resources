### 7.4 VACANT LAND

Lawfully created vacant land parcels within a buffer zone of an existing lawfully commenced development will not be sterilised   and single residential dwellings will be permitted on these parcels.  The location of single resident dwellings will be subject to consideration of the effect of residing within the buffer area. A buffer element that takes the form of immature landscape planting cannot be relied on in all cases.
