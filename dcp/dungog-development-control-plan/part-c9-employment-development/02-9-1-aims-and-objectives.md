### 9.1 AIMS & OBJECTIVES

In adopting this policy, Council has the following objectives, which will be taken into consideration with regard to employment development:

¾ To encourage economic development and the creation of employment opportunities within the Shire.

¾ To ensure that employment development does not adversely affect the amenity of any public place or adjoining property.

¾ To encourage good design and appearance of employment development consistent with Council's desire to enhance the character of the Shire.
