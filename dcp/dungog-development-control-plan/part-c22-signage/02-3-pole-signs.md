## 3. Pole signs

Pole or pylon advertising structures and associated advertisements require the submission and approval of a development application. These will be assessed on their merits. Council will specifically consider the impact of the structure on the amenity of the locality, the size, shape and scale of the proposed advertisement and height of the structure in comparison to buildings located on and around the subject land. The advertising structure and advertisement must be wholly located within the boundary of the subject land.
