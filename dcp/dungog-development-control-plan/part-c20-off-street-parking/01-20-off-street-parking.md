## 20.   OFF STREET PARKING

This plan, which may be cited as "Dungog Development Control Plan No. 1" – Off Street Parking, constitutes a Development Control Plan as provided for by part 3 division 6 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act, 1979.

This section of the Development Control Plan applies to all areas within Dungog Shire.
