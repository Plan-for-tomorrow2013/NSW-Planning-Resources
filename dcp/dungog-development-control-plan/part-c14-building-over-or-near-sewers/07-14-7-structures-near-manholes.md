### 14.7 STRUCTURES NEAR MANHOLES

14.7.1 The minimum clearance distance from the centre of the manhole to a structure shall be in accordance with Drawing WS0023.

14.7.2 No sewer manhole shall be enclosed on more than three (3) sides. The fourth side shall be left fully open and clear of structures or associated works at all times.

14.7.3 A minimum three (3) metres vertical clearance shall be allowed above the surface of the manhole cover.

14.7.4 Minimum path width required for access to manhole for maintenance requirements shall be 900 mm.

14.7.5 All works carried out adjacent to sewer mains and manholes are not to damage or destabilise the mains or manholes.
