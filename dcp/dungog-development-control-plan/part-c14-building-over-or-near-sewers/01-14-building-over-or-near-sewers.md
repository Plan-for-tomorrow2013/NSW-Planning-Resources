## 14.  BUILDING OVER OR NEAR SEWERS

This plan, which may be cited as “Dungog Development Control Plan No. 1” – Building Over Sewers, constitutes a Development Control Plan as provided for by Section 72 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act, 1979.
