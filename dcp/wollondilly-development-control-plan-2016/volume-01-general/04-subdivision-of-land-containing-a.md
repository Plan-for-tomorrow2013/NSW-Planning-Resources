#### 4. Subdivision of land containing a heritage item and/or land within a heritage conservation area or a Landscape Conservation Area.

1\. Must not compromise or adversely affect any historic layout of the subject lot and heritage significance of the original lot pattern.

2\. Must not compromise the curtilage of any heritage item or significant complimentary building, garden, driveway or other relic.

3\. Where a heritage impact assessment is required, it must consider the likely location of future buildings and/or building envelopes.
