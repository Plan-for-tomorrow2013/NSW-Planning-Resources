#### 9. Safety and Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design Objectives

1\. To achieve safe buildings and public spaces which discourage criminal activity.

2\. To achieve buildings and places designed to ensure that women and girls feel safe.

#### Controls

1\. All development must be designed in accordance with the Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design (CPTED) Guidelines including allowance for casual surveillance of streets and other public places.

2\. All development within WCP must consider design elements that contribute to the creation of a sense of community ownership of public spaces.

3\. Security lighting shall be required around buildings and open space areas.

4\. Buildings are to be configured such that no areas of potential concealment are available for criminal activities.
