### 1.1 Introduction

Clause 6.3 of Wollondilly Local Environmental Plan requires a development control plan to address certain matters prior to the grant of development consent for the development of land within an Urban Release Area. This volume details how this Development Control Plan satisfies the requirements of this clause across its volumes.
