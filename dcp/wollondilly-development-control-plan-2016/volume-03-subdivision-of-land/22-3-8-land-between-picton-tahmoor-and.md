### 3.8 Land Between Picton, Tahmoor and Thirlmere (commonly known as PTTAG)

#### Application

1\. This section applies to the land identified on the map below:

#### Objectives

1\. To ensure future subdivision of the land has regard to the capacity of downstream

2\. To ensure aboriginal heritage is appropriately considered in the assessment of subdivisions

#### Controls

1\. Prior to the grant of consent for any subdivision of land to which this clause applies must demonstrate that all downstream stormwater infrastructure, including culverts, are adequate for post development flows or can and will be upgraded to accommodate such flows.

2\. The consent authority shall consult with local aboriginal groups as a part of the assessment for any subdivision application within the PTTAG precinct and may require additional archaeological assessment on a site by site basis.
