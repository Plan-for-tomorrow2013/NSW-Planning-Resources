#### 12. Housing Delivery

12.1. To ensure that residential development provides a range of dwelling types and sizes to meet the needs of the whole community in a way that promotes social equity.

12.2. To ensure that residential land resources are not underdeveloped by inappropriate development staging that would lead to sterilisation.

12.3. To ensure the delivery of appropriate affordable housing is not hindered by the inappropriate provision of parking to Secondary Dwellings that would permit the use of the building in a manner other than that intended by the Secondary Dwellings provisions of Wollondilly Local Environmental Plan, 2011.

12.4. To ensure that land within medium density zones is neither overdeveloped nor underdeveloped.

12.5. To ensure land within low density residential zones is not overdeveloped.
