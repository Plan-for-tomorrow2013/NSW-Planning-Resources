#### 13. Economic Development

13.1. To ensure that residential development is not antipathetic to the objectives of business zones. 13.2. To ensure that business development in residential areas is undertaken in a way that is sensitive to surrounding land uses.

13.3. To provide guidance in the determination of whether a development is or is not farm stay accommodation.

13.4. To ensure that developments for visitor accommodation are not inappropriately used for long term residential purposes.
