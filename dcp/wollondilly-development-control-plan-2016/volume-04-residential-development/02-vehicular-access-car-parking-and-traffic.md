#### 2. Vehicular Access, Car Parking and Traffic

2.1. To ensure a clear path of travel between parking spaces and the dwelling. 2.2. To ensure that vehicular access and car parking are designed in a way that minimises risk to occupants. 2.3. To reduce the incidence of vehicles parking across road verges. 2.4. To ensure that development is provided with adequate car parking based on the likely intensity of the proposed use. 2.5. To encourage the use of a variety of transport modes.
