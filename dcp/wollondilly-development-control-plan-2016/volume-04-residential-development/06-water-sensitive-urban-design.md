#### 6. Water Sensitive Urban Design

6.1. To encourage the retention of land as “deep soil” exposed for infiltration of rainwater. 6.2. To retain, as far as practicable, natural stormwater flows. 6.3. To ensure stormwater from buildings is collected and conveyed in a manner that does not cause environmental harm.
