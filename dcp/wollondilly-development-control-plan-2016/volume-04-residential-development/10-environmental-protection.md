#### 10. Environmental Protection

10.1. To ensure that the development of land does not result in adverse impacts on the biophysical environment. 10.2. To consider the impact of development on the natural environment in balance with the reasonable expectation that a dwelling can be constructed on rural allotments where Wollondilly Local Environmental Plan 2011 permits the construction of a dwelling.
