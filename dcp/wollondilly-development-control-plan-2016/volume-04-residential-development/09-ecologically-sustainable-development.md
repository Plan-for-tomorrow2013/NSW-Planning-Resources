#### 9. Ecologically Sustainable Development

9.1. To reduce the need to export fill from a site. 9.2. To ensure that the development of land for residential purposes is carried out in a way that makes efficient use of natural resources. 9.3. To ensure buildings are designed with energy efficiency in mind.
