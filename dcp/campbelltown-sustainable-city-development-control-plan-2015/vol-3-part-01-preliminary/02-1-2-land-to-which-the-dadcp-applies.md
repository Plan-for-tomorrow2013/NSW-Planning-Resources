### 1.2 Land to which the DADCP Applies

The DADCP applies to the areas that have been marked as a ‘Deferred Matter” under Campbelltown Local Environmental Plan 2015.

A reference to ‘deferred areas’ under Volume 1 and the DADCP of the Plan, shall mean the areas that are marked as a  ‘Deferred Matter” under the Land Application Map of Campbelltown Local Environmental Plan 2015 (the CLEP). The Land Application Map of the CLEP is available from the NSW Legislation website at: www.legislation.nsw.gov.au.
