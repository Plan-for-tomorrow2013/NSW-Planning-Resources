### 14.2 Background -  Classification of Heavy Vehicles

For the purpose of this Part, heavy vehicles comprise Heavy Vehicles Type 1, and Heavy Vehicles Type 2, as defined in Table 14.2.1 below. Table 14.2.1 Heavy Vehicle Types.
