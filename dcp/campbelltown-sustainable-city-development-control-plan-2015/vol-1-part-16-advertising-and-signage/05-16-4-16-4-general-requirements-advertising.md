#### Areas 1, 2 and 3 under Tables 16.1 and 16.2 above

Area 1 covers areas with generally high off-street ambient lighting e.g. some major shopping/commercial centres with a significant number of off-street illuminated advertising devices and lights.(eg Blaxland Road) Area 2 covers areas with generally medium off-street ambient lighting e.g. small to medium shopping/ commercial centres (eg Queen Street) . Area 3 covers areas with generally low levels of off- street ambient lighting e.g. rural and residential areas.
