#### Appendix 1 - Biodiversity Statement

In circumstances where impacts on native biodiversity cannot be avoided a Biodiversity Statement shall be prepared in order to demonstrate: a)

a)	 the development has been sited, designed, constructed and managed

the development has been sited, designed, constructed and managed in a manner that minimises the impact on native biodiversity and

in a manner that minimises the impact on native biodiversity and maintains habitat connectivity as much as practicable;

maintains habitat connectivity as much as practicable; b)

b)	 that the impact is essential for the development and limited

that the impact is essential for the development and limited to the extent necessary to facilitate the safe and orderly use

to the extent necessary to facilitate the safe and orderly use of the land for the purpose of the development;

of the land for the purpose of the development;

communities; iii)

management; v)
