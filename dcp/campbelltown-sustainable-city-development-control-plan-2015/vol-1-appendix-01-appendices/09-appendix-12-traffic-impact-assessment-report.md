#### Appendix 12 – Traffic Impact Assessment Report

For large-scale developments, residential apartment buildings, mixed use developments and the like a traffic impact assessment report shall be submitted with the development application.

A traffic impact assessment report shall address the following: i)

i)	 the existing traffic environment;

the existing traffic environment; ii)

ii)	 traffic generation anticipated from the proposed development;

traffic generation anticipated from the proposed development; iii)

iii)	the cumulative impact of traffic in the locality;

the cumulative impact of traffic in the locality; iv)

iv)	the need for traffic improvements in the locality;

the need for traffic improvements in the locality; v)

v)	 proposed traffic egress/ingress to arterial/sub arterial roads; and

proposed traffic egress/ingress to arterial/sub arterial roads; and vi)

vi)	sight distance and other safety issues.

sight distance and other safety issues.
