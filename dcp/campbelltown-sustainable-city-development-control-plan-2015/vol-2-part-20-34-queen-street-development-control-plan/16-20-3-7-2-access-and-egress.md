#### 20.3.7.2 Access and Egress

#### Objectives

• To ensure safe and continuous access to and from buildings during flood events to minimise

risk to life.

• To prioritise accessible, resilient means of access for building occupants and emergency ser­

vices.

• To provide flood-resilient protection measures for basement parking and storage areas.

#### Controls Preference for Ramps over Platform Lifts

1\.	 Where vertical access to elevated areas is required, ramps shall be the preferred option. Plat­

form lifts are only permissible where ramps are not feasible due to site constraints such as

limited space or significant level differences.

2\.	 The preference for ramps is based on the following:

• Accessibility and Inclusion: Ramps comply better with universal design standards; platform

lifts may fail mechanically, restricting access for mobility-impaired users.

• Suitability for External Use: Platform lifts are vulnerable to flood damage, weather expo­

sure, and vandalism, making them unsuitable for outdoor locations.

• Functionality in High-Traffic Areas: Platform lifts are not recommended in busy public

spaces due to operational limitations and potential congestion; ramps offer more reliable,

user-friendly access.

• Reliability and Resilience: Ramps require less maintenance and provide greater durability

and resilience in flood-prone or high-use environments.

• Development proposals must demonstrate that ramps have been prioritised, with clear

justification provided for any platform lifts proposed.

#### Flood Protection Measures for Basement Parking and Storage

1\.	 Basement parking areas must provide evacuation routes at or above PMF leading to safe areas

or first-level pedestrian access points.

2\.	 Basement carparks entrance levels and openings to be 500mm above the determined 1% AEP

level. Basement car parks to be designed in accordance with Council’s Campbelltown (Sustain­

able City) Development Control Plan 2009 Volume 2 Engineering Design for Development - June

2009- section 4.13.8 – Underground Car Parks. Consideration should also be provided for high­

er flood events by utilising flood barriers or similar flood mitigation methods approved by the

Council to reduce hazard risk. Basement pumps are to be designed to allow for the PMF event.

3\.	 Suitable storage areas for goods must be provided at or above PMF to minimise flood damage

risk.

4\.	 Storage tanks to be located above the PMF level plus 500mm freeboard and/or structurally de­

signed to resist uplift forces.
