#### 20.3.7.1 General Development Controls Relating to Flood

### Management

#### Objectives

• To ensure that development does not increase flood risk to the site or surrounding areas.

• To promote flood-resilient design and construction that minimises damage and protects oc­

cupants during flood events.

#### Controls Flood Impact Considerations

1\.	 The flood impact of any proposed development must be assessed to ensure that the develop­

ment does not increase flood effects on other properties or the broader floodplain.

2\.	 The assessment must consider:

• The loss of flood storage resulting from the development;

• Changes in flood hazard, including increased risk to life and/or property;

• Alterations to flood levels and velocities caused by changes in flood conveyance, including

the effects of fencing types and styles;

• The cumulative impact of multiple potential developments within the site.

• The Flood Impact and Risk Assessment (FIRA), must demonstrate that these matters have

been adequately addressed and that the development will not result in adverse flood im­

pacts.

#### Flood-Resilient Design and Construction

1\.	 The design, materials, and construction of the proposed development must comply with the

principles set out in the publication titled Reducing Vulnerability of Buildings to Flood Damage –

Guidance on Building in Flood Prone Areas (NSW Government).

2\.	 This includes measures aimed at:

• Minimising structural damage;

• Reducing recovery costs;

• Protecting occupants during and after flood events. Reference: https://www.ses.nsw.gov.au/media/2247/building_guidelines.pdf

3\.	 Protection of utilities such as electrical, plumbing, and heating, ventilation, and air-condition­

ing (HVAC) and other mechanical services shall be provided in the event of flooding.

4\.	 Engineering flood solutions shall form part of any future development application for the site to

implement the masterplan.
