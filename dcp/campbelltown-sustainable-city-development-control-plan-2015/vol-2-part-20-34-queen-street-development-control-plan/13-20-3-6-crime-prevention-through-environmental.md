#### 20.3.6 Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design

### (CPTED)

#### Objectives

•	 To ensure development integrates CPTED principles through the design of the public domain,

communal areas and buildings.

•	 To promote safety through design for future residents, workers and vistors on the site.

•	 To enhance public safety and residential amenity by reducing opportunities for crime.

#### Controls

1\.	 Any future development application is to address the CPTED principles as outlined in the Crime

Prevention and Assessment of Development Applications Guideline (2001).
