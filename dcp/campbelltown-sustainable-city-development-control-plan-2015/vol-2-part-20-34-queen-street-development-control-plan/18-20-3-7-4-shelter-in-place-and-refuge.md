#### 20.3.7.4 Shelter-in-Place and Refuge

#### Objectives

• To ensure safe and adequate sheltering for all building occupants during flood events that

exceed safe evacuation thresholds.

• To provide refuge areas designed and equipped to support occupants for the duration of a

flood event and subsequent recovery period.

• To promote resilient building design incorporating appropriate services and facilities to

maintain occupant safety and wellbeing during flooding.

#### Controls Shelter-in-Place Requirements

1\.	 Shelter-in-place must be provided for all occupants (residents, workers, and visitors) in ac­

cordance with the Shelter-in-Place Guidelines for Flash Flooding issued by the Department of

Planning and Housing Infrastructure (DPHI), January 2025. Shelter must provide access to a

safe indoor refuge area above the PMF, where occupants can remain until floodwaters recede

and the site is safe and serviceable.

2\.	 Shelter-in-place or vertical evacuation measures must satisfy the following:

• Refuge shelters must be adequate and fit for purpose in terms of size, design, equipment,

and supplies, and maintained as such in perpetuity.

• Unless otherwise advised by Council, refuge facilities must be designed to support a mini­

mum refuge stay of 48 hours.

• Large residential buildings are recommended to be equipped with emergency back-up pow­

er, water supply, and sewerage for all residential units and common areas, including lifts.

• Where building design and back-up systems allow some residents to remain safely in indi­

vidual apartments for extended periods, those residents must retain access to a communal

refuge area of adequate size providing emergency support and supplies.

#### Communal Safe Area or Refuge

3\.	 A communal safe area of refuge must be permanently provided at a floor level at least 0.5

metres above the PMF, Permanent habitable upper floor levels and essential services are to be

located above the determined PMF level plus 500mm freeboard where onsite occupants may

safely evacuate via ‘shelter-in-place’ until floodwaters subside, equipped with at minimum:

• Emergency electricity supply and lighting;

• Clean water for drinking, washing, and toilet flushing;

• Functional bathroom and toilet facilities;

• Suitable food provisions;

• Personal washing and drying facilities;

• Medical equipment including a first aid kit;

• Battery-powered radio and relevant communications equipment;

• Minimum floor space of 2.0m2 per person.

4\.	 The communal refuge area shall be clearly identifiable, easily accessible to the public,

and appropriately signed. Signage must be provided at street level to guide occupants

and visitors to the refuge during flood events.

5\.	 Non-residential ground floor spaces to be 500mm above the determined 1% AEP level. A

minimum separate floor space of 2.0m2 per person ‘shelter-in-place’ above the PMF level

plus 500mm freeboard to be provided for occupants and visitors as part of the develop­

ment.
