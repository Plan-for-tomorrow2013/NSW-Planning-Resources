#### 20.1.3 Relationship with Campbelltown (Sustainable City)

### DCP

This DCP forms part of Volume 2 of the Campbelltown (Sustainable City) DCP (‘CSCDCP’) and

provides additional controls to guide development at 34 Queen Street, Campbelltown.

All other relevant provisions in CSCDCP apply.

Where there is an inconsistency between this Part and any other provision of the CSCDCP,

this Part applies to the extent of the inconsistency.
