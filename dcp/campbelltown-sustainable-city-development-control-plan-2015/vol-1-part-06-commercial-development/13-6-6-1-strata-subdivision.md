#### 6.6.1 Strata Subdivision

#### Design Requirements

a)	 No more than 50% of the required car parking within a strata title subdivision shall be allocated to individual commercial units within a multi-unit complex.

b)	 All car parking spaces that are allocated to  individual units shall be  proportioned in number to the size of the units.

c)	 No car parking spaces shall be created as a separate allotment.

d)	 No internal or outdoor storage space shall be created as a separate allotment.

e)	 No common property car parking spaces shall be fenced off from other parts of the development.
