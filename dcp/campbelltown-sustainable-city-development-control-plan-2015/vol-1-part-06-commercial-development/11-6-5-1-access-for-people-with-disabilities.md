#### 6.5.1   Access for People with Disabilities

a)	 Mixed use development shall comply with the minimum access requirements contained within  the BCA , the Disability (Access to Premises — Buildings) Standards 2010 and Australian Standard 1428 – Design for Access and Mobility (as amended).
