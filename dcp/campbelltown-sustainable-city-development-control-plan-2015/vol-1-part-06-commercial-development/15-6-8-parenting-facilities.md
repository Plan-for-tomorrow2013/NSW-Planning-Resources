### 6.8 Parenting Facilities

#### Objective:

#### Parenting

#### Facilities

■ Ensure that adequate parenting facilities are provided within large scale commercial and retail developments. ■ Provide easily accessible, safe and adequately equipped parenting facilities suitable for feeding and other purposes related to the care of babies/infants by both female and male carers.
