### 10.3 Car Parking and Access

#### Objectives:

### General Design

### Requirements

■ Ensure efficient and safe vehicular and pedestrian movements to and from the place of public worship. ■ Ensure that the location and design of driveways and parking areas, waste access and collection areas are practical, easily maintained, convenient, safe and suitably landscaped. ■ Ensure that the surrounding street network and intersections continue to operate safely and effectively. ■ Ensure that sufficient car parking spaces are provided on site. ■ Minimise parking arising from places of public worship on local streets.
