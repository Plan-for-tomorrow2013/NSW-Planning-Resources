### 10.4 10.4 Landscaping

### Landscaping

Objectives: ■ To encourage the planting of native and low water consumption plants and trees. ■ To enhance the existing streetscape and promote a scale and density of planting that softens the visual impact of buildings, while maintaining opportunities for passive surveillance. ■ To increase the amenity of car parking areas.
