### 11.5 Building Setbacks

1\. Properties fronting Bensley and Oxford Road shall have a front setback of 10m that includes 60% soft landscaping.

2\. The rear setback of properties adjoining the existing development of Ingleburn (the north west of Caledonia) shall maintain a minimum of 80% soft landscaping.

3\. All other setbacks are to be provided in general accordance with the relevant provisions of Volume 1 of the DCP.
