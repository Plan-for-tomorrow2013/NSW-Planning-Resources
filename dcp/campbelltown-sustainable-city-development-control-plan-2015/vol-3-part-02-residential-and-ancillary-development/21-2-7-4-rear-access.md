#### 2.7.4 Rear Access

a)	 Where there is no access to a rear lane or rear street  directly available from the back  of a dwelling within a multi-dwelling development, each dwelling shall be provided with a separate and direct access from the backyard to the front yard that does not pass through any habitable area of the dwelling (Refer to Figure 2.7.8 for a suggested design solution).

b)	 For the purpose of 2.7.4 a) above, the direct access from the rear to the front of the dwelling shall have a minimum width of 0.9 metres and shall not be obstructed by hot water systems, air conditioning units, gardens or anything

that results in the obstruction of the access way.
