## Part 2 Residential and Ancillary Development on Urban Land

### 2.1 Application

#### Application

Part 2 applies to land zoned 2(b) residential 2B under LEP 2002 and sets out controls relating to the following residential and ancillary development:

• ‘Single dwellings’, ‘secondary dwellings’, ‘domestic outbuildings’ swimming pools/spas and fencing;

• ‘Multi dwellings’; and

• Residential subdivision.

The design requirements contained within Part 2 Volume 3 complement the provisions contained in Part 2, Volume 1 of the Plan.

#### Important Note:  For the purpose of the DADCP, multi dwelling include “dual

occupancy”, “dual occupancy (attached)”, “integrated housing development” and “multi dwellings” as defined under Campbelltown (Urban Area) Local

#### Environmental Plan 2002.
