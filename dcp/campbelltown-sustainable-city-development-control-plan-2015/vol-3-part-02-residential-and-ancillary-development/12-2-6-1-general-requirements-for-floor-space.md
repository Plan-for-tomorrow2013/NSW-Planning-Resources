#### 2.6.1 General Requirements for Floor Space Ratio (FSR) and Deep Soil Planting

a)	 The total (FSR) applicable to the sum of all buildings  undertaken  on a residential allotment, including a dwelling house, domestic outbuildings, and a secondary dwelling shall not exceed 0.55:1.

b)	 A dwelling house, domestic outbuildings

Figure 2.6.1– Example of a dwelling house.

and a secondary dwelling shall satisfy the following provisions relating to deep soil planting:

i)	 no more than 30% of the area forward of any building line shall be surfaced with impervious materials; and

ii)	 a minimum of 20% of the total site area shall be available for deep soil planting.

Floor Space Ratio (FSR) means the ratio of the building gross floor area to the site total area (refer to definition of gross floor area on next page).
