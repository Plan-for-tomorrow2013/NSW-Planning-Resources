#### 3.6.5  Attached Dwellings - Zones R2 Low Density Residential Zone

#### Low Density

#### Objectives:

#### Residential Development

■ Encourage quality-designed attached dwellings  that make a positive contribution to the streetscape and amenity of the neighbourhood.
