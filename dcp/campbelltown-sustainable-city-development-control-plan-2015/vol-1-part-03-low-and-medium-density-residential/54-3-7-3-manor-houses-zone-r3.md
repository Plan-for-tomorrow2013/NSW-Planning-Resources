#### 3.7.3 Manor Houses - Zone R3

#### Medium

#### Objectives:

■ Ensure that all Manor House developments are designed  to  enhance the streetscape character of established residential neighbourhoods.

#### Density Residential Development

■ Ensure that all Manor House developments offer a high standard of internal and external amenity for its occupants in addition to maintaining the amenity of other residents in the locality.
