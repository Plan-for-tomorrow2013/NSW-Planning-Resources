#### 3.7.2.11 Site Services for Multi Dwelling Housing - Zone R3

a)	 The location, design and construction of utility services shall satisfy requirements of the relevant servicing authority and Council.

b)	 Adequate provision shall be made available for all essential services (i.e water, sewerage, electricity, gas, telephone, internet and stormwater drainage).

c)	 All site services shall be placed underground.

d)	 All communication dishes, antennae and the like shall be located to minimise visual prominence.
