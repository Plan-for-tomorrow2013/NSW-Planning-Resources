#### 3.7.1 Attached Dwellings  - Zone R3

#### Medium

#### Objectives:

■ Ensure that attached dwellings are designed  to  enhance the  streetscape character of established residential neighbourhoods.

#### Density Residential Development

■ Ensure that  attached dwellings  offer a high standard of amenity for occupants and maintains the amenity of adjoining residents and acknowledges the dimensional constraints of the existing under sized allotments.
