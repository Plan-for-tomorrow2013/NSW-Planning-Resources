#### 5.2.5. Dual (Parallel) Street Frontages

1\. The secondary frontage is to be detailed with the same architectural features as the primary elevation.

2\. The secondary frontage must include an alternate dwelling entry.

3\. A minimum setback of 3m is to be provided to the secondary frontage, including articulation.

4\. Articulation to the secondary frontage to a public road must not exceed 60% of the lot frontage.

5\. Private open space is to be located to the side of the dwelling.

6\. Fencing is to be consistent on all lots adjoining Menangle Road.
