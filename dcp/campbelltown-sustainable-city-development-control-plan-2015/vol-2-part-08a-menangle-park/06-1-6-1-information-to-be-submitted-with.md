### 1.6. Development Applications

#### 1.6.1. Information to be submitted with Development Applications

Council’s checklist for information required to be referred to prior to preparation of any application and is available at www.campbelltown.nsw.gov.au.

The information required includes (but not limited to) the following:

► Survey Plan ► Site Plan ► Site Analysis Plan ► Floor Plan ► Elevation Plan ► Section Plan ► Shadow Diagrams ► Landscape Plan ► Statement of Environmental Effects ► Stormwater Concept Plan

► Waste Management Plan ► Subdivision Plan ► Advertising Structure ► Photomontage and Scale Model ► NatHERS / BASIX Certificate ► Heritage Impact Statement ► Access Report ► Flora and Fauna Reports ► Contamination Report ► Bushfire Report
