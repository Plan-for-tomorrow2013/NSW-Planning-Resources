### 7.3. Land Uses

The Menangle Park Town Centre will incorporate a variety of integrated land uses to activate the precinct, meet the needs of future residents and provide a central community hub for residents and visitors to congregate.

Menangle Park Town Centre will integrate three primary land uses, being retail, commercial and residential which will be intersected by Main Street in the northern portion and a local road in the southern portion.
