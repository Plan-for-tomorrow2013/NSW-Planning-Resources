### 1.5. Relationship to other Planning Documents

#### 1.5.1. Campbelltown Council Planning Documents

The controls applicable to development of land within Menangle Park are generally detailed in this Part. When a development control is not specified in this Part, development should be consistent with all other relevant controls contained in Volume 1 of the Campbelltown (Sustainable City) DCP 2015. Where there is an inconsistency between controls in Volume 1 and any other part of this DCP, this Part will prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

Campbelltown City Council’s Engineering Design for Development applies to development specified in this Part.
