#### 7.3.2 Commercial

The Town Centre will facilitate a range of commercial floorspace that will generate local employment, provide essential services and enable community uses for residents. The presence of commercial uses within the town Centre will ensure its activation during business hours.

Note: Large scale commercial applications may, at the discretion of Council, need to be supported by an economic feasibility assessment.
