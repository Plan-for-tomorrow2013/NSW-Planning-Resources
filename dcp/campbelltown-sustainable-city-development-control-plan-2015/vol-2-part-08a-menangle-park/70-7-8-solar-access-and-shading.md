### 7.8. Solar Access and Shading

Objectives

a. To protect the solar amenity of the Town Centre and ensure that public places receive an adequate mix of sunlight and shading.

Controls

1\. Built form is to be considerate of solar access to open space, Main Street and Howes Creek riparian corridor and minimise overshadowing of adjoining lands and balance seasonality objectives.

2\. Any Development Application for the construction of buildings is required to submit detailed solar access diagrams for between 9am and 3pm mid-winter to demonstrate sufficient solar access is maintained to public and private spaces and streets.

3\. Any significant expanses of pavement shall be accompanied by a shading strategy.
