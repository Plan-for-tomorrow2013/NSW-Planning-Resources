### 6.3. Cut, Fill and Retaining Walls

Objectives

a. To ensure that retaining walls are structurally sound to achieve long term structural integrity.

b. To ensure slope stabilisation techniques are implemented to preserve and enhance the natural features and characteristics of the site and to maintain the long-term structural integrity of any retaining wall.

c. To ensure that any retaining walls located on the property boundary have the appropriate written legal agreements in place, i.e., easements, to enable access to repair and/or maintenance of retaining walls.

d. To ensure retaining walls are located to minimise any potential stormwater, visual, amenity or overlooking impact on adjoining properties or on the public domain.

e. To guide the design and construction of low height and aesthetically pleasing retaining walls through the regulation of acceptable materials, colours and finishes.
