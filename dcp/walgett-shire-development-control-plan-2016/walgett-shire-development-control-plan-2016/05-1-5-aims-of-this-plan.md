### 1.5. Aims of this Plan

The aims of this plan are as follows:

Define development standards that deliver the outcomes desired by the community and Council; Provide clear and concise development guidelines for various forms of development; Encourage innovation in design and development by not over‐specifying development controls; Provide certainty of development outcomes for developers and the community.
