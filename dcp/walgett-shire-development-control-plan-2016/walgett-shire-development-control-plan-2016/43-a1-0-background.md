#### A1.0 Background

Many development sites have one or more forms of utility infrastructure located on them, especially in urban areas.  Common examples within the Walgett Shire include: Council water mains. Council sewer or effluent mains. Council stormwater mains or channels. Electricity mains, both above and below ground. Telecommunication lines, both above and below ground.

Most of Council’s water supply and stormwater drainage mains are located on public land, while most sewerage or effluent mains are on private land.

Council infrastructure is protected via a range of measures, including: Provisions of the Local Government Act 1993 (eg Section 59A, 191A, 634 & 635). Easements registered on land titles. Identification markers (posts and signs).

This appendix outlines standards required by Council for developments which could affect Council’s utility infrastructure.
