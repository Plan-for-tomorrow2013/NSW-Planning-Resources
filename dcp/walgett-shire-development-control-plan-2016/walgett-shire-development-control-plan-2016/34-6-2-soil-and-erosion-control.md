### 6.2 Soil and Erosion Control

Walgett Shire is located within a semi-arid area and most (about 85%) is flood plain. Ground cover vegetation generally covers 60 to 70% of the soil.

Generally erosion and sediment control measures are not required on land with a gradient less than 1:50 (11º).  On sites with gradients higher than 1:50 or where there is evidence of existing erosion applicants should consider:

The need to manage runoff to prevent any land degradation including offsite sedimentation; Cut and fill will be minimised and the site stabilised during and after construction; and Arrangements in place to prompt revegetation of earthworks to minimise erosion.
