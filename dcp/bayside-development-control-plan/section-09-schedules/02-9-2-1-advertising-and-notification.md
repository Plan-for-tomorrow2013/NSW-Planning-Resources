#### 9.2.1 Advertising & Notification Requirements for Local Development

This chapter is the subject of an ongoing review by Council and will be updated upon completion of this review. In the interim, Council will refer to current advertising and notification requirements for local development, contained in Part 2 of Botany Bay Development Control Plan 2013 and Part 8 of Rockdale Development Control Plan 2011, as well as the relevant provisions of the Bayside Engagement and Communications Strategy.
