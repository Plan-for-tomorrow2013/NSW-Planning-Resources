#### 9.2.2 External Notification

Some development, in addition to requiring the consent from Council, will also require approval, concurrence or referral (for comment) from a public authority, State Government Department or identified agency.

In determining the external notification requirements of a development application consideration is required to be given to the integrated development and designated development provisions outlined in the Environmental Planning & Assessment Act 1979.

The table below provides a guide for the external notification requirements for development applications.
