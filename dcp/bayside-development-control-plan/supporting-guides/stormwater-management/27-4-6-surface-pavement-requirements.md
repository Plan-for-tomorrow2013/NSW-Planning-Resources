### 4.6 Surface Pavement Requirements

For sites that fall to the rear, the excessive use of impervious surface pavement is not permitted, and deep soil

landscaping must be maximised. Any surface pavement used shall be porous (e.g., permeable

pavement/pavers) to the satisfaction of council. Porous pavement is detailed further in section 7.4.
