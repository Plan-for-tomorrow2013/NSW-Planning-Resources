## 6 On-Site Detention (OSD) Systems

On-site stormwater detention (OSD) systems shall be provided for all industrial development and development,

which infiltration system is not permitted or feasible (e.g., low absorption areas or where other physical

limitations prevent effective absorption). The OSD Policy aims to ensure that developments will not increase the

risk of flooding at any downstream properties, in all flood events up to and including the 1% AEP storm event.

Some development is exempted from OSD and this is detailed in section 6.9.
