### 5.5 Absorption Exemptions and offsets

Absorption system offsets are available as per section 7.2.2. The requirement to provide absorption systems is exempted for one-off minor developments, minor additions,

and repairs where the proposed development will increase the sites impervious area by less than or equal to

60m2. (except where required by section 4). This exemption can be applied only once.
