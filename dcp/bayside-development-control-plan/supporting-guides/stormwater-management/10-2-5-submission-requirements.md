### 2.5 Submission Requirements

Plans and documents as per sections 2.1, 2.2 & 2.3. Calculations can be provided as part of the stormwater

plans for small-medium scale development. For large-scale high-density development, a supplementary report

demonstrating how the stormwater plans comply with the technical specification is required. Geotechnical information in accordance with Section 5.1 of this guideline for any on-site infiltration system or

nominal absorption rate obtained from council shall be provided.
