### 2.2 Calculations

Calculations are required to support the information contained on the stormwater concept plan and the

stormwater detailed plan. Calculation sheets for the design of on-site infiltration systems are included in

Appendix C. Other types of calculation should be based on Australian Rainfall and Runoff (AR&R).
