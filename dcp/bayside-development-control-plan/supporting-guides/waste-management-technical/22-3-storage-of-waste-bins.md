## 3. STORAGE OF WASTE BINS

Does the development provide a separate bin storage area for the commercial units within the development? Is the Development Application accompanied by a scaled plan illustrating the bin storage area for the commercial units? What is the size of the bin storage area and dimensions? The Development Application is to be accompanied by a scaled plan illustrating the layout of the bin storage area?
