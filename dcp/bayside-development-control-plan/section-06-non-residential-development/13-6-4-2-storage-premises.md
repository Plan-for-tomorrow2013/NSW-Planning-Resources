#### 6.4.2 Storage premises

| Objective |  | Control |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| General |  |  |  |
| O1. | To ensure Storage Premises are suitably designed to accommodate the requirements and safety of users of the premises and not be used for a purpose other than storage. | C1. | A central administration office is identifiable and directly accessible from the public domain and a range of sizes of storage units are provided on-site to accommodate user requirements. |

| Objective |  |
|---|---|
| Setbacks and Design |  |
| O2. | To achieve developments that enhance the amenity and streetscape of the locality. |

| Control |  |
|---|---|
| C2. | A Plan of Management must provide details about the operation of the premises, including consideration of: a. Safety and security b. Loading and unloading c. Movement of items d. Any other matters that in the opinion of council has the potential to cause amenity or safety risks |
| C3. | Lighting is to be provided at all building entry and exit points to ensure safe access. |
| C4. | On-site surveillance, such as CCTV is required to provide coverage of all areas which provide access to storage units. |
| C5. | Storage units are secured by perimeter fencing and provide access from the public domain via entries which require security clearance. |
| C6. | Not less than 10% of the development site shall be landscaped. On sites over 2,000m² the front landscaped setbacks are additional to the 10% requirement. The majority of landscaping shall front the street/s to which the development fronts and include side and rear landscaped areas. |

| C12. | On-site manoeuvring areas can accommodate a heavy rigid vehicle moving through all areas of the site in a forward gear. This control only applies to single storey premises. |
| C13. | Car parking rate for self storage premises should be determined through a survey of other self storage premises in the vicinity to determine the generation of car parking and traffic movements. This survey should be submitted with any development application for a self storage premises. |
