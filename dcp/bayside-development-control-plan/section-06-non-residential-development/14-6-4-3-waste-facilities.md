#### 6.4.3 Waste Facilities

| Objective |  | Control |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1. | To ensure waste facilities are not located in an area that would increase the risk of collisions between wildlife and aircraft flying to or from Sydney Airport. | C1. | A proponent for a new or expanded waste facility should refer to and comply with the National Airports Safeguarding Framework Guideline C – Managing Wildlife Strike Risk. |
