#### 7.14.2 Desired Future Character

The Page Street Neighbourhood Centre is anticipated to develop into a high quality Neighbourhood Centre with a balanced mix of retail, commercial, community and residential uses which cater for the needs of the local community. If the existing Centre is redeveloped in the future, consolidation of the five existing allotments is required and vehicular access must be from the rear of the centre, preferably to a basement car park. Public domain improvements, such as landscaping, lighting of the pedestrian walkway, provision of a shared zone in the rear laneway and a pedestrian crossing in Holloway Street would be required with any future redevelopment of the centre.
