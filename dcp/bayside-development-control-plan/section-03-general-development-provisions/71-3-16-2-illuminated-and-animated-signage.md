#### 3.16.2 Illuminated and Animated Signage

| Objective |  |
|---|---|
| O1. | To preserve the character of an area through high quality and appropriate illuminated signage. |
| O2. | To encourage the use of renewable energy sources to illuminate signage where possible. |
| O3. | To ensure that illuminated signage does not adversely impact on the safety and security of pedestrians, motorists and the general public. |

| Control |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Lighting intensity must not unreasonably impact on any residential properties adjoining the sign or that is within its locality. |
| C2. | Illuminated signage is to be switched off between 10pm and 6am daily, where the illuminated signage: a. has an advertising signage area larger than 1m² and/or b. is located adjacent to or in the vicinity of residential dwellings |
| C3. | Electric wiring to illuminated signage is to be concealed. |
| C4. | Illumination of a sign (with the exception of floodlit signs) must not be external to the sign i.e. surrounding the sign. Illumination must be part of the sign. |
| C5. | Illuminated signage is encouraged to be powered by solar power. |
| C6. | Illuminated signage must minimise the spill effects or escape of light beyond the subject sign. |
