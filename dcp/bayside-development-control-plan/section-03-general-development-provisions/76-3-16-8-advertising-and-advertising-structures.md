#### 3.16.8 Advertising and Advertising Structures

Advertising structure means a structure or vessel that is principally designed for, or that is used for, the display of an advertisement. This part deals with third party Advertising Structures with an advertising display area of 45m² or less.

| Objective |  |
|---|---|
| O1. | To minimise visual clutter and improve the aesthetic appeal of the Bayside LGA by minimising the extent of additional advertising. |
| O2. | To ensure advertising structure and advertising is integrated into the built form. |
| O3. | To ensure advertising structures and advertising does not dominate or detract from the host building. |

| Control |  |
|---|---|
| C1. | Third party advertising on public infrastructure is not permitted. |
| C2. | Advertising or advertising structures: a. are to be integrated into new building forms and designed in a manner that complements the architectural quality of the building b. are to sit flush with the building façade c. are to be of a scale that complements the size of the building d. should not project above the predominant parapet line of the building |
| C3. | No more than one (1) advertisement is permitted per building. |
| C4. | The number of existing signs on the site and in the vicinity of proposed advertising signs is to be considered in determining the appropriateness of a location for new signage. New proposals should not cumulatively create unacceptable visual clutter. |

|---|---|
| O1. | To restrict new free-standing advertising structures only to Hensley Athletic Field. |

| C1. | No new free-standing advertising structures are permitted with the expectation of free- standing advertising structures at Hensley Athletic Field. |
| C2. | New free-standing advertising structures at Hensley Athletic Field shall minimise unnecessary supporting structures to minimise visual clutter. |

| C4. | Multiple signs are to be separated and placed at appropriate intervals to allow adequate space for landscaping. |
| C5. | A layered (three tiers) of landscaping treatment is required in all planted areas.
