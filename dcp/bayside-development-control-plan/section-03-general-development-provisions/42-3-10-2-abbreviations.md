#### 3.10.2 Abbreviations

• AEP - Annual Exceedance Probability

• DA - Development Application

• DCP -  Development Control Plan

• DPE - Department of Planning and Environment

• FDM - Floodplain Development Manual

• FRMP -  Floodplain Risk Management Plan

• FRMS -  Floodplain Risk Management Study

• LEP -  Local Environmental Plan

• LGA -  Local Government Area

• PMF - The largest flood that could conceivably be expected to occur at a particular location, usually estimated from probable maximum precipitation.

• SES - State Emergency Service
