#### 3.10.4 Land to Which This Chapter Applies

This chapter of the DCP applies to all flood-prone land within Bayside Council, being all land potentially inundated by floods up to and including a probable maximum flood (PMF).

Flood prone land is mapped for the following:

• 1% AEP plus 0.5m freeboard affected land

• PMF affected land (both available at: https://maps.bayside.nsw.gov.au/Intramaps98/?module=Flood)

Council can provide a site specific flood advice letter or identify the need to obtain further information from a qualified civil engineer.
