#### 3.10.11 Land Use Categories

The criteria for proposals potentially affected by flooding are structured in recognition that different controls are applicable to different land uses and flood hazards.

The major land use categories that have been adopted are listed in Schedules – Sub-section 9.5.1. The specific uses, as defined by the applicable Environmental Planning Instruments, have been grouped within each of these categories based on a similar level of potential vulnerability to flooding hazards.
