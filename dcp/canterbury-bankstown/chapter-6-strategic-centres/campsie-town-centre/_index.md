### Notes

- **Precedence over general chapter**: Section 1's explanation states that Chapter 6.3's development controls prevail over any other inconsistent DCP control. This means Chapter 6.1 (General Requirements, in `../general/`) is the fallback/default, and 6.3 overrides it specifically for the Campsie precinct where the two conflict — important for any chatbot answering "what setback applies in Campsie."
- **Source date**: cover page reads "June 2023 (Amended April 2025)" — month/year only, no exact day. `effective_date`/`amended_date` fields omitted from front matter per the guide; noted as plain text in each file instead.
- **Clause 3.7 scope ambiguity**: titled "Exceptions," refers only to the "side setback area," but sits directly after the Rear Setbacks subsection with no clarification of whether it also applies to rear setbacks. Flagged in `03-setbacks.md`, not resolved either way.
- **Figures**: all figures (2a–2c, 3a–3b, 4a–4c) are noted inline in their respective files and logged in the chapter-level [`appendices/_index.md`](../appendices/_index.md) — none converted to image assets yet.
