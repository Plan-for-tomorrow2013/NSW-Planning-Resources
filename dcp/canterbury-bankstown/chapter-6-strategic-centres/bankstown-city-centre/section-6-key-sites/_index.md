## Source anomalies flagged across this section

- **KS2/KS3 street-name and address discrepancies** between the Section 6 overview page and each site's own heading ("Richard Road" vs "Rickard Road" for KS2; "67-70" vs "67-69" Rickard Road for KS3) — see notes in [00-overview.md](00-overview.md) and [02-ks2-aldi-store-site.md](02-ks2-aldi-store-site.md).
- **Page 181 (KS7) text corruption**: the source PDF's text layer is garbled on this page (custom font encoding issue); design principles f–j were transcribed from a rasterised page image rather than extracted text — see note in [07-ks7-8-14-west-terrace.md](07-ks7-8-14-west-terrace.md).
- **KS9 duplicate "6.9.7" section number**: used once for Controls under Signage strategy and public art, and again for the genuinely separate Staging and implementation sub-section — see note in [ks9-bankstown-central/07-signage-public-art.md](ks9-bankstown-central/07-signage-public-art.md).
- **KS9 "Figure 80" cross-reference**: a control in 6.9.2 (Public domain interface) cites "Figure 80" for an awning requirement, but Figure 80 is actually the KS8 design principles figure — likely a source error (probably meant Figure 83 or 91) — see note in [ks9-bankstown-central/03-public-domain-interface.md](ks9-bankstown-central/03-public-domain-interface.md).
- **KS9 "Ground flood apartments" typo**: reproduced as printed (not silently corrected) with a `(sic)` flag — see note in [ks9-bankstown-central/04-building-layout-form-design.md](ks9-bankstown-central/04-building-layout-form-design.md).
- **KS9 truncated note under 6.9.4 control C9(b)**: a sentence is cut off mid-clause at a page break in the source and does not resume — see note in [ks9-bankstown-central/05-transport-parking.md](ks9-bankstown-central/05-transport-parking.md).
