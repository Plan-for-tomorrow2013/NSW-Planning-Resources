### Notes

- **Character Areas (2.1.2–2.1.9) use Character statement + Principles only** — no separate Objectives or Controls tier for these eight subsections (the "P1. Development must achieve and satisfy the outcomes expressed in the above character statement" principle effectively does the job an Objective would). This differs from 1.3's stated three-tier Objectives/Principles/Controls structure — flagged since a reader expecting all three tiers in every subsection of 6.2 won't find them here.
- **2.2 Connecting with Country uses Objectives + a "Design Guidance" (G-numbered) tier** instead of Controls — a third tier label not seen elsewhere in this chapter so far. Preserved distinctly rather than relabelled; see the note in `10-connecting-with-country.md`.
- **Duplicate principle text**: P2 in 2.1.6 (Eds & Meds) and P2 in 2.1.7 (Memorial Park) are word-for-word identical ("Allow for active rooftop uses, particularly on large format, specialised health and education buildings"), despite Memorial Park not being an education/health character area. Reproduced as printed in both files, flagged as likely a source copy-paste artifact.
- **Figure 7 caption anomaly**: captioned "Axonometric view of the civic food and culture character area" in the source — appears to be a leftover caption fragment from the preceding Food and Culture subsection rather than a deliberate combined label. Flagged in `05-civic.md`.
- **Undefined acronym**: "CWCF" (used in 2.2, clearly = Connecting with Country Framework per O1's full name) is not spelt out inline in the pages converted for this subsection. May be defined in Appendix 1 (Definitions) — not yet converted.
- **Key Sites (KS1–KS9)** are referenced throughout these figures but Section 6 (Key Sites) has not been converted yet — cross-references are noted but not yet resolvable to full key site descriptions.
