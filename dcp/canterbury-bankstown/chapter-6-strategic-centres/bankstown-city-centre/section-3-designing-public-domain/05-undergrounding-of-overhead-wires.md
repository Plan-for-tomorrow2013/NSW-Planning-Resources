# Chapter 6.2 — Bankstown City Centre: 3.3 Undergrounding of Overhead Wires

Developments in Bankstown City Centre must maintain consistency with the objectives and controls regarding the underlining [sic — "undergrounding"] of overhead wires in the following Chapters of the CBDCP 2023:

- 5.1 Residential Accommodation (Former Bankstown LGA), Section 14
- 6.1 Strategic Centres (General Requirements), Section 8

These controls will apply to all MU1 Mixed Use, R4 High Density Residential, R3 Medium Density Residential and R2 Low Density Residential Zoned land within the Bankstown City Centre Area.

> **Note:** the source text reads "underlining of overhead wires" — almost certainly a typo for "undergrounding," which is both the subsection's own heading and the term used in the cross-referenced chapters (see [Chapter 6.1, clause 8.21](../../general/08-general-design-and-amenity.md)). Reproduced exactly as printed.

## Cross-chapter references

This subsection contains no independent controls — it directs applicants to comply with:
- **Chapter 5.1 — Residential Accommodation (Former Bankstown LGA), Section 14** — not part of Chapter 6 and not yet converted in this repo.
- **[Chapter 6.1 — Strategic Centres (General Requirements), Section 8, clause 8.21](../../general/08-general-design-and-amenity.md)** — already converted; requires all overhead wires along street frontages to be undergrounded as part of new buildings, redundant power poles removed and replaced with underground-supplied street lighting columns, all at the landowner's expense.
