# Chapter 6.2 — Bankstown City Centre: 3.4.2 Solar Access to Public Open Space

## Objectives

**O1.** Ensure for adequate solar amenity to parks and spaces for the enjoyment of residents and visitors in the city centre.

**O2.** Provide high levels of solar access to ensure that trees and vegetation in public open spaces thrive, for amenity and to reduce urban heat gain.

**O3.** Model building form to minimise the impact on solar access to public open space.

**O4.** To balance sunlight access to the principal useable parts of public open space with shaded areas to enable a range of activities and planting of vegetation.

## Controls

**C1.** Development applications must include overshadowing diagrams that confirm the minimum amount of solar access is achieved each open space identified in Figure 26.
