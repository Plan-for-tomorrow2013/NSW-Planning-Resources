### Notes

- **Significant anomaly — duplicated controls in 3.1.3**: Controls C10, C11 and C12 are verbatim duplicates of C7, C8 and C9. Flagged prominently in `03-laneways-shared-zones-service-lanes.md` — recommend confirming with Council whether this is a source drafting error before treating C10–C12 as distinct from C7–C9.
- **Typo flagged in 3.3**: "underlining of overhead wires" in the source almost certainly means "undergrounding" (matches the subsection's own heading and the cross-referenced Chapter 6.1 clause 8.21 wording). Reproduced as printed.
- **P7 typo flagged in 3.1.1**: "the low term establishment" almost certainly means "the long term establishment." Reproduced as printed.
- **Cross-chapter reference**: 3.3 has no independent controls of its own — it defers entirely to Chapter 5.1 (not part of Chapter 6, not yet converted) and [Chapter 6.1 Section 8 clause 8.21](../../general/08-general-design-and-amenity.md) (already converted, linked directly).
- **Key Sites precedence**: 3.1.1 clause C2 states Section 6 (Key Sites) takes precedence over Section 3 wherever the two conflict — same precedence pattern as Chapter 6.3 over 6.1, but internal to 6.2 itself.
- **Figures**: streetscape typology diagrams (Figures 13–19) are dimensioned engineering cross-sections and were logged but not transcribed in detail, per the conversion guide's treatment of image-only content. All figures (12–28) logged in the chapter-level [`appendices/_index.md`](../../appendices/_index.md).
- **Zones**: 3.2 names E1, E2, MU1, SP2; 3.3 names MU1, R4, R3, R2.
