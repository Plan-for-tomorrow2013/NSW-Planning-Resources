### Chapter scope

6.1 applies generally to both strategic centres (no LGA or per-centre split — see its own explanation text). 6.2 and 6.3 are each scoped to a single named centre. This mirrors the former-LGA-split pattern used elsewhere in the repo, but split by **strategic centre** rather than by former LGA — flagged explicitly since this is a new scope-split pattern not previously seen in this repo (Chapter 5 splits by former LGA; Chapter 4 is council-wide "general"; Chapter 6 splits by centre within one LGA-merged council).

### Chapter 6.2 — Bankstown City Centre: scope note, conversion in progress

**This sub-document is far larger than anything else converted in this repo so far — 221 pages, versus 22 pages for 6.1 and 10 pages for 6.3.** Its own table of contents lists:

- Section 1 – Introduction (incl. 1.1 Application of this DCP, 1.2 Vision, 1.3 How to read this DCP)
- Section 2 – Understanding Place (2.1 Character Areas, 2.2 Connecting with Country)
- Section 3 – Designing the Public Domain (3.1 Streets, 3.2 Tree canopy and green cover, 3.3 Undergrounding of overhead wires, 3.4 Open space, 3.5 Community infrastructure)
- Section 4 – Built Form (4.1 Minimum lot sizes/frontages/isolated sites, 4.2 The building envelope [with sub-sections 4.2.3–4.2.6 on street wall, setbacks, separation, floorplates], 4.3 Residential ground floor frontage, 4.4 Building interface with open space, 4.5 Building interface and active frontages, 4.6 Visual diversity/articulation/fine grain, 4.7 Materials and finishes, 4.8 Rooftop and podium communal open space)
- Part 5 – General Provisions (5.1–5.13: dwelling mix, parking, heritage, solar energy, urban cooling, energy/water management, signage/lighting, waste management, flood-affected design, underground floor space, late-night trading interfaces, building address/numbering, general requirements)
- Section 6 – Key Sites (6.1–6.9, nine individually named key sites)
- Appendix 1 (A1.1 Definitions, A1.2 Figures list)

This structure doesn't map cleanly onto the flat "one file per section" rule used for 6.1 and 6.3 — many of 6.2's numbered sub-sections (e.g. 4.2.3, 4.2.4, 4.2.5, 4.2.6) have their own Objectives/Principles/Controls sets and multiple tables/maps each, and clause numbering resets per numbered heading (e.g. multiple "C1"s across different sub-sections) rather than running continuously like 6.1's and 6.3's clause numbers. Converting it responsibly — full clause text, every table, every map logged — is realistically its own multi-part job, not something to compress into this pass without risking exactly the kind of silent quality loss the conversion guide is meant to prevent.

**Recommended next step:** convert 6.2 in its own dedicated pass(es), most likely split by its top-level Sections (1, 2, 3, 4, Part 5, Section 6, Appendix 1) given the page count — happy to start with whichever part matters most to you first (Section 4 – Built Form is likely the highest-traffic one for a chatbot, since it's where street wall heights, setbacks, separation distances and floorplate controls live).

Do **not** create a `bankstown-city-centre/` folder or mark this "in progress" in the top-level council index until there's at least one converted file to put in it, per the conversion guide.
