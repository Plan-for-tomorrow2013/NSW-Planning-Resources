# Chapter 1.1 — Introduction and Administration

Source: *Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023, Chapter 1.1 — Introduction
and Administration*, dated June 2023 (Amended March 2026).

This sub-chapter sets out the DCP's citation, adoption/commencement dates, land
application, objectives, its relationship to other planning instruments, a step-by-step guide
to using the DCP, the definitions used throughout the DCP, and the full amendment history
with savings provisions. It applies council-wide (not split by former LGA) — see "Land
where this DCP applies" in Section 1, which scopes the whole DCP to the Canterbury-
Bankstown Local Government Area with no former-LGA distinction. It is kept as a `general/`
scope folder rather than an LGA-split folder for this reason.
