# Section 22 — Seniors housing

> Source: DCP 2023–Chapter 4.3 (Amended August 2024), page 39

## Explanation

The *State Environment Planning Policy (Housing) 2021* allows seniors housing in heritage conservation areas, which may require a different building footprint and size than other residential accommodation. The SEPP requires seniors housing to complement heritage conservation areas. This section seeks to achieve this, along with the relevant controls in the SEPP and Seniors Housing Design Guide.

## Objectives

- **O1** To ensure that seniors housing maintains and conserves existing contributory buildings.
- **O2** To ensure seniors housing is compatible with the character and significance of the heritage conservation areas as set out in the Area Character Statements.
- **O3** To discourage site consolidation for the purposes of seniors housing.

## Development controls

22.1 Development must retain and appropriately conserve any existing contributory buildings.

22.2 Development should maintain the existing subdivision pattern by locating on lots in heritage conservation areas that meet the requirements of the *State Environment Planning Policy (Housing) 2021* and avoiding site consolidations.
