# Chapter 4.3 — Heritage Conservation Areas

Applies to Canterbury-Bankstown's seven Heritage Conservation Areas (HCAs): Ashbury, Crinan Street Shops, Duntroon Street, Floss Street, Melford Street, Railway Street, and Starkey Street (the last six all located in Hurlstone Park; Ashbury is further divided into four precincts). Covers contributory building rankings, form/massing/scale, infill development, roofs, verandahs, driveways/garages, fences, landscaping, outbuildings, windows/doors, materials/colour, utility infrastructure, treatment of non-contributory buildings, retail/commercial buildings, residential flat buildings, subdivision, demolition, a Hurlstone Park first-floor-conversion incentive, secondary dwellings, Ashbury-specific controls, and seniors housing.

This sub-chapter applies council-wide in the sense that its general sections (1–20, 22) apply to all seven HCAs; **Section 21 contains controls specific to the Ashbury HCA only** and prevails over the rest of the chapter where inconsistent, for that HCA. It is not split by former-LGA folders in this repo, since all seven HCAs sit within the DCP's single Chapter 4.3 structure.

## Known anomalies (flagged, not silently resolved)

- **Duplicate clause label**: Section 2, clause 2.4 has two sub-items both labelled "(d)" in the source (likely should be "(f)"). Preserved as printed.
- **Section 19, clause 19.6** refers to "the criteria below" but the section ends immediately after — no further criteria follow in the source PDF. Possibly an incomplete clause or a cross-reference to controls elsewhere in the DCP/LEP.
- **Section 14, clause 14.1** reads "Alterations and additions to be bulk, height, scale..." — likely a missing word in the source (e.g. "regarding"), preserved verbatim.
- **Section 18 vs Chapter 4.2 Section 17 (demolition)**: minor inconsistency in how the archival-recording body is named ("NSW OEH Heritage Division" here vs "NSW Office of Environmental and Heritage – Heritage Division" in 4.2) — both refer to the same body, preserved as printed in each source.
- **Section 20 (Secondary dwellings) is materially less restrictive than Chapter 4.2 Section 18** (which covers individually-listed heritage items): HCA secondary dwellings may be incorporated into the principal dwelling via internal modification on the ground *or first floor* (20.2(b)), whereas for heritage items this is expressly prohibited (4.2 clause 18.9). This appears to be an intentional policy distinction, not a conversion error.

## Follow-up needed

- The **Heritage Conservation Area Character Statements** (containing HCA/precinct boundary maps and the per-property Building Contribution Ranking used throughout this chapter) are referenced extensively but are a separate Council document not included in the four source PDFs converted for Chapter 4. These should be sourced and converted separately (likely as a `supporting-guides/` or `appendices/` item) since without them the Building Contribution Ranking (1/2/3) for any given property cannot be looked up.
- Likewise the **Heritage Guide** referenced throughout Chapters 4.1–4.4 is a separate document not yet in this repo.
