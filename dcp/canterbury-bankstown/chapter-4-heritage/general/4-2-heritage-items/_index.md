# Chapter 4.2 — Heritage Items

Applies to heritage items listed in the Canterbury-Bankstown LEP 2023 (Schedule 5, Part 1), plus draft heritage items, IHO-affected properties, and section 170 Register-affected properties. Covers external form and setting, interiors, roofs, verandahs, driveways/garages, fences, landscaping, outbuildings, windows/doors, materials and colour, utility infrastructure, retail/commercial buildings, residential flat buildings, adaptive reuse, subdivision, demolition, and secondary dwellings.

This sub-chapter applies council-wide (not split by former LGA).
