# Chapter 4.1 — Introduction

Sets out the framework for Chapter 4 (Heritage) of the Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023: what places of heritage significance are, the three-step heritage conservation process (investigate / assess / manage significance), definitions used throughout Chapter 4, and general information on consent requirements, building style periods, and where to find out whether a property is heritage-listed.

This sub-chapter applies council-wide (not split by former LGA).
