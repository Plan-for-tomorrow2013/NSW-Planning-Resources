# Chapter 4 — Heritage

Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 4, covers heritage conservation across the LGA: individually-listed heritage items, the seven Heritage Conservation Areas, and development near (but not on) places of heritage significance.

Chapter 4 is **not split by former LGA** — all four sub-chapters apply council-wide (Chapter 4.3 additionally contains an Ashbury-HCA-specific section, and a Hurlstone-Park-specific incentive section, but these are internal to the single Chapter 4.3 document, not a former-LGA folder split). All sub-chapter content therefore sits under `general/`.
