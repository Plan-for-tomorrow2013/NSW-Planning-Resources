### Notes

- **All three sub-chapters are council-wide** (no former-LGA split) — each was checked
  individually per the standard rule ("check each chapter's own application wording"
  rather than assuming). 8.1 applies to E3/SP4 land generally; 8.2 and 8.3 are each scoped
  to a specific named road corridor (Canterbury Road; Hume Highway) rather than a former
  LGA, so they still sit under `general/` alongside 8.1.
- **8.1** (General Requirements) applies council-wide across Zones E3/SP4, split internally
  by zone rather than LGA. Its own objectives/clauses (O4, O7, 2.7) refer to "the
  enterprise corridors," which reads like boilerplate — worth checking against Chapter 7's
  precinct-DCP wording issues, and now also against 8.2/8.3, which are literally about
  enterprise *corridors* (Canterbury Road, Hume Highway), during a future reconciliation
  pass.
- **8.2** (Canterbury Road Enterprise Corridor) organises controls by desired-character
  precinct tied to LEP zone (Urban Core/Centres/General/Enterprise/Residential — E1, E3
  'Area 9' vs not, R4). Its development-control numbering restarts at "C1" within every
  numbered subsection rather than running sequentially — cite clauses with their
  subsection (e.g. "5.3 C1"), not "C1" alone. Two source typos are preserved and flagged
  inline (5.2 C10 "will not be support"; 6.1 C4 "sites were a laneway").
- **8.3** (Hume Highway Enterprise Corridor) covers the Remembrance Driveway landscape
  corridor, Rookwood Village Centre, and four named Greenacre Motor Alley sites — not all
  seven precincts shown on its own Figure 2a Precinct Plan (Bass Hill Town Centre, Yagoona
  Town Centre, etc. are outside this chapter's scope, not missing from the DCP overall).
  Its Section 6 (167 Hume Highway) is a large, structurally distinct site-specific mini-DCP
  — closer in style to Chapter 7's newer precinct DCPs (7.5/7.6/7.7) than to the rest of
  8.3 — and was split into its own subfolder with one file per numbered subsection
  (6.1–6.15) rather than kept as a single Section 6 file, following the same "2+ internal
  sections gets its own folder" logic the conversion guide applies to sub-documents.
- Objectives O1–O9 repeat verbatim across 8.3 Sections 4, 5, 7 and 8 — deliberate shared
  boilerplate across every site this chapter covers, not a conversion error.
- Appendix 1 to 8.3 (suitable street trees) was genuine tabular data and was converted in
  full rather than logged as an unconverted figure; 22 other figures across 8.2 and 8.3
  (site plans, cross-sections, building-envelope massing diagrams) remain unconverted —
  see each sub-document's `appendices/_index.md`.
