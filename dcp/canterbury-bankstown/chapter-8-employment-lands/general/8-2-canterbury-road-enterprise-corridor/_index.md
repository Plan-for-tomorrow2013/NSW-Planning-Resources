### Anomalies flagged during conversion

1. **No amendment/gazettal table in the source**, same as 8.1 — `effective_date`/
   `amended_date` omitted, day-level date unknown.
2. **Clause numbering restarts at "C1" within every numbered subsection** (3.1, 3.2, 4.1,
   4.2, 4.3, 5.1–5.4, 6.1, 6.2), rather than running sequentially like Chapter 8.1's
   2.1–2.40 style. A bare clause citation like "C1" is ambiguous without its subsection —
   always cite as e.g. "5.3 C1", not just "C1".
3. **Two source typos preserved verbatim**, each flagged inline where they occur: clause
   5.2 C10 ("will not be support" — evidently "supported"); clause 6.1 C4 ("sites were a
   laneway" — evidently "where").
4. **Precedence clause**: Section 1's Explanation states Chapter 8.2's controls prevail over
   other DCP chapters' controls where inconsistent (if applicable to the application). The
   same wording pattern appears in Chapter 8.3 (introduction and the 167 Hume Highway
   sub-section) and in the Chapter 7 precinct DCPs — a recurring drafting pattern for
   site/corridor-specific chapters, not unique to this document.
5. Section 2 (Desired character) precinct C1 "Urban Core" is defined entirely by
   cross-reference to Chapter 7's Canterbury Local Centre precinct DCP rather than
   containing its own controls — noted, not duplicated here.
6. No duplicate clause numbers were found within any subsection.
