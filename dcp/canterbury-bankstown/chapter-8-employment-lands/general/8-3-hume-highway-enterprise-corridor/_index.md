## Chapter 8.3 — Hume Highway Enterprise Corridor

June 2023 (Amended April 2025). Applies council-wide (no former-LGA split) — confirmed
from the document's own wording, which addresses named sites/precincts along the Hume
Highway (Remembrance Driveway corridor, Rookwood Village Centre, and four numbered
Greenacre Motor Alley sites) rather than splitting by former Bankstown/Canterbury LGA.

Of the seven precincts shown on the corridor's own Figure 2a Precinct Plan, this chapter
only sets controls for the Remembrance Driveway corridor (which runs through all of them),
Rookwood Enterprise Zone (as "Rookwood Village Centre"), and Greenacre Motor Alley. The
other four named precincts (Meccano Set Gateway, Bass Hill Town Centre, Residential
Precinct, Yagoona Town Centre, Chullora Technology Park) are not addressed by Chapter 8.3
— they aren't necessarily missing from the DCP overall, just outside this chapter's scope.

### Anomalies flagged during conversion

1. **No amendment/gazettal table in the source**, same as 8.1/8.2 — `effective_date`/
   `amended_date` omitted, day-level date unknown.
2. **Objectives O1–O9 are repeated verbatim** across Sections 4, 5, 7 and 8 (Rookwood
   Village Centre and the three Greenacre Motor Alley sites) — deliberate boilerplate
   reused across every site-specific area covered by this chapter (excluding Section 6,
   which has its own distinct objective sets per subsection). Not an error; noted so a
   reader doesn't mistake the repetition for a copy-paste mistake.
3. **Section 6 (167 Hume Highway) is structurally different** from the rest of the chapter —
   see its own `_index.md` for the full breakdown. It reads as a newer, more detailed
   precinct-DCP style consistent with Chapter 7's 7.5/7.6/7.7, embedded inside the older
   sequential-clause style used by the rest of 8.3.
4. **Two source typos preserved and flagged inline**: Section 5.3 C10 in 8.2 ("will not be
   support") is a different document — within 8.3 itself, clause 8.5's duplicated "from the
   from the" was corrected with a flag (see `08-greenacre-motor-alley-315-hume-highway-banksia-road.md`).
5. A naming inconsistency in Section 4 ("Rookwood Road Village" vs "Rookwood Village
   Centre") is flagged in that file.
6. **Clause numbering**: Sections 3–5, 7–8 use chapter-wide sequential numbering (3.1;
   4.1–4.7; 5.1–5.4; 7.1–7.4; 8.1–8.7), each section's own sequence — not a single
   running count across the whole document. Section 6 instead restarts at "C1" within
   each of its own 15 numbered subsections (6.1–6.15), matching the pattern already
   flagged for Chapter 8.2. No duplicate clause numbers were found within any section or
   subsection.
7. Appendix 1 (tree species table) was genuine tabular data, not an image, so it was
   converted in full rather than logged as an unconverted figure.
