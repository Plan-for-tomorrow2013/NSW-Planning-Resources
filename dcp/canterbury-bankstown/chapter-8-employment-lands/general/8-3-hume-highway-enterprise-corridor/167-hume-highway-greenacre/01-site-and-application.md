# Section 6 — Greenacre Motor Alley (167 Hume Highway in Greenacre)

## Introduction

### 6.1 Land to which this section applies

This section of the DCP applies to the following properties as shown in Figure 6a below.

**Table 1: Subject Site**

| Address | Real Property ID | Site Area (approximately) |
|---|---|---|
| 167 Hume Highway, Greenacre | Lot 402, DP 631754 | 11,750m² |

> **Figure 6a: 167 Hume Highway, Greenacre** — an aerial site map showing the subject
> site boundary. Not converted — see `../appendices/_index.md`.

### 6.2 Application

This DCP applies to the subject site described in Table 1. This site specific DCP should be
read in conjunction with other chapters of the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control
Plan 2023.

If there are any inconsistencies between the objectives and controls in this chapter and any
other objectives and controls in this DCP, the objectives and controls in this chapter will
prevail, but only to the extent of that inconsistency.

In accordance with the *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021* (Chapter 4), the
Apartment Design Guide (ADG) apply to any residential application for the subject site.
Where there is any inconsistency between the ADG and this chapter of this DCP, the ADG
will prevail (other than those matters specified within the *State Environmental Planning
Policy (Housing) 2021* (Chapter 4)).

> **Precedence chain note:** this creates a three-tier precedence stack specific to 167
> Hume Highway: (1) the Apartment Design Guide prevails over this chapter for residential
> applications, except (2) where the Housing SEPP Chapter 4 itself specifies otherwise, and
> separately (3) this chapter (6.1–6.15) prevails over other DCP chapters generally. Worth
> surfacing explicitly since it differs from the simpler single-tier precedence statement used
> elsewhere in 8.1/8.2/8.3.
