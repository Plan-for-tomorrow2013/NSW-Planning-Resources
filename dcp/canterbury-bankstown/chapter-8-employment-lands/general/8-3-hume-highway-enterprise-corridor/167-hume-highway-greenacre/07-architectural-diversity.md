# 6.8 Architectural diversity and articulation

## Objectives

**O1** To ensure the scale, modulation and façade articulation of development responds to
its context.

**O2** To achieve architectural diversity and visual interest in development on the site.

## Development controls

**C1** The building length should not exceed 45m.

**C2** If the building length exceeds 45m, it should be broken into two or more
components, so no length is longer than 45m, before which a minimum 3x3m inset is
to be provided at all levels and be treated to provide visual relief.

**C3** Each component of a building should have a different architectural character to the
street or public domain and be articulated into smaller components at a scale or grain
that reflects the interior and exterior uses and circulation of the building and site.
