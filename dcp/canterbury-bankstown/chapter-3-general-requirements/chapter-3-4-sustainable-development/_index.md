Chapter 3.4 of the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 sets water
conservation and energy minimisation requirements for development. **Unlike the other
Chapter 3 sub-chapters converted so far, this chapter has an explicit "Application of this
chapter" section**: it applies only to buildings classified Class 5–9 under the Building Code
of Australia, for new proposals, extensions ≥5,000m² GFA, or extensions below 5,000m² that
expand the existing floor area by 50% or more — and it does not apply to a change of use.
Controls scale with gross floor area (5,000m² is the recurring threshold separating a basic
requirement from an additional site water management plan / larger-development
verification pathway). There is no former-LGA split.
