Chapter 3.6 of the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 sets sign
location/design and illumination/reflectance controls. Controls in Section 2 vary
substantially by planning zone (R2/R3/R4; E1/E2/MU1; E3/E4; SP4), and illumination limits
in Section 3 vary by a separate set of ambient-lighting "Zones" (1–5) defined in Section 4 —
see the note in [04-definitions.md](04-definitions.md) flagging that these are two different
zone systems sharing the same naming convention in the source. There is no "Application of
this chapter" scoping section and no former-LGA split — the chapter applies council-wide.
