### Scope note
Chapter 3.1 did not include an "Application of this chapter" section in the source PDF. The
converted folder therefore uses the council-wide Chapter 3 structure and does not create a
former-LGA subfolder.

Chapters 3.2, 3.3, 3.5, 3.6 and 3.7 likewise contain no "Application of this chapter" section
and no former-LGA split — each applies council-wide, though individual rate tables within
3.2 (Parking) do vary by former Canterbury LGA / former Bankstown LGA and by named
centre.

**Chapter 3.4 (Sustainable Development) is the exception**: it has an explicit "Application of
this chapter" section restricting it to Class 5–9 buildings under the Building Code of
Australia, for new proposals or extensions meeting specific gross-floor-area thresholds, and
excluding changes of use. See [chapter-3-4-sustainable-development/_index.md](chapter-3-4-sustainable-development/_index.md)
for detail — don't assume its scoping pattern applies to other Chapter 3 sub-chapters.

### Anomalies flagged during this conversion pass
- **3.3 Waste Management:** the "standard bin dimensions" table (clause 2.3) prints all
  three dimension columns under the header "Height" in the source PDF — very likely a
  template labelling error, flagged rather than silently corrected. See
  [chapter-3-3-waste-management/_index.md](chapter-3-3-waste-management/_index.md).
- **3.4 Sustainable Development:** Appendix 2 restarts clause numbering (`1`, `1.1`…`1.11`)
  independently of the chapter's `2.x`/`3.x` numbering, and references 25 unconverted
  figures. See [chapter-3-4-sustainable-development/_index.md](chapter-3-4-sustainable-development/_index.md).
- **3.6 Signs:** the term "Zone" is used for two unrelated systems in the source — planning
  zones (Section 2) and ambient-lighting zones 1–5 (Section 3/4). See
  [chapter-3-6-signs/_index.md](chapter-3-6-signs/_index.md).
- No duplicate clause numbers were found in 3.2, 3.3, 3.5, 3.6, or 3.7.
