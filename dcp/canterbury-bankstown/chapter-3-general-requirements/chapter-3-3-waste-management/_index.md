Chapter 3.3 of the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 sets council-wide
requirements for waste management systems in development, covering standard bin
generation rates and sizes, residential bin storage (individual and communal, including
residential flat buildings and mixed use development), commercial and industrial waste
storage/collection, and specific uses (boarding houses, child care, educational
establishments and places of public worship). It must be read in conjunction with Council's
Waste Design for New Developments Guides (A–G) and the Demolition and Construction
Guide; where this chapter and a Guide conflict, this chapter prevails. There is no
"Application of this chapter" scoping section and no former-LGA split — the chapter applies
council-wide.
