## Development controls

**2.1** The weekly generation rates per dwelling are:

| General waste | Recycling | Garden organics |
|---|---|---|
| 140L | 120L | 120L* |

> Note: All bin allocations are rounded up to the next whole number (for example the
> calculation of 4.4 bins will be rounded to 5 bins).
> \* Residential flat buildings, shop top housing and mixed use development are
> allocated 1 bin per 10 dwellings.

**2.2** The bin sizes for residential development are:

| Residential development | General waste | Recycling | Garden organics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attached dwellings, dwelling houses, dual occupancies, secondary dwellings, semi-detached dwellings | 140L | 240L | 240L |
| Manor houses, multi dwelling housing, multi dwelling housing (terraces) | 140L / 240L / 660L / 1,100L | 240L / 660L / 1,100L | 240L |
| Residential flat buildings, shop top housing, mixed use development | 660L / 1,100L or hook lift bin with compactor | 660L or 1,100L | 240L |

> Note: It is important to understand the size of bins that will be allocated by Council
> for the development. Check with Council if you need assistance in identifying the
> most appropriate bin size(s).

**2.3** The standard bin dimensions are:

| Standard bin type | Height | Width | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 140L mobile garbage bin | 930mm | 530mm | 610mm |
| 240L mobile garbage bin | 1,060mm | 580mm | 730mm |
| 660L bulk bin | 1,250mm | 1,370mm | 850mm |
| 1,100L bulk bin | 1,470mm | 1,370mm | 1,245mm |
| Hook lift/compactor bin (10m³–30m³) | 2.5m | 2.5m | 6m |

> Note: Dimensions are a guide only and may differ depending on the manufacturer. An
> additional 15cm is to be provided around each bin in the design of the waste bin
> storage area, to ensure it can function effectively and efficiently, and to avoid damage
> to walls and doors from bins scraping against them.
>
> **Conversion note:** the source table's three dimension columns are all headed
> "Height" in the printed PDF (Table: "The standard bin dimensions are"). Based on the
> figures given (e.g. 930mm / 530mm / 610mm for a 140L bin), the second and third
> columns are almost certainly Width and Depth rather than a second and third
> "Height" — the source table heading appears to be a labelling error carried over from
> the Council's template rather than a substantive content issue. Column labels above
> have been inferred accordingly; the printed figures themselves are reproduced exactly
> as shown. Verify against the current published PDF before relying on the column
> labels.

**2.4** The standard service frequencies for residential development are:

*Table 4: 140L and 240L bin sizes*

| Residential development | General waste | Recycling | Organics |
|---|---|---|---|
| All residential development | One collection per week | One collection per fortnight | One collection per fortnight |

*Table 5: 660L bin size*

| Residential development | General waste | Recycling | Organics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Less than 26 dwellings | One collection per week | One collection per fortnight | One collection per fortnight |
| 26–40 dwellings | One collection per week | One collection per week | One collection per fortnight |
| 41–70 dwellings | Two collections per week | One collection per week | One collection per fortnight |
| Greater than 70 dwellings | Discuss with Council | Discuss with Council | Discuss with Council |

*Table 6: 1,100L bin size*

| Residential development | General waste | Recycling | Organics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Less than 26 dwellings | Prohibited | Prohibited | Prohibited |
| 26–40 dwellings | One collection per week | One collection per fortnight | One collection per fortnight |
| 41–70 dwellings | Two collections per week | One collection per week | One collection per fortnight |
| 71–100 dwellings | Two collections per week | Two collections per week | One collection per fortnight |
| Greater than 100 dwellings | Discuss with Council | Discuss with Council | Discuss with Council |

*Table 7: Minimum size for bulky waste area*

| Residential flat buildings, shop top housing and mixed use development | Minimum area required |
|---|---|
| Less than 21 dwellings | 4m² |
| 21 to 30 dwellings | 5m² |
| 31 to 40 dwellings | 6m² |
| 41 to 50 dwellings | 7m² |
| 51 to 60 dwellings | 8m² |
| 61 to 70 dwellings | 9m² |
| 71 to 80 dwellings | 10m² |
| 81 to 90 dwellings | 11m² |
| 91 to 100 dwellings | 12m² |
| Greater than 100 dwellings | 13m² + 2m² per 50 additional dwellings (or part thereof) above 100 dwellings |
