Chapter 3.7 of the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 sets landscape
design controls (existing vegetation retention, deep soil zones, communal open space
quality, and minimum canopy tree planting rates) and biodiversity controls (retention of
native vegetation, bushland buffer zones, and habitat features). It must be read in
conjunction with Council's Landscape Guide. There is no "Application of this chapter"
scoping section and no former-LGA split — the chapter applies council-wide.
