Chapter 3.2 of the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 sets the off-street
car, bicycle and service vehicle parking rates required for each land use, plus design and
layout controls (driveways, headroom, loading docks, basement parking, bicycle parking,
accessible parking and electric vehicle charging infrastructure). It must be read in
conjunction with the Development Engineering Standards Guide. The chapter applies
council-wide; it does not contain an "Application of this chapter" section restricting it to
particular building classes or a former-LGA split, though several rate tables within Section 2
vary by former Canterbury LGA / former Bankstown LGA and by named centre.
