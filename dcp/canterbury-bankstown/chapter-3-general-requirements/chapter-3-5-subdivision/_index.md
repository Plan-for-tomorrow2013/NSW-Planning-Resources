Chapter 3.5 of the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 sets subdivision
controls covering battle-axe lots (lot dimensions, access handle width and construction) and
housing estates (solar orientation of lots, road/lane reserve widths, and security/graffiti-
minimisation setbacks where a site adjoins a railway corridor or open stormwater drain).
There is no "Application of this chapter" scoping section and no former-LGA split — the
chapter applies council-wide.
