# Section 6 — Flooding, Drainage and Water Sensitive Urban Design

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.9 — 297–299 Canterbury Road, Revesby
> (June 2023, Amended August 2024), page 14.

## Objectives

**O1** Reduce the risk to human life and damage to property caused by flooding.

**O2** Ensure development does not result in detrimental increases in the potential
flood affectation of adjacent or nearby properties.

**O3** Ensure the development does not significantly adversely impact the overland flow
path along the western side of the site.

**O4** Incorporate water sensitive urban design principles into the open space and/or
landscaping provided along the overland flow path.

## Development controls

**6.1** New development is to be setback from the western boundary by at least 6m to
maintain the existing overland flow path.

**6.2** Provide water sensitive landscaping and water sensitive stormwater
infrastructure within the development. Particular consideration should be given to
incorporating water sensitive elements in the treatment of the overland flow path.

**6.3** A Flood Emergency Response Plan is to be submitted with the application for
proposals with habitable spaces on the lower ground floor areas and/or basements which
are below flood planning level. This needs to be prepared in consultation with the NSW
State Emergency Service and Canterbury-Bankstown Council.

**6.4** Development of lots that are impacted by an existing overland flood path or
contain existing underground stormwater infrastructure must obtain consent for the
relocation and/or upgrade of the existing stormwater drainage infrastructure as
necessary within the proposed overland flow corridor.

**6.5** Any part of the building within the nominated flood planning levels is to be
built from flood compatible materials to minimise damage or erosion from floodwater.

**6.6** Implement the relevant flood planning controls including the
Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023, Canterbury-Bankstown Development
Control Plan 2023 and Council's *Development Engineering Standards Guide*.
