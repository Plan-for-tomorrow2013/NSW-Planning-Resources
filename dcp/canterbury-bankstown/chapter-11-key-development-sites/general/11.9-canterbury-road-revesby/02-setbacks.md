# Section 2 — Setbacks

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.9 — 297–299 Canterbury Road, Revesby
> (June 2023, Amended August 2024), page 7.

## Objectives

**O1** To create opportunities for high quality landscaping, an enhanced public domain
and provide open spaces on site.

**O2** To provide adequate tree canopy to mitigate air quality from the industrial area
and Canterbury Road.

## Development controls

**2.1** Development must achieve the following minimum setbacks:

| Minimum setbacks | |
|---|---|
| Front setback / Canterbury Road (southern boundary) | 15m |
| Side setback / Mavis Street (eastern boundary) | 10m |
| Rear setback / 9 Mavis Street (northern boundary) | See clause 2.2 below |
| Side setback (western boundary) | 6m |

Encroachments may be permitted for architectural features to provide articulation to
the elevations except for the rear setback in accordance with clause 2.2 below.

**2.2** Setbacks to the northern boundary to the property at 9 Mavis Street is a minimum
of 27m, which includes consideration of the existing right of way to 299A Canterbury
Road, Revesby. Encroachments by the basement area, building or any vertical
architectural features within the right of accessway is not permitted.

> **Note (narrower precedence statement)**: Where there is an inconsistency between the
> existing right of way and these controls, the terms of the right of way prevail. This
> is a second, narrower precedence statement in addition to the chapter-level clause in
> Section 1 — see the sub-chapter `_index.md` for the equivalent pattern in 11.1.
