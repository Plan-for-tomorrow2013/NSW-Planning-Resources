# Section 3 — Built Form and Design Excellence

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.9 — 297–299 Canterbury Road, Revesby
> (June 2023, Amended August 2024), page 8.

## Objectives

**O1** To create a high quality built form which balances the needs of medical service
provision with the human-scale.

**O2** To achieve design excellence in the future development of this site.

**O3** To deliver a safe environment with high levels of amenity for patients, staff and
visitors.

## Development controls

**3.1** Any application on this site for a hospital must be referred to the State Design
Review Panel. The Panel's recommendations must be incorporated into the detailed
design of the development.

> This referral requirement is not paralleled in any other Chapter 11 sub-chapter
> converted so far — see the sub-chapter `_index.md`.

**3.2** Minimise large expanses of blank walls through articulation, fenestration,
architectural detailing and the use of a range of materials.

**3.3** The corners of new development that face the street should use architectural
features and materials to reinforce the corner.

**3.4** Incorporate passive surveillance and CPTED design principles into the built
form, particularly in any areas that are intended to operate 24 hours.

**3.5** Provide covered entries to all main entries to public facilities such as
awnings, canopies or porticos.

**3.6** All substations and fire stairs are to be incorporated into the building form
and must not be located within any setbacks.

**3.7** Utilise high quality building materials.

**3.8** Design building components including the structural framing, roofing and
facade for longevity.
