## Note

Figure 2 is different in kind from every other Chapter 11 figure converted so far — it
is not a site plan, setback diagram, or map of the subject site, but a set of aerial/
precedent photographs of *other* buildings included as design inspiration. It carries
its own third-party photo credits (Queensland Health; John Gollings, Landscape
Architecture) in the source document, which should be preserved if this figure is later
converted to an image asset.

## Recommended follow-up

Convert Figure 1 (site map) before relying on this sub-chapter for site-boundary or
legal-description questions. Figure 2 is illustrative/aspirational rather than a
regulatory diagram, so its conversion is lower priority than Figure 1.
