## Section 1 — Development Site at 30–46 Auburn Road, Regents Park

### Explanation

The Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 and Canterbury-Bankstown
Development Control Plan 2023 combine to regulate effective and orderly development,
consistent with *Connective City 2036*.

The Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 is Council's principal planning
document. It provides objectives, zones and development standards such as lot sizes,
floor space ratios and building heights.

The Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 supports the LEP by providing
additional objectives and development controls to enhance the function, design and
amenity of the site at 30–46 Auburn Road, Regents Park.

> **Note (precedence clause)**: If applicable to an application, the development
> controls of Chapter 11.7 of this DCP will prevail if there is an inconsistency with
> any other development controls in this DCP.

### Objectives

**O1** To ensure development is compatible with the desired character of the site at
30–46 Auburn Road, Regents Park.

**O2** To achieve good design in terms of building form, bulk, architectural treatment,
visual amenity and landscape.

**O3** To ensure the building form and design provide appropriate amenity to residents
in terms of access to sunlight, privacy and protection from freight railway noise.

**O4** To ensure development is compatible with the prevailing suburban character and
amenity of neighbouring residential areas.

### Desired character

Residential development will be compatible with the character of the neighbouring Zone
R2 Low Density Residential and railway corridor.

### Development controls

**Dwelling yield**

**1.1** The maximum number of dwellings permissible on the site is 85.

**Storey limit (not including basements)**

**1.2** The storey limit is three storeys with the exception of the dwellings fronting
Auburn Road where the storey limit is two storeys, generally in accordance with Figure 1.

> See Figure 1 in the source PDF — not converted, logged below. Figure 1 is the sole
> source of the specific site layout (curved terrace rows facing the existing railway
> track, a residential flat building fronting Auburn Road with underground carpark
> access, and the two-storey/three-storey storey-limit split) that clause 1.2 refers to
> as "generally in accordance with."

**1.3** Development may contain attics provided:
(a) the attic does not give the external appearance of a storey; and
(b) the pitch of the roof creating the space does not exceed 35 degrees; and
(c) the external enclosing walls do not exceed a height of 300mm measured vertically
from the floor level of the attic, but does not include gabled end walls; and
(d) there is no balcony, terrace, and the like forming part of the attic; and
(e) the attic accommodates no more than two small rooms (for the purposes of a bedroom
and/or study) and an ensuite plus an internal link to the storey below; and
(f) the gross floor area of the attic does not exceed 60% of the gross floor area of the
storey immediately below; and
(g) one or more dormers may form part of the attic.

**1.4** The design of dormers must:
(a) must be compatible with the scale, form, and pitch of the roof; and
(b) must not project above the ridgeline of the main roof; and
(c) must not exceed a width of 2m; and
(d) the number of dormers must not dominate the roof plane.

**Setbacks**

**1.5** Development must achieve the following minimum setbacks subject to appropriate
acoustic treatment:
(a) minimum 1m setback to the railway boundary; and
(b) minimum 10m setback for dwellings to the northern boundary. The setback area must
be common facilities only, including road, driveway or open space.

**Access**

**1.6** Vehicular egress and ingress on the site must be from Auburn Road and be
facilitated by traffic management devices to minimise the impact upon the existing
Auburn Road traffic flow and appropriately accommodate traffic flows from the site into
Auburn Road. All costs associated with the provision of pedestrian protection measures
and traffic management devices must be borne by the applicant.

**Acoustic privacy**

**1.7** The consent authority must be satisfied that mitigation measures to control road
and rail noise and vibration have been incorporated into the development so that the
development complies with the following:
(a) Australian Standard AS/NZS 2107–2016, *Acoustics – Recommended design sound levels
and reverberation times for building interiors*;
(b) Australian Standard AS 3671–1989, *Acoustics – Road traffic noise intrusion –
Building siting and construction*;
(c) BS 6472:1992, *Guide to evaluation of human exposure to vibration in buildings (1 Hz
to 80 Hz)*;
(d) NSW Road Noise Policy (Environment Protection Authority);
(e) Interim Guidelines for Councils: Consideration of Rail Noise and Vibration in the
Planning Process (a joint document of the Rail Infrastructure Corporation and the State
Rail Authority, published November 2003).

## Notes / anomalies

- **Amendment date**: cover page reads "June 2023 (Amended August 2024)" with no
  amendment table supplied and no day-level dates given. Per the conversion guide,
  `effective_date`/`amended_date` are omitted from front matter rather than guessed.
- **Zones**: the document names the *neighbouring* zone (R2 Low Density Residential) in
  its "Desired character" statement but does not state the site's own LEP zone anywhere
  in the supplied pages. `zones` omitted from front matter as a result — worth confirming
  against the LEP rather than assuming R2 applies to the site itself.
- **Figure 1** is unconverted and is the sole source of the specific building layout,
  density basis (1 dwelling per 250m² against a 21,180m² site area, broadly consistent
  with but not identical to the 85-dwelling maximum in clause 1.1), and the geographic
  extent of the two-storey/three-storey split referenced in clause 1.2 — the same
  stronger-caveat pattern already flagged for 11.1's Figures 12.1.5/12.1.6, 11.2's Figure
  1b, 11.5's Figure 1c, and 11.6's Section 4 diagrams/photo. Worth converting before
  relying on this sub-chapter for site-specific massing advice.
- **Figure 1's own drawn density calculation (1 dwelling per 250m² on a 21,180m² site ≈
  85 dwellings) appears consistent with clause 1.1's 85-dwelling cap** — noted as a
  cross-check rather than an anomaly, since the figure and the numbered clause agree.
- **Precedence clause** and general structure (Explanation → Objectives → Desired
  character → numbered Development controls) match the pattern already established for
  11.1–11.6 and 11.8.
- No former-LGA content; no B2/E1 zone-naming issue and no stray cross-chapter section
  reference of the kind flagged for 11.4 were found in this sub-chapter.
