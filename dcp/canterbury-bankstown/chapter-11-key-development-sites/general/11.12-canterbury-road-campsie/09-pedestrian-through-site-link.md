# Section 9 — Pedestrian Through Site Link

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.12 — 445 Canterbury Road, Campsie
> (June 2023, Amended April 2025), page 21. This section has no objectives — it opens
> directly with development controls, consistent with Section 8.

## Development controls

**9.1** The pedestrian through site link shall be a minimum width of 6m and dedicated
to Council, in accordance with the planning agreement.

**9.2** Landscaping shall reinforce the entry to this midblock link from Canterbury
Road and the rear laneway. Careful consideration must be given to landscape selection
and lighting to ensure surveillance is maintain from the street and building.

> Preserved as printed: "...to ensure surveillance **is maintain** from the street and
> building" — likely intended as "is maintained". See the sub-chapter `_index.md`.

**9.3** The design of the link must carefully consider the 2.5m change in topography of
the site and ensure that materials and finishes selection are of high quality and
durable. The inclusion of a lift, use of tactile surfaces for visually impaired persons
and other accessibility treatments must be considered in the design.

**9.4** The pedestrian through site link is to include provision of public art in
accordance with a Public Art Strategy submitted for the site as part of a future
application for a new hospital development.
