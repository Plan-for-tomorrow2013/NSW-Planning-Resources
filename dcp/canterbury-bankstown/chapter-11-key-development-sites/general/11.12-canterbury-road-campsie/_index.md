# 11.12 — 445 Canterbury Road, Campsie

Site-specific precinct DCP for a proposed **hospital** at 445 Canterbury Road, Campsie
(Zone E3 Productivity Support), anchoring the "Campsie Medical Precinct" within the
Campsie Strategic Centre / Campsie Town Centre masterplan. Thirteen internal sections
covering the introduction, desired character, key design principles, site layout and
setbacks, built form, solar access (including protection of future apartment
development across Canterbury Road), public domain/deep soil/landscaping, a pocket
park, a pedestrian through-site link, access and movement (a new rear laneway and
public mid-block link), materials and finishes, water sensitive urban design/flooding/
energy efficiency, and public art. Carries the chapter-level precedence clause. No
former-LGA content — sits under `general/`.

Given 13 internal sections, kept as its **own folder** per the conversion guide's 2+
section rule — the **second-largest Chapter 11 sub-chapter by section count**, after
11.1 (17 sections) and ahead of 11.10 (10 sections).

## Notes / anomalies

- **Second hospital-specific sub-chapter, same scoping pattern as 11.9**: the
  applicability clause reads *"This chapter applies to development for the purpose of a
  hospital at 445 Canterbury Road, Campsie. For other development types, the other
  chapters of the DCP apply."* — almost identical wording to 11.9's hospital-only
  scoping for 297–299 Canterbury Road, Revesby. This is now a confirmed pattern for
  hospital sites specifically (as distinct from 11.8's broader
  "residential accommodation purpose" scoping).
- **Site consolidation precondition**: key design principle (j) requires the site's six
  separate lots (Lot 3 DP 337683; Lots A and B DP 355656; Lots A and B DP 416123; Lot 15
  DP 3995; Lots A and B DP 391661; Lot 13 DP 3995) to be consolidated into a single lot
  before any redevelopment commences. This is a new requirement type not seen in any
  other Chapter 11 sub-chapter converted so far (11.9 gave a legal lot/DP description but
  did not require consolidation as a precondition).
- **Same amendment date and a shared naming inconsistency as 11.10**: 11.12's cover page
  reads "June 2023 (Amended April 2025)" — matching 11.10 (Chullora Marketplace) rather
  than the "Amended August 2024" seen in 11.1–11.9. **Both 11.10 and 11.12 also share the
  same mis-naming of the *State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021*** — both
  render it "**State Environment Planning Policy** (Housing) 2021" (missing "-al"),
  whereas 11.8 (clause 1.11) correctly renders it "State Environmental Planning Policy
  (Housing) 2021". The co-occurrence of the same amendment date *and* the same naming
  slip in exactly these two sub-chapters (and not the others) suggests 11.10 and 11.12
  may share a common drafting template or were amended together, separately from
  11.1–11.9 — worth keeping in mind for the ongoing cross-chapter reconciliation pass.
  Preserved verbatim in both sub-chapters rather than silently corrected.
- **Several likely source typos preserved verbatim** (not corrected): clause 4.5 "A new
  public laneway will be dedicated **at to** Council" (extra word); clause 6.2(b) "...on
  the 21 June **onto to** at least 1m²..." (extra word); clause 7.5 "...how the proposed
  landscaping **meetings** Water Sensitive Urban Design..." (should likely read "meets");
  clause 9.2 "...to ensure surveillance **is maintain** from the street and building"
  (should likely read "is maintained"); clause 11.3 "E-glass or similar should be
  **consider** especially for the western" (sentence appears to trail off incomplete —
  "the western" is missing a noun, e.g. "elevation" or "facade"). Five typos in one
  sub-chapter is more than usual for this repo — flagged together here rather than
  individually re-explained in each section file, though each is also noted inline
  where it occurs.
- **Address range discrepancy in Figure 3's own caption**: the chapter, Table 1 and every
  other figure describe the site as "445 Canterbury Road, Campsie," but Figure 3's
  embedded title block reads "445-459 Canterbury Rd, Campsie" — a wider address range.
  This likely reflects that the underlying masterplan/landscaping concept report
  considered a broader precinct extent than the final six-lot, ~4,414m² consolidated site
  described in Table 1. Not resolved here; flagged rather than assumed.
- **Building height expressed differently by frontage but tied to the same RL**: clause
  5.1 gives a 44.1m maximum height on the Canterbury Road frontage and 45.5m on the
  northern side of the building, **both expressed as "RL 72.75m AHD."** This is
  consistent with a sloping site (Section 5, O2: "development responds to the site's
  topography") — the same absolute roof datum produces two different "building height"
  figures depending on which frontage's ground level the height is measured from — rather
  than being a contradiction. Preserved as printed; not flagged as an error, but worth
  understanding rather than assuming a copy-paste mistake.
- **Seven unconverted figures** — the most of any Chapter 11 sub-chapter after 11.1 (10).
  See `appendices/_index.md`. Several are structurally important: Figure 4/5 are the sole
  source of the specific setback dimensions in Table 2 (clause 4.1), and Figure 7 is the
  sole source of the rear laneway's indicative alignment (clause 10.1).
- No former-LGA content; Zone E3 Productivity Support (the site's own zone) is stated
  explicitly in Section 1 — the first Chapter 11 sub-chapter to state its own site's LEP
  zone directly in the introduction (rather than only referencing neighbouring zones, as
  in 11.10's Zone R2/SP2 setback calibrations).
