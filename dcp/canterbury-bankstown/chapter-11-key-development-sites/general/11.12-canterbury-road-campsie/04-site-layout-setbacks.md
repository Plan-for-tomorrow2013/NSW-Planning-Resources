# Section 4 — Site Layout and Setbacks

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.12 — 445 Canterbury Road, Campsie
> (June 2023, Amended April 2025), pages 10–13.

## Objectives

**O1** To provide appropriate setbacks to street frontages and common boundaries that
allow for mature trees, deep soil landscaping and provide a buffer to residential uses.

**O2** To provide setbacks to the building envelope to ensure that solar access is
provided and maintained to existing and future residential development on surrounding
properties as per applicable design guidelines, such as the Apartment Design Guide.

**O3** To provide an eastern side setback for a through site link and ensure amenity,
deep soil and safety. To provide a new landscaped public pocket park.

**O4** To provide for a rear laneway from Stanley Street through to the eastern
boundary for future connection further to the east.

**O5** To minimise bulk and scale impacts to adjoining properties by introducing a
street wall setback on the Canterbury Road frontage.

**O6** To create an appropriate building envelope respectful to the desired future
context.

## Development controls

**4.1** Development must achieve the minimum setbacks identified in Table 2 and
demonstrated in Figure 4.

**Table 2: Setbacks from site boundaries**

| Site boundary | Minimum setback |
|---|---|
| Canterbury Road | 3.5m clear setback from Canterbury Road (south property boundary) for the predominant street wall. An additional 6m setback (9.5m setback in total) consistent with Figure 5 for the two floors above. An additional setback consistent with Figure 5 for rooftop plant above approximately 39.5m height from the Canterbury Road ground level. A horizontal indent at approximately 15m-17m above the Canterbury Road existing ground level to refer to Canterbury Road emerging context street wall height, dividing the street wall into generally equal upper and lower sections. |
| Stanley Street | 3.5m from western property boundary |
| East boundary | 6m from eastern property boundary |
| North boundary | Adequate setback from the northern property boundary to enable a minimum 9m wide rear access laneway |

**4.2** Encroachments by the basement area, building or any vertical architectural
features within the deep soil areas of the setbacks is not permitted.

**4.3** Functional elements such as lift overruns, air conditioning units, plant
equipment, vent stacks and communication devices are to be visually discreet and
setback and/or screened to minimise visibility, particularly from the public domain.

**4.4** Site infrastructure including electricity substations, kiosks, fire boosters
and hydrants and fire stairs are to be integrated into the building form and using
similar materials and finishes or screened by landscape elements. Locating any
services in setbacks should be avoided.

**4.5** A new public laneway will be dedicated at to Council as a public road to be
used to access the hospital. There will be no vehicular access from Canterbury Road.

**4.6** A pocket park of a minimum 205m² in area will be provided in the north-western
corner of the site for the use of the public, staff and patients.
