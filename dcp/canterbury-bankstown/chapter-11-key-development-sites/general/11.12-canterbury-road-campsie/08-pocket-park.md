# Section 8 — Pocket Park

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.12 — 445 Canterbury Road, Campsie
> (June 2023, Amended April 2025), page 20. This section has no objectives — it opens
> directly with a single development control.

## Development controls

**8.1** The pocket park will be a minimum area of 205m² and provide a place of repose,
be safe and easy to access and have high amenity using planting, street furniture,
public art and lighting. Planting must be selected carefully to ensure that
surveillance is ensured.
