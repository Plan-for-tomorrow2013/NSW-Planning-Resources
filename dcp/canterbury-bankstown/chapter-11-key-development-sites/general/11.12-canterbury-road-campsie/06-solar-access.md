# Section 6 — Solar Access

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.12 — 445 Canterbury Road, Campsie
> (June 2023, Amended April 2025), pages 16–17.

## Objectives

**O1** To ensure that the development maximises solar access to any communal areas,
private open spaces and internal areas within the hospital.

**O2** To minimise overshadowing to any adjoining sites, open space and the public
domain.

**O3** To ensure that solar access to apartments and communal and private open space on
the southern side of Canterbury Road that are redeveloped in the future to include
shop top housing receive compliant solar access in accordance with the *State
Environment Planning Policy (Housing) 2021* (Chapter 4) and the Apartment Design
Guide.

> Preserved as printed: "*State Environment Planning Policy*" (missing "-al") — see the
> sub-chapter `_index.md`.

**O4** To ensure that communal and private open space areas in the hospital site
receiving direct sun also have provision for shading devices. These shading devices
must integrate with the hospital's design language and be of high-quality materials
and finishes that will withstand outdoor conditions.

**O5** To ensure that shading elements are incorporated into the building facades that
are integrated with the design language of the building.

## Development controls

**6.1** The hospital proposal shall demonstrate acceptable solar access is provided to
the private open space and internal living areas of existing residential properties on
the southern side of Canterbury Road and on western neighbouring sites.

**6.2** The hospital proposal shall demonstrate that it does not prevent apartment
development on the southern side of Canterbury Road at 412-416 Canterbury Road and 1-5
Robertson Street, Campsie otherwise reasonably complying with either of the following,
as relevant:
(a) ADG Objective 4A-1 Design Criteria: living rooms and private open spaces of at
least 70% of apartments in a building receive a minimum of two hours direct sunlight
between 9am and 3pm at mid-winter (noting also the relevant Design Guidelines under
this objective); or
(b) for apartment development below the thresholds where the ADG would apply: a
minimum of two hours direct sunlight between 9am and 3pm on the 21 June onto to at
least 1m² of the living room windows and a minimum 50% of the required minimum area of
private open spaces.

> Preserved as printed: clause 6.2(b) reads "...on the 21 June **onto to** at least
> 1m²..." — likely contains an extra word in the source. See the sub-chapter
> `_index.md`.

**6.3** The hospital proposal must ensure that it does not prevent any communal open
space as part of these apartment developments otherwise receiving the minimum solar
access as stated in the Apartment Design Guide.

**6.4** Solar access plans, including view from the sun studies shall be required to
demonstrate requirements in clauses 6.1, 6.2 and 6.3. In demonstrating compliance, at
least three redevelopment scenarios for the properties listed in clause 6.2 are to be
provided to account for possible different lot amalgamation.

**6.5** Adequate solar access will be provided to communal, open space areas and
private areas within the hospital site and plans shall be provided that demonstrate
solar access. Any landscaping plans provided will indicate all shading elements to
these spaces.
