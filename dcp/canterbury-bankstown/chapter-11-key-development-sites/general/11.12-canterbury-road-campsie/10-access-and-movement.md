# Section 10 — Access and Movement

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.12 — 445 Canterbury Road, Campsie
> (June 2023, Amended April 2025), pages 22–23.

## Objectives

**O1** To provide a functional, safe and easy to navigate movement network throughout
the site and hospital where pedestrian and cyclists are given priority, with due
regard to emergency vehicle movement, during the operating hours of the hospital.

**O2** To provide functional and clear access and set down area for emergency vehicles
that minimises potential conflict with pedestrians and other vehicles on the site.

**O3** To ensure safety for pedestrians crossing to the pocket park from vehicles
entering the sites, particularly emergency vehicles.

**O4** To provide for vehicle and pedestrian access from Stanley Street that will
facilitate movements, servicing, parking and appropriate addressing of the buildings.

**O5** To ensure for universal design within the building and site, signage devices and
wayfinding elements.

## Development controls

**10.1** To deliver a rear two-way laneway of a minimum 9 metre width from Stanley
Street through to the eastern boundary in anticipation of future connection to Una
Street. This public road will be dedicated to Council in accordance with the planning
agreement (see Figure 7).

> See Figure 7 ("Rear laneway (indicative alignment) and eastern pedestrian link") in
> the source PDF — a site plan showing the laneway's indicative alignment (shown in
> orange) sweeping from Stanley Street across the northern boundary, and a separate
> eastern pedestrian link (shown in blue) along the boundary with "No 443 Single Storey
> Brick Cottage." Not converted, logged in
> [`../appendices/_index.md`](../appendices/_index.md). This figure is the sole source
> of the laneway's specific indicative alignment.

**10.2** The design of the laneway shall incorporate manoeuvring paths to accommodate
the largest vehicles that will use the site including emergency, waste collection and
delivery vehicles.

**10.3** The entry to the basement car parking off the laneway shall be located to
prioritise pedestrian safety.

**10.4** The building entry from Canterbury Road should:
(a) be at the same general level as the footpath and accessible directly from the
street, and
(b) provide a positive street address in the form of entries, lobbies and clear
glazing, which positively contribute to street activity and promote passive
surveillance.
