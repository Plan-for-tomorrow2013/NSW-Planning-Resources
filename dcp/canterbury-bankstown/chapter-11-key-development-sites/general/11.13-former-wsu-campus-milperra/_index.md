# 11.13 — Former WSU Campus, Milperra

**Status:** Converted (7 sections + 2 text appendices; site-specific precinct DCP for 2 and 2A Bullecourt Avenue, Milperra — the former Western Sydney University Bankstown/Milperra campus)

**Applies to:** Land at 2 Bullecourt Avenue (Lot 2, DP 1291984, ~19,640m²) and 2A Bullecourt Avenue (Lot 2, DP 101147, ~202m²), Milperra, plus the C2 Woodland Conservation Zone shown on the sub-chapter's Concept Plan. ## Internal zone/scope split (relevant to the chapter-level "Note on internal former-LGA splits")

Unlike most Chapter 11 sub-chapters, 11.13 has a genuine **multi-zone internal split** driven by its own Structure Plan/Concept Plan (Figure 2), not just a scale or building-type transition: it contains R1-family residential zoning (of at least three visually distinct built-form categories per the plan legend), a B1/E1 local centre and childcare area, RE1 public open space, and a C2 Environmental Conservation (Cumberland Plain Woodland) zone, all within the one site. This is a different kind of internal variation from 11.4's E1/R4 split, 11.6's development-type split, 11.10's storey-height-map-driven split, or 11.11/11.1's scale-transition-only pattern — it is closer to a genuine LEP zoning mix than any other Chapter 11 site converted so far.

**Flag — zone legend anomaly:** the Concept Plan (Figure 2) legend labels three different housing typologies — "Premium Detached Housing (Min. 10m lot width)", "Residential Three Storey", and "Low Rise Residential (Two storey max.)" — all under the single heading "R1 Zone". This may be a legend-drafting artefact (three different LEP zones intended, e.g. R1/R2/R3, mislabelled identically) rather than three genuinely identical zonings sharing one code. Recommend checking against the LEP zoning map before relying on this for zone-based filtering.

## Numbering pattern (relevant to the chapter-level "Note on internal structure/numbering")

11.13 uses **Objectives (O1, O2…) and Development controls (C1, C2…)** throughout, with both counters **restarting independently in every numbered sub-section** (3.1, 3.2, 3.2.1 and its three further sub-groups, 3.3–3.9, 4.1–4.7, 5, 6, 7 and 7.1) — the same convention the chapter-level index previously described as unique to 11.14. **This means 11.14 was not, in fact, the only Chapter 11 sub-chapter to use this pattern** — 11.13 does too, independently, across an even larger number of restarting counters (at least 20 separate O#/C# sequences). Appendix A additionally introduces a fourth labelling convention, "G#" (restarting under each of its own four headings). See the chapter-level `_index.md` for the necessary correction to the "first Chapter 11 sub-chapter to use O#/C# labelling" claim.
