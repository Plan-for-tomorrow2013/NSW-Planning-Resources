# 4.3.2 — Aboriginal Heritage

## Objectives

**O1.** To acknowledge, respect and celebrate Aboriginal heritage as an integral part of
development and placemaking.

## Controls

**C1.** In accordance with the *Guide to Investigating, Assessing and Reporting on
Aboriginal Cultural Heritage in NSW* (OEH, 2011), impacts to Aboriginal
archaeological sites, areas of archaeological potential and Aboriginal cultural sites
are to be avoided or minimised.

**C2.** Any Aboriginal heritage investigations in the Riverwood Estate must include an
updated extensive search of the Aboriginal Heritage Information Management
System (AHIMS) database in order to determine if new sites have been registered
in the precinct or its immediate surrounds. AHIMS searches must include a buffer
of at least 200 metres in order to account for known errors in the AHIMS data.

**C3.** Prior to the submission of any DA involving physical works, consultation must be
undertaken with Aboriginal stakeholders to:
(a) Expand on Aboriginal social and cultural values and connections to the
Riverwood Estate and surrounds.
(b) Ascertain how the development may best maximise the presence, visibility and
celebration of Aboriginal histories and living cultures.
