## Note on local numbering

Each of 4.3.1, 4.3.2 and 4.3.3 restarts its own **C1, C2, C3…** control numbering (and its
own O1, O2… objective numbering) from 1. This is the same "locally-restarting numbering"
pattern flagged at the whole-chapter level for 11.14 (see the sub-document `_index.md`) —
a control labelled "C1" in 4.3.1 is unrelated to "C1" in 4.3.2 or 4.3.3. Cross-references
should always cite the sub-subsection together with the control label (e.g. "4.3.2 C2", not
just "C2").
