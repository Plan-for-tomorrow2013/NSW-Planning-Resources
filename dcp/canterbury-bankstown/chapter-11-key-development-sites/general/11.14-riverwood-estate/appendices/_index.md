## Notes on figure content (from visual inspection of the source PDF)

- **Figure 2** (Structure Plan) shows the site bounded by Washington Avenue, Belmore
  Road and Roosevelt Avenue, with Virginia Place running through the centre, buildings
  up to 8 storeys either side of a public through-site link, and a single indicative taller
  building (up to 12 storeys) near the south-west of the site.
- **Figure 5** (height map) shows a 12-storey tower footprint, 8-storey wings either side
  of the through-site link, 6-storey end elements, and 4-storey street-wall elements
  along Virginia Place.
- **Figure 6** (setbacks map) shows a 15m ground-level setback (green) and 3m upper-level
  setback (pale) wrapping the site perimeter, with the through-site link crossing the
  middle.
- **Figure 9** (Virginia Place section) is a fully dimensioned 20m-wide street cross-section
  (verge, shared path, planting/seating, parking/planting, travel lanes, building setback)
  — this one contains dimension data that could be converted to a table if a more precise
  data extraction is wanted later; left as an image-only reference for now per this pass's
  scope.
