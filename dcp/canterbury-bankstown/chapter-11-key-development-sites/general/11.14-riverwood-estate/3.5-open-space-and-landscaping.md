# 3.5 — Open Space and Landscaping

## Objectives

**O1.** To prioritise tree retention to ensure significant trees are retained and conserved as
much as possible.

**O2.** To contribute to the overall 30 per cent tree canopy target, and ensure sufficient
deep soil is provided to support healthy growth of mature trees.

## Controls

**C1.** Landscape design and minimum landscaped areas are to be provided in accordance
with the relevant provisions of the DCP 2023.

**C2.** Development must be designed to retain existing high value canopy trees on the site
where possible.

**C3.** The placement of gardens and landscaping on the verges should be reduced only to
locations of key significance across the site, such as outside key community
facilities or services.

**C4.** All trees planted within the public domain (streets, parks and verges) must be
installed by the developer using a minimum pot size of 75 litres but no greater than
200 litres.

**C5.** A consistent deep soil zone should be provided along all street boundaries to
facilitate a generous landscaped buffer with substantial canopy trees.

**C6.** A continuous canopy coverage of street trees must be provided at least 10-15m
intervals either on the landscaped verge or in the parking lane.

**C7.** Tree plantings must be strategically placed along pedestrian and cycle paths to
provide natural shade and help mitigate the effects of urban heat.

**C8.** All street furniture and other outdoor fixtures must be designed to match the
Council's furniture palette.

**C9.** Development must ensure that the current and future use of any existing parking
lanes is not impeded.

**C10.** The placement of trees along the road reserve must also provide sufficient
separation among other trees to ensure potential for their robust growth.

**C11.** The placement of amenities such as bins and toilets must be planned in a way that
does not limit potential infrastructure developments, like pedestrian crossings.

**C12.** Deep soil targets are to be provided in accordance with the Apartment Design Guide
(ADG).

**C13.** Deep soil areas must be consolidated to allow for effective planting. These areas
should be identified based on local soil conditions, free from structures and
services, and connected to the local groundwater to help manage storm water
where needed.

**C14.** Where landscaping is provided within the public domain and includes elements
that require more than minimal maintenance, the applicant must enter into a
Planning Agreement or other suitable mechanism to allocate ongoing funding and
responsibility. The planning agreement must:
(a) Demonstrate how ongoing maintenance will be managed without cost to Council.
(b) Ensure landscaping is maintained in perpetuity to the satisfaction of Council.

**C15.** Kerbside planting must:
(a) Provide a clear functional benefit, such as delineating pedestrian zones or
improving safety; and
(b) Be designed using low-maintenance species and materials.
