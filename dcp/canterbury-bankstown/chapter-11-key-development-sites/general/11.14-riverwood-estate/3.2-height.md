# 3.2 — Height

## Objectives

**O1.** To provide a range of building heights whilst maintaining a high level of residential
amenity and solar access.

## Controls

**C1.** Building heights must not exceed the maximum height in storeys as shown in
Figure 5.

> Figure 5 (Maximum Building Height in Storeys Map) shows a range from 4 storeys
> (along Virginia Place) up to a single 12-storey tower (indicated on the Structure Plan,
> Figure 2, at a specific footprint near the centre-west of the site), with 6- and 8-storey
> components elsewhere. See [`appendices/_index.md`](appendices/_index.md).
