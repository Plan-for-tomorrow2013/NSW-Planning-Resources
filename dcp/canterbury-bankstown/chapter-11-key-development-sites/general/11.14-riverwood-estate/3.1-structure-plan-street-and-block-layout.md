# 3.1 — Structure Plan, Street and Block Layout

## Objectives

**O1.** To establish a comprehensive framework guiding the initial stage of development for
the Riverwood Estate.

**O2.** To outline the proposed road network, building locations, open space provisions,
through-site pedestrian links, infrastructure upgrades and essential connections to
adjacent areas.

**O3.** To retain part of the existing street network and street trees, wherever possible.

**O4.** To enhance the street network to ensure high amenity, safety, urban greening, and
permeability for all users.

**O5.** To ensure new streets are accessible and footpaths will be important to support
opportunities for walking for older people and people with a disability.

**O6.** To create a residential neighbourhood with opportunity for retail uses to support the
future population of the site.

**O7.** To ensure a mix of dwelling types are provided, and that housing is flexible,
encourages diversity, and caters for the needs of the existing and future resident
population.

**O8.** To enable non-residential uses that meet the needs of local residents.

## Controls

**C1.** Development must be consistent with the Structure Plan at Figure 2, and the
Indicative Land Use Plan at Figure 3.

**C2.** Virginia Place is to be realigned and widened in accordance with the alignment
illustrated in Figure 3.

**C3.** All streets intended for dedication to Council must be free from any basement or
underground encroachments.

**C4.** Building layouts are to be designed to deliver deep soil areas that support mature
canopy trees and absorb stormwater runoff.

**C5.** Development must ensure that waste collection and parking demands are fully
accommodated on-site, without reliance on the surrounding street network.

**C6.** Development at the site must incorporate the undergrounding of powerlines along
Washington Avenue, Roosevelt Avenue and Virginia Place to improve pedestrian
amenity and avoid interference with the proposed building structures. The proposal
must demonstrate the future undergrounding of power lines along Belmore Road is
not impacted by the proposed development.

**C7.** A through site link is to be provided between Belmore Road and Virginia Place in
accordance with Figure 4, and is to:
(a) Be open to the sky.
(b) Be accessible to pedestrians 24 hours a day.
(c) Be designed to be accessible for people of all abilities.
(d) Be designed to ensure pedestrian safety through the limiting of vehicular access
(other than temporary maintenance or emergency vehicles).
(e) Provide a safe environment, including appropriate lighting and clear straight
sightlines.
(f) Provide a legible, unobstructed pedestrian footway for their entire length without
fencing or gates that restrict pedestrian access.
(g) The through site link is to allow public access as easement on title. The easement
must be registered prior to the occupation of the site.
(h) The land owner is to maintain the through site link in perpetuity, not Council.
