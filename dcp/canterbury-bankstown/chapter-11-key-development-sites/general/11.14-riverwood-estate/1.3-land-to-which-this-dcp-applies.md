# 1.3 — Land to Which this DCP Applies

This section of the DCP applies to part of the land within the Riverwood Estate, as outlined
in blue and illustrated in Figure 1.

The proposed first stage of development is located to the north east of the Riverwood
Estate bound by Washington Avenue to the north, Belmore Road North to the east, and
Roosevelt Avenue to the south.
