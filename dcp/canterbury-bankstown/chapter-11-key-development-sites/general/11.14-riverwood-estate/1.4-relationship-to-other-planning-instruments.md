# 1.4 — Relationship to Other Planning Instruments and DCPs

This DCP is for the purposes of Section 4.15 of the *Environmental Planning and Assessment
Act 1979* (EP&A Act).

This DCP adopts the provisions of the Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023 (**DCP 2023**) in
accordance with 3.43(3) of the EP&A Act.

This DCP is to be read in conjunction with the provisions and requirements of:
- The Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 (LEP 2023); and
- Any other relevant policy adopted by Canterbury-Bankstown Council.
- Any other relevant policy adopted by the Department of Planning, Housing and
  Infrastructure.

References in this DCP to the LEP 2023 or DCP 2023 are also references to any replacement
of those plans.

**If there is an inconsistency between this DCP and the provisions of the DCP 2023, this DCP
prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.**

> Note: this "prevails over DCP 2023" wording mirrors the standalone-precinct override
> clause used by other Chapter 11 sub-chapters (e.g. 11.11 Section 1: "the development
> controls of Chapter 11.11 of this DCP will prevail if there is an inconsistency..."),
> though it is stated here at the level of the whole 11.14 chapter rather than a single
> section — consistent with 11.14's much larger, multi-section scope.

## Definitions used in this DCP

- **'Consent Authority'** means:
  - Canterbury-Bankstown Council in its capacity under the *Environmental Planning
    and Assessment Act 1979*; or
  - The Canterbury-Bankstown Local Planning Panel (CBLPP); or
  - The Land and Environment Court of New South Wales; or
  - The Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure.
- **'Council'** means Canterbury-Bankstown Council in its capacities outside the
  *Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979*, such as a landowner or authority
  under the *Local Government Act 1993*, or similar.
- **'SSP'** refers to State Significant Precinct, being areas identified by the NSW
  Government as having state or regional importance due to their social, economic, or
  environmental value.
- **The 'Court'** refers to the Land and Environment Court of New South Wales,
  established on 1 September 1980 by the *Land and Environment Court Act 1979* (the
  Court Act).
