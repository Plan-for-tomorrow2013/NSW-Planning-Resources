## Development controls

### Site coverage

Lots 450m² and over: maximum 50% for two storey dwelling houses, maximum 60% for single
storey dwelling houses.

Lots less than 450m²: maximum 60%.

> All ancillary development (e.g. garden sheds) is included in the maximum site coverage.

### Maximum floor area

Maximum 330m² for all lots.

### Storey limit

Maximum two storey limit.

### Front setback

Minimum 4.5m.

### Articulation zone

1.5m beyond front building line and a maximum 25% of building width.

### Side setbacks

Lots up to 18m wide: 0.9m to 5.5m in height then above that height 0.9m plus 1/4 the
height over 5.5m.

Lots over 18m wide: 1.5m to 5.5m in height then above that height 1.5m plus 1/4 the
height over 5.5m.

### Rear setbacks

**Detached front access house lots** — minimum 3m for single storey with a rear wall
height of up to 4.5m high, otherwise a minimum 8m applies.

**Detached front access house lots less than 30m deep at any point perpendicular to the
front boundary** — minimum 3m setback for single storey with a rear wall height up to
4.5m high, otherwise a minimum 6m applies.

**Rear setbacks for lots along the western boundary of the precinct** — an additional 3m
in the form of the 'landscaped area' results in rear setbacks for these lots being a
minimum 6m for single storey with a rear wall of up to 4.5m high, otherwise a minimum 11m
applies.

**Rear access lots** — minimum 1m setback to rear lane for garages and accessory
dwellings if applicable.

### Secondary road setbacks

**Detached house corner lots**: minimum 3m setback to the secondary road boundary.

**Dual frontage lots**: minimum 4.5m setback to the secondary road boundary.

### Landscape

- The minimum landscaped area is:
  (a) Lots 300–450m²: minimum 15% of total lot
  (b) Lots 450–600m²: minimum 20% of total lot
  (c) Lots 600–900m²: minimum 30% of total lot
- The minimum dimension of a landscaped area is 1.5m.
- At least 50% of the landscaped area is to be located behind the front building line.
- For lots less than 18m wide at least 25% of the area to the front of the building line
  must be landscaped.
- For lots at least 18m wide at least 50% of the area to the front of the building line
  must be landscaped.

### Private open space

The minimum area for the principal private open space is 24m², at least 4m wide, flat
and directly accessible from a living room.

### Car parking and access

- All dwelling houses are to provide at least one off-street car parking space.
- A car parking space may comprise of a garage, car port or open car parking space.
- Front access car parking spaces are required to be setback at least 5.5m from the road
  boundary.
- Front access car parking spaces are required to be setback at least 1m behind the
  front building line.
- Rear access car parking spaces require 1m setback from the rear lane boundary.
- On corner lots, side access car parking spaces are required to be setback at least 3m
  from the secondary road boundary.
- On double frontage lots the car parking can be located on either frontage but not on
  both.

### Maximum garage door width

- Maximum garage door width varies with lot size:
  (a) 8–12m lot width: 3.2m.
  (b) Over 12m lot width: 6m.
- Corner lots and dual frontage lots with car parking access from the secondary road to
  have a maximum garage door width of 6m.

### Earthworks and drainage

- Excavation for basements:
  (a) Excavation permitted under the building footprint to provide a basement.
  (b) Maximum floor area for basement is 45m².
- Excavation outside the building footprint:
  (a) The maximum depth of excavation on a site outside the building footprint is 1m and
      must not extend more than 2m beyond the external wall of the dwelling house or
      ancillary development.
  (b) Excavation associated with swimming pools must not exceed the depth of the pool
      structure.
- Fill associated with the dwelling or garage must be contained within the external
  walls of the building or by a retaining wall with a height no greater than 1.5m above
  ground level (existing).
- Retaining walls:
  (a) Retaining walls to be no greater than 1m high.
  (b) Retaining walls not associated with the cut and fill from a dwelling or garage must
      have a height above or below existing ground level not exceeding 0.6m if located
      less than 0.5m from a side or rear boundary, or 1m if located at least 1m from a
      boundary.
- For drainage requirements refer to Council's *Development Engineering Standards Guide*,
  with the exception of section 7. The Potts Hill Residential Precinct is designed to
  accommodate precinct wide on-site stormwater detention, located at various locations
  across the precinct. Accordingly, individual lot on-site detention is not required.

### Fences

- A front fence is to be provided on all detached dwelling lots.
- Fences in the front setback area from a primary road are to be maximum 1.2m high and a
  minimum 50% open construction.
- Other fences to be maximum 1.8m high.
- The maximum height permitted for the fence is to be calculated from ground level at
  that point.
- Front side fences to extend 2m back from the front building line, and should match the
  front fence height and design.
- On corner lots, the front fencing is to continue around the corner to the secondary
  street frontage for a minimum 30% of the lot length.
- Metal fencing not permitted forward of the front building line.

### Eaves

- Eaves are required on all external walls.
- Eaves to be at least 450mm from the fascia.

### Passive surveillance

- A living area must be located so as to look over the primary road to provide passive
  surveillance.
- The main pedestrian entry of the dwelling is to be visible from the street.

### Subdivision

- Subdivision of detached dwelling lots not permitted.
