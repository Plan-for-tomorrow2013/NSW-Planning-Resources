## Development controls

### Brunker Road apartment site setbacks

Minimum 3m native landscape setback along the rear of the lot adjacent to the bush
regeneration zone.

> See diagram in source PDF (site plan showing Potts Hill Reservoir, new internal road,
> bush regeneration zone, and 3m native landscape zone in setback) — not converted,
> logged in [`../appendices/_index.md`](../appendices/_index.md).

### North east apartment site setbacks

- Minimum 3m native landscape setback along the northern boundary of the lot adjacent to
  the bush regeneration zone.
- A tree preservation setback to the new shared road (No. 5) to the west to protect and
  maintain the street frontage trees.

> See diagram in source PDF (site plan showing new shared road No. 5, new internal road
> No. 1, tree preservation setback, and a significant tree marked for preservation if
> possible) — not converted, logged in [`../appendices/_index.md`](../appendices/_index.md).
