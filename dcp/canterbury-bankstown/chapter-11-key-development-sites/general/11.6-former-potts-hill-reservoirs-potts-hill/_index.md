# 11.6 — Former Potts Hill Reservoirs Site, Potts Hill

Site-specific precinct DCP for the former Potts Hill Reservoirs site (Western Residential
Precinct), Potts Hill, covering dwelling houses, dual occupancies and attached dwellings,
residential flat buildings, and community housing within specific areas shown on the
chapter's own Land Application Map (Figure 1). Carries the chapter-level precedence clause
("the development controls of Chapter 11.6 of this DCP will prevail if there is an
inconsistency with any other development controls in this DCP"), stated once in Section 1
and applying to the whole sub-chapter. No former-LGA content — kept under `general/`.

**Structural anomaly**: unlike every other Chapter 11 sub-chapter converted so far
(11.1–11.5, 11.7, 11.8), Sections 2–5 of 11.6 are **not** written as sequentially numbered
development-control clauses (no "1.1, 1.2, 1.3…" style). Instead each is a series of
sub-headed bullet/standard blocks (e.g. "Site coverage", "Maximum floor area", "Storey
limit"...) with no clause numbers at all. Only Section 1 (Introduction) uses the
Objectives O1–O4 format seen elsewhere. This has been preserved exactly as printed —
nothing has been renumbered or restructured to match the other sub-chapters' style — but
it means clause-level cross-referencing (e.g. "11.6 clause 1.4") is not possible for
Sections 2–5; cite by section and sub-heading instead (e.g. "11.6 Section 3, Side
setbacks").

Given 5 internal sections, kept as its **own folder** per the conversion guide's 2+
section rule — the largest Chapter 11 sub-chapter by section count after 11.1 (17
sections) and 11.4 (8 sections).

## Notes / anomalies

- **Amendment date**: cover page reads "June 2023 (Amended August 2024)" with no
  amendment table supplied to confirm which specific clauses changed on that date, and no
  day-level date given for either the original or amended date. Per the conversion guide,
  `effective_date` and `amended_date` are omitted from front matter rather than guessed.
- **Zones**: no LEP zone codes (e.g. R2, R3, E4) are stated anywhere in the supplied
  pages — the site is instead defined entirely by the Land Application Map (Figure 1),
  which splits it into "Section 1 – Detached houses", "Section 2 – Attached houses",
  "Section 3 – Apartments", "Section 4 – Community housing" and "Open Space" areas. Note
  this internal map legend numbering (Section 1–4) is **different from and not aligned
  with** the document's own printed Section 1–5 structure used for the actual heading
  text — e.g. the map's "Section 3 – Apartments" area corresponds to the document's
  "Section 4 – Residential flat buildings", not the document's own Section 3. This is
  worth flagging clearly so a reader doesn't conflate the two numbering schemes. `zones`
  omitted from front matter as a result.
- **Figure 1 (Land Application Map)** is the sole source of which specific lots fall into
  which of the four development-type areas (detached houses / attached houses /
  apartments / community housing) — not converted, logged in `appendices/_index.md`.
- **Section 4 (Residential flat buildings)** contains three further unconverted figures
  (two site-setback diagrams for the Brunker Road and North East apartment sites, and one
  aerial photograph for tree preservation) — logged in `appendices/_index.md`.
- **Section 2 rear-setback control** is unusually complex, varying by lot type (detached
  front-access / shallow detached front-access / western-boundary lots / rear-access
  lots) and by rear wall height threshold (4.5m) — preserved verbatim as printed rather
  than tabulated for brevity, since the source itself presents it as nested bullet text.
- **External document references** (not converted, cited by name only, consistent with
  the conversion guide's treatment of external references): Council's *Development
  Engineering Standards Guide* (Section 2, 3, 5 — drainage), Council's *Australian White
  Ibis Management Plan* (June 2018) (Section 2 — landscaping), and the *State
  Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021* (Section 5 — community housing floor-area
  controls for secondary dwellings).
- Recommend confirming with Council whether the map-legend "Section" numbers and the
  document's own "Section" numbers were intended to align — this looks like a drafting
  inconsistency worth a support-desk query rather than something to silently reconcile
  here.
