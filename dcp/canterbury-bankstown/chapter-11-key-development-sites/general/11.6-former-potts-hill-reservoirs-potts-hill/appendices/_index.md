## Note on map-legend numbering

The Land Application Map's own internal legend uses "Section 1" through "Section 4" to
label development-type areas (Detached houses / Attached houses / Apartments / Community
housing). This numbering **does not correspond** to the document's own Section 1–5
heading structure (Introduction / Dwelling houses / Dual occupancies and attached
dwellings / Residential flat buildings / Community housing) — e.g. the map's "Section 3 –
Apartments" corresponds to the document's Section 4 ("Residential flat buildings"), not
the document's own Section 3. Readers should not conflate the two. See the sub-chapter
`_index.md` for further detail.

## Recommended follow-up

- Convert Figure 1 (Land Application Map) as an image asset before treating lot-level
  development-type determinations as reliable from text alone.
- Convert the two Section 4 site-setback diagrams and the tree-preservation aerial photo
  before relying on this sub-chapter for site-specific advice regarding the apartment
  sites' setbacks or tree retention.
