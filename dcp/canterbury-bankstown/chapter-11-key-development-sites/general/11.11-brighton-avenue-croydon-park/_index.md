   not waste). This 11.11 citation is independent and explicitly labels Chapter 3.3 as
   Waste Management, which is a plausible, different claim from the earlier one. Needs
   checking against the actual Chapter 3.3 content — don't assume it either confirms or

   the section file rather than silently corrected to "encouraged."
