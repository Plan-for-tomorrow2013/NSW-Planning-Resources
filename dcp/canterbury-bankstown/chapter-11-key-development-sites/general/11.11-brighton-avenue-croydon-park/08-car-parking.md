# Section 8 — Car Parking

## Objectives

**O1** To locate and design car parking which is adapted to the site topography and building
design and which does not increase the bulk and scale of the building.

**O2** To ensure car parking does not impact upon deep soil landscaping controls.

## Development controls

**8.1** Driveways are not to dominate the street setback area.

**8.2** Car park entry is to be located behind the building line and integrated within the
building. Any driveway between the front boundary and the building is to have a
maximum 1:20 gradient.

**8.3** Basement car park areas are to be predominantly under building footprints, where
possible.
