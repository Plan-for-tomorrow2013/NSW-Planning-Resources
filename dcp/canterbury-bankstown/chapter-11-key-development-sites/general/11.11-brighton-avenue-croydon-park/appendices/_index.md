- **Figure F.1** is a site plan showing building footprints, storey counts (4, 5, 6 storeys
  visible), an articulation zone, a street setback articulation zone, and open space

  articulation zone.

not tabular data), consistent with the conversion guide's instruction to log rather than
convert image-only figures.
