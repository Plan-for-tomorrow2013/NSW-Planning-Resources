# Section 1 — Introduction

> **Sub-chapter:** 11.15 — 75A, 75B & 75C Marco Avenue, Revesby ("Key Development Site")
> **Cover date:** June 2023 (Amended August 2025). See this sub-chapter's [`_index.md`](_index.md) for important flags about this sub-chapter's own chapter numbering and dating before relying on it.

*Note: This section is explanatory text only — no O#/C# clause numbering applies.*

## 1.1 Relationship to other plans and sections of this Development Control Plan

The controls in this section apply to the land at 75A, 75B and 75C Marco Avenue, Revesby.

Unless otherwise stated, all development on this land must comply with the Objectives, Principles and Controls in this section.

In the event of an inconsistency between this section and the other provisions of the CB DCP 2023, the provisions in this section shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
