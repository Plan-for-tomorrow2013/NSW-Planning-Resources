# 4.6 Landscaping

## Objectives

O1. Prioritise tree retention to ensure significant trees are conserved as much as possible.

O2. Contribute to the overall minimum 30% tree canopy target, and ensure sufficient deep
    soil is provided to support healthy growth of mature trees.

## Controls

C1. Landscape design and minimum landscaped areas are to be provided in accordance with the
    relevant provisions of the Canterbury Bankstown DCP 2023.

C2. Deep soil targets are to be provided in accordance with the Apartment Design Guide (ADG).

C3. Where site conditions allow, deep soil areas should be consolidated to one location on
    site for effective planting, derived from local parent geology, and services and
    connected to the local groundwater to assist with detention of stormwater (where
    required).
