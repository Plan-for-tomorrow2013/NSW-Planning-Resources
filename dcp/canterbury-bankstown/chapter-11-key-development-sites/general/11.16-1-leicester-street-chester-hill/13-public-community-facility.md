# 4.1 Public Community Facility

> **Note:** this section has no Objectives — only Principles and Controls, the same
> departure from the 1.2.1 hierarchy already seen in 1.3 (Vision and Character).

## Principles

P1. The public community facility is to be integrated into the design of the development and
    located on the southern side of the publicly accessible open space.

P2. The community facility should be visible from Charles Place to help wayfinding from
    Waldron Road.

P3. The design should allow passive surveillance along Frost Lane and the public open space
    on the site.

## Controls

C1. The community facility is to be dedicated to Council prior to occupation of the site and
    delivered as a 'warm shell' fitout limited to essential services (gas, water, electricity,
    mechanical) and concrete floors/external facade/concrete or exposed ceilings to facilitate
    a 3rd party fitout by Council.

C2. The community facility is to have a minimum Gross Floor Area of 2,000m². Council will
    determine the use of the community facility as part of a separate process.

C3. The community facility must be accessible from Frost Lane and the central publicly
    accessible open space on the site.
