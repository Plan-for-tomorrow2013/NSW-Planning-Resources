# 3.3 Private Communal Space

> **Note:** as printed, this section lists "Principles" before "Objectives" — the only
> section in this sub-chapter (and, so far, in Chapter 11) to invert the usual
> Objectives→Principles→Controls order set out in 1.2.1. Preserved in the order printed.

## Principles

P1. Ensure communal courtyards and roof terraces maximise comfort, solar access, and
    landscaping.

P2. Ensure tree species selected for planting within Private Communal Spaces are resilient
    and drought tolerant.

## Objectives

O1. Provide high-quality communal recreation areas for residents that are complimentary to
    publicly accessible open space.

## Controls

C1. Private Communal Spaces provided within developments must be designed with a minimum
    width of 24m.

C2. Private Communal Spaces provided within the development is to be equal to 25% of the
    total site.

C3. Private Communal Spaces must achieve a minimum of 50% direct sunlight to the usable part
    of the Private Communal Space for a minimum of 2 hours between 9am and 3pm on 21 June.

C4. Trees must be provided within Private Communal Spaces to ensure 25% of the communal space
    is covered by mature tree canopy. Where it is not possible to deliver 25% tree canopy
    cover, it is required to demonstrate alternative solutions that will minimise urban heat
    island effects to the same extent as 25% mature tree canopy coverage (i.e. Green Roofs,
    Green walls, etc.).

C5. Drought tolerant planting is to be used for landscape planting in private communal open
