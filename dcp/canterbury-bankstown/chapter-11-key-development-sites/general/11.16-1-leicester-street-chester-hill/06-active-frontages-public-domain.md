# 2.1 Active Frontages and improving the Public Domain

## 2.1.1 Ground floor frontage

### Objectives

O1. Provide ground floor frontages that are accessible and integrates with the design of the
    public domain.

O2. Prioritise pedestrian activity and street-level vibrancy along Priam, Bent, Leicester
    Streets, and Frost Lane.

O3. Increase safety by ensuring adequate surveillance is provided throughout the site.

O4. Increase the number of safe travel routes and levels of passive surveillance throughout
    the Chester Square precinct.

O5. Consideration must be made to safety measures such as surveillance and lighting in the
    Public Domain and through site links.

### Principles

P1. Ensure the delivery of the Public Domain works on the Chester Square site, in accordance
    with the approved Public Domain Plan, including its staging and funding provisions.

P2. Encourage seamless, walkable connections to Waldron Road and the Chester Hill Station
    area.

P3. Facades must have a high level of expressed detail and tactile material quality.

P4. The base of facades must meet the public domain in a well-considered manner that takes
    account of any slope.

P5. All entries and exits are to be legible from the public domain.

P6. Ground level design must incorporate transparent facades and frequent entries to enhance
    street activation.

P7. Ground-floor residences must incorporate privacy measures (e.g. raised sills, landscaping)
    but still present an engaging interface with the street.

### Controls

C1. Deliver the Public Domain in accordance with the staging as outlined in the approved
    Public Domain Plan.

C2. A Maintenance Plan is to be provided to Council detailing how the publicly accessible
    open space on the site will be maintained by the land owner through a regular schedule of
    cleaning and maintenance work.

C3. Active frontages must be provided in accordance with Figure 8.

C4. Active uses must fully occupy the ground floor frontages within the active frontages
    identified in Figure 8 and must not be taken up by services.

C5. Active Frontages must be considered in detail and the following must be incorporated
    within their design:

    (a) A nominal 500mm interface zone at the frontage must be set aside to create interest
        and variety in the streetscape, to be used for entries, opening of windows, seating
        ledges, benches, and general articulation.

C6. Fire escapes and service doors must be seamlessly incorporated within the visual design
    of the facade with the use of high-quality materials.

C7. Residential frontage uses are permissible at ground level in accordance with Figure 8.

C8. All car parking and service entries are to be located on secondary frontages or laneways
    to minimise curb cuts along the Leicester Street active frontages.

C9. Large-format retail uses should be sleeved by smaller retailers or commercial tenancies
    at street level; or placed below the street level (where topography permits).
