# 1.3 Vision and Character

> **Note:** this section has no numbered Objectives and no Controls — only a prose "Locality
> Statement" followed by Principles P1–P6. This departs from the Objectives→Principles→
> Controls hierarchy set out in 1.2.1.

## Locality Statement

The Chester Square redevelopment envisions a dynamic, pedestrian-friendly precinct that
integrates high-quality urban design with a strong sense of community. At its heart, the
development will feature a vibrant, publicly accessible central open space of approximately
2,800m², providing a welcoming gathering place for residents, visitors, and workers alike.
This civic plaza will serve as a focal point for social interaction, events, and recreation,
surrounded by lush landscaping, shaded seating areas, and high-quality public domain
improvements.

The precinct will also incorporate a modern, multi-purpose community facility spanning
2,000m², ensuring access to essential services and fostering social cohesion. Designed to be
an inclusive and active hub, this facility will accommodate a variety of uses, catering to
the needs of the diverse local population. Enhanced pedestrian pathways and improved
connectivity will define the public domain, with activated laneways and widened Frost Lane
creating seamless links between surrounding streets and transport nodes. Streetscape
enhancements along Charles Place, Waldron Road and other key frontages will reinforce the
walkability and accessibility of the precinct, inviting visitors to explore its dynamic mix
of offerings.

At the core of the development will be a diverse range of mixed-use retail spaces,
contributing to a lively and engaging street-level experience. A curated selection of
specialty shops, cafés, restaurants, and essential services will transform Chester Square
into a vibrant, publicly accessible central hub, activating the precinct during both day and
night.

Through carefully considered design and a commitment to public benefit, the Chester Square
redevelopment will redefine Chester Hill as a thriving, people-centric destination, where
community life, convenience, and commerce converge in a vibrant and sustainable urban
setting.

## Principles

P1. Create a vibrant and well-designed mixed-use precinct at Chester Square, featuring lively
    streets that are pedestrian-friendly and filled with detail. The development is to
    showcase a distinctive skyline with slender towers set back from the streets, enhancing
    the area's character and sense of scale.

P2. Ensure that the public domain is of high quality and amenity.

P3. Contribute to a thriving centre at street level, with well-designed active street
    frontages.

P4. Prioritise pedestrian movements at a precinct level to enhance pedestrian safety and
    enjoyment of the development and broader Chester Hill.

P5. Reinforce the distinctive attributes and qualities of Chester Hill within the precinct.

P6. Promote urban and architectural design quality through planning interventions that
    foster design excellence.
