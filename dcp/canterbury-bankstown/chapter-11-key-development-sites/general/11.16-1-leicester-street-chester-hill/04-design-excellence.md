# 1.4 Design Excellence

## Objectives

O1. Ensure that large, prominent and complex developments are of a high quality and have
    varied design.

O2. Ensure that development individually and collectively contributes to the unique
    characteristics of the precinct and the overall urban design quality of the LGA.

## Principles

P1. Ensure the palette of materials chosen for high impact areas, such as parks and plazas,
    correlates to high quality façade design.

P2. Demonstrate how development proposed along Frost Lane provides design excellence in
    context of the Public Domain, particularly along Charles Place.

## Controls

C1. The following documents are required to be provided by suitably qualified consultants to
    demonstrate Design Excellence:

    (a) Site and context analysis;
    (b) Public domain layout, including levels, uses, access and circulation, dedications and
        hierarchy of spaces;
    (c) Built form massing and dimensioned envelopes;
    (d) Overshadowing analysis;
    (e) Stormwater management strategy;
    (f) Traffic management and servicing strategy, parking numbers and location;
    (g) Ecologically sustainable development strategies and benchmark commitments (including
        connection to green infrastructure).
