# 3.1 Building Envelope and Height

## Objectives

O1. Support the maximum building heights on the site (up to 60m in select tower locations),
    while ensuring street-level amenity and transitions to adjacent buildings.

## Principles

P1. Ensure the scale and massing of the Chester Square development integrates harmoniously
    with surrounding streets, open spaces, and lower-scale neighbourhoods.

P2. The tallest tower(s) may be up to 60m (approximately 18 storeys), subject to detailed
    design excellence assessment.

P3. Secondary towers may range between 35m and 55m in height to create a modulated skyline
    and minimise overshadowing on primary public spaces, such as the planned public square
    and new communal open spaces.

## Controls

C1. A minimum 2-storey (approx. 6.5m) and maximum 3-storey (approx. 10m) street wall/podium
    height applies along primary streets (e.g. Priam, Bent, Leicester, and Frost Lane).

C2. Towers above 8 storeys must be set back from the street wall by at least 3m. Along Frost
    Lane, where a narrower corridor exists, towers must be set back a minimum of 12m from the
    centreline of the lane to ensure pedestrian amenity and to mitigate wind impacts.

C3. A maximum tower footprint of 700m² GFA applies for residential portions of buildings
    above 8 storeys, to maintain slender tower proportions and allow for cross-ventilation.

C4. Linear north–south oriented residential buildings are limited to 8 storeys (approx. 29m)
    to preserve access to sunlight and views for surrounding areas.

C5. Buildings above 8 storeys must not have elevations that exceed 45m horizontally, with a
    recess of at least 3m deep for longer elevations, ensuring visual articulation and
    improved cross-ventilation.

C6. East-west oriented buildings are limited to 18m depth to maximise natural light and
    ventilation.

C7. Along Leicester Street, a 5m minimum setback is required up to 2 storeys to preserve
    existing trees. Above this, the upper storeys must be set back at least 8m from the
    property boundary.

C8. On Priam and Bent Streets, a 3m setback applies up to 3 storeys. Above this, the upper
    storeys require a 6m setback from the property boundary to maintain a comfortable public
    realm and footpath environment.

C9. Along Frost Lane, a 3m setback is required up to 3 storeys. Above this, the upper storeys
    require a 12m setback from the lane's centreline.
