# 4.4 Waste Management

> ("Construction" then "Operation"), rather than running a single sequential O# count for
> the whole section — the same kind of nested-numbering-restart pattern already flagged for

## Objectives

### Construction

O1. Reduce the amount of construction and demolition waste generated and minimise resource
    recovery through waste avoidance, source separation and recycling.

O2. Ensure waste from within developments can be collected and disposed of in a manner that
    is healthy, efficient, minimises disruption to amenity, and is conducive to the overall
    minimisation of waste generated.

O3. To ensure development incorporates well-designed and adaptable bin storage and
    collection facilities that are convenient, accessible to occupants, and avoid adverse
    visual impacts on the streetscape.

O4. To maximise residential amenity and minimise adverse environmental and health related
    impacts associated with waste management such as odour and noise from bin storage and
    collection areas.

O5. To ensure bin storage and collection areas are designed to integrate with and meet the
    requirements for Council's domestic waste services.

### Operation

O1. To maximise resource recovery and encourage source separation of waste, reuse and
    recycling by ensuring development provides adequate and appropriate bin storage and
    collection areas.

O2. To ensure that the disposal of waste is managed appropriately and efficiently.

O3. To ensure well-designed and responsive bin storage and collection facilities that are
    convenient and accessible to occupants.

## Controls

C1. Provisions relating to waste management in the Canterbury Bankstown DCP 2023 apply and
    must be designed in accordance with the following Council Waste Design for New
    Developments:

    (a) Guide B, Residential Flat Buildings.
    (b) Guide E, Mixed Use Development.
    (c) Guide F, Commercial and industrial development.

    *The Guides can be downloaded at Council's website:*
    https://www.cbcity.nsw.gov.au/planning-and-building/planning-controls-and-policies/canterbury-bankstown-development-control-plan
