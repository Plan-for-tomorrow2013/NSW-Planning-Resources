# 1.2 Application of this DCP

This DCP applies to land covered by the LEP, as defined in Figure 3.

This DCP outlines the framework for future development on the subject site, detailing the
objectives and controls necessary to achieve the desired outcomes. Any development
application must demonstrate how it meets these objectives and controls.

The controls provide clear and measurable standards for implementing the objectives. If any
inconsistencies arise between this DCP and the LEP, the LEP prevails. Additionally, the
provisions in this DCP chapter work in conjunction with other relevant DCP chapters. In case
of any conflict between the controls in this DCP and those in another, the provisions of this
DCP will prevail.

## 1.2.1 How to read this DCP

This DCP Chapter is structured around a hierarchy of criteria encompassing objectives,
principles and controls. The intent is to provide clear, direct and informative development
controls and design guidance for all new development in Chester Square.

When addressing the criteria, it is important to consider the purpose of each step, as
outlined on this page. Collectively these elements create a comprehensive framework that will
enable Chester Square opportunities that align with the vision.

**Objectives** — These are the 'why' we have the principles and controls in place.

**Principles** — These are the 'what' we want statements. They provide clear direction as to
what should be done, without saying how to do it. How these are achieved comes down to
individual site circumstances, design and merit.

**Controls** — These tell the applicant 'what' we want, and 'how' to do it. They are
definitive, and easily assessable, and provide clear guidance on exactly what is expected.
