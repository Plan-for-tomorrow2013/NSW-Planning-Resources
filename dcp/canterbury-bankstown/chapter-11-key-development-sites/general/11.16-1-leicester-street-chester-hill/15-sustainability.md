# 4.3 Sustainability

## Objectives

O1. Establish benchmarks to achieve ecologically sustainable development.

O2. Ensure the efficient use of resources in building design, construction, and operation
    and reduce overall environmental impact.

O3. Ensure that development is resilient against the effects of climate change.

O4. Reduce urban heat and increase canopy coverage and ground absorption of water.

O5. Minimise discharge of stormwater from the site.

O6. Reduce water demand over time and protect the downstream environment.

O7. Minimise the impact of new development on the existing stormwater system.

## Principles

P1. Development is to consider the use of hard-wearing materials that can provide longevity
    and withstand climatic extremes, including wind, rain and temperature extremes through:

    (a) Minimising applied finishes where possible.
    (b) Specifying durable materials, to ensure longevity and withstand the extremes of
        weather and climate.
    (c) Specify materials and finishes which weather or age favourably over time, rather than
        deteriorate or oxidise.
    (d) All new development should consider the provision of roof overhangs (eaves) and
        incorporating additional shading to balconies/verandas, where possible.

P2. Green roofs are encouraged, and are to be planted using locally native plants including
    a variety of plant sizes and layers of planting.

P3. All new development must consider strategies to incorporate renewable energy design
    features, such as solar panels.

P4. All new development must consider strategies to remove the requirement for gas
    appliances for cooking in new buildings.

## Controls

C1. Stormwater from roofs, streets, pavements and lawns are to be captured and re-used,
    wherever possible.

C2. All new development should aim to achieve a 5-star rating under the Green Star, or other
    alternative sustainability and/or net zero benchmarks.

C3. NABERS for Apartment ratings are to be managed by strata or building management for all
    eligible buildings in the redevelopment.

C4. Solid fuel heating and cooking systems are not permitted in any development.

C5. Gas cooktops, gas ovens or gas internal space heating systems are not permitted in any
    residential development. Instead, electric systems must be installed and clearly marked
    on development application plans.

C6. All new non-residential development including, business, office and retail premises,
    tourist and visitor accommodation, health services facilities and educational
    establishments are to use only electricity for all energy requirements associated with
    normal operations.
