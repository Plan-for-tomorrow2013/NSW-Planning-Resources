# 2.2 Public Art

## Objectives

O1. To integrate public art with the Public Domain that enhances the user experience of
    publicly accessible spaces and creates visual interest.

## Principles

P1. To promote the creation of opportunities for the creative industry through public art
    improvements of the built environment.

P2. To provide public art that embraces the diverse history and contemporary community of
    Chester Hill and also taking a "Connecting with Country" approach by celebrating and
    exploring the local indigenous history and culture of the subject site.

## Controls

C1. Artistic elements encompass creative features in areas such as paving, window treatments,
    canopy treatments, signage, balustrading, and wayfinding.

C2. Public art is encouraged within civic spaces, along blank walls or as screening elements
    to provide visual interest within the public realm.

C3. Prepare and submit a Public Art Strategy with the first major development application on
    the site to define key locations across the subject site that will feature public art and
    the type of public art provided.
