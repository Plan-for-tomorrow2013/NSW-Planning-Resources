# 1.1 Explanation

The chapter of this DCP applies to development on the Chester Square site (1 Leicester
Street, Chester Hill).

This chapter provides additional detailed provisions to the Canterbury Bankstown Local
Environmental Plan (CBLEP) 2023 for development within the subject site that will:

1. Recognise and reinforce the distinctive character of Chester Square through guiding
   development that positively contributes to the centre and its surrounding neighbourhood.
2. Enhance the public domain through improvements that facilitate the delivery of lively
   public spaces.
