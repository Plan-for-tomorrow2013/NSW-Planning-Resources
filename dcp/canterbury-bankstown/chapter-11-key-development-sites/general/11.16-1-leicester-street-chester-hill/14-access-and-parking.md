# 4.2 Access and Parking

> **Note:** the Section 4 divider page's own mini-Contents labels this section "4.2 Access"
> (omitting "and Parking"), while the master Contents page and this section's own heading
> both read "Access and Parking" — a minor title inconsistency, flagged rather than resolved.

## Objectives

O1. Create a pedestrian prioritised precinct.

O2. Promote active frontages linking into and supporting Waldron Road.

O3. Minimise impacts of vehicular access and servicing on access across the subject site.

## Principles

P1. A maximum of two vehicle access points are provided from Leicester Street, one on Priam
    Street, one on Bent Street for residential and non-residential vehicles. A maximum of two
    vehicle access points is permitted on Frost Lane and must be for residential vehicles
    only.

## Controls

C1. Provide an appropriate level of on-site parking to cater for a mix of development and
    vehicle types, including cars, motorcycles and bicycles.

C2. A Green Travel Plan must be produced for the site that outlines the travel demand
    management strategy including measures, targets and a monitoring strategy by the land
    owner and tenants. Managing travel demand relating to the retail uses proposed should be
    a key objective in this Green Travel Plan.

C3. Produce a Delivery and Servicing Plan for the site that considers how loading and
    servicing demand can be managed at peak times along with providing wider community
    benefit through 'last mile' deliveries.

C4. Prior to the lodgement of the first development application for construction of the
    mixed use development, the applicant must engage with TfNSW to discuss refinement of the
    microsimulation modelling, consider the wider impacts of the development on the Chester
    Hill road network beyond the current study area which could alleviate congestion issues
    whilst aligning with the future vision for the suburb.

## 4.2.1 Pedestrian Access

### Controls

C1. A through-site link and pedestrian right of way at least 6m wide must be provided between
    Frost Lane and Leicester Street. The route should be universally accessible and
    accessible to the public 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

C2. A through-site link and pedestrian entry into the shopping centre must be provided
    between Bent Street and Priam Street approximately mid-block between Leicester Street
    and Frost Lane. The route should be accessible from at least 7am to 9pm daily, with the
    exception of instances where the shopping centre is closed.

## 4.2.2 Cycling

### Controls

C1. Bicycle parking and end of trip facilities are to be provided for all development on the
    site in accordance with the provisions in Section 5.2.3 Bicycle parking and end of trip
    facilities in [Chapter 6.2 Bankstown City Centre Development Control Plan](../../../../chapter-6-strategic-centres/general/6.2-bankstown-city-centre/_index.md).

C2. Bicycle parking and cycling facilities must be clearly signposted and located in an area
    that is convenient to access from within the building and from the street.
