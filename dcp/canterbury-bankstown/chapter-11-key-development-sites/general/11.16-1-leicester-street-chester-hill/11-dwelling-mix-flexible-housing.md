# 3.2 Dwelling Mix and Flexible Housing

## Objectives

O1. Provide a varied range of dwelling sizes and types (including adaptable dwellings) to
    cater for different ages, incomes, household structures, and physical abilities.

O2. Promote the design of buildings that are adaptable and incorporate flexible apartments to
    suit the changing lifecycle housing needs of residents over time.

## Principles

P1. Where practical, family-friendly ground-floor units with private courtyards should be
    concentrated near communal open space or public parks.

P2. The design of two and three bedroom apartments must include shared family spaces and
    integrated storage suitable for families such as large toys, prams and other similar
    items.

P3. Communal open space should cater for those of all ages, including play spaces for
    families, and entertaining spaces for adults and families.

## Controls

C1. Development must demonstrate a mix of one, two, and three bedroom dwellings (including
    adaptable dwellings), with a proportion of adaptable or universally designed layouts to
    suit accessibility and support ageing in place.

C2. The following dwelling mix is required for the site:

    (a) Studio: between 5–10%
    (b) 1 bedroom: between 10–30%
    (c) 2 bedroom: between 40–75%
    (d) 3 or more bedroom: minimum 10%

C3. The dwelling mix requirements may be altered only if the development serves the purpose
    of public housing or if the applicant represents a community housing or nonprofit
    organisation.
