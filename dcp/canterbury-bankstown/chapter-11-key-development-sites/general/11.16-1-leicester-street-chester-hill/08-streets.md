## 2.3.1 Frost Lane and Charles Place

### Objectives

O1. Design the lane, shared zones and service lanes to encourage pedestrian and cyclist
    amenity and safety.

O2. Encourage active uses such as outdoor dining and retail activity along the lane without
    compromising safe pedestrian access and use.

O3. Integrate tree planting and Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD) along Frost Lane that
    will thrive in the reduced sunlight areas along this laneway.

### Principles

P1. The design of Frost Lane, Charles Place, Priam Street and Bent Street is to be consistent
    with the intent and design of the Public Domain Plan prepared by Aspect for this site and
    surrounding public domain.

> See Figure 13 in the source PDF — street activation example, Kiona Land, Double Bay
> (illustrative photograph, not converted).

### Section A

#### Controls

C1. The design of Frost Lane is to be consistent with Figure 14 and the Public Domain Plan
    prepared by Aspect Studios.

C2. Frost Lane must be widened to an overall corridor of approximately 14m, accommodating
    short-stay parking and loading zones, outdoor dining, and well-defined pedestrian zones.

C3. Frost Lane must:

    (a) Be fully open to the sky,
    (b) Provide direct throughways with direct sightlines,
    (c) Be clear of all obstructions, including stairs and escalators.

## 2.3.2 Priam, Bent and Leicester Streets

### Section B

#### Objectives

O1. Provide enhanced footpath widths for safe, comfortable pedestrian circulation.

#### Principles

P1. Integrate two-way cycle paths or protected lanes where feasible.

> See Figure 15 in the source PDF — Plan and Section of Bent and Priam Street — Commercial
> Interface (not converted).

### Section C

#### Controls

C1. The design of Priam, Bent and Leicester Streets is to be consistent with Figures 15, 16
    and 17 and the Public Domain Plan prepared by Aspect Studios. Bent and Leicester Streets
    are to be designed in accordance with Figures 15, 16 and 17 and are to cater for
    pedestrians and cyclists where specified.

C2. Where new trees are proposed along Priam, Bent and Leicester Street, trees must be
    incorporated on the development's side, with additional infill trees allowed along the
    non-development side of street, to use Council approved species that achieve a height at
    maturity of at least 10 metres and canopy spread of at least 6 metres.

C3. Water Sensitive Urban Design (WSUD) features must be integrated along Priam, Bent and
    Leicester Street, to Council's specifications and approval, including but not limited to
    a mix of:

    (a) rain gardens,
    (b) passively irrigated verges to all streets,
    (c) located trees within planted zones.
