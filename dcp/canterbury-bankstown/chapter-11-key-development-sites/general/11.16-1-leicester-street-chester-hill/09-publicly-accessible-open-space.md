# 2.4 Publicly Accessible Open Space

## Objectives

O1. Create a vibrant public space, activated by ground floor retail and communal uses that
    are open and welcoming to the whole community.

O2. Maintain or maximise solar access to Publicly Accessible Open Space(s) during the day
    when they are most used throughout the year.

O3. Ensure the successful growth and survival of trees and vegetation planted within Publicly
    Accessible Open Space(s), particularly selecting tree and landscape species that will
    thrive in shaded areas such as along Frost Lane.

## Principles

P1. Celebrate the multi-cultural population of Chester Hill through the provision of a
    diverse and socially inclusive public open space.

P2. Integrate public domain elements seamlessly with the public domain streetscape on
    Leicester Street and Frost Lane, with level transitions through the open space designed
    to create accessible and welcoming spaces.

P3. Integrate substantial tree planting and understorey planting areas, including lawn, to
    create a comfortable climate all year round.

## Controls

C1. A Publicly Accessible Open Space, with a minimum dimension width of 40m and a minimum
    area of 2,000m², must be provided within the subject site of this DCP.

    (a) The total publicly accessible area is to be at 2,800m² inclusive of footpaths, stairs,
        lifts and other circulation areas.

C2. A minimum of 70% of the Publicly Accessible Open Space must achieve 3 hours of direct
    sunlight between 10am and 3pm on 21 June.

C3. A minimum of 70% of the Publicly Accessible Open Space must achieve wind conditions
    suitable for sitting and outdoor dining, demonstrated through wind modelling with points
    taken in multiple locations.

C4. The Publicly Accessible Open Space must include a through-site link from Frost Lane to
    the open space for pedestrians that is:

    (a) A minimum 6m width,
    (b) Open to sky,
    (c) Aligned with breaks in buildings,
    (d) Visible from Waldron Road and seamlessly integrates with the upgraded public domain
        design of Charles Place.

C5. Through site access is required 24 hours a day and must include DDA compliant ramps /
    lifts and must not rely on lifts, escalators or other mechanical modes of transport.

C6. Tree Planting must be incorporated within the Publicly Accessible Open Space to provide a
    minimum of 30% tree canopy coverage across the open space. Tree planting should:

    (a) Where 30% tree canopy coverage is not achieved, alternative approaches such as green
        roofs are to be demonstrated;
    (b) use predominantly local native species in the design of the planting, with a focus on
        supporting local 'biodiversity' in accordance with the Government Architect NSW
        'Biodiversity in Place Framework'.

C7. The Publicly Accessible Open Space is to be designed with deep soil zones at interfaces
    to Leicester Street and Frost Lane to allow planting of trees in deep soil. Any planting
    of trees in a structure over deep soil are to be provided with Apartment Design Guide
    (ADG) compliant soil volumes in a combination of raised or mounded soil volumes.

C8. No balconies to be located above ground level deep soil zones.

C9. A Plan of Management is required between Council and the Applicant in relation to matters
    such as but not limited to maintenance scheduling, access, and notice of repairs.
