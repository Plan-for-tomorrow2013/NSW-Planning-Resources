# Section 10 — Rooftop Solar Features and Lighting

> Source: Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 11.10 — Chullora Marketplace,
> Greenacre (June 2023, Amended April 2025), page 25. This is the final section of the
> sub-chapter.

## Objectives

**O1** To encourage and maximise the use of renewable energy to lessen the carbon
footprint of the proposed development.

**O2** To avoid adverse implications of light spill/glare to the surrounding
residential areas.

## Development controls

**10.1** The development will include the installation of a solar photovoltaic (PV)
system to supply energy to the common areas of buildings, communal open space
including outdoor lighting and any other parts of the development.

**10.2** The development application must provide a roof plan showing the area(s)
allocated to PVs, necessary access requirements for cleaning and maintenance, other
plant and equipment that may include solar water heater storage tanks, ventilators,
wind generators, air conditioning units and satellite dishes and antennae. Details
including connections to battery storage within individual apartments are to be
provided within DA plans.

**10.3** The solar panels must not adversely affect the architectural presentation of
the building or views from the streetscape.

**10.4** The use, location and placement of photovoltaic solar panels is to take into
account surrounding built forms and the likely permissible built form on adjacent
properties.

**10.5** The development is to minimise light spill into the adjacent residential
areas.
