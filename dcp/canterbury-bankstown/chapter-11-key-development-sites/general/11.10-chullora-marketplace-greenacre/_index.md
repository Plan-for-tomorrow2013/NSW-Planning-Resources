# 11.10 — Chullora Marketplace, Greenacre

Site-specific precinct DCP for the redevelopment of the Chullora Marketplace shopping
centre site at 353–355 Waterloo Road, Greenacre, into a mixed-use local centre (Zone E1
Local Centre) with a Central Civic Plaza, a new "High Street," terrace housing, shop top
housing, residential flat buildings, and mixed-use buildings, while retaining existing
commercial floor space. Ten internal sections covering the introduction/key design
principles, the Central Civic Plaza and public open space, access and movement, land
use and site layout, built form, architectural diversity, facade design/streetscape
activation, landscaping and public domain, heritage (former Liebentritt's Pottery /
Chullora Drive-in site), and rooftop solar/lighting. Carries the chapter-level
precedence clause. No former-LGA content — sits under `general/`.

Given 10 internal sections, kept as its **own folder** per the conversion guide's 2+
section rule — the second-largest Chapter 11 sub-chapter by section count after 11.1
(17 sections), ahead of 11.4 (8) and 11.6 (5).

## Notes / anomalies

- **Different amendment date from every other Chapter 11 sub-chapter converted so
  far**: 11.10's cover page reads "June 2023 (Amended April 2025)", whereas 11.1–11.9 all
  read "June 2023 (Amended August 2024)". No amendment table is supplied to explain what
  changed, and no day-level dates are given either way. Per the conversion guide,
  `effective_date`/`amended_date` are omitted from front matter rather than guessed —
  but this is the first sign of a Chapter 11 sub-chapter being amended on a different
  schedule from its siblings, echoing the pattern already flagged for 10.6/10.7 carrying
  a different amendment date from the rest of Chapter 10.
- **Self-referencing clause number**: clause 5.10 reads *"Despite **clause 5.10** which
  requires a minimum setback of 15m, Council may require a setback greater than 15m…"* —
  clause 5.10 refers to itself; the 15m setback is actually established by the
  immediately preceding **clause 5.9**, not 5.10. This is almost certainly a drafting
  error (clause 5.10 should read "Despite clause 5.9"), preserved verbatim rather than
  silently corrected. This is the same kind of self-referencing clause anomaly already
  flagged for Chapter 10.5 — see the top-level council index.
- **Cross-reference confirmed, not just cited**: clause 3.12 explicitly names "**Chapter
  3.2 Parking** of the Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023" — this
  confirms that Chapter 3.2 of this DCP is indeed titled "Parking," resolving the
  previously-flagged uncertainty around 11.8's and 11.9's un-annotated citations of
  "Chapter 3.2." See the top-level council index for the updated cross-chapter
  reconciliation note.
- **Staged/progressive development mechanism**: unique among Chapter 11 sub-chapters
  converted so far, 11.10 explicitly allows the site to be redeveloped "progressively and
  incrementally" while the existing shopping centre keeps operating, via an
  **indicative concept plan** (covering the whole site, lodged with the first DA) against
  which each stage's detailed plan must demonstrate consistency. Not paralleled in
  11.1–11.9.
- **Ecological/overshadowing conditions specific to Norfolk Reserve**: before lodging an
  indicative concept plan or first-stage DA, the applicant must obtain advice from a
  suitably qualified ecologist to (a) minimise overshadowing of Norfolk Reserve, (b) avoid
  overshadowing its ecologically endangered communities/threatened-species habitat, and
  (c) avoid other adverse environmental impacts on those communities/habitat — the same
  "adjacent ecologically sensitive reserve" theme recurring across 11.5 (Georges River
  foreshore), 11.6 (bush regeneration zone/Potts Hill Reservoir) and now 11.10 (Norfolk
  Reserve).
- **Heritage significance**: the site (355 Waterloo Road specifically) is listed in the
  LEP as an archaeological site — the former Liebentritt's Pottery, described in the LEP's
  own statement of significance as being of **state** heritage significance, plus the
  former Chullora Drive-in (one of Sydney's first drive-in theatres, opened December
  1956). Section 9 requires a heritage impact statement, NSW Heritage Office excavation
  permitting under the *Heritage Act 1977*, archaeological monitoring, an unexpected-finds
  protocol, salvage of brick/tile debris for reuse, and a Heritage Interpretation Plan —
  the most detailed heritage-specific development controls of any Chapter 11 sub-chapter
  converted so far (more detailed than the heritage references in 11.1 or 11.4).
- **Consultation with Transport for NSW**: Waterloo Road is classified as a regional
  road; clause 3.4 area and the standalone "Consultation with the Transport for NSW" note
  in Section 3 require a Transport Impact Assessment Report scoped in consultation with
  TfNSW before each stage's DA, covering access arrangements, network/intersection
  upgrades, road safety, pedestrian accessibility, public transport access, air
  quality/noise, and cumulative impact on Waterloo/Como/Norfolk Roads.
- **Four unconverted figures**: a site map (Figure 1), an indicative structure plan with
  a detailed colour-coded legend (Figure 2), two typical street-section diagrams (Figure
  3), and a Waterloo Road cycleway concept cross-section (Figure 4) — see
  `appendices/_index.md`.
- No former-LGA content; the sub-chapter instead references Zone E1 Local Centre (for
  retail/commercial primacy, clause (b) of the key design principles) and Zone R2 Low
  Density Residential / Zone SP2 Infrastructure (for setback controls in Section 5) —
  these are LEP zone references used to calibrate setbacks and land use, not a
  former-LGA split, consistent with the treatment of zone references elsewhere in this
  repo (e.g. 8.1's E3/SP4, 11.4's E1/R4).
