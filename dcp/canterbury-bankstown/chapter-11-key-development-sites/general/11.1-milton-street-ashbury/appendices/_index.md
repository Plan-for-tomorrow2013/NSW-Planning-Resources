## Stronger caveat: Figures 12.1.5 and 12.1.6

As with Chapter 11.2's Figure 1b (setback plan) and Chapter 11.4's setback tables, Figures
12.1.5 and 12.1.6 here are the **sole source** of the specific numeric setback and
separation distances for individual buildings within the precinct — the clause text (Sections
2, 4, 5, 6) states general minimums and cross-refers to "Figure 12.1.5" / "Figure 12.1.6"
without restating the per-building figures. A reader needing an exact setback or
separation distance for a specific building on this site must consult the source PDF's
Figures 12.1.5 and 12.1.6 directly; this repo's section text is not a substitute data source
for those numbers.
