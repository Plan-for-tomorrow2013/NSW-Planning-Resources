## Zoning label discrepancy: B2 vs E1

Figure 1a's map key labels the precinct's northern strip (along Lakemba Street) **"B2
Zone"**. Every other reference to that same area in this sub-chapter — the Section 2
character statement, and every development-control table in Sections 3, 4, 6 and 8 —
calls it the **"E1 zone (local centre)"**. This is the same style of zone-name mismatch
already flagged for Chapters 7.5 and 7.6 (confirmed there in both sub-chapters). It now
recurs in 11.4, suggesting this may be a shared drafting-template issue across the
council's newer precinct-style DCP chapters rather than an isolated error in either
document. Recommended for the same future cross-chapter reconciliation pass already
proposed at the top-level council index for the Chapter 7 instances.
