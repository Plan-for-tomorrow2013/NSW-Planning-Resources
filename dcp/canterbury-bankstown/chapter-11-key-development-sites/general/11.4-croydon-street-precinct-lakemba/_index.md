# 11.4 — Croydon Street Precinct, Lakemba

*June 2023 (Amended April 2025)*

A stand-alone, site-specific precinct DCP for the Croydon Street Precinct, Lakemba —
194–214 Lakemba Street, 5–19 Croydon Street and 53–55 Railway Parade, Lakemba, bounded
by Lakemba Street, Croydon Street, Railway Parade and Jubilee Park, immediately north of
Lakemba Train Station. Two internal zones: E1 (local centre, along Lakemba Street) and R4
(high density residential, the rest of the precinct) — an internal zone split, not a
former-LGA split, consistent with the treatment already used for 8.1's E3/SP4 split.

Like 11.1, 11.2 and 11.3, this sub-chapter carries its own precedence clause: the
development controls of Chapter 11.4 prevail over other DCP controls where there is an
inconsistency, for applications on this site (Section 1).

**No former-LGA split.** Sits under `general/`, consistent with the other Chapter 11
sub-chapters.

**8 internal sections** — gets its own folder with one file per section, per the conversion
guide's 2+ section rule.

## Anomalies and notes flagged during conversion

- **B2/E1 zone-name mismatch**: Figure 1a's map key labels the E1-zoned northern strip
  "B2 Zone" instead. This is the *same* mismatch pattern already flagged for Chapters 7.5
  and 7.6 — its recurrence here strengthens the case that it's a shared drafting-template
  issue rather than an isolated error. See `appendices/_index.md` for the full note.
- **Stray cross-reference**: the Figure 3b setback table cites "section F10.10 of this DCP"
  for the overland-flow-path ground-floor setback, a section number that doesn't exist
  anywhere in this repo's structure. The actual corresponding control is this same
  sub-chapter's clause 8.4. Same style of anomaly as Chapter 10.2/10.5's stray "Chapter
  3.3" references — worth including in the same future reconciliation pass.
- **Clause 3.1** reads "recommedations" (typo for "recommendations"), preserved verbatim.
- **Clause 8.10** switches between "overland flood path" and "overland flow path" within
  the same clause — preserved verbatim as inconsistent-but-synonymous usage.
- **Amendment date has no day** ("Amended April 2025" only, no amendment table) —
  `amended_date` omitted from all section front matter.
- **Precedence-clause pattern**: this is now the fourth Chapter 11 precedence-clause
  sub-chapter (after 11.1, 11.2, 11.3), continuing the pattern already flagged for
  Chapter 7's newer precinct DCPs and Chapter 8.3 Section 6 — recommended for the same
  future cross-chapter reconciliation pass.
- **Different amendment date to 11.1/11.3** (April 2025 vs August 2024 for 11.1/11.3) —
  each Chapter 11 sub-chapter appears to be amended independently; not itself an anomaly,
  but worth being aware of when citing "current as at" dates across sub-chapters.
