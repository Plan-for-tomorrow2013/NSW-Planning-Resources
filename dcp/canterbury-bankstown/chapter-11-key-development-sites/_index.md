Chapter 11 is different in kind from Chapters 1–10: rather than grouping controls by land
use or topic, each sub-chapter is a stand-alone, site-specific mini-DCP for a single named
development site somewhere in the LGA (an address, a precinct, or a former-use site such as
"Former Riverlands Golf Course Site"). This is structurally closer to the newer precinct
DCPs already seen in Chapter 7 (7.5 Canterbury Local Centre, 7.6 Belmore and Lakemba
Precinct, 7.7 Punchbowl and Wiley Park) and to Chapter 8.3 Section 6 (167 Hume Highway) than
to the topic-based chapters — each Chapter 11 sub-chapter carries its own precedence
statement to similar effect ("the development controls of Chapter 11.X will prevail if
there is an inconsistency with any other development controls in this DCP"), consistent
with the precedence wording used in those other precinct-DCP sub-chapters. All sub-chapters
converted so far carry this, though 11.16 expresses it as ordinary prose within its
"Application of this DCP" section rather than as a standalone numbered clause.

## Scope check

- **11.1** — no former-LGA content. Site-specific precinct DCP for the Milton Street
  Precinct, Ashbury (Lots A, B, C DP 30778), bounded by Milton Street, WH Wagener Oval,
  Trevenar Street and Yabsley Avenue, immediately adjacent to the Ashbury Heritage
  Conservation Area. 17 internal sections covering siting, height/density, setbacks
  (front, side/rear, upper level), building separation, view corridors, building depth,
  deep soil, communal open space, vehicular/pedestrian entries, a new road connecting
  Milton Street to the oval, basement parking, excavation, drainage, tree retention and
  waste management. Kept as its **own folder** (not a flat file) per the conversion
  guide's 2+ section rule — the first Chapter 11 sub-chapter large enough to need one.
  Carries the chapter-level precedence clause, plus a second, narrower precedence
  statement scoped specifically to its waste-management section.

  an explicit inconsistency/precedence clause (11.2 controls prevail over other DCP

  entire sub-chapter is one section (Section 1) covering explanation, objectives, and five
  development controls (1.1–1.5), each cross-referencing one of three site diagrams

  the second flat-file sub-chapter in this repo after 10.3.

## Notes

- **Chapter 11 sub-chapters vary hugely in size.** 11.2 is 5 pages with one section; 11.3
  is 8 pages with one (sub-headed) section; 11.5 is 11 pages (as supplied) with one
  (sub-headed) section; 11.7 is 6 pages with one section; 11.8 is 5 pages with one
  section; 11.4 is 24 pages with 8 sections; 11.6 is 21 pages with 5 sections; 11.9 is 14
  pages with 6 sections; 11.10 is 25 pages with 10 sections; 11.12 is 26 pages with 13
  sections and 7 figures; 11.1 is the largest so far at 41 pages with 17 sections and 10
  figures. Do not assume future 11.x sub-chapters will be short — check each one's own
  page count and section structure as it is supplied, per the conversion guide.
- **11.5 may be an incomplete supply rather than a genuinely short chapter.** Unlike 11.2
  and 11.3, where every stated objective maps to a clear numbered control, 11.5's stated
  objectives O4 (heritage), O5 (flood/contamination/bushfire), O8 (access network) and O9
  (infrastructure) have no standalone consent-test clause in the supplied pages — only
  passing mentions inside the subdivision concept-plan requirements (clause 1.3). The PDF
  as supplied ends at clause 1.11 on page 11 with no indication (e.g. a "continued" marker
  or an incomplete final sentence) of whether further pages exist. Recommend confirming
  the source PDF's true page count before treating this sub-chapter as complete.
- **11.6's own "attached draft plan of subdivision detail"**, referenced by name in its
  Section 5 (Community housing) subdivision clause, is not included in the supplied
  pages — the PDF as supplied ends immediately after that reference. Similar in kind to
  the 11.5 incomplete-supply concern above, though 11.6's own objectives all otherwise
  appear to be addressed; worth confirming with Council whether a subdivision plan
  attachment exists that wasn't included in this supply.
