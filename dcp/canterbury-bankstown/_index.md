### Notes
- **Chapter 1** in Council's numbering appeared as "1.1 Introduction and Administration" in the source PDFs seen so far — confirm whether Chapter 1 has further sub-chapters (1.2, 1.3...) once that PDF is reconverted.
- **Chapter 4 (Heritage)** is council-wide, not split by former LGA — all four sub-chapters (4.1–4.4) sit under `chapter-4-heritage/general/`. Chapter 4.3 has one Ashbury-HCA-specific section and one Hurlstone-Park-specific section internal to that single document; this is not a former-LGA folder split. Chapter 4.4 is a single-section sub-document kept as a flat file per the conversion guide, rather than its own folder. See `chapter-4-heritage/_index.md` for two follow-up documents needed (Heritage Guide; Heritage Conservation Area Character Statements) and each sub-chapter's own `_index.md` for source anomalies flagged during conversion (a duplicate clause label, an apparently incomplete clause, and minor cross-chapter wording differences).
- **Chapter 5** is the only chapter confirmed so far to split by former LGA. Don't assume other chapters split the same way — check each chapter's own "Application of this chapter" wording before deciding whether it needs an LGA-level subfolder. Chapter 3 was checked sub-chapter by sub-chapter during its conversion: only 3.4 (Sustainable Development) has an "Application of this chapter" section, and none of the seven Chapter 3 sub-chapters split by former LGA (see chapter-3-general-requirements/_index.md for the reasoning). Chapter 4 was likewise checked sub-chapter by sub-chapter and found not to split by former LGA. Chapters 7.5 and 7.6 were both checked and also do not split by former LGA — each defines its own single precinct/local-centre application area (Figure 1) with no LGA-based wording.
- Folders for "not started" chapters are intentionally not created yet — create them only when there's a converted file to put in them, so an empty folder never implies work that hasn't happened.
- **Chapter 6 (Strategic Centres)** splits by **strategic centre**, not by former LGA — a new scope-split pattern for this repo (Chapter 5 splits by former LGA; Chapter 4 is fully council-wide "general"). 6.1 (General Requirements) applies across both centres and sits in `chapter-6-strategic-centres/general/`; 6.3 (Campsie Town Centre) is centre-specific and sits in `chapter-6-strategic-centres/campsie-town-centre/`. 6.2 (Bankstown City Centre) has **not** been converted — at 221 pages it's roughly 10–20x the size of any other sub-chapter converted so far in this repo, with its own multi-level section numbering (Sections 1–6, a "Part 5," and an Appendix). See `chapter-6-strategic-centres/_index.md` for its full structure and a recommended approach for converting it in dedicated parts rather than in one pass. Source anomalies found in 6.1 and 6.3 (a section-heading singular/plural mismatch, an unnumbered clause-support paragraph, a figure-caption/clause percentage mismatch, and a scope ambiguity in an "Exceptions" clause) are flagged in each sub-chapter's own `_index.md`.
- **Chapter 7 (Commercial Centres) is now fully converted — 7 of 7 sub-chapters done.** It combines *four* different scope patterns in one chapter — see `chapter-7-commercial-centres/_index.md` for the full breakdown: 7.1 (General Requirements) is council-wide across all of Chapter 7 (like 6.1); 7.4 (Neighbourhood Centres) is council-wide but scoped to 'Area 1' land only; 7.2 (City West) and 7.3 (City East) split by geographic grouping with one boilerplate section per named centre; and 7.5/7.6/7.7 (Canterbury Local Centre / Belmore and Lakemba Precinct / Punchbowl and Wiley Park) are newer, much more detailed standalone "precinct DCP" chapters (Understanding Place, Public Domain Design, Building Design, General Provisions, Key Sites, Appendices, ~120–142 pages and 40–55 files each) that explicitly supersede older centre sections previously inside 7.3, each via its own clause-1.1 precedence wording. A cross-chapter reconciliation pass is recommended (not yet done) covering three patterns that turned up **independently in more than one of the precinct-DCP chapters**, suggesting shared drafting-template issues rather than isolated errors: (1) an amendment-date discrepancy between title page and filename, confirmed in both 7.6 and 7.7 but not 7.5; (2) a B2/E1 zone-name mismatch, confirmed in both 7.5 and 7.6; (3) verbatim-duplicated controls between the water-efficiency and energy-efficiency sustainability sub-sections, confirmed in both 7.5 and 7.6, in the same two sub-sections (5.4.3/5.4.4) in each. Full anomaly lists are in `chapter-7-commercial-centres/_index.md` and each sub-chapter's own `_index.md`. One outstanding filename fix remains in 7.5 (`06-connecting-to-country.md` needs renaming to `10-connecting-to-country.md`) that couldn't be actioned without that file's actual content — see `chapter-7-commercial-centres/canterbury-local-centre/_index.md`.
