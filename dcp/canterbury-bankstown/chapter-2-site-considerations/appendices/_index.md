# Chapter 2 — Appendices (flat-file sub-chapters)

Tracks image-only content (maps, figures, plans) referenced by sub-chapters that are converted
as a single flat file rather than their own folder, since a flat file has no `appendices/`
subfolder of its own. Sub-chapters converted into their own folder (e.g. 2.2 Flood Risk
Management) keep their appendix tracking inside that folder instead — see
`2.2-flood-risk-management/appendices/_index.md`.
