# Chapter 2.3 — Tree Management

Council-wide (not split by former LGA). Sets out the permit system, prescribed-tree criteria,
exemptions, pruning standards, and penalties for tree work across Canterbury-Bankstown, made
under the *State Environmental Planning Policy (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021*. Must be
read with Council's Tree Management Manual.
