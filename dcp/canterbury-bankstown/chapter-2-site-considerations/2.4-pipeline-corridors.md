# Chapter 2.4 — Pipeline Corridors

## Section 1 — Moomba to Sydney Pipeline Corridor

### Explanation

The pipeline corridor (Licence 15) crosses through the Canterbury-Bankstown Local Government
Area. The *State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021* and
Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 require development on land adjacent to
the pipeline corridor to consider potential safety risks or risks to the integrity of the
pipeline and to consequently introduce planning controls to manage the level of risk.

The Canterbury-Bankstown Development Control Plan 2023 supports the LEP by providing
objectives and development controls to manage the level of risk.

> See Figure 1a: Map of the Moomba to Sydney Ethane Pipeline (refer to red line) in the source
> PDF — the pipeline corridor crosses Chester Hill, Bankstown, Campsie, Canterbury, Roselands,
> Panania and Revesby. Image-only; not converted. Logged in the chapter appendix note below.

### Objectives

**O1** To identify matters for consideration in the assessment of development on land adjacent to the pipeline corridor.

**O2** To provide for consultation with relevant pipeline operators about certain development during the assessment process.

**O3** To ensure development incorporate measures to adequately mitigate the impacts of exposure to a pipeline.

**O4** To minimise potential risks to the integrity and safe operation of a pipeline.

### Development controls

#### Pipeline easement

**1.1** The pipeline operator that manages the pipeline easement must approve any proposed works within the easement or within 3m of the pipeline through the Third Party Works Authorisation process.

#### Residential development

**1.2** This clause applies to development for the purposes of residential accommodation on land identified as 'Area 2' on the Hazard Area Map as follows:

(1) The objective of this clause is to allow residential development on land adjacent to the pipeline corridor provided:
(a) buildings incorporate measures to adequately mitigate the impacts of exposure to the pipeline,
(b) buildings incorporate measures for features exposed to the pipeline to withstand a heat flux of 20kW/m²,
(c) buildings provide reliable evacuation access.

(2) Before granting development consent, Council must be satisfied that the development:
(a) complies with appropriate performance solutions assessed by the verification method in accordance with the National Construction Code (BCA Volume 1, A2G2(2)(b)), and
(b) complies with the performance standards as if the development is setback '3m from boundary' under the National Construction Code (BCA Volume 1, Table CV1), including but not limited to:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(i) development must be 'TYPE A' construction regardless of rise in storey and must incorporate fire-resisting construction in accordance with the details specified in the National Construction Code (BCA Volume 1, C2D2), noting that the fire-resistance level is achieved when subjecting a system to the Australian Standard AS 1530.4, *Methods for fire tests on building materials, components and structures*,
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(ii) development must protect openings that face the pipeline in accordance with the National Construction Code (BCA Volume 1, C4D5), noting that if passive protection or performance solutions are relied upon the system would need to achieve the same fire resistance level or better as the fire resisting element it is located within,
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(iii) development must protect service openings in accordance with the National Construction Code (BCA Volume 1, C4D15),
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(iv) development must protect the construction joints, spaces and the like in and between building elements in a manner identical to a tested prototype in accordance with the Australian Standard AS 1530.4 to achieve the required fire-resistance level,
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(v) fire stair exits must discharge into locations that are shielded away from the pipeline location, and
(c) submits an appropriate emergency response plan for use by building occupants, and
(d) submits a report prepared by a qualified consultant to the satisfaction of Council, which verifies that if all of the commitments relating to the building design and construction listed in the report are fulfilled, the development will comply with this clause.

(3) In this clause, *qualified consultant* means a consultant who holds a current registration as a 'Building Surveyor-Unrestricted' or 'Certifier-Fire Safety' for the purposes of section 6(4) of the *Building and Development Certifiers Act 2018*.

**1.3** Development may be carried out using performance solutions in accordance with the National Construction Code (BCA Volume 1, A2G1 – (1) or (3)) provided the development satisfies or exceeds the relevant performance requirements outlined in clause 1.2. The design fire must address:
(a) vertical fire spread,
(b) horizontal fire spread, and
(c) a fire brigade intervention scenario in the National Construction Code that is not less than the requirements in clause 1.2.

#### Safety management studies

**1.4** Council requires the following development on land shown in Figures 1–4 to submit a safety management study to assess the land use and construction risks:
(a) the erection of a new building for the following purposes: correctional centres, early education and care facilities, educational establishments, entertainment facilities, function centres, health services facilities, hotel or motel accommodation, places of public worship, respite day care centres, seniors housing, service stations, shops,
(b) the erection of a new building for the purpose of residential accommodation (not including boarding houses, co-living housing and seniors housing), if the new building has a density of one dwelling per 200m² of site area or higher,
(c) the erection of a new building for the purpose of boarding houses or co-living housing, if the new building has six or more boarding rooms or private rooms,
(d) the subdivision of land, if the site has a residential density of 50 dwellings per hectare or higher.

**1.5** Council may require other development on land adjacent to the pipeline corridor to submit a safety management study to assess the land use and construction risks.

**1.6** The safety management study must be prepared in accordance with the Australian Standard AS 2885, *Pipelines – gas and liquid petroleum* in consultation with the pipeline operator.
