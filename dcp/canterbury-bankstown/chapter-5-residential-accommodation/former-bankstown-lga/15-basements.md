# Section 15 — Basements

## Explanation

This section applies to dwelling houses, dual occupancies, semi-detached dwellings and
group homes.

## Basement size

### Objectives

- **O1** To ensure the basement is well-designed and integrates into the overall design of
  the development.
- **O2** To ensure the basement is subservient in size and scale to the dwellings on the site.
- **O3** To ensure the basement and earthworks do not have a detrimental impact on the
  environment, neighbouring properties and natural features on the site.

### Development controls

- **15.1** Development may propose a basement only where:
  (a) each dwelling has a minimum lot width of 7.5m at the front building line after any
  proposed subdivision; and
  (b) compliance with the Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 (clause
  6.2) can be demonstrated.
- **15.2** Applications must submit a geotechnical report to assess soil conditions,
  groundwater impacts and potential effects on adjoining properties, and to inform
  appropriate design and construction measures.
- **15.3** Despite clauses 15.5, 15.6 and 15.7, Council may not support basement parking
  where Council determines that vehicles cannot safely enter or exit the basement.
- **15.4** Where a basement is proposed, only one set of controls (15.5, 15.6, 15.7 or 15.8)
  may apply, depending on whether the basement provides parking or not. These controls
  cannot be combined.
- **15.5** This clause applies to dwelling houses on sites that are less than 15m wide at the
  front building line. Where the basement is providing parking:
  (a) the combined area of parking, storage, plant rooms and any areas used for
  mechanical services or ducting must not be more than 50m²; and
  (b) any area in the basement beyond the limit specified in subclause (a) must be
  included in the calculation of gross floor area; and
  (c) the parking must be tandem parking.
- **15.6** This clause applies to dwelling houses on sites that are at least 15m wide at the
  front building line. Where the basement is providing parking:
  (a) the combined area of parking, storage, plant rooms and any areas used for
  mechanical services or ducting must not be more than 50m²; and
  (b) any area in the basement beyond the limit specified in subclause (a) must be
  included in the calculation of gross floor area; and
  (c) the parking may be side by side parking.
- **15.7** This clause applies to dual occupancies, semi-detached dwellings and group
  homes. Where the basement is providing parking:
  (a) the combined area of parking, storage, plant rooms and any areas used for
  mechanical services or ducting must not be more than 25m² where:
  (i) the lot width is up to 10m at the front building line after any proposed
  subdivision, and
  (ii) the dwelling has fewer than three bedrooms; or
  (b) the combined area of parking, storage, plant rooms and any areas used for
  mechanical services or ducting must not be more than 50m² in all other circumstances;
  and
  (c) any area in the basement beyond the limit specified in subclauses (a) and (b) must be
  included in the calculation of gross floor area; and
  (d) the parking must be tandem parking.
- **15.8** Where the basement is not providing parking:
  (a) the combined area of storage, plant rooms and any areas used for mechanical
  services or ducting must not be more than 20m²; and
  (b) any area in the basement beyond the limit specified in subclause (a) must be
  included in the calculation of gross floor area; and
  (c) pedestrian access must only be provided internally or from the side or rear of the
  dwellings. Pedestrian access from the side or rear of the dwellings must not result in
  stairs or access encroaching into the minimum setback areas.

## Setbacks

### Objectives

- **O4** To ensure the basement does not adversely impact the streetscape, public domain,
  landscaped areas and amenity.

### Development controls

- **15.9** The basement must not extend beyond the perimeter of the floor level
  immediately above. For this clause, the perimeter includes the front porch.

## Landscaped areas and driveways

### Objectives

- **O5** To ensure the site has landscaped areas of sufficient size to enhance the streetscape
  and provide a high level of amenity.
- **O6** To provide deep soil zones to allow for and support healthy plant and tree growth.
- **O7** To protect existing street trees and on-street parking.

### Development controls

- **15.10** This clause applies to dwelling houses on sites that are less than 15m wide at the
  front building line. The width of the driveway must not be more than 3.5m or 40% of the
  site width at the front building line, whichever is the lesser.
- **15.11** This clause applies to dwelling houses on sites that are at least 15m wide at the
  front building line. The width of the driveway must not be more than 40% of the site
  width at the front building line.
- **15.12** This clause applies to dual occupancies, semi-detached dwellings and group
  homes. The width of the driveway must not be more than 3.5m or 40% of the lot width at
  the front building line after any proposed subdivision, whichever is the lesser.
- **15.13** The driveway and adjoining retaining walls must integrate into the overall design
  of the dwelling house and landscaped areas. Large areas of blank, minimally or poorly
  articulated retaining walls are not acceptable.
