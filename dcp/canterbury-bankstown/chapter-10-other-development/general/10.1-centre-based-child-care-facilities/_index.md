# Chapter 10.1 — Centre-Based Child Care Facilities

Applies council-wide (checked clause-by-clause — no former-LGA split anywhere in this
sub-chapter, unlike Section 4 of 10.4 in this same chapter). Sets out development controls
for centre-based child care facilities: trip-generation/traffic management (environmental
capacity and level-of-service tests, mandatory Traffic Impact Study), site layout and
building envelope controls (storey limits, setbacks by residential zone), building design and
energy efficiency (solar access, facade/roof design, front fences), acoustic privacy
(mandatory Acoustic Report, noise limits), open space and landscape (outdoor play area
rules, deep soil zones, private open space), safety and security, and site facilities
(utilities, substations, food premises). Sourced from the June 2023 edition, amended
August 2025.

Must be read in conjunction with the *State Environmental Planning Policy (Transport and
Infrastructure) 2021* and the Education and Care Services National Law and Regulations
(per Section 1).
