# 10.6 – Commercial Land Uses

Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 10 (Other Development), sub-chapter 10.6. Source
PDF cover date: June 2023 (Amended August 2024) — note this is a different amendment date
to the other 10.x sub-chapters converted so far (10.1–10.5, 10.8 are all "Amended April
2025"; 10.7 is also "Amended August 2024"). Not an error — sub-chapters within the same
DCP chapter can be amended on different schedules — but flagged since it means Chapter 10
is not uniformly current as at a single date.

Covers three unrelated commercial land uses grouped under one sub-chapter: amusement
centres, massage services premises, and restricted premises (i.e. licensed sex-related
retail such as adult bookshops — distinct from sex services premises, which have their own
sub-chapter, 10.7).

## Scope

No "Application of this chapter" wording and no former-LGA split anywhere in the source.
Council-wide, split internally by **zone** only in Section 4 (restricted premises exclusion
zone). Sits under `general/`.

## Anomalies / notes flagged during conversion

- **Amendment date differs from sibling sub-chapters** — see above.
- **Section 1's objectives (O1, O2) are generic** ("regulate certain types of development")
  and do not individually enumerate amusement centres / massage services premises /
  restricted premises the way the section explanation does — preserved as printed rather
  than expanded, since the conversion guide requires preserving text as-is, not improving it.
- **Cross-chapter relationship**: 10.6 Section 3 (massage services premises) and 10.7 (sex
  services premises) are related but distinct land uses under NSW planning law; 10.6 does
  not itself impose the location-exclusion-zone controls that 10.7 does for sex services
  premises — Section 3 here is limited to operator-qualification evidence only. No location
  or separation-distance controls for massage services premises appear anywhere in this
  sub-chapter.
- No duplicate clause numbers or partial-date issues beyond the year/month-only amendment
  date noted above (day omitted from `amended_date` per the conversion guide, so the field
  is omitted from all section front matter here).
