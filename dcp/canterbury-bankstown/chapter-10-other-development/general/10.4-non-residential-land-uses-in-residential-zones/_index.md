# Chapter 10.4 — Non-Residential Land Uses in Residential Zones

Applies to **Zone R2 Low Density Residential, Zone R3 Medium Density Residential and Zone
R4 High Density Residential**, council-wide at the document level (checked per Section 1's
explanation, which states this directly — no "Application of this chapter" clause needed).
Sets out controls for four categories of non-residential use in residential zones: health
consulting rooms, neighbourhood shops, serviced apartments, and other non-residential
development generally, plus shared site-facilities controls. Sourced from the June 2023
edition, amended August 2025.

**Section 4 (Serviced Apartments) is the only part of this sub-chapter with an internal
former-LGA split** — clauses 4.5–4.11 (former Bankstown LGA) vs 4.12–4.14 (former
Canterbury LGA) — see that section file for detail. Clause 5.2 is also
former-Canterbury-LGA-specific (Building Height Plane), but the rest of Section 5 and all
of Sections 1, 2, 3 and 6 apply uniformly.
