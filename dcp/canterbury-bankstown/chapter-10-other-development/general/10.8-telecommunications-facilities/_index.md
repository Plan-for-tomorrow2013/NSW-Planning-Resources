# 10.8 – Telecommunications Facilities

Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 10 (Other Development), sub-chapter 10.8. Source
PDF cover date: June 2023 (Amended April 2025).

Sets out development controls for the location and design of telecommunications facilities
(mobile phone base stations, antennas, and domestic satellite dishes). Explicitly notes
Council is **not** the consent authority for low-impact telecommunications facilities
regulated under the *Telecommunications (Low-Impact Facilities) Determination 1997* —
those are state-exempt. Covers location (EMR precautionary approach, sensitive land uses,
heritage exclusions), urban design (visual amenity, co-location, height in Zone E1,
domestic satellite dishes), and construction standards/access.

## Scope

No former-LGA split. Council-wide, with the only zone-specific control being the Zone E1
height provision (3.4) and the Zone R2/R3/R4 domestic satellite dish provisions (3.5–3.6).
Sits under `general/`.
