# Section 5 – Access

## Consideration

**C1** A matter of consideration is whether suitable access is provided.

## Development controls

**5.1** Sex services premises will be regarded in a similar way to any other traffic generating use. Safe vehicle and pedestrian access must be provided appropriate for the size of operation proposed.

**5.2** Sex services premises should, wherever possible, provide access for people with disabilities in accordance with the requirements of the Building Code of Australia. Larger establishments (over five rooms) must provide a minimum of one room with an ensuite located and designed to be suitable for use by people with disabilities.
