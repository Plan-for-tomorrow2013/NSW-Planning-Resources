# 10.7 – Sex Services Premises

Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, Chapter 10 (Other Development), sub-chapter 10.7. Source
PDF cover date: June 2023 (Amended August 2024).

Regulates the location, design and operation of sex services premises (decriminalised as a
legitimate land use under the *Disorderly Houses Amendment Act 1995*). Uses a
"consideration / development control" structure throughout (each section states a matter of
consideration, "C1", followed by numbered development controls), rather than the
"objectives / development controls" structure used in most other Chapter 10 sub-chapters.
Covers location and separation-distance requirements, neighbourhood impact, parking,
access, amenity, signage, safety and security, visual amenity, health and hygiene,
management issues, and closure/application procedures.

## Scope

No former-LGA split anywhere in the source. Council-wide, with the only geographic controls
being distance-based (from sensitive uses, other sex services premises, and state roads)
rather than zone- or LGA-based. Sits under `general/`.

## Anomalies / notes flagged during conversion

- **Numbering convention differs from most of Chapter 10**: this sub-chapter uses lettered
  "consideration" points (C1) ahead of numbered development controls in every section,
  rather than the "Objectives (O1, O2…) / Development controls" structure used in 10.1–10.6
  and 10.8. Preserved as printed — this is a genuine structural difference in the source, not
  a conversion inconsistency.
- **Section 2's state-roads table has two split entries** ("Stacey Street" and "Wiley
  Avenue" each appear twice with different From/To segments) — preserved exactly as printed;
  not a duplication error, see the inline note in
  [`02-location.md`](02-location.md).
- **Cross-reference to 10.5**: clauses 2.1–2.2 here are the reciprocal restriction to 10.5
  Places of Public Worship clauses 3.3–3.4 (each land use must avoid reasonable view of, and
  100m proximity to, the other). Cross-linked inline in both sub-chapters.
- **Possible duplication with Chapter 9.1 / 10.5 Appendix 1 road datasets**: the state-roads
  table here is structurally similar to, but not identical to (no "Regional roads" table),
  the appendix already converted for 10.5 and previously for Chapter 9.1 — flagged for a
  future cross-chapter reconciliation pass rather than resolved now.
- **Partial amendment date** ("Amended August 2024", no day, no amendment table) —
  `effective_date`/`amended_date` omitted from all section front matter per the conversion
  guide.
