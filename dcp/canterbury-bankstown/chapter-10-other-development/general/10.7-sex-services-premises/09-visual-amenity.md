# Section 9 – Visual Amenity

## Consideration

**C1** A matter of consideration is the likely visual or traffic impact (if any) on a main road.

## Development controls

### Visual amenity

**9.1** Sex services premises are not permitted to front state roads.

**9.2** Sex services premises may locate in a complex of industrial units which have an entrance on a state road if the individual unit does not have frontage to a state road, and is located at least 100m away from the state road.
