## Appendix 1 — State and regional roads in the former Bankstown Local Government Area

This is genuine tabular/text data (two tables: "State roads" and "Regional roads", each with
Road / From / To columns), so per the conversion guide it has been converted in full as a
markdown table rather than only logged here. Referenced from
[Section 3, clause 3.x location/traffic content is not directly linked to this appendix in
the source](../03-location-and-traffic-management.md) — the appendix is not explicitly
cross-referenced by clause number anywhere in the 10.5 source PDF, so its purpose (likely
supporting the state-road-adjacent height/setback provisions in Section 4, or simply
retained from a shared council-wide roads schedule also used elsewhere in this DCP) is
not stated. Flagged as an anomaly: **this appendix is identical in structure, and appears
to be the same underlying dataset, as the state/regional roads appendix already converted
for Chapter 9.1 (Appendix 1, Industrial Precincts)** — worth a cross-chapter reconciliation
pass to confirm whether the two are meant to be the same maintained list (see main chapter
`_index.md`).
