## Note on internal former-LGA splits

None of the eight sub-chapters split at the **document** level by former LGA. 10.4 has an
internal former-LGA split confined to specific clauses/sections (Section 4, Serviced
Apartments). 10.1, 10.2, 10.3, 10.6, 10.7 and 10.8 have no former-LGA content at all. 10.5
has no document-level former-LGA content, but its **Appendix 1** (state and regional roads)
is itself scoped to the former Bankstown LGA by its own heading — handled as an
appendix-level note rather than a folder split (see 10.5's own `_index.md`).

## Note on amendment dates

Sub-chapters within this chapter carry two different amendment dates in their source PDFs:
10.1–10.5 and 10.8 are "Amended April 2025"; 10.6 and 10.7 are "Amended August 2024". This
means Chapter 10 as a whole is not uniformly current as at a single date — flagged for
awareness, not resolved (each sub-chapter's own front matter reflects only what its own
cover page states, with the day omitted since only month/year is given).
