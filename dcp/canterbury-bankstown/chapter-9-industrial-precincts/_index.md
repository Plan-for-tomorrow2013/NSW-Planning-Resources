# Chapter 9 — Industrial Precincts

Status: **converted (1 of 1 supplied sub-chapter — 9.1; unconfirmed whether further
sub-chapters 9.2+ exist, see notes below)**.

Chapter 9 sets out development controls for Canterbury-Bankstown's industrial precincts
(Zone E4 General Industrial), supporting *Connective City 2036*'s objective of retaining
and managing industrial land for industrial and employment purposes.

## Scope check

9.1's own text was checked clause-by-clause, the same way Chapters 1.1, 3, 4, 6.1, 7.1,
7.5, 7.6, 8.1 etc. were checked, before deciding the folder shape:

- 9.1 has **no** "Application of this chapter" section and no document-level former-LGA
  split — it applies council-wide to Zone E4 land, so it sits under `general/` rather than
  a former-LGA folder.

  analogous to Chapter 8.1's internal E3/SP4 zone split, not a document-level LGA split,
  and does not change the folder placement.
