# Section 2 – Neighbourhood Centres

> Note: the Contents page lists this section as "Section 2 – Desired character"; the in-body heading printed on the page itself reads "SECTION 2–NEIGHBOURHOOD CENTRES". Both are preserved here per the source.

## Desired character

### C1 Neighbourhood centres identified as 'Area 1' on the Land Zoning Map

Neighbourhood centres will provide a range of small-scale retail, business and community uses that serve the needs of people who live or work in the surrounding neighbourhood. The built form is generally compatible with the prevailing suburban character and amenity of the surrounding residential areas.
