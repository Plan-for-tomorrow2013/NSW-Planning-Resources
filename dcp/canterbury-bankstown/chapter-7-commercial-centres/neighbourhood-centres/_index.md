## Chapter 7.4 — Neighbourhood Centres

Applies council-wide to every neighbourhood centre identified as 'Area 1' on the Land Zoning Map (small groups of shops meeting daily shopping needs) — not split by former LGA or by individual centre name, unlike 7.2/7.3/7.5/7.6/7.7. This is its own numbered sub-document (distinct scope from 7.1 General Requirements, which applies to all Chapter 7 centres including these), so it gets its own folder per the conversion guide rather than sitting inside `general/`.
