### How this chapter is structured (four distinct scope patterns in one chapter)

- **7.1 General Requirements** — council-wide design controls applying to *every* commercial centre in the chapter (active frontages, facades, bulk, corner buildings, roofs, materials, awnings, general amenity). Analogous to Chapter 6.1's role for Strategic Centres.
- **7.4 Neighbourhood Centres** — council-wide, but scoped specifically to centres identified as 'Area 1' on the Land Zoning Map (storey limits, setbacks) — a distinct policy layer from 7.1, not a duplicate of it.
- **7.2 City West / 7.3 City East** — split by geographic grouping (not former LGA), each containing one boilerplate section per named local/village/small-village centre (near-identical E1-zone setback controls, differing mainly in structure-plan figures and the occasional bespoke clause set, as seen with Roselands in 7.3). 7.2's conversion confirmed the same "boilerplate section per named centre" structure as 7.3, with four of its twelve centres (Bass Hill, Chester Hill, Georges Hall, Sefton) stating numeric setbacks directly rather than via the shared Appendix 1 typology used by the other eight.
- **7.5 Canterbury Local Centre / 7.6 Belmore and Lakemba Precinct / 7.7 Punchbowl and Wiley Park** — a newer, much more detailed "precinct DCP" template (Understanding Place, Public Domain Design, Building Design, General Provisions, Key Sites, Appendices) issued as standalone chapters between March–June 2026, each **explicitly superseding** the older brief centre sections that used to live inside 7.3 (Canterbury/Belmore/Lakemba/Punchbowl/Wiley Park), via each new chapter's own clause 1.1 precedence wording ("this chapter prevails ... except in respect of Chapter 4 Heritage"). **Confirmed directly in all three of 7.5, 7.6 and 7.7's own clause 1.1 text.** Now that all three are complete, the template consistently runs to roughly 120–142 pages and 40–55 files per chapter — 7.6's own 142 pages/7-section structure is the largest of the three, followed by 7.5 (122 pages/43 files) and 7.7 (132 pages/54 files).
