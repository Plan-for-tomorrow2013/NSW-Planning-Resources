## Chapter 7.1 — General Requirements

Council-wide design controls that apply across **all** Chapter 7 commercial centres (City West, City East, Neighbourhood Centres, and the standalone area-specific chapters), covering active street frontages, facade design, visual bulk, corner buildings, roof design, materials and finishes, awning design, and general design/amenity (sunlight, livable housing, front fences, safety and security, food premises, overhead wires).

This mirrors the role Chapter 6.1 plays for Chapter 6 (Strategic Centres) — a shared baseline that sits alongside, and is supplemented by, the centre-specific chapters (7.2 City West, 7.3 City East, 7.5 Canterbury Local Centre, 7.6 Belmore and Lakemba, 7.7 Punchbowl and Wiley Park).
