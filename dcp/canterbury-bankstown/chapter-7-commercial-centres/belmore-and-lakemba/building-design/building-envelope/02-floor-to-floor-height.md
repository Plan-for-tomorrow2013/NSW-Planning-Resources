## 4.2.2 Floor to floor height

### Objectives

**O1.** Ensure that floor to floor heights provide adequate amenity for building occupants, support a different range of uses and allow for flexibility of uses over time.

**O2.** To ensure the ground floor of buildings have a consistent floor to floor height to provide a well proportioned and quality interface to the public domain.

> See Figure 39. Key design guidance for floor to floor heights to ensure amenity, flexibility and a consistent street wall — three illustrated principles: provide amenity for building occupants; enable adaptivity and flexibility for changing uses; consistent ground floor height along active frontages.

### Principles

**P1.** Where the site has a single frontage, the entry to the basement for a HRV (Heavy Rigid Vehicle) waste truck will require a higher clearance. This change in height should be integrated into the design to ensure for a quality facade.

### Controls

**C1.** Development must comply with the minimum floor to floor heights shown in Table 3. This control does not apply to dwelling house, dual occupancy, semi-detached dwellings or multi-dwelling housing development.

**C2.** Waste truck entries should be located on secondary frontages.

**C3.** Where sites have a single frontage, the basement entry will require a 4.5m clearance. Dark materials and finishes should be used to hide and integrate the entry to the basement and any service cupboards.

### Table 3. Minimum floor-to-floor heights

| Floor-to-floor height (m) | Ground Floor | First Floor (Commercial) | Upper Levels | Lift Core (rooftop levels) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | 4.4m | N/A | 3.2m | 3m |
| Commercial | 4.4m | 3.7m | 3.7m | 3m |
| Mixed Use Commercial / Residential | 4.4m | 3.7m | 3.3m | 3m |

Waste truck access — Heavy Rigid Vehicle (HRV): 4.5m clearance.
