## 4.2.1 Building heights and storeys

The maximum building heights in the Precinct are stipulated in the CBLEP 2023. This section of the DCP provides further guidance on the bulk, scale and design of buildings, ensuring for careful consideration of impacts on streets, shared lanes, open spaces and pedestrian links.

### Objectives

**O1.** To design tall buildings that allow for adequate provision of sunlight to streets and open space for pedestrian amenity and to allow trees to grow.

**O2.** To design a distinctive and legible skyline to identify the core of the Local Centres.

**O3.** To ensure buildings are designed in consideration of wind impacts to the public domain.

**O4.** To design elegant slender towers that have consideration for view sharing.

**O5.** To ensure tall buildings express a strong architectural response defined by a clearly identified concept or idea to create a sense of place.

**O6.** To provide for landmark towers on prominent corners to improve the legibility of the Precinct.

> See Figure 37. Design guidance for the bulk, scale and design of tall buildings — four illustrated principles: slender, elegant towers well separated; height and scale responds to character and context; minimise wind impacts for pedestrians in the public domain; solar access and shade for amenity and plantings.

### Principles

**P1.** Concentrate tall buildings around the Metro station and civic areas to maximise housing provision in well located areas.

**P2.** Ensure that towers over 4 or 5 storeys are setback above a podium to mitigate bulk and dominance of the street.

**P3.** Ensure the height and scale of new developments are site specific and developed in response to key features of the site and setting.

**P4.** Manage wind conditions to promote outdoor planting, including green roofs, green cover and other landscaping elements as well as tables, chairs and other furniture used by building occupants on balconies and outdoor areas on buildings.

### Controls

**C1.** A wind impact assessment is required for:

  a) Buildings over 25m in height.

  b) Buildings without a podium on a corner sites.

**C2.** Buildings without a podium are permitted on corner sites. In cases where a podium is not proposed, the building is to:

  a) Be designed to mitigate down drafts to the public domain in accordance with a Wind Impact Assessment report;

  b) Be designed 'in the round' (i.e. consideration of the design quality in relation to how the building is viewed from within the Precinct, from the street level, from within other buildings, from the street and from a distance); and

  c) Provide a high-quality ground plane for pedestrians at street level.

**C3.** Development must not exceed the number of storeys shown for the site on Figure 38. Building height in Storeys Map.
