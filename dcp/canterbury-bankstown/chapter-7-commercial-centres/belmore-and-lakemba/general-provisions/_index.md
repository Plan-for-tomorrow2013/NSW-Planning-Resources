### Notes for resuming / cross-checking

- **5.4 Sustainability was split into its own subfolder** (`sustainability/`, 6 files for 5.4.1–5.4.6), following the same precedent as 4.2 The building envelope — each of its 6 sub-sections has a complete, independent Objectives/Controls treatment, and 5.4.2 alone carries four detailed tables.
- **5.5 Flood development controls was kept as a single flat file** despite having 7 numbered sub-subsections (5.5.1–5.5.7) — most of these are short (a handful of controls each, several without their own Objectives) and are tightly interdependent (shelter-in-place, evacuation, basement design and management plans all reference the same PMF-affected "Spot 1/5/8" sites), so splitting them further would fragment cross-references without adding retrieval value.
- **Confirmed anomaly (new):** Section 5.4.4 (Water efficiency) Controls C5–C6 are verbatim duplicates of Section 5.4.3's Controls C4–C5, referencing "energy target achievement" and NABERS inside a water-efficiency clause. Verified against the source page image (p.113) — genuine source duplication, not a conversion artifact. Flagged in `sustainability/04-water-efficiency.md`, not silently corrected.
- Tables 6 (parking rates), 7–10 (shading/sun-angle, in 5.4.2) and 11 (shelter-in-place design criteria) were all converted to markdown tables; Tables 7–10 were cross-checked against rasterized page images (pp.110–111) because the raw PDF text layer mis-renders degree symbols as trailing zeros.
