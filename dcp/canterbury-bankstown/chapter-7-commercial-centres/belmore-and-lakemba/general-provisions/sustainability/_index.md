## Section 5.4 — Sustainability

Covers environmental sustainability requirements for the Precinct: urban cooling, reduction of the urban heat island effect (including detailed shading/reflectance tables and sun-angle tables), BASIX/energy efficiency, water efficiency, all-electric building requirements, and rooftop solar PV requirements. Pages 108–115 of the source PDF.

Given its own folder — like Section 4.2 The building envelope — because it comprises six internally distinct sub-sections, each with a complete Objectives/Controls treatment (and in 5.4.2's case, four detailed tables).

### Confirmed anomaly: duplicated controls, 5.4.3 vs 5.4.4

Section 5.4.4 (Water efficiency) Controls C5–C6 are verbatim duplicates of Section 5.4.3's Controls C4–C5, incorrectly referencing "energy target achievement" and NABERS inside the water-efficiency clause. Verified against the source page image (p.113) — the duplication exists in the source PDF, not an artifact of conversion. Flagged in `04-water-efficiency.md`; not silently corrected.
