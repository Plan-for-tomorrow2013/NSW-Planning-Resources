## 5.3 Underground floor space

### Objectives

**O1.** To facilitate large floorplate uses underground, while maximising activation and permeability at street level.

### Principles

**P1.** Uses permitted for underground floor space include uses that generally require large floor areas, including neighbourhood supermarkets, larger floorplate retail premises over 1000m² GFA, gym and entertainment facilities.

**P2.** Larger sites that can accommodate the above mentioned uses and demonstrate improved streetscape and public domain impacts.

**P3.** The underground uses should not go beyond the building footprint to ensure opportunities for deep soil planting and open space in setback areas are maintained.

**P4.** Entries to the underground floor space should have an appropriate, active, ground floor frontage.
