## Chapter 7.6 — Belmore and Lakemba Precinct

Standalone, area-specific DCP chapter for the Belmore and Lakemba Precinct — issued **Amendment 13, dated June 2026** ("Final"). Replaces the former Section 3 (Belmore Local Centre) and Section 8 (Lakemba Local Centre) of Chapter 7.3 City East, which the current 7.3 Contents page explicitly marks "(Replaced by Chapter 7.6)" and which no longer contain any body text in the 7.3 PDF (see `../city-east/_index.md`). 142 pages: Understanding Place, Public Domain Design, Building Design, General Provisions (including flood controls), 4 named Key Sites, and Appendices.

**Clause 1.1 (Application of this Chapter)** confirms the Precinct is comprised of the two local centres Belmore and Lakemba, and that this chapter **prevails over other CBDCP 2023 chapters to the extent of any inconsistency, except Chapter 4 Heritage** — the same precedence pattern as Chapter 7.5 (Canterbury) and (presumably) Chapter 7.7 (Punchbowl and Wiley Park).

Status: **not started** — page map captured below for resuming; only the title page, welcome page, Contents, and clause 1.1 (application/precedence wording) have been read.
