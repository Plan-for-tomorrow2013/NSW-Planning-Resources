# 2.2.7 Industrial lands

The industrial land character areas play a vital role in supporting the local economy by offering employment opportunities and essential services. There are two character areas.

The Punchbowl Road Industrial area features medium-scale, low-rise light industrial uses with direct access to Punchbowl Road, connecting to larger employment lands in Greenacre and Enfield. This area is well-established and situated at the northern periphery of the Precinct. By contrast, the Lakemba Street Industrial area is a smaller, fine grain cluster of light industrial tenancies embedded within the residential context.

There is a strong commitment to preserving existing industrial zoned land with unchanged controls. As the surrounding context evolves, a sensitive and functional interface between industrial lands and residential areas will be essential for ensuring for amenity and mitigating impacts.

In particular, the Lakemba Street Industrial area presents opportunities to enhance streetscape amenity through tree planting, landscaping, and the introduction of pedestrian and cycle friendly improvements linking Belmore and Lakemba Stations. Over time, tenancies could incorporate complementary uses such as cafés or other local retail offers to activate the street and contribute to a more walkable neighbourhood.

Redevelopment of industrial sites should prioritise:
- Improving the public domain and mitigating urban heat outcomes through landscaping, permeable paving and tree canopy planting;
- Reduction of building bulk through modulation of the building envelope and improvement in design quality;
- Simplicity in design using quality materials;
- Minimal quality signage;
- Sustainable design and operation; and
- Safe, legible access for pedestrians, cyclists and service vehicles.

## Principles

**P1.** Retain and protect industrial zoned land to safeguard employment and essential services for the Belmore and Lakemba Precinct and surrounding areas.

**P2.** Minimise land use conflicts between residential and industrial zones by ensuring developments:
a) Provide generous setbacks, screening and landscape buffers;
b) Manage the bulk and scale through modulation of the building envelope and facade treatment;
c) Mitigate noise, pollution and other operational impacts; and
d) Safely accommodate truck access while protecting pedestrians and cyclists safety.

**P3.** Industrial areas should be designed as inviting environments for workers, businesses, visitors and passers-by. Integrate tree canopy and high quality landscaping, create lunch and social gathering spaces and implement effective surveillance and safety measures.

**P4.** Industrial lands adjoining public open spaces, such as Parry Park, should protect and enhance adjacent biodiversity assets, integrating landscaping and setbacks that support ecological function.

**P5.** New developments in the Lakemba Street industrial area should contribute to a high quality streetscape through:
a) Setbacks that allow for deep soil planting and support increased tree canopies;
b) Opportunities for street activation through features such as cafés, outdoor seating, and inviting entrances to tenancies; and
c) Limited driveway crossovers to ensure a pedestrian friendly environment.

**P6.** All new development must align with Chapter 9 Industrial Precincts of the CBDCP 2023, which outlines objectives, principles and controls related to industrial development including setbacks, landscaping, transport and access, building design and sustainability.
