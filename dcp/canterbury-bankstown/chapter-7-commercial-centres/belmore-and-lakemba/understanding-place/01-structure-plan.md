# 2.1 Structure Plan

The Structure Plan provides an overview of the intended outcomes for the Precinct. The Structure Plan has been informed by analysis of the opportunities and constraints for the Local Centres. These opportunities include the desired land uses, built form character, housing types, open space, streetscape improvements and pedestrian and cycling connections.

Key elements of the Structure Plan consist of:
- Enhance the vibrant high streets on Haldon Street and Burwood Road to support street life, retail activity and cultural expression.
- High rise urban living focused around and between Belmore and Lakemba Metro stations.
- New and enhanced parks and plazas.
- New social infrastructure that supports a growing community.
- Green connections, shared lanes and pedestrian links that support walking and cycling in key locations.
- Increased tree canopy and green cover to create cool, green and attractive neighbourhoods and to reduce urban heat gain.
- High quality built form outcomes based on robust design and planning and reflective of existing character.

The Structure Plan is shown in Figure 6.

## Objectives

**O1.** To provide an overview of the intended outcomes for the Belmore and Lakemba Precinct.

## Principles

**P1.** Development should align with the overarching elements of the Structure Plan, as shown in Figure 6.
