# 2.2.2 Belmore High Street

The Belmore High Street character area will be revitalised and showcase a diverse mix of retail and urban services, complemented by new residential developments that enhance the area's liveliness and appeal.

The character area will feature an improved pedestrian environment and activated shared lanes, making it more accessible and attractive for walking and cycling in and around the new Metro station. The local services and shops will provide convenient amenities.

Enhancements to Burwood Road will prioritise the pedestrian experience, incorporating traffic calming measures and urban greening while maintaining essential north-south transport connections for vehicles and buses.

Burwood Road will maintain a diverse mix of retail and commercial services, complemented by residential apartments above. A 2-3 storey street wall characterised by brick and masonry finishes will define the architectural identity of the area with additional storeys set back to enhance the traditional streetscape. Pre-1950 shop facades will be retained and sympathetically integrated into new development.

Shared lanes will facilitate service vehicle access while improving the pedestrian and cycling network creating opportunities for retail activation behind Burwood Road. Enhanced pathways, greenery and cycling connections along the railway line and Leylands Parade will link this character area to key destinations, including the Metro station, Belmore Sports and Recreation Precinct and higher density residential areas between Belmore and Lakemba.

The Canterbury Leagues Club key site is a significant attraction within the Belmore Local Centre and represents a substantial landholding strategically located near the station. This site presents a significant opportunity to catalyse Belmore Local Centre, through mixed use development with high rise apartments. It will feature pedestrian friendly links and shared lanes, improved interfaces and greening as well as a new publicly accessible open space that will serve as a green focal point, with activated edges to encourage community gathering, enjoyment and amenity.

## Principles

**P1.** Built Form along Burwood Road: New developments along Burwood Road should retain and enhance the fine-grain traditional high street character by:
a) Maintaining a street wall height of 2-3 storeys that aligns with the predominant existing building height and architectural detailing including solid to void ratios, awnings, parapets and windows. See Section 4.2.3 Street wall.
b) Retaining pre-1950 shop facades and sympathetically integrating them into the design of new development. Key elements such as the historic parapets and awnings should be restored and uncharacteristic and intrusive features removed.
c) Fostering the fine-grain active street frontages with glazing and openings that animate Burwood Road at street level. Mitigate noise and create a lively pedestrian atmosphere. See Section 4.3.2 Active street frontage and edges.
d) Using materials that reflect the traditional face brickwork and masonry of surrounding buildings including heritage and pre-1950 facades. See Section 4.5 Materials and finishes.
e) Setting back the upper levels above the street wall to provide green podium communal spaces and passive surveillance. See Section 4.6 Rooftop and communal open space.

**P2.** Lanes: Lanes will be enhanced to better accommodate pedestrians and cyclists, while also facilitating service vehicles and driveways, creating a high-amenity network that promotes safety and accessibility for all users.

**P3.** Development interfacing lanes should manage servicing and driveway access within setbacks while also providing increased amenity, activity and greening. Specifically:
a) For commercial uses, consider opportunities for active lane edges, including cafes, outdoor seating, planters and where possible deep soil.
b) For residential uses, provide high quality landscaping, deep soil, and apartments with private entries. Refer to Section 3.3 Streets, shared lanes, pedestrian links and arcades.

**P4.** The Leagues Club key site; development on this site must provide improved permeability, active edges to streets and shared lanes and deliver new open space, as identified the KS-3 plan. See Section 6 Key Sites.
