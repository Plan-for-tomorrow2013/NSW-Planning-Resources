# 2.2.6 Canterbury Road

The Canterbury Road character area will remain as a vital vehicle connection continuing to provide employment and services for the local area, while also offering opportunities for improved amenity, greening and alternate connections to make the area more attractive and pedestrian and cyclist friendly.

The character area will primarily showcase medium-format commercial and retail developments, alongside mixed-use buildings that are 6 to 8 storeys. Additionally, this area contains lower-scale public buildings, such as Belmore South Public School and St Joseph's Catholic Church and School, as well as low-scale dwellings that are expected to remain as low density. This will create a diversity of scales and uses that requires careful consideration of interfaces.

A setback at ground level for commercial uses along Canterbury Road will create more spacious footpaths, verges and landscaping, providing protection for pedestrians from traffic, and allowing for deep soil areas to increase tree canopy. Generous front setbacks for residential dwellings will allow for increased screening, privacy, landscaping and deep soil.

New residential apartments will be strategically located along key intersections and situated above ground away from Canterbury Road. Apartments will be designed to mitigate noise and pollution and provide adequate sunlight and outlook.

Behind Canterbury Road, lanes will be extended to enhance connectivity, facilitating an improved east-west pedestrian and cycling network safely distanced from vehicular traffic. Lanes will feature generous setbacks for landscaping and offer opportunity for the creation of secondary frontages that may include outdoor seating for retail establishments.

## Principles

**P1.** Ground floor commercial and retail spaces along Canterbury Road will feature active edges along frontages and at key corners, High quality paving and landscaping including deep soil areas for tree canopy and vegetation should be provided within setbacks. See Section 4.3.2 Active street frontages and edges and Section 3.4 Tree canopy, green cover and deep soil.

**P2.** New development will enhance lanes by incorporating generous setbacks for landscape and potentially create active edges, where possible, while balancing servicing needs and driveways. If required, development will provide an additional setback for land dedication to enable lane extensions. See Section 3.3 Streets, shared lanes, pedestrian links and arcades.

**P3.** New development will be sympathetic to the range of typologies, heights and uses along Canterbury Road. In particular, new development that interfaces the low scale areas to the north of Canterbury Road, Belmore South Public School and St Joseph Catholic Church, will provide a considered interface. See Section 4.2 The building envelope.

**P4.** New mixed-use developments should be concentrated at key cross streets, with residential units situated above ground level. These apartments should be designed and oriented to mitigate amenity impacts from noise and pollution, while ensuring for solar access, views, landscaping and high-quality podium and rooftop space. See Section 4.2 The building envelope and 4.6 Rooftop and communal open space.
