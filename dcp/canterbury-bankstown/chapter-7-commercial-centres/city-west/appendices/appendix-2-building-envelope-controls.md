# Appendix 2 — Building envelope controls for each dwelling on each lot

Applies to the Former Bass Hill Drive-In Theatre Site, Bass Hill (see Section 2, clauses
2.18–2.23).

## General clauses

**1.** Council must not grant consent to the erection of a dwelling house unless the
proposed lot is at least 7m wide at the front building line.

**2.** Council may require one car parking space per dwelling to locate forward of the front
building line (in the form of a hardstand) to avoid garages and driveways dominating
the front of a dwelling and landscaped area when viewed from the street.

**3.** The schedule and diagrams of indicative houses (Types A to J) are for guidance
purposes only.

following table:

## Notes on the table above

- **"Nil setback"** appears as printed in the source — a value of zero for that boundary.
- **"Nil setback to shared driveway" / "1 to shared driveway"** etc. are printed exactly as
  in the source; a small number of lots (73–86, on the internal driveway loop) have a
  setback value that is qualified by "to shared driveway" rather than a plain figure — this
  qualifier has been preserved rather than dropped.
- **Lot 71–72**: printed in the source as a single combined row spanning both lot
  numbers, with "–" in every numeric column and the note "These allotments do not exist
  in Figures 17 or 18." Reproduced here as a single row rather than split into two,
  matching the source layout.
- **"–" (em dash) in Special requirements** is a printed value meaning "none", and is
  distinct from a blank cell (see next point).
- **Blank Special requirements cells**: in several places in the source PDF, the Special
  requirements cell for one lot is a taller merged cell that visually spans two (and in
  one confirmed case, effectively three) consecutive lot rows, printing the note once at
  the top of the span rather than repeating it. Confirmed by visual inspection of the
  source PDF for Lots 3–4, 34–35, and 48–49/50–51 (each pair shares one note, printed
  against the first lot of the pair only). Where a lot's Special requirements cell is blank
  in the table above and the row immediately above it has an identical or near-identical
  setback profile, treat the note above as likely applying to the blank lot too, but this has
  **not** been individually visually confirmed for every such blank cell (e.g. Lots 9–13,
  26–28, 56–57, 88–94) — cross-check the source PDF (pages 85–97) before relying on
  a specific lot's special requirements where its cell is blank.
