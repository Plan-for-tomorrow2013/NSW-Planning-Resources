## Chapter 7.2 — City West

Covers 12 local/small-village centres in the western part of the LGA. Follows the **exact same pattern as Chapter 7.3 City East** (one section per centre, sharing near-identical E1-zone front/side/rear setback boilerplate, structure plan figures, and — per Section 1's explanation — the same "Local Area Plans" framework), so conversion should be fast once resumed: most sections will closely mirror `city-east/02-belfield-small-village-centre.md` etc. in structure, differing mainly in the structure-plan figure and any centre-specific bespoke clauses (Greenacre and Padstow are the largest sections and worth checking first for bespoke content, the way Roselands had extra clauses in 7.3).
