### Structural pattern (confirmed matches 7.3 City East)

Like 7.3, most City West centres set their setback controls by cross-referencing Appendix 1
("Setback Type S1" plus a per-centre setback figure), with a two- or four-storey street wall
requirement depending on the centre's scale. Four centres (Sections 2, 4, 7, 12 — Bass Hill,
Chester Hill, Georges Hall, Sefton) instead state numeric zero/side/rear setbacks directly in
their own clauses, without reference to Appendix 1 — noted individually in each of those
section files.

### Supersession check (per the chapter-level note in `chapter-7-commercial-centres/_index.md`)

Checked as part of this conversion: **no City West centre carries a "(Replaced by...)"
notice**, and no standalone precinct-DCP-style chapter (of the kind issued for Canterbury,
Belmore/Lakemba, or Punchbowl/Wiley Park in 7.5/7.6/7.7) has been issued for any of the
twelve City West centres as at this source PDF (August 2025 amendment). Unlike 7.3, this
chapter has not (yet) had any of its centres superseded by a separate chapter.

### Site-specific / key development site provisions

- **Bass Hill** (Section 2) has by far the most site-specific content of any City West centre:
  five separate site-specific control sets (713–727 Hume Highway; 731–737 & 753 Hume
  Highway; 739 Hume Highway split across two zones; and the Former Bass Hill Drive-In
  Theatre Site, which is also the subject of Appendix 2's full lot-by-lot schedule).
- **Padstow** (Section 9) has site-specific controls for 1–17 Segers Avenue (a named key
  development site) and 71–87 Howard Road (a new rear lane requirement).
- **Revesby** (Section 11) has the most detailed design-excellence/urban-design-study
  requirements of any City West centre, for 7A–17 Marco Avenue and 11–17 Marco Avenue,
  including a mandatory Design Review Panel referral (clause 11.4(q)) and flood/overland
  flow path controls (clauses 11.3, and Objective O9) — the only flooding-specific
  development controls found anywhere in this sub-chapter.
- **Yagoona** (Section 13) has site-specific controls for 399–403 Hume Highway & 81
  Brancourt Avenue, including a heritage item reference (clause 13.4, item at 401 Hume
  Highway — see Chapter 4 Heritage, not duplicated here).

### Anomalies found during conversion

- **Section 2 (Bass Hill), clause 2.24**: likely dropped word in source ("...connections
  important destinations") — preserved verbatim with a note.
- **Section 6 (East Hills), clause 6.2**: clause text cites "Figure 6d" but the only setback
  figure printed in that section is captioned "Figure 6c" — figure-number mismatch,
  preserved as printed in both places.
- **Appendix 2 lot schedule**: several Special requirements cells are blank because the
  source table visually merges that cell with the row above (confirmed for Lots 3–4,
  34–35, 48–49, 50–51 by direct inspection of the source PDF) — see the notes section in
  `appendices/appendix-2-building-envelope-controls.md` for the full list of blank cells
  that have **not** been individually re-confirmed against the source.
- **Lot 71–72**: printed in the source as one combined row (not two), with a note that
  these allotments "do not exist in Figures 17 or 18" — reproduced as a single row.
