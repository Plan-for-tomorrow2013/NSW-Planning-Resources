## KS-4 — 186-206 Canterbury Road

> Part of Section 6 Key Sites. See [31-key-sites-overview.md](31-key-sites-overview.md) for
> the Objectives and General Controls applicable to all Key Sites, and the Key Sites Map.
> Located in the Canterbury East character area — see
> [08-canterbury-east.md](08-canterbury-east.md), which already flags this site (there
> described via Principle P5's "key site to the north" of the Canterbury Hotel) as a heritage
> setback concern.

### Vision

This site is a catalyst for the Local Centre and for the southern side of Canterbury Road. The
site is comprised of sixteen lots and is bounded by Canterbury Road, Tincombe and Minter
Streets and is located to the north of the heritage listed Canterbury Hotel, a notable
building in Canterbury's urban fabric. The site will be highly visible from the public domain
and **will be the tallest building in the Local Centre**. This site will provide a mix of uses
in proximity to the new Metro.

> **Cross-reference**: this confirms the 24-storey site identified on Figure 40 (Building
> Height in Storeys Map, near Tincombe Street/Minter Street) in
> `19-building-envelope-height-storeys.md` is KS-4.

### Design Principles

**P1.** Create a quality outcome for the Local Centre and consider regional views by
demonstrating design excellence across all levels of the development, reflecting on the
Canterbury's history and materiality, its Connection to Country and establishing a celebrated
presence evident in the prominence of its tallest elements.

**P2.** Set the buildings back from the Tincombe Street and Canterbury Road intersection to
retain views to the Canterbury Hotel along Canterbury Road from the north and south and
provide public domain enhancement to all surrounding streets. Use face brick that is
complementary to the Canterbury Hotel throughout the development.

**P3.** Locate the tallest buildings near the station and transition height down towards the
eastern residential area. Ensure appropriate separation is provided to reduce bulk and
maximise solar access to neighbouring sites.

**P4.** Create a new pedestrian through site link connecting Canterbury Road to Minter Street
along a new civic plaza.

**P5.** Create a civic plaza of 500m² minimum. The courtyard will be activated by retail and
commercial premises, provide seating, high quality landscaping, shade, solar access and
integrate public art. It's entry points should be legible from the public domain, with at
least one side open to the street. It must be located along the new laneway, and remain
accessible to the public 24h/7.

**P6.** Activate Canterbury Road and Tincombe Streets and create a destination in the Local
Centre by supporting a diverse commercial, food and retail offer. Large format retail uses
such as supermarkets are to be below ground floor located at basement level to maximise ground
level activation.
