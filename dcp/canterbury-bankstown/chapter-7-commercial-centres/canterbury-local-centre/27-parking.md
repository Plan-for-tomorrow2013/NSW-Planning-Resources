## 5.2 Parking

> Part of Section 5 General Provisions. See [26-dwelling-mix.md](26-dwelling-mix.md) for the Section 5 file map.

### Objectives

**O1.** Promote sustainable transport and limit vehicular movements by restricting private
parking and facilitating increasing access to, and use of, communal modes of transport within
the Local Centre.

Require a lower minimum parking rate in the Local Centre 'Core' near the Canterbury Metro
station, with a slightly higher minimum parking rate outside of the Local Centre 'Core' to
minimise a reliance on private motor vehicle trips.

### Principles

**P1.** Encourage basement vehicular parking instead of at-grade or above ground parking.

**P2.** Provide flexibility for the efficient use of car parking in development by allow
off-site users to use car parking.

**P3.** Where provided, design above ground car parking so that it can be adapted to alternate
uses over time.

**P4.** Consider the location of car park and service vehicle entrances and exits to ensure the
amenity of public domain is maintained and improved where possible.

**P5.** To facilitate adaptation of car parking to other uses in the long term, above ground
car parking is to remain as part of the common property and not part of, or attached to,
individual strata units.

### Controls

**C1.** All car parking within the Local Centre must be provided in accordance with the rates
as specified in Table 8 and Figure 63. If a land use is not included in Table 8, the car
parking rates in Chapter 2 Chapter 3.2 of the CBDCP 2023 will apply.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Residential flat buildings/Shop top housing | Studio | 0 | 0.5 space per dwelling | 0 | 0.75 space per dwelling |
| Residential flat buildings/Shop top housing | 1 Bedroom | 0 | 0.5 space per dwelling | 0.2 space per dwellings | 0.75 space per dwelling |

> Note: Office premises' maximum rate is per **1000m²** (a tenth the intensity of Retail's and
> Education's per-100m² maximum) — confirmed by visual inspection at 250 DPI; not a
> text-extraction artefact.

**C2.** Parking for non-residential uses, and non-staff related parking can be considered
collectively and be shared if parking is accessible to the public and the hours of use have
different peak demand times.

**C3.** If the total Gross Floor Area (GFA) of retail premises exceeds 2,000m², a Transport
Impact Assessment for the development proposed is to be prepared and submitted.
