# 1.1 Application of this Chapter

This Chapter of the Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, (CBDCP 2023), supports the Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 (LEP), by providing additional objectives, principles and development controls to enhance the function, design and amenity of development within the Canterbury Local Centre, (also referred to as 'the Local Centre' in this Chapter).

This Chapter of the CBDCP 2023 applies to all development on the land within the Canterbury Local Centre as identified in Figure 1. Other Chapters of the CBDCP 2023 must be addressed as part of Development Applications lodged for development in the Local Centre. Where there is an inconsistency between the controls in this Chapter and other Chapters in the CBDCP 2023 in relation to development in the Local Centre, this chapter prevails to the extent of the inconsistency except in respect of Chapter 4 Heritage.
