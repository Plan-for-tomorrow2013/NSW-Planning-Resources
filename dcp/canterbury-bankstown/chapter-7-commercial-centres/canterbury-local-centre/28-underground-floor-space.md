## 5.3 Underground Floor Space

> Part of Section 5 General Provisions. See [26-dwelling-mix.md](26-dwelling-mix.md) for the Section 5 file map.

### Objectives

**O1.** To facilitate large floorplate uses underground, while maximising activation and
permeability at street level.

### Principles

**P1.** Uses permitted for underground floor space include uses that generally require large
floor areas, including neighbourhood supermarkets, larger floorplate retail premises over
1000m² GFA, gym and entertainment facilities.

**P2.** Larger sites that can accommodate the above mentioned uses and demonstrate improved
streetscape and public domain impacts.

**P3.** The underground uses should not go beyond the building footprint to ensure
opportunities for deep soil planting and open space in setback areas are maintained.

**P4.** Entries to the underground floor space should have an appropriate, active, ground
floor frontage.

### Controls

**C1.** The basement levels used must have a minimum floor to ceiling height of 3.7 metres.

**C2.** The basement levels used must be the upper-most basement levels, closest to ground
floor.

**C3.** The floor space is not used for the purposes of car parking.

**C4.** Does not result in the relocation of car parking at street level.

**C5.** Is located on a site with a minimum site area of no less than 3,000m².

**C6.** Any proposed or existing underground floor space is in addition to the maximum Floor
Space Ratio for that site under the Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023 and any
relevant applicable SEPPs. Any existing underground floor space on a site counts towards the
maximum 1:1 underground floor space permitted.
