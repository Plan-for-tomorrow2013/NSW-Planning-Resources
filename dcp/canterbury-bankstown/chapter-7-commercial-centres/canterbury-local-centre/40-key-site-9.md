### Vision

To create a development with a sense of place that reflects the heritage of Canterbury Local
Centre and provides an address and connection with the Cooks River.

### Key Objectives

**O1.** Break down the scale of the development so it appears like several buildings.

**O2.** Integrate the development to its natural and historical context.

**O3.** Minimize the number of materials used in facade and rationalize the design.

**O4.** Establish a positive architectural gateway to the south of the precinct.

**O5.** Establish an engaging public realm interface to Canterbury Road and Close Street.

### Design Principles

**P1.** Use materials that reflects Canterbury's heritage and history and responds to the
proximity of the Sugar House and the Canterbury Hotel on Canterbury Road. This includes face
brick, stone and concrete.

**P2.** The facade along Close Street is to appear visually broken into three different
buildings, each one in a different but complimentary face brick or masonry and separated from
each other by a recessed vertical indentation. Each indentation's length to depth ratio must
range between 2:1 and 3:1. The total length of the two indentations must be equal to 15% or
more of the total length of the Close Street facade.

**P3.** The facade along Canterbury Road is to appear visually broken into two buildings
separated by one recessed vertical indentation of 1.5m depth minimum and a length to depth
ratio ranging between 2:1 to 3:1.

**P4.** A portion of the development that is visually expressed as a building, (as opposed to
an indentation) must be taller than the adjacent indentations by at least 1.5m so that the
visual differentiation between the buildings is also reflected in the skyline of the
development and fully perceived from the public domain.

**P5.** The development must provide opportunity for rooftop communal open space that takes
advantage of its prime location along the Cooks River and Lesley Muir Reserve.

**P6.** The development must provide a generous double height internal public plaza at the
Close Street intersection extending further into a pedestrian connection through to the Cooks
River. The plaza should be designed with high quality material finishes and landscaping, and
should be sleeved with active uses on the ground floor such as cafe, restaurant, corner shop or
commercial premises.

**P7.** The development should provide commercial ground floor along Canterbury Road and close
street to the extent of the B2 zone.

> **Cross-reference**: another explicit reference to a **B2 zone** in this chapter (see the
> same cross-reference note in `22-building-interface.md`, Table 7, re: Broughton Street) —
> further corroborating that a B2 zone genuinely applies somewhere in this chapter's area,
> separate from the E1 zone referenced elsewhere.

**P8.** The landscape design and the plant selection throughout the development should reflect
and take cues from the Cooks River, its history, its natural landscape and its ecology.

**P9.** If a new DA is lodged to redevelop the site, appropriate setbacks and street wall
height must be considered to Canterbury Rd, close St, Lesley Muir Reserve and the Cooks River
Pathway.
