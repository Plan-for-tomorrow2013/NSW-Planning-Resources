## Chapter 7.5 — Canterbury Local Centre

Standalone, area-specific DCP chapter for the Canterbury Local Centre — issued **Amendment
12, March 2026** ("Final"). This is a much larger and more comprehensive document than the
~5-page Canterbury section it replaces inside Chapter 7.3 (see `../city-east/_index.md` for
the full conflict writeup): 122 pages covering Understanding Place, Public Domain Design,
Building Design, General Provisions (including flood/heat/sustainability controls), and 9
named Key Sites, plus Appendices (Definitions, Figures List, Table List). Structurally this
matches the "precinct DCP" template also used for Chapter 7.6 (Belmore and Lakemba) and
Chapter 7.7 (Punchbowl and Wiley Park).

**Clause 1.1 (Application of this Chapter)** confirms this chapter **prevails over other
CBDCP 2023 chapters to the extent of any inconsistency, except Chapter 4 Heritage**, for
development within the Canterbury Local Centre application area (Figure 1).

**Status: DONE — fully converted, 43 of 43 files (2026-08-27 session).** All six sections and
all three appendices are converted. See the section table and consolidated anomaly list below.

### Consolidated source anomalies (full list — see each file for detail)

1. **Section 2.2 file split**: converted as overview + one file per character area (Core,
   North, East, South), each bundling that area's own key streets/lanes — see
   `05-character-areas-overview.md`.
2. **Figure 8 (Robert Street cross-section, p.15) has hidden, non-rendering text** with a
   different, wrong set of dimensions — not visible on the actual page. See
   `06-canterbury-core.md`.
3. **The same hidden-text pattern ("Map to be updated based on the 3D Model") recurs on
   Figures 45, 47 and 48** (Street wall height / Front setbacks / Upper storey setbacks
   maps) — see `20-building-envelope-floor-heights-street-wall.md`.
4. **Unlabeled objectives/principles recur throughout the chapter** (a numbered item followed
   by an unlabeled elaboration paragraph with no number of its own) — first flagged in Section
   2.3, confirmed as a chapter-wide pattern in Section 3.2 (`13-public-space-and-publicly-accessible-space.md`)
   and recurring in Sections 3.4–3.6, 4.1, 4.3, 5.4, and Canterbury Core's Principle P9.
5. **Table 4 (Section 4.1, p.52)** requires a minimum lot size per Control C1(a), but every
   row's "Column 3" (minimum lot size) is printed as a dash — no figure is actually given.
   See `18-minimum-lot-sizes.md`.
6. **Section 4.2.4's Principle "P3" is printed twice** with two unrelated principles (p.63).
   See `21-building-envelope-setbacks-separation.md`.
7. **Section 4.2.5 Control C4 cites "Figure 78, page 109 in the appendix"** for how upper
   storey setbacks are measured — this is wrong on both figure number and page. The correct
   reference is **Figure 79, p.117** (Appendix 1). Resolved by this session — see
   `41-appendix-1-definitions.md`, `39-key-site-8.md`, `40-key-site-9.md`.
8. **Section 5.2 Control C1 cross-references "Chapter 2 Chapter 3.2" of the CBDCP** — two
   chapter numbers given together, likely a leftover from editing. See `27-parking.md`.
9. **Section 5.4 has two sub-sections both numbered "5.4.2"** ("Urban cooling and
   environmental sustainability" and "Reduction of the Urban Heat Island Effect"), and its
   control numbering (C1/C2/C3...) restarts multiple times within 5.4.2 without renumbering.
   See `29-sustainability-urban-cooling.md`.
10. **Section 5.4.2 Controls C1/C2 cross-reference "Table 7" and "Table 8"** for shading
    percentages, but those table numbers belong to unrelated tables elsewhere in the chapter
    (Active street frontage design guidance, and Car parking requirements). The actual
    shading table is **Table 9** (Parts A and B). See `29-sustainability-urban-cooling.md`.
11. **Section 5.4.4 (Water Efficiency) Controls C5/C6 are verbatim duplicates of Section
    5.4.3's C4/C5** (both about "NABERS"/"energy target achievement" rather than a
    water-specific equivalent). See `30-sustainability-energy-water-solar.md`.
12. **Winter solstice is dated inconsistently**: Table 12 (Section 5.4.2) captions itself "21
    July (Winter Solstice)", while KS-2's Principle P1 (Section 6) refers to "the winter
    solstice on the 21st June". Astronomically the Southern Hemisphere winter solstice falls
    around 21 June. See `29-sustainability-urban-cooling.md` and `33-key-site-2.md`.
13. **Section 6's own internal Contents list (p.96) gives wrong page numbers for every Key
    Site** (e.g. KS-1 at "p.92", before Section 6 even starts). The correct pages are in the
    chapter's front-of-document page map (reproduced above). See
    `31-key-sites-overview.md`.
14. **Cross-referencing Figure 40 (Building Height Map)**: the tallest sites in the Local
    Centre (24 storeys and 23 storeys) correspond to Key Sites KS-4 and (likely) one of
    KS-2/KS-3/KS-7/KS-8 — confirmed for KS-4, whose own vision text says it "will be the
    tallest building in the Local Centre". See `19-building-envelope-height-storeys.md` and
    `35-key-site-4.md`.
15. **KS-9's address is given three different ways** across the source ("1-3 Close Street" vs
    "1-13 Close Street"; "242-258" vs "244-258" Canterbury Road). See `40-key-site-9.md`.
16. **Appendix 2 (Figures List) is out of sync with the actual body from Figure 64 (Key Sites
    Map) onward** — every figure number is off by one through KS-6, an extra phantom figure
    ("Adaptive reuse... Bang Bang, Elsternwick") is listed but doesn't appear to exist in the
    body, and every page number is ~8 pages lower than the actual page. Figures 1–63 (Sections
    1–5) are reliable. See `42-appendix-2-figures-list.md`.
17. **Appendix 3 (Table List) omits Tables 9–12 entirely** (the shading/sun-angle tables from
    Section 5.4.2), stopping at Table 8. See `43-appendix-3-table-list.md`.
18. **A definition in Appendix 1 (Probable maximum flood) appears to be cut off mid-sentence**
    in the source. See `41-appendix-1-definitions.md`.
19. **"Shared lanes" (Appendix 1 definition) gives an 8pm–7am vehicle-priority window**, while
    the near-identical control in Section 3.3 gives 10pm–7am. See
    `41-appendix-1-definitions.md`.
20. **Zones**: still not fully confirmed chapter-wide. E1 is the dominant zone referenced
    throughout; R4 is confirmed for some Key Site interfaces (KS-7, KS-8); a B2 zone is
    referenced twice (Table 7 re: Broughton Street, and KS-9 Principle P7 re: Canterbury
    Road/Close Street) but never defined with its own boundary in the body text read so far.
    Front matter across this chapter's files reflects only what's explicitly stated per
    section — treat as provisional.
21. **Amendment-date discrepancy check (from the prior session)**: this chapter's own
    filename/title page remained consistent ("AMENDMENT_12", "March 2026 Final") throughout
    this full conversion — no internal amendment-number contradiction was found anywhere in
