## Chapter 7.5 — Canterbury Local Centre

Standalone, area-specific DCP chapter for the Canterbury Local Centre — issued **Amendment 12, March 2026** ("Final"). This is a much larger and more comprehensive document than the ~5-page Canterbury section it replaces inside Chapter 7.3 (see `../city-east/_index.md` for the full conflict writeup): 122 pages covering Understanding Place, Public Domain Design, Building Design, General Provisions (including flood controls), and 9 named Key Sites, plus Appendices (Definitions, Figures List, Table List). Structurally this matches the newer "precinct DCP" template also used for Chapter 7.6 (Belmore and Lakemba) and Chapter 7.7 (Punchbowl and Wiley Park) — a fourth structural pattern for this repo, distinct from the older "one boilerplate section per centre" template used in 7.2/7.3.

**Clause 1.1 (Application of this Chapter)** confirms this chapter **prevails over other CBDCP 2023 chapters to the extent of any inconsistency, except Chapter 4 Heritage**, for development within the Canterbury Local Centre application area (Figure 1).

Status: **not started** — page map captured below for resuming; only the title page, welcome page, Contents, and clause 1.1 (application/precedence wording) have been read.
