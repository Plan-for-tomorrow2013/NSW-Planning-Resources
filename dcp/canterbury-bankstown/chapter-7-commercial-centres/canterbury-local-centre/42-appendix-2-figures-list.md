### Reconciliation against this conversion — major anomaly, confirmed by visual inspection

**Figures 1–63** (everything through the end of Section 5) match what this conversion found
almost exactly, with one minor exception: this list gives Figure 43 as p.60, while this
conversion's own footer-based page tracking placed it at p.59 (`20-building-envelope-floor-heights-street-wall.md`)
— a 1-page discrepancy, not otherwise significant.

**Figures 64–79** (Section 6, Key Sites, and Appendix 1) are **significantly out of sync**
with the actual body:

1. **Every figure number in this list is one lower than its actual printed caption** from
   "Key Sites Map" through "KS6" — e.g. this list's "Figure 64. Key Sites Map" is actually
   captioned **"Figure 65: Key Sites Map"** in the body (confirmed by direct visual inspection
   of p.99 — see `31-key-sites-overview.md`). The same off-by-one applies to KS1 (list: 65,
   body: 66), KS2 (list: 66, body: 67), the Harold Park photo (list: 67, body: 68), KS3 (list:
   68, body: 69), KS4 (list: 69, body: 70), KS5 (list: 70, body: 71), and KS6 (list: 71, body:
   72).
2. **This list includes a "Figure 72: Adaptive reuse by 6 degree architects, Bang Bang,
   Elsternwick" that does not appear to exist anywhere in the actual Section 6 body** as
   converted in this session (files 31–40, covering every page from 96 to 112 — no such image
   or caption was found). This phantom entry is likely why the numbering resynchronises: it
   supplies the "extra" figure needed to bridge the -1 offset above back to matching numbers
   from KS7 onward — this list's Figure 73 (KS7) does match the body's actual "Figure 73"
   caption (confirmed, p.107), and every figure number from KS7 through Figure 78 matches the
   body exactly.
3. **Every page number for Figures 64–78 in this list is roughly 8 pages lower than the actual
   page** (e.g. this list's "p.91" for the Key Sites Map vs the actual p.99; "p.104" for
   Figure 78 vs the actual p.112). This is consistent with content having been added earlier
   in the chapter (most likely within Section 5, which this conversion found to be dense and
   possibly extended) after this Figures List was originally compiled, shifting Section 6
   forward by about 8 pages without the list being regenerated.
4. **Figure 79 has no page number at all** in this list (confirmed by visual inspection,
   trailing off after "setbacks." with no number) — it is actually on **p.117**, in Appendix 1
   (see `41-appendix-1-definitions.md`).

**Net effect for anyone using this list to navigate**: for Figures 1–63, the list is reliable
enough to use directly. For Figures 64 onward (all of Section 6 and Appendix 1), **use the
page and figure numbers found in this conversion's own files (31–41) instead of this
Appendix's list** — they were independently confirmed against the actual rendered pages,
whereas this list was not.
