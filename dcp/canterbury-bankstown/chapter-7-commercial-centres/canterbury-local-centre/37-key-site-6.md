## KS-6 — 146-150 Canterbury Road

> Part of Section 6 Key Sites. See [31-key-sites-overview.md](31-key-sites-overview.md) for
> the Objectives and General Controls applicable to all Key Sites, and the Key Sites Map.
> Located in the Canterbury East character area — see
> [08-canterbury-east.md](08-canterbury-east.md), which already identifies this site as "a
> second key site... at the corner of Canterbury Road and Church Street" with "a mix of
> strata and heritage".

### Vision

This triangular site establishes an architectural landmark at the northern gateway of
Canterbury while retaining the heritage listed police station along Canterbury Road and
providing a softer interface to the residential area south and East to the site.

### Design Principles

**P1.** Integrate the heritage item in the development by locating the primary building
envelope along Church Street rather than Canterbury Road. This creates opportunities for
landscaped courtyards framing the heritage building, enhancing and preserving its view.

**P2.** Provide upper storey setbacks and articulation to the tower and podium to address its
prominent location at the northern gateway to the Local Centre and reduce visual bulk to the
residential area to the east and the south.

**P3.** Deliver high quality landscape treatment that integrates the heritage item into the
development and enhances its amenity.

**P4.** Provide a 3m planted buffer setback along the southern edge

**P5.** Retain and Integrate the existing historic shop at 154 Canterbury Road into the
development. Together with the heritage item they create a cluster of historical buildings on
the south-western corner of the site where a network of character filled courtyards and
alleys leverages the historical built legacy of the precinct.

**P6.** Deliver high quality landscape treatment that connects the old and the new buildings
together, emphasizing the historic nature of the site in the design, lighting and the choice
of materials.

**P7.** Provide for appropriate adaptive reuse and restoration of the police station building
as part of any new redevelopment

**P8.** The degree of retention of the heritage listed police station building is to be
confirmed by a heritage assessment to be undertaken as part of any future redevelopment of the
site.
