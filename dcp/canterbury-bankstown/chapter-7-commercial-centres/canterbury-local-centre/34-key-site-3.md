## KS-3 — 153-189 Canterbury Road

> Part of Section 6 Key Sites. See [31-key-sites-overview.md](31-key-sites-overview.md) for
> the Objectives and General Controls applicable to all Key Sites, and the Key Sites Map.
> Located in the Canterbury Core character area — see
> [06-canterbury-core.md](06-canterbury-core.md).

### Vision

A triangulated block with address to Canterbury Road, Jeffrey Street and Pierson and Aldi
Lane. This KS is comprised of two-story heritage shopfronts that provide character, scale and
memory of Canterbury's past.

### Design Principles

**P1.** Sensitively retain and integrate the historic shopfronts on Canterbury Road.

**P2.** Enhance the public domain and activate the lane ways.

**P3.** Provide a podium design that is sympathetic to the historic facades in scale,
materials and finishes.

**P4.** Provide upper storey setbacks above the historic shopfronts to provide separation
between the old and the new buildings. Provide greenery in this triangular setback.

**P5.** Establish two separate towers, one along Jeffrey Street and the other on Aldi Lane and
Canterbury Road to minimise bulk, optimise ventilation and solar access to buildings and
public space and preserve views to the sky from the public domain and from towers themselves.

**P6.** Create a through site link or an arcade within the building footprint from 173-175
Canterbury Road into Pierson lane.
