## KS-1 — 59A King Street, 186-206 Canterbury

> Part of Section 6 Key Sites. See [31-key-sites-overview.md](31-key-sites-overview.md) for
> the Objectives and General Controls applicable to all Key Sites, and the Key Sites Map.
> Located in the Canterbury North character area — see
> [07-canterbury-north.md](07-canterbury-north.md).

A street block in single ownership bounded by King, James, Searle and Princess Streets located
adjacent to the Canterbury Park Racecourse and Canterbury Park. This block benefits from a 6m
'lane way' between the lots fronting King Street and Searle Street.

### Vision

A green, leafy, cluster of medium rise residential apartments set in landscape, with a
diversity of apartment typologies that have access to a network of generous open spaces
connected throughout the site.

### Design Principles

**P1.** Enhance amenity, privacy and local character with a 6m landscaped street setback to
King Street, James Street and Princess Street that includes provision for deep soil planting
and large trees.

**P2.** Provide a 3m front setback along Searle Street to create an intimate green character.

**P3.** Utilise the existing north-south 'laneway' to create communal pedestrian connections
across the block.

**P4.** Retain the significant trees and create a rear green buffer across sites and green
heart to enhance amenity and habitat.

**P5.** Provide a variety of height and scale across the site, including lower scale at the
rear along Searle Street, and provide a transition in building bulk to the senior citizen
centre.
