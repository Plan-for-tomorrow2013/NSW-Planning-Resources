Public space includes streets, squares, parks, through site links and laneways. Public
spaces are the enduring elements of a city or place which people share as social and cultural
space. These civic elements form the framework for the Local Centre and work in cooperation
with the built form interface on private land to enliven and create a unique place inclusive
to all people. To ensure for a high-quality functioning Local Centre, a public domain
framework is essential, working together with the built form interface to create engagement,
interest and elevating the pedestrian experience.

> A humane city—with carefully designed streets, squares and parks creates pleasure for those
> who live, work and play there every day, as well as visitors and passers-by. Everyone should
> have the right to easily accessible open spaces, just as they have the right to clean water.
> Well-designed neighbourhoods inspire people to live in them. — Richard Rogers.
