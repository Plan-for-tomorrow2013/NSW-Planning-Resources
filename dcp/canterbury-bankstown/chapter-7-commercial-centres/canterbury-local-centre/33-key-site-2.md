## KS-2 — 73-87 Jeffrey Street and 67-71A Jeffrey Street

> Part of Section 6 Key Sites. See [31-key-sites-overview.md](31-key-sites-overview.md) for
> the Objectives and General Controls applicable to all Key Sites, and the Key Sites Map.
> Located in the Canterbury Core character area — see
> [06-canterbury-core.md](06-canterbury-core.md). This is the Key Site that delivers
> Canterbury Square (see Principle P10 in `06-canterbury-core.md`).

### Vision

The civic and vibrant heart of the Local Centre. These two sites address Jeffrey Street South
and have a common boundary with a newly created Jeffrey Lane. This development will create a
new 1,500sqm public plaza, Canterbury Square, that will activate the heart of the Local Centre
and extend Pierson Lane to the northern edge of 61-17A Jeffrey Street. The existing Jeffrey
Lane will be closed off. The Square will be reinforced by high quality buildings that will set
a benchmark for design excellence within the Local Centre and be a public space to be enjoyed
by the community.

### Design Principles

**P1.** Deliver a high quality civic square of at least 1,500m² of public open space to anchor
the development as a new civic hub for the Local Centre that works in cooperation with the new
civic high street, Robert Street.

Provide a minimum of 2 hours solar access to 50% of the area of the square at the winter
solstice on the 21st June.

> **Cross-reference/anomaly**: this page dates the winter solstice as **21 June**, while
> Table 12 (Sun Angles during Winter Solstice, `29-sustainability-urban-cooling.md`) is
> captioned "21 July". Confirmed by visual inspection on both pages — this is a genuine
> internal inconsistency in the source about which date is used as "the winter solstice"
> (astronomically, in the Southern Hemisphere it falls around 21 June, so this page's date is
> the more likely correct one). Flagged here and cross-referenced from the Section 5.4 file;
> not resolved by this conversion.

**P2.** Extend Pierson Lane to the northern edge of 61-71A Jeffrey Street to replace the
existing Jeffrey Lane. This will remove vehicle circulation from the northern edge of the
square.

**P3.** Relocate the existing supermarket underground to maximise active uses at street level.

**P4.** Activate Canterbury Square on Jeffrey Street South and Pierson Lane, where possible
with fine grain shopfronts.

**P6.** Mitigate urban heat gain in the design and material selection for the square. Use
permeable paving, water features, misters and lush planting.

**P7.** Have a higher ratio of solid to void in building facades in response to Canterbury's
heritage character.

**P8.** Select materials and finishes that have longevity, minimal maintenance and are robust.
Reinforce the heritage character in Canterbury using face brickwork and masonry.

> **Source anomaly**: Design Principles skip directly from P4 to P6 — no P5 is printed
> (confirmed by visual inspection across pp.101–102). Preserved as printed rather than
> renumbered.

### Landscape Principles

**P1.** Ensure partnership with First Nations people in the planning and delivery of public
open space.

**P2.** Minimum 40% canopy coverage across the site.

**P3.** Minimum 50% of the open space must have a minimum of 2 hours of continuous solar
access.

**P4.** Minimum 50% of the site must be deep soil zone – ensure all deep soil zone are free
draining and not covered, all paving within deep soil zone should be permeable.

**P5.** Include a variety of active and passive recreational activity options.

**P6.** Include a playground area of minimum 350m².

**P7.** Include an open lawn area of minimum 500m².

**P8.** Arrange the space to allow direct, clear sightlines from surrounding areas into and
through the space as per CPTED design principles.

**P9.** Include public art as part of the design of the open space.
