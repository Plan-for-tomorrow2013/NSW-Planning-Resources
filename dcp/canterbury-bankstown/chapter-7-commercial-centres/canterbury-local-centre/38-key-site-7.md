## KS-7 — 17-23 Robert Street and 13 Broughton Street

> Part of Section 6 Key Sites. See [31-key-sites-overview.md](31-key-sites-overview.md) for
> the Objectives and General Controls applicable to all Key Sites, and the Key Sites Map.
> Located in the Canterbury Core character area — see
> [06-canterbury-core.md](06-canterbury-core.md). Works together with KS-8 (adjacent — see
> `39-key-site-8.md`) to form the Robert Street civic street frontage.

### Vision

This significant site is a key component to the vitality of the civic areas of the Local
Centre and is located at the corner of Robert and Broughton Streets adjacent to the station.
Robert Street is the primary civic street and works together with Jeffrey Street South and
Square to form the Local Centre's core. Taller slender building will act as landmarks within
the core which will be read from inside and outside of the Local Centre.

### Design Principles

**P1.** The site forms part of a civic street wall on the northern side of Robert Street.

**P2.** Activate the ground floor with fine grain shopfronts on both Robert and Broughton
Street frontages and provide an engaging urban environment for visitors and residents.

**P3.** Provide a 3m street setback at the frontage to Robert Street to widen the street and
establish well designed high amenity public domain to support the commercial activity of the
shopfronts and the movement of pedestrians in the Local Centre.

**P4.** Locate vehicle access and services on the secondary frontage, Broughton Street, away
from Robert Street and the corner.

**P5.** Create a place-based design that has been considered from all facades and responds to
orientation with special attention given to the architectural treatment of the corner, which
is visible from the station, to act as a cue in navigating to Robert Street.

**P6.** Create a slender, simply articulated tower design and 3 storey street wall to enhance
solar exposure and amenity to Robert Street.

**P7.** Establish at a minimum, a 3m wide deep soil planting zone along the western boundary
capable of supporting mature tree growth to interface with the R4 residential zone.

**P8.** Provide high quality landscape design to the ground floor and podium rooftop
reflecting Connecting to Country principles, enhancing local amenity, establishing habitat, and
supporting passive cooling of the environment.

**P9.** Provide a high quality communal rooftop that has planting that responds to climate and
provides shade for users.
