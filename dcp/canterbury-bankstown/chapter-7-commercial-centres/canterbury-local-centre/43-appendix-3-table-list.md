### Source anomaly, confirmed by visual inspection

**This list stops at Table 8 and omits Tables 9 through 12 entirely**, even though all four
exist in the chapter body and are cited by cross-reference elsewhere in the chapter:

| Table (not in this list) | Caption | Page (confirmed by this conversion) |
|---|---|---|
| 09 | Shading Requirements | 90 |
| 10 | Maximum External Surface Reflection | 90 |
| 11 | Sun Angles during Summer Solstice | 90 |
| 12 | Sun Angles during Winter Solstice | 91 |

See `29-sustainability-urban-cooling.md` for these four tables in full, along with the related
anomaly where Controls C1/C2 of Section 5.4.2 cross-reference "Table 7" and "Table 8" for
shading percentages that don't actually match either this Table List or the tables those
control numbers point to elsewhere in the chapter (Table 7 is Active street frontage design
guidance, and Table 8 is Car parking requirements — neither is about shading). The actual
shading-percentage table is Table 9 (Parts A and B).

This is the same pattern found in Appendix 2 (Figures List, see
`42-appendix-2-figures-list.md`): appendix summary lists in this chapter that were compiled
before some later content (Tables 9–12, and the extra ~8 pages that appear before Section 6)
was added, and never regenerated to match.
