## KS-5 — 214-224 Canterbury Road and 2-8 Close Street

> Part of Section 6 Key Sites. See [31-key-sites-overview.md](31-key-sites-overview.md) for
> the Objectives and General Controls applicable to all Key Sites, and the Key Sites Map.
> Located in the Canterbury South character area — see
> [09-canterbury-south.md](09-canterbury-south.md), which already discusses this site as
> "Key site 5"/"Key Site-5".

### Vision

These sites are located directly south of the train line and are bounded by Canterbury Road,
Close Street and future open space to the east. The vision for KS-5 is to create buildings
that respond to the natural environment and reference connection to the Sugar House heritage
item further to the east along the Cooks River pathway.

### Design Principles

**P1.** Provide a pedestrian through site link along the northern edge of the site from
Canterbury Road to link to the future open space and to the Cooks River Pathway.

**P2.** Provide a sensitive interface with the residential strata building at 226 Canterbury
Road that transitions and reduces bulk and ensures for solar access.

**P3.** Buildings should be designed so windows and balconies provide surveillance of the
parkland.

**P4.** Have greater solid to void ratios in facades to reflect Canterbury's heritage
character.

**P5.** Provide active edges along Canterbury Road and active street frontage to the future
open space.

**P6.** As building are visible from the open space and from Campsie Centre, particular
consideration should be provided to the form and design as viewed from the wider context.

**P7.** Provide a minimum of 3m green interface setback along the boundary with adjacent
sites.

**P8.** Access and service should be located along Close Street.

**P9.** Activate Canterbury with fine grain tenancies on the ground floor.

**P10.** Coordinate with TFNSW prior ot the lodgement of a DA to explore opportunities to
provide servicing to the potential reuse of the heritage listed signal box along the northern
boundary of the site.
