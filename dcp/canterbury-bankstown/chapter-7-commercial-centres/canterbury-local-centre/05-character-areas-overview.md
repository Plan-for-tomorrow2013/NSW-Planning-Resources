The Local Centre comprises four distinct character areas and a network of key streets and
lanes, each with specific existing and desired future qualities. Applicants must identify
the character area of their development site and demonstrate how their proposal responds to
both the surrounding context and the future vision for that area and its street interface. A
context and site analysis plan must be submitted with any Development Application to support
this.

A site analysis plan that demonstrates a context and site analysis is required with a
Development Application to demonstrate understanding of the manner a site integrates with the
desired character area.

The Local Centre is comprised of key streets with different desired future characters. The
hierarchy, typology and character of streets will provide guidance as to how the building
interface should be designed. As part of a Development Application, applicants must be
cognisant of the desired future character of the street interface.
