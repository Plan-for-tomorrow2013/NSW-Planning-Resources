## KS-8 — 9-15 Robert Street

> Part of Section 6 Key Sites. See [31-key-sites-overview.md](31-key-sites-overview.md) for
> the Objectives and General Controls applicable to all Key Sites, and the Key Sites Map.
> Located in the Canterbury Core character area — see
> [06-canterbury-core.md](06-canterbury-core.md). Works together with KS-7 (adjacent — see
> `38-key-site-7.md`) to provide the Robert Street civic street frontage.

### Vision

This mid-block site is at the heart of Robert Street and has the potential to set a benchmark
for design quality for the Local Centre. This site works together with KS-7 to provide a
civic street frontage activated by fine grained shopfronts. Uses may include hospitality,
commercial and retail at street level with residential uses above to activates the Centre
Core. The site will deliver a service lane on the eastern boundary to ensure the Robert Street
frontage has optimal activation and pedestrian safety and that waste servicing will occur
behind the development.

### Design Principles

**P1.** Ground floor activation with fine grain shopfronts along Robert Street which turns the
corner onto the service lane, Robert Lane, creates a destination and attractions to the new
high street.

**P2.** A 3m street setback and public domain improvement delivers a high amenity pedestrian
friendly environment supporting the vibrancy of the street.

**P3.** Establish at a minimum, a 3m wide deep soil planting zone along the western boundary
capable of supporting mature tree growth to interface with the R4 residential zone.

**P4.** High quality materials and finishes reminiscent of Canterbury heritage to be utilised
for the podium, retails shopfronts and public domain. Face brickwork and masonry is
recommended.

**P5.** Slender built form and a 3 storey street wall enhances solar exposure and amenity to
Robert Street, with a 3m upper-level setback along Robert Street and 2m upper-level setback
along the new laneway.

**P6.** A 9m wide two-way service lane will be delivered along the site's eastern boundary. It
will service the development and ensure the Robert Street frontages remain dedicated to
commercial activity with high amenity. In the future the lane will extend as a pedestrian lane
up to John Street

**P7.** Provide high quality landscape design to the podium rooftop reflecting connection to
country principles, enhancing local amenity, establishing habitat, and supporting passive
cooling of the urban environment.
