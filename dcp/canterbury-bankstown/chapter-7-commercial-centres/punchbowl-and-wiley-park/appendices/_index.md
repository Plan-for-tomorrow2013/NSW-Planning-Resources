### Why these matter beyond a plain reference list

The Figures List and Table list are not just reference material — they are the
**authoritative resolution** for a figure/table numbering inconsistency that recurs
throughout Sections 4 and 5 of this chapter, where several figures and tables are printed
with small "page-local" caption numbers that don't match the chapter-wide numbers used
in the accompanying Control text's cross-references. See `02-figures-list.md` and
`03-table-list.md` for the full reconciliation tables mapping each affected figure/table to its
correct chapter-wide number and the file it was converted in.

## Figures not converted (image only)

Every figure referenced across Sections 1–6 of this chapter is image-only content (maps,
axonometric massing views, street sections, design-guidance diagrams) and has not been
converted. Each section file notes its own figures inline (`> See Figure...`) and lists them
under a `## Figures` heading. The complete authoritative list of all 57 figures, with page
numbers, is in `02-figures-list.md`.
