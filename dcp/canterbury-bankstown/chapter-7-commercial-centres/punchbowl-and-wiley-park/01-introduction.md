# Section 1 — Introduction

## 1.1 Application of this Chapter

This Chapter of the Canterbury-Bankstown DCP 2023, (CBDCP 2023), supports the
Canterbury-Bankstown Local Environmental Plan 2023, (LEP), by providing objectives,
principles and development controls to enhance the functionality, design and amenity of
development within the Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct, (also referred to as 'the
Precinct' in this Chapter).

The Precinct is comprised of two Village Centres, Punchbowl and Wiley Park, as noted in
Canterbury Bankstown Council's Local Strategic Planning Statement.

This Chapter of the CBDCP 2023 applies to all development on the land within the
Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct as identified in Figure 3.

Other Chapters, as relevant, of the CBDCP 2023 must also be addressed as part of a
Development Application (DA).

**Where there is an inconsistency between the controls in this Chapter and other Chapters
of the CBDCP 2023, this chapter prevails to the extent of the inconsistency with the
exception of Chapter 4 Heritage.**

## 1.2 Vision for Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct

### Vision

The Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct will build on the strong cultural and community
foundation of its Village Centres. The dynamic multi-cultural character instilled by multiple
places of worship and schools will be enhanced by the addition of further population
density and housing choice.

At their heart, these Village Centres will offer a diverse and culturally rich mix of services
and amenities in a highly walkable and comfortable environment, with access to new Metro
stations. Punchbowl and Wiley Park will have safe and leafy connections for children and
families to access important urban and social services including schools, places of worship,
retail and hospitality venues and new and enhanced open space.

Punchbowl will be a thriving Village Centre, with a web of retail, hospitality, services and
community uses.

Corner shops at Wattle Street will bookmark the northern gateway to Punchbowl to draw
visitors in from Greenacre along a tree-lined Highclere Avenue.

The Boulevarde will continue to be a focal point for the Village Centre, vibrant and walkable,
with direct access to the new Metro station and a new civic plaza for community activities
and events.

Development will rejuvenate underutilised sites including the former baby health clinic and
the Rossmore Avenue car park to reinforce and build on the energy of the Village Centre.

Wiley Park will have a new village heart that provides everyday services and amenities,
attracting people to visit and linger. An expanded Village Centre will focus retail activation
off busy King Georges Road in quieter pockets along Lakemba Street and Stanlea Parade.

Canterbury Road will continue to be an important vehicle connection providing services
and employment opportunities for the local area. An improved streetscape with widened
footpaths and enhanced landscaping will transform the character of the corridor.

A neighbourhood centre on Wattle Street will bookmark the northern gateway to the Village
Centre to draw visitors in from Greenacre along a tree-lined Highclere Avenue.

New buildings will be well-designed, in a way that provides sensitive interfaces and
transitions between high-rise and low-rise areas, ensuring that new urban areas retain a
human scale and community feel.

## 1.3 How to read this DCP

This DCP Chapter is structured by a hierarchy of criteria encompassing objectives,
principles and controls. The intent is to provide clear, direct and informative development
controls and design guidance for all new development in the Punchbowl and Wiley Park
Precinct.

When addressing the criteria, it is important to consider the purpose of each step, as
outlined below. Collectively, these elements create a comprehensive framework that will
enable the Precinct to deliver high quality renewal opportunities that align with the Vision
for the Precinct.

- **Objectives** — These are the 'why' we have the principles and controls in place.
- **Principles** — These are the 'what' we want statements. They provide clear direction as to what should be done, without saying how to do it. How these are achieved comes down to individual site circumstances, design and merit.
- **Controls** — These state to the applicant 'how' to achieve the objectives and principles. They are clear and measurable standards to guide development.
