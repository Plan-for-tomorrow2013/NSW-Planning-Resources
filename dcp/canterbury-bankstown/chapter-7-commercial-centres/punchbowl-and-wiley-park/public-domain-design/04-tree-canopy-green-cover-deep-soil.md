# Section 3.4 — Tree Canopy, Green Cover and Deep Soil

Trees are essential for their contribution to the amenity and character of the Precinct. When
appropriately selected, located, planted and maintained, trees provide a multitude of
benefits to the urban environment including reducing heat gain, providing shade and
mitigating climate change.

One of the most effective solutions to mitigate urban heat gain is expanding tree canopy
cover. Trees provide shade, cool the environment through transpiration and reduce surface
temperature in densely built areas, where heat can become trapped by concrete and
asphalt. Temperature differences between locations with canopy and without canopy gaps
can be significant.

Green cover provides further greening and shade to buildings and to the site itself. This will
protect buildings from radiant heat gain and provide a pleasing aesthetic to the public
domain.

Deep soil is 'landscaped area'. These areas of soil are not covered by buildings or structures.
Deep soil provides for the planting of canopy trees, increases water filtration and reduces
stormwater runoff. It is beneficial to co-locate deep soil with communal open space to
provide shade and amenity for those using these spaces.

## Objectives

- **O1** To provide tree canopy for shade to maximise amenity to public space during the periods of the day they are most used for recreation or repose.
- **O2** To improve and enhance environmental biodiversity and mitigate heat at ground level.
- **O3** To ensure for maximum tree canopy in development on private land and public land.
- **O4** To create a continuous tree canopy, mitigate climate change and to improve the health and wellbeing of people and ecosystems.
- **O5** To provide green cover to soften building form, create amenity for occupants and provide for a green aesthetic to the public domain.
- **O6** To maximise canopy coverage by bundling or undergrounding aerial power cables.
- **O7** To provide deep soil to increase tree canopy, landscape, water infiltration and the reduction of stormwater runoff.

## Principles

- **P1** Ensure existing trees are protected and that additional trees are planted in the public and private domains.
- **P2** Provide tree planting appropriate to the local climate.
- **P3** Provide for recreational equipment, seating, landscaping, absorptive ground treatments and an integrated consistent material palette for hard landscaped areas.
- **P4** Ensure for adequate deep soil depth and width for the growth of large trees, shrubs and vegetation generally.
- **P5** In new development ensure for articulated facades for green cover. In alterations to existing buildings, provide fixed planting and rooftop greenery.

## Controls

- **C1** As part of a Development Application for residential, mixed use or non-residential development, street trees are to be provided to all public frontages to the satisfaction of Council.
- **C2** New street trees must have a minimum pot size of 75 litres but no greater than 200 litres.
- **C3** Street trees should be planted with clearances as set out in Table 29 below.
- **C4** A Public Frontage Plan (PFP) shall be prepared prior to the issue of a Construction Certificate. This plan will identify:
  (a) All frontages of the development site between the gutter and the building line, including the footpath, drainage, entries and other built objects.
  (b) Any existing trees, bus shelters, signage and other structures or services that may impede the planting of new street trees.
  (c) A qualified landscape architect shall prepare the PFP and specify the size in litres, the spacing and species in coordination with Council. The PFP must be prepared after test pits have been completed to ensure for services coordination.
- **C5** Trees will be spaced depending on their size: small – 5–7m, medium 7–10m and large 10–15m.
- **C6** Please refer to tree base treatments in the Appendices.
- **C7** Minimum tree canopy cover is required on private land as per Table 30 below and Figure 29.
- **C8** Tree canopy cover refers to the layer of leaves, branches, and stems of trees that cover the ground when viewed from above, expressed as proportion of the total site area, calculated in accordance with the following:
  (a) Canopy area is to include the expected mature size, (crown spread at maturity), of existing and proposed trees.
  (b) Only trees with a minimum crown spread of 3m in diameter are to be included. Trees with a crown spread less than 3m diameter are to be excluded from tree canopy cover calculations.
  (c) Any overlap in canopy area is to be counted once.
  (d) Canopy area extending outside of site boundaries is to be excluded.
  (e) Canopy area extending within a development site from trees planted in adjoining sites is to be excluded.
  (f) Trees located over structures, including podiums and rooftops, are to be included.
- **C9** The design of the canopy trees should be laid out such that sufficient room is provided for the trees to achieve their desired mature size. Canopy overlap should not exceed 25%.
- **C10** New development within the E1 Local Centre zone, must deliver 50% of the site area as 'green cover' which can be any combination of the following:
  - Deep soil zones;
  - Green walls where orientation is appropriate;
  - Layered planting on structure (including small and medium trees, as per the Apartment Design Guide);
  - Green roofs and awnings; or
  - Permanent planting on balconies and podiums, (including built-in planter boxes).

## Table 29 — Street tree planting clearances

| Streetscape element | Indicative minimum clearance |
|---|---|
| Street intersection (approach side) | 10m from intersection kerb line |
| Street intersection (non-approach side) | 7m from intersection kerb line |
| Traffic signals | >10m from signal pole on approach side |
| Bus stops | 3m |
| Pedestrian crossings (approach side) | 10m |
| Pedestrian crossings (non-approach side) | 7m |
| Shared lanes | 1m |
| Driveways (approach side) | 3m |
| Driveways (non-approach side) | 2m |
| Stormwater inlet/outlet | 2m |
| Street light pole | 3m |
| Underground service pit | 1m |
| Cycleways | 0.5m |

## Table 30 — Minimum tree canopy cover for private land

| Zone | Type of development | Minimum canopy coverage* |
|---|---|---|
| Within E1 Local Centre Zone | Non residential | 5% |
| Within E1 Local Centre Zone | Mixed use including residential | 5% |
| Within R2 Low Density Residential Zone, R3 Medium Density Residential Zone and R4 High Density Residential Zone | Residential development | 20% |

*Whether on structure or deep soil.
