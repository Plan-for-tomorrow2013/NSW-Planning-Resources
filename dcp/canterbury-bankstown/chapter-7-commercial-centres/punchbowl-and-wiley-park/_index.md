## Chapter 7.7 — Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct

Standalone, area-specific DCP chapter for the Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct — issued **Amendment 14, dated April 2026** ("Final", title page also reads March 2026 — see note below). Replaces the former Section 10 (Punchbowl Village Centre) and Section 12 (Wiley Park Village Centre) of Chapter 7.3 City East, which the current 7.3 Contents page explicitly marks "(Replaced by Chapter 7.7)" and which no longer contain any body text in the 7.3 PDF (see `../city-east/_index.md`). 132 pages: Understanding Place, Public Domain Design, Building Design, General Provisions, 6 named Key Sites, and Appendices.
