## Chapter 7.7 — Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct

Standalone, area-specific DCP chapter for the Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct — issued
**Amendment 14, dated April 2026** ("Final", title page also reads March 2026 — date
discrepancy noted, not resolved, same pattern as the 7.6 discrepancy). Replaces the former
Section 10 (Punchbowl Village Centre) and Section 12 (Wiley Park Village Centre) of Chapter
7.3 City East, which the current 7.3 Contents page explicitly marks "(Replaced by Chapter
7.7)" and which no longer contain any body text in the 7.3 PDF (see `../city-east/_index.md`).

### Clause 1.1 precedence wording — confirmed

Clause 1.1 (Application of this Chapter) carries the same precedence wording as Chapters
7.5 and 7.6: **"this chapter prevails to the extent of the inconsistency with the exception of
Chapter 4 Heritage."** This resolves the item the original scaffold flagged as "not yet
directly confirmed — only the Contents page has been read." See `01-introduction.md`.

Note also: Section 6.1's general key-site Control C9 states a second, narrower precedence
rule specific to key sites ("these provisions prevail to the extent of any inconsistency") that
is **not** qualified with the Chapter 4 Heritage carve-out found in Clause 1.1 — flagged in
`key-sites/01-key-sites-overview.md` rather than assumed to inherit Clause 1.1's exception.

### Suburb split in Section 2 vs Section 6 — confirmed

Per the scaffold's question about whether Section 2 (Understanding Place) and Section 6
(Key Sites) distinguish Wiley Park and Punchbowl as separate areas: they do so
**differently**. Section 6's six key sites split cleanly by suburb (KS-1–3 in Wiley Park, KS-4–6
in Punchbowl). Section 2's six character areas split two ways at once — two are named for a
Village Centre (Punchbowl Village, Wiley Park Village), three are named for a built-form
typology recurring across both suburbs (Urban/Mid-rise/Low-rise living), and one is a
corridor connecting both (Canterbury Road) — so a single suburb's sites fall into multiple,
differently-shaped character areas rather than one area per suburb. See
`understanding-place/_index.md`.

### Anomalies found during conversion

- **Section 2.2** claims "seven character areas have been identified" but only six are ever
  named, numbered or mapped anywhere in the chapter (2.2.1–2.2.6) — not resolved.
- **Section 3.2** has two Principles printed as lettered "(f)"/"(g)" with no preceding
  "(a)"–"(e)" — likely a drafting error, preserved as printed.
- **Section 3.3**'s Controls jump from C3 (with its own lettered sub-items a–h) straight to
  C9 — Controls C4–C8 do not exist anywhere in the source, confirmed against page
  images.
- **Figure and table numbering** is inconsistent from Section 4.2.3 onward: several
  figures/tables carry small "page-local" in-page captions (e.g. "Figure 4", "Table 51")
  that don't match the chapter-wide numbers used in Control cross-references and in
  the chapter's own official Figures List/Table list (Section 7.2/7.3). **Fully reconciled** —
  see `appendices/02-figures-list.md` and `appendices/03-table-list.md` for complete
  mapping tables, and `building-design/_index.md` / `general-provisions/_index.md` for
  the folder-level summaries.
- **Section 6 numbering**: the chapter's own Contents page numbers KS-4/5/6 as
  6.6/6.7/6.8 (skipping "6.5"), while the body headings for those same sections read
  6.5/6.6/6.7 with no gap, and KS-4's Contents-page title includes "Existing" where the
  body heading omits it. This repo follows the internally-consistent body-heading
  numbering — see `key-sites/_index.md` and `key-sites/05-ks4-punchbowl-childrens-centre.md`.
- **Section 5.4.2** (Reduction of the Urban Heat Island Effect): the Summer Solstice sun
  angle table has two rows both labelled "Northeast / Southeast ± 22.5°"; the Winter
  Solstice table has two rows both labelled "Northwest ± 22.5°" — both confirmed
  genuinely printed that way in the source via page image inspection, not an extraction
  artefact.
- Numerous small apparent dropped words/typos across Sections 4–7 (e.g. "suit[e]s",
  "as apart of the sites re-development", "The Boulevard[e]") — each flagged individually
  in its own file's Notes section, preserved verbatim rather than silently corrected.

### Sections deferring to other chapters of the wider CBDCP 2023

- Section 2.2.5 (Low-rise living character area) and Section 4.2.4 Control C2(a) (front
  setbacks for "Area 1" land) both defer to **Chapter 5 Residential Accommodation**
  rather than setting their own numeric setback controls.
- Section 5.2 Control C1/C4 and Section 3.3's stormwater Control C3(e) defer to
  **"Chapter 3.2 – Parking"** and **"Section 3 – Stormwater Drainage Systems"**
  respectively of the wider CBDCP 2023.
- Several Objectives/Controls reference external NSW state instruments not converted
  here: BASIX, the Apartment Design Guide (ADG), NABERS, the WELS Scheme, the
  Building Code of Australia, and SEPP (Transport and Infrastructure) 2021.
