### Anomalies found in this section

- **5.4.2**: Control text cross-references "Table 7/8/9/10" while the printed table captions
  read "Table 51/52/53/54" — same numbering inconsistency pattern as Section 4. Also,
  the Winter Solstice sun-angle table (Table 10) lists two rows both labelled "Northwest ±
  22.5°" rather than five distinct orientations.
- **5.4.4**, Control C5: refers to "energy target achievement" in a Water Efficiency
  subsection — likely copy-pasted from the equivalent Energy Efficiency clause (5.4.3,
  Control C4) without updating "energy" to "water". Preserved verbatim.
- Several minor apparent dropped words across 5.1–5.4.6 (e.g. "suit[e]s", "consultant
  [that] is to be submitted", "build[ing] roof") — each flagged in its own file's Notes
  section rather than silently corrected.
