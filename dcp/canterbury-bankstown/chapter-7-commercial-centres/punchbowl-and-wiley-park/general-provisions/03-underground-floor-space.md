# Section 5.3 — Underground Floor Space

## Objectives

- **O1** To facilitate large floorplate uses underground while maximising activation and permeability at street level.

## Principles

- **P1** Uses permitted for underground floor space generally require large floor areas, including neighbourhood supermarkets, larger floorplate retail premises over 1000m² GFA, gym and entertainment facilities.
- **P2** Larger sites can accommodate the above mentioned uses as long as it is demonstrated with improved streetscape and public domain impacts.
- **P3** The underground uses should not expand beyond the building footprint to ensure that deep soil planting and open space in setback areas are maintained.
- **P4** Entries to the underground floor space should have an appropriate, active, ground floor frontage.

## Controls

None printed for this subsection — Objectives and Principles only.
