# Section 4.2.1 — Building Height and Storeys

The maximum building heights in the Precinct are stipulated in the CBLEP 2023. This
section of the DCP provides further guidance on the bulk, scale and design of buildings,
ensuring for careful consideration of impacts on streets, shared lanes, open space and
pedestrian links.

> See Figure 33: Design guidance for the bulk, scale and design of tall buildings — not converted, image only. Callouts: 1 Slender, elegant towers well separated; 1 Height and scale responds to character and context; 2 Minimise wind impacts for pedestrians in the public domain; 3 Solar access and shade for amenity and plantings; 4 (uncaptioned fourth callout).

## Objectives

- **O1** To design taller buildings that allow for adequate provision of sunlight to streets and open space for pedestrian amenity and to allow trees to grow.
- **O2** To design a distinctive and legible skyline to identify the Village Centres and Metro stations.
- **O3** To ensure buildings are designed in consideration of wind impacts to the public domain.
- **O4** To design elegant slender towers that have consideration for view sharing.
- **O5** To ensure taller buildings express a strong architectural response defined by a clearly identified concept or idea to create a sense of place.
- **O6** To provide for landmark towers on prominent corners to improve the legibility of the Precinct.

## Controls

- **C1** A wind impact assessment is required for:
  (a) Buildings over 25m in height.
  (b) Buildings without a podium on a corner site.
- **C2** Buildings without a podium are permitted on corner sites. In cases where a podium is not proposed, the building is to:
  (a) Be designed to mitigate down drafts to the public domain in accordance with a Wind Impact Assessment report;
  (b) Be designed 'in the round' (i.e. consideration of the design quality when being viewed from within the Precinct, from the street level, from within other buildings, from the street and from a distance); and
  (c) Provide a high quality ground plane for pedestrians at street level.
- **C3** Development must not exceed the number of storeys shown for the site on Figure 34 Maximum building height in storeys map.

## Principles

- **P1** Concentrate taller buildings around the Metro stations and civic areas to maximise housing provision in well located areas.
- **P2** Ensure that towers over 4 or 5 storeys are setback above a podium to mitigate bulk and dominance of the street.
- **P3** Ensure the height and scale of new developments are site specific and developed in response to key features of the site and setting.
- **P4** Manage wind conditions [to] promote outdoor planting, including green roofs, green cover and other landscaping elements as well as tables, chairs and other furniture used by building occupants on balconies and outdoor areas on buildings.

## Notes

- Principle P4 is printed in the source as "Manage wind conditions promote outdoor
  planting..." with an apparent missing word (likely "to") between "conditions" and
  "promote". Preserved verbatim with `[to]` inserted only in this note for readability; the
  clause text itself reproduces the source exactly, without silent correction.
- The numeric maximum building heights themselves are set by the CBLEP 2023 (Local
  Environmental Plan), not this DCP chapter; Figure 34 expresses those LEP heights
  converted to a storey count for DCP purposes.
