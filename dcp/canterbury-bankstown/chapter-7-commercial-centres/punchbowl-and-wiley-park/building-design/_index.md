## Section 4 — Building Design

This section focuses on the design of buildings and includes setbacks, street wall heights,
floor plate areas, streetscape activation, materials and finishes and open space and
landscaping. It is the largest and most technically dense section of Chapter 7.7, containing
the numeric building envelope controls (heights, setbacks, separation, floorplate limits)
that most directly govern development capacity.

### Figure and table numbering anomaly (chapter-wide issue, concentrated in this section)

From Section 4.2.3 onward, this chapter's figures and tables are captioned in the source PDF
with small, page-local numbers that restart independently of the chapter's earlier running
sequence (Figures 33–35 in 4.2.1–4.2.2), while cross-references within the *text* of Controls
sometimes cite a different, much higher number for the same figure (e.g. Control C1 in
Section 4.2.3 cites "Figure 38" for a map captioned "Figure 6"; Control C1 in Section 4.3.2
cites "Figure 47" for a map captioned "Figure 17"). This pattern recurs through Sections 4.2.3
–4.3.2 and has been preserved exactly as printed in each individual section file, with an
anomaly note in each affected file rather than silently reconciling the numbers. It is unclear
whether this reflects two different figure-numbering passes in the source document's
production (e.g. a page-local numbering used during drafting that was never fully
reconciled with a chapter-wide numbering used in cross-references) — not resolved.

A related instance: Table "16" (Section 4.3.2, Active street frontage design guidance)
occupies the position between Figure 15 and Figure 17 in the page sequence, suggesting
figures and tables may share one running number sequence in the source rather than each
having their own — also not resolved.

to **Chapter 5 Residential Accommodation** of the wider CBDCP 2023:

- Section 4.2.4 Front setbacks, Control C2(a), for land in "Area 1" (the low-rise living character area).
