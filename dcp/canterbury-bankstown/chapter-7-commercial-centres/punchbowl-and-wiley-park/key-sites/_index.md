## Section 6 — Key Sites

Six named key sites across the Precinct: three in Wiley Park (KS-1, KS-2, KS-3) and three in
Punchbowl (KS-4, KS-5, KS-6). Each key site subsection is Vision + Design Principles only —
none of the six individual key-site subsections contain their own numbered Objectives or
Controls; instead, the general Objectives (O1–O11) and Controls (C1–C9) in Section 6.1
apply to all six sites, layered with each site's own Design Principles.

### Numbering anomaly (Contents page vs. body headings)

This chapter's own Contents page (page 108, see `../_index.md`) numbers KS-4, KS-5 and
KS-6 as **6.6, 6.7 and 6.8** respectively (skipping "6.5" entirely) and titles KS-4 as "Existing
Punchbowl Children's Centre". The body headings actually printed at the start of each of
those three sections instead read **6.5, 6.6 and 6.7** (continuing directly on from 6.4 KS-3
with no gap), and KS-4's body heading omits "Existing" from the title. This repo follows the
internally-consistent **body heading numbering** (6.5–6.7) for file naming and front matter
across this folder, and documents the Contents page's different numbers/wording as
printed, without correcting the Contents page — see `05-ks4-punchbowl-childrens-centre.md`
for the full detail. This resolves (in the sense of documenting, not reconciling) the item the
original scaffold flagged as "Confirm on resuming" regarding this chapter's suburb split
across Section 6.

### Confirms the suburb split flagged in the original scaffold

Per the scaffold's note asking whether Section 6 (like Section 2) distinguishes Wiley Park
and Punchbowl as separate areas: **confirmed** — KS-1, KS-2 and KS-3 are all in Wiley Park;
KS-4, KS-5 and KS-6 are all in Punchbowl. The split is clean (no key site straddles both
suburbs), unlike Section 2's character areas, which cut across both suburbs by built-form
typology rather than by suburb boundary.
