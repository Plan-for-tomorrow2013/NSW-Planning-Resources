# Section 6.5 — KS-4 Punchbowl Children's Centre, Punchbowl

## Vision

The Punchbowl Children's Centre is located on Punchbowl Road near the Metro station and
Rest Park. It also has frontage to Urunga Parade at the rear of the site. The former Baby
Health Centre building at the north western corner of the site is heritage listed and will be
sympathetically retained and reused as part of future redevelopment. The key site will
deliver residential, retail and commercial uses along Punchbowl Road and Rest Park to
activate these edges.

## Design Principles

- **P1** A Heritage Conservation Management Plan should accompany the proposal to ensure the development is sensitive in bulk and scale, uses, landscaping, materials and the location of the new buildings do not negatively impact the heritage item.
- **P2** Deliver a tree lined street along Urunga Parade through the provision of new tree planting, landscaping and high quality paving.
- **P3** Protect the view corridor towards the former Baby Health Centre from the north-east and south-west directions along Punchbowl Road. Ensure all new buildings on the site are located behind the rear building line of the heritage item.
- **P4** Adaptively reuse the former Baby Health Centre building as a[]part of the site[']s re-development.
- **P5** Provide a landscaped buffer between the former Baby Health Centre and any future development of the site.
- **P6** Provide a retail ground level setback along Punchbowl Road and Rest Park featuring space for active uses including outdoor dining as well as landscaping and high quality paving, enhancing amenity, pedestrian movement and activation of the public domain.
- **P7** Create ground level apartments that interface with the Urunga Parade featuring private landscaping, deep soil and direct street access, enhancing amenity, access and passive surveillance of the public domain. Ensure rail noise is mitigated.
- **P8** Provide high quality active frontages along identified streets.
- **P9** Create a landmark tower along Urunga Parade, informed by its heritage context.
- **P10** Provide high quality landscape design on the rooftop reflecting Connecting with Country principles, enhancing local amenity, establishing habitat, and supporting passive cooling of the urban environment.

## No numbered Objectives or Controls

Like KS-1, KS-2 and KS-3, this key site has no separately numbered Objectives or Controls —
only a Vision statement and Design Principles (P1–P10). The general key-site Controls (C1–
C9) in Section 6.1 apply in addition, including the mandatory Heritage-sensitive design
approach reinforced here by P1, P3–P5 given the heritage-listed former Baby Health Centre
building on this site.

## Numbering and title anomaly

This section is headed **"6.5 KS-4..."** in the body text of the source PDF, and the section
title in the body omits the word "Existing" (reading just "Punchbowl Children's Centre,
Punchbowl"). This chapter's own Contents page (page 108), however, lists this same entry
as **"6.6 KS-4 Existing Punchbowl Children's Centre, Punchbowl"** — one section number
higher, and with "Existing" included in the title. The Contents page skips "6.5" entirely,
jumping from 6.4 (KS-3) to 6.6 (KS-4), while the body headings for KS-4, KS-5 and KS-6
themselves run 6.5, 6.6, 6.7 consistently and without gaps. This repo follows the **body
heading numbering** (6.5, 6.6, 6.7 for KS-4, KS-5, KS-6) as authoritative for file naming and
front matter, since it is internally consistent, and notes the Contents page's numbering and
wording as printed without correcting it there.
