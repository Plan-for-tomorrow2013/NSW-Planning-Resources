# Section 2.1 — Structure Plan

The Structure Plan provides an overview of the intended outcomes for the Punchbowl and
Wiley Park Precinct. The Structure Plan has been informed by analysis of the opportunities
and constraints of the Village Centres. These opportunities include desired land uses, built
form character, housing types, open space, streetscape improvements and pedestrian and
cycling connections.

Key elements of the Structure Plan consist of:

- Revitalised and vibrant Village Centres with active high streets.
- New and enhanced parks and plazas.
- New social infrastructure that supports a growing community.
- Green connections, shared lanes and pedestrian links that support walking, cycling and social interaction.
- Increased tree canopy and green cover to create cool, green and attractive neighbourhoods and to reduce urban heat gain.
- High-quality built form outcomes that reflect character of place and protect sensitive interfaces.
- The introduction of creative industries in employment areas.
