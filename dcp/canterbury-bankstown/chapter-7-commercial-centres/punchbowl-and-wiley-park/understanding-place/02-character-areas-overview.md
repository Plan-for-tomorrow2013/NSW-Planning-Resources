# Section 2.2 — Character Areas (Overview)

The Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct will be comprised of different areas of distinct
character. Through detailed analysis, seven character areas have been identified.
Applicants are to identify which character area a development site is located in and be
cognisant of the key attributes of the existing context, where relevant, and the desired
future context for the character area.

> See Figure 10: Key features of Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct character areas — not converted, image only.

## Objectives

- **O1** To achieve the desired future character of the Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct and guide development to deliver this desired character.

## Controls

- **C1** Development must consider and address the outcomes expressed in the character area statements and principles.
- **C2** Development Applications must be accompanied by a site and context analysis plan that demonstrates an understanding of the manner a site integrates and is consistent with the desired future character area.

## Anomaly

The introductory text above states that "seven character areas have been identified", but
only **six** numbered character area subsections (2.2.1–2.2.6) appear in the Contents page,
the section headings, and Figure 10's legend (which lists six image captions, 1–6).
Preserved as printed; the identity of a possible seventh character area could not be
determined from this chapter's text — the discrepancy may be a drafting error carried over
from an earlier version of the Precinct boundary, or the "seven" figure may include the
Precinct as a whole as an implicit area. Not resolved.
