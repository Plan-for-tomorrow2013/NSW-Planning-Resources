# Section 2.2.6 — Canterbury Road (Character Area)

The Canterbury Road character area will continue to be an important vehicular connection
as well as providing employment opportunities for the local area. Commercial and retail
development is prioritised along the corridor with some mixed use development
concentrated at key intersections including Broadway and Duncan Streets, Punchbowl
Road, Rosemont Street and Victoria Road.

The corridor will support mixed use and commercial infill opportunities ranging from 4 to 8
storeys. A minimum 5m ground floor to ceiling height will support large format commercial
uses including showroom retail, bulky goods, as well as business uses located below
residential apartments.

A setback from Canterbury Road at ground level will enable opportunities for streetscape
improvements including widened footpaths, verges and landscaping. Similarly rear
setbacks and setbacks on side streets will enable increased opportunities for deep soil and
greening to soften the corridor and improve pedestrian amenity.

Within mixed use development, residential uses are to be located above the ground floor,
well separated and oriented away from the busy roads to provide for the best amenity
outcomes.

The corridor will link back to Punchbowl and Wiley Park Village character areas via
enhanced connections along Broadway, Rossmore Avenue and King Georges Road.

**Built form features shown in Figure 19**: 6–8 storey commercial and mixed use
developments; front setback for paving, landscaping, trees and incidental retail activation;
residential apartments preferably oriented away from Canterbury Road; communal open
space on podiums and rooftops; vehicle access, servicing and loading away from
Canterbury Road.

## Principles

- **P1** Ground-floor commercial and retail spaces along Canterbury Road will feature active edges along their frontages and at key corners. Additionally, there will be opportunities for landscape improvements. These will include enhanced paving and deep soil areas for tree canopy and plantings to enhance amenity for pedestrians along this busy vehicular thoroughfare. Refer to Section 4.3.2 Active street frontages and edges and Section 3.4 Tree canopy, deep soil and green cover.
- **P2** New development will contribute to enhanced lanes by incorporating generous setbacks of 3 to 6 metres to facilitate ground level landscaping and opportunity to create secondary frontages balanced with servicing needs and driveways. Additionally, extended lanes will provide improved permeability and alternative pedestrian and cycle routes away from Canterbury Road. Refer to Section 3.3 Streets, shared lanes, pedestrian links and arcades.
- **P3** New development will be sympathetic to the variety of scales and uses along Canterbury Road. In particular, new developments that interface with the low scale residential areas to the north and Punchbowl Public School will provide a sensitive interface that considers scale, setbacks, appropriate privacy and landscaping. Refer to Section 4.2 The building envelope.
- **P4** New mixed-use developments should be concentrated at key cross streets, with residential units situated above ground level. These units must be designed and oriented to mitigate amenity impacts from noise and pollution originating from Canterbury Road while also ensuring adequate solar access, views, landscaping and high-quality podium and rooftop space. Refer to Section 4.2 The building envelope and 4.6 Rooftop and communal open space.
