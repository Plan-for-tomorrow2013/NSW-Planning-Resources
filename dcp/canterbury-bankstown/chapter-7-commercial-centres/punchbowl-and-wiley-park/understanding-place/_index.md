## Section 2 — Understanding Place

This section establishes the unique social, spatial and urban context that reflects the
Punchbowl and Wiley Park Precinct. Proposed development must contribute to delivering
the Vision for Punchbowl and Wiley Park by adhering to the Structure Plan and
demonstrating how a site's development contributes to the character areas and has regard
for Connecting with Country principles.

### Structure

The six character areas (2.2.1–2.2.6) split the Precinct two ways at once: two are named for
a Village Centre (Punchbowl Village, Wiley Park Village), three are named for a built-form
typology that recurs across both centres (Urban living, Mid-rise living, Low-rise living), and
one is named for a corridor that connects the two centres (Canterbury Road). A site's
character area is therefore not simply "which suburb" — a Punchbowl site could sit in the
Punchbowl Village, Mid-rise living, Low-rise living, or Canterbury Road character area
depending on its location, and likewise for Wiley Park.

### Anomaly

Section 2.2's introductory text states "seven character areas have been identified," but
only six are named, numbered and mapped (2.2.1–2.2.6) anywhere in this chapter — see
`02-character-areas-overview.md` for detail. Not resolved.
