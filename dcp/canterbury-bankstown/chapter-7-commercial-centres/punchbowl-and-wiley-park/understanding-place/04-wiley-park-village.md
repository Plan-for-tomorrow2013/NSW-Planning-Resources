# Section 2.2.2 — Wiley Park Village (Character Area)

The implementation of the new Metro station and additional population density will inject
activity into this character area. The land in this location is comprised of two key sites
which will provide housing and active uses at street level.

Wiley Park Village character area will provide a range of retail and other services, as well as
being a place that will attract the community to linger. The existing station area will be
expanded and will focus retail activation off busy King Georges Road on side streets such
as Lakemba Street and Stanlea Parade, where it is quieter with greater amenity. Additional
setbacks will assist in re-imagining these as higher quality environments for active uses.

New high-rise mixed-use residential developments will be encouraged near the Metro
station to concentrate housing, activities and amenities in this highly accessible area.

A continuous and active 4 storey street wall is proposed along King Georges Road, Lakemba
Street and Stanlea Parade to provide a consistent built form character. Tower forms are to
be set back from the street and be well-separated to ensure good solar access and view
sharing. The architectural character will incorporate textured materials such as face brick
and timber, reflecting the existing masonry character of Wiley Park.

## Principles

- **P1** Built form: New developments along Lakemba Street shall ensure for:
  (a) A street wall height of between 4 to 6 storeys that aligns with the predominant existing parapet height and is designed to reflect the detailing of neighbouring buildings including parapets and windows. See Section 4.2.3 Street wall.
  (b) A fine-grain character with vibrant active frontages with glazing and openings that are engaging. See Section 4.3.2 Active street frontages and edges.
  (c) Materials that reflect the traditional brickwork and masonry of surrounding buildings including heritage. See Section 4.5 Materials and finishes.
  (d) Upper level setbacks above the street wall to provide green podium communal spaces and passive surveillance. See Section 4.6 Rooftop and communal open space.
- **P2** Lanes: Wiley Lane and Stanlea Parade will be enhanced to better accommodate pedestrians and cyclists, while also facilitating service vehicles and driveways, creating a high-amenity network of lanes that promotes safety and accessibility for all users.
- **P3** Development interfacing lanes should:
  - For commercial uses: consider opportunities for active lane edges, including cafes, outdoor seating, planters and where possible deep soil.
  - For residential uses: provide high quality landscaping, deep soil, and apartments with private entries. See Section 3.3 Streets, shared lanes, pedestrian links and arcades.
- **P4** Key sites: Development on the key sites are to provide improved permeability and interfaces to streets and deliver new open space. See Section 6 Key sites KS1 and KS3.

## Controls

None printed for this character area — Principles only.
