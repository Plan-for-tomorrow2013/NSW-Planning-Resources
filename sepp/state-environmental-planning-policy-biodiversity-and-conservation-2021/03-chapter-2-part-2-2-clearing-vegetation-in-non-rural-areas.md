# State Environmental Planning Policy (Biodiversity and Conservation) 2021

### Part 2.2 Clearing vegetation in non-rural areas

#### 2.6 Clearing that requires permit or approval

(1) A person must not, in a non-rural area of the State to which Part 2.3 applies, do the following without the authority conferred by a permit granted by the council under that part—

- (a) clear vegetation,
- (b) clear prescribed vegetation.

(2) A person must not, in a non-rural area of the State that exceeds the biodiversity offsets scheme threshold, do the following without the authority conferred by an approval granted by the Native Vegetation Panel under Part 2.4—

- (a) clear native vegetation,
- (b) clear prescribed native vegetation.

(3) Subsection (2) does not apply to—

- (a) clearing on biodiversity certified land under the *Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016*, Part 8, or
- (b) clearing of vegetation that poses a potential risk to human life or property, or
- (c) clearing of dead vegetation.

(4) Clearing of vegetation is not authorised under this section unless the conditions to which the authorisation is subject are complied with.

(5) Subsection (4) extends to a condition that imposes an obligation on the person who clears the vegetation that must be complied with before or after the clearing is carried out.

(6) For the purposes of the Act, section 4.3, clearing vegetation that requires a permit or approval under this Chapter is prohibited if the clearing is not carried out in accordance with the permit or approval.

(7) In this section, vegetation or native vegetation is ***prescribed***, if—

- (a) the vegetation or native vegetation—
  - (i) is listed on a register of significant trees kept by the council, or
  - (ii) has a height greater than or equal to 10m, or
  - (iii) has a canopy area of 100m<sup>2</sup> or more, or
  - (iv) has an area of 100m<sup>2</sup> or more,
  - (v) has a diameter at standard height of 30cm or more, or
  - (vi) is part of an area or areas occupied, or periodically or occasionally occupied, by a species or ecological community, including any biotic or abiotic component, or
  - (vii) whether living or dead that has a hollow that—
    - (a) can be seen, and
    - (b) has a width of at least 5cm, and
    - (c) appears to have depth, and
    - (d) is at least 1m above the ground, or
- (b) the vegetation or native vegetation is located on the following land—
  - (i) land in the following zones—
    - (A) R4 High Density Residential,
    - (B) RE1 Public Recreation,
    - (C) SP2 Infrastructure,
    - (D) C2 Environmental Conservation,
    - (E) C3 Environmental Management,
    - (F) C4 Environmental Living,
  - (ii) land that is public land or a public place within the meaning of the *Local Government Act 1993*,
  - (iii) land identified in an environmental planning instrument as the following—
    - (A) an environmentally sensitive area,
    - (B) a foreshore area,
    - (C) a riparian area,
    - (D) a scenic protection area,
    - (E) a nature reserve,
  - (iv) land owned or in the control of a public authority.

**s 2.6:** Am 2026 (583), Sch 1[8] [9].

#### 2.7 Clearing that does not require permit or approval

(1) A permit or approval to clear vegetation is not required under this Chapter if it is clearing of a kind that is authorised under the *Local Land Services Act 2013*, section 60O or Part 5B.

(2) Despite the *Local Land Services Act 2013*, section 60O(a)(i) and (b)(i), subsection (1) does not apply to clearing that is part of or ancillary to the carrying out of exempt development or complying development.

(3) A permit or approval is not required under this chapter for clearing for a traditional Aboriginal cultural activity, other than a commercial cultural activity.

(4) A permit or approval is not required under this chapter for the removal of vegetation that is authorised in writing by a council.

(5) A council must not give a written authorisation under subsection (4) unless the council is satisfied vegetation to be cleared poses an imminent risk to human life or habitable property.

> **Note—** See also Part 2.5, which sets out certain clearing that does not require a permit or approval under this Chapter if the clearing—
>
> - (a) is carried out on certain primary production land in Zone R5, C2, C3 or C4, and
> - (b) complies with the requirements of Part 2.5.

**s 2.7:** Am 2024 (42), Sch 1.38[5]; 2026 (583), Sch 1[10].

#### 2.8 Clearing permitted without development consent

Clearing of vegetation in a non-rural area of the State is permitted without development consent if—

- (a) the clearing—
  - (i) is not ancillary to the carrying out of other development, and
  - (ii) does not require a permit or approval, and
- (b) the vegetation is not—
  - (i) a heritage item or an Aboriginal object, or
  - (ii) located in a heritage conservation area or Aboriginal place of heritage significance.
