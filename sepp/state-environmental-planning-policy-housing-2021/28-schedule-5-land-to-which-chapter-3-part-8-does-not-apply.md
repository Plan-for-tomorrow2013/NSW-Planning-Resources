# State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021

## Schedule 5 Land to which Chapter 3, Part 8 does not apply

section 119

> **Note.** It is intended that descriptions of land will be entered in this Schedule if special provision is made for that land by a local environmental plan made in accordance with principles set out in a Direction under section 117 of the Act given in conjunction with Chapter 3, Part 8.
