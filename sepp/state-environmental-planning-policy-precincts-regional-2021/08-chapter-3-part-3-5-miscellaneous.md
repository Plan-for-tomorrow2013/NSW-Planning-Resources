# State Environmental Planning Policy (Precincts—Regional) 2021

### Part 3.5 Miscellaneous

**pt 3.5:** Ins 2024 (427), Sch 1[5].

#### 3.22 Thermal energy from waste development

(1) This section applies to the following Activation Precincts—

- (a) Parkes Activation Precinct,
- (b) Wagga Wagga Activation Precinct,
- (c) Moree Activation Precinct.

(2) Development consent must not be granted to thermal energy from waste development unless the consent authority is satisfied that the development is consistent with the *NSW Energy from Waste Policy Statement* published by the Environment Protection Authority in June 2021.

(3) In this section—

***energy recovery*** has the same meaning as in the *Protection of the Environment Operations (General) Regulation 2022*, section 142(1).

***thermal energy from waste development*** means development for the purposes of electricity generating works, industries or waste or resource management facilities involving, or carried out to enable, the thermal treatment of waste involving or resulting in energy recovery from the waste.

***thermal treatment*** has the same meaning as in the *Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997*, Schedule 1.

***waste*** has the same meaning as in the *Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997*.
