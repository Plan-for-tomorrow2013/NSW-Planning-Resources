## Schedule 7 Complying development—Chapter 5

(Section 5.20)

**sch 7, hdg:** Am 2022 (350), Sch 1[10].

> **Note.** *State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying Development Codes) 2008* specifies complying development and the complying development conditions for that development under that Policy.
