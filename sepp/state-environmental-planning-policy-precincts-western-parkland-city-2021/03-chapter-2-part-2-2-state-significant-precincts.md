# State Environmental Planning Policy (Precincts—Western Parkland City) 2021

### Part 2.2 State significant precincts

#### 2.7 State significant precincts

(1) Each Appendix made under this Chapter describes a State significant precinct.

(2) The provisions in an Appendix made under this Chapter relating to the carrying out of development on a State significant precinct have effect.

**s 2.7:** Am 2022 (835), Sch 4[4].

#### 2.8 Guidelines for proposals for State significant precinct listing

(1) The Minister may publish guidelines for proposals to amend an Appendix made under this Chapter.

(2) This section does not preclude an amendment of an Appendix made under this Chapter without compliance with those guidelines.

**s 2.8:** Am 2022 (835), Sch 4[4].
