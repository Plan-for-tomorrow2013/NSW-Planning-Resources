# State Environmental Planning Policy (Precincts—Western Parkland City) 2021

## Schedule 10 Retail and commercial development restricted

(Section 6.60)

Development for the purpose of—

- clubs,
- fast food take-away restaurants,
- hotels,
- local retail or commercial buildings,
- medical centres,
- restaurants.
